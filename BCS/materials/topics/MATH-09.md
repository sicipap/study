# Ratio & proportion

> **Why it matters:** Ratio (অনুপাত) and proportion (সমানুপাত) are asked directly (mean/third/fourth proportional, combining ratios) and also sit inside partnership, age, mixture and speed questions. BCS and bank papers use them every year.

## Key concepts
- **Ratio a : b** compares two quantities of the **same kind and unit**. a is the antecedent (পূর্ব রাশি), b the consequent (উত্তর রাশি). A ratio has no unit.
- Multiplying or dividing both terms by the same non-zero number does not change the ratio.
- **Proportion:** a : b = c : d (written a : b :: c : d). Then **ad = bc** (product of extremes = product of means).
- **Continued proportion (ক্রমিক সমানুপাত):** a : b = b : c → **b² = ac**; b is the **mean proportional (মধ্য সমানুপাতী)**.
- **Inverse ratio (ব্যস্ত অনুপাত)** of a : b is b : a (or 1/a : 1/b).
- **Compound ratio (মিশ্র অনুপাত)** of a : b and c : d is ac : bd.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| 🔥 Mean proportional of a and b | √(ab) |
| 🔥 Third proportional to a and b | b²/a |
| Fourth proportional to a, b, c | bc/a |
| Duplicate ratio of a : b | a² : b² |
| Sub-duplicate ratio | √a : √b |
| Triplicate ratio | a³ : b³ |
| Sub-triplicate ratio | ∛a : ∛b |
| Divide N in ratio a : b | aN/(a + b) and bN/(a + b) |
| Combine A : B = a : b and B : C = c : d | A : B : C = ac : bc : bd |
| Divide in ratio 1/x : 1/y : 1/z | multiply by LCM of x, y, z to get whole numbers first |
| Number x added to each term of a : b to make c : d | solve (a + x)/(b + x) = c/d |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5. Find A : B : C.
Steps: Make B equal: A : B = 8 : 12, B : C = 12 : 15 → 8 : 12 : 15.
**Answer: 8 : 12 : 15**

**Example 2.** Find the mean proportional between 4 and 16.
Steps: √(4 × 16) = √64 = 8.
**Answer: 8**

**Example 3.** Find the third proportional to 4 and 6.
Steps: 6²/4 = 36/4 = 9.
**Answer: 9**

**Example 4.** Find the fourth proportional to 3, 5 and 12.
Steps: 5 × 12/3 = 20.
**Answer: 20**

**Example 5.** What number must be added to each term of 7 : 11 to make it 3 : 4?
Steps: (7 + x)/(11 + x) = 3/4 → 28 + 4x = 33 + 3x → x = 5.
**Answer: 5**

**Example 6.** Divide Tk 1,200 between two people in the ratio 3 : 5.
Steps: 3 + 5 = 8 parts; one part = 150 → 450 and 750.
**Answer: Tk 450 and Tk 750**

## Common traps
- Third proportional to a and b is **b²/a**, not a²/b.
- When combining A : B and B : C, first make the **common term B** equal.
- For shares in ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4, convert to 6 : 4 : 3 (multiply by LCM 12). Do not use 2 : 3 : 4.
- Change units to be the same before forming a ratio (e.g. 50 paisa : Tk 2 = 1 : 4).

## Quick revision
- a : b = c : d ⇒ ad = bc.
- Mean proportional = √(ab).
- Third proportional = b²/a; fourth = bc/a.
- Duplicate = a² : b²; sub-duplicate = √a : √b.
- A : B : C = ac : bc : bd.
- Compound ratio of a : b and c : d = ac : bd.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Tk 780 is divided in the ratio 5 : 8. The larger share is:
(a) Tk 300 (b) Tk 480 (c) Tk 468 (d) Tk 520

**2.** If A : B = 3 : 4 and B : C = 6 : 7, then A : B : C = ?
(a) 3 : 4 : 7 (b) 18 : 24 : 7 (c) 9 : 12 : 14 (d) 6 : 8 : 7

**3.** The mean proportional between 9 and 25 is:
(a) 17 (b) 15 (c) 16 (d) 225

**4.** The third proportional to 9 and 12 is:
(a) 16 (b) 15 (c) 18 (d) 6.75

**5.** The fourth proportional to 4, 9 and 12 is:
(a) 18 (b) 16 (c) 36 (d) 27

**6.** The duplicate ratio of 3 : 4 is:
(a) 6 : 8 (b) 9 : 16 (c) √3 : 2 (d) 27 : 64

**7.** If a : b = 2 : 3, then (2a + 3b) : (3a + 2b) = ?
(a) 12 : 13 (b) 1 : 1 (c) 13 : 12 (d) 4 : 9

**8.** Tk 2,600 is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4. B gets:
(a) Tk 800 (b) Tk 600 (c) Tk 1,200 (d) Tk 866

**9.** What number must be subtracted from each of 15 and 19 so that the ratio becomes 3 : 4?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 3

**10.** Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If 9 is added to each, the ratio becomes 3 : 4. The smaller number is:
(a) 15 (b) 6 (c) 9 (d) 12

|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | 780/13 = 60; 8 × 60 = 480 |

| 3 | b | √(9 × 25) = 15 |

| 5 | d | 9 × 12/4 = 27 |
| 6 | b | 3² : 4² = 9 : 16 |
