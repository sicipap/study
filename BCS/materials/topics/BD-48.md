# Important dates & "firsts" of Bangladesh

> **Why it matters:** "First President", "first Speaker", "first woman …" and national-day dates are the easiest marks in any Bangladesh-affairs section — and the easiest to confuse. Learn them as tables.

## National days and key dates 🔥🔥
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **21 February** | **শহীদ দিবস** (1952 Language Movement) and **International Mother Language Day** (UNESCO, declared **17 November 1999**) |
| **7 March** | **Historic speech** of Sheikh Mujibur Rahman at the **Racecourse (now Suhrawardy Udyan), 1971**; in UNESCO's Memory of the World Register (2017) |
| **25 March** | **Operation Searchlight (1971)**; observed as **Genocide Day (গণহত্যা দিবস)** since **2017** |
| **26 March** | **Independence Day (স্বাধীনতা দিবস)** |
| **10 April 1971** | **Mujibnagar Government** formed; **Proclamation of Independence** issued |
| **17 April 1971** | Mujibnagar Government sworn in at **Baidyanathtala, Meherpur** (renamed Mujibnagar) |
| **14 December** | **Martyred Intellectuals Day (শহীদ বুদ্ধিজীবী দিবস)** |
| **16 December** | **Victory Day (বিজয় দিবস)**: Pakistani forces surrendered at the **Racecourse, 1971** |
| **10 January 1972** | Sheikh Mujibur Rahman returned home from Pakistan |
| **4 November 1972** | **Constitution adopted**; in force from **16 December 1972** |
| **7 March 1973** | **First parliamentary election** |
| **17 September 1974** | Bangladesh joined the **UN** (136th member) |
| **2 December 1997** | **CHT Peace Accord** |
| **1 August 2015** | Enclave exchange took effect |
| **25 June 2022** | **Padma Bridge** inaugurated |
| **5 August 2024** | Sheikh Hasina resigned during the mass uprising |
| **8 August 2024** | **Interim government** took oath (Chief Adviser **Dr Muhammad Yunus**) |

### Surrender on 16 December 1971
- Pakistani commander **Lt Gen A. A. K. Niazi** signed the instrument of surrender before **Lt Gen Jagjit Singh Aurora** (Joint Command).
- **Group Captain A. K. Khandker** represented Bangladesh.
- Commander-in-Chief of the Bangladesh forces: **General M. A. G. Osmani**.

## Quick revision
- Mujibnagar Government: formed **10 April**, sworn in **17 April 1971**.
- First PM **Tajuddin Ahmad**; first Speaker **Shah Abdul Hamid**; first Chief Justice **A. S. M. Sayem**; first CEC **Justice M. Idris**.
- Constitution adopted **4 Nov 1972**, effective **16 Dec 1972**.
- First election **7 March 1973**.
- First woman PM **Khaleda Zia**; first woman Speaker **Shirin Sharmin Chaudhury**.
- First caretaker Chief Adviser **Justice Muhammad Habibur Rahman (1996)**.
- First submarines **2017**; first satellite **2018**.
