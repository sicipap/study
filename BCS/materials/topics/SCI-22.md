# Food science & preservation

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask types of sugars (milk sugar, fruit sugar), which chemical is in tea/chilli/turmeric, pasteurisation and its inventor, food preservatives (sodium benzoate, KMS), harmful adulterants (formalin, calcium carbide) and Bangladesh food-safety laws.

## Nutrients and food chemistry 🔥
**Six food components**: carbohydrate, protein, fat, vitamins, minerals, water. Energy: **carbohydrate ≈ 4 kcal/g, protein ≈ 4 kcal/g, fat ≈ 9 kcal/g**.

| Carbohydrate | Type | Found in |
|---|---|---|
| **Glucose** (grape sugar) | Monosaccharide | Fruits, blood sugar |
| **Fructose (fruit sugar)** | Monosaccharide | Fruits, **honey**; **sweetest natural sugar** |
| Galactose | Monosaccharide | Part of lactose |
| **Sucrose (cane sugar)** | Disaccharide (glucose + fructose) | **Sugarcane, beet** — table sugar |
| **Lactose (milk sugar)** | Disaccharide (glucose + galactose) | **Milk** |
| **Maltose (malt sugar)** | Disaccharide (glucose + glucose) | Germinating grain |
| **Starch** | Polysaccharide | Rice, wheat, potato |
| **Cellulose** | Polysaccharide | Plant cell walls; **humans cannot digest it** — acts as **roughage (fibre)**, prevents constipation |
| **Glycogen ("animal starch")** | Polysaccharide | Stored in **liver and muscles** |

- **Proteins** are made of **amino acids** (about **20** kinds; **9 are essential** for adults and must come from food). Animal proteins (meat, fish, egg, milk) are "**first-class**" (complete) proteins. **Pulses (ডাল)** are called the "**poor man's meat**". Soybean ≈ 40% protein.
- Important food proteins: **casein (milk)**, **albumin (egg white)**, **gluten (wheat)**, **myosin (muscle)**.
- **Fats**: saturated fats (ghee, butter, animal fat) are solid at room temperature; unsaturated fats (soybean, mustard, sunflower oil) are liquid. **Hydrogenation** of vegetable oil with a **nickel catalyst** makes **vanaspati (Dalda)**, which may contain harmful **trans fat**.
- **Milk** is nearly complete food but is **poor in iron and vitamin C**. Honey mainly contains fructose and glucose.

## Food tests
- **Starch + iodine → blue-black**. **Glucose (reducing sugar) + Benedict's solution (heated) → brick-red**. **Protein + biuret reagent → violet/purple**. Fat leaves a **translucent spot** on paper.

## Microbes in food
- **Fermentation**: **yeast** converts sugar into **alcohol + CO₂** (bread rises, wine/beer). **Lactobacillus** (lactic acid bacteria) turns **milk into curd (দই)/yoghurt**. **Acetobacter** makes **vinegar**. Cheese uses rennet and bacteria.
- **Spoilage** is caused by bacteria, moulds (fungi) and yeast, plus enzymes and oxidation.
- **Aflatoxin**, a cancer-causing toxin, is made by the mould ***Aspergillus flavus*** on damp **groundnuts** and grains. **Botulism** (*Clostridium botulinum*) comes from badly canned food.

## Food preservation methods 🔥🔥
| Method | Principle / example |
|---|---|
| **Drying / dehydration** | Removes water microbes need — dried fish (শুঁটকি), rice, pulses, milk powder |
| **Salting** | Salt draws water out (osmosis) — salted hilsa, pickles |
| **Sugaring** | High sugar (osmosis) — **jam, jelly**, murabba |
| **Pickling** | **Vinegar (acetic acid)** or oil — আচার |
| **Refrigeration / freezing** | **Low temperature slows** microbial growth (fridge ≈ 4 °C; freezer ≈ −18 °C) |
| **Canning** | Heat-sterilised, airtight container — **Nicolas Appert** (France, early 1800s), "father of canning" |
| **Pasteurisation** | **Louis Pasteur** (1860s). Milk heated to **≈ 63 °C for 30 min** or **72 °C for 15 s**, then cooled quickly. **Kills harmful germs** without boiling |
| **UHT** | **≈ 135–140 °C for 2–5 seconds** → long shelf life without refrigeration |
| **Smoking** | Smoke dries and adds antimicrobial compounds — fish, meat |
| **Irradiation** | **Gamma rays (cobalt-60)** kill microbes and insects; stops sprouting of potato/onion |
| **Vacuum packing / freeze drying** | Removes oxygen / water |
| **Chemical preservatives** | See table below |

| Permitted preservative | Used in |
|---|---|
| **Sodium benzoate** | **Acidic foods**: fruit juices, sauces, ketchup, soft drinks |
| **Potassium metabisulphite (KMS)** | **Squashes, fruit juices**, wine (releases SO₂) |
| Sorbic acid / potassium sorbate | Cheese, bakery items |
| Citric acid, vinegar, salt, sugar | Natural preservatives |

## Harmful adulterants (Bangladesh context) 🔥
| Chemical | Illegal misuse | Harm |
|---|---|---|
| **Formalin** (formaldehyde) | To keep **fish, fruit, milk** "fresh" | Cancer, liver/kidney damage |
| **Calcium carbide** | **Artificial ripening** of mango, banana (gives acetylene) | Contains arsenic/phosphorus traces; nerve damage |
| **DDT / insecticides** | On **dried fish** | Toxic, cancer |
| **Textile dyes** | Colouring sweets, spices | Cancer, liver damage |
| **Urea / hydros** | Whitening **puffed rice (মুড়ি)** | Kidney damage |

- **Safe Food Act, 2013 (নিরাপদ খাদ্য আইন)**; under it the **Bangladesh Food Safety Authority (BFSA)** was set up (functional from **2015**).
- **BSTI** (Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution) sets product standards and certification marks.
- **Artificial sweeteners**: saccharin (hundreds of times sweeter than sugar), aspartame, sucralose. **MSG (tasting salt)** is a flavour enhancer.
- **BMI** = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²; normal **18.5–24.9**; 25–29.9 overweight; 30+ obese.

## Quick revision
- **Milk sugar = lactose; fruit sugar = fructose; cane sugar = sucrose**; animal starch = glycogen.
- Humans can't digest **cellulose** (roughage).
- **Tea: caffeine + tannin; chilli: capsaicin; turmeric: curcumin; tomato: lycopene.**
- **Pasteurisation (Pasteur)**: 63 °C/30 min or 72 °C/15 s; **UHT** ≈ 135–140 °C for seconds.
- **Canning: Nicolas Appert.**
- **Sodium benzoate** and **KMS** are permitted preservatives.
- **Formalin** (fish, fruit) and **calcium carbide** (ripening) are illegal adulterants.
- Curd: **Lactobacillus**; bread/alcohol: **yeast**; aflatoxin: *Aspergillus flavus*.
- **Safe Food Act 2013; BFSA 2015.**
