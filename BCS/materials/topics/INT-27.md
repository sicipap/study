# International days & years

> **Why it matters:** "When is World … Day?" is a quick, frequently asked MCQ. Many days mark the founding of a UN body (WHO — 7 April, FAO — 16 October, UN — 24 October). Also learn the International Years declared by the UN for the current and recent years.

## Days linked to Bangladesh 🔥
| Date | Day | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **21 February** | **International Mother Language Day** | Declared by **UNESCO** on **17 November 1999**; first observed **2000**; honours the **1952 Language Movement** martyrs |
| **25 March** | International Day of Remembrance of the Victims of Slavery (UN) | In Bangladesh, 25 March is observed nationally as **Genocide Day** (1971) |
| **2 April** | **World Autism Awareness Day** | Bangladesh has been active on autism resolutions at the UN |

**1.** World Environment Day is observed on —

(a) 7 April (b) 1 December (c) 31 May (d) 10 October

**3.** International Day of Peace is observed on —
(a) 24 October (b) 2 October (c) 10 December (d) 21 September

**4.** World Food Day (16 October) marks the founding of —
(a) WFP (b) IFAD (c) FAO (d) UNICEF

**5.** When is World Population Day?
(a) 11 July (b) 20 June (c) 8 September (d) 12 August

**6.** Human Rights Day is observed on —
(a) 9 December (b) 10 December (c) 20 November (d) 18 December

**7.** World Press Freedom Day is on —
(a) 1 May (b) 8 May (c) 3 May (d) 17 May

**8.** The UN declared 2025 the International Year of —
