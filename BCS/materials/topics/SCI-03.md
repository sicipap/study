# Work, energy, power

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask units (joule, watt, kWh, HP), energy conversions in devices, "when is work zero?", and simple calculations of kinetic energy and power.

## Work (কাজ) 🔥
- **Work = Force × displacement in the direction of force**: **W = F s cos θ**.
- Unit: **joule (J)** = 1 N × 1 m. Work is a **scalar**. Dimension: ML²T⁻².
- **Work is zero** when:
  - there is **no displacement** (pushing a wall, holding a heavy load while standing still);
  - force is **perpendicular** to displacement (θ = 90°): carrying a load on the head while walking on level ground; the Moon going round the Earth (centripetal force ⟂ motion).
- **Negative work**: friction on a moving body; work done by gravity on a body thrown upward.
- Other work/energy units: **1 erg = 10⁻⁷ J** (CGS); **1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J**; **1 calorie ≈ 4.2 J**; 1 electron-volt (eV) = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J.

## Energy (শক্তি) 🔥
Energy is the **capacity to do work**. Unit is the same as work: **joule**.

| Form | Formula / fact |
|---|---|
| **Kinetic energy (গতিশক্তি)** | **KE = ½ mv²**. Doubling speed → KE becomes **4 times** |
| **Potential energy (বিভবশক্তি)** | **PE = mgh** (gravitational). Also stored in a stretched spring, wound watch-spring, water in a dam |
| Others | Heat, light, sound, electrical, chemical, nuclear, magnetic energy |

- **Law of conservation of energy (শক্তির নিত্যতা সূত্র):** energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it only changes form. Total energy of the universe is constant.
- **Einstein's mass–energy relation: E = mc²** (1905) — mass can be converted into energy (basis of nuclear energy).
- A freely falling body: PE decreases, KE increases, total (PE + KE) stays constant.
- **Main source of energy on Earth: the Sun**. The Sun's energy comes from **nuclear fusion**.

## Energy conversions in devices 🔥
| Device | Conversion |
|---|---|
| **Electric motor / fan** | Electrical → **mechanical** |
| **Generator / dynamo** | **Mechanical → electrical** |
| Electric bulb | Electrical → light (+ heat) |
| Electric heater / iron | Electrical → heat |
| **Battery / cell (discharging)** | **Chemical → electrical** |
| Storage battery (charging) | Electrical → chemical |
| **Solar cell** | **Light (solar) → electrical** |
| Microphone | Sound → electrical |
| Loudspeaker | Electrical → sound |
| Steam engine / car engine | Heat (chemical) → mechanical |
| Photosynthesis | Light → chemical |
| Hydroelectric plant (Kaptai) | Potential energy of water → kinetic → electrical |
| Nuclear reactor | Nuclear → heat → electrical |

## Power (ক্ষমতা) 🔥
- **Power = work done per unit time**: **P = W/t**. It measures the **rate** of doing work.
- Unit: **watt (W) = 1 J/s** (after James Watt, improver of the steam engine).
- **1 horsepower (HP) = 746 W** (the term HP was coined by James Watt).
- 1 kW = 1,000 W; 1 MW = 10⁶ W.
- **kilowatt-hour (kWh)** is a unit of **energy**, not power. The "unit" on an electricity bill = **1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J = 3.6 MJ**.
- Electrical power: **P = VI = I²R = V²/R**.

### Worked examples
1. A 1,000 W heater runs for 2 hours. Energy used? → 1 kW × 2 h = **2 kWh (2 units)**.
2. A 2 kg ball moves at 3 m/s. KE? → ½ × 2 × 3² = **9 J**.
3. A 10 kg load is raised 5 m (g = 9.8). PE? → 10 × 9.8 × 5 = **490 J**.
4. A 60 W bulb burns 5 hours a day for 30 days. Units? → 60 × 5 × 30 = 9,000 Wh = **9 kWh**.

## Efficiency (কর্মদক্ষতা)
- **Efficiency = (useful output ÷ input) × 100%**. It is always **less than 100%** in real machines because of friction and heat loss.
- Simple machines (lever, pulley, inclined plane, screw, wedge, wheel and axle) give **mechanical advantage**; they do not reduce total work.
- Levers: **1st class** — fulcrum in middle (scissors, see-saw, pliers); **2nd class** — load in middle (nutcracker, wheelbarrow); **3rd class** — effort in middle (forceps/tweezers, fishing rod, human forearm).

## Quick revision
- Unit of work and energy: **joule**; unit of power: **watt**.
- **1 HP = 746 W**; **1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J** (unit of energy).
- **KE = ½mv²**, **PE = mgh**; double speed → 4 times KE.
- Pushing a wall or carrying a load on level ground → **work done = 0** (in physics).
- **E = mc²** (Einstein, 1905).
- **Generator: mechanical → electrical; motor: electrical → mechanical.**
- **Battery: chemical → electrical**; solar cell: light → electrical.
- Nutcracker = 2nd class lever; forceps = 3rd class lever.
