# ICT in Bangladesh: satellite, submarine cables, cyber law

> **Why it matters:** Bangladesh-specific ICT facts are asked in almost every BCS and bank paper: the launch of Bangladesh's satellite, the landing stations of SEA-ME-WE 4 and 5, and the laws on cyber crime (ICT Act 2006 → Digital Security Act 2018 → Cyber Security Act 2023 → 2025 ordinance).

| Event | Year / fact |

## Bangladesh Satellite-1 (বাংলাদেশ স্যাটেলাইট-১) 🔥🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Former name | **Bangabandhu Satellite-1** (বঙ্গবন্ধু স্যাটেলাইট-১) |
| Type | **Geostationary communication satellite** — Bangladesh's **first** satellite |
| 🔥 Launch date | **12 May 2018** (Bangladesh time; it was still 11 May in the USA) |
| Launch site | **Kennedy Space Center, Florida, USA** |
| 🔥 Rocket / launcher | **SpaceX Falcon 9** (the first launch of the Block 5 version) |
| 🔥 Manufacturer | **Thales Alenia Space** (France–Italy) |
| 🔥 Orbital slot | **119.1° East** (bought from Russia's **Intersputnik**) |
| Transponders | **40**: **26 Ku-band + 14 C-band** |
| Ground stations | Main: **Gazipur**; backup: **Betbunia, Rangamati** |
| Operator | **Bangladesh Satellite Company Limited (BSCL)** |
| Expected life | About **15 years** |
| Uses | Direct-to-home (DTH) TV, TV channel broadcasting, internet and communication for remote areas, disaster communication |

- With this launch Bangladesh became the **57th country** to own a satellite (the figure most guides give).
- It was later renamed from "Bangabandhu Satellite-1" to "Bangladesh Satellite-1"; older guidebooks use the old name.

## Submarine cables (সাবমেরিন ক্যাবল) 🔥🔥
Submarine cables are **optical fibre cables under the sea** that carry most international internet traffic. In Bangladesh they are run by **Bangladesh Submarine Cable PLC (BSCPLC)**, earlier **Bangladesh Submarine Cable Company Limited (BSCCL)**.

**SEA-ME-WE** = **South East Asia–Middle East–Western Europe**.

| Cable | Landing station | Connected |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **SEA-ME-WE 4** (1st cable) | **Cox's Bazar** (Jhilongja) | **2006** (21 May 2006) |
| 🔥 **SEA-ME-WE 5** (2nd cable) | **Kuakata, Patuakhali** | **2017** |
| **SEA-ME-WE 6** (3rd cable, under construction) | Cox's Bazar | Expected to be in service around 2025–26 |

- Memory hook: **4 → Cox's Bazar**, **5 → Kuakata**.
- Before 2006, Bangladesh used only **satellite (VSAT)** links for the internet.
- **ITC (International Terrestrial Cable):** licensed private companies (from **2012**) bring bandwidth over land from India, as a backup to submarine cables.

## Cyber and ICT laws of Bangladesh 🔥🔥
| Law | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Bangladesh Telecommunication Act 2001** | Created **BTRC** (the telecom regulator) |
| 🔥 **ICT Act 2006** (তথ্য ও যোগাযোগ প্রযুক্তি আইন, ২০০৬) | First main cyber law; recognised **electronic records and digital signatures**; created the **Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA)** and a **Cyber Tribunal**. Amended in **2013** (harsher punishment). Its **Section 57** (online defamation/"hurting religious sentiment") was heavily criticised |
| **Pornography Control Act 2012** | Punishes making and spreading pornography, including online |
| 🔥 **Digital Security Act 2018** (ডিজিটাল নিরাপত্তা আইন, ২০১৮) | Passed in **September 2018**; repealed Section 57 of the ICT Act (and related sections); created the **Digital Security Agency**. Widely criticised for curbing free speech |
| 🔥 **Cyber Security Act 2023** (সাইবার নিরাপত্তা আইন, ২০২৩) | Passed in **September 2023**; **replaced the Digital Security Act 2018**; the agency became the **National Cyber Security Agency (NCSA)**; reduced some punishments (e.g. many defamation offences made fine-only) |
| 🔥 **Cyber Security Ordinance 2025** (সাইবার সুরক্ষা অধ্যাদেশ, ২০২৫) | Issued by the **interim government** in **2025**; **replaced the Cyber Security Act 2023** and removed several speech-related offences |

- **Sequence to remember:** **ICT Act 2006 → Digital Security Act 2018 → Cyber Security Act 2023 → Cyber Security Ordinance 2025.**
- **BGD e-GOV CIRT** is the national Computer Incident Response Team.

> 🔄 Update: cyber laws were changed in 2025 and may be amended again (a parliament must pass or re-enact ordinances). Check the latest version and any data-protection law before the exam.

## Quick revision
- Bangladesh's first satellite launched **12 May 2018**, **Falcon 9**, Kennedy Space Center; made by **Thales Alenia Space**.
- Orbital slot **119.1° E**; **40 transponders** (26 Ku + 14 C); main ground station **Gazipur**, backup **Betbunia**.
- Bangladesh = **57th** satellite-owning country.
- SEA-ME-WE 4: **Cox's Bazar, 2006**; SEA-ME-WE 5: **Kuakata, 2017**.
- SEA-ME-WE = **South East Asia–Middle East–Western Europe**.
- ICT Act **2006** (amended 2013); Digital Security Act **2018**; Cyber Security Act **2023**; Cyber Security Ordinance **2025**.
- BTRC formed under the Telecommunication Act **2001**.
