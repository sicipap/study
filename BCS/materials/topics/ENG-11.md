# Subject-verb agreement

> **Why it matters:** This is the single most tested grammar area in error detection: "one of the …", "each/every", "either … or", "as well as", "a number of / the number of", and collective nouns.

## Basic rule
A **singular subject takes a singular verb**, and a **plural subject takes a plural verb**. "He **writes**." "They **write**." Find the real subject and ignore words between the subject and the verb:
- "**The quality** of the mangoes **is** good." (not *are*)
- "**The leader**, with all his followers, **was** arrested."

## Rules exams love 🔥
| # | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Two subjects joined by **and** → plural | "Rahim **and** Karim **are** friends." |
| 2 | **and** but one person/thing or one idea → singular | "**The poet and philosopher is** dead." (one person) "**Bread and butter is** my breakfast." "**Slow and steady wins** the race." |
| 3 | **each / every** + noun joined by and → singular | "**Every** boy and girl **was** present." "**Each** man and woman **has** a vote." |
| 4 | **as well as, with, along with, together with, besides, in addition to, like, including, accompanied by** → verb agrees with the **first** subject | "The captain, **as well as** the players, **was** happy." |
| 5 | **either … or / neither … nor / or / nor / not only … but also** → verb agrees with the **nearer** subject | "Neither the teacher nor **the students were** present." "Either you or **he is** wrong." |
| 6 | **one of the + plural noun + singular verb** 🔥 | "**One of the boys is** absent." |
| 7 | **one of the + plural noun + who/that + plural verb** | "He is one of **the best players who have** played for Bangladesh." |
| 8 | **each, every, everyone, everybody, anyone, someone, no one, nobody, nothing, either, neither** → singular | "**Everyone is** here." "**Neither** of the answers **is** correct." |
| 9 | **A number of** + plural noun → **plural** | "**A number of** students **were** absent." |
| 10 | **The number of** + plural noun → **singular** | "**The number of** students **is** increasing." |
| 11 | **many a** + singular noun → **singular** | "**Many a man has** fallen." |
| 12 | **More than one** + singular noun → singular | "**More than one** student **was** punished." |
| 13 | **Collective noun** as a unit → singular; as individuals → plural | "The **jury has** given its verdict." "The **jury are** divided in their opinions." |
| 14 | Always-plural nouns → plural | "The **police are** coming." "**Cattle are** grazing." "My **scissors are** blunt." |
| 15 | Plural-looking singulars → singular | "**Mathematics is** easy." "**The news is** true." |
| 16 | Amount, distance, time, money as one sum → singular | "**Ten miles is** a long way." "**Five thousand taka is** enough." "**Two years is** a long time." |
| 17 | Title of a book / country name → singular | "***Gulliver's Travels* is** a satire." "**The United States is** rich." |
| 18 | **The + adjective** (people) → plural | "**The rich are** not always happy." "**The poor need** help." |
| 19 | **None / all / some / most / half / part of + noun** → agrees with the noun after "of" | "**Some of the milk is** sour." "**Some of the boys are** late." "**Half of the money was** spent." |
| 20 | **There / Here** + verb + subject → verb agrees with what follows | "**There is** a book." "**There are** many books." |
| 21 | Relative pronoun → verb agrees with the antecedent | "It is **I who am** responsible." |
| 22 | **Not only X but also Y** → agrees with Y | "Not only the students but also **the teacher was** there." |

## Tricky subjects
- **Gerund / infinitive / clause** as subject → singular: "**Walking** in the morning **is** good." "**What he says is** true."
- **Percentage / fraction** + uncountable → singular; + plural noun → plural: "**Sixty percent of the land is** fertile." "**Sixty percent of the voters were** women."
- **a pair of** → singular: "**A pair of** shoes **is** under the bed." but "My **trousers are** torn."
- **The majority** → plural if people are meant: "**The majority** of students **are** girls."

## Common mistakes
| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| One of my friends are a doctor. | One of my friends **is** a doctor. |
| The number of cars are increasing. | The number of cars **is** increasing. |
| A number of students was absent. | A number of students **were** absent. |
| Each of the boys have a book. | Each of the boys **has** a book. |
| The teacher, as well as the students, were present. | … **was** present. |
| Neither he nor I are guilty. | Neither he nor I **am** guilty. |
| Many a student have failed. | Many a student **has** failed. |
| The news are good. | The news **is** good. |
| The police has caught the thief. | The police **have** caught the thief. |

## Quick revision
- 🔥 **One of the + plural noun + singular verb**.
- 🔥 **A number of** → plural; **the number of** → singular.
- 🔥 **as well as / with / along with** → verb follows the **first** subject.
- **either/neither … or/nor** → verb follows the **nearer** subject.
- **each, every, many a, more than one** → singular.
- **The rich, the poor, police, cattle, people** → plural.
- **news, mathematics, ten miles, five thousand taka** → singular.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** "One of the students ___ absent today."
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been

**2.** "A number of people ___ injured in the accident."
(a) was (b) is (c) has been (d) were

**3.** "The Principal, along with the teachers, ___ attended the meeting."
(a) have (b) were (c) has (d) are

**4.** "Neither the manager nor the clerks ___ present."
(a) was (b) is (c) were (d) has been

**5.** "Many a soldier ___ his life in the war."
(a) lost (b) have lost (c) were lost (d) are losing

**6.** "Bread and butter ___ my favourite breakfast."
(a) are (b) have been (c) were (d) is

**7.** "The rich ___ not always happy."
(a) are (b) is (c) was (d) has

**8.** Which sentence is correct?
(a) The number of accidents are rising. (b) Each of the girls have passed. (c) The police has arrested him. (d) Ten miles is a long distance.

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | One of the + plural noun + singular verb |
| 2 | d | A number of = many, plural |
| 3 | c | along with: verb follows the first subject |
| 4 | c | Nearer subject "clerks" is plural |
| 5 | a | many a + singular verb; "lost" is past and fits |
| 6 | d | One dish, one idea |
| 7 | a | the + adjective = people, plural |
| 8 | d | A distance taken as one sum |
