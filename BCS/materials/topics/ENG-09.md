# Tense

> **Why it matters:** Tense questions fill "right form of verb" and error-detection items: "since/for", "no sooner … than", "it is time + past", present tense after "if/when" for the future, and verbs never used in continuous forms.

## The 12 tenses 🔥
| Tense | Simple | Continuous | Perfect | Perfect continuous |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Present** | He **writes** | He **is writing** | He **has written** | He **has been writing** |
| **Past** | He **wrote** | He **was writing** | He **had written** | He **had been writing** |
| **Future** | He **will write** | He **will be writing** | He **will have written** | He **will have been writing** |

## Present tenses
**Present indefinite (simple)** — habits, general and universal truths, timetables:
- "The sun **rises** in the east." "Honesty **is** the best policy." "The train **leaves** at 6 pm."
- Signal words: always, usually, often, every day, daily, generally.
- After **if, when, until, unless, as soon as, before, after** in a future sense 🔥: "I will wait **until** he **comes**" (not *will come*). "**If** it **rains**, we will stay home."
- Headlines and **vivid narration**: "Now the hero **enters**."

**Present continuous** — action now or a fixed future plan: "Look! It **is raining**." "I **am leaving** tomorrow."
- 🔥 **Stative verbs are not used in continuous forms**: **know, understand, believe, like, love, hate, want, wish, see, hear, smell, taste, seem, belong, own, possess, contain, remember, prefer**. "I **know** him" (not *am knowing*). "I **see** a bird" (not *am seeing*).

**Present perfect** — past action with present result, or with just/already/yet/ever/never/recently/lately/so far:
- "I **have** just **finished** the work."
- 🔥 **Never with a definite past time** (yesterday, last year, ago, in 2020): "I **saw** him yesterday" (not *have seen*).

**Present perfect continuous** — action began in the past and is still going on, with **since / for**:
- "He **has been reading** **since** morning." "It **has been raining for** two hours."
- **since** + point of time (since 2010, since Monday); **for** + period (for two years).

## Past tenses
- **Past indefinite:** yesterday, ago, last, in 1971: "Bangladesh **became** independent **in 1971**."
- **Past continuous:** "I **was reading** when he **came**." (long action interrupted by a short one)
- **Past perfect:** of two past actions, the **earlier** one is past perfect 🔥: "The patient **had died before** the doctor **came**." "The train **had left** before we **reached** the station."

## Future tenses
- **shall** with I/we, **will** with others (traditional rule); modern English uses **will** for all. For determination/promise the rule reverses: "You **shall** be punished."
- **Future perfect:** "By next June I **will have finished** the course." (with **by** + future time)
- **be going to** for plans and signs: "It **is going to** rain."

## Special structures 🔥
| Structure | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **It is time / It is high time** | + subject + **past** verb | "It is high time we **left**." |
| **No sooner … than** | **had + subject + V3 … than + past** | "**No sooner had** he **reached** the station **than** the train **left**." |
| **Hardly / Scarcely … when/before** | **had + subject + V3 … when + past** | "**Hardly had** I **gone** out **when** it began to rain." |
| **Since** (conjunction) | main clause present perfect + since + past simple | "It **has been** ten years **since** he **died**." |
| **Wish** (unreal present) | + past (be → **were**) | "I wish I **were** a bird." "I wish I **knew** the answer." |
| **As if / as though** | + past; be → **were** | "He talks **as if** he **were** a king." |
| **Would rather** (present) | + subject + past | "I would rather you **went** now." |
| **Before** after a negative | "**It was not long before** he **came**." | |

- **Sequence of tenses:** a past main verb is followed by a past verb in the clause: "He said that he **was** ill." Exception — universal truth: "He said that the earth **moves** round the sun."
- **lest … should**: "Walk fast **lest** you **should** miss the train."

## Common mistakes
| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| I am knowing him for years. | I **have known** him for years. |
| I have seen him yesterday. | I **saw** him yesterday. |
| He is ill since Monday. | He **has been** ill since Monday. |
| I will wait till he will come. | I will wait till he **comes**. |
| It is time we go home. | It is time we **went** home. |
| No sooner did he come when it rained. | No sooner **had he come than** it rained. |
| He has been living here since two years. | … **for** two years. |
| He said that he is busy. | He said that he **was** busy. |

## Quick revision
- 🔥 **since** + point of time; **for** + period.
- 🔥 **No sooner had … than**; **Hardly/Scarcely had … when**.
- 🔥 **It is (high) time + past**.
- Present perfect is **never** used with yesterday/ago/last.
- Earlier of two past actions → **past perfect**.
- Future after **if/when/until** → **present simple**.
- **know, like, see, belong** are not used in continuous tenses.
- Universal truths stay in the **present** in reported speech.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** "He ___ ill since last Friday."
(a) is (b) was (c) has been (d) had

**2.** "It is high time we ___ the problem."
(a) solved (b) solve (c) will solve (d) have solved

**3.** "No sooner had he left ___ the guests arrived."
(a) when (b) then (c) before (d) than

**4.** "I will call you when I ___ home."
(a) will reach (b) reach (c) reached (d) shall reach

**5.** Which sentence is correct?
(a) I have seen him yesterday. (b) I am knowing the answer. (c) I saw him two days ago. (d) He is living here since 2015.

**6.** "The train ___ before we reached the station."
(a) left (b) has left (c) had left (d) leaves

**7.** "The teacher said that the earth ___ round the sun."
(a) moved (b) moves (c) had moved (d) was moving

**8.** "Hardly had I entered the room ___ the phone rang."
(a) than (b) then (c) and (d) when
