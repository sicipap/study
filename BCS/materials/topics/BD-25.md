# Location, area, borders, enclaves (2015), maritime boundary

> **Why it matters:** Almost every exam asks at least one question on Bangladesh's latitude/longitude, border lengths, border districts, the 2015 enclave exchange or the 2012/2014 maritime verdicts.

## Location (অবস্থান) 🔥
- Latitude: **20°34′ N to 26°38′ N**. Longitude: **88°01′ E to 92°41′ E**.
- The **Tropic of Cancer (কর্কটক্রান্তি রেখা, about 23.5° N)** passes through the **middle** of Bangladesh (west to east through districts such as Jhenaidah, Magura, Faridpur, Cumilla, Khagrachhari and Rangamati).
- Bangladesh Standard Time = **GMT + 6 hours**. The standard meridian is **90° E**.
- Neighbours: **India** on the west, north and east; **Myanmar** on the south-east; the **Bay of Bengal (বঙ্গোপসাগর)** on the south.
- Bangladesh does not touch Nepal or Bhutan. India's narrow **Siliguri corridor ("Chicken's Neck")** separates them.

## Area (আয়তন)
- Official area: **1,47,570 sq km (56,977 sq miles)**. This is the figure used in most textbooks and exams.
- Some recent government publications give **1,48,460 sq km**, adding land gained after the enclave exchange and river-bed changes. If both appear as options, choose **1,47,570 sq km** unless the question says "updated/revised".

| Direction | Place |

## Borders (সীমানা) 🔥
Two sets of figures are in use. Know both:

| Border | Border Guard Bangladesh / most exam guides | BBS Statistical Yearbook |
|---|---|---|
| With India | **4,156 km** | 3,715 km |
| With Myanmar | **271 km** (some write 283 km) | 280 km |
| Coastline (উপকূল) | **711 km** (some write 710) | 716 km |

- **Indian states touching Bangladesh: 5** — West Bengal, Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram.
  - Longest border: **West Bengal**. Shortest: **Mizoram**.
- **Border districts:** **30 districts** touch India, **3 districts** touch Myanmar (**Rangamati, Bandarban, Cox's Bazar**). **Rangamati** touches both India and Myanmar, so there are **32 border districts** in all.
- The **Naf river (নাফ নদী)** forms part of the Bangladesh–Myanmar border.
- **Divisions with no land border with India:** **Barishal** (coastal) and, since Mymensingh division was carved out in 2015, **Dhaka** division.

## Enclaves (ছিটমহল) exchange 2015 🔥🔥
- **Land Boundary Agreement (স্থল সীমান্ত চুক্তি)** signed **16 May 1974** by **Sheikh Mujibur Rahman** and **Indira Gandhi** (the Mujib–Indira Pact).
- Bangladesh ratified it quickly (**3rd Amendment of the Constitution, 1974**). India did not until **2015**.
- 2011: an additional **Protocol** was signed (Manmohan Singh's Dhaka visit).
- May 2015: India's Parliament passed the **Constitution (100th Amendment) Act**.
- **6 June 2015:** instruments of ratification exchanged in Dhaka (Narendra Modi's visit).
- **Midnight of 31 July 2015 (effective 1 August 2015):** enclaves physically exchanged.

| | Number | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Indian enclaves that became **Bangladeshi** | **111** | **17,160.63 acres** |
| Bangladeshi enclaves that became **Indian** | **51** | **7,110.02 acres** |

- Bangladesh gained about **10,050 acres** net.
- The 111 enclaves were in **Lalmonirhat (59), Panchagarh (36), Kurigram (12), Nilphamari (4)**.
- **দহগ্রাম–আঙ্গরপোতা** (Patgram, Lalmonirhat) remained with Bangladesh. It is linked to the mainland by the **তিনবিঘা করিডোর**, leased from India in **1992** and open **24 hours since 2011**.

## Maritime boundary (সমুদ্রসীমা) 🔥🔥
| Dispute | Tribunal | Verdict date | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bangladesh vs Myanmar** | **ITLOS** (International Tribunal for the Law of the Sea), **Hamburg, Germany** | **14 March 2012** | Bangladesh got about **1,11,631 sq km** of sea area |
| **Bangladesh vs India** | **Permanent Court of Arbitration (PCA), The Hague** | **7 July 2014** | Of **25,602 sq km** disputed area, Bangladesh got **19,467 sq km** |

- After both verdicts Bangladesh has about **1,18,813 sq km** of territorial sea, EEZ and continental shelf.
- **South Talpatti (দক্ষিণ তালপট্টি / New Moore)** island, near the Hariabhanga river mouth, fell on India's side; it has since disappeared under the sea.
- Under **UNCLOS 1982**: territorial sea **12 nautical miles**, contiguous zone **24 NM**, **EEZ 200 NM**, continental shelf up to **350 NM** (where the shelf extends).
- Bangladesh's own law: **Territorial Waters and Maritime Zones Act, 1974** (amended 2021).

## Quick revision
- Bangladesh lies between **20°34′–26°38′ N** and **88°01′–92°41′ E**.
- The **Tropic of Cancer** crosses the middle of the country; time zone **GMT+6**.
- Area **1,47,570 sq km** (revised figure 1,48,460 sq km in some sources).
- 5 Indian states border Bangladesh; longest with **West Bengal**, shortest with **Mizoram**.
- 3 districts border Myanmar: **Rangamati, Bandarban, Cox's Bazar**.
- LBA 1974 → implemented **31 July 2015 midnight**: **111** enclaves in, **51** out.
- Myanmar case: **ITLOS, 14 March 2012**; India case: **PCA, 7 July 2014 (19,467 sq km)**.
- Total sea area: **1,18,813 sq km**.
