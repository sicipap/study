# Legislature: Jatiya Sangsad, Speaker, committees

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the name and size of Parliament, quorum, gap between sessions, the architect of the Sangsad Bhaban, the first and first woman Speaker, who administers which oath, and the standing committees (especially the Public Accounts Committee).

## Jatiya Sangsad (জাতীয় সংসদ) 🔥
- Article **65**: Parliament is called the **"জাতীয় সংসদ" (House of the Nation)**. It is **unicameral** (এক কক্ষ বিশিষ্ট).
- 🔥 **350 members**: **300** directly elected from single-member territorial constituencies + **50 reserved women's seats**.
- Reserved women members are **elected by the 300 MPs** in proportion to party strength (they are not directly elected by the people).
- Constituency **no. 1 is Panchagarh-1**; **no. 300 is Bandarban** (Parbatya Bandarban).
- Qualification: citizen, **25 years** old (Art. 66).
- Term: **5 years** from the first meeting (Art. 72).
- 🔥 Gap between two sessions: **not more than 60 days** (Art. 72).
- 🔥 **Quorum: 60 members** (Art. 75).
- Voter age: **18 years**.
- **Floor crossing** (Art. 70): an MP loses the seat if he resigns from his party or votes against it.

## Sangsad Bhaban (সংসদ ভবন) 🔥
- Designed by American architect 🔥 **লুই আই. কান (Louis I. Kahn)**.
- At **শেরে বাংলা নগর**, Dhaka. Work began in the early 1960s (Pakistan period); inaugurated on **28 January 1982**.
- Before that, Parliament sat in the old **Tejgaon** building (now the Prime Minister's Office).

## Speaker (স্পিকার) 🔥
- Article **74**: the Speaker and Deputy Speaker are elected by the MPs from among themselves.
- Presides over Parliament; decides whether a bill is a **money bill**; keeps order.
- 🔥 Acts as **President** when the office is vacant or the President is absent (Art. 54).
- The **President's oath** is administered by the **Speaker**.

| Fact | Answer |
|---|---|
| First Speaker (Constituent Assembly, 1972) | **শাহ আবদুল হামিদ** |
| First Speaker of the first Jatiya Sangsad (1973) | **মোহাম্মদ মোহাম্মদুল্লাহ** |
| 🔥 **First woman Speaker** (and youngest) | **ড. শিরীন শারমিন চৌধুরী** (2013) |

## Who administers the oath? (Third Schedule) 🔥
| Office | Oath given by |
|---|---|
| **President** | **Speaker** |
| Prime Minister, ministers, Speaker, Deputy Speaker | **President** |
| **Chief Justice** | **President** |
| Other Supreme Court judges | **Chief Justice** |
| Chief Election Commissioner & Election Commissioners, CAG, PSC members | **Chief Justice** |
| **Members of Parliament** | **Speaker** |

## Law-making 🔥
- **Ordinary bill:** introduced by a minister (government bill) or an ordinary MP (**private member's bill**); three readings; then sent to the President, who must assent within **15 days** (Art. 80).
- **Money bill** (Art. 81): can be introduced only with the **President's recommendation**; the Speaker certifies it.
- **Budget (annual financial statement, Art. 87):** presented by the **Finance Minister** usually in **June**; fiscal year **1 July – 30 June**.
- **Constitution amendment bill** (Art. 142): needs **two-thirds** of total MPs.
- Tools of control: **question hour**, adjournment motion, point of order, **walkout**, **no-confidence**, and committees.

## Parliamentary committees (সংসদীয় কমিটি) 🔥
- Article **76** provides for **standing committees**.
- 🔥 **Public Accounts Committee (PAC, সরকারি হিসাব সম্পর্কিত কমিটি)** — examines government accounts and the **CAG's audit reports**; generally regarded as the most important committee.
- **Estimates Committee** — examines budget estimates.
- **Committee on Public Undertakings** — examines state enterprises.
- **Committee on Privileges**, **Committee on Government Assurances**, **Petition Committee**, **Library Committee**, **House Committee**.
- **Business Advisory Committee** and **Rules of Procedure Committee** — chaired by the **Speaker**.
- **Standing committees on ministries** — one for each ministry. Since the 7th Parliament (1996–2001) they have been chaired by **MPs, not ministers**.

- The **6th Parliament** was the **shortest** — it sat for only about **12 days** in March 1996 (some books say 11) and passed the **13th amendment**.
- The **12th Parliament** was **dissolved on 6 August 2024**.

> 🔄 Update: Parliament was dissolved in August 2024, and reform proposals discussed a **bicameral** Parliament and other changes. Check the latest election result, the current Speaker, and whether the structure of Parliament has changed.

## Quick revision
- Name: **Jatiya Sangsad** (Art. 65); **unicameral**; **350** seats (300 + 50).
- Quorum **60**; gap between sessions ≤ **60 days**; term **5 years**.
- Architect: **Louis I. Kahn**; inaugurated **28 January 1982**.
- Constituency 1 **Panchagarh-1**; 300 **Bandarban**.
- Speaker: **Art. 74**; acts as President (**Art. 54**).
- First woman Speaker: **Dr. Shirin Sharmin Chaudhury** (2013).
- Speaker administers oath to the **President** and **MPs**.
- **PAC** examines the CAG's audit reports.
- Money bill: only on the **President's recommendation** (Art. 81).
