# Heights & distances

> **Why it matters:** Heights and distances (উচ্চতা ও দূরত্ব) apply trigonometry to towers, trees, ladders, kites and shadows. Bank and BCS papers ask one-step versions using 30°, 45° and 60°.

## Key concepts
- **Line of sight:** the line from the eye to the object.
- **Angle of elevation (উন্নতি কোণ):** the angle **above** the horizontal when you look **up** at an object.
- **Angle of depression (অবনতি কোণ):** the angle **below** the horizontal when you look **down** at an object.
- 🔥 The angle of elevation of B from A **equals** the angle of depression of A from B (alternate angles).
- Almost every problem forms a right triangle: height is the **opposite** side, horizontal distance is the **adjacent** side.
  - **tan θ = height ÷ horizontal distance**
  - Use **sin θ** when the slanted length (ladder, string) is the hypotenuse: height = length × sin θ.
- Take √3 ≈ 1.732 and √2 ≈ 1.414 if a decimal answer is needed.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| θ | tan θ | Height h from distance d | Distance from height |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30° | 1/√3 | h = d/√3 | d = h√3 |
| 45° | 1 | h = d | d = h |
| 60° | √3 | h = d√3 | d = h/√3 |

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Ladder/string of length L at angle θ with ground | height = L sin θ; foot distance = L cos θ |
| 🔥 Shadow equal to the height | sun's elevation = 45° |
| Elevation changes from α to β after walking d towards the tower | h = d ÷ (cot α − cot β) |
| 30° → 60° after walking d | h = (√3/2) d |
| Tree breaks; top touches ground at angle θ, x from the foot | total height = x tan θ + x/cos θ |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** From a point 30 m from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of its top is 60°. Find the height.
Steps: h = 30 × tan 60° = 30√3 ≈ 51.96.
**Answer: 30√3 m**

**Example 2.** A 20 m ladder makes 60° with the ground. How high up the wall does it reach?
Steps: 20 × sin 60° = 20 × √3/2 = 10√3.
**Answer: 10√3 m**

**Example 3.** From the top of a 50 m tower, the angle of depression of a car is 30°. How far is the car from the tower?
Steps: Angle of elevation from the car = 30°. d = 50 × √3 = 50√3.
**Answer: 50√3 m**

**Example 4.** Walking 20 m towards a tower, the angle of elevation of its top changes from 30° to 60°. Find the height.
Steps: h = 20/(cot 30° − cot 60°) = 20/(√3 − 1/√3) = 20/(2/√3) = 10√3.
**Answer: 10√3 m**

**Example 5.** A pole's shadow is equal to its height. Find the sun's angle of elevation.
Steps: tan θ = h/h = 1 → θ = 45°.
**Answer: 45°**

## Common traps
- Using sin when the question gives the **horizontal distance** (use tan).
- Treating the angle of depression as measured from the vertical — it is from the **horizontal**.
- 30° gives the **shorter** height (d/√3); 60° gives the taller one (d√3). Check your answer is sensible.
- In ladder problems, the ladder is the hypotenuse.

## Quick revision
- tan θ = height/distance.
- Elevation = depression (alternate angles).
- 45° ⇒ height = distance.
- Ladder: height = L sin θ.
- 30° → 60° over d ⇒ h = (√3/2)d.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** From a point 50 m from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of its top is 45°. The height of the tower is:
(a) 50√3 m (b) 50 m (c) 25 m (d) 100 m

**2.** A pole of height 10√3 m casts a shadow 10 m long. The sun's angle of elevation is:
(a) 30° (b) 45° (c) 90° (d) 60°

**3.** A 10 m ladder makes 60° with the ground. The height it reaches on the wall is:
(a) 5√3 m (b) 5 m (c) 10√3 m (d) 10/√3 m

**4.** A kite is flying on a 100 m string that makes 30° with the ground. The height of the kite is:
(a) 100√3 m (b) 50√3 m (c) 50 m (d) 100 m

**5.** From the top of a 100 m cliff, the angle of depression of a boat is 30°. The distance of the boat from the foot of the cliff is:
(a) 100 m (b) 100/√3 m (c) 50 m (d) 100√3 m

**6.** When the length of a tower's shadow equals its height, the sun's elevation is:
(a) 45° (b) 30° (c) 60° (d) 90°

**7.** A 6 m pole casts a shadow of 2√3 m. The angle of elevation of the sun is:
(a) 30° (b) 60° (c) 45° (d) 75°

**8.** A tree breaks and its top touches the ground 10 m from the foot, making 30° with the ground. The original height of the tree was:
(a) 10 m (b) 20 m (c) 10√3 m (d) 20√3 m

**9.** Walking 40 m towards a tower, the angle of elevation of its top changes from 30° to 60°. The height of the tower is:
(a) 20√3 m (b) 40√3 m (c) 20 m (d) 40 m

**10.** The angle of elevation of the top of a 30 m tower from a point on the ground is 30°. The distance of the point from the tower is:
(a) 30 m (b) 10√3 m (c) 60 m (d) 30√3 m

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | tan 45° = 1 → h = 50 |
| 2 | d | tan θ = 10√3/10 = √3 → 60° |
| 3 | a | 10 × sin 60° = 5√3 |
| 4 | c | 100 × sin 30° = 50 |
| 5 | d | d = 100/tan 30° = 100√3 |
| 6 | a | tan θ = 1 → 45° |
| 7 | b | tan θ = 6/(2√3) = √3 → 60° |
| 8 | c | Standing part 10/√3 + broken part 20/√3 = 30/√3 = 10√3 |
| 9 | a | h = (√3/2) × 40 = 20√3 |
| 10 | d | d = 30/tan 30° = 30√3 |
