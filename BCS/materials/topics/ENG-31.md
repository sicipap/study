# Correction of sentences / error detection

> **Why it matters:** Every BCS and bank paper has "Which sentence is correct?" or "Find the error" questions. The same 50 or so traps repeat: subject–verb agreement, uncountable nouns, wrong prepositions, tense with *since/for*, and redundancy.

## 1. Subject–verb agreement 🔥
| Incorrect | Correct | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| One of my friend is a doctor. | One of my **friends is** a doctor. | *one of* + plural noun + singular verb |
| Each of the boys have a pen. | Each of the boys **has** a pen. | *each, every, either, neither* → singular |
| The Principal, along with the teachers, were present. | … **was** present. | *along with, as well as, with, together with* do not change the subject |
| Neither he nor I are guilty. | Neither he nor I **am** guilty. | *either/neither … or/nor*: verb agrees with the **nearer** subject |
| A number of students is absent. | A number of students **are** absent. | *a number of* = many → plural |
| The number of students are fifty. | The number of students **is** fifty. | *the number of* → singular |
| The cattle is grazing. | The cattle **are** grazing. | *cattle, police, people, poultry, gentry* → always plural |
| The police has arrested him. | The police **have** arrested him. | same rule |
| Mathematics are my favourite subject. | Mathematics **is** … | *mathematics, physics, economics, news* → singular |
| The news are true. | The news **is** true. | |
| Twenty miles are a long distance. | Twenty miles **is** a long distance. | amount/distance as one unit → singular |
| The rich is not always happy. | The rich **are** … | *the + adjective* = plural class |
| He is one of the best boys who **has** passed. | … who **have** passed. | *one of those who* → plural verb (exam answer) |

## 2. Nouns: uncountable words 🔥
Words that **never take -s or "a/an"**: *advice, information, furniture, luggage, baggage, scenery, machinery, poetry, equipment, bread, mischief*, and *hair* (meaning all the hair on the head).
| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| He gave me an advice. | He gave me **some advice / a piece of advice**. |
| I need some informations. | I need some **information**. |
| The sceneries of Cox's Bazar are charming. | The **scenery** of Cox's Bazar **is** charming. |
| My luggages are lost. | My **luggage is** lost. |
| He bought new furnitures. | He bought new **furniture**. |
| His hairs are grey. | His **hair is** grey. |

## 3. Pronouns
| Incorrect | Correct | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Between you and I | Between you and **me** | object after preposition |
| Let he go. | Let **him** go. | object after *let* |
| He is taller than me. (formal test) | He is taller than **I** (am). | exam prefers subjective case after *than* |
| I and you will go. | **You and I** will go. | order: 2nd, 3rd, 1st person (for good things) |
| One should do his duty. | One should do **one's** duty. | *one → one's* |
| Everyone of them | **Every one** of them | *every one* (two words) before *of* |

## 4. Tense errors 🔥
| Incorrect | Correct | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| He has been ill since three days. | He has been ill **for** three days. | *for* + period; *since* + point of time |
| I am living here since 2010. | I **have been living** here since 2010. | *since* → present perfect (continuous) |
| I have seen him yesterday. | I **saw** him yesterday. | past time word → past simple |
| He did not came. | He did not **come**. | *did* + V1 |
| Have you ever went there? | Have you ever **gone** there? | *have* + V3 |
| No sooner had he come **when** I left. | No sooner had he come **than** I left. | *no sooner … than* |
| Hardly had he gone **than** it rained. | Hardly had he gone **when** it rained. | *hardly/scarcely … when/before* |
| If I was you… | If I **were** you… | unreal condition |
| Walk fast lest you **should not** miss the bus. | … lest you **should** miss the bus. | *lest* already means "so that not" |

## 5. Adjectives & comparison 🔥
| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| He is senior **than** me. | He is senior **to** me. (*senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior, prefer* → *to*) |
| I prefer tea **than** coffee. | I prefer tea **to** coffee. |
| This is **more better**. | This is **better**. (no double comparative) |
| She is the **most perfect** lady. | She is a **perfect** lady. (*perfect, unique, ideal, round* have no comparison) |
| His house is bigger than **me**. | His house is bigger than **mine**. |
| He is one of the tallest **boy**. | … one of the tallest **boys**. |
| He is **a** honest man. | He is **an** honest man. (sound, not letter) |
| He studies in **an** university. | … in **a** university. ("yu" sound) |

## 6. Preposition errors 🔥
| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| He died **from** cholera. | He died **of** cholera. (disease → *of*) |
| She is married **with** a doctor. | She is married **to** a doctor. |
| He is good **in** English. | He is good **at** English. |
| He is blind **from** one eye. | He is blind **of** one eye. |
| I am angry **on** him. | I am angry **with** him (person) / **at** his conduct (thing). |
| He congratulated me **for** my success. | … **on** my success. |
| He is fond **with** music. | He is fond **of** music. |
| Listen me. | Listen **to** me. |

## 7. Redundancy (extra words) 🔥
| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| Let us **discuss about** it. | Let us **discuss** it. |
| He **returned back**. | He **returned**. |
| **Cope up with** | **Cope with** |
| **Order for** a book | **Order** a book |
| **Repeat again** | **Repeat** |
| **Though** he is poor, **but** he is honest. | Though he is poor, he is honest. (or *yet*) |
| **Both** Rahim **as well as** Karim | **Both** Rahim **and** Karim |
| **Unless** you **do not** work hard… | **Unless** you work hard… |
| The **reason** is **because**… | The reason is **that**… |

## 8. Verb patterns (gerund / infinitive)
| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| He made me **to cry**. | He made me **cry**. (*make, let, see, hear* + bare infinitive) |
| Would you mind **to open** the door? | Would you mind **opening** the door? |
| I look forward to **meet** you. | I look forward to **meeting** you. |
| He avoided **to meet** me. | He avoided **meeting** me. |
| He enjoyed during the holidays. | He **enjoyed himself** during the holidays. |

## Quick revision
- **One of + plural noun + singular verb.**
- **Cattle, police, people** → plural; **news, mathematics** → singular.
- **Advice, information, furniture, scenery, luggage** → no plural.
- **Since** + point, **for** + period; *since* → present perfect.
- **Senior/junior/superior/prefer + to**, not *than*.
- **Died of** a disease; **married to**; **good at**.
- **Discuss** (no *about*); **return** (no *back*); **cope with** (no *up*).
- **No sooner … than**; **Hardly/Scarcely … when**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Which sentence is correct?
(a) One of my friend is a lawyer. (b) One of my friends are a lawyer. (c) One of my friends is a lawyer. (d) One of my friend are a lawyer.

**2.** Which sentence is correct?
(a) He gave me an advice. (b) He gave me a piece of advice. (c) He gave me advices. (d) He gave me some advices.

**3.** Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He has been ill since three days. (b) He is ill since three days. (c) He was ill since three days. (d) He has been ill for three days.

**4.** Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is senior to me. (b) He is senior than me. (c) He is more senior than me. (d) He is senior from me.

**5.** Choose the correct sentence.
(a) Let us discuss about the matter. (b) Let us discuss the matter. (c) Let us discuss on the matter. (d) Let us discuss over the matter.

**6.** Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The cattle is grazing in the field. (b) The cattles are grazing in the field. (c) The cattles is grazing in the field. (d) The cattle are grazing in the field.

**7.** Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He died from cholera. (b) He died by cholera. (c) He died of cholera. (d) He died with cholera.

**8.** Choose the correct sentence.
(a) No sooner had he arrived when the train left. (b) No sooner had he arrived than the train left. (c) No sooner he arrived than the train left. (d) No sooner did he arrived than the train left.

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | One of + plural noun + singular verb |
| 2 | b | Advice is uncountable; use "a piece of advice" |
| 3 | d | "for" with a period of time + present perfect |
| 4 | a | senior takes "to", not "than" |
| 5 | b | discuss is transitive; no "about" |
| 6 | d | cattle is always plural and has no -s |
| 7 | c | die of a disease |
| 8 | b | No sooner ... than, with inversion "had he" |
