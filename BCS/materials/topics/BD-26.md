# Rivers & bridges (Padma Bridge etc.)

> **Why it matters:** Questions on river origins, confluences, trans-boundary rivers and bridge facts (length, location, opening date) appear in nearly every BCS and bank exam.

## Rivers: basic facts 🔥
- Bangladesh is called **"নদীমাতৃক দেশ"** (land of rivers).
- Number of rivers: traditionally "**about 700**". In **2023** the **National River Conservation Commission (জাতীয় নদী রক্ষা কমিশন)** published a list of **1,008 rivers**. Older BIWTA figure: 230. Use the figure that matches the question's source.
- **Trans-boundary (আন্তঃসীমান্ত) rivers: 57** — **54 with India**, **3 with Myanmar** (**নাফ, সাঙ্গু, মাতামুহুরী** in most guides).
- The **Naf** forms part of the Bangladesh–Myanmar border.

## Major river systems
| River | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **পদ্মা (Padma)** | The Ganges (গঙ্গা) enters at **Chapainawabganj/Rajshahi**; joins the **Jamuna at Goalundo (Rajbari)** and then the **Meghna at Chandpur** |
| **যমুনা (Jamuna)** | Main flow of the **Brahmaputra** (from Tibet, via Assam), enters at **Kurigram**; shifted to the present Jamuna course after the **1787** earthquake/flood |
| **পুরাতন ব্রহ্মপুত্র** | Old course, flows past Mymensingh |
| **মেঘনা (Meghna)** | Formed by the **সুরমা** and **কুশিয়ারা** (both from the **Barak** river of Assam/Manipur); meets the Padma at **Chandpur** and falls into the Bay of Bengal. Often called the **widest** and, in most exam guides, the longest river inside Bangladesh |
| **কর্ণফুলী (Karnaphuli)** | Rises in the **Lushai hills (Mizoram)**; **Chattogram port** is on it; **Kaptai dam** built on it |
| **তিস্তা (Teesta)** | From **Sikkim**; **Teesta Barrage** at **Dalia (Lalmonirhat/Nilphamari)**; water sharing unresolved |
| **হালদা (Halda)** | Chattogram; the only **natural carp breeding ground (রুই জাতীয় মাছের প্রাকৃতিক প্রজনন ক্ষেত্র)** in South Asia; declared **"Bangabandhu Fisheries Heritage"** in 2020 |
| **সাঙ্গু ও হালদা** | Both rise and end inside Bangladesh (exam favourite) |
| **বুড়িগঙ্গা** | Dhaka city stands on its bank |
| **শীতলক্ষ্যা** | Narayanganj |
| **করতোয়া** | Mahasthangarh (Bogura) on its bank |
| **গড়াই–মধুমতী** | Main distributary of the Padma towards the south-west |

- **Longest river (by exam convention):** Meghna. Some sources name the Padma or the Surma, so read the options carefully.
- **Kaptai Lake (কাপ্তাই হ্রদ)** is the **largest artificial lake**, formed by the Kaptai dam (1960s). It feeds the **Karnaphuli Hydro Power Station**, the country's only hydro plant.

## Water treaties 🔥
- **Farakka Barrage (ফারাক্কা বাঁধ)**: on the Ganges in **Murshidabad, West Bengal**; commissioned **21 April 1975**. **Farakka Long March** led by **Maulana Bhashani** on **16 May 1976**.
- **Ganges Water Sharing Treaty (গঙ্গা পানিবণ্টন চুক্তি)**: signed **12 December 1996**, valid for **30 years**, so it **expires in December 2026**. Renewal is a hot current-affairs issue.
- **Joint Rivers Commission (যৌথ নদী কমিশন)** Bangladesh–India: formed **1972**.

## Bridges 🔥🔥
| Bridge | River / place | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **পদ্মা সেতু (Padma Multipurpose Bridge)** | Padma; **Mawa (Munshiganj)** – **Janjira (Shariatpur)** | **6.15 km**, longest bridge in Bangladesh; **41 spans, 42 pillars**; two-storey (road on top, rail below); built with **own funds**; main contractor **China Major Bridge Engineering Company**; inaugurated **25 June 2022**, open to traffic **26 June 2022**; rail link on the bridge opened **10 October 2023** |
| **যমুনা সেতু (Jamuna Bridge)** — named **Bangabandhu Bridge** in 1998 | Jamuna; **Bhuapur (Tangail) – Sirajganj** | **4.8 km**; opened **23 June 1998**; carries road, rail, gas and power lines. Renamed **Jamuna Bridge** in 2025 |
| **যমুনা রেলসেতু** | Parallel to the Jamuna Bridge | **4.8 km**, country's **longest dedicated rail bridge**; opened to trains in **2025** |
| **হার্ডিঞ্জ ব্রিজ** | Padma; **Paksey (Pabna)** | **Railway bridge, opened 1915** (British era), about **1.8 km** |
| **লালন শাহ সেতু** | Padma; Paksey (Pabna) – Bheramara (Kushtia) | Road bridge, **2004**, next to Hardinge Bridge |
| **সৈয়দ নজরুল ইসলাম সেতু** | Meghna; **Bhairab – Ashuganj** | Road bridge, 2002 |
| **খান জাহান আলী সেতু (Rupsha Bridge)** | Rupsha, **Khulna** | 2005 |
| **শাহ আমানত সেতু** | Karnaphuli, **Chattogram** | 2010 |
| **মৈত্রী সেতু** | **Feni river**; **Ramgarh (Khagrachhari) – Sabroom (Tripura, India)** | Inaugurated **9 March 2021** |
| **পায়রা সেতু (Lebukhali)** | Payra river, **Patuakhali** | 2021 |

- **Memory hook:** Padma **6.15** → Jamuna **4.8** → Hardinge/Lalon Shah about **1.8** km.

> 🔄 Update: bridge and project names have been changed several times since 2024; check the current official name in a newspaper before the exam.

## Quick revision
- 57 trans-boundary rivers: **54 India + 3 Myanmar**.
- Padma + Jamuna meet at **Goalundo**; Padma + Meghna at **Chandpur**.
- Surma + Kushiyara → **Meghna** (both from the **Barak**).
- **Halda** = natural carp breeding ground (Chattogram).
- Farakka commissioned **1975**; Ganges Treaty **12 Dec 1996**, 30 years.
- Padma Bridge **6.15 km**, **Mawa–Janjira**, opened **25 June 2022**.
- Jamuna Bridge **4.8 km**, opened **23 June 1998**.
- Hardinge Bridge (rail) **1915**; Maitree Setu on the **Feni** river, **2021**.

(a) Chandpur (b) Goalundo (c) Bhairab (d) Aricha

**3.** Which two rivers join to form the Meghna?
(a) Surma and Kushiyara (b) Padma and Jamuna (c) Teesta and Dharla (d) Gorai and Madhumati

**4.** The Ganges Water Sharing Treaty was signed in —
(a) 1975 (b) 1972 (c) 2011 (d) 1996

**5.** Which river is known as a natural breeding ground of carp fish?
(a) Karnaphuli (b) Sangu (c) Halda (d) Matamuhuri

**6.** Maitree Setu between Bangladesh and India is built over which river?
(a) Feni (b) Teesta (c) Muhuri (d) Naf

**7.** How many trans-boundary rivers does Bangladesh share with India?
(a) 57 (b) 54 (c) 3 (d) 51

**8.** The Hardinge Bridge is located at —
(a) Bhairab (b) Mawa (c) Sirajganj (d) Paksey

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | Padma Bridge is 6.15 km long. |
| 2 | b | They meet at Goalundo, Rajbari. |
| 3 | a | Surma and Kushiyara, both branches of the Barak, form the Meghna. |
| 4 | d | Signed 12 December 1996 for 30 years. |
| 5 | c | The Halda in Chattogram. |
| 6 | a | Feni river, Ramgarh–Sabroom. |
| 7 | b | 54 with India; 57 in total including 3 with Myanmar. |
| 8 | d | Hardinge Bridge (1915) is at Paksey, Pabna. |
