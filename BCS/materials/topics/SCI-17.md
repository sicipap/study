# Human body: digestion, blood, circulation

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask enzymes and where they act, the largest gland, blood cell counts and life span, blood groups (universal donor/recipient), normal blood pressure and heart rate, and who discovered blood circulation.

## Digestive system (পরিপাকতন্ত্র) 🔥
| Part | Juice / enzyme | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Mouth** | Saliva: **ptyalin (salivary amylase)** | **Starch → maltose**; digestion of starch begins here |
| **Stomach** | Gastric juice: **HCl**, **pepsin**, **rennin** (infants) | HCl kills germs, makes medium acidic (pH ≈ 2); **pepsin: protein → peptides**; rennin curdles milk |
| **Liver** | **Bile** (stored in the **gall bladder**) | **Emulsifies fat**; bile has **no enzyme** |
| **Pancreas** | Pancreatic juice: **amylase, trypsin, lipase** | Starch, protein, fat digestion |
| **Small intestine** | Intestinal juice (maltase, sucrase, lactase, lipase, peptidases) | **Digestion completes**; **most absorption** through **villi** |
| **Large intestine** | — | **Absorbs water**, forms faeces |

- **Liver is the largest gland** (and largest internal organ, ≈ 1.5 kg). It makes bile, stores **glycogen**, iron and vitamins A, D, B₁₂; detoxifies; makes urea. Jaundice is a liver disorder (bilirubin rises).
- **Pancreas is a mixed gland**: exocrine (digestive enzymes) and endocrine (**insulin, glucagon**).
- **Small intestine** is the **longest part** of the alimentary canal (≈ 6–7 m). Whole canal ≈ 9 m.
- Final products: carbohydrate → **glucose**; protein → **amino acids**; fat → **fatty acids + glycerol**.
- **Teeth**: adults **32** (permanent), children **20** (milk teeth). **Enamel** is the **hardest substance in the body**. Largest salivary gland: **parotid** (swells in **mumps**).
- **Appendix** is a vestigial organ attached to the large intestine.

## Blood (রক্ত) 🔥🔥
- Blood is a **fluid connective tissue**. An adult has about **5–6 litres** (≈ 7–8% of body weight). **pH ≈ 7.4** (slightly alkaline).
- **Plasma ≈ 55%** (about 90% water, plus proteins such as albumin, fibrinogen, globulin); **blood cells ≈ 45%**.

| Cell | Normal count (per mm³) | Life span | Main job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RBC (erythrocyte, লোহিত কণিকা)** | **≈ 4.5–5.5 million** (men ≈ 5 m, women ≈ 4.5 m) | **≈ 120 days** | Carries **O₂** by **haemoglobin (iron-containing)**; **no nucleus** in mammals |
| **WBC (leucocyte, শ্বেত কণিকা)** | **≈ 4,000–11,000** | Hours to days (lymphocytes longer) | **Defence**; lymphocytes make **antibodies**; neutrophils most numerous, eat germs |
| **Platelet (thrombocyte, অণুচক্রিকা)** | **≈ 1.5–4 lakh** | ≈ 7–10 days | **Blood clotting**; count falls in **dengue** |

- RBCs are made in the **red bone marrow** (in the fetus, also liver/spleen). Old RBCs are destroyed in the **spleen** ("graveyard of RBC").
- **Haemoglobin**: men ≈ 13–17 g/dL, women ≈ 12–15 g/dL (values vary slightly between books). Low haemoglobin/RBC = **anaemia (রক্তস্বল্পতা)**. Uncontrolled WBC increase = **leukaemia (blood cancer)**.
- **Clotting** needs **vitamin K**, **calcium**, fibrinogen → fibrin; usually 3–8 minutes. **Haemophilia** ("bleeder's disease") is a hereditary, **X-linked** disorder in which blood does not clot.
- **Heparin** (from liver) prevents clotting inside vessels.

## Blood groups 🔥🔥
- **ABO blood groups** discovered by **Karl Landsteiner (1900–01)** (Nobel 1930). **Rh factor**: Landsteiner & Wiener (**1940**), named after the **rhesus monkey**.

| Group | Antigen on RBC | Antibody in plasma | Can receive from |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | A | anti-B | A, O |
| B | B | anti-A | B, O |
| **AB** | A and B | **none** | **All → universal recipient** |
| **O** | **none** | anti-A and anti-B | O only → **universal donor** |

- **O negative (O−)** is the true universal donor; **AB positive** the universal recipient.
- **Rh incompatibility**: an **Rh− mother** carrying an **Rh+ baby** can cause **erythroblastosis fetalis** in later pregnancies.
- A healthy adult can donate blood about **every 4 months**.

## Heart and circulation (হৃৎপিণ্ড ও রক্ত সংবহন) 🔥
- **Blood circulation** was discovered by **William Harvey (1628)**. **Capillaries** were discovered by **Marcello Malpighi**.
- Human heart has **4 chambers**: 2 **atria (অলিন্দ)** and 2 **ventricles (নিলয়)**. Right side carries **deoxygenated**, left side **oxygenated** blood. The **left ventricle** has the thickest wall (pumps to the whole body).
- The heart's natural **pacemaker** is the **SA (sinoatrial) node**. Heart weighs about **300 g**; covered by the **pericardium**.
- **Normal heart rate ≈ 72 beats/min** (range 60–100); one cardiac cycle ≈ **0.8 s**. Heart sounds: "lub-dub" (valve closure).
- **Normal blood pressure ≈ 120/80 mmHg** (systolic/diastolic); **140/90 or more = hypertension**. Measured with a **sphygmomanometer**.

| Vessel | Carries | Exception |
|---|---|---|
| **Artery (ধমনী)** | Blood **away from** the heart; oxygenated | **Pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated** blood (to lungs) |
| **Vein (শিরা)** | Blood **to** the heart; deoxygenated; has valves | **Pulmonary vein carries oxygenated** blood (from lungs) |
| **Capillary** | Exchange between blood and tissues | — |

- **Aorta** is the **largest artery**; **vena cava** the **largest vein**.
- **Double circulation**: pulmonary (heart → lungs → heart) and systemic (heart → body → heart).
- **First human heart transplant: Christiaan Barnard, 1967, South Africa**.
- **Lymph** is a colourless fluid; lymph nodes filter germs.

## Quick revision
- Starch digestion starts in the **mouth (ptyalin)**; protein in the **stomach (pepsin)**.
- **Bile** from the liver emulsifies fat, has no enzyme; **liver = largest gland**.
- Digestion completes and absorption happens in the **small intestine**.
- **RBC life 120 days**; made in bone marrow; destroyed in spleen.
- Platelets help clotting; vitamin K needed; haemophilia = no clotting.
- **O = universal donor, AB = universal recipient**; Landsteiner discovered ABO.
- **BP 120/80 mmHg; heart rate 72/min**; pacemaker = SA node.
- **Harvey (1628)** discovered circulation; pulmonary vein carries oxygenated blood.
