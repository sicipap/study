# Population & Census 2022, literacy

> **Why it matters:** Census 2022 figures (total population, growth rate, density, literacy, religion) and census history are asked in almost every exam from 2023 onward.

## Census basics 🔥
- Census is conducted by the **Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS)**, under the **Statistics and Informatics Division**, Ministry of Planning.
- Bangladesh holds a census **every 10 years**.

| Census | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|

## Census 2022 key figures 🔥🔥
The **preliminary report** (July 2022) and the **final/adjusted report** (after the Post Enumeration Check, 2023) give different totals. Exams use both, so read the question.

| Indicator | Preliminary report (2022) | Final adjusted (BBS) |
|---|---|---|
| **Total population** | **16,51,58,616** (165.16 million) | **16,98,28,911 (169.83 million)** |
| Annual growth rate (2011–2022) | **1.22%** | — |
| Density | **1,119 per sq km** | higher (about 1,150) |
| Sex ratio | **98.07** men per 100 women | — |
| Literacy (7 years and above) | **74.66%** (male 76.56%, female 72.82%) | — |
| Third gender (হিজড়া) | **12,629** | — |

- **Women outnumber men** in Bangladesh for the first time in census history (Census 2022).
- **Religion (Census 2022):** Muslim **91.04%**, Hindu **7.95%**, Buddhist **0.61%**, Christian **0.30%**, others **0.12%**.
- **Ethnic population:** **16,50,159** people of **50 groups**; the largest group is the **Chakma** (see BD-38).
- **Most populous division / district:** **Dhaka** division / **Dhaka** district.
- **Least populous division:** **Barishal**. **Least populous district:** **Bandarban**.
- Bangladesh is about the **8th most populous country** in the world (UN estimates).

> 🔄 Update: check the latest figure in a newspaper or the Bangladesh Economic Review. BBS's annual **Sample Vital Statistics (SVRS)** gives yearly estimates of population, literacy, life expectancy and fertility that differ from the census.

## Literacy (সাক্ষরতা) 🔥
- In Bangladesh's census, a person aged **7 years and above** who can **read and write a simple letter** is literate.
- Literacy rate rose from about **51.8% (2011 census)** to **74.66% (2022 preliminary)**.
- Adult literacy (15+) is reported separately in SVRS.
- **International Literacy Day:** **8 September** (UNESCO).
- **Bureau of Non-Formal Education (উপানুষ্ঠানিক শিক্ষা ব্যুরো)** runs adult literacy programmes.

## Population policy and days
- **National Population Policy:** latest **2012**.
- **World Population Day:** **11 July**.
- **BBS established:** **1974**.
- Other BBS surveys: **HIES** (income & poverty, latest 2022), **Labour Force Survey**, **Agriculture Census** (2019), **Economic Census** (2023).
