# Environment, pollution, greenhouse effect

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the main greenhouse gas, ozone-layer damage and the Montreal Protocol, climate treaties (Kyoto, Paris), acid rain gases, arsenic limits in Bangladesh, and ecology terms (ecosystem, food chain, 10% law).

## Ecology basics 🔥
- **Ecology** (term by **Ernst Haeckel, 1866**) studies the relationship between organisms and their environment. **Ecosystem** (term by **A. G. Tansley, 1935**) = living (biotic) + non-living (abiotic) parts working together.
- **Producers** (green plants) → **primary consumers** (herbivores) → **secondary consumers** (carnivores) → **tertiary consumers**. **Decomposers** (bacteria, fungi) return nutrients to soil.
- **Food chain** example: grass → grasshopper → frog → snake → hawk. Linked food chains form a **food web**.
- **10% law (Lindeman)**: only about **10% of energy** passes to the next trophic level; so food chains are short.
- **Sun is the ultimate source of energy** for ecosystems. Energy flow is **one-way**; nutrients **cycle**.
- For ecological balance a country should have about **25% of its land under forest**.

## Greenhouse effect and global warming (গ্রিনহাউস প্রভাব) 🔥🔥
- Sunlight (short-wave) passes through the air; the Earth re-emits **infrared (long-wave) heat**, which **greenhouse gases trap**, warming the planet. Without it, Earth's average temperature would be about **−18 °C** instead of ≈ **15 °C**.
- **Svante Arrhenius (1896)** first calculated warming from CO₂.

| Greenhouse gas | Main sources / fact |
|---|---|
| **Carbon dioxide (CO₂)** | **Main human-caused GHG**; burning fossil fuels, deforestation |
| **Methane (CH₄)** | **Rice paddies**, cattle, landfills, gas leaks; much stronger per molecule than CO₂ |
| **Nitrous oxide (N₂O)** | **Nitrogen fertilizers** |
| **CFCs**, HFCs | Refrigerators, ACs, aerosol sprays |
| **Water vapour** | Most abundant greenhouse gas (natural) |
| Ozone (lower atmosphere) | Smog |

- CO₂ was about **280 ppm before the Industrial Revolution** and passed **420 ppm around 2023** (Mauna Loa observatory).
- Effects: **sea-level rise**, melting glaciers, stronger cyclones, salinity intrusion, floods, droughts. **Bangladesh is one of the most climate-vulnerable countries** (low-lying coastal delta).

> 🔄 Update: check the latest CO₂ concentration and global temperature records in a newspaper or the WMO/NOAA reports.

## Ozone layer (ওজোন স্তর) 🔥🔥
- Ozone (**O₃**) is concentrated in the **stratosphere** (≈ 15–35 km); it **absorbs harmful ultraviolet (UV-B) rays** that cause skin cancer and cataracts.
- Measured in **Dobson units (DU)**.
- Destroyed mainly by **CFCs (chlorofluorocarbons)**, which release **chlorine**; also halons, carbon tetrachloride.
- **Ozone hole over Antarctica** was reported in **1985** (British Antarctic Survey).
- **Vienna Convention (1985)** → **Montreal Protocol (1987)** to phase out ozone-depleting substances. **World Ozone Day: 16 September**. **Kigali Amendment (2016)** adds **HFCs**.
- Ozone near the ground is a **pollutant** (smog).

## Pollution (দূষণ) 🔥
| Pollutant | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Carbon monoxide (CO)** | Vehicle exhaust, incomplete burning | **Binds haemoglobin** → oxygen shortage, death |
| **SO₂ and NOₓ** | Coal, oil burning, vehicles, brick kilns | **Acid rain** (pH < 5.6), respiratory disease |
| **Particulate matter (PM2.5, PM10)** | Dust, brick kilns, vehicles | Lung and heart disease; Dhaka often among the world's most polluted cities |
| **Lead** | Old petrol, batteries, paint | Brain damage in children |
| **Smog** | Smoke + fog | Poor visibility, breathing trouble |
| **Nitrates/phosphates** (fertilizer runoff) | Farms, sewage | **Eutrophication** — algal bloom, fish die (low oxygen) |
| **Mercury** | Industry | **Minamata disease** (Japan) |
| **Cadmium** | Mining | **Itai-itai disease** (Japan) |
| **Methyl isocyanate (MIC)** | Pesticide plant | **Bhopal gas tragedy, India (1984)** |

- **BOD (biochemical oxygen demand)** measures organic water pollution: higher BOD = more polluted.
- **Noise** is measured in **decibels (dB)**; long exposure above ≈ 85 dB harms hearing.

## Bangladesh environment facts 🔥
- **Arsenic in groundwater**: first detected in **1993** (Chapai Nawabganj). **WHO guideline: 0.01 mg/L (10 µg/L)**; **Bangladesh standard: 0.05 mg/L (50 µg/L)**. It causes **arsenicosis** (skin lesions, cancer).
- **Department of Environment** set up in **1989**; **Bangladesh Environment Conservation Act 1995**.
- **Polythene shopping bags banned in 2002**.
- **Bangladesh Climate Change Strategy and Action Plan (BCCSAP) 2009**; **Climate Change Trust Fund** created from Bangladesh's own budget (2009–10).
- Major pollution sources: brick kilns, vehicles, tanneries (moved from Hazaribagh to **Savar** tannery estate), textile dyeing, plastic waste.

## Quick revision
- **CO₂ = main greenhouse gas** from human activity; water vapour is the most abundant.
- Methane from **rice fields and cattle**; N₂O from fertilizers.
- **Ozone layer in the stratosphere** absorbs UV; destroyed by **CFCs**.
- **Montreal Protocol 1987**; Kyoto 1997; **Paris 2015** (1.5–2 °C).
- **Acid rain: SO₂ + NOₓ**; CO binds haemoglobin.
- **Eutrophication** = excess nutrients → algal bloom.
- Arsenic limit: **WHO 0.01 mg/L, Bangladesh 0.05 mg/L**.
- World Environment Day **5 June**; Ozone Day **16 September**; UNEP HQ **Nairobi**.

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | CO₂ from fossil fuels is the main human-caused GHG |
| 2 | a | Chlorine from CFCs breaks down ozone |
| 3 | b | Montreal Protocol (1987) phases out ozone-depleting substances |
| 4 | d | SO₂ and NOₓ form sulphuric and nitric acids |
| 5 | c | Bangladesh standard 0.05 mg/L; WHO 0.01 mg/L |
| 6 | a | Green plants make food by photosynthesis |
| 7 | d | 5 June, since the 1972 Stockholm Conference |
| 8 | a | Anaerobic decay in paddies and cattle digestion release CH₄ |
