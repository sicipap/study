# Modals & causative verbs

> **Why it matters:** Gap-filling questions test the right modal (*must, should, ought to, may, might, need, dare, used to*) and causative patterns (*have it done, get him to do, make him do*). These are common in bank and BCS papers.

## Modal auxiliaries: rules
- Modals: **can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must, ought to**; semi-modals: **need, dare, used to**.
- A modal is followed by the **bare infinitive (V1)**: *He can **swim**.* (✗ can swims, ✗ can to swim) — except **ought to** and **used to**.
- Modals have **no -s** in the third person: *She **must** go.*
- Modals do not take *do* in questions/negatives: *Can he swim? He cannot swim.*

## Uses of each modal 🔥
| Modal | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **can** | ability (present), permission (informal) | I **can** speak English. **Can** I go now? |
| **could** | past ability; polite request | He **could** run fast as a boy. **Could** you help me? |
| **may** | permission (formal); possibility | **May** I come in, sir? It **may** rain today. |
| **might** | weaker possibility; past of *may* | He **might** come, but I doubt it. |
| **may** (wish) | prayer / wish | **May** you live long! **May** Allah bless you. |
| **shall** | future with I/we (formal); offers; command in law | **Shall** I open the window? |
| **should** | duty, advice | You **should** respect your parents. |
| **ought to** | moral duty, obligation | We **ought to** help the poor. |
| **will** | future; willingness | I **will** help you. |
| **would** | past habit; polite request; conditional | He **would** walk by the river every evening. **Would** you mind waiting? |
| **must** | strong necessity; logical certainty | You **must** obey the law. He **must** be tired after the journey. |
| **must not** | prohibition | You **must not** smoke here. |
| **need not** | absence of necessity | You **need not** come tomorrow. |
| **dare** | courage | How **dare** you insult me? He **dare not** go there. |
| **used to** | past habit (no longer true) | I **used to** play football. |

**Must not vs need not:** *must not* = it is forbidden; *need not* = it is not necessary.
**Need / dare** as modals are used mainly in **negatives and questions**: *Need he go? He need not go.* As main verbs they take *to*: *He needs to go.*

## Perfect modals (modal + have + V3) 🔥
| Form | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **should have + V3** | duty not done in the past (regret) | You **should have come** earlier. |
| **must have + V3** | strong logical conclusion about the past | The road is wet; it **must have rained**. |
| **could have + V3** | past ability/possibility not used | You **could have won** the match. |
| **may / might have + V3** | past possibility | He **may have missed** the bus. |
| **need not have + V3** | did something unnecessary | You **need not have bought** milk; we had plenty. |
| **would have + V3** | third conditional | I **would have helped** you if I had known. |

## Causative verbs 🔥
Used when someone **causes** another person to do something.

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **have + person + V1** | arrange for someone to do | I **had the mechanic repair** my car. |
| **have + thing + V3** | get something done by someone | I **had my car repaired**. I **had my hair cut**. |
| **get + person + to + V1** | persuade someone to do | I **got him to sign** the paper. |
| **get + thing + V3** | same as *have + thing + V3* | She **got her watch mended**. |
| **make + person + V1** (no *to*) | force | The teacher **made him stand** up. |
| **let + person + V1** (no *to*) | allow | **Let** him **go**. Father **let** me **use** his car. |
| **help + person + (to) V1** | assist (both correct) | He **helped me (to) carry** the bag. |

Passive of **make**: *to* comes back.
- Active: *They **made** him **work**.* → Passive: *He **was made to work**.*

Common exam items:
- ✓ *I had my **photograph taken**.* ✗ *I had my photograph take.*
- ✓ *He made me **laugh**.* ✗ *He made me to laugh.*
- ✓ *I got him **to repair** it.* ✗ *I got him repair it.*

## Quick revision
- Modal + **bare V1**; only **ought to / used to** take *to*.
- **May** = permission, possibility, wish ("May you live long").
- **Must not** = prohibited; **need not** = not necessary.
- **Should have + V3** = regret about the past; **must have + V3** = certain deduction.
- **have / get + thing + V3**: I had my hair **cut**.
- **make / let + person + V1** (no *to*); **get + person + to V1**.
- Passive: He **was made to** work.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** You ____ respect your elders.
(a) ought (b) should (c) need (d) dare

**2.** ____ you live long!
(a) Can (b) Must (c) May (d) Shall

**3.** The road is wet. It ____ last night.
(a) must have rained (b) should rain (c) must rain (d) can rained

**4.** I had my hair ____.
(a) cut (b) to cut (c) cutting (d) cuts

**5.** The teacher made him ____ up.
(a) to stand (b) standing (c) stood (d) stand

**6.** You ____ come tomorrow; it is a holiday.
(a) must not (b) need not (c) dare not (d) ought not

**7.** I got the carpenter ____ the chair.
(a) repair (b) repaired (c) to repair (d) repairing

**8.** Passive of "They made him work" is:
(a) He was made work. (b) He was made working. (c) He made to work. (d) He was made to work.

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | should + V1 for duty; "ought" would need "to" |
| 2 | c | May expresses a wish or prayer |
| 3 | a | must have + V3 for a certain deduction about the past |
| 4 | a | have + thing + V3 |
| 5 | d | make + person + bare infinitive |
| 6 | b | need not = not necessary |
| 7 | c | get + person + to + V1 |
| 8 | d | In the passive, make takes "to" |
