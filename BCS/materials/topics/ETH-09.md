# RTI Act 2009, Citizen Charter, Ombudsman

> **Why it matters:** The Right to Information Act (তথ্য অধিকার আইন) 2009 is asked in nearly every paper: its time limits, the Information Commission, fines and appeals. Citizen Charter and Ombudsman questions ask about origins (UK, Sweden) and the Bangladesh position.

## Right to Information Act 2009 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Name | **তথ্য অধিকার আইন, ২০০৯** (Act No. **20** of 2009) |
| Background | Began as the **RTI Ordinance 2008** (caretaker government) |
| Passed by Parliament | **29 March 2009** |
| Published in the Gazette | **6 April 2009** |
| Total sections | **37** |
| Information Commission set up | **1 July 2009** |
| Right given to | **Every citizen** (section 4) |
| Officer who gives information | **Designated Officer** (দায়িত্বপ্রাপ্ত কর্মকর্তা) in each authority |
| World's first RTI-type law | **Sweden, 1766** (Freedom of the Press Act) |
| International Day for Universal Access to Information | **28 September** |

### Time limits 🔥
| Step | Time limit |
|---|---|
| Designated officer gives information | **Within 20 working days** of the request |
| If more than one unit or authority is involved | **Within 30 working days** |
| Information about a person's **life or death, arrest or release from jail** | **Within 24 hours** (preliminary information) |
| If unable to give information, officer informs the applicant with reasons | **Within 10 working days** |
| Appeal to the appellate authority | **Within 30 days** |
| Appellate authority decides | **Within 15 days** |
| Complaint to the Information Commission | Within 30 days after the appeal result or time limit |
| Information Commission settles a complaint | Normally **45 days**, extendable up to **75 days** |

### Information Commission (তথ্য কমিশন) 🔥
- An **independent statutory body**.
- **One Chief Information Commissioner + two Information Commissioners** (3 in all); **at least one must be a woman**.
- Term: **5 years or until age 67**, whichever comes first.
- Can impose a **fine of Tk 50 per day** of delay on a designated officer, up to a **maximum of Tk 5,000**, and recommend departmental action.

### Exemptions
- Section 7 lists information that **need not be disclosed** (e.g. harm to security, sovereignty, foreign relations, ongoing investigations, trade secrets, personal privacy, Cabinet documents).
- Certain **security and intelligence agencies** listed in the schedule are exempt, **but information on corruption and human rights violations** in those agencies must still be given.
- Authorities must also publish information **proactively** (section 6).

## Citizen Charter (নাগরিক সনদ) 🔥
- A public statement by a service-giving office about **what services it provides, how, in what time, at what cost, who is responsible, and how to complain**.
- First introduced in the **United Kingdom in 1991** under Prime Minister **John Major**.
- Introduced in Bangladesh government offices in **2007**; now displayed in offices and on websites.
- Linked to the **Grievance Redress System (অভিযোগ প্রতিকার ব্যবস্থা, GRS)**, through which citizens can lodge complaints if the promised service is not given.

## Ombudsman (ন্যায়পাল) 🔥
- A Swedish word meaning **"representative" or "agent" (of the people)**: an independent officer who investigates **complaints against government administration**.
- First established in **Sweden in 1809**. Later: Finland (1919), Denmark (1955), **New Zealand (1962, first in the Commonwealth)**, UK (Parliamentary Commissioner, 1967).
- **Bangladesh:**
  - Provided for in **Article 77** of the Constitution.
  - **Ombudsman Act 1980** was passed, but **no general Ombudsman has been appointed** so far.
  - A **Tax Ombudsman** was created under the **Tax Ombudsman Act 2005**; the office began in 2006 and was **abolished in 2011**.
- India's equivalent: **Lokpal** (Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act 2013).

> 🔄 Update: check whether a general Ombudsman has been appointed or the law changed after state-reform proposals.

## Quick revision
- RTI Act 2009: passed 29 March, gazetted 6 April, 37 sections; Information Commission from 1 July 2009.
- 20 working days; 30 if several units; 24 hours for life-and-death or arrest information.
- Fine Tk 50 per day, maximum Tk 5,000.
- Commission: 1 chief + 2 commissioners, at least one woman; 5 years or age 67.
- First RTI law: Sweden 1766.
- Citizen Charter: UK 1991 (John Major); Bangladesh 2007.
- Ombudsman: Sweden 1809; Bangladesh Article 77, Ombudsman Act 1980.
