# Ecosystem, biodiversity, protected areas of BD

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask who coined "ecosystem", the parts of a food chain, levels of biodiversity, IUCN categories, and Bangladesh facts: the Sundarbans (World Heritage and Ramsar site), Ramsar sites, national parks, ECAs and St. Martin's Island.

## Ecosystem (বাস্তুতন্ত্র) 🔥
- An **ecosystem** is a community of living things interacting with each other and with their **non-living environment**.
- 🔥 The term **"ecosystem"** was coined by **Arthur G. Tansley** (British ecologist) in **1935**.
- **Ecology:** the study of relationships between organisms and their environment; the word was coined by **Ernst Haeckel** (1866).

| Component | Examples / role |
|---|---|
| **Abiotic (অজীব)** | Sunlight, water, air, soil, temperature, minerals |
| 🔥 **Producers (উৎপাদক)** | **Green plants, algae** — make food by **photosynthesis** |
| **Primary consumers** | **Herbivores** (deer, grasshopper, zooplankton) |
| **Secondary / tertiary consumers** | **Carnivores** (frog, snake, tiger) |
| 🔥 **Decomposers (বিয়োজক)** | **Bacteria and fungi** — break down dead matter and return nutrients to soil |

- **Food chain:** grass → deer → tiger. **Food web:** many connected food chains.
- **Trophic level:** each step in a food chain.
- 🔥 **10% law (Lindeman, 1942):** only about **10% of energy** passes from one trophic level to the next; the rest is lost mostly as heat.
- The **Sun** is the ultimate source of energy for almost all ecosystems.
- **Types:** terrestrial (forest, grassland, desert) and aquatic (pond, river, sea, wetland).

## Biodiversity (জীববৈচিত্র্য) 🔥
- The **variety of life** on Earth. The word **"biodiversity"** was coined by **Walter G. Rosen** (1985) and popularised by **E. O. Wilson**.
- 🔥 **Three levels:** **genetic diversity**, **species diversity**, **ecosystem diversity**.
- **Biodiversity hotspot:** a region with very high species richness that is seriously threatened; idea by **Norman Myers (1988)**. Bangladesh is in the **Indo-Burma hotspot**.
- **Conservation:**
  - **In-situ (স্বস্থানে):** protecting species **in their natural habitat** — national parks, wildlife sanctuaries, biosphere reserves.
  - **Ex-situ (স্থানান্তরে):** **outside** the natural habitat — **zoos, botanical gardens, seed banks, gene banks**.
- **Endemic species:** found only in one place.

### IUCN Red List categories 🔥
**IUCN** (International Union for Conservation of Nature), founded **1948**, headquarters **Gland, Switzerland**, publishes the **Red List** of threatened species.
Order: **Extinct (EX) → Extinct in the Wild (EW) → Critically Endangered (CR) → Endangered (EN) → Vulnerable (VU) → Near Threatened (NT) → Least Concern (LC)**; plus **Data Deficient (DD)**.
- **Threatened** = CR + EN + VU.

## The Sundarbans (সুন্দরবন) 🔥🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Type | **World's largest single tract of mangrove forest** |
| Total area | About **10,000 sq km**; about **62%** (about **6,017 sq km**) is in **Bangladesh**, the rest in India |
| Districts (Bangladesh) | Mainly **Khulna, Satkhira, Bagerhat** (with small parts of Patuakhali and Barguna) |
| Name | Usually said to come from the **Sundari tree** (*Heritiera fomes*) |
| 🔥 **UNESCO World Heritage Site** | **1997** — Bangladesh's **only natural** World Heritage site |
| 🔥 **Ramsar site** | **1992** — Bangladesh's **first Ramsar site** |
| Famous animals | **Royal Bengal Tiger**, spotted deer, estuarine crocodile, Irrawaddy and Ganges dolphins |
| Tiger count | **125** tigers in the Bangladesh Sundarbans (survey results announced in **2024**; the previous count was 114) |

> 🔄 Update: tiger numbers change with each survey; check the latest Forest Department figure.

## Protected areas of Bangladesh 🔥
**Main laws:** **Bangladesh Wildlife (Conservation and Security) Act 2012**; **Bangladesh Biodiversity Act 2017**; **Environment Conservation Act 1995** (for ECAs).

### Ramsar sites (2)
1. 🔥 **Sundarbans** — **1992** (first)
2. 🔥 **Tanguar Haor** (Sunamganj) — **2000**

### Selected national parks (জাতীয় উদ্যান)
| National park | District | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Bhawal National Park** | **Gazipur** | One of the oldest and best-known |
| **Madhupur National Park** | Tangail | Sal forest |
| 🔥 **Lawachara National Park** | **Moulvibazar** | Rainforest; home of the **Hoolock gibbon** (only ape of Bangladesh) |
| **Himchari National Park** | Cox's Bazar | Hills and waterfalls by the sea |
| **Satchari National Park** | Habiganj | |
| **Ramsagar National Park** | Dinajpur | Around the large Ramsagar dighi |
| **Nijhum Dwip National Park** | Noakhali | Island; spotted deer |
| **Kaptai National Park** | Rangamati | |

### Selected wildlife sanctuaries (বন্যপ্রাণী অভয়ারণ্য)
**Rema-Kalenga** (Habiganj), **Char Kukri-Mukri** (Bhola), **Pablakhali** (Rangamati), **Chunati** (Chattogram), **Teknaf** (Cox's Bazar), and the three sanctuaries inside the Sundarbans (East, West and South).
- **Safari parks:** **Dulahazra, Cox's Bazar** (the first, 1999) and **Gazipur**.

### Ecologically Critical Areas (ECA — পরিবেশগতভাবে সংকটাপন্ন এলাকা) 🔥
Declared under the **Environment Conservation Act 1995**. The first **eight ECAs (1999)** were:
**Sundarbans** (10 km around it), **Cox's Bazar–Teknaf beach**, 🔥 **St. Martin's Island**, **Sonadia Island**, **Hakaluki Haor**, **Tanguar Haor**, **Marjat Baor** (Jhenaidah), and **Gulshan–Baridhara Lake** (Dhaka). More were added later (e.g. rivers around Dhaka).

### Marine and island facts
- 🔥 **St. Martin's Island (সেন্ট মার্টিন দ্বীপ / নারিকেল জিঞ্জিরা):** Bangladesh's **only coral island**, in Teknaf, Cox's Bazar.
- **Swatch of No Ground:** Bangladesh's **first marine protected area (2014)** — whales, dolphins, sharks.

## Quick revision
- "Ecosystem" coined by **Tansley (1935)**; "ecology" by **Haeckel**.
- Producers = green plants; decomposers = **bacteria and fungi**.
- **10% energy law** (Lindeman).
- Biodiversity levels: **genetic, species, ecosystem**.
- IUCN HQ: **Gland, Switzerland**; Red List.
- Sundarbans: World Heritage **1997**, Ramsar **1992**; ~62% in Bangladesh.
- Ramsar sites of Bangladesh: **Sundarbans, Tanguar Haor**.
- Lawachara: **Hoolock gibbon**; St. Martin's: **only coral island**.
- ECAs are declared under the **Environment Conservation Act 1995**.
