# Clauses (noun, adjective, adverb)

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask you to identify the kind of clause ("The underlined part is a noun/adjective/adverb clause"), choose the right relative pronoun (who/whom/whose/which/that), and combine sentences.

## Basics
- A **clause** is a group of words with a **subject and a finite verb**. A **phrase** has no finite verb.
  - *in the morning* → phrase. *when the sun rose* → clause.
- **Principal (main / independent) clause:** can stand alone.
- **Subordinate (dependent) clause:** cannot stand alone; works as a **noun, adjective or adverb**.
- Sentence types: **simple** (one main clause), **compound** (two main clauses + *and, but, or, so*), **complex** (main clause + subordinate clause).

## 1. Noun clause 🔥
Does the work of a **noun**. Usually begins with **that, what, whether, if, who, why, how, where, when, whatever**.

| Function | Example |
|---|---|
| **Subject** of a verb | **What he said** is true. / **That he is honest** is known to all. |
| **Object** of a verb | I know **that he is honest**. / Tell me **where you live**. |
| **Object of a preposition** | Pay attention to **what the teacher says**. |
| **Complement** of a verb | This is **what I wanted**. / The truth is **that he failed**. |
| **In apposition** to a noun | The news **that he had died** shocked us. |

Test: replace the clause with "it" or "this". *I know it.* → noun clause.

## 2. Adjective (relative) clause 🔥
Qualifies a **noun or pronoun**, usually coming right after it. Begins with a **relative pronoun** (**who, whom, whose, which, that**) or **relative adverb** (**where, when, why**).

| Relative word | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **who** | person, subject | The man **who came here** is my uncle. |
| **whom** | person, object | The boy **whom you punished** is innocent. |
| **whose** | possession | This is the girl **whose father is a doctor**. |
| **which** | things, animals | The pen **which I bought** is lost. |
| **that** | persons or things (defining clauses) | This is the house **that Jack built**. |
| **where** | place | This is the village **where I was born**. |
| **when** | time | I remember the day **when we met**. |
| **why** | reason | Tell me the reason **why you are late**. |

Rules:
- **Defining (restrictive)** clause: no commas; needed to identify the noun. *Students who work hard succeed.*
- **Non-defining (non-restrictive)** clause: with commas; extra information. *My father, **who is a doctor**, lives in Khulna.* Do **not** use *that* here.
- **That** is preferred after **superlatives, all, only, none, nothing, everything**: *He is the best man **that** I know. All **that** glitters is not gold.*

Noun clause vs adjective clause (same word *that*):
- *The news **that he won** is true.* → **noun clause** (apposition; "that" is only a conjunction).
- *The news **that he brought** is true.* → **adjective clause** ("that" = which, object of *brought*).

## 3. Adverb clause 🔥
Modifies a **verb, adjective or adverb**; tells when, where, why, how, on what condition, etc.

| Kind | Conjunctions | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** | when, while, before, after, since, until, as soon as | Wait **until I return**. |
| **Place** | where, wherever | Go **wherever you like**. |
| **Reason / cause** | because, since, as | He was absent **because he was ill**. |
| **Purpose** | so that, in order that, lest | He works hard **so that he may succeed**. |
| **Result** | so … that, such … that | He is **so** weak **that he cannot walk**. |
| **Condition** | if, unless, provided | **If you run**, you will catch the bus. |
| **Concession / contrast** | though, although, even if | **Though he is poor**, he is honest. |
| **Comparison** | than, as … as | He is taller **than I am**. |
| **Manner** | as, as if, as though | Do **as I tell you**. |

Test: the clause answers **when / where / why / how / under what condition**.

## Identifying quickly
| Sentence | Clause type |
|---|---|
| I know **where he lives**. | Noun (object of *know*) |
| This is the house **where he lives**. | Adjective (qualifies *house*) |
| Stay **where you are**. | Adverb (place, modifies *stay*) |

Same word *where*, three different clauses: the job the clause does decides its type.

## Quick revision
- Clause = subject + finite verb; phrase has no finite verb.
- **Noun clause** = subject / object / complement / apposition; test with "it".
- **Adjective clause** follows a noun: **who, whom, whose, which, that, where, when, why**.
- **Adverb clause** tells time, place, reason, purpose, result, condition, concession, comparison, manner.
- Non-defining clauses take **commas** and never *that*.
- Use **that** after superlatives, *all, only, nothing*.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** "I know **that he is honest**." The bold part is a/an:
(a) adjective clause (b) noun clause (c) adverb clause (d) phrase

**2.** "The man **who came yesterday** is my uncle." The bold part is a/an:
(a) adjective clause (b) noun clause (c) adverb clause (d) principal clause

**3.** "He was absent **because he was ill**." The bold part is an adverb clause of:
(a) time (b) purpose (c) reason (d) condition

**4.** This is the girl ____ father is a doctor.
(a) who (b) whom (c) which (d) whose

**5.** "Stay **where you are**." The bold part is a/an:
(a) noun clause (b) adjective clause (c) adverb clause (d) phrase

**6.** All ____ glitters is not gold.
(a) what (b) that (c) whom (d) whose

**7.** "**What he said** is true." The bold clause works as:
(a) the subject (b) the object (c) the complement (d) an adjective

**8.** "**Though he is poor**, he is honest." The bold part is an adverb clause of:
(a) reason (b) result (c) condition (d) concession

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Object of the verb "know" |
| 2 | a | Qualifies the noun "man" |
| 3 | c | "because" introduces reason |
| 4 | d | whose shows possession |
| 5 | c | Adverb clause of place modifying "stay" |
| 6 | b | "that" is used after all, only, superlatives |
