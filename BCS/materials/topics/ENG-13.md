# Right form of verbs

> **Why it matters:** "Right form of verbs" items test fixed structures: "as if + were", "It is time + past", "would rather", "lest … should", "no sooner … than", conditionals, and verbs after modals and prepositions.

## Step-by-step method
1. Find the **subject** (singular/plural, person).
2. Look for **time words** (yesterday, since, for, already, tomorrow).
3. Check for **fixed structures** (below).
4. Check whether the verb is after a **modal**, a **preposition** or **"to"**.

## Fixed structures 🔥
| Structure | Verb form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **as if / as though** (unreal) | past; be → **were** for all subjects | "He behaves **as if** he **were** a king." "She talks as if she **knew** everything." |
| **I wish / If only** (present) | past; be → **were** | "I wish I **were** rich." |
| **wish** (past) | had + V3 | "I wish I **had studied** harder." |
| **It is time / high time / about time** | subject + **past** | "It is high time we **changed** the system." |
| **It is time** + to-infinitive | | "It is time **to go**." |
| **would rather / would sooner** + subject | past | "I'd rather you **stayed** here." |
| **would rather** (same subject) | bare verb | "I **would rather die** than beg." |
| **had better** | bare verb | "You **had better take** rest." |
| **lest** | **should** + bare verb | "Work hard **lest** you **should fail**." |
| **No sooner … than** | had + subject + V3 … than + past | "No sooner **had** the bell **rung** than the students **left**." |
| **Hardly / Scarcely … when/before** | had + subject + V3 … when + past | "Scarcely **had** he **arrived** when it **started** to rain." |
| **Since** (time) | present perfect + since + past | "Ten years **have passed** since he **left**." |
| **Suggest / propose / insist / demand / recommend that** | subject + (should) + **bare verb** (subjunctive) | "The doctor suggested that he **(should) take** rest." "I insist that he **be** present." |
| **May** (wish) | may + subject + bare verb | "**May** you **live** long!" |
| **Let** | object + bare verb | "Let him **go**." |
| **would that** | past | "Would that I **were** young again!" |

## Conditional sentences 🔥
| Type | If-clause | Main clause | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** (fact) | present | present | "If you heat ice, it **melts**." |
| **1** (possible) | **present** | **will** + V1 | "If it **rains**, I **will stay** home." |
| **2** (unreal present) | **past** (were) | **would** + V1 | "If I **were** a bird, I **would fly**." |
| **3** (unreal past) | **had + V3** | **would have** + V3 | "If he **had worked** hard, he **would have passed**." |

- Inversion: "**Had** I **known**, I would have helped." "**Were** I you, I would accept." "**Should** it rain, stay home."
- **Unless** = if not; do not add another "not": "Unless you **work** hard, you will fail."

## Verbs after modals, prepositions and "to"
- **Modal + bare verb:** "He can **swim**." "You must **go**." "He ought **to go**" (ought takes *to*).
- **Preposition + gerund:** "He is fond **of swimming**." "He left **without saying** goodbye." "He insisted **on going**."
- **"to" as preposition + gerund:** look forward to, with a view to, be used to, be accustomed to, object to: "I am used **to walking**."
- **used to + V1** (past habit): "I **used to walk** daily."
- **make / let / see / hear + object + bare verb** (active); **was made to + V1** (passive).
- **need / dare** as modals (negative/question): "You **need not go**." "How **dare** he **enter**?"
- **Causative:** **have / get something done**: "I **had** my hair **cut**." "Get the letter **typed**."

## Subject-verb and tense clues
| Clue | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| every day, always, usually | present simple | "He **goes** to school every day." |
| now, at present, look!, listen! | present continuous | "Look! The baby **is sleeping**." |
| just, already, yet, ever, recently | present perfect | "I have just **finished**." |
| since / for (still going on) | present perfect (continuous) | "He **has been living** here since 2015." |
| yesterday, ago, last, in 2010 | past simple | "He **came** yesterday." |
| before / after two past actions | earlier one past perfect | "He **had left** before I came." |
| tomorrow, next week | future | "He **will come** tomorrow." |
| by + future time | future perfect | "By 2030 I **will have retired**." |
| universal truth | present | "The sun **rises** in the east." |

## Common mistakes
| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| He talks as if he was a leader. | … as if he **were** a leader. |
| It is time we start. | It is time we **started**. |
| Walk carefully lest you fall down. (exam style) | … lest you **should fall** down. |
| If I was you, I would go. | If I **were** you, I would go. |
| If he had come, I would help him. | … I **would have helped** him. |
| I am used to get up early. | I am used to **getting** up early. |
| He suggested me to go. | He suggested that I **(should) go**. |
| He can sings. | He can **sing**. |

## Quick revision
- 🔥 **as if / wish + were** (for every subject).
- 🔥 **It is (high) time + past**.
- 🔥 **lest + should + V1**.
- **would rather + subject + past**; **had better + V1**.
- Conditional 3: **had + V3 → would have + V3**.
- **suggest/insist/demand that + (should) + V1**.
- Preposition + **-ing**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** "He speaks as if he ___ the owner of the house."
(a) is (b) was (c) were (d) had

**2.** "Move carefully lest you ___."
(a) should fall (b) fall down (c) will fall (d) fell

**3.** "It is high time the government ___ steps."
(a) take (b) took (c) will take (d) has taken

**4.** "If I had seen him, I ___ him."
(a) would tell (b) will tell (c) had told (d) would have told

**5.** "I would rather you ___ now."
(a) left (b) leave (c) will leave (d) leaving

**6.** "He insisted on ___ there."
(a) go (b) to go (c) going (d) gone

**7.** "The teacher demanded that the student ___ the truth."
(a) tells (b) tell (c) told (d) telling

**8.** "Had I known the truth, I ___ differently."
(a) would act (b) will act (c) acted (d) would have acted
