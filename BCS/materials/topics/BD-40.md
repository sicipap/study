# Archaeological & World Heritage sites

> **Why it matters:** The three World Heritage Sites, who built Paharpur, Shat Gombuj Masjid, Mahasthangarh, Mainamati, Lalbagh Fort and Ahsan Manzil are among the most repeated Bangladesh-affairs MCQs.

## UNESCO World Heritage Sites of Bangladesh 🔥🔥
Bangladesh has **3** World Heritage Sites (2 cultural, 1 natural):

| Site | Type | Year |
|---|---|---|
| **Historic Mosque City of Bagerhat (বাগেরহাটের ঐতিহাসিক মসজিদের শহর)** | Cultural | **1985** |
| **Ruins of the Buddhist Vihara at Paharpur (পাহাড়পুর বৌদ্ধ বিহার)** | Cultural | **1985** |
| **The Sundarbans (সুন্দরবন)** | Natural | **1997** |

## Paharpur — Somapura Mahavihara (সোমপুর মহাবিহার) 🔥
- Location: **Badalgachhi, Naogaon**.
- Built by the **Pala king ধর্মপাল** (late 8th – early 9th century).
- The **largest Buddhist monastery south of the Himalayas**; about **177 monks' cells** around a huge cross-shaped central temple with terracotta plaques.
- First noticed by **Buchanan-Hamilton** in the early 19th century; excavated in the 1920s–30s.

## Bagerhat — Khalifatabad 🔥
- Built by **খান জাহান আলী** in the **15th century**; the old city name was **খলিফাতাবাদ**.
- **ষাট গম্বুজ মসজিদ (Shat Gombuj / Sixty Dome Mosque):** despite the name it has **77 domes** on the roof (81 counting the 4 corner towers) and **60 stone pillars**. It is the **largest medieval mosque** in Bangladesh.
- Also: **Khan Jahan's tomb**, **Nine Dome Mosque**, **Singair Mosque**.

## Dhaka's historic buildings 🔥
| Building | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **লালবাগ কেল্লা (Lalbagh Fort)** | Begun **1678** by Prince **Muhammad Azam** (son of Aurangzeb); continued by **Shaista Khan**; left **unfinished** after the death of his daughter **পরী বিবি** (1684). Old name **কেল্লা আওরঙ্গাবাদ** |
| **আহসান মঞ্জিল** | Kumartoli, on the **Buriganga**; built by **Nawab Abdul Ghani** (completed **1872**), named after his son **Khwaja Ahsanullah**; called the **"Pink Palace"**; museum since **1992** |
| **বড় কাটরা** | Built **1644** under **Shah Shuja** |
| **ছোট কাটরা** | Built by **Shaista Khan** (1663) |
| **বিনত বিবির মসজিদ** | **1454**; **oldest surviving mosque in Dhaka** |
| **তারা মসজিদ (Star Mosque)** | Armanitola; decorated with star-shaped mosaic |
| **ঢাকেশ্বরী মন্দির** | **National temple** |
| **কার্জন হল** | University of Dhaka; foundation laid by **Lord Curzon** (1904) |

## Museums 🔥
- **বরেন্দ্র গবেষণা জাদুঘর (Varendra Research Museum), Rajshahi (1910):** the **first museum** of Bangladesh.
- **Bangladesh National Museum**, Shahbag: began as the **Dhaka Museum (1913)**; national museum from **1983**.
- **Liberation War Museum:** opened **22 March 1996** at Segunbagicha; moved to **Agargaon** in **2017**.
- **Folk Art and Crafts Museum:** Sonargaon (founded by Zainul Abedin, 1975).
- **Department of Archaeology (প্রত্নতত্ত্ব অধিদপ্তর)** protects sites.

## Quick revision
- 3 World Heritage Sites: **Bagerhat (1985), Paharpur (1985), Sundarbans (1997)**.
- Paharpur = **Somapura Mahavihara**, **Dharmapala**, Naogaon.
- Shat Gombuj Masjid: **Khan Jahan Ali**, **77 domes**, 60 pillars.
- Mahasthangarh = **Pundranagar**, oldest city, Bogura.
- Shalban Vihara: **Mainamati, Cumilla**.
- Lalbagh Fort: **Prince Azam (1678)**, unfinished after **Pari Bibi**'s death.
- Ahsan Manzil: **Nawab Abdul Ghani**, Pink Palace.
- First museum: **Varendra Research Museum (1910)**.
