# Fractions, decimals, simplification

> **Why it matters:** Simplification (সরলীকরণ) with BODMAS, recurring decimals (আবৃত দশমিক) and comparing fractions appear in nearly every primary-teacher, bank and 9th–20th grade paper, and sometimes in BCS.

## Key concepts
- **Proper fraction (প্রকৃত ভগ্নাংশ):** numerator < denominator (3/5). **Improper (অপ্রকৃত):** numerator ≥ denominator (7/4). **Mixed (মিশ্র):** 1¾ = 7/4.
- **Like fractions (সমহর):** same denominator; **unlike (অসমহর):** different denominators.
- 🔥 **BODMAS order:** **B**rackets → **O**f → **D**ivision → **M**ultiplication → **A**ddition → **S**ubtraction. Division and multiplication are done **left to right** as they appear; so are addition and subtraction.
- Bracket order: ( ) first, then { }, then [ ]. A bar (রেখা বন্ধনী) over numbers is solved before everything else.
- "Of" means multiply, and is done before ÷: 12 ÷ 1/2 of 8 = 12 ÷ 4 = 3.
- **Terminating decimal:** a fraction in lowest terms terminates only if its denominator has **no prime factors other than 2 and 5** (3/40 = 0.075). Otherwise it recurs (5/6 = 0.8333…).

## Recurring decimal to fraction 🔥
| Type | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Pure recurring 0.āb̄ | recurring digits ÷ as many 9s | 0.2323… = 23/99 |
| Pure one digit 0.ā | digit ÷ 9 | 0.777… = 7/9 |
| Mixed recurring 0.a b̄ | (all digits − non-recurring digits) ÷ (9s for recurring, then 0s for non-recurring) | 0.1222… = (12 − 1)/90 = 11/90 |
| 0.999… | equals exactly 1 | 0.333… + 0.666… = 1 |

## Formula and shortcut table
| Item | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Compare fractions | Convert to decimals, or cross-multiply: a/b > c/d if ad > bc |
| 1/(n(n + 1)) | 1/n − 1/(n + 1) (telescoping sums) |
| Decimal places in a product | Sum of decimal places of the factors: 0.5 × 0.05 × 0.005 = 0.000125 |
| Divide decimals | Make the divisor whole: 0.04 ÷ 0.002 = 40 ÷ 2 = 20 |
| Fraction of a number | "x/y of N" = N × x/y |
| Difference of fractions of a number | If a/b of N − c/d of N = k, then N = k ÷ (a/b − c/d) |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Simplify: 8 − 4 ÷ 2 × 3 + 1
Steps: 4 ÷ 2 = 2; 2 × 3 = 6; 8 − 6 + 1 = 3.
**Answer: 3**

**Example 2.** Express 0.1222… (2 recurring) as a fraction.
Steps: (12 − 1)/90 = 11/90.
**Answer: 11/90**

**Example 3.** Which is the largest: 3/5, 5/8, 7/11, 2/3?
Steps: 0.6, 0.625, 0.636, 0.667.
**Answer: 2/3**

**Example 4.** Find 1/(1×2) + 1/(2×3) + … + 1/(9×10).
Steps: Each term = 1/n − 1/(n + 1). All middle terms cancel: 1 − 1/10 = 9/10.
**Answer: 9/10**

**Example 5.** One-third of a number is 12 more than one-fourth of it. Find the number.
Steps: N/3 − N/4 = N/12 = 12 → N = 144.
**Answer: 144**

**Example 6.** Simplify 1 + 1/(1 + 1/(1 + 1/2)).
Steps: 1 + 1/2 = 3/2 → 1/(3/2) = 2/3 → 1 + 2/3 = 5/3 → 1/(5/3) = 3/5 → 1 + 3/5 = 8/5.
**Answer: 8/5**

## Common traps
- Doing multiplication before division when division comes first. 12 ÷ 3 × 2 = 8, **not** 2.
- 0.3666… is **not** 36/99; it is (36 − 3)/90 = 11/30.
- 0.999… is exactly 1, not "slightly less than 1".
- Continued fractions: always start from the **bottom**.

## Quick revision
- BODMAS; ÷ and × are left to right.
- "Of" is done before ÷.
- 0.āb̄ = ab/99; 0.a b̄ = (ab − a)/90.
- Terminating decimal ⇔ denominator has only 2s and 5s.
- 1/(n(n + 1)) = 1/n − 1/(n + 1).
- Decimal places add up in multiplication.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Simplify: 12 ÷ 3 × 2 − 4 + 1
(a) 5 (b) 1 (c) 3 (d) 7

**2.** 0.333… + 0.666… = ?
(a) 0.9 (b) 1 (c) 0.99 (d) 1.1

**3.** 0.3666… (6 recurring) as a fraction is:
(a) 36/99 (b) 4/11 (c) 11/30 (d) 33/100

**4.** Which is the smallest fraction?
(a) 5/6 (b) 7/9 (c) 3/4 (d) 11/15

**5.** 1.1 × 1.1 × 1.1 = ?
(a) 1.331 (b) 13.31 (c) 1.0331 (d) 0.1331

**6.** 0.04 ÷ 0.002 = ?
(a) 2 (b) 20 (c) 200 (d) 0.2

**7.** Which fraction gives a terminating decimal?
(a) 7/12 (b) 5/6 (c) 2/15 (d) 3/40

**8.** 1/2 + 1/6 + 1/12 + 1/20 + 1/30 = ?
(a) 5/6 (b) 6/7 (c) 4/5 (d) 1

**9.** One-third of a number is 12 more than its one-fourth. The number is:
(a) 48 (b) 72 (c) 144 (d) 96

**10.** 1 + 1/(1 + 1/(1 + 1/2)) = ?
(a) 5/3 (b) 3/2 (c) 13/8 (d) 8/5

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a | 12 ÷ 3 = 4, × 2 = 8, 8 − 4 + 1 = 5 |
| 2 | b | 1/3 + 2/3 = 1 |
| 3 | c | (36 − 3)/90 = 33/90 = 11/30 |
| 4 | d | 0.833, 0.778, 0.75, 0.733 → 11/15 smallest |
| 5 | a | 1.21 × 1.1 = 1.331 |
| 6 | b | 40 ÷ 2 = 20 |
| 7 | d | 40 = 2³ × 5 → 3/40 = 0.075 |
| 8 | a | Telescoping: 1 − 1/6 = 5/6 |
