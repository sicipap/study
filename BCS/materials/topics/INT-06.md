# UN: formation, charter, organs

> **Why it matters:** The UN is one of the most asked areas: UN Day, the San Francisco Conference, number of articles and official languages, the six organs, Security Council voting and veto, and the date Bangladesh joined (17 September 1974).

## Formation (জাতিসংঘ গঠন) 🔥
| Step | Date / fact |
|---|---|
| Name "United Nations" | Coined by US President **Franklin D. Roosevelt**; first used in the **Declaration by United Nations, 1 January 1942** (26 nations) |
| **Atlantic Charter** | 14 August 1941 (Churchill & Roosevelt) |
| **Dumbarton Oaks** (Washington) | August–October 1944: draft proposals for the UN |
| **Yalta Conference** | February 1945: Security Council **veto** formula agreed |
| 🔥 **San Francisco Conference** | **25 April – 26 June 1945**; delegates of **50** countries |
| 🔥 **Charter signed** | **26 June 1945** (**Poland** signed later, so there were **51 original members**) |
| 🔥 **Charter in force / UN founded** | **24 October 1945** → **UN Day** |
| First General Assembly | **10 January 1946**, **London** (Methodist Central Hall) |

- 🔥 **HQ: New York** (Manhattan). The land was donated by **John D. Rockefeller Jr.** Other main offices: **Geneva, Vienna, Nairobi**.
- 🔥 **Members: 193** (as of 2026). The latest member is **South Sudan (14 July 2011)**. **Switzerland** joined only in **2002**.
- **Non-member observer states: 2** — the **Holy See (Vatican)** and **Palestine** (observer state since **29 November 2012**).
- **China's seat:** the Republic of China (Taiwan) was replaced by the **People's Republic of China** on **25 October 1971** (UNGA Resolution **2758**).
- **UN flag:** white UN emblem (world map in olive branches) on a light blue background; adopted 1947.
- UN turned **80** on 24 October 2025.

## The UN Charter (জাতিসংঘ সনদ) 🔥
- 🔥 A **Preamble**, **19 chapters** and **111 articles**. It begins: "**We the peoples of the United Nations…**"
- 🔥 **Six official languages:** **English, French, Russian, Chinese, Spanish** and **Arabic** (Arabic added in **1973**). **Working languages** of the Secretariat: **English and French**.

| Article / Chapter | Subject |
|---|---|
| Article 1 | Purposes of the UN (peace and security, friendly relations, cooperation) |
| Article 2(4) | Members must not use **force** against another state's territory or independence |
| Article 23 | Composition of the Security Council |
| Article 27 | Voting in the Security Council (the **veto**) |
| **Chapter VI** | **Peaceful settlement** of disputes |
| 🔥 **Chapter VII** (Articles 39–51) | **Action** against threats to peace (sanctions, military force) |
| 🔥 **Article 51** | Right of **self-defence** |
| Chapter XIV (Articles 92–96) | International Court of Justice |
| Article 97 | Secretary-General appointed by the GA on the SC's recommendation |
| Article 99 | SG may bring any threat to peace to the SC's attention |
| Article 108 | Amending the Charter |

## Six principal organs (প্রধান অঙ্গ) 🔥
| Organ | Seat | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **General Assembly (সাধারণ পরিষদ)** | New York | **All 193** members, **one country one vote**; "important questions" need a **two-thirds** majority; regular session opens each **September** |
| 🔥 **Security Council (নিরাপত্তা পরিষদ)** | New York | **15** members: **5 permanent (P5)** + **10 non-permanent** elected for **2 years** |
| **Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC)** | New York | **54** members, **3-year** terms |
| **Trusteeship Council** | New York | Supervised trust territories; 🔥 **suspended operations on 1 November 1994** after **Palau** (the last trust territory) became independent |
| 🔥 **International Court of Justice (ICJ)** | **The Hague**, Netherlands | **15 judges**, **9-year** terms; the **only principal organ not in New York** |
| **Secretariat (সচিবালয়)** | New York | Headed by the **Secretary-General** |

### Security Council details 🔥
- 🔥 **P5 (veto powers):** **USA, UK, France, Russia, China**.
- The Council grew from **11 to 15** members in **1965**.
- A decision needs **9 votes out of 15**. On substantive matters, a **"no" vote by any P5 member is a veto**. (An abstention is not a veto.)
- The **USSR/Russia** has used the veto most often overall.
- 🔥 **China's first veto (25 August 1972)** blocked **Bangladesh's admission** to the UN.
- Security Council **decisions are binding** on all members (Article 25; enforcement under Chapter VII); **GA resolutions are only recommendations**.
- **Uniting for Peace** resolution (**377 A, 1950**): the GA can act if the SC is blocked by veto (used for "emergency special sessions").

## Bangladesh and the UN 🔥
| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Joined the UN** | **17 September 1974**, as the **136th member** |
| 🔥 **First speech in Bangla** at the UN | **Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman**, **25 September 1974**, 29th UNGA |
| **Non-permanent SC member** | **1979–80** and **2000–01** |
| **President of the UN General Assembly** | **Humayun Rasheed Choudhury**, **41st session (1986)** |
| Peacekeeping | Began **1988**; one of the **top troop contributors** (see INT-08) |

## Quick revision
- UN Day: **24 October 1945**; Charter signed **26 June 1945** at **San Francisco**.
- Charter: **111 articles, 19 chapters**; **6 official languages** (Arabic added 1973).
- **6 organs**; ICJ is at **The Hague**; Trusteeship Council suspended **1994**.
- Security Council: **15 members**, **P5** with veto, 10 elected for **2 years**; decisions need **9 votes**.
- ECOSOC: **54** members; ICJ: **15** judges.
- Members: **193**; newest **South Sudan (2011)**; observers: **Holy See, Palestine**.
- Bangladesh joined **17 September 1974** as the **136th** member; Bangabandhu's Bangla speech **25 September 1974**.
