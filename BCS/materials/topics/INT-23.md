# Borders, straits, canals; Durand, McMahon, Radcliffe lines

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask "which line separates …", "which strait joins … and …", and "which canal links …". The Durand, McMahon and Radcliffe lines are asked almost every year, along with the Suez and Panama canals and the Malacca, Hormuz and Palk straits.

## Famous boundary lines (সীমারেখা) 🔥
| Line | Between | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Durand Line** | **Afghanistan – Pakistan** | Drawn in **1893** by **Sir Mortimer Durand** (British India) and **Amir Abdur Rahman Khan** (Afghanistan); about **2,640 km**; Afghanistan has never formally accepted it |
| 🔥 **McMahon Line** | **India – China** (eastern sector, **Arunachal Pradesh** – Tibet) | Proposed by **Sir Henry McMahon** at the **Simla Convention (1914)**; **China does not accept it** |
| 🔥 **Radcliffe Line** | **India – Pakistan** (1947), including India – East Pakistan (now **Bangladesh**) | Drawn by **Sir Cyril Radcliffe**, published on **17 August 1947**; he had about five weeks and had never visited India before |
| **Line of Control (LoC)** | India – Pakistan in **Kashmir** | 1949 ceasefire line renamed by the **Simla Agreement (1972)** |
| **Line of Actual Control (LAC)** | India – China | De facto border after the **1962** war |
| 🔥 **38th Parallel** | **North Korea – South Korea** | Division in 1945; now the **Demilitarized Zone (DMZ)** after the 1953 armistice |
| **17th Parallel** | North – South **Vietnam** | Geneva Accords, **1954** (until reunification in 1976) |
| 🔥 **49th Parallel** | **USA – Canada** (western part) | Longest straight border |
| **Oder–Neisse Line** | **Germany – Poland** | Set after WWII (1945) |
| **Maginot Line** | **France** (facing Germany) | French fortifications of the 1930s |
| **Siegfried Line** | **Germany** (facing France) | German defensive line |
| **Hindenburg Line** | Germany (WWI) | German defensive line in France, 1917 |
| **Mannerheim Line** | **Finland – USSR** | Finnish defensive line |
| **Curzon Line** | **Poland – Russia/USSR** | Proposed in **1919–20** by British Foreign Secretary **Lord Curzon** |
| **Mason–Dixon Line** | Within the **USA** (Pennsylvania – Maryland) | Symbolic North–South divide |
| **Green Line** | **Israel** (1949 armistice line) / also divides **Cyprus** and Nicosia | |
| **Blue Line** | **Israel – Lebanon** | UN-drawn withdrawal line (2000) |

## Bangladesh's borders 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Neighbours | **India** (west, north, east) and **Myanmar** (south-east) |
| 🔥 Border with India | about **4,156 km** (the **5th-longest land border in the world**) |
| 🔥 Border with Myanmar | about **271 km** |
| Total land border | about **4,427 km** |
| Indian states bordering Bangladesh | **5**: **West Bengal** (longest border), **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** |
| Districts on the Myanmar border | **Cox's Bazar, Bandarban, Rangamati** |
| Coastline | about **711 km** (official figure; some books say 580 km or 716 km) |
| Enclaves (ছিটমহল) | Exchanged on **31 July – 1 August 2015** under the **Land Boundary Agreement (1974)** and its 2011 protocol: **111 Indian enclaves** to Bangladesh and **51 Bangladeshi enclaves** to India |

- Longest land border in the world: **Canada – USA** (about **8,891 km**).
- Countries with the most neighbours: **China** and **Russia** (**14** each).

## Canals (খাল) 🔥
| Canal | Country | Joins | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Suez** | **Egypt** | **Mediterranean (Port Said) – Red Sea (Suez)** | Designed by **Ferdinand de Lesseps**; opened **17 November 1869**; about **193 km**; **no locks** (sea-level); 🔥 nationalised by **Nasser** on **26 July 1956** → **Suez Crisis**; the ship *Ever Given* blocked it in March 2021 |
| 🔥 **Panama** | **Panama** | **Atlantic (Caribbean) – Pacific** | Opened **15 August 1914** (built by the USA); about **82 km**; uses **locks**; handed to Panama on **31 December 1999** (Torrijos–Carter Treaties, 1977) |
| **Kiel** | **Germany** | **North Sea – Baltic Sea** | Opened **1895**; among the world's busiest artificial waterways |
| **Corinth** | **Greece** | Gulf of Corinth – Saronic Gulf (Aegean) | Opened 1893 |
| **Grand Canal** | **China** | **Beijing – Hangzhou** | 🔥 **Longest (and oldest large) artificial canal** in the world, about 1,700–1,800 km |
| **Soo Canals** (Sault Ste. Marie) | USA – Canada | **Lake Superior – Lake Huron** | |
| **Welland** | Canada | **Lake Erie – Lake Ontario** (bypasses **Niagara Falls**) | |
| **Manchester Ship Canal** | UK | Manchester – Irish Sea | |
| **Volga–Don** | Russia | Volga – Don rivers | |

## Quick revision
- Durand Line: **Afghanistan–Pakistan (1893)**; McMahon Line: **India–China (1914)**; Radcliffe Line: **India–Pakistan/Bangladesh (1947)**.
- 38th parallel: **Koreas**; 17th: **Vietnams** (1954); 49th: **USA–Canada**; Oder–Neisse: **Germany–Poland**; Maginot: **France**.
- Bangladesh–India border **4,156 km**; Bangladesh–Myanmar **271 km**; 5 Indian states.
- Gibraltar: **Mediterranean–Atlantic**; Malacca: **Indian Ocean–Pacific**; Hormuz: **Iran–Oman**; Palk: **India–Sri Lanka**; Bab-el-Mandeb: **Red Sea–Gulf of Aden**.
- Suez: **Mediterranean–Red Sea, 1869, de Lesseps**; Panama: **Atlantic–Pacific, 1914**; Kiel: **North Sea–Baltic**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The Durand Line is the border between —
(a) India and China (b) Afghanistan and Pakistan (c) India and Pakistan (d) Iran and Afghanistan

**2.** The McMahon Line separates —
(a) India and China (b) India and Nepal (c) China and Russia (d) Pakistan and China

**3.** Which strait joins the Mediterranean Sea with the Atlantic Ocean?
(a) Bosphorus (b) Dover (c) Gibraltar (d) Hormuz

**4.** The Palk Strait lies between —
(a) India and Maldives (b) Malaysia and Indonesia (c) Iran and Oman (d) India and Sri Lanka

**5.** The Suez Canal connects the Mediterranean Sea with the —
(a) Red Sea (b) Black Sea (c) Arabian Sea (d) Caspian Sea

**6.** Which line divides North Korea and South Korea?
(a) 17th parallel (b) 49th parallel (c) 38th parallel (d) 24th parallel

**7.** The Kiel Canal is in —
(a) Greece (b) Germany (c) Netherlands (d) Denmark

**8.** The Radcliffe Line was drawn in —
(a) 1893 (b) 1914 (c) 1972 (d) 1947

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Drawn in 1893 by Mortimer Durand with Amir Abdur Rahman |
| 2 | a | Simla Convention 1914; eastern India–Tibet (China) border |
| 3 | c | Gibraltar separates Spain and Morocco |
| 4 | d | Palk Strait separates Tamil Nadu (India) and Sri Lanka |
| 5 | a | Suez links Port Said on the Mediterranean with Suez on the Red Sea |
| 6 | c | The Koreas were divided at the 38th parallel |
| 7 | b | Kiel Canal joins the North Sea and the Baltic in Germany |
| 8 | d | Cyril Radcliffe drew it at partition in 1947 |
