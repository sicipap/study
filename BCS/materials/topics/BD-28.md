# Climate & seasons of Bangladesh

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask about the type of climate, the six seasons and their Bangla months, the wettest/driest places, record temperatures, Kalbaishakhi and cyclone signals.

## Type of climate 🔥
- Bangladesh has a **tropical monsoon climate (ক্রান্তীয় মৌসুমি জলবায়ু)**: hot, humid summers, heavy summer rain and mild, dry winters.
- The **south-west monsoon (দক্ষিণ-পশ্চিম মৌসুমি বায়ু)** brings most of the rain (June–October).
- The **north-east wind** blows in winter and is dry.
- Hottest month: **April**. Coldest month: **January**. Rainiest month: usually **July**.
- Average annual rainfall: exam guides give about **203 cm**; it varies from about 150 cm in the west to over 400 cm in the north-east.

## Six seasons (ষড়ঋতু) 🔥🔥
| Season | Bangla months | Approx. English months |
|---|---|---|
| **গ্রীষ্ম (Summer)** | **বৈশাখ – জ্যৈষ্ঠ** | mid-April – mid-June |
| **বর্ষা (Rainy)** | **আষাঢ় – শ্রাবণ** | mid-June – mid-August |
| **শরৎ (Autumn)** | **ভাদ্র – আশ্বিন** | mid-August – mid-October |
| **হেমন্ত (Late autumn)** | **কার্তিক – অগ্রহায়ণ** | mid-October – mid-December |
| **শীত (Winter)** | **পৌষ – মাঘ** | mid-December – mid-February |
| **বসন্ত (Spring)** | **ফাল্গুন – চৈত্র** | mid-February – mid-April |

- **Memory hook:** গ্রী–ব–শ–হে–শী–ব (each season = 2 months).
- **Revised Bangla calendar** (Bangla Academy): **1 Baishakh = 14 April** every year.
- **Climatically** (Meteorological Department) there are **3 seasons**: **summer (March–May)**, **rainy season (June–October)**, **winter (November–February)**.

## Extremes and records 🔥
| Fact | Answer |
|---|---|
| Place with the **highest rainfall** | **লালাখাল, Jaintiapur (Sylhet)**; Sylhet region is the wettest |
| Place with the **lowest rainfall** | **লালপুর (Natore)** |
| **Lowest recorded temperature** | **2.8 °C at Srimangal (Moulvibazar), 4 January 1968** |
| **Highest recorded temperature** | **45.1 °C at Rajshahi, 30 May 1972** |
| Hottest region | North-west (Rajshahi, Chuadanga, Jashore belt) |
| Coldest region | North (Panchagarh–Tetulia) and Srimangal |

> 🔄 Update: new temperature records are sometimes set (heatwaves of 2023–2024, cold waves in Tetulia); check the latest Meteorological Department report.

## Storms and disasters 🔥
- **কালবৈশাখী (Nor'wester):** violent thunderstorm in **March–May**, coming from the **north-west**.
- **Tornado:** the **Saturia–Manikganj tornado (26 April 1989)** is regarded as the deadliest tornado in world history.
- **Cyclone season:** April–May and October–November.

| Cyclone | Date | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Bhola cyclone** | **12 November 1970** | Deadliest; about **5 lakh** deaths |
| **1991 cyclone** | **29 April 1991** | Chattogram coast; about 1.4 lakh deaths |
| **সিডর (Sidr)** | **15 November 2007** | South-west coast, Sundarbans |
| **আইলা (Aila)** | **25 May 2009** | Khulna–Satkhira |
| **আম্ফান (Amphan)** | **20 May 2020** | Super cyclone |
| **মোখা (Mocha)** | **May 2023** | Cox's Bazar–Myanmar coast |
| **রেমাল (Remal)** | **26 May 2024** | Khulna–Barishal coast |

- Cyclone names come from a list of the **WMO/ESCAP Panel on Tropical Cyclones**; each member country proposes names.

### Cyclone warning signals (sea ports)
| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Distant cautionary / distant warning (দূরবর্তী সতর্ক সংকেত) |
| 3 | Local cautionary |
| 4 | **Local warning (স্থানীয় হুঁশিয়ারি সংকেত)** |
| 5–7 | **Danger (বিপদ সংকেত)** |
| 8–10 | **Great danger (মহাবিপদ সংকেত)** |
| 11 | **Communication failure (যোগাযোগ বিচ্ছিন্ন)** |

### Floods
- Major floods: **1988**, **1998** (longest-lasting), 2004, 2007, Sylhet 2022, and the **August 2024** eastern flood (Feni, Noakhali, Cumilla).
- **Flash floods (আকস্মিক বন্যা)** hit the haor region in April–May.

## Institutions
- **Bangladesh Meteorological Department (আবহাওয়া অধিদপ্তর)**: Agargaon, Dhaka; runs the Storm Warning Centre.
- **SPARRSO** (Space Research and Remote Sensing Organization): satellite weather images.
- **Flood Forecasting and Warning Centre (FFWC)**: under the Bangladesh Water Development Board.

## Quick revision
- Climate: **tropical monsoon**.
- 6 seasons × 2 months; **শরৎ = ভাদ্র–আশ্বিন**, **হেমন্ত = কার্তিক–অগ্রহায়ণ**.
- 3 climatic seasons: summer, rainy, winter.
- Highest rainfall **Lalakhal (Sylhet)**; lowest **Lalpur (Natore)**.
- Record low **2.8 °C Srimangal (1968)**; record high **45.1 °C Rajshahi (1972)**.
- Kalbaishakhi comes from the **north-west** in **March–May**.
- Signal **8–10 = great danger**; **11 = communication cut**.
- Deadliest cyclone: **12 November 1970**.
