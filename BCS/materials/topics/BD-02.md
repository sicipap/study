# Pala & Sena dynasties

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask who founded the Palas, who built Paharpur, the longest-ruling dynasty, Atish Dipankar, the Kaivarta revolt, Ballal Sena's kulinism and Lakshman Sena's fall to Bakhtiyar Khalji.

## Pala dynasty (পাল বংশ, c. 750 – 12th century) 🔥
- Founded by **গোপাল**, who was **elected (নির্বাচিত)** by the local chiefs around **750 CE** to end **মাৎস্যন্যায়**. This is often cited as the first example of an elected king in Bengal.
- The Palas ruled for about **400 years**: the **longest-ruling dynasty** in the history of Bengal.
- Religion: **Buddhism (বৌদ্ধ)**. They patronised Buddhist monasteries and learning.
- The oldest specimen of Bangla literature, the **চর্যাপদ**, was composed in the Pala period.

| King | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **গোপাল** | Founder; elected king (c. 750) |
| 🔥 **ধর্মপাল** (c. 781–821) | Greatest Pala king in power; built **সোমপুর মহাবিহার (Paharpur, Naogaon)** and **বিক্রমশীলা মহাবিহার** (Bihar); fought the **ত্রিপক্ষীয় সংগ্রাম** for Kanauj |
| **দেবপাল** | Empire at its widest; patron of **Nalanda** |
| **প্রথম মহীপাল** | Revived the empire; called the **"second founder" of the Pala dynasty** |
| **দ্বিতীয় মহীপাল** | Faced the **Kaivarta revolt (কৈবর্ত বিদ্রোহ)**; killed by rebels |
| **রামপাল** | Recovered Varendra from the Kaivartas; his life is told in **সন্ধ্যাকর নন্দীর "রামচরিত"** |
| **মদনপাল** | Commonly named as the last notable Pala king |

### Tripartite struggle (ত্রিপক্ষীয় সংগ্রাম)
A long three-way war for control of **Kanauj** among the **Palas (Bengal)**, the **Gurjara-Pratiharas** and the **Rashtrakutas**.

### Kaivarta revolt (কৈবর্ত বিদ্রোহ) 🔥
- Led by **দিব্য (দিব্যোক)**, a Kaivarta chief, against Mahipala II. The rebels captured **Varendra**.
- It is often called the **first successful rebellion / peasant revolt** in Bengal's history.
- **দিব্যক জয়স্তম্ভ** (Dibar Dighi, Naogaon) is linked to it.

### Somapura Mahavihara (Paharpur) 🔥
- In **Naogaon** district; built by **Dharmapala**.
- One of the largest Buddhist monasteries south of the Himalayas.
- **UNESCO World Heritage Site (1985)**.

## Atish Dipankar (অতীশ দীপঙ্কর) 🔥
- Born **982 CE** at **বজ্রযোগিনী, বিক্রমপুর (Munshiganj)**.
- Great Buddhist scholar; head (অধ্যক্ষ) of **Vikramashila** monastery.
- Went to **Tibet** (1042) on invitation and died there (1053).

## Other dynasties of the period (south-east Bengal)
| Dynasty | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **দেব বংশ** | **শালবন বিহার (Mainamati, Cumilla)** was built by **শ্রীভবদেব** of this dynasty |
| **চন্দ্র বংশ** | Ruled Vanga-Samatata in the 10th–11th century; best-known king **শ্রীচন্দ্র**; capital **বিক্রমপুর** |
| **বর্মন বংশ** | Ruled East Bengal after the Chandras; capital Vikrampur |

## Sena dynasty (সেন বংশ) 🔥
- The Senas came from **কর্ণাট (Karnataka, South India)**. They were **Hindu (Brahmanical)** and patrons of **Sanskrit**.
- Founder: **সামন্ত সেন**. The first independent Sena king who built the kingdom was **বিজয় সেন** (he defeated the last Palas).
- Capitals: **বিজয়পুর**, later **বিক্রমপুর**; Lakshman Sena lived at **নদীয়া (নুদিয়া)**.

| King | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **সামন্ত সেন** | Founder of the line |
| **বিজয় সেন** | Real founder of Sena rule in Bengal |
| 🔥 **বল্লাল সেন** | Introduced **কৌলীন্য প্রথা (kulinism)**; wrote **"দানসাগর"** and started **"অদ্ভুতসাগর"** |
| 🔥 **লক্ষ্মণ সেন** | Last powerful Sena king; completed "অদ্ভুতসাগর"; fled when **বখতিয়ার খলজি** attacked Nadia (1204) |

### Lakshman Sena's court poets (পঞ্চরত্ন)
- **জয়দেব** — **"গীতগোবিন্দ"**
- **ধোয়ী** — **"পবনদূত"**
- **হলায়ুধ** — **"ব্রাহ্মণসর্বস্ব"**
- Also **উমাপতি ধর** and **শরণ**.

### End of the Senas
- In **1204** (some sources: 1203 or 1205) **ইখতিয়ার উদ্দিন মুহম্মদ বিন বখতিয়ার খলজি** attacked **Nadia** with about **17–18 horsemen**; Lakshman Sena fled to **Vikrampur**. This began Muslim rule in Bengal (see BD-03).
- Sena rule continued in East Bengal (Vikrampur) for some decades under his sons.

## Pala vs Sena
| | Pala | Sena |
|---|---|---|
| Religion | Buddhist | Hindu (Brahmanical) |
| Origin | Local (Bengal) | Karnataka (South India) |
| Duration | ~400 years | ~100+ years |
| Famous for | Paharpur, Vikramashila, Charyapada | Kulinism, Sanskrit literature |

## Quick revision
- Pala founder **Gopala**, elected c. **750**; dynasty ruled ~**400 years** (longest).
- **Dharmapala** built **Paharpur (Somapura) Vihara** and **Vikramashila**.
- **Mahipala I** = second founder of the Palas.
- **Kaivarta revolt** leader **Divya (Divyok)**; Ramacharita by **Sandhyakar Nandi**.
- **Atish Dipankar**: born 982, Bajrayogini, Munshiganj; died in Tibet.
- **Shalban Vihara** (Mainamati) built by **Bhavadeva** of the Deva dynasty.
- Senas came from **Karnataka**; **Ballal Sena** introduced **kulinism**.
- **Jayadeva** wrote **Gitagovinda** in Lakshman Sena's court.
- **Bakhtiyar Khalji** took Nadia in **1204**.
