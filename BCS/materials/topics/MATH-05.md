# Percentage

> **Why it matters:** Percentage (শতকরা) is the base of profit-loss, interest and data questions. BCS and bank papers regularly ask "more than / less than", price rise and consumption, successive change, exam pass-mark and election questions.

## Key concepts
- **Percent** means "per hundred": x% = x/100.
- x% of y = y% of x (e.g. 8% of 50 = 50% of 8 = 4).
- To convert a fraction to % multiply by 100: 3/8 = 37.5%.
- Useful fraction–percent pairs: 1/2 = 50%, 1/3 = 33⅓%, 1/4 = 25%, 1/5 = 20%, 1/6 = 16⅔%, 1/8 = 12½%, 1/9 = 11 1/9%, 1/10 = 10%.
- **Percentage change** = (change ÷ original) × 100. Always divide by the **original** value.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Situation | Formula |
|---|---|
| A is r% more than B → B is less than A by | r/(100 + r) × 100 % |
| A is r% less than B → B is more than A by | r/(100 − r) × 100 % |
| 🔥 Price rises r%; cut in consumption to keep spending the same | r/(100 + r) × 100 % |
| Price falls r%; increase in consumption to keep spending the same | r/(100 − r) × 100 % |
| 🔥 Successive changes a% and b% (use − for decrease) | a + b + ab/100 % |
| Increase r% then decrease r% | net **decrease** of r²/100 % |
| Population after n years (growth r%) | P(1 + r/100)ⁿ |
| Population n years ago | P ÷ (1 + r/100)ⁿ |
| Area when length +a% and breadth +b% | a + b + ab/100 % (successive change) |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** A's salary is 25% more than B's. By what percent is B's salary less than A's?
Steps: 25/(100 + 25) × 100 = 20%.
**Answer: 20%**

**Example 2.** The price of rice rises by 25%. By what percent must a family reduce consumption so that spending stays the same?
Steps: 25/125 × 100 = 20%.
**Answer: 20%**

**Example 3.** A price is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. What is the net change?
Steps: 20 − 20 + (20 × −20)/100 = −4.
**Answer: 4% decrease**

**Example 4.** A student needs 40% to pass. He gets 180 marks and fails by 20 marks. Find the full marks.
Steps: Pass mark = 180 + 20 = 200 = 40% of total → total = 200 ÷ 0.40 = 500.
**Answer: 500**

**Example 5.** A town has 10,000 people, growing 10% a year. What is the population after 2 years?
Steps: 10000 × 1.1 × 1.1 = 12,100.
**Answer: 12,100**

**Example 6.** In an election between two candidates, the winner gets 60% of the votes and wins by 300 votes. Find the total votes.
Steps: Difference = 60% − 40% = 20% of total = 300 → total = 1500.
**Answer: 1500**

## Common traps
- "25% more" and "25% less" are not symmetric: if A is 25% more than B, B is only 20% less than A.
- +20% then −20% is **not** zero change; it is a 4% loss.
- Percentage change uses the **old** value as the base.
- In two-candidate elections, the winning margin is (winner% − loser%) of total valid votes.

## Quick revision
- x% of y = y% of x.
- r% more ⇒ other is r/(100 + r) × 100 % less.
- Price up r% ⇒ cut consumption by r/(100 + r) × 100 %.
- Successive a%, b% ⇒ a + b + ab/100.
- Up r% then down r% ⇒ loss of r²/100 %.
- Population: P(1 + r/100)ⁿ.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** What is 25% of 30% of 400?
(a) 30 (b) 120 (c) 75 (d) 40

**2.** 0.5 is what percent of 5?
(a) 1% (b) 10% (c) 5% (d) 50%

**3.** A's income is 20% less than B's. By what percent is B's income more than A's?
(a) 20% (b) 16⅔% (c) 25% (d) 30%

**4.** The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percent should consumption be reduced to keep expenditure unchanged?
(a) 25% (b) 15% (c) 22% (d) 20%

**5.** A number is first increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. The net result is:
(a) 1% decrease (b) no change (c) 1% increase (d) 2% decrease

**6.** A candidate who gets 30% marks fails by 50 marks. Another who gets 45% gets 25 marks more than the pass mark. What is the pass mark?
(a) 150 (b) 200 (c) 225 (d) 175

**7.** A town's population of 20,000 grows 5% every year. What will it be after 2 years?
(a) 22,000 (b) 22,500 (c) 22,050 (d) 21,050

**8.** If 30% of A = 40% of B, then A : B = ?
(a) 3 : 4 (b) 4 : 3 (c) 7 : 3 (d) 3 : 7

**9.** In a two-candidate election, the winner got 60% of the votes and won by 300 votes. Total votes cast were:
(a) 1200 (b) 900 (c) 1800 (d) 1500

**10.** The length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and the breadth decreased by 10%. The area:
(a) increases 10% (b) increases 8% (c) increases 12% (d) decreases 8%

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a | 30% of 400 = 120; 25% of 120 = 30 |
| 2 | b | (0.5 ÷ 5) × 100 = 10% |
| 3 | c | 20/(100 − 20) × 100 = 25% |
| 4 | d | 25/125 × 100 = 20% |
| 5 | a | 10 − 10 − 100/100 = −1% |
| 6 | b | 15% of total = 75 → total 500; pass = 150 + 50 = 200 |
| 7 | c | 20000 × 1.05² = 22,050 |
| 8 | b | 0.3A = 0.4B → A/B = 4/3 |
| 9 | d | 20% of total = 300 → 1500 |
| 10 | b | 20 − 10 + (20 × −10)/100 = +8% |
