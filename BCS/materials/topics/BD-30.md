# Agriculture: crops, varieties, research institutes

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask about rice seasons, crop varieties (zinc rice, salt-tolerant rice), the jute genome, tea, fisheries and the location of research institutes.

## Agriculture at a glance 🔥
- Agriculture (crops, livestock, fisheries, forestry) is about **11–12% of GDP** but employs about **45% of the workforce** (Labour Force Survey 2022).
- **Main crop:** **rice (ধান)**. Bangladesh is commonly cited as the **3rd largest rice producer** (after China and India).
- **Jute:** **2nd largest producer** (after India) and the **largest exporter of raw jute**. Jute is called the **"সোনালি আঁশ" (golden fibre)**.
- **Hilsa:** Bangladesh produces the largest share of the world's hilsa (ইলিশ).
- **National Agriculture Day (জাতীয় কৃষি দিবস / নবান্ন):** **1 Agrahayan**. **National Jute Day:** **6 March**. **National Tea Day:** **4 June**.

## Crop seasons (ফসলের মৌসুম) 🔥
| Season | Period | Main crops |
|---|---|---|
| **রবি (Rabi)** | Kartik–Falgun (about Oct–Mar), dry winter | **Boro rice**, wheat, potato, mustard, lentil, onion |
| **খরিফ-১ (Kharif-1)** | Chaitra–Jaistha (about Mar–Jun) | **Aus rice**, jute |
| **খরিফ-২ (Kharif-2)** | Asharh–Ashwin (about Jun–Oct) | **Aman rice** |

- Three rice crops: **আউশ, আমন, বোরো**. **Boro gives the largest production** today; Aman covers a large area and is the traditional main crop.
- Wheat grows mostly in the north (Dinajpur, Rangpur, Rajshahi).

## Varieties and breakthroughs 🔥🔥
| Variety / event | Fact |
|---|---|
| **BRRI dhan62** | **World's first zinc-enriched rice** (released **2013**) |
| **BRRI dhan47, BINA dhan-8, BINA dhan-10** | **Salt-tolerant** rice for the coast |
| **BRRI dhan51, BRRI dhan52** | **Submergence (flood)-tolerant** rice |
| **BRRI dhan56, BRRI dhan57** | **Drought-tolerant** rice |
| **BR1 (চান্দিনা)** | One of BRRI's first varieties (1970) |
| **Jute genome** | Tossa jute genome decoded in **2010** by a team led by **Dr Maqsudul Alam**; the jute-attacking fungus genome in **2012**; white (deshi) jute in **2013** |
| **Bt brinjal (বিটি বেগুন)** | First GM food crop released in Bangladesh (**2013–14**), by BARI |
| **সোনালী ব্যাগ** | Biodegradable jute-polymer bag invented by **Dr Mubarak Ahmad Khan** |

- BRRI has released **more than 100** rice varieties.

## Fisheries and livestock
- National fish **ইলিশ**; it got **GI (Geographical Indication) status in 2017** — the 2nd GI product after Jamdani.
- Bangladesh ranks among the top countries in **inland open-water fish capture** and **aquaculture** (FAO reports).
- Famous breed: **Black Bengal goat**.
- Specialised banks: **Bangladesh Krishi Bank (1973)**, **Rajshahi Krishi Unnayan Bank (1987)**.

## Quick revision
- Rice seasons: **Aus (Kharif-1), Aman (Kharif-2), Boro (Rabi)**; Boro gives the most rice.
- **BRRI dhan62** = world's first zinc rice.
- Jute genome decoded **2010**, **Dr Maqsudul Alam**.
- BRRI and BARI: **Gazipur**; BINA: **Mymensingh**; BJRI: **Dhaka**; BSRI: **Ishwardi**; Tea Research: **Srimangal**.
- First tea garden: **Malnicherra, Sylhet, 1854**.
- Wheat & Maize Research Institute: **Dinajpur** (2017).
