# Transport: ports, airports, railways

> **Why it matters:** The number and location of seaports, international airports, Biman's founding, the first railway line and cross-border trains are common one-line MCQs.

## Seaports (সমুদ্রবন্দর) 🔥🔥
| Port | Location | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **চট্টগ্রাম বন্দর (Chattogram Port)** | Karnaphuli river mouth, Chattogram | **Largest and busiest** port; handles over **90%** of the country's seaborne trade; Port Commissioners Act **1887**; run by **Chittagong Port Authority** |
| **মোংলা বন্দর (Mongla Port)** | **Bagerhat**, on the **Pasur** river | **2nd** seaport; started in **1950** at **Chalna** (moved to Mongla in 1954) |
| **পায়রা বন্দর (Payra Port)** | **Kalapara, Patuakhali**, on the Rabnabad channel | **3rd** seaport; operations began **13 August 2016** |
| **মাতারবাড়ী (Matarbari)** | Moheshkhali, Cox's Bazar | **First deep-sea port**, under construction (Japan) |

- Chattogram port terminals include the **New Mooring Container Terminal (NCT)** and the newer **Patenga Container Terminal**; the **Bay Terminal** is planned.

## Land ports and river ports
- **Bangladesh Land Port Authority:** **2001**.
- **Largest land port:** **বেনাপোল (Jashore)** — the main route to Kolkata (Petrapole on the Indian side).
- Other land ports: **Akhaura** (Brahmanbaria, to Agartala), **Burimari** (Lalmonirhat), **Hili** (Dinajpur), **Sonamasjid** (Chapainawabganj), **Tamabil** (Sylhet), **Bhomra** (Satkhira), **Teknaf** (Cox's Bazar, to Myanmar).
- **River ports:** Dhaka **Sadarghat** is the largest river port; managed by **BIWTA** (1958). **BIWTC** (1972) runs state water transport.

## Airports (বিমানবন্দর) 🔥🔥
- **Civil Aviation Authority of Bangladesh (CAAB)** manages airports.
- **International airports: 3**

| Airport | City | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **হযরত শাহজালাল আন্তর্জাতিক বিমানবন্দর** | Dhaka (Kurmitola) | Largest; named **Zia International** until **2010**; third terminal soft-opened **7 October 2023** |
| **শাহ আমানত আন্তর্জাতিক বিমানবন্দর** | Chattogram (Patenga) | |
| **ওসমানী আন্তর্জাতিক বিমানবন্দর** | Sylhet | |

- **Cox's Bazar Airport** is being upgraded to international standard with a runway extending into the sea.
- Domestic airports: **Jashore, Saidpur (Nilphamari), Rajshahi (Shah Makhdum), Barishal, Cox's Bazar**.
- The old **Tejgaon airport** is now used by the Air Force.

### Biman Bangladesh Airlines (বিমান) 🔥
- Founded on **4 January 1972**.
- **First domestic flight:** **4 February 1972**, Dhaka–Chattogram (a DC-3).
- **First international flight:** **4 March 1972**, **Dhaka–London**.
- Logo: **বলাকা** (a white stork/crane in flight).
- Slogan: **"আকাশে শান্তির নীড়"**.

## Railways (রেলপথ) 🔥🔥
- **First railway line** in present-day Bangladesh: **দর্শনা – জগতি (Kushtia)**, opened **15 November 1862**, about **53 km**.
- **Ministry of Railways** separated from the Communications Ministry on **4 December 2011**.
- Two zones: **East zone** (mainly **metre gauge**, HQ **Chattogram**) and **West zone** (mainly **broad gauge**, HQ **Rajshahi**).
- Gauges: **broad (1,676 mm)**, **metre (1,000 mm)**, and **dual gauge**.
- Total route: about **3,000+ km**.
- **Largest station:** **Kamalapur, Dhaka** (opened 1968–69).

### Cross-border trains
| Train | Route | Started |
|---|---|---|
| **মৈত্রী এক্সপ্রেস** | **Dhaka – Kolkata** | **14 April 2008** |
| **বন্ধন এক্সপ্রেস** | **Khulna – Kolkata** | **2017** |
| **মিতালী এক্সপ্রেস** | **Dhaka – New Jalpaiguri** | **2022** |

- New lines: **Dohazari–Cox's Bazar** (2023), **Padma Rail Link** (2023–24), **Jamuna Railway Bridge** (2025), **Akhaura–Agartala** link (2023).

## Roads (সড়কপথ)
- **Roads and Highways Department (RHD)**; **BRTA** (licences, 1987); **BRTC** (state buses).
- National highway **N1**: Dhaka – Chattogram – Teknaf.
- **First expressway:** **Dhaka–Mawa–Bhanga** (2020).
- Bangladesh joined the **Asian Highway** network in **2009** (AH1, AH2, AH41 pass through).

## Quick revision
- Seaports: **Chattogram (1st), Mongla (2nd), Payra (3rd)**; Matarbari = first deep-sea port.
- Largest land port: **Benapole**.
- **3** international airports: Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet.
- Biman founded **4 January 1972**; logo **Balaka**.
- First railway: **Darshana–Jagati, 15 Nov 1862**.
- Railway Ministry: **4 Dec 2011**.
- Maitree Express **2008**, Bandhan **2017**, Mitali **2022**.
