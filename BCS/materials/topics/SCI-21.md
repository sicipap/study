# Genetics, DNA, biotechnology

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the father of genetics, DNA structure and its discoverers, human chromosome number and sex determination, genetic disorders, and biotechnology milestones (Dolly, Bt brinjal, jute genome, DNA fingerprinting, CRISPR).

## Mendel and heredity (বংশগতি) 🔥
- **Gregor Johann Mendel** (Austrian monk) is the **father of genetics**. He experimented on **pea plants (মটরশুঁটি)** and published in **1866**; his work was rediscovered in **1900** (de Vries, Correns, Tschermak).
- **Laws**: (1) **Law of segregation**, (2) **Law of independent assortment**. Dominant and recessive traits.
- **Monohybrid cross (F₂)**: phenotype ratio **3 : 1**, genotype ratio **1 : 2 : 1**. **Dihybrid cross (F₂)**: **9 : 3 : 3 : 1**.
- Term **"genetics"**: **William Bateson (1905)**. Term **"gene"**: **Wilhelm Johannsen (1909)**. **Mutation** theory: **Hugo de Vries**. Chromosome theory of inheritance: Sutton & Boveri; **T. H. Morgan** (fruit fly) showed genes lie on chromosomes.

## Chromosomes and sex determination 🔥
- **Humans have 46 chromosomes (23 pairs)**: **22 pairs autosomes + 1 pair sex chromosomes**. Gametes (sperm/egg) have 23.
- **Female = XX; male = XY**. The mother always gives X; the **father's sperm (X or Y) decides the sex of the child**.
- A **gene** is a segment of DNA on a chromosome that codes for a trait/protein.

| Genetic disorder | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Down syndrome** | **Extra chromosome 21 (trisomy 21)** → 47 chromosomes |
| **Turner syndrome** | Female with only one X (**XO**, 45) |
| **Klinefelter syndrome** | Male with **XXY** (47) |
| **Haemophilia** (blood doesn't clot) | **X-linked recessive** → mostly in **males** |
| **Colour blindness** (red-green) | **X-linked recessive** → mostly in males |
| **Thalassaemia** | Inherited haemoglobin disorder (autosomal recessive); **common in Bangladesh**; needs regular blood transfusion |
| Sickle-cell anaemia | Abnormal haemoglobin; sickle-shaped RBCs |

## DNA and RNA 🔥🔥
- DNA (**deoxyribonucleic acid**) is the **hereditary material**. It was first isolated as "nuclein" by **Friedrich Miescher (1869)**.
- **Double-helix model: James Watson & Francis Crick (1953)**, using X-ray images by **Rosalind Franklin** and Maurice Wilkins. Watson, Crick and Wilkins got the **Nobel Prize in 1962**.
- A **nucleotide** = sugar + phosphate + nitrogen base.

| | DNA | RNA |
|---|---|---|
| Sugar | **Deoxyribose** | **Ribose** |
| Bases | **A, T, G, C** | **A, U, G, C** (**uracil instead of thymine**) |
| Strands | **Double** | Usually single |
| Location | Mainly **nucleus** (also mitochondria, chloroplast) | Nucleus and cytoplasm |

- **Base pairing: A = T (2 hydrogen bonds), G ≡ C (3 hydrogen bonds)**.
- Flow of information ("central dogma"): **DNA → RNA → protein** (replication, transcription, translation).
- **Human Genome Project (1990–2003)**: human DNA has ≈ **3 billion base pairs** and about **20,000–25,000 genes**.
- Some viruses (e.g. HIV, SARS-CoV-2) have **RNA** as their genetic material.

## Evolution
- **Charles Darwin**: ***On the Origin of Species* (1859)** — **natural selection** ("survival of the fittest", phrase by Herbert Spencer). Voyage on HMS *Beagle*; Galápagos finches.
- **Lamarck**: **inheritance of acquired characters** (use and disuse; giraffe's neck example).

## Biotechnology (জৈবপ্রযুক্তি) 🔥🔥
- Term "biotechnology": **Karl Ereky (1919)**. **Genetic engineering / recombinant DNA** began in **1972–73** (**Paul Berg**; **Cohen & Boyer**).
- Tools: **restriction enzyme = "molecular scissors"**; **DNA ligase = "molecular glue"**; **plasmid** (circular bacterial DNA) as a **vector**; ***E. coli*** as the common host.

| Milestone | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Human insulin from GM bacteria** | First recombinant drug, marketed **1982** (Humulin) |
| **Dolly the sheep** — first mammal **cloned** from an adult cell | Born **1996** (announced 1997), **Ian Wilmut**, Roslin Institute, **Scotland** |
| **DNA fingerprinting** | **Alec Jeffreys (1984)**; used in **crime investigation and paternity tests** |
| **PCR** (copies DNA millions of times) | **Kary Mullis (1983)** |
| **CRISPR-Cas9** gene editing | **Jennifer Doudna & Emmanuelle Charpentier** — Nobel Chemistry **2020** |
| **First test-tube (IVF) baby** | **Louise Brown, 1978, UK** (Robert Edwards) |
| **Bt crops** | Carry a gene from the bacterium ***Bacillus thuringiensis*** → make their own insecticide (Bt cotton, **Bt brinjal**) |
| **Golden Rice** | Rice engineered to make **β-carotene (provitamin A)** |

## Bangladesh in biotechnology 🔥
- **Bt brinjal (বিটি বেগুন)**: approved in **2013** and released to farmers in **2014** — the **first GM food crop in South Asia**.
- **Jute genome**: a team led by Bangladeshi scientist **Dr. Maqsudul Alam** decoded the **tossa jute genome (announced 2010)**, and later the genome of the jute-attacking fungus ***Macrophomina phaseolina*** (2012) and white jute. He had earlier decoded the **papaya** genome.
- **Tissue culture** is widely used to produce disease-free plantlets (banana, potato, orchids).

## Quick revision
- **Mendel = father of genetics** (pea plant); F₂ ratios **3:1** and **9:3:3:1**.
- Humans: **46 chromosomes**; **XX female, XY male**; father determines sex.
- **Down syndrome = trisomy 21**; haemophilia & colour blindness are **X-linked**.
- **Watson & Crick (1953)**: double helix; Rosalind Franklin's X-ray photo.
- **DNA has thymine; RNA has uracil**; A–T, G–C.
- **Dolly (1996)**: first cloned mammal; **DNA fingerprinting**: Alec Jeffreys.
- **Restriction enzyme = molecular scissors**; plasmid = vector.
- **Bt brinjal (2013)**; **jute genome**: Maqsudul Alam (2010).
