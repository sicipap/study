# Average

> **Why it matters:** Average (গড়) questions are quick and common in bank, primary and BCS papers: average of natural/odd/even numbers, a teacher joining a class, replacing a person, and cricket batting averages.

## Key concepts
- **Average (গড়)** = sum of the quantities ÷ number of quantities.
- So **sum = average × number**. Almost every average problem is solved by working with **totals**.
- If every number is increased (or multiplied) by k, the average also increases (or is multiplied) by k.
- For numbers in an **arithmetic sequence** (equal gaps), average = (first + last)/2 = the middle term.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item | Formula / value |
|---|---|
| Average | Sum ÷ n |
| Average of first n natural numbers | (n + 1)/2 |
| Average of first n odd numbers | n |
| Average of first n even numbers | n + 1 |
| Average of consecutive numbers | (first + last)/2 |
| Average of squares of first n natural numbers | (n + 1)(2n + 1)/6 |
| 🔥 New person joins; average changes | New person = new avg × (n + 1) − old avg × n |
| 🔥 One of n people replaced; average rises by d | New person = old person + n × d |
| One number removed | Removed = old total − new total |
| Runs needed in next innings for new average | New avg × (innings + 1) − old total |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Find the average of the first 50 natural numbers.
Steps: (50 + 1)/2 = 25.5.
**Answer: 25.5**

**Example 2.** The average of 5 numbers is 20. When one number is removed, the average becomes 18. Find the removed number.
Steps: Old total = 100; new total = 4 × 18 = 72. Removed = 28.
**Answer: 28**

**Example 3.** The average age of 30 students is 15 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average becomes 16. Find the teacher's age.
Steps: 16 × 31 − 15 × 30 = 496 − 450 = 46.
**Answer: 46 years**

**Example 4.** The average weight of 8 men increases by 2 kg when a man weighing 40 kg is replaced by a new man. Find the new man's weight.
Steps: 40 + 8 × 2 = 56.
**Answer: 56 kg**

**Example 5.** A batsman's average in 10 innings is 40. How many runs must he score in the 11th innings to make his average 42?
Steps: 42 × 11 − 40 × 10 = 462 − 400 = 62.
**Answer: 62**

## Common traps
- Averaging two averages directly when the groups are of different sizes. Always use totals.
- When the teacher joins a class of 30, divide by **31**.
- Average of first n **even** numbers is n + 1 (not n); of first n **odd** numbers is n.
- For "average of 11 numbers; first 6 and last 6" questions, the 6th number is counted twice.

## Quick revision
- Sum = average × number.
- First n natural: (n + 1)/2; odd: n; even: n + 1.
- Replacement: new = old + n × change.
- Joining: new = new avg × (n + 1) − old total.
- Equal-gap numbers: average = middle term.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The average of the first 10 natural numbers is:
(a) 5 (b) 5.5 (c) 6 (d) 10

**2.** The average of the first 20 even numbers is:
(a) 20 (b) 22 (c) 21 (d) 10.5

**3.** The average of 6 numbers is 30. The average of the first 4 is 25. The average of the last two is:
(a) 40 (b) 35 (c) 45 (d) 80

**4.** The average of 11 numbers is 50. The average of the first 6 is 49 and of the last 6 is 52. The 6th number is:
(a) 50 (b) 52 (c) 54 (d) 56

**5.** The average weight of 8 people increases by 2.5 kg when a person weighing 65 kg is replaced by a new person. The new person weighs:
(a) 85 kg (b) 75 kg (c) 67.5 kg (d) 80 kg

**6.** The average age of 39 students is 15 years. When the teacher is included, the average becomes 16. The teacher's age is:
(a) 40 (b) 45 (c) 55 (d) 56

**7.** A cricketer's average in 10 innings is 32. How many runs must he score in the next innings to raise his average by 4?
(a) 36 (b) 76 (c) 72 (d) 80

**8.** The average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 61. The largest of them is:
(a) 63 (b) 67 (c) 69 (d) 65

**9.** The average of three numbers is 20 and they are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3. The largest number is:
(a) 20 (b) 40 (c) 30 (d) 36

**10.** The average of 7 numbers is 21. If each number is multiplied by 3, the new average is:
(a) 63 (b) 24 (c) 21 (d) 147

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | (10 + 1)/2 = 5.5 |
| 2 | c | n + 1 = 21 |
| 3 | a | 180 − 100 = 80 → 80/2 = 40 |
| 4 | d | 294 + 312 − 550 = 56 |
| 5 | a | 65 + 8 × 2.5 = 85 |
| 6 | c | 16 × 40 − 15 × 39 = 640 − 585 = 55 |
| 7 | b | 36 × 11 − 320 = 76 |
| 8 | d | Middle term 61 → 57, 59, 61, 63, 65 |
| 9 | c | Sum 60 → 10, 20, 30 |
| 10 | a | Average also multiplied by 3 → 63 |
