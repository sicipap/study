# Work & time

> **Why it matters:** Work and time (কাজ ও সময়) is one of the most repeated arithmetic topics in BCS, bank and 9th–20th grade exams: two people working together, men-days-hours, efficiency and sharing wages.

## Key concepts
- If a person finishes a job in **n days**, his **one day's work = 1/n**.
- If one day's work is 1/n, the job takes **n days**.
- Work done together in one day = sum of individual one-day works.
- **Efficiency (দক্ষতা)** is inversely proportional to time: if A is twice as efficient as B, A takes half the time.
- **Man-days rule:** more men → fewer days (inverse); more work → more days (direct).
- Wages are shared in the ratio of **work done** (i.e. one-day works × days worked), not the ratio of days taken.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Situation | Formula |
|---|---|
| 🔥 A takes x days, B takes y days; together | **xy/(x + y)** days |
| A, B, C take x, y, z days; together | 1 ÷ (1/x + 1/y + 1/z) |
| A + B together take T days, A alone x days; B alone | xT/(x − T) days |
| 🔥 Men–days–hours–work | **M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂** |
| A is k times as efficient as B | Time ratio A : B = 1 : k |
| Wages of A and B for a joint job | Ratio 1/x : 1/y = y : x |
| Fraction done in d days together | d × (1/x + 1/y) |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** A can do a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. How long will they take together?
Steps: 10 × 15/(10 + 15) = 150/25 = 6.
**Answer: 6 days**

**Example 2.** A and B together finish a job in 12 days. A alone can do it in 20 days. How long will B alone take?
Steps: B's one day = 1/12 − 1/20 = (5 − 3)/60 = 1/30.
**Answer: 30 days**

**Example 3.** 12 men finish a job in 18 days. How many days will 8 men take?
Steps: 12 × 18 = 8 × D → D = 27.
**Answer: 27 days**

**Example 4.** A is twice as efficient as B. Together they finish a job in 14 days. How long will A alone take?
Steps: Let B's one day = x, A's = 2x. 3x = 1/14 → A's one day = 2/42 = 1/21.
**Answer: 21 days**

**Example 5.** A can do a job in 20 days and B in 30 days. They work together for 6 days, then A leaves. How long does B take to finish?
Steps: 6 days together = 6 × (1/20 + 1/30) = 6 × 1/12 = 1/2. Remaining 1/2 by B = 1/2 × 30 = 15.
**Answer: 15 days**

**Example 6.** A and B can do a job in 6 and 8 days. They earn Tk 1,400 together. What is A's share?
Steps: Work ratio 1/6 : 1/8 = 4 : 3. A = 4/7 × 1400 = 800.
**Answer: Tk 800**

## Common traps
- Adding days (10 + 15 = 25) instead of adding one-day works.
- "Twice as efficient" means **half the time**, not double.
- In men–hours problems, include **every** factor given (men, days, hours, work amount).
- Wages go in the ratio of work done, which is the **inverse** of the days each takes.

## Quick revision
- One day's work = 1/n.
- Together: xy/(x + y).
- M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂.
- Efficiency ∝ 1/time.
- Wages ∝ work done.
- "3 men = 5 women" → convert everyone to one unit first.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** A can do a job in 12 days and B in 24 days. Together they will finish it in:
(a) 18 days (b) 8 days (c) 6 days (d) 9 days

**2.** A and B together can do a job in 10 days; A alone in 15 days. B alone will take:
(a) 30 days (b) 25 days (c) 20 days (d) 5 days

**3.** 15 men can finish a job in 20 days. 25 men will finish it in:
(a) 15 days (b) 10 days (c) 12 days (d) 33 days

**4.** A, B and C can do a job in 10, 12 and 15 days respectively. Together they will take:
(a) 5 days (b) 3 days (c) 6 days (d) 4 days

**5.** A is twice as efficient as B. Together they finish a job in 12 days. B alone will take:
(a) 18 days (b) 24 days (c) 36 days (d) 30 days

**6.** 10 men working 8 hours a day finish a job in 12 days. How many days will 8 men working 6 hours a day take?
(a) 20 days (b) 16 days (c) 15 days (d) 18 days

**7.** A can do a job in 20 days and B in 30 days. They work together for 8 days, then A leaves. B finishes the rest in:
(a) 12 days (b) 10 days (c) 8 days (d) 15 days

**8.** A can do 1/3 of a job in 5 days and B can do 2/5 of it in 10 days. Together they will finish the whole job in:
(a) 9 3/8 days (b) 10 days (c) 8 days (d) 20 days

**9.** A and B can finish a job in 10 and 15 days respectively. They do it together and receive Tk 3,000. A's share is:
(a) Tk 1,200 (b) Tk 1,500 (c) Tk 2,000 (d) Tk 1,800

**10.** 3 men or 5 women can do a job in 12 days. How long will 6 men and 5 women take to do it?
(a) 6 days (b) 4 days (c) 3 days (d) 5 days

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | 12 × 24/36 = 8 |
| 2 | a | 1/10 − 1/15 = 1/30 |
| 3 | c | 15 × 20 = 25 × D → D = 12 |
| 4 | d | (6 + 5 + 4)/60 = 1/4 → 4 days |
| 5 | c | 3x = 1/12 → B's x = 1/36 |
| 6 | a | 10 × 8 × 12 = 8 × 6 × D → D = 20 |
| 7 | b | 8 × (1/20 + 1/30) = 2/3; remaining 1/3 × 30 = 10 |
| 8 | a | A 15 days, B 25 days; 15 × 25/40 = 75/8 = 9 3/8 |
| 9 | d | Ratio 1/10 : 1/15 = 3 : 2 → 3/5 × 3000 = 1800 |
| 10 | b | 5 women = 3 men → 9 men; 3 men take 12 days → 9 men take 4 |
