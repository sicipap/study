## Quick revision
- George Eliot = **Mary Ann Evans**; Mark Twain = **Samuel Clemens**; Lewis Carroll = **Charles Dodgson**.
- Boz = **Dickens**; Elia = **Charles Lamb**; O. Henry = **William Sydney Porter**.
- Father of English poetry = **Chaucer**; Bard of Avon = **Shakespeare**.
- **Shylock** → *The Merchant of Venice*; **Iago** → *Othello*; **Caliban** → *The Tempest*.
- **Heathcliff** → *Wuthering Heights*; **Mr Darcy** → *Pride and Prejudice*.
- **Sherlock Holmes** → **Conan Doyle**; **Hercule Poirot** → **Agatha Christie**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Shylock is a character in:
(a) Othello (b) The Merchant of Venice (c) The Tempest (d) Twelfth Night

**2.** The real name of George Eliot was:
(a) Mary Ann Evans (b) Charlotte Brontë (c) Mary Shelley (d) Jane Austen

**3.** Who is called the "Father of English poetry"?
(a) Shakespeare (b) Spenser (c) Milton (d) Chaucer

**4.** Heathcliff is the hero of:
(a) Jane Eyre (b) Pride and Prejudice (c) Wuthering Heights (d) Great Expectations

**5.** "Elia" was the pen name of:
(a) Charles Lamb (b) Charles Dickens (c) William Hazlitt (d) Thomas De Quincey

**6.** Sherlock Holmes was created by:
(a) Agatha Christie (b) Arthur Conan Doyle (c) R. L. Stevenson (d) Edgar Allan Poe

**7.** Iago is the villain in:
(a) Macbeth (b) Hamlet (c) King Lear (d) Othello

**8.** Mark Twain's real name was:
(a) William Sydney Porter (b) Eric Arthur Blair (c) Samuel Langhorne Clemens (d) Charles Dodgson

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | The Jewish moneylender in *The Merchant of Venice* |
| 2 | a | George Eliot = Mary Ann Evans |
| 3 | d | Chaucer, author of *The Canterbury Tales* |
| 4 | c | Emily Brontë's novel |
| 5 | a | *Essays of Elia* by Charles Lamb |
| 6 | b | Conan Doyle's detective |
| 7 | d | Iago deceives Othello |
| 8 | c | Mark Twain = Samuel Langhorne Clemens |
