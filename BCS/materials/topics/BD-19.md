# Constitution: key articles (President, PM, Parliament, Judiciary, EC, PSC, CAG)

> **Why it matters:** Article-number questions are common in BCS, bank and PSC exams: e.g. Art. 48 (President), 56 (ministers), 65 (Parliament), 70 (floor crossing), 93 (ordinance), 94 (Supreme Court), 102 (writ), 118 (Election Commission), 127 (CAG), 137 (PSC), 141A (emergency).

## The Executive (Part IV, Art. 48–64) 🔥
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **48** | **President** — elected by **Members of Parliament**; minimum age **35**; acts on the PM's advice except in appointing the **PM** and the **Chief Justice** |
| **49** | **Prerogative of mercy** (pardon, reprieve, remission) |
| **50** | Term of the President: **5 years**; not more than **two terms** (consecutive or not) |
| **51** | President's **immunity** |
| 🔥 **52** | **Impeachment** of the President (needs **two-thirds** of MPs) |
| **53** | Removal on ground of physical or mental incapacity |
| 🔥 **54** | **Speaker acts as President** in a vacancy or absence |
| **55** | **Cabinet**, headed by the **Prime Minister**; collectively responsible to Parliament |
| 🔥 **56** | **Ministers**; President appoints as PM the MP who commands the majority; up to **one-tenth** of ministers may be non-MPs (technocrats) |
| **57** | Tenure of the Prime Minister |
| **58** | Tenure of other ministers |
| 58B–58E | Non-party caretaker government (added 1996; **omitted in 2011**) |
| **59–60** | **Local government** |
| **61** | **Supreme command of the defence services** vests in the **President** |
| **63** | War (declared only with Parliament's assent) |
| 🔥 **64** | **Attorney-General** |

## The Legislature (Part V, Art. 65–93) 🔥
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **65** | **Parliament — "জাতীয় সংসদ" (House of the Nation)**; 300 elected seats + **50** reserved women's seats |
| **66** | Qualifications of MPs (citizen, **25 years** of age) |
| **67** | Vacation of seats |
| 🔥 **70** | **Floor crossing** — an MP loses the seat if he **resigns from his party or votes against it** in Parliament |
| **72** | Sessions — gap between two sessions **not more than 60 days**; Parliament lasts **5 years** from its first meeting |
| **73** | President's address to Parliament |
| 🔥 **74** | **Speaker and Deputy Speaker** |
| **75** | Rules of procedure; **quorum 60** members |
| **76** | **Standing committees** |
| 🔥 **77** | **Ombudsman** |
| **78** | Privileges and immunities of Parliament and MPs |
| **80** | Legislative procedure (President's assent within **15 days**) |
| **81** | **Money bills** |
| **83** | No tax except by an Act of Parliament |
| **84** | Consolidated Fund and Public Account |
| 🔥 **87** | **Annual financial statement (budget)** |
| **90** | Appropriation Act |
| 🔥 **93** | **Ordinance-making power** of the President (when Parliament is dissolved or not in session) |

## The Judiciary (Part VI, Art. 94–117) 🔥
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **94** | **Supreme Court** = **Appellate Division + High Court Division** |
| **95** | Appointment of judges by the President |
| **96** | Tenure of judges — retire at **67**; removal (Supreme Judicial Council) |
| **97** | Acting Chief Justice |
| **98** | Additional judges |
| **100** | Seat of the Supreme Court — **Dhaka** |
| **101** | Jurisdiction of the High Court Division |
| 🔥 **102** | **Writ jurisdiction** of the High Court Division |
| **103** | Jurisdiction of the Appellate Division |
| **104** | Power to do **complete justice** |
| **105** | Review of its own judgments |
| **106** | **Advisory jurisdiction** (President may seek the Supreme Court's opinion) |
| **108** | Supreme Court as a **court of record** (can punish for contempt) |
| **111** | Supreme Court's law is **binding** on all subordinate courts |
| **114–116A** | Subordinate courts; their control; independence of judicial officers |
| **117** | **Administrative tribunals** |

## Elections (Part VII, Art. 118–126) 🔥
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **118** | **Election Commission**: **Chief Election Commissioner** + not more than **4** Election Commissioners; appointed by the President; term **5 years** |
| **119** | Functions — electoral rolls, elections of **President** and **Parliament**, delimitation of constituencies |
| **121** | One electoral roll for each constituency |
| **122** | Voter qualification — citizen, **18 years** |
| **123** | Time of elections — within **90 days before** Parliament's term ends (or within 90 days after dissolution) |

## Comptroller and Auditor-General (Part VIII, Art. 127–132)
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **127** | Establishment of the office of **CAG** (appointed by the President) |
| **128** | Functions — audits public accounts |
| **129** | Term — **5 years or age 65**, whichever comes first |
| **132** | CAG's reports are laid before Parliament (examined by the **Public Accounts Committee**) |

## Services and PSC (Part IX, Art. 133–141) 🔥
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| **133–136** | Appointment, conditions and tenure of public servants |
| 🔥 **137** | Establishment of the **Public Service Commission (PSC)** |
| **138** | Appointment of the chairman and members (by the President) |
| **139** | Term — **5 years or age 65** |
| **140** | Functions — recruitment tests, advice on appointments, promotions, discipline |
| **141** | PSC's **annual report** to the President (laid before Parliament) |

## Emergency, amendment and others
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **141A** | **Proclamation of emergency** (needs PM's **prior countersignature**) |
| 141B / 141C | Suspension of certain rights / of their enforcement |
| 🔥 **142** | **Amendment** of the Constitution (two-thirds) |
| **145A** | International treaties to be laid before Parliament |
| **148** | Oaths of office (Third Schedule) |
| **152** | Interpretation (definitions) |
| **153** | Commencement, citation, authentication (Bangla text prevails) |

> 🔄 Update: post-2024 reform proposals include changes to the President's election, the Election Commission, the caretaker system, and a bicameral Parliament. The articles above are as written; check whether any change has been adopted.

## Quick revision
- President: **Art. 48**; mercy **49**; impeachment **52**; Speaker acts **54**.
- Cabinet **55**; ministers and PM **56**; supreme command **61**; Attorney-General **64**.
- Parliament **65**; floor crossing **70**; Speaker **74**; Ombudsman **77**.
- Budget **87**; ordinance **93**.
- Supreme Court **94**; writ **102**; advisory jurisdiction **106**.
- Election Commission **118**; CAG **127**; PSC **137**.
- Emergency **141A**; amendment **142**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** সংবিধানের কোন অনুচ্ছেদে সংসদ সদস্যদের দলত্যাগ (floor crossing) সম্পর্কে বলা হয়েছে?
(a) Article 65 (b) Article 70 (c) Article 74 (d) Article 77

**2.** The Ombudsman is provided for in —
(a) Article 77 (b) Article 87 (c) Article 118 (d) Article 127

**3.** The President's power to issue ordinances is in —
(a) Article 48 (b) Article 49 (c) Article 93 (d) Article 141A

**4.** The Public Service Commission is established under —
(a) Article 118 (b) Article 127 (c) Article 102 (d) Article 137

**5.** Writ jurisdiction of the High Court Division is in —
(a) Article 94 (b) Article 102 (c) Article 104 (d) Article 106

**6.** Which article provides for the Comptroller and Auditor-General?
(a) Article 127 (b) Article 118 (c) Article 137 (d) Article 64

**7.** The Election Commission is established under —
(a) Article 65 (b) Article 122 (c) Article 118 (d) Article 142

**8.** The Attorney-General is appointed under —
(a) Article 61 (b) Article 95 (c) Article 55 (d) Article 64

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Article 70: resigning from the party or voting against it vacates the seat |
| 2 | a | Article 77 provides for an Ombudsman |
| 3 | c | Article 93: ordinances when Parliament is not in session or dissolved |
| 4 | d | Article 137 establishes the PSC |
| 5 | b | Article 102: writs such as habeas corpus and mandamus |
| 6 | a | Article 127 establishes the office of the CAG |
| 7 | c | Article 118: CEC and up to 4 Election Commissioners |
| 8 | d | Article 64 provides for the Attorney-General |
