# AI, machine learning, LLMs, robotics

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the father of AI, the Turing test, types of machine learning, what GPT stands for, which company made ChatGPT/Gemini, and the Sophia robot. Recent BCS and bank papers include AI questions every year.

## Artificial Intelligence (কৃত্রিম বুদ্ধিমত্তা) 🔥
- **AI** is the ability of machines to do tasks that normally need **human intelligence**: learning, reasoning, problem-solving, understanding language, recognising images.
- 🔥 **John McCarthy** coined the term **"Artificial Intelligence"** (1955 proposal) for the **Dartmouth Conference (1956)**. He is the **father of AI** and also created the **LISP** language.
- 🔥 **Alan Turing** proposed the **Turing Test** in his 1950 paper *"Computing Machinery and Intelligence"*: if a human cannot tell a machine from a human in conversation, the machine is "intelligent".
- **Types by capability:** **Narrow/weak AI** (one task, e.g. voice assistants — all AI today), **General AI (AGI)** (human-level in all tasks, not yet achieved), **Super AI** (beyond humans, hypothetical).

### Branches of AI
| Branch | Meaning / example |
|---|---|
| **Machine learning (ML)** | Learning from data without explicit programming |
| **Deep learning** | ML with **multi-layer artificial neural networks** |
| **NLP** (Natural Language Processing) | Understanding human language: translation, chatbots |
| **Computer vision** | Understanding images/video: face recognition |
| **Expert system** | Copies a human expert's decisions using rules (**MYCIN** for medicine, DENDRAL for chemistry) |
| **Robotics** | Machines that sense and act |
| **Speech recognition** | Siri (Apple), Alexa (Amazon), Google Assistant |

## Machine learning (মেশিন লার্নিং) 🔥
- Term coined by **Arthur Samuel** (IBM) in **1959**.

| Type | How it learns | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Supervised learning** | From **labelled data** (input + correct answer) | Spam filter, loan-default prediction, image classification |
| 🔥 **Unsupervised learning** | From **unlabelled data**; finds patterns/groups (**clustering**) | Customer segmentation |
| 🔥 **Reinforcement learning** | By **trial and error with rewards/penalties** | Game-playing AI (AlphaGo), robots, self-driving |

- **Neural network:** a model inspired by the **human brain's neurons**. **Geoffrey Hinton** is called the **"godfather of deep learning/AI"**.
- **Training data** teaches the model; **test data** checks it. **Overfitting** = model memorises training data and fails on new data.

## Large language models (LLMs) and generative AI 🔥
- An **LLM** is a deep-learning model trained on **huge amounts of text** to understand and generate language. Most are built on the **Transformer** architecture.
- 🔥 **GPT = Generative Pre-trained Transformer.**
- **Generative AI** creates new content: text, images, audio, video, code.
- **Prompt:** the instruction given to an AI. **Token:** a small piece of text the model processes. **Hallucination:** when an AI confidently gives false information.

## Robotics (রোবটিক্স) 🔥
- 🔥 The word **"robot"** comes from the Czech word **"robota" (forced labour)**, first used in **Karel Čapek**'s play ***R.U.R.*** (1920).
- The word **"robotics"** was first used by **Isaac Asimov** (1941), who also gave the **Three Laws of Robotics**.
- **Joseph Engelberger** is called the **father of industrial robotics**; **Unimate** (1961, used by General Motors) was the **first industrial robot**.
- 🔥 **Sophia** is a humanoid robot made by **Hanson Robotics** (Hong Kong; David Hanson). In **2017** it became the **first robot to be given citizenship** (by **Saudi Arabia**). Sophia visited **Dhaka** in **December 2017** for the Digital World event.
- Main parts of a robot: **sensors** (input), **controller** (brain), **actuators** (motors that move), power supply.
- Uses: manufacturing (car assembly), surgery (**da Vinci surgical robot**), space exploration (Mars rovers), defence, bomb disposal, household (robot vacuum).
- **Drone (UAV — Unmanned Aerial Vehicle):** a flying robot.
