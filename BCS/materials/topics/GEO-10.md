# Environmental conventions: Ramsar, CBD, Montreal

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the year and place of each convention and what it deals with — Ramsar (wetlands, Iran, 1971), CBD (biodiversity, Rio, 1992), Montreal (ozone, 1987), CITES, Basel, Stockholm and so on. A single table answers most questions.

## Master table of conventions 🔥🔥
| Convention | Signed / adopted (place, year) | Deals with | Key extra fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Ramsar Convention** | **Ramsar, Iran, 2 February 1971** (in force 1975) | **Wetlands of international importance** | **2 February = World Wetlands Day**; oldest modern global environmental treaty |
| **World Heritage Convention** | **Paris (UNESCO), 1972** | Cultural and natural heritage | Sundarbans listed 1997 |
| 🔥 **CITES** | **Washington DC, 3 March 1973** (in force 1975) | **International trade in endangered species** of wild animals and plants | **3 March = World Wildlife Day**; secretariat Geneva |
| **CMS (Bonn Convention)** | **Bonn, Germany, 1979** | **Migratory species** of wild animals | |
| **UNCLOS** | **Montego Bay, Jamaica, 1982** | Law of the sea | EEZ 200 nautical miles |
| **Vienna Convention** | **Vienna, 1985** | Framework for **protecting the ozone layer** | |
| 🔥 **Montreal Protocol** | **Montreal, Canada, 16 September 1987** (in force 1989) | **Phasing out ozone-depleting substances (CFCs, halons)** | **16 September = Ozone Day**; universally ratified; **Kigali Amendment (2016)** phases down **HFCs** |
| 🔥 **Basel Convention** | **Basel, Switzerland, 1989** (in force 1992) | **Transboundary movement of hazardous wastes** | Stops rich countries dumping waste in poor ones |
| 🔥 **UNFCCC** | **Rio de Janeiro, 1992** (in force 1994) | **Climate change** | Yearly **COP**; secretariat Bonn |
| 🔥 **CBD (Convention on Biological Diversity)** | **Rio de Janeiro, 1992** (in force **29 December 1993**) | **Biodiversity** | Secretariat **Montreal**; **22 May = International Day for Biological Diversity** |
| **UNCCD** (Desertification) | **Paris, 1994** (in force 1996) | **Desertification and land degradation** | **17 June = Desertification and Drought Day** |
| **Kyoto Protocol** | **Kyoto, Japan, 1997** (in force 2005) | Binding greenhouse-gas cuts for developed countries | Under UNFCCC |
| **Rotterdam Convention** | **Rotterdam, 1998** | **Prior informed consent** for trade in hazardous **chemicals and pesticides** | |
| **Cartagena Protocol** | **2000** | **Biosafety** — safe handling of **living modified organisms (GMOs)** | Under CBD |
| 🔥 **Stockholm Convention** | **Stockholm, 2001** (in force 2004) | **Persistent Organic Pollutants (POPs)**, e.g. DDT, dioxins | Not to be confused with the 1972 Stockholm Conference |
| **Nagoya Protocol** | **Nagoya, Japan, 2010** | **Access and benefit-sharing** from genetic resources | Under CBD |
| **Minamata Convention** | **Kumamoto, Japan, 2013** (in force 2017) | **Mercury** | Named after Minamata disease (mercury poisoning) |
| 🔥 **Paris Agreement** | **Paris, December 2015** (in force 2016) | Climate: well below 2 °C, aim 1.5 °C | Under UNFCCC |
| **Kunming–Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework** | **Montreal, December 2022 (CBD COP15)** | New biodiversity targets to 2030 | **"30 by 30"**: protect **30% of land and 30% of oceans by 2030** |

**Memory hooks**
- **"Ramsar = Wetland, Iran, '71"**; **"Montreal = Ozone, '87"**; **"Basel = Waste, '89"**; **"Stockholm 2001 = POPs"**; **"Rotterdam = chemicals (PIC)"**; **"Minamata = Mercury"**.
- **Rio 1992 gave three "Rio conventions":** **UNFCCC, CBD** (signed there) and **UNCCD** (negotiated as a result, adopted 1994).

## CBD in detail 🔥
- Opened for signature at the **Earth Summit, Rio, 5 June 1992**.
- 🔥 **Three objectives:**
  1. **Conservation** of biological diversity
  2. **Sustainable use** of its components
  3. **Fair and equitable sharing of benefits** from genetic resources
- **Protocols:** **Cartagena (biosafety, 2000)** and **Nagoya (access and benefit-sharing, 2010)**.
- The **USA** has signed but **not ratified** the CBD.

## Ramsar Convention in detail 🔥
- Full name: **Convention on Wetlands of International Importance, especially as Waterfowl Habitat**.
- Signed at **Ramsar, Iran**, on **2 February 1971**.
- **Montreux Record:** list of Ramsar sites whose ecology is under threat.
- 🔥 **Bangladesh** joined in **1992**. **Ramsar sites of Bangladesh:** **Sundarbans (1992)** and **Tanguar Haor (2000)**.

## Major environment conferences 🔥
| Conference | Year / place | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **UN Conference on the Human Environment** | **Stockholm, June 1972** | **First global environment conference**; created **UNEP** (HQ **Nairobi**, Kenya); led to **World Environment Day (5 June)** |
| **Brundtland Commission report *Our Common Future*** | **1987** | Defined **sustainable development**: meeting present needs without harming future generations' ability to meet theirs |
| 🔥 **Earth Summit (UNCED)** | **Rio de Janeiro, June 1992** | **Agenda 21**, Rio Declaration, UNFCCC, CBD, Forest Principles |
| **World Summit on Sustainable Development (Rio+10)** | **Johannesburg, 2002** | |
| **Rio+20** | **Rio, 2012** | *The Future We Want*; started the process leading to the **SDGs (2015)** |

## Bangladesh's own environmental laws (quick list)
- **Department of Environment (DoE)** set up in **1989**.
- **Environment Policy 1992** (updated **2018**).
- 🔥 **Environment Conservation Act 1995** — basis for **Ecologically Critical Areas (ECA)** and environmental clearance.
- **Wildlife (Conservation and Security) Act 2012**; **Biodiversity Act 2017**.

## Quick revision
- Ramsar: **wetlands, Iran, 1971**; World Wetlands Day **2 February**.
- CITES: **trade in endangered species, Washington, 1973**.
- Montreal Protocol: **ozone, 1987**; Kigali Amendment **2016** (HFCs).
- Basel: **hazardous waste, 1989**; Rotterdam: **chemicals, 1998**; Stockholm: **POPs, 2001**; Minamata: **mercury, 2013**.
- CBD: **Rio 1992**, in force **1993**, three objectives; Cartagena (biosafety), Nagoya (ABS).
- Stockholm Conference **1972** → **UNEP (Nairobi)** and World Environment Day.
- Kunming–Montreal Framework (2022): **30 by 30**.
- Bangladesh Ramsar sites: **Sundarbans, Tanguar Haor**.

(a) Stockholm (b) Kyoto (c) Montreal (d) Rio de Janeiro

(a) Rotterdam Convention (b) Minamata Convention (c) Vienna Convention (d) Stockholm Convention

**7.** The headquarters of UNEP is in:
(a) Nairobi (b) Geneva (c) New York (d) Paris

**8.** The Cartagena Protocol under the CBD deals with:
(a) Mercury (b) Biosafety of living modified organisms (c) Climate finance (d) Ozone-depleting substances

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a | Wetlands of international importance |
| 2 | c | Ramsar, Iran, 1971 |
| 3 | b | Phases out CFCs and other ozone-depleting substances |
| 4 | d | Earth Summit, Rio, 1992 |
| 5 | c | Basel, 1989 — hazardous waste |
| 6 | d | Stockholm Convention, 2001 |
| 7 | a | UNEP was created in 1972 with HQ in Nairobi |
| 8 | b | Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety, 2000 |
