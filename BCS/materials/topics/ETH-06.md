# Rule of law, accountability, transparency

> **Why it matters:** These three are the pillars of good governance that exams test most. Typical questions: Dicey's three principles of rule of law, which constitutional article gives equality before law, the types and tools of accountability, and how transparency is ensured in Bangladesh.

## Rule of law (আইনের শাসন) 🔥
- **Meaning:** everyone, including the government and its officials, is **subject to the law**, and no one is above it. Power is exercised according to known laws, not the ruler's wish.
- The concept was made famous by the British jurist **A. V. Dicey** in ***Introduction to the Study of the Law of the Constitution* (1885)**.
- **Dicey's three principles:**
  1. **Supremacy of law** (absence of arbitrary power): no one can be punished except for a definite breach of law proved in an ordinary court.
  2. **Equality before law:** all persons, officials included, are subject to the same ordinary law and courts.
  3. **Rights come from the ordinary law of the land** (in Britain, from judicial decisions; the constitution is the result of those rights).

### Rule of law in the Constitution of Bangladesh 🔥
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **7** | All powers belong to the people; the **Constitution is the supreme law** |
| **22** | The State shall ensure the **separation of the judiciary from the executive** |
| **27** | **All citizens are equal before law** and are entitled to equal protection of law |
| **31** | **Right to protection of law**: to be treated only in accordance with law |
| **35** | Protection in respect of trial and punishment (no double punishment, no forced self-incrimination, speedy and public trial) |

- The judiciary was formally **separated from the executive on 1 November 2007**, following the Supreme Court's directions in the **Masdar Hossain case** (1999).

> 🔄 Update: article numbers refer to the Constitution as it stood in 2024; check for amendments made in the later reform process.

## Accountability (জবাবদিহিতা) 🔥
- **Meaning:** public officials must **answer for their actions and decisions** and face consequences for wrongdoing.
- **Types:**
| Type | To whom / how |
|---|---|
| Political | Government to Parliament and to voters (elections) |
| Administrative | Officials to their superiors, through rules and hierarchy |
| Legal / judicial | Through courts and judicial review |
| Financial | Through audit of public money |
| Social | To citizens, media and civil society |
| Horizontal / vertical | Horizontal = one state body checks another; vertical = citizens check the state |

### Institutions of accountability in Bangladesh
| Institution | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Parliament and its standing committees** (e.g. **Public Accounts Committee**) | Constitution **Article 76** |
| **Comptroller and Auditor General (মহা হিসাব-নিরীক্ষক ও নিয়ন্ত্রক)** | Constitution **Articles 127–132** |
| **Ombudsman (ন্যায়পাল)** | Constitution **Article 77** (general Ombudsman not yet appointed) |
| **Anti-Corruption Commission** | ACC Act 2004 |
| **Information Commission** | RTI Act 2009 |
| **Judiciary**, free media, civil society | — |

## Transparency (স্বচ্ছতা) 🔥
- **Meaning:** decisions and their reasons are **open and information is freely available** to those affected.
- **Transparency makes accountability possible**: people cannot hold officials to account for what they cannot see.
- **Tools in Bangladesh:**
  - **Right to Information Act 2009** and the Information Commission.
  - **Citizen Charter** (service, time and fees displayed).
  - **Public Procurement Act 2006** and **Public Procurement Rules 2008**; **e-GP (electronic government procurement)** launched in **2011**.
  - **Whistleblower protection:** জনস্বার্থ সংশ্লিষ্ট তথ্য প্রকাশ (সুরক্ষা প্রদান) আইন, **2011**.
  - Proactive disclosure on government websites and the national portal.

## How they link together
Rule of law sets the rules → transparency lets people see what is done → accountability makes officials answer for it. All three reduce **corruption** and build **public trust**.

## Quick revision
- Rule of law: A. V. Dicey (1885); three principles: supremacy of law, equality before law, rights from ordinary law.
- Article 27: equality before law; Article 31: right to protection of law.
- Article 22: separation of judiciary; implemented 1 November 2007 (Masdar Hossain case).
- Article 77: Ombudsman; Articles 127–132: Comptroller and Auditor General.
- Public Accounts Committee = financial accountability through Parliament.
- Transparency tools: RTI Act 2009, Citizen Charter, e-GP (2011), Whistleblower Act 2011.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The concept of "rule of law" was made famous by:
(a) John Austin (b) A. V. Dicey (c) Montesquieu (d) Harold Laski

**2.** Which article of the Constitution of Bangladesh says that all citizens are equal before law?
(a) Article 7 (b) Article 22 (c) Article 27 (d) Article 31

**3.** Separation of the judiciary from the executive in Bangladesh was formally put into effect on:
(a) 1 November 2007 (b) 16 December 1972 (c) 1 January 2009 (d) 26 March 2001

**4.** Which constitutional article provides for the Ombudsman in Bangladesh?
(a) Article 55 (b) Article 65 (c) Article 102 (d) Article 77

**5.** Which of the following is NOT one of Dicey's principles of rule of law?
(a) Supremacy of law (b) Rule by decree of the executive (c) Equality before law (d) Rights coming from the ordinary law of the land

**6.** The Public Accounts Committee of Parliament mainly ensures which type of accountability?
(a) Religious (b) Financial (c) Military (d) Social

**7.** Which law of Bangladesh most directly supports transparency by giving citizens access to information?
(a) Right to Information Act 2009 (b) Penal Code 1860 (c) Evidence Act 1872 (d) Code of Criminal Procedure 1898

**8.** The Comptroller and Auditor General of Bangladesh is provided for in which articles of the Constitution?
(a) Articles 94–97 (b) Articles 137–141 (c) Articles 127–132 (d) Articles 118–126

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Dicey, Introduction to the Study of the Law of the Constitution (1885). |
| 2 | c | Article 27: equality before law. |
| 3 | a | Following the Masdar Hossain case directions. |
| 4 | d | Article 77 provides for the Ombudsman. |
| 5 | b | Rule by executive decree is the opposite of rule of law. |
| 6 | b | The PAC examines accounts of public spending. |
| 7 | a | The RTI Act gives every citizen the right to information. |
| 8 | c | Articles 127–132 (Part VIII); 137–141 are the PSC, 118–126 the Election Commission. |
