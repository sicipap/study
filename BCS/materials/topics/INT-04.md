# World War I, Versailles, League of Nations

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the immediate cause of WWI (Sarajevo, 1914), the two alliances, the date of the Treaty of Versailles, Wilson's Fourteen Points, and the founding, HQ and failure of the League of Nations.

## World War I (প্রথম বিশ্বযুদ্ধ) 🔥
- Duration: **28 July 1914 – 11 November 1918** (about four years).
- 🔥 **Immediate cause:** assassination of **Archduke Franz Ferdinand**, heir to the **Austro-Hungarian** throne, and his wife Sophie at **Sarajevo (Bosnia)** on **28 June 1914**, by **Gavrilo Princip**, a Bosnian Serb linked to the "Black Hand" group.
- **Austria-Hungary declared war on Serbia on 28 July 1914**; the alliance system then pulled in the great powers.
- **Long-term causes (M-A-I-N):** **M**ilitarism, **A**lliances, **I**mperialism, **N**ationalism.

### The two camps
| Before the war | During the war |
|---|---|
| **Triple Alliance (1882):** Germany, Austria-Hungary, Italy | **Central Powers:** 🔥 **Germany, Austria-Hungary, Ottoman Empire (Turkey), Bulgaria** |
| **Triple Entente (1907):** Britain, France, Russia | **Allied Powers:** Britain, France, Russia, **Italy (switched sides, 1915)**, Japan, Serbia, **USA (from 1917)** and others |

### Key events
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1914 | **First Battle of the Marne**: German advance on Paris stopped; trench warfare began |
| 1915 | **Gallipoli campaign** (Allies vs Ottomans); sinking of the British liner **Lusitania** by a German U-boat |
| 1916 | **Verdun** and the **Somme**; 🔥 **tanks first used by Britain at the Somme**; **Battle of Jutland** (biggest naval battle) |
| 🔥 **6 April 1917** | **USA entered the war** (after unrestricted submarine warfare and the **Zimmermann Telegram**) |
| March 1918 | Russia (after the Bolshevik Revolution) left the war by the **Treaty of Brest-Litovsk** |
| 🔥 **11 November 1918** | **Armistice** signed in a railway carriage at **Compiègne**, France; Kaiser **Wilhelm II** abdicated |

- New weapons: tanks, poison gas, machine guns, submarines, aircraft.
- Four empires collapsed: **German, Austro-Hungarian, Ottoman and Russian**.
- About **9–10 million soldiers** died (total deaths including civilians are much higher; figures vary).
- **Bangladesh link:** 🔥 **Kazi Nazrul Islam** joined the **49th Bengal Regiment** (1917) and served at **Karachi** during WWI.

## Paris Peace Conference and the Treaty of Versailles 🔥
- The **Paris Peace Conference** opened in January 1919.
- **"Big Four":** **Woodrow Wilson** (USA), **David Lloyd George** (UK), **Georges Clemenceau** (France), **Vittorio Orlando** (Italy).
- 🔥 **Wilson's Fourteen Points** (8 January 1918): peace programme; the **14th point** proposed a **"general association of nations"** → the League of Nations.

### Treaty of Versailles
- 🔥 Signed **28 June 1919** in the **Hall of Mirrors**, Palace of **Versailles**, near Paris (exactly five years after the Sarajevo killing). In force 10 January 1920.
- Terms imposed on **Germany**:
  - **War Guilt Clause (Article 231):** Germany accepted responsibility for the war.
  - **Reparations:** fixed in 1921 at **132 billion gold marks**.
  - **Alsace-Lorraine** returned to **France**; the **Saar** coal area placed under the League for 15 years.
  - **Rhineland** demilitarised; army limited to **100,000** men; no air force, no submarines.
  - **Polish Corridor** given to Poland; **Danzig** became a free city under the League.
  - All German **colonies** taken and given to the victors as **League "mandates"**.
- It was called a **"dictated peace" (Diktat)**; German anger helped **Hitler's** rise and so contributed to **WWII**.

### Other peace treaties
| Treaty | With |
|---|---|
| **St. Germain** (1919) | Austria |
| **Neuilly** (1919) | Bulgaria |
| **Trianon** (1920) | Hungary |
| **Sèvres** (1920) | Ottoman Empire; replaced by the **Treaty of Lausanne (1923)**, which recognised modern **Turkey** |

## League of Nations (লীগ অব নেশনস / জাতিপুঞ্জ) 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Idea | 🔥 US President **Woodrow Wilson** (Nobel Peace Prize 1919) |
| Covenant | Part I of the Treaty of Versailles |
| Came into being | 🔥 **10 January 1920** |
| 🔥 HQ | **Geneva, Switzerland** (Palais des Nations) |
| Founding members | **42** (maximum **58**, in 1934–35) |
| First Secretary-General | **Sir Eric Drummond** (UK) |
| Organs | Assembly, Council, Secretariat; linked bodies: **Permanent Court of International Justice** (The Hague, 1922) and the **ILO** (1919) |
| Dissolved | **April 1946**: the final Assembly voted on **18 April 1946** and it formally ceased on **20 April 1946**; assets passed to the **UN** |

- 🔥 **The USA never joined**: the US Senate refused to ratify the Treaty of Versailles.
- **Germany** joined in 1926 and left in 1933; **Japan** left in 1933 (after invading **Manchuria**, 1931); **Italy** left in 1937 (after invading **Abyssinia/Ethiopia**, 1935); the **USSR** joined in 1934 and was **expelled in 1939** (for attacking Finland).
- **Why it failed:** no army of its own; decisions needed **unanimity**; major powers absent or leaving; weak economic sanctions; appeasement of aggressors.
- The **ILO** is the only major League body that survives; it became the **UN's first specialized agency (1946)**.

## Quick revision
- WWI: **28 July 1914 – 11 November 1918**; spark: **Franz Ferdinand** killed at **Sarajevo**, 28 June 1914.
- Central Powers: **Germany, Austria-Hungary, Ottoman Empire, Bulgaria**.
- USA entered in **April 1917**; tanks first used at the **Somme (1916)**.
- Treaty of Versailles: **28 June 1919**, Hall of Mirrors; Article **231** = war guilt.
- **Fourteen Points** by **Woodrow Wilson**; the 14th proposed the League.
- League of Nations: **10 January 1920**, **Geneva**; USA never joined; dissolved **April 1946**.
- Kazi Nazrul Islam served in the **49th Bengal Regiment** in WWI.
