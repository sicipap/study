# Chinese & Russian revolutions, Arab Spring

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for the leaders and dates of the 1911 and 1949 Chinese revolutions, the Long March, the February and October revolutions of 1917 (Lenin, "Peace, Land, Bread"), the fall of the USSR, and where the Arab Spring began (Tunisia, Mohamed Bouazizi).

## Chinese revolutions (চীনের বিপ্লব) 🔥
### 1911 Revolution (Xinhai Revolution)
- 🔥 Began with the **Wuchang Uprising on 10 October 1911** ("Double Tenth", Taiwan's National Day).
- Overthrew the 🔥 **Qing (Manchu) dynasty**, China's last imperial dynasty; the last emperor **Puyi** abdicated in **1912**.
- 🔥 Leader: **Dr Sun Yat-sen** — "**Father of the Nation**" (of modern China). **Republic of China** proclaimed on **1 January 1912**.
- Sun's "**Three Principles of the People**": **nationalism, democracy, people's livelihood**.
- **Kuomintang (KMT)**, the Nationalist Party, was founded in **1912**; later led by **Chiang Kai-shek**.
- **May Fourth Movement (1919):** student protest against the Versailles decision to give Shandong to Japan.

### Communist Revolution (1949)
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **1921** | **Chinese Communist Party (CCP)** founded in **Shanghai** |
| 1927 | Civil war between the **KMT** and the **CCP** began |
| 🔥 **1934–35** | **Long March** (about **10,000–12,500 km**, "25,000 li"), led by **Mao Zedong**, from Jiangxi to **Yan'an** (Shaanxi) |
| 1937–45 | War against **Japan** (KMT and CCP in an uneasy united front) |
| 🔥 **1 October 1949** | **Mao Zedong** proclaimed the **People's Republic of China** at **Tiananmen**, Beijing |
| 1949 | **Chiang Kai-shek** and the KMT fled to **Taiwan** |

- Mao's ideas: "**Mao Zedong Thought**"; *Little Red Book*; "**Political power grows out of the barrel of a gun**".
- After 1949: **Great Leap Forward (1958–62)** — famine; 🔥 **Cultural Revolution (1966–76)** — Red Guards; 🔥 **Deng Xiaoping's "Reform and Opening Up" (1978)** — market reforms ("socialism with Chinese characteristics"); **Tiananmen Square protests (1989)**.
- First Premier: **Zhou Enlai**.

## Russian Revolution (রুশ বিপ্লব) 🔥
- Background: autocratic rule of **Tsar Nicholas II** (**Romanov dynasty**), poverty, and huge losses in **WWI**.
- **1905 Revolution:** began with "**Bloody Sunday**" (January 1905); led to a limited parliament (**Duma**).
- 🔥 **February Revolution (March 1917 by the Western calendar):** **Tsar Nicholas II abdicated**; a **Provisional Government** was formed (later led by **Alexander Kerensky**). Workers' councils called **Soviets** grew powerful.
- 🔥 **October Revolution (25 October 1917 Old Style = 7 November 1917 New Style):** the **Bolsheviks** under 🔥 **Vladimir Lenin** seized power in **Petrograd** (St Petersburg), storming the **Winter Palace**. **Leon Trotsky** organised the uprising.
- 🔥 Bolshevik slogan: **"Peace, Land, Bread"** and "All power to the Soviets".
- Russia left WWI by the **Treaty of Brest-Litovsk (March 1918)**. The Tsar's family was executed in **1918**.
- **Civil War (1917–1922): Reds (Bolsheviks) vs Whites.** Trotsky built the **Red Army**.
- 🔥 **USSR** (Union of Soviet Socialist Republics) formed on **30 December 1922**.
- **Lenin's New Economic Policy (NEP), 1921**; Lenin died in **1924**; **Joseph Stalin** took over — **Five-Year Plans (from 1928)**, collectivisation, purges.
- **Bolsheviks vs Mensheviks:** the two factions of the Russian Social Democratic Party split in **1903**; Bolshevik = "majority".
- Ideas behind it: **Karl Marx & Friedrich Engels** — ***The Communist Manifesto* (1848)**; Marx's ***Das Kapital* (1867)**.

### End of the USSR
- **Mikhail Gorbachev** (from 1985): **glasnost** (openness) and **perestroika** (restructuring).
- Failed coup against Gorbachev: **August 1991**. 🔥 **USSR dissolved on 26 December 1991** (Gorbachev resigned on 25 December); **15 independent republics** emerged; **Boris Yeltsin** led Russia.
- **CIS (Commonwealth of Independent States)** formed in December 1991.

## Arab Spring (আরব বসন্ত) 🔥
- A wave of **anti-government protests** across the Arab world from **late 2010**, driven by unemployment, corruption, dictatorship and social media.
- 🔥 **Began in Tunisia**: street vendor 🔥 **Mohamed Bouazizi** set himself on fire in **Sidi Bouzid** on **17 December 2010**.

| Country | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Tunisia** ("**Jasmine Revolution**") | President **Zine El Abidine Ben Ali** fled on **14 January 2011**; the only country to make a (for a time) democratic transition. The **Tunisian National Dialogue Quartet** won the **Nobel Peace Prize 2015** |
| 🔥 **Egypt** | Protests at **Tahrir Square**, Cairo; **Hosni Mubarak** resigned on **11 February 2011**. **Mohamed Morsi** (Muslim Brotherhood) elected 2012, removed by the army in **July 2013**; **Abdel Fattah el-Sisi** later became president |
| 🔥 **Libya** | Civil war; NATO air campaign under **UNSC Resolution 1973**; **Muammar Gaddafi** killed on **20 October 2011** |
| **Yemen** | **Ali Abdullah Saleh** handed over power (2012); later civil war. **Tawakkol Karman** won the **Nobel Peace Prize 2011** |
| 🔥 **Syria** | Protests from **March 2011** (Daraa) turned into a long **civil war**; the **Assad regime fell on 8 December 2024** |
| **Bahrain** | Protests at the **Pearl Roundabout** put down with help from GCC (Saudi) forces |
| Morocco, Jordan | Limited reforms by the monarchs |

- Results: mostly **instability, civil war and refugee crises** (except partial success in Tunisia); rise of **ISIS** in Syria/Iraq.

## Quick revision
- 1911 Revolution: **Wuchang, 10 Oct 1911**; ended the **Qing** dynasty; leader **Sun Yat-sen**.
- CCP founded **1921**; **Long March 1934–35**; PRC proclaimed **1 Oct 1949** by **Mao**.
- Cultural Revolution **1966–76**; Deng's reforms **1978**.
- February Revolution 1917: **Tsar Nicholas II abdicated**; October Revolution: **Lenin's Bolsheviks** (7 Nov 1917 NS).
- Slogan **"Peace, Land, Bread"**; USSR formed **30 Dec 1922**; dissolved **26 Dec 1991**.
- Arab Spring began in **Tunisia** with **Mohamed Bouazizi** (17 Dec 2010); **Jasmine Revolution**.
- Mubarak resigned **11 Feb 2011**; Gaddafi killed **20 Oct 2011**.
