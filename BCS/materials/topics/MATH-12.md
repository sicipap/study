# Age problems

> **Why it matters:** Age problems (বয়স সংক্রান্ত সমস্যা) are simple linear equations in disguise. Bank, primary-teacher and 9th–20th grade papers ask them often, and many can be solved in seconds by testing the options.

## Key concepts
- Take the **present age** as the unknown (x). Then:
  - n years **ago** → x − n
  - n years **later / hence** → x + n
- The **difference between two people's ages never changes**.
- In n years, **each** person grows n years older, so the **sum** of k people's ages increases by k × n.
- If a family's average age is A now and nobody joined or left, the average n years ago was A − n.

## Formula and shortcut table
| Situation | How to set up |
|---|---|
| Present ages in ratio a : b | Ages = ak and bk |
| After n years ratio becomes c : d | (ak + n)/(bk + n) = c/d |
| n years ago ratio was c : d | (ak − n)/(bk − n) = c/d |
| A is m times B now; after n years p times | A = mB and A + n = p(B + n) |
| Sum of ages S now; k people | Sum after n years = S + kn |
| Age difference D | Constant forever |
| 🔥 Shortcut | Put each option into the conditions; the one that fits all is the answer |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** A father is 3 times as old as his son. After 10 years he will be twice as old. Find their present ages.
Steps: Son = x, father = 3x. 3x + 10 = 2(x + 10) → x = 10.
**Answer: Son 10, father 30**

**Example 2.** The present ages of A and B are in the ratio 4 : 5. After 5 years the ratio will be 5 : 6. Find their ages.
Steps: (4k + 5)/(5k + 5) = 5/6 → 24k + 30 = 25k + 25 → k = 5.
**Answer: 20 and 25 years**

**Example 3.** Ten years ago a mother was 4 times as old as her daughter. After 10 years she will be twice as old. Find the daughter's present age.
Steps: M − 10 = 4(D − 10) and M + 10 = 2(D + 10) → M = 2D + 10. So 2D = 4D − 40 → D = 20 (M = 50).
**Answer: 20 years**

**Example 4.** The sum of the ages of a father and son is 60. Six years ago the father was 5 times as old as the son. Find the son's age.
Steps: F = 60 − S. 60 − S − 6 = 5(S − 6) → 54 − S = 5S − 30 → S = 14.
**Answer: 14 years**

**Example 5.** A man's age 15 years hence will be 4 times his age 15 years ago. Find his present age.
Steps: x + 15 = 4(x − 15) → 3x = 75 → x = 25.
**Answer: 25 years**

## Common traps
- Applying "years ago" to only one person — **both** people change by the same years.
- Forgetting that a new baby adds its own (small) age to the family total.
- Mixing up "twice as old" (×2) with "two years older" (+2).
- Answering with the wrong person's age — re-read whose age is asked.

## Quick revision
- Let present age = x; ago → subtract, hence → add.
- Age difference is constant.
- Family of k: total rises by k each year.
- Ratio problems: ak, bk, then apply the time shift.
- Test options to save time.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** A father is 4 times as old as his son. After 5 years he will be 3 times as old. The father's present age is:
(a) 40 (b) 36 (c) 45 (d) 32

**2.** The present ages of two brothers are in the ratio 3 : 4. After 5 years the ratio will be 4 : 5. The elder brother's present age is:
(a) 15 (b) 25 (c) 20 (d) 16

**3.** The sum of a father's and son's ages is 60. Six years ago the father was 5 times as old as the son. The son's present age is:
(a) 12 (b) 14 (c) 16 (d) 10

**4.** A is 2 years older than B, and B is twice as old as C. If the total of their ages is 27, B's age is:
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 10

**5.** At marriage, the average age of a husband and wife was 23. After 5 years they have a 1-year-old child. The average age of the family now is:
(a) 19 (b) 23 (c) 28.5 (d) 18

**6.** The ages of A and B are in the ratio 5 : 7. Four years ago the ratio was 3 : 5. B's present age is:
(a) 10 (b) 21 (c) 14 (d) 28

**7.** Ten years ago A was half as old as B. If their present ages are in the ratio 3 : 4, the sum of their present ages is:
(a) 35 (b) 45 (c) 28 (d) 42

**8.** The average age of 3 children is 12. When a fourth child is included, the average becomes 10. The fourth child's age is:
(a) 6 (b) 2 (c) 10 (d) 4

**9.** A mother is 30 years older than her daughter. After 5 years the mother will be 3 times as old as the daughter. The daughter's present age is:
(a) 15 (b) 5 (c) 10 (d) 12

**10.** A man's age 15 years hence will be 4 times his age 15 years ago. His present age is:
(a) 25 (b) 20 (c) 30 (d) 35

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a | 4x + 5 = 3(x + 5) → x = 10 → father 40 |
| 2 | c | (3k + 5)/(4k + 5) = 4/5 → k = 5 → 15, 20 |
| 3 | b | 54 − S = 5(S − 6) → S = 14 |
| 4 | d | C = x, B = 2x, A = 2x + 2 → 5x + 2 = 27 → x = 5 → B = 10 |
| 5 | a | Total 46 + 10 + 1 = 57 → 57/3 = 19 |
| 6 | c | (5k − 4)/(7k − 4) = 3/5 → k = 2 → 10, 14 |
| 7 | a | 3k − 10 = (4k − 10)/2 → k = 5 → 15 + 20 = 35 |
| 8 | d | 4 × 10 − 3 × 12 = 4 |
| 9 | c | D + 35 = 3(D + 5) → D = 10 |
| 10 | a | x + 15 = 4(x − 15) → x = 25 |
