# National Integrity Strategy (NIS) 2012

> **Why it matters:** The National Integrity Strategy (জাতীয় শুদ্ধাচার কৌশল) is Bangladesh's main policy for honesty and ethics in public and social life. Exams ask when it was approved, how it defines integrity, and above all **how many and which state and non-state institutions** it covers.

## Basic facts 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Full title (Bangla) | **"সোনার বাংলা গড়ার প্রত্যয়: জাতীয় শুদ্ধাচার কৌশল"** |
| English title | *National Integrity Strategy of Bangladesh: Committed to Golden Bengal* |
| Approved by the Cabinet | **18 October 2012** |
| Coordinating office | **Cabinet Division** (মন্ত্রিপরিষদ বিভাগ), through an NIS implementation unit |
| Integrity (শুদ্ধাচার) means | **Behavioural excellence influenced by morality and honesty** (নৈতিকতা ও সততা দ্বারা প্রভাবিত আচরণগত উৎকর্ষ) |
| Vision (রূপকল্প) | A **happy and prosperous Golden Bengal** (সুখী-সমৃদ্ধ সোনার বাংলা) |
| Mission (অভিলক্ষ্য) | Establish **good governance** by ensuring integrity in **state institutions and society** |
| Total institutions covered | 🔥 **16 = 10 state + 6 non-state** |

The strategy says integrity also includes loyalty to the **customs, norms and principles** of society, and applies to both individuals and institutions.

## The 10 state institutions (রাষ্ট্রীয় প্রতিষ্ঠান) 🔥
| # | Institution |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Executive branch and public administration** (নির্বাহী বিভাগ ও জনপ্রশাসন) |
| 2 | **National Parliament** (জাতীয় সংসদ) |
| 3 | **Judiciary** (বিচার বিভাগ) |
| 4 | **Election Commission** (নির্বাচন কমিশন) |
| 5 | **Attorney General's Office** (অ্যাটর্নি জেনারেলের কার্যালয়) |
| 6 | **Public Service Commission** (সরকারি কর্ম কমিশন) |
| 7 | **Comptroller and Auditor General** (মহা হিসাব-নিরীক্ষক ও নিয়ন্ত্রকের কার্যালয়) |
| 8 | **Ombudsman** (ন্যায়পাল) |
| 9 | **Anti-Corruption Commission** (দুর্নীতি দমন কমিশন) |
| 10 | **Local government institutions** (স্থানীয় সরকার প্রতিষ্ঠান) |

## The 6 non-state institutions (অরাষ্ট্রীয় প্রতিষ্ঠান) 🔥
| # | Institution |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Political parties** (রাজনৈতিক দল) |
| 2 | **Private-sector industrial and commercial organisations** (বেসরকারি শিল্প ও বাণিজ্যিক প্রতিষ্ঠান) |
| 3 | **NGOs and civil society** (এনজিও ও সুশীল সমাজ) |
| 4 | **Family** (পরিবার) |
| 5 | **Educational institutions** (শিক্ষা প্রতিষ্ঠান) |
| 6 | **Media** (গণমাধ্যম) |

Memory aid for the non-state six: **"Party, Private, People's groups, Parents, Pupils' schools, Press"**.

## Implementation structure
- **National Integrity Advisory Council** (জাতীয় শুদ্ধাচার উপদেষ্টা পরিষদ), headed by the **head of government (Prime Minister)**: the top policy body.
- An **executive committee** to supervise implementation.
- **Cabinet Division** coordinates through the **NIS unit**.
- Each ministry/division has an **Ethics Committee** (নৈতিকতা কমিটি) and an **Integrity Focal Point** (শুদ্ধাচার ফোকাল পয়েন্ট) officer.
- Each office prepares a yearly **integrity action plan** (শুদ্ধাচার কর্মপরিকল্পনা) and reports progress; it is linked with the **Annual Performance Agreement (APA)**.

## Integrity Award (শুদ্ধাচার পুরস্কার) 🔥
- Introduced under the **শুদ্ধাচার পুরস্কার প্রদান নীতিমালা, 2017** (Integrity Award Policy 2017), later revised.
- Given every year to **government officials and employees** for honest, efficient and ethical service, to encourage integrity.

## Why the NIS matters
- It treats integrity as a **shared responsibility** of the state and society, not only of the ACC.
- It links **ethics with good governance**, the Constitution's ideals and development goals.
- Criticism: slow implementation and weak enforcement of action plans.

> 🔄 Update: the implementation bodies and award rules have been revised over time; check the Cabinet Division's latest NIS guidelines.

## Quick revision
- NIS approved by the Cabinet on 18 October 2012.
- Title: "সোনার বাংলা গড়ার প্রত্যয়: জাতীয় শুদ্ধাচার কৌশল".
- Integrity = behavioural excellence influenced by morality and honesty.
- 16 institutions: 10 state + 6 non-state.
- Non-state: political parties, private sector, NGOs/civil society, family, educational institutions, media.
- Top body: National Integrity Advisory Council, headed by the Prime Minister.
- Coordinated by the Cabinet Division; Integrity Award policy 2017.
