# Hills, islands, Sundarbans, haors

> **Why it matters:** Highest peak, the only coral island, the largest island, Sundarbans facts and the largest haor are classic one-line MCQs in BCS, bank and primary exams.

## Physical divisions of Bangladesh 🔥
| Type | Share of land | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Tertiary hills (টারশিয়ারি যুগের পাহাড়)** | about **12%** | Chittagong Hill Tracts, Sylhet and Mymensingh–Netrokona hills |
| **Pleistocene terraces (প্লাইস্টোসিন কালের সোপান)** | about **8%** | **বরেন্দ্রভূমি (Barind Tract)**, **মধুপুর ও ভাওয়ালের গড়**, **লালমাই পাহাড়** (Cumilla) |
| **Recent floodplains (সাম্প্রতিক প্লাবনভূমি)** | about **80%** | Most of the country |

- **Barind Tract:** Rajshahi, Naogaon, Chapainawabganj, Dinajpur, Rangpur, Joypurhat, Bogura area; red, clay soil; drought-prone.
- **Madhupur Tract:** Tangail, Mymensingh; **Bhawal Garh:** Gazipur.

## The Sundarbans (সুন্দরবন) 🔥🔥
- The **largest single mangrove forest (ম্যানগ্রোভ বন)** in the world.
- Total area about **10,000 sq km**; about **6,017 sq km (about 62%)** lies in Bangladesh, the rest in India (West Bengal).
- Districts: **Khulna, Bagerhat, Satkhira** (some sources add parts of Patuakhali and Barguna).
- Name: from the **সুন্দরী গাছ (Heritiera fomes)**. Other trees: গেওয়া, গরান, কেওড়া, গোলপাতা.
- **UNESCO World Heritage Site:** **6 December 1997** (the Bangladesh part is the **798th** site on the list).
- **Ramsar site** since **1992**, Bangladesh's first.
- Animals: **Royal Bengal Tiger**, spotted deer, estuarine crocodile, Irrawaddy dolphin.
- **Tiger census:** **114** (2018); **125** (survey result published in **2024**).
- Three **wildlife sanctuaries**: Sundarbans East, West and South. **Karamjal** (Bagerhat) is a deer and crocodile breeding centre.
- Main rivers: **পশুর, শিবসা**. **Mongla port** is at its edge.
- **Sundarbans Day:** **14 February**.

> 🔄 Update: check the latest tiger census figure before the exam.

## Haors, beels and lakes (হাওর, বিল, হ্রদ) 🔥
- **Haor** = large, bowl-shaped wetland in the north-east that fills in the monsoon.
- **7 haor districts:** Sunamganj, Sylhet, Habiganj, Moulvibazar, Netrokona, Kishoreganj, Brahmanbaria. **Sunamganj has the most.**

| Wetland | Location | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **হাকালুকি হাওর** | Moulvibazar & Sylhet | **Largest haor** in Bangladesh |
| **টাঙ্গুয়ার হাওর** | Sunamganj | **Second Ramsar site** of Bangladesh (**2000**); "mother fishery" |
| **বাইক্কা বিল** | Hail haor, Moulvibazar | Fish sanctuary |
| **চলন বিল** | Pabna, Natore, Sirajganj | **Largest beel** |
| **কাপ্তাই হ্রদ** | Rangamati | **Largest artificial lake** |
| **বগা লেক (বগাকাইন)** | Ruma, Bandarban | Natural hill lake |
| **রাতারগুল** | Gowainghat, Sylhet | **Freshwater swamp forest** |
| **মাধবকুণ্ড** | Barlekha, Moulvibazar | Best-known waterfall |

## Quick revision
- Land: hills **12%**, Pleistocene terraces **8%**, floodplains **80%**.
- Highest peak (official): **Tajingdong (Bijoy)**, Bandarban.
- Only coral island: **St Martin's**; largest island: **Bhola**; only hilly island: **Maheshkhali**.
- Sundarbans: World Heritage **1997**, Ramsar **1992**, about **62%** in Bangladesh.
- Largest haor: **Hakaluki**; Ramsar haor: **Tanguar (2000)**.
- Largest beel: **Chalan Beel**; largest artificial lake: **Kaptai**.
- Freshwater swamp forest: **Ratargul**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Which is the only coral island of Bangladesh?
(a) Maheshkhali (b) St Martin's (c) Kutubdia (d) Sonadia

**2.** The officially recognised highest peak of Bangladesh is —
(a) Keokradong (b) Chimbuk (c) Garo hill (d) Tajingdong

**3.** In which year was the Sundarbans declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site?
(a) 1997 (b) 1985 (c) 1992 (d) 2000

**4.** The largest haor of Bangladesh is —
(a) Tanguar haor (b) Hail haor (c) Hakaluki haor (d) Shanir haor

**5.** Which is the only hilly island of Bangladesh?
(a) Bhola (b) Hatiya (c) Sandwip (d) Maheshkhali

**6.** Tanguar haor is located in which district?
(a) Sylhet (b) Moulvibazar (c) Netrokona (d) Sunamganj

**7.** The Barind Tract belongs to which type of landform?
(a) Pleistocene terrace (b) Tertiary hill (c) Recent floodplain (d) Coastal delta

**8.** Ratargul is famous as a —
(a) coral reef (b) freshwater swamp forest (c) mangrove forest (d) salt marsh

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | St Martin's (Narikel Jinjira) is the only coral island. |
| 2 | d | Tajingdong (Bijoy) in Bandarban is the official answer. |
| 3 | a | Inscribed on 6 December 1997. |
| 4 | c | Hakaluki haor, Moulvibazar and Sylhet. |
| 5 | d | Maheshkhali, Cox's Bazar. |
| 6 | d | Tanguar haor is in Sunamganj; Ramsar site since 2000. |
| 7 | a | Barind, Madhupur and Lalmai are Pleistocene terraces. |
| 8 | b | Ratargul, Gowainghat, Sylhet. |
