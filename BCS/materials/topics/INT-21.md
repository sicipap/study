# Human rights: UDHR, CEDAW, CRC

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask when the UDHR was adopted (10 December 1948), how many articles it has, who chaired the drafting, the dates of CEDAW and the CRC, Bangladesh's reservations to CEDAW, the Human Rights Council, and which treaties Bangladesh has (or has not) joined.

## Background (মানবাধিকারের ইতিহাস)
| Document | Year | Country |
|---|---|---|
| **Magna Carta** | **1215** | England (limited the king's power) |
| **Bill of Rights** | 1689 | England |
| **US Declaration of Independence** / **US Bill of Rights** | 1776 / 1791 | USA |
| **Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen** | 1789 | France |

- **Generations of rights** (Karel Vasak): **1st** — civil and political; **2nd** — economic, social and cultural; **3rd** — collective/solidarity rights (development, environment, peace).

## UDHR (Universal Declaration of Human Rights / মানবাধিকারের সর্বজনীন ঘোষণাপত্র) 🔥
- 🔥 Adopted by the UN General Assembly on **10 December 1948** at the **Palais de Chaillot, Paris** (Resolution **217 A**) → **10 December = Human Rights Day**.
- 🔥 **30 articles.** 48 countries voted in favour, none against, 8 abstained.
- 🔥 Drafting committee chaired by **Eleanor Roosevelt** (USA). Other key drafters: **René Cassin** (France), **John Humphrey** (Canada), **P. C. Chang** (China), **Charles Malik** (Lebanon).
- **Article 1:** "All human beings are born **free and equal in dignity and rights**."
- It is a **declaration**, **not a legally binding treaty**, but many of its rules are now customary law.
- 🔥 It is the **world's most translated document** (Guinness record).

### International Bill of Human Rights 🔥
= **UDHR (1948)** + **ICCPR** (International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights) + **ICESCR** (International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights). The two covenants were adopted in **1966** and came into force in **1976**.

## CEDAW (নারীর প্রতি সকল প্রকার বৈষম্য বিলোপ সনদ) 🔥
- 🔥 **Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women**: adopted **18 December 1979**; in force **3 September 1981**.
- Called the "**international bill of rights for women**"; **30 articles**; monitored by the **CEDAW Committee**.
- 🔥 **Bangladesh ratified in 1984** with **reservations**. Its remaining reservations are on 🔥 **Article 2** (policy measures to end discrimination) and **Article 16.1(c)** (equal rights in marriage and divorce); reservations on some other articles were withdrawn in **1997**.
- The **USA has signed but not ratified** CEDAW.
- Related: **Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action** (4th World Conference on Women, **1995**); **International Women's Day, 8 March**; **UN Women** (2010).

## CRC (শিশু অধিকার সনদ) 🔥
- 🔥 **Convention on the Rights of the Child**: adopted **20 November 1989** (→ **World Children's Day**); in force **2 September 1990**.
- Defines a **child as anyone under 18** (unless national law sets majority earlier).
- **54 articles**; four core principles: **non-discrimination**, **best interests of the child**, **right to life, survival and development**, **respect for the child's views**.
- 🔥 The **most widely ratified** human-rights treaty; 🔥 the **USA is the only UN member that has not ratified it**.
- 🔥 **Bangladesh ratified it on 3 August 1990**, among the **first 20+ countries** to do so.
- **UNICEF** promotes the CRC. Optional protocols (2000) on **children in armed conflict** and on the **sale of children**.

## UN human-rights machinery 🔥
| Body | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Commission on Human Rights** | 1946–2006 (first chair: Eleanor Roosevelt) |
| 🔥 **Human Rights Council (HRC)** | Replaced the Commission in **2006**; HQ **Geneva**; 🔥 **47 members** elected by the GA for 3 years; runs the **Universal Periodic Review (UPR)** of every UN member |
| 🔥 **OHCHR** (High Commissioner for Human Rights) | Created **1993** after the **Vienna World Conference**; HQ **Geneva**; High Commissioner **Volker Türk** (Austria) since 2022 (as of early 2026) |
| Treaty bodies | Committees for each treaty (Human Rights Committee for ICCPR, CEDAW Committee, CRC Committee…) |

- 🔥 **OHCHR fact-finding report (February 2025)** on Bangladesh's **July–August 2024 uprising** estimated that **up to 1,400 people** may have been killed, and found possible crimes against humanity.

## Human rights in Bangladesh
- Constitution **Part III** (Articles **26–47A**): **fundamental rights**.
- 🔥 **National Human Rights Commission (NHRC)**: under the **NHRC Act 2009**.
- NGOs: **Amnesty International** (London, **1961**, Nobel Peace **1977**); **Human Rights Watch** (New York, 1978).

> 🔄 Update: check the latest figure in a newspaper or the Bangladesh Economic Review. Check who currently heads the OHCHR and any new UN human-rights reports on Bangladesh.

## Quick revision
- UDHR: **10 December 1948**, Paris; **30 articles**; chaired by **Eleanor Roosevelt**.
- International Bill of Rights = **UDHR + ICCPR + ICESCR** (covenants 1966, in force 1976).
- CEDAW: **18 Dec 1979**, in force **1981**; Bangladesh ratified **1984**; reservations on **Art. 2 and 16.1(c)**.
- CRC: **20 Nov 1989**, in force **2 Sept 1990**; child = **under 18**; Bangladesh ratified **1990**; USA not ratified.
- Human Rights Council: **2006**, **Geneva**, **47** members, **UPR**.
- Bangladesh is **not** a party to the **1951 Refugee Convention**.
