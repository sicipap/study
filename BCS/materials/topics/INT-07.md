# UN specialized agencies & HQs

> **Why it matters:** "Where is the headquarters of …?" and "Which is the oldest agency?" are among the most common international MCQs. Learn the HQ city of each agency, the founding year of the famous ones, and Bangladesh's links (UNESCO heritage, Mother Language Day).

## What is a specialized agency? (বিশেষায়িত সংস্থা)
- An **autonomous** international organization linked to the UN by an agreement through **ECOSOC** (Articles 57 and 63 of the Charter). It has its own members, budget and head.
- 🔥 There are **15 specialized agencies** (commonly counted, with the World Bank Group as one).
- **Funds and programmes** (UNICEF, UNDP, WFP, UNEP…) are **not** specialized agencies; they are part of the UN system and report to the GA/ECOSOC.

## The 15 specialized agencies 🔥
| Agency | Full name | HQ | Founded |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FAO** | Food and Agriculture Organization | 🔥 **Rome** | **16 October 1945** (→ World Food Day) |
| **ICAO** | International Civil Aviation Organization | 🔥 **Montreal** (Canada) | 1944 convention; began 1947 |
| **IFAD** | International Fund for Agricultural Development | **Rome** | 1977 |
| **ILO** | International Labour Organization | 🔥 **Geneva** | 🔥 **1919** (oldest agency born under the League); **first UN specialized agency (1946)**; Nobel Peace 1969 |
| **IMF** | International Monetary Fund | **Washington, D.C.** | 1944 (Bretton Woods) |
| **IMO** | International Maritime Organization | 🔥 **London** (only UN agency HQ in the UK) | Convention 1948; in force 1958 |
| **ITU** | International Telecommunication Union | 🔥 **Geneva** | 🔥 **1865** (as the International Telegraph Union) — **oldest** of all |
| **UNESCO** | UN Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization | 🔥 **Paris** | **16 November 1945** |
| **UNIDO** | UN Industrial Development Organization | **Vienna** | 1966 (specialized agency from 1985) |
| **UN Tourism** (formerly **UNWTO**) | World Tourism Organization | 🔥 **Madrid** | 1975 |
| **UPU** | Universal Postal Union | 🔥 **Bern** (Switzerland) | 🔥 **1874** (second oldest); 9 October = World Post Day |
| **WHO** | World Health Organization | 🔥 **Geneva** | **7 April 1948** (→ World Health Day) |
| **WIPO** | World Intellectual Property Organization | **Geneva** | 1967 |
| **WMO** | World Meteorological Organization | **Geneva** | 1950 (23 March = World Meteorological Day) |
| **World Bank Group** | IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID | **Washington, D.C.** | 1944 (Bretton Woods) |

**Memory trick by city**
- **Geneva (5):** ILO, ITU, WHO, WIPO, WMO.
- **Rome (2 agencies):** FAO, IFAD (plus the **WFP** programme).
- **Washington (2):** IMF, World Bank.
- One each: **Paris** (UNESCO), **London** (IMO), **Montreal** (ICAO), **Bern** (UPU), **Vienna** (UNIDO), **Madrid** (UN Tourism).

## Bangladesh links 🔥
- 🔥 **UNESCO** declared **21 February** as **International Mother Language Day** on **17 November 1999**; first observed in **2000**.
- **UNESCO World Heritage Sites in Bangladesh (3):** **Historic Mosque City of Bagerhat** (Sixty Dome Mosque, **1985**), **Paharpur Buddhist Vihara** (**1985**), **Sundarbans** (natural, **1997**).
- **Intangible cultural heritage (UNESCO list):** **Baul songs**, **Jamdani** weaving (2013), **Mangal Shobhajatra** (2016), **Shital Pati** (2017), **Rickshaw painting of Dhaka** (2023).
- **Bangabandhu's 7 March speech** was added to UNESCO's **Memory of the World Register** on **30 October 2017**.

## Quick revision
- 15 specialized agencies; **oldest: ITU (1865)**, then **UPU (1874)**.
- **ILO (1919)** was the first to become a UN specialized agency (1946).
- Geneva: **ILO, ITU, WHO, WIPO, WMO**; Rome: **FAO, IFAD**; Washington: **IMF, World Bank**.
- UNESCO **Paris**; IMO **London**; ICAO **Montreal**; UPU **Bern**; UNIDO **Vienna**; UN Tourism **Madrid**.
- IAEA **Vienna**; WTO **Geneva**; UNEP **Nairobi**; UNU **Tokyo**; UNICEF **New York**.
- WHO founded **7 April 1948**; FAO **16 October 1945**.
