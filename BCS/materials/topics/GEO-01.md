# Earth: latitude, longitude, time zones

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the tilt of Earth's axis, the Prime Meridian and International Date Line, how many minutes one degree of longitude equals, Bangladesh's standard time meridian (90° E), and time-difference sums.

## Earth: basic facts 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Shape | **Oblate spheroid / geoid** — flattened at the poles, bulging at the equator |
| Equatorial radius | About **6,378 km** (polar radius about 6,357 km) |
| Equatorial circumference | About **40,075 km** |
| 🔥 Rotation (আহ্নিক গতি) | **West to east**; about **23 h 56 min 4 s** (one sidereal day); causes **day and night** |
| 🔥 Revolution (বার্ষিক গতি) | About **365 days 5 h 48 min 46 s**; causes **seasons** (with the tilt) |
| 🔥 Axis tilt | **66½° to the orbital plane**, i.e. **23½° from the vertical** |
| Mean distance from the Sun | About **149.6 million km** (1 astronomical unit); sunlight takes about **8 min 20 s** |
| **Perihelion** (nearest to Sun) | Around **3 January** (about 147 million km) |
| **Aphelion** (farthest from Sun) | Around **4 July** (about 152 million km) |

- Because the year is about 365¼ days, a **leap year** of 366 days comes every 4 years (February has 29 days).

## Latitude (অক্ষাংশ) 🔥
- **Latitude** is the **angular distance north or south of the equator**. Lines of latitude are called **parallels**; they are **parallel circles**.
- Range: **0° (equator) to 90° N and 90° S (poles)**.
- 🔥 The **equator (0°)** is the **longest parallel** (a great circle); it divides Earth into the Northern and Southern Hemispheres.
- 1° of latitude ≈ **111 km**.

| Parallel | Latitude | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Equator (বিষুবরেখা)** | **0°** | Day and night about equal all year |
| 🔥 **Tropic of Cancer (কর্কটক্রান্তি রেখা)** | **23½° N** | Sun overhead on **21 June**; **passes through the middle of Bangladesh** |
| **Tropic of Capricorn (মকরক্রান্তি রেখা)** | **23½° S** | Sun overhead on **22 December** |
| **Arctic Circle** | **66½° N** | 24-hour day in midsummer |
| **Antarctic Circle** | **66½° S** | |

## Longitude (দ্রাঘিমাংশ) 🔥
- **Longitude** is the **angular distance east or west of the Prime Meridian**. Lines of longitude are **meridians**, half-circles joining the poles; all meridians are **equal in length**.
- Range: **0° to 180° E and 180° W** (360° in all).
- 🔥 **Prime Meridian (0°)** passes through the **Royal Observatory, Greenwich, London**. It was fixed at the International Meridian Conference in **1884**.
- 🔥 **International Date Line (আন্তর্জাতিক তারিখ রেখা):** roughly along **180°**, mostly through the **Pacific Ocean**; it **zig-zags** to avoid land.
  - Crossing it **from west to east** (Asia → America): **go back one day** (subtract a day).
  - Crossing it **from east to west** (America → Asia): **gain one day** (add a day).

## Longitude and time 🔥🔥
Earth turns **360° in 24 hours**, so:
- **1° of longitude = 4 minutes** of time.
- **15° of longitude = 1 hour.**
- Places **east** of Greenwich are **ahead** (time +); places **west** are **behind** (time −).
- **GMT** (Greenwich Mean Time) is now expressed as **UTC** (Coordinated Universal Time).
- **Local time** depends on the sun at each meridian; **standard time** is the time fixed for a whole country or zone.
- The world has **24 standard time zones** of 15° each (in practice, more because of half-hour zones).

**Worked examples**
1. When it is **12 noon at Greenwich**, what time is it at **90° E**? → 90 × 4 = 360 min = 6 h ahead → **6:00 pm**.
2. Time difference between **Dhaka (90° E)** and a place at **75° W**? → 90 + 75 = 165°; 165 × 4 = 660 min = **11 hours** (Dhaka ahead).
3. Two places differ in time by **2 hours**. Longitude difference? → 120 min ÷ 4 = **30°**.

## Bangladesh: location and time 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Latitude | About **20°34′ N to 26°38′ N** |
| Longitude | About **88°01′ E to 92°41′ E** |
| 🔥 **Bangladesh Standard Time (BST)** | **UTC/GMT + 6 hours**, based on the **90° E** meridian |
| Tropic of Cancer | **Crosses the middle of the country** |
| Daylight saving time | Tried once in **2009** (clocks put forward one hour from 19 June to 31 December 2009) |

- **India:** UTC + 5:30; **Nepal:** UTC + 5:45; **Myanmar:** UTC + 6:30. So Bangladesh is **30 minutes ahead of India**.
- **China** uses **one** time zone (UTC + 8) despite its size. **France** has the **most time zones** when overseas territories are counted (12); **Russia** has **11**.

## Solstices and equinoxes 🔥
| Date | Event | Sun overhead at |
|---|---|---|
| **21 March** | **Vernal (spring) equinox** — day = night everywhere | Equator |
| 🔥 **21 June** | **Summer solstice** — **longest day** in the Northern Hemisphere | **Tropic of Cancer** |
| **23 September** | **Autumnal equinox** — day = night | Equator |
| 🔥 **22 December** | **Winter solstice** — **shortest day** in the Northern Hemisphere | **Tropic of Capricorn** |

## Quick revision
- Axis tilt: **23½°** (66½° to the orbital plane).
- 1° longitude = **4 minutes**; 15° = **1 hour**.
- Prime Meridian: **Greenwich**; Date Line: about **180°**, in the Pacific.
- BST = **GMT + 6**, meridian **90° E**.
- Tropic of Cancer **23½° N** crosses Bangladesh.
- Longest day in the north: **21 June**; shortest: **22 December**.
- Equinoxes: **21 March** and **23 September**.
- Perihelion ≈ **3 January**; aphelion ≈ **4 July**.
