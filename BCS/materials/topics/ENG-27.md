# Transformation of sentences

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask you to change a sentence without changing its meaning: simple ↔ complex ↔ compound, affirmative ↔ negative, degree of comparison, assertive ↔ interrogative/exclamatory, and "too … to" ↔ "so … that".

## Kinds of sentence by structure 🔥
| Kind | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | **one** finite verb (one main clause) | "**Being ill**, he could not come." |
| **Compound** | **two or more main clauses** joined by coordinating conjunctions (and, but, or, so, therefore) | "He was ill, **and so** he could not come." |
| **Complex** | **one main clause + one or more subordinate clauses** (because, if, when, who, that, though) | "**As he was ill**, he could not come." |

## Simple ↔ Complex ↔ Compound 🔥
| Simple | Complex | Compound |
|---|---|---|
| **Being** poor, he could not buy it. | **As** he was poor, he could not buy it. | He was poor **and so** he could not buy it. |
| **In spite of** being rich, he is unhappy. | **Though** he is rich, he is unhappy. | He is rich, **but** he is unhappy. |
| **Without** working hard, you cannot succeed. | **If** you **do not** work hard / **Unless** you work hard, you cannot succeed. | Work hard, **or** you cannot succeed. |
| He confessed **his guilt**. | He confessed **that he was guilty**. | He was guilty **and** he confessed it. |
| **At sunrise** we started. | We started **when** the sun rose. | The sun rose **and** we started. |
| He is too weak **to walk**. | He is **so** weak **that** he cannot walk. | He is very weak **and so** he cannot walk. |
| I saw a **wounded** bird. | I saw a bird **which was wounded**. | I saw a bird **and** it was wounded. |
| He worked hard **to pass**. | He worked hard **so that he might pass**. | He wanted to pass, **so** he worked hard. |

### Key conversion tools
| Simple uses | Complex uses | Compound uses |
|---|---|---|
| participle (**being, having + V3**) | **as, since, because** | **and, so, therefore** |
| **in spite of / despite** + noun/gerund | **though / although** | **but, yet, still** |
| **without** + gerund; **by** + gerund | **if / unless** | **or, otherwise, else** |
| **to** + infinitive (purpose) | **so that / in order that** | **and** |
| **too … to** | **so … that … not** | **very … and so … not** |
| noun / phrase | noun / adjective / adverb clause | two main clauses |

## Affirmative ↔ Negative 🔥
| Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|
| He is **always** punctual. | He is **never** late / **never** unpunctual. |
| **Only** Rahim can do it. | **None but** Rahim can do it. |
| **Only** a fool can say this. | **None but** a fool can say this. |
| He is **only** ten. | He is **not more than** ten. |
| **Everyone** loves his country. | **There is no one who does not** love his country. |
| As soon as he came, I left. | **No sooner** had he come **than** I left. |
| I **doubt** whether he will come. | I **am not sure** that he will come. |
| He is **greater** than I. | I am **not so great as** he. |
| I **remember** the day. | I **do not forget** the day. |
| He **failed** to come. | He **did not come**. |
| He is **honest**. | He is **not dishonest**. |
| Both of them came. | **Not only** X **but also** Y came. |

## Degrees of comparison 🔥
| Superlative | Comparative | Positive |
|---|---|---|
| Rahim is **the tallest** boy in the class. | Rahim is **taller than any other** boy in the class. | **No other** boy in the class is **as tall as** Rahim. |
| Dhaka is **one of the largest** cities in Asia. | Dhaka is **larger than most other** cities in Asia. | **Very few** cities in Asia are **as large as** Dhaka. |
| Shakespeare is **the greatest** dramatist. | Shakespeare is **greater than any other** dramatist. | **No other** dramatist is **as great as** Shakespeare. |

- "**one of the + superlative**" → "**than most other**" → "**very few … as … as**".
- Comparative "A is better than B" → Positive "**B is not so good as A**".

## Assertive ↔ Interrogative ↔ Exclamatory
| Assertive | Interrogative / Exclamatory |
|---|---|
| **Everybody** knows him. | **Who** does not know him? |
| **Nobody** can do it. | **Who** can do it? |
| It is **no use** crying. | **What is the use of** crying? |
| He **never** tells a lie. | **Does** he **ever** tell a lie? |
| It is a **very** fine day. | **What a** fine day it is! |
| He is **very** clever. | **How** clever he is! |
| I **wish** I were young again. | **Would that** I were young again! / **If only** I were young again! |

Note: an **affirmative** assertive becomes a **negative** rhetorical question, and vice versa.

## Imperative ↔ Assertive
- "**Do** it." → "You **should** do it." — "**Don't** smoke." → "You **should not** smoke." — "**Let** us go." → "**We should** go."

## Common mistakes
| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| Inspite of he is rich, he is unhappy. | **In spite of being** rich, he is unhappy. / **Though** he is rich, … |
| He is too weak that he cannot walk. | He is **so** weak **that** he cannot walk. |
| No other boy is taller than Rahim. (as the positive form) | No other boy is **as tall as** Rahim. |
| Rahim is taller than any boy in the class. | … **than any other** boy … |
| Though he is poor but he is honest. | **Though** he is poor, he is honest. / He is poor **but** honest. |

## Quick revision
- 🔥 **Simple** = one finite verb; **compound** = two main clauses; **complex** = main + subordinate.
- 🔥 **too … to** ↔ **so … that … not**.
- 🔥 **in spite of** (simple) ↔ **though** (complex) ↔ **but** (compound).
- **Only** → **none but** (person), **not more than** (number).
- Superlative → **than any other** → **no other … as … as**.
- **Everybody knows** → "**Who does not know**?"
- **very** → "**What a / How**" (exclamatory).

## Practice MCQ
**1.** "Being ill, he could not attend the class." This is a —
(a) complex sentence (b) compound sentence (c) simple sentence (d) mixed sentence

**2.** Complex form of "He is too weak to walk" —
(a) He is so weak that he cannot walk. (b) He is weak and cannot walk. (c) Being weak, he cannot walk. (d) He is very weak to walk.

**3.** Negative of "Only Allah can help us" —
(a) Allah only cannot help us. (b) None but Allah can help us. (c) Nobody can help Allah. (d) Allah can help us alone.

**4.** Comparative of "Karim is the best boy in the class" —
(a) Karim is better than all boys. (b) No other boy is as good as Karim. (c) Karim is better than any boy. (d) Karim is better than any other boy in the class.

**5.** Interrogative of "Everybody loves his motherland" —
(a) Does everybody love his motherland? (b) Who does not love his motherland? (c) Who loves his motherland? (d) Does not everybody love motherland?

**6.** Compound form of "Though he is rich, he is not happy" —
(a) In spite of his riches, he is not happy. (b) Being rich, he is not happy. (c) He is rich but he is not happy. (d) He is not happy as he is rich.

**7.** Exclamatory form of "It is a very beautiful flower" —
(a) What a beautiful flower it is! (b) How a beautiful flower it is! (c) What beautiful flower! (d) Is it a very beautiful flower!

**8.** Positive degree of "Dhaka is one of the busiest cities in Asia" —
(a) No other city in Asia is as busy as Dhaka. (b) Dhaka is busier than any other city in Asia. (c) Dhaka is busier than most other cities in Asia. (d) Very few cities in Asia are as busy as Dhaka.

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | Only one finite verb, "could attend" |
| 2 | a | too … to becomes so … that … not |
| 3 | b | Only → none but |
| 4 | d | Superlative → than any other + singular |
| 5 | b | Affirmative assertive → negative question |
| 6 | c | though → but in the compound |
| 7 | a | very + adjective + noun → What a … |
| 8 | d | one of the + superlative → very few … as … as |
