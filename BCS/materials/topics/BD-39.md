# Culture: festivals, music, art, cinema

> **Why it matters:** UNESCO intangible heritage items, Pohela Boishakh, folk-music regions, Zainul Abedin and other artists, sculptures and cinema "firsts" appear in BCS, bank and primary exams.

## Festivals (উৎসব) 🔥
- **পহেলা বৈশাখ (Bengali New Year, 14 April):** The Bangla year (**বঙ্গাব্দ / ফসলি সন**) was introduced under Emperor **Akbar** for revenue collection; the calendar was worked out by **আমির ফতেউল্লাহ সিরাজী**. Traders open new account books (**হালখাতা**).
  - **ছায়ানট** has held its dawn programme under the **রমনা বটমূল** since **1967**.
  - The **Mangal Shobhajatra (মঙ্গল শোভাযাত্রা)**, started in **1989** by the **Faculty of Fine Arts (চারুকলা), University of Dhaka**, was inscribed by **UNESCO** as intangible cultural heritage on **30 November 2016**. In **2025** the procession was renamed **"বর্ষবরণ আনন্দ শোভাযাত্রা"**.
- **নবান্ন:** harvest festival on **1 Agrahayan** (also National Agriculture Day).
- **বৈসাবি:** new-year festival of the hill peoples (see BD-38).
- **Ekushey Book Fair (অমর একুশে গ্রন্থমেলা):** every February at **Bangla Academy** (and Suhrawardy Udyan). Started on **8 February 1972** by **চিত্তরঞ্জন সাহা** (Muktadhara) with a few books on a mat; Bangla Academy took charge in **1978**; named "অমর একুশে গ্রন্থমেলা" in **1984**.
- Religious festivals: Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Azha, **Durga Puja** (biggest Hindu festival), **Buddha Purnima**, Christmas.
- **রাস মেলা:** Dublar Char (Sundarbans).

## Fine arts and sculpture (চারুকলা ও ভাস্কর্য) 🔥🔥
- **জয়নুল আবেদিন — শিল্পাচার্য:** famous for his **1943 famine sketches (দুর্ভিক্ষের চিত্রমালা)**, the scroll **"নবান্ন"** and **"মনপুরা ৭০"**. Founded the **Government Institute of Fine Arts, Dhaka (1948)** — now the Faculty of Fine Arts, DU — and the **Folk Art and Crafts Foundation, Sonargaon (1975)**.
- **কামরুল হাসান — "পটুয়া":** made the 1971 poster of Yahya Khan **"এই জানোয়ারদের হত্যা করতে হবে"**; gave the **final design of the national flag**.
- **এস এম সুলতান:** Narail; peasants painted as strong figures; founded **শিশুস্বর্গ**.
- **মুর্তজা বশীর, মোহাম্মদ কিবরিয়া, সফিউদ্দীন আহমেদ, রশিদ চৌধুরী:** other leading artists.
- **নভেরা আহমেদ:** first modern sculptor of Bangladesh; worked with **হামিদুর রহমান** on the **Central Shaheed Minar** design.

## Cinema (চলচ্চিত্র) 🔥🔥
| Fact | Answer |
|---|---|
| First film made in Dhaka | **"The Last Kiss" (1931)**, silent |
| **First full-length Bangla talkie of East Bengal/Bangladesh** | **"মুখ ও মুখোশ" (1956)**, director **আবদুল জব্বার খান** |
| **FDC** (Film Development Corporation) | Founded **3 April 1957** (bill placed by Sheikh Mujibur Rahman as provincial minister); **3 April = National Film Day** |
| First Liberation War feature film | **"ওরা ১১ জন" (1972)**, **চাষী নজরুল ইসলাম** |
| **জহির রায়হান** | "জীবন থেকে নেয়া" (1970), documentary **"Stop Genocide" (1971)**; went missing on **30 January 1972** |
| **ঋত্বিক ঘটক** | **"তিতাস একটি নদীর নাম" (1973)**, from the novel by **অদ্বৈত মল্লবর্মণ** |
| **তারেক মাসুদ** | **"মাটির ময়না" (2002)** — first Bangladeshi film at **Cannes** (Directors' Fortnight, FIPRESCI award) and first Oscar submission |
| **"রেহানা মরিয়ম নূর" (2021)** | **আবদুল্লাহ মোহাম্মদ সাদ**; first Bangladeshi film in Cannes' official **Un Certain Regard** section |
| National Film Awards | Started in **1975** |

## Institutions
- **Bangla Academy:** **3 December 1955**, Burdwan House, Dhaka.
- **Bangladesh Shilpakala Academy:** **1974**.
- **Bangladesh National Museum:** Shahbag (see BD-40).
- **Munier Chowdhury** wrote the play **"কবর"** in jail in **1953** about the Language Movement.

## Quick revision
- 5 UNESCO intangible items: **Baul (2008), Jamdani (2013), Mangal Shobhajatra (2016), Shital Pati (2017), Rickshaw painting (2023)**.
- Bangla san introduced under **Akbar**; calendar by **Fatehullah Shirazi**.
- Bhawaiya = **Rangpur**; Gambhira = **Chapainawabganj**.
- Zainul Abedin = **Shilpacharya**, famine sketches **1943**.
- Jagroto Chowrongi = **first Liberation War sculpture**.
- First talkie **Mukh O Mukhosh (1956)**; FDC **3 April 1957**.
- Matir Moina (2002) = first film at Cannes.
