## SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation / সার্ক) 🔥
- 🔥 First proposed by President **Ziaur Rahman** of Bangladesh (**1980**).
- Founded at the **first summit in Dhaka, 7–8 December 1985**; **8 December = SAARC Charter Day**.
- 🔥 Secretariat: **Kathmandu, Nepal** (opened 17 January 1987). **First SG: Abul Ahsan** (Bangladesh).
- **7 founding members:** Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka.
- 🔥 **8th member: Afghanistan**, joined **April 2007** (14th summit, New Delhi).
- **9 observers**, including China, Japan, the USA, the EU, Iran, South Korea, Australia, Mauritius and Myanmar.
- **SAFTA** (South Asian Free Trade Area): signed **2004** (12th summit, Islamabad), in force **1 January 2006**.
- Other bodies: SAARC Development Fund (**Thimphu**), **South Asian University** (**New Delhi**), SAARC Food Bank.
- 🔥 **Last summit: 18th, Kathmandu, November 2014.** The **19th summit (Islamabad, 2016)** was cancelled after India and others pulled out; SAARC has been largely inactive since.
- Decisions are by **unanimity**; bilateral disputes are excluded from its agenda.

## ASEAN (Association of Southeast Asian Nations / আসিয়ান) 🔥
- Founded by the **Bangkok Declaration, 8 August 1967**.
- 🔥 **5 founders:** **Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand**.
- Later members: **Brunei (1984)**, **Vietnam (1995)**, **Laos & Myanmar (1997)**, **Cambodia (1999)**, and 🔥 **Timor-Leste (26 October 2025)** — the **11th member**.
- Secretariat: **Jakarta**. **ASEAN Charter** 2007 (in force 2008). **ASEAN Community** launched 2015.
- **ASEAN Regional Forum (ARF, 1994):** security dialogue; 🔥 **Bangladesh joined the ARF in 2006**.
- **RCEP** (signed 2020, in force 2022): ASEAN + China, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand — the world's largest free-trade agreement.
- Chair rotates yearly in alphabetical order (Malaysia 2025, Philippines 2026).

## BIMSTEC (বিমসটেক) 🔥
- Began on **6 June 1997** (Bangkok Declaration) as **BIST-EC**: **Bangladesh, India, Sri Lanka, Thailand**.
- **Myanmar** joined in December 1997 (→ BIMST-EC); **Nepal and Bhutan** in **2004**, when it was renamed.
- 🔥 Full name: **Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation**.
- 🔥 **7 members:** Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand (5 South Asian + 2 South-East Asian).
- 🔥 **Permanent Secretariat: Dhaka** (opened **2014**).
- **BIMSTEC Charter** adopted at the **5th summit (Colombo, 2022)**.
- **6th summit: Bangkok, April 2025**; **Bangladesh took over the chair**.

## European Union (EU / ইউরোপীয় ইউনিয়ন) 🔥
| Step | Year |
|---|---|
| **Schuman Declaration** (9 May = **Europe Day**) | 1950 |
| **European Coal and Steel Community (ECSC)**, Treaty of Paris | 1951 |
| 🔥 **Treaty of Rome** → **EEC** and Euratom | **1957** (in force 1958) |
| 🔥 **Maastricht Treaty** → **European Union** | signed **1992**, in force **1 November 1993** |
| **Euro** introduced (cash in 2002) | **1999** |
| **Lisbon Treaty** (Article 50 = exit clause) | signed 2007, in force 2009 |
| 🔥 **Brexit:** UK left the EU | **31 January 2020** (referendum 23 June 2016) |

- **6 founding members:** Belgium, France, West Germany, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands.
- 🔥 **27 members** after Brexit; the latest to join was **Croatia (2013)**.
- **Seats:** Commission & Council — **Brussels**; European Parliament — **Strasbourg** (official seat) and Brussels; Court of Justice — **Luxembourg**; **European Central Bank — Frankfurt**.
- **Eurozone:** **21** members after **Bulgaria adopted the euro on 1 January 2026**. **Schengen area:** 29 countries (Bulgaria and Romania fully from 2025).
- **Nobel Peace Prize 2012.** Anthem: "Ode to Joy"; flag: a circle of **12 gold stars** on blue.
- Other leaders (as of early 2026): European Council President **António Costa**; European Parliament President **Roberta Metsola**.
- **Bangladesh:** the EU's **Everything But Arms (EBA)** scheme gives duty-free, quota-free access; Bangladesh is its largest beneficiary. After LDC graduation it will seek **GSP+**.

## African Union (AU / আফ্রিকান ইউনিয়ন)
- Predecessor: 🔥 **Organisation of African Unity (OAU)**, founded **25 May 1963** in **Addis Ababa** (**25 May = Africa Day**).
- **AU launched 9 July 2002, Durban** (South Africa), based on the **Sirte Declaration (1999)**.
- HQ: **Addis Ababa, Ethiopia**. **55 members** (all African states). **Morocco** left the OAU in 1984 and **rejoined in 2017**.
- Some members are suspended after military coups.
- 🔥 The AU became a **permanent member of the G20** in **September 2023** (New Delhi summit).
- **Agenda 2063**; **AfCFTA** (African Continental Free Trade Area), secretariat in **Accra**; **Pan-African Parliament** in Midrand, South Africa.

## Quick revision
- SAARC: **Dhaka, 8 Dec 1985**; secretariat **Kathmandu**; **8** members; 8th **Afghanistan (2007)**; idea of **Ziaur Rahman**.
- ASEAN: **Bangkok, 8 Aug 1967**; secretariat **Jakarta**; **11** members; newest **Timor-Leste (2025)**.
- BIMSTEC: **6 June 1997**; **7** members; secretariat **Dhaka**.
- EU: **Maastricht 1992/1993**; **27** members; HQ **Brussels**; Brexit **31 Jan 2020**.
- AU: **2002, Durban**; HQ **Addis Ababa**; **55** members; OAU **1963**.
- Bangladesh joined the **ARF in 2006**.
