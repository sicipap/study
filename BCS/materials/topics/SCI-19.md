# Vitamins, minerals & deficiency diseases

> **Why it matters:** "Lack of which vitamin causes night blindness / scurvy / rickets / beriberi?" and "which mineral prevents goitre / anaemia?" are asked in almost every job exam. Learn the tables below by heart.

## Vitamins: basics 🔥
- The word "vitamine" was coined by **Casimir Funk (1912)**. Vitamins are needed in small amounts and give **no energy (calories)**.
- **Fat-soluble: A, D, E, K** (stored in the body, mainly liver). **Water-soluble: B-complex and C** (not stored much; excess passes in urine; B₁₂ is an exception, stored in liver).
- **Vitamin C** is the vitamin **most easily destroyed by heat/cooking** and air.
- Vitamins the body can make: **D (in skin, from sunlight)** and **K (by intestinal bacteria)**.

## Vitamin deficiency table 🔥🔥🔥
| Vitamin (chemical name) | Deficiency disease / symptom | Good sources |
|---|---|---|
| **A (retinol)** | **Night blindness (রাতকানা)**, **xerophthalmia** (dry eye), skin problems | **Carrot, ripe mango, pumpkin, green leafy vegetables**, liver, **fish-liver oil**, egg yolk, milk, **mola fish (মলা মাছ)**; plants give **β-carotene (provitamin A)** |
| **B₁ (thiamine)** | **Beriberi (বেরিবেরি)** — nerve and heart problems | Whole grains, **husk of rice (unpolished/ঢেঁকিছাটা rice)**, pulses, yeast |
| **B₂ (riboflavin)** | **Cracks at the corners of the mouth, sore tongue and lips (angular stomatitis / ariboflavinosis, মুখে ঘা)** | Milk, egg, liver, green vegetables |
| **B₃ (niacin / nicotinic acid)** | **Pellagra** — the "4 Ds": dermatitis, diarrhoea, dementia, death | Meat, fish, groundnut, whole grains |
| B₅ (pantothenic acid) | Rare (burning feet) | Widely present |
| **B₆ (pyridoxine)** | Anaemia, skin problems, convulsions | Meat, fish, grains |
| B₇ (biotin) | Skin/hair problems (rare) | Egg yolk, liver |
| **B₉ (folic acid)** | **Megaloblastic anaemia**; **birth defects (neural tube)** in pregnancy | Green leafy vegetables, liver, pulses |
| **B₁₂ (cyanocobalamin)** — contains **cobalt** | **Pernicious anaemia**, nerve damage | **Only animal foods**: meat, fish, egg, milk, liver |
| **C (ascorbic acid)** | **Scurvy (স্কার্ভি)** — **bleeding and swollen gums**, slow wound healing | **Amla (আমলকী)** (very rich), **guava**, lemon, orange, green chilli, tomato |
| **D (calciferol)** | **Rickets (রিকেটস)** in children (bent legs); **osteomalacia** in adults | **Sunlight** ("**sunshine vitamin**"), **fish-liver oil**, egg yolk, milk |
| **E (tocopherol)** | **Infertility / reproductive problems**, muscle weakness | Vegetable oils, nuts, green leaves; an **antioxidant**; called the "**anti-sterility vitamin**" |
| **K (phylloquinone)** | **Delayed blood clotting**, bleeding (haemorrhage) | Green leafy vegetables; made by **gut bacteria**; newborns often given a vitamin K injection |

**Memory hooks:**
- *A for eyes (রাতকানা); B₁ Beriberi; C for gums (scurvy); D for bones (rickets); K for Klotting.*
- **James Lind (1747)** showed that citrus fruit (lemon) prevents scurvy in sailors.
- Bangladesh runs a national **Vitamin A capsule** campaign for children (6–59 months) to prevent night blindness.

## Minerals (খনিজ লবণ) 🔥🔥
| Mineral | Role | Deficiency | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Calcium (Ca)** | **Bones and teeth**, blood clotting, muscle action | **Rickets, osteoporosis** (weak bones), tooth decay | **Milk**, small fish with bones, green vegetables |
| **Iron (Fe)** | Part of **haemoglobin** (O₂ transport) | **Anaemia (রক্তস্বল্পতা)** | Liver, meat, **spinach, kochu shak (কচু শাক)**, pulses, jaggery |
| **Iodine (I)** | Makes **thyroxine** | **Goitre (গলগণ্ড)**; **cretinism** in children | **Iodised salt**, sea fish, seaweed |
| Phosphorus (P) | Bones, teeth, **ATP**, DNA | Weak bones | Milk, meat, grains |
| **Sodium (Na)** | Fluid balance, nerve impulses | Muscle cramps, low BP (e.g. after diarrhoea/sweating) | Common salt |
| **Potassium (K)** | Nerve, muscle and **heart** function | Muscle weakness, irregular heartbeat | Banana, potato, coconut water |
| **Fluorine (F)** | Hardens tooth enamel | **Dental caries**; excess → **fluorosis** (mottled teeth) | Fluoridated water, toothpaste |
| **Zinc (Zn)** | Growth, immunity, wound healing | Stunted growth; **zinc is given with ORS in diarrhoea** | Meat, seafood, pulses |
| Magnesium (Mg) | Enzymes, muscles | Muscle cramps | Green vegetables, nuts |
| Cobalt (Co) | Part of **vitamin B₁₂** | Anaemia | Animal foods |

## Protein and energy deficiency 🔥
| Disease | Cause | Signs |
|---|---|---|
| **Kwashiorkor** | Mainly **protein** deficiency | Swollen belly and legs (**oedema**), skin/hair changes |
| **Marasmus** | **Protein + calorie (energy)** deficiency | Extreme thinness, "skin and bones" |

- Energy per gram: **carbohydrate ≈ 4 kcal, protein ≈ 4 kcal, fat ≈ 9 kcal** (fat gives the most energy).
- **Soybean** is a very rich plant protein source (≈ 40% protein). Six food groups: carbohydrate, protein, fat, vitamins, minerals, **water** (≈ 60–70% of body weight).
- A **balanced diet (সুষম খাদ্য)** contains all six in proper proportion.

## Quick revision
- **A → night blindness; B₁ → beriberi; B₃ → pellagra; C → scurvy; D → rickets; K → poor clotting.**
- **B₁₂ contains cobalt**, found only in animal foods; lack → pernicious anaemia.
- Folic acid lack → anaemia and birth defects.
- **Fat-soluble: A, D, E, K**; vitamin C is destroyed by cooking.
- **Sunshine vitamin = D**; amla and guava are rich in **vitamin C**.
- **Iron → anaemia; iodine → goitre; calcium → bones; fluorine → tooth decay.**
- **Kwashiorkor = protein lack; marasmus = protein + energy lack.**
- Fat gives 9 kcal/g, the highest.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Night blindness is caused by the deficiency of —
(a) vitamin A (b) vitamin B₁ (c) vitamin C (d) vitamin D

**2.** Scurvy is caused by the deficiency of —
(a) vitamin K (b) vitamin E (c) vitamin C (d) vitamin B₁₂

**3.** Rickets in children is due to lack of —
(a) vitamin A (b) vitamin D (c) iron (d) vitamin B₂

**4.** Which vitamin contains cobalt?
(a) Vitamin B₁ (b) Vitamin B₆ (c) Vitamin E (d) Vitamin B₁₂

**5.** Beriberi is caused by the deficiency of —
(a) thiamine (b) riboflavin (c) niacin (d) folic acid

**6.** Which of the following vitamins is water-soluble?
(a) Vitamin A (b) Vitamin D (c) Vitamin K (d) Vitamin C

**7.** Deficiency of iron in the diet causes —
(a) goitre (b) scurvy (c) anaemia (d) pellagra

**8.** Kwashiorkor is mainly caused by lack of —
(a) fat (b) protein (c) vitamin A (d) calcium

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a | Vitamin A is needed for rhodopsin in the retina |
| 2 | c | Vitamin C lack → bleeding gums (scurvy) |
| 3 | b | Vitamin D lack → soft, bent bones |
| 4 | d | B₁₂ = cyanocobalamin, contains cobalt |
| 5 | a | Vitamin B₁ (thiamine) lack → beriberi |
| 6 | d | B-complex and C are water-soluble |
| 7 | c | Iron is needed for haemoglobin |
| 8 | b | Kwashiorkor = protein deficiency |
