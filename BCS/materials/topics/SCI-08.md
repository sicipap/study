# Atom, radioactivity, fission & fusion

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask who discovered radioactivity and the subatomic particles, properties of α, β, γ rays, fission vs fusion (atom bomb vs hydrogen bomb vs the Sun), parts of a nuclear reactor, uses of isotopes and Rooppur facts.

## The nucleus and its particles 🔥
| Particle | Charge | Discovered by |
|---|---|---|
| **Electron** | −1 | **J. J. Thomson (1897)** (cathode rays) |
| **Proton** | +1 | **Ernest Rutherford** (1919; Goldstein saw anode/canal rays in 1886) |
| **Neutron** | 0 | **James Chadwick (1932)** |
| **Nucleus** | + | **Rutherford (1911)**, gold-foil (α-scattering) experiment |

- **Atomic number (Z)** = number of protons. **Mass number (A)** = protons + neutrons.
- **Isotopes (আইসোটোপ)**: same Z, different A (term by **Frederick Soddy**). Hydrogen has 3: **protium (¹H), deuterium (²H, D), tritium (³H, radioactive)**. **Heavy water = D₂O** (deuterium found by Harold Urey, 1931).
- **Isobars**: same A, different Z (e.g. ⁴⁰Ar, ⁴⁰Ca). **Isotones**: same number of neutrons.
- **Satyendra Nath Bose** (Dhaka University) developed Bose–Einstein statistics (1924); particles called **bosons** are named after him. The **Higgs boson** ("God particle") was confirmed at **CERN in 2012**.

## Radioactivity (তেজস্ক্রিয়তা) 🔥🔥
- Discovered by **Henri Becquerel (1896)** in **uranium** salts.
- **Marie and Pierre Curie** discovered **polonium and radium (1898)**; Marie Curie coined the word "radioactivity" and won Nobel Prizes in **Physics (1903)** and **Chemistry (1911)**.
- Radioactivity is a **nuclear** property; it is not affected by temperature, pressure or chemical combination.
- Elements with Z > 82 (after lead/bismuth) are generally radioactive; e.g. **uranium, thorium, radium, plutonium**.

| Ray | Nature | Charge | Penetrating power | Ionising power |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Alpha (α)** | **Helium nucleus (⁴₂He)** | +2 | **Least** (stopped by paper) | **Most** |
| **Beta (β)** | Fast **electron** | −1 | Medium (stopped by thin aluminium) | Medium |
| **Gamma (γ)** | **Electromagnetic wave** (photon) | **0** | **Most** (needs thick **lead**/concrete) | Least |

- α and β were named by **Rutherford**; γ rays were discovered by **Paul Villard (1900)**. γ rays are **not deflected** by electric or magnetic fields.
- **Half-life (অর্ধায়ু)**: time for half the nuclei to decay. **Carbon-14: ≈ 5,730 years** → **radiocarbon dating** of fossils and old wood (developed by **Willard Libby**).
- Units: **becquerel (Bq)** (SI) = 1 decay/s; **curie (Ci) = 3.7 × 10¹⁰ Bq**. Radiation dose: sievert (Sv), gray (Gy). Detector: **Geiger–Müller counter**.

## Uses of radioisotopes 🔥
| Isotope | Use |
|---|---|
| **Cobalt-60** | **Cancer treatment (radiotherapy)**, **food irradiation**/sterilising |
| **Iodine-131** | **Thyroid** diagnosis and treatment |
| **Carbon-14** | **Age determination (dating)** |
| **Uranium-235**, Plutonium-239 | Nuclear fuel, bombs |
| Phosphorus-32 | Agriculture research, blood disorders |
| Technetium-99m | Medical imaging (most used diagnostic isotope) |
| Sodium-24 | Detecting blood clots / leaks in pipes |

## Nuclear fission and fusion 🔥🔥
| | **Fission (ফিশন/বিভাজন)** | **Fusion (ফিউশন/সংযোজন)** |
|---|---|---|
| What | A heavy nucleus **splits** into two lighter nuclei | Light nuclei **join** to form a heavier nucleus |
| Example | **U-235 + slow neutron → Ba-141 + Kr-92 + 3 neutrons + ≈200 MeV** | **Hydrogen (deuterium + tritium) → Helium** + energy |
| Discovered | **Otto Hahn & Fritz Strassmann (1938)**; explained by Lise Meitner | Needs **millions of degrees** temperature |
| Where | **Atom bomb**, nuclear power plant | **Sun and stars**, **hydrogen bomb** |
| Energy per reaction | Large | **Much larger** per unit mass; little radioactive waste |

- The energy comes from **mass defect**, via **E = mc²** (Einstein).
- **Chain reaction**: neutrons released by fission split more nuclei. Uncontrolled → **atom bomb**; controlled → **reactor**.
- A hydrogen bomb needs a **fission bomb as a trigger** to reach the fusion temperature.
- **Hiroshima (6 August 1945)** — uranium bomb "Little Boy"; **Nagasaki (9 August 1945)** — plutonium bomb "Fat Man".

## Nuclear reactor (পারমাণবিক চুল্লি)
| Part | Material / job |
|---|---|
| **Fuel** | **Enriched uranium (U-235)**, or plutonium-239 |
| **Moderator** | **Heavy water (D₂O)** or **graphite** (also ordinary water) — **slows down neutrons** |
| **Control rods** | **Cadmium or boron** — **absorb neutrons** to control the reaction |
| **Coolant** | Water, heavy water, liquid sodium, CO₂ — carries heat away |
| **Shield** | Thick concrete/lead |

- First nuclear reactor: **Enrico Fermi, Chicago Pile-1 (1942)**.
- Major accidents: **Chernobyl (Ukraine, 1986)**, **Fukushima (Japan, 2011)**.
- **IAEA** (International Atomic Energy Agency): founded **1957**, HQ **Vienna**.

## Bangladesh and nuclear energy 🔥
- **Bangladesh Atomic Energy Commission (BAEC)**: established **1973**.
- **Research reactor**: **TRIGA Mark-II, 3 MW**, at **AERE, Savar** — commissioned in **1986**.
- **Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant (রূপপুর পারমাণবিক বিদ্যুৎকেন্দ্র)**: at **Ishwardi, Pabna**, on the Padma; built with **Russia (Rosatom)**; **2 units × 1,200 MW = 2,400 MW**, VVER-1200 reactors. First fuel (uranium) arrived in **October 2023**, when Bangladesh became a nuclear-fuel-holding country.

## Quick revision
- Radioactivity: **Becquerel (1896)**; radium & polonium: **the Curies (1898)**.
- Electron **Thomson**, proton **Rutherford**, neutron **Chadwick (1932)**, nucleus **Rutherford (1911)**.
- **α = helium nucleus** (least penetrating); **γ = EM wave**, no charge, most penetrating.
- **C-14 half-life ≈ 5,730 years** → dating.
- **Fission → atom bomb & reactors; fusion → Sun and hydrogen bomb.**
- Moderator **heavy water/graphite**; control rods **cadmium/boron**.
- Cobalt-60: cancer therapy; Iodine-131: thyroid.
- Rooppur: Pabna, Russia, 2 × 1,200 MW.
