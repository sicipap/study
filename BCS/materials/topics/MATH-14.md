# Pipes & cisterns

> **Why it matters:** Pipes and cisterns (নল ও চৌবাচ্চা) is "work and time" with a twist: a filling pipe does positive work, an emptying pipe or leak does negative work. It appears in bank and 9th–20th grade papers and sometimes in BCS.

## Key concepts
- **Inlet (পূরণকারী নল):** fills the tank. If it fills in x hours, its work per hour = **+1/x**.
- **Outlet / leak (নির্গমন নল / ছিদ্র):** empties the tank. If it empties a full tank in y hours, its work per hour = **−1/y**.
- Net work per hour when several pipes are open = sum of + and − parts.
- If the net is positive, the tank fills; if negative, it empties.
- A "tank 1/3 full" means only 2/3 remains to be filled.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Situation | Time |
|---|---|
| 🔥 Two inlets, x and y hours | xy/(x + y) |
| 🔥 Inlet x hours, outlet y hours (y > x) | **xy/(y − x)** to fill |
| Three pipes (x, y fill; z empties) | 1 ÷ (1/x + 1/y − 1/z) |
| Pipe fills in x hours; with a leak takes y hours; leak alone empties in | xy/(y − x) |
| A is k times as fast as B; together T | B alone = (k + 1)T; A alone = (k + 1)T/k |
| Part of the tank already full (f) | Time = (1 − f) × full time |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours and pipe B in 12 hours. How long do they take together?
Steps: 6 × 12/(6 + 12) = 72/18 = 4.
**Answer: 4 hours**

**Example 2.** A pipe fills a tank in 4 hours and another empties it in 6 hours. If both are opened, when will the tank be full?
Steps: 4 × 6/(6 − 4) = 24/2 = 12.
**Answer: 12 hours**

**Example 3.** Pipes A and B fill a tank in 10 and 15 hours; pipe C empties it in 20 hours. All three are opened. Find the time to fill.
Steps: 1/10 + 1/15 − 1/20 = (6 + 4 − 3)/60 = 7/60 → 60/7 = 8 4/7 hours.
**Answer: 8 4/7 hours**

**Example 4.** A tap fills a tank in 8 hours. Because of a leak it takes 10 hours. How long will the leak take to empty the full tank?
Steps: 8 × 10/(10 − 8) = 40.
**Answer: 40 hours**

**Example 5.** Pipes A and B fill a tank in 20 and 30 minutes. Both are opened, and B is closed after 6 minutes. How long in all does it take to fill the tank?
Steps: In 6 minutes: 6 × (1/20 + 1/30) = 1/2 filled. A fills the other half in 10 minutes. Total = 6 + 10 = 16.
**Answer: 16 minutes**

## Common traps
- Adding the emptying pipe's rate instead of subtracting it.
- Forgetting the tank's starting level (half full, 1/3 full…).
- In "pipe closed after t minutes" problems, add the time before closing to the time after.
- The leak question asks the time to empty a **full** tank by the leak alone.

## Quick revision
- Inlet +1/x, outlet −1/y.
- Two inlets: xy/(x + y).
- Inlet and outlet: xy/(y − x).
- Leak alone: xy/(y − x), where y is the slower (leaky) time.
- Partly full tank: multiply by the part left.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Pipe A fills a tank in 20 minutes and pipe B in 30 minutes. Together they fill it in:
(a) 25 minutes (b) 12 minutes (c) 10 minutes (d) 15 minutes

**2.** A pipe fills a tank in 5 hours and another empties it in 10 hours. With both open, the tank fills in:
(a) 10 hours (b) 15 hours (c) 7.5 hours (d) 3 1/3 hours

**3.** Pipes A and B fill a tank in 12 and 15 hours, and pipe C empties it in 20 hours. With all three open, the tank fills in:
(a) 8 hours (b) 12 hours (c) 10 hours (d) 15 hours

**4.** A pipe fills a tank in 6 hours. Because of a leak it takes 8 hours. The leak alone can empty the full tank in:
(a) 14 hours (b) 24 hours (c) 2 hours (d) 48 hours

**5.** Two pipes fill a tank in 10 and 15 hours. When a third pipe is also opened, the tank fills in 12 hours. The third pipe alone empties the full tank in:
(a) 12 hours (b) 6 hours (c) 30 hours (d) 20 hours

**6.** A pipe fills a tank in 12 minutes. The tank is already 1/3 full. The time needed to fill it is:
(a) 4 minutes (b) 6 minutes (c) 9 minutes (d) 8 minutes

**7.** Pipes A and B fill a tank in 30 and 45 minutes. Both are opened. After how many minutes should A be closed so that the tank is full in exactly 24 minutes?
(a) 14 (b) 12 (c) 16 (d) 10

**8.** A pipe fills a tank in 3 hours and another empties it in 5 hours. If both are opened on an empty tank, it fills in:
(a) 8 hours (b) 2 hours (c) 7.5 hours (d) 15 hours

**9.** Three taps fill a tank in 10, 20 and 30 hours respectively. All three together will fill it in:
(a) 5 hours (b) 5 5/11 hours (c) 6 hours (d) 20 hours

**10.** Pipe A is 3 times as fast as pipe B. Together they fill a tank in 36 minutes. B alone will fill it in:
(a) 144 minutes (b) 108 minutes (c) 48 minutes (d) 72 minutes

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | 20 × 30/50 = 12 |
| 2 | a | 5 × 10/(10 − 5) = 10 |
| 3 | c | (5 + 4 − 3)/60 = 1/10 → 10 hours |
| 4 | b | 6 × 8/(8 − 6) = 24 |
| 5 | a | 1/10 + 1/15 − 1/12 = 1/12 → emptying pipe = 1/12 |
| 6 | d | 2/3 × 12 = 8 |
| 7 | a | t/30 + 24/45 = 1 → t/30 = 7/15 → t = 14 |
| 8 | c | 3 × 5/(5 − 3) = 7.5 |
| 9 | b | (6 + 3 + 2)/60 = 11/60 → 60/11 = 5 5/11 |
| 10 | a | A = 3x, B = x; 4x = 1/36 → x = 1/144 |
