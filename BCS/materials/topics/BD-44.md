# Foreign policy & Rohingya crisis

> **Why it matters:** The core principle of foreign policy, recognition dates of key countries, major treaties with India, and Rohingya facts (dates, camps, ICJ case, Bhasan Char) are asked in BCS preliminary, written and viva.

## Principles of foreign policy 🔥🔥
- Guiding motto: **"সকলের সাথে বন্ধুত্ব, কারো সাথে বৈরিতা নয়" (Friendship to all, malice towards none)**.
- **Article 25** of the Constitution sets the principles: respect for national sovereignty and equality, **non-interference** in internal affairs, **peaceful settlement** of disputes, respect for **international law and the UN Charter**, support for oppressed peoples, and **general and complete disarmament**.
- Other themes: economic diplomacy, labour migration and remittance, climate diplomacy, UN peacekeeping, and a **balanced relationship** with major powers.
- The **Ministry of Foreign Affairs** is at Segunbagicha, Dhaka; diplomats are trained at the **Foreign Service Academy**.

## Rohingya crisis (রোহিঙ্গা সংকট) 🔥🔥
### Background
- The **Rohingya** are a mostly Muslim minority from **Rakhine State (old name Arakan)** of **Myanmar**.
- Myanmar's **1982 Citizenship Law** did not recognise them, leaving them **stateless**.
- Bangladesh officially calls them **"Forcibly Displaced Myanmar Nationals" (FDMN)**, not refugees, because it is not a party to the 1951 Refugee Convention.

### Waves of influx
| Year | About |
|---|---|
| **1978** | About 2 lakh (most returned) |
| **1991–92** | About 2.5 lakh |
| **From 25 August 2017** | About **7 lakh+** fled a military crackdown — the largest influx |

- Today about **1.1–1.3 million** Rohingya live in Bangladesh.
- Camps: **Ukhiya and Teknaf upazilas, Cox's Bazar**. **Kutupalong** is described as the **world's largest refugee camp**.
- **Bhasan Char (ভাসানচর), Noakhali:** relocation began in **December 2020**; built with the Bangladesh Navy.
- **Repatriation Arrangement** with Myanmar: signed **23 November 2017** (Naypyidaw). No large-scale repatriation has happened so far.

### International justice
- **ICJ (International Court of Justice):** **The Gambia v. Myanmar** genocide case filed in **November 2019** (on behalf of the OIC); **provisional measures ordered on 23 January 2020**.
- **ICC (International Criminal Court):** authorised an investigation in **November 2019** (crimes partly committed on Bangladeshi soil, since Bangladesh is an ICC member). In **November 2024** the ICC Prosecutor sought an **arrest warrant for Myanmar's military chief Min Aung Hlaing**.
- **Argentina** also opened a universal-jurisdiction case.

### Recent situation (as of 2025)
- Fighting between Myanmar's army and the **Arakan Army** has put most of Rakhine, including the border area, under Arakan Army control; new arrivals entered Bangladesh in **2024–25**.
- Funding cuts to humanitarian aid have reduced food rations in the camps.
- The UN held a **high-level conference on the Rohingya** during the UN General Assembly in **September 2025**.

## Quick revision
- Motto: **Friendship to all, malice towards none**; **Article 25**.
- First to recognise: **Bhutan** (6 Dec 1971); Pakistan **22 Feb 1974**; China **31 Aug 1975**.
- Friendship Treaty with India: **19 March 1972**.
- Rohingya home: **Rakhine**; stateless under the **1982 law**.
- Biggest influx from **25 August 2017**; camps in **Ukhiya & Teknaf**.
- Bhasan Char relocation from **Dec 2020**.
- ICJ case: **The Gambia v. Myanmar (2019)**.
