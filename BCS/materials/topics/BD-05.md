# Plassey 1757, Buxar, Dewani

> **Why it matters:** Plassey (23 June 1757) is one of the most-asked dates in all exams. Also asked: the conspirators, Buxar (1764), the Dewani grant (1765), dual government and the famine of 1770 (ছিয়াত্তরের মন্বন্তর).

## Siraj-ud-Daulah (সিরাজউদ্দৌলা)
- Grandson of **Alivardi Khan**; became Nawab in **April 1756** at a young age (about 23).
- Enemies inside the family: his aunt **ঘসেটি বেগম** and cousin **শওকত জং** (Nawab of Purnia).
- Conflict with the English: they fortified **Calcutta (Fort William)** without permission, misused trade permits (দস্তক) and sheltered **কৃষ্ণদাস**, a fugitive.
- Siraj captured the **Kasimbazar** factory and **Calcutta (June 1756)** and renamed Calcutta **"আলীনগর"**.
- **অন্ধকূপ হত্যা (Black Hole tragedy):** a story of English prisoners dying in a small room, told by **হলওয়েল**. Historians consider it **greatly exaggerated or invented**.
- **Clive** and **Admiral Watson** retook Calcutta (January 1757); Siraj had to sign the **Treaty of Alinagar (February 1757)**.

## Battle of Plassey (পলাশীর যুদ্ধ) 🔥🔥
- **Date: 23 June 1757.** Place: **পলাশীর আম্রকানন**, on the bank of the **ভাগীরথী** (Nadia district, West Bengal).
- English commander: **রবার্ট ক্লাইভ**.
- Result: the Nawab's army lost because of **betrayal**, not strength. This began British rule in Bengal (and later India).

| Side | People |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Conspirators (traitors)** | **মীর জাফর** (commander-in-chief/প্রধান সেনাপতি), **জগৎ শেঠ**, **রায় দুর্লভ**, **উমিচাঁদ (আমির চাঁদ)**, **ইয়ার লতিফ**, **ঘসেটি বেগম** |
| **Loyal to Siraj** | **মীর মদন** (killed in battle), **মোহনলাল**, French officer **সিনফ্রে** |

- Siraj fled but was captured and **killed on 2 July 1757** by **মোহাম্মদী বেগ** on the orders of Mir Jafar's son **মীরন**.

## After Plassey: puppet Nawabs
| Nawab | Period | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **মীর জাফর** | 1757–1760 | Puppet of the English; gave huge sums of money and land to the Company |
| 🔥 **মীর কাসিম** | 1760–1763 | Able ruler; moved capital to **মুঙ্গের (Munger)**; abolished internal duty for everyone, which angered the English; fought them |
| **মীর জাফর** (again) | 1763–1765 | Restored by the English |
| **নাজমুদ্দৌলা** | 1765 onward | Nawab only in name |

- **Robert Clive** became the **first Governor of Bengal (1758)**.

## Battle of Buxar (বক্সারের যুদ্ধ) 🔥
- **Date: 22 October 1764.** Place: Buxar, Bihar.
- **Allies (against the English):** **মীর কাসিম** + **সুজাউদ্দৌলা** (Nawab of Awadh/অযোধ্যা) + Mughal emperor **দ্বিতীয় শাহ আলম**.
- English commander: **হেক্টর মনরো**. The English won.
- Plassey was won by treachery; **Buxar was a real military victory**, and it made the English the true power of north-east India.

## The Dewani (দেওয়ানি লাভ) 🔥
- **Treaty of Allahabad, 12 August 1765:** Emperor **Shah Alam II** granted the **Dewani (right to collect revenue)** of **বাংলা, বিহার ও উড়িষ্যা** to the East India Company.
- In return the Company paid the emperor **26 lakh rupees a year**.

## Dual government (দ্বৈত শাসন)
- Introduced by **Clive** in **1765**: the Company held the **Dewani (revenue)**, while the Nawab kept the **Nizamat (administration, law and order)** but had no money or power. Power without responsibility for the Company; responsibility without power for the Nawab.
- 🔥 Result: the **ছিয়াত্তরের মন্বন্তর** — the great famine of **1770** (Bangla year **১১৭৬**). About **one-third** of Bengal's people died.
- **Warren Hastings** abolished dual government in **1772**.
- Under the **Regulating Act 1773**, Warren Hastings became the **first Governor-General (of Bengal)** in **1774**.

## Quick revision
- Plassey: **23 June 1757**, mango grove on the **Bhagirathi**.
- Chief traitor: **Mir Jafar**, the Nawab's commander-in-chief.
- Loyal commanders: **Mir Madan, Mohanlal**, Frenchman **Sinfray**.
- Siraj killed by **Muhammadi Beg** (2 July 1757).
- Calcutta renamed **Alinagar** by Siraj.
- Buxar: **22 October 1764**; Mir Qasim + Shuja-ud-Daulah + Shah Alam II vs **Hector Munro**.
- Dewani: **12 August 1765**, from **Shah Alam II**; 26 lakh a year.
- Dual government: **Clive (1765)**; ended by **Warren Hastings (1772)**.
- Famine of 1770 = **ছিয়াত্তরের মন্বন্তর** (1176 BS).
