# South China Sea, Taiwan, Indo-Pacific

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for the South China Sea claimants, the "nine-dash line", the 2016 arbitration award, the Spratly and Paracel islands, the "One China" policy and Resolution 2758, and the Indo-Pacific concept (who coined it, Bangladesh's Indo-Pacific Outlook).

## South China Sea (দক্ষিণ চীন সাগর) 🔥
- A marginal sea of the **Pacific Ocean**, about **3.5 million sq km**, connected to the Indian Ocean through the **Strait of Malacca**.
- Why it matters: about **one-third of global shipping** passes through it; rich **fishing grounds**; possible **oil and gas**.
- 🔥 **Claimants: China, Taiwan, Vietnam, the Philippines, Malaysia and Brunei.** (Indonesia is not a claimant to the islands, but China's line overlaps Indonesia's EEZ near the **Natuna Islands**.)
- 🔥 **Nine-dash line (নাইন-ড্যাশ লাইন):** China's U-shaped claim covering about **90%** of the sea; it came from an **eleven-dash line** drawn by the Republic of China in **1947**. China's 2023 map shows a **ten-dash line** (adding a dash east of Taiwan).

### Disputed features
| Feature | Claimants / status |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Spratly Islands** (Nansha) | China, Taiwan, Vietnam **(all)**; Philippines, Malaysia, Brunei **(some)** |
| 🔥 **Paracel Islands** (Xisha) | China (controls since **1974**) vs Vietnam (and Taiwan) |
| **Scarborough Shoal** | China vs Philippines; China has controlled it since a **2012** stand-off |
| **Second Thomas Shoal** | Philippines keeps marines on a grounded ship, **BRP Sierra Madre** (since 1999) |
| **Mischief, Fiery Cross, Subi reefs** | Turned into **artificial islands** with airstrips by China (from about **2013**) |

### The 2016 arbitration 🔥
- 🔥 **Philippines v. China**: case filed in **2013** under **UNCLOS**; heard by an arbitral tribunal with the **Permanent Court of Arbitration (PCA), The Hague**.
- 🔥 **Award: 12 July 2016** — the **nine-dash line has no legal basis** under UNCLOS; China's island-building harmed the environment; none of the Spratly features can generate a 200-nm EEZ.
- **China rejected** the award ("null and void").
- ASEAN and China signed a **Declaration on the Conduct of Parties (DOC)** in **2002**; talks on a binding **Code of Conduct (COC)** are still going on.
- The **USA** carries out **Freedom of Navigation Operations (FONOPs)** and does not accept China's claims.
- Local names: Philippines — "**West Philippine Sea**"; Vietnam — "**East Sea**".

## Taiwan (তাইওয়ান) 🔥
- Official name: 🔥 **Republic of China (ROC)**; capital **Taipei**; currency **New Taiwan Dollar**; parliament **Legislative Yuan**.
- After losing the civil war, **Chiang Kai-shek** and the **Kuomintang (KMT)** moved the ROC government to Taiwan in **1949**; the **People's Republic of China (PRC)** was proclaimed on 1 October 1949.
- 🔥 **UNGA Resolution 2758 (25 October 1971):** the **PRC replaced the ROC** in the UN and on the Security Council.
- 🔥 **"One China" principle (PRC):** there is only one China and Taiwan is part of it. **"One China" policy (USA and others):** recognise the PRC as the government of China and keep unofficial ties with Taiwan.
- **USA:** Shanghai Communiqué (**1972**, Nixon's visit); diplomatic ties with the PRC from **1 January 1979**; the **Taiwan Relations Act (1979)** allows arms sales to Taiwan.
- **Diplomatic allies of Taiwan:** only about **12** states (as of 2025), e.g. **Paraguay, Guatemala, Eswatini, the Holy See, Marshall Islands, Palau, Tuvalu**. **Nauru** switched to the PRC in **January 2024**.
- Leaders (as of early 2026): President 🔥 **Lai Ching-te** (DPP), since **20 May 2024**. **Tsai Ing-wen** (2016–2024) was Taiwan's **first woman president**.
- Taiwan's **TSMC** makes most of the world's most advanced **semiconductors**, which adds to Taiwan's strategic importance.
- **Taiwan Strait crises:** 1954–55, 1958, 1995–96; large Chinese military drills after US Speaker **Nancy Pelosi's visit (August 2022)** and in later years.
- 🔥 **Bangladesh follows the "One China" policy.**

## Indo-Pacific (ইন্দো-প্যাসিফিক) 🔥
- The region linking the **Indian Ocean and the Pacific Ocean**, from East Africa to the US west coast.
- 🔥 Japan's PM **Shinzo Abe** gave the idea in his "**Confluence of the Two Seas**" speech to the **Indian Parliament (August 2007)**; he launched the "**Free and Open Indo-Pacific (FOIP)**" vision in **2016**.
- The USA renamed **Pacific Command** as **Indo-Pacific Command (INDOPACOM)** in **May 2018**.
- **IPEF** (Indo-Pacific Economic Framework), launched by the USA in **May 2022** with **14** partners (Bangladesh is not a member).
- **ASEAN Outlook on the Indo-Pacific:** 2019.
- Related groups: **QUAD** and **AUKUS** (see INT-11). China sees the Indo-Pacific strategy as an effort to contain it.
- **India:** **SAGAR** (Security and Growth for All in the Region, 2015).

### Bangladesh's Indo-Pacific Outlook 🔥
- 🔥 Released in **April 2023**; it has **4 guiding principles** and **15 objectives**.
- Key ideas: a **free, open, peaceful, secure and inclusive** Indo-Pacific; based on the UN Charter and **UNCLOS**; "**friendship towards all, malice towards none**"; no military alliance; focus on the **blue economy**, trade and connectivity.
- Bangladesh sits at the head of the **Bay of Bengal**, a key part of the Indo-Pacific.

## Other related terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **String of Pearls** | Theory that China is building a chain of ports around India (e.g. **Gwadar, Hambantota, Kyaukphyu**) |
| **Malacca Dilemma** | China's dependence on the **Strait of Malacca** for oil imports (term linked to **Hu Jintao**, 2003) |
| **Grey-zone tactics** | Pressure below the level of war, e.g. coast-guard and militia boats |

## Quick revision
- SCS claimants: **China, Taiwan, Vietnam, Philippines, Malaysia, Brunei**.
- **Nine-dash line**: China's claim; from the ROC's 1947 eleven-dash line.
- **Spratly** (many claimants) and **Paracel** (China vs Vietnam) islands.
- **PCA award, 12 July 2016** (Philippines v. China): nine-dash line has no legal basis.
- Taiwan = **Republic of China**; PRC took the UN seat by **Resolution 2758 (1971)**.
- Taiwan's president: **Lai Ching-te** (since May 2024).
- Indo-Pacific idea: **Shinzo Abe (2007)**; Bangladesh's Indo-Pacific Outlook: **April 2023**.
