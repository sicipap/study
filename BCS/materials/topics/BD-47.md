# Bangladesh current affairs (last 12 months)

> **Why it matters:** Every exam has 3–6 current-affairs questions (latest budget, office-holders, awards, sports results, big project openings). This note gives the confirmed background and a checklist; you must fill in the newest facts yourself from newspapers and a monthly current-affairs magazine.

## How to study current affairs
- Read one national daily (Bangla or English) every day; note only **names, dates, numbers, places, "first"**.
- Keep a one-page table per month. Revise it every Friday.
- Exams usually ask about events **3–12 months before** the exam date.
- Always write the **source and date** next to a figure; numbers are revised often.
