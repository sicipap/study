# UN Secretaries-General & peacekeeping

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for the order and nationality of the Secretaries-General, "firsts" (first Asian, first African, died in office, Nobel winners), how the SG is appointed, the first peacekeeping mission, and Bangladesh's role as a leading peacekeeper.

## The Secretary-General (মহাসচিব)
- 🔥 Appointed by the **General Assembly** on the **recommendation of the Security Council** (**Article 97**). In practice, any P5 member can block a candidate.
- Term: **5 years** (by convention; not fixed in the Charter), usually renewable once.
- Described as the UN's **"chief administrative officer"** (Article 97).
- Trygve Lie told Dag Hammarskjöld it was "**the most impossible job on this earth**".
- 🔥 **No woman** has yet been Secretary-General (as of 2026).

| 3 | 🔥 **U Thant** | **Burma (Myanmar)** | 1961–1971 | **First Asian** SG |
| 4 | **Kurt Waldheim** | **Austria** | 1972–1981 | |
| 5 | **Javier Pérez de Cuéllar** | **Peru** | 1982–1991 | First from **Latin America** |

| 7 | 🔥 **Kofi Annan** | **Ghana** | 1997–2006 | First from **sub-Saharan Africa** and from UN staff; **Nobel Peace 2001** jointly with the **UN** |

- **Deputy Secretary-General** (post created 1997): **Amina J. Mohammed** (Nigeria), since 2017.
- 🔥 **Selection of the 10th SG** takes place in **2026**; the winner takes office on **1 January 2027**.

## UN peacekeeping (শান্তিরক্ষা মিশন) 🔥
- Peacekeeping is **not mentioned by name in the Charter**. Hammarskjöld called it **"Chapter Six and a Half"** (between peaceful settlement, Ch. VI, and enforcement, Ch. VII).
- Missions are set up by the **Security Council**. Peacekeepers wear **blue helmets/berets** ("Blue Helmets").
- Principles: **consent of the parties, impartiality, and no use of force except in self-defence and defence of the mandate**.
- 🔥 **First mission: UNTSO** (UN Truce Supervision Organization), **May 1948**, Middle East (HQ **Jerusalem**); still running.
- 🔥 **29 May** is the **International Day of UN Peacekeepers** (the day UNTSO began in 1948).
- **First armed peacekeeping force: UNEF I (1956)** during the **Suez Crisis**, proposed by Canada's **Lester B. Pearson** (Nobel Peace 1957).
- **UNMOGIP (1949):** monitors the India–Pakistan ceasefire in **Kashmir**; one of the oldest missions.
- 🔥 **UN Peacekeeping Forces** won the **Nobel Peace Prize in 1988**.
- Run by the **Department of Peace Operations (DPO)**, called DPKO until 2019.

## Bangladesh in peacekeeping 🔥
- 🔥 First participation: **1988**, with **15 army officers** in **UNIIMOG** (UN Iran–Iraq Military Observer Group). Next came **UNTAG, Namibia (1989)**.
- Bangladesh is consistently **one of the largest troop- and police-contributing countries**, often ranked **1st or 2nd** in the world.
- Bangladeshi peacekeepers have served in **40+ countries** and many have lost their lives on duty.
- 🔥 **Sierra Leone** honoured Bangladeshi peacekeepers: President **Ahmad Tejan Kabbah** declared **Bangla an honorary official language** (**December 2002**).
- Bangladesh has also sent **all-women police units** and many female peacekeepers.

> 🔄 Update: check the latest figure in a newspaper or the Bangladesh Economic Review. Also check Bangladesh's current rank among troop contributors (the UN publishes monthly figures).

## Quick revision
- SG appointed by **GA on SC recommendation** (Art. 97); 5-year term by convention.
- First SG **Trygve Lie** (Norway); first Asian **U Thant** (Burma); first African **Boutros-Ghali** (Egypt).
- **Hammarskjöld** died in office (1961) and got a posthumous Nobel.
- **Kofi Annan** and the UN: Nobel Peace **2001**.
- Current SG **António Guterres** (Portugal), 9th SG, term ends **31 Dec 2026**.
- First peacekeeping mission **UNTSO (1948)**; Peacekeepers' Day **29 May**; Nobel **1988**.
- Bangladesh's first mission: **UNIIMOG, 1988**; Bangla honorary official language of **Sierra Leone (2002)**.
