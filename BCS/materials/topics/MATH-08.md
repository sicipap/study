# Compound interest

> **Why it matters:** Compound interest (চক্রবৃদ্ধি মুনাফা) is a favourite of bank recruitment and BCS papers, especially the "difference between CI and SI" shortcut, half-yearly compounding and population growth.

## Key concepts
- In **compound interest**, the interest of each period is **added to the principal**, and the next interest is calculated on this larger amount. So interest grows every year.
- In the first year (or first period), CI = SI. From the second year, CI > SI.
- **Compound amount (চক্রবৃদ্ধি মূলধন, A)** = P + CI.
- Population growth and depreciation (value decreasing every year) use the same formula.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| 🔥 Amount | **A = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ** |
| Compound interest | CI = A − P = P[(1 + r/100)ⁿ − 1] |
| Half-yearly compounding | rate r/2, time 2n |
| Quarterly compounding | rate r/4, time 4n |
| Depreciation / decrease | A = P(1 − r/100)ⁿ |
| 🔥 CI − SI for **2 years** | P(r/100)² |
| CI − SI for **3 years** | P(r/100)²(3 + r/100) |
| Amounts A₁ (year n) and A₂ (year n + 1) | r = (A₂ − A₁)/A₁ × 100 |
| Sum doubles in n years (CI) | becomes 4 times in 2n, 8 times in 3n years |
| Handy values at 10% | 1.1² = 1.21, 1.1³ = 1.331 |
| Handy values at 5% | 1.05² = 1.1025, 1.05³ = 1.157625 |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Find the compound interest on Tk 10,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years.
Steps: A = 10000 × 1.21 = 12,100. CI = 2,100.
**Answer: Tk 2,100**

**Example 2.** Find the difference between CI and SI on Tk 8,000 at 5% for 2 years.
Steps: P(r/100)² = 8000 × (0.05)² = 8000 × 0.0025 = 20.
**Answer: Tk 20**

**Example 3.** Find the CI on Tk 5,000 for 1 year at 8% per annum, compounded half-yearly.
Steps: Rate 4% per half-year, 2 periods. A = 5000 × 1.04² = 5,408. CI = 408.
**Answer: Tk 408**

**Example 4.** A sum amounts to Tk 2,420 in 2 years and Tk 2,662 in 3 years at compound interest. Find the rate and the principal.
Steps: r = (2662 − 2420)/2420 × 100 = 242/2420 × 100 = 10%. P = 2420 ÷ 1.21 = 2,000.
**Answer: 10%, Tk 2,000**

**Example 5.** A sum doubles in 5 years at compound interest. In how many years will it become 8 times?
Steps: 8 = 2³ → 3 × 5 = 15 years.
**Answer: 15 years**

## Common traps
- Under CI, "doubles in 5 years → 4 times in 10 years" (not 15, as SI would give for 4 times).
- For half-yearly compounding, halve the rate **and** double the time.
- CI − SI shortcut P(r/100)² works only for **2 years**.
- In the first year CI and SI are equal; a question asking "CI for 1 year" (yearly) is just SI.

## Quick revision
- A = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ.
- CI − SI (2 yrs) = P(r/100)².
- CI − SI (3 yrs) = P(r/100)²(3 + r/100).
- Half-yearly: r/2 and 2n.
- Successive amounts: rate = difference ÷ earlier amount × 100.
- 1.1² = 1.21; 1.1³ = 1.331.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The compound amount of Tk 5,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years is:
(a) Tk 6,000 (b) Tk 6,050 (c) Tk 6,500 (d) Tk 6,655

**2.** The compound interest on Tk 8,000 at 5% per annum for 2 years is:
(a) Tk 800 (b) Tk 840 (c) Tk 820 (d) Tk 8,820

**3.** The difference between CI and SI on Tk 5,000 at 4% for 2 years is:
(a) Tk 8 (b) Tk 16 (c) Tk 4 (d) Tk 80

**4.** The difference between CI and SI on a sum for 2 years at 10% is Tk 50. The sum is:
(a) Tk 500 (b) Tk 2,500 (c) Tk 10,000 (d) Tk 5,000

**5.** A sum doubles in 4 years at compound interest. It will become 8 times in:
(a) 16 years (b) 12 years (c) 32 years (d) 8 years

**6.** The CI on Tk 10,000 for 1 year at 20% per annum, compounded half-yearly, is:
(a) Tk 2,000 (b) Tk 2,200 (c) Tk 2,100 (d) Tk 2,400

**7.** A sum amounts to Tk 2,420 in 2 years and Tk 2,662 in 3 years at compound interest. The rate is:
(a) 8% (b) 12% (c) 9% (d) 10%

**8.** A town's population of 8,000 grows 10% a year. After 3 years it will be:
(a) 10,648 (b) 10,400 (c) 10,560 (d) 11,000

**9.** The difference between CI and SI on Tk 1,000 at 10% for 3 years is:
(a) Tk 30 (b) Tk 21 (c) Tk 31 (d) Tk 33.10

**10.** At what rate of compound interest will Tk 1,000 become Tk 1,210 in 2 years?
(a) 10% (b) 10.5% (c) 11% (d) 21%

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | 5000 × 1.21 = 6050 |
| 2 | c | 8000 × 1.1025 = 8820; CI = 820 |
| 3 | a | 5000 × (0.04)² = 8 |
| 4 | d | P × (0.1)² = 50 → P = 5000 |
| 5 | b | 8 = 2³ → 3 × 4 = 12 years |
| 6 | c | 10% per half-year, 2 periods: 10000 × 1.21 − 10000 = 2100 |
| 7 | d | (2662 − 2420)/2420 × 100 = 10% |
| 8 | a | 8000 × 1.331 = 10,648 |
| 9 | c | CI = 331, SI = 300 → 31 (or 1000 × 0.01 × 3.1) |
