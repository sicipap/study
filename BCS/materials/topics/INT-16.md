# Kashmir, India–Pakistan, Afghanistan

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for the Instrument of Accession (1947), the LoC and the Simla Agreement, the Indus Waters Treaty, Article 370 (revoked 2019), Kargil and Siachen, the 2025 India–Pakistan clash, the Durand Line, and the Taliban's return (August 2021).

## Kashmir dispute (কাশ্মীর সমস্যা) 🔥
- At partition (**August 1947**), princely states could join India or Pakistan. **Jammu & Kashmir** was a **Muslim-majority** state ruled by a Hindu king, **Maharaja Hari Singh**.
- In **October 1947** tribal fighters from Pakistan entered Kashmir. 🔥 Hari Singh signed the **Instrument of Accession** to India on **26 October 1947**.
- **First India–Pakistan war (1947–48).** India took the issue to the **UN (1 January 1948)**. 🔥 **UNSC Resolution 47 (April 1948)** called for a **plebiscite** (never held).
- Ceasefire on **1 January 1949**; **UNMOGIP** (UN observers) set up in 1949.

### Who controls what
| Area | Controlled by |
|---|---|
| **Jammu & Kashmir** and **Ladakh** | **India** (union territories since 2019) |
| 🔥 **Azad Jammu & Kashmir** (capital **Muzaffarabad**) and **Gilgit-Baltistan** | **Pakistan** |
| 🔥 **Aksai Chin** | **China** (claimed by India) |
| **Shaksgam Valley** | Ceded by **Pakistan to China** in **1963** (India disputes it) |

- 🔥 **Line of Control (LoC):** the de facto border between Indian- and Pakistani-held Kashmir; the 1949 ceasefire line was renamed the **LoC by the Simla Agreement (1972)**.
- **Line of Actual Control (LAC):** the India–China de facto border.
- 🔥 **Siachen Glacier:** India took control in **1984 (Operation Meghdoot)**; called the **world's highest battlefield**.

### Article 370 🔥
- **Article 370** of India's constitution gave **special status** to Jammu & Kashmir (with **Article 35A**).
- 🔥 India **revoked it on 5 August 2019**; the state was split into two **union territories**, **Jammu & Kashmir** and **Ladakh** (from 31 October 2019).
- India's **Supreme Court upheld** the move in **December 2023**.

## India–Pakistan wars and agreements 🔥
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1947–48 | First Kashmir war |
| 🔥 **1960** | **Indus Waters Treaty** (Karachi, 19 September), **Nehru–Ayub Khan**, brokered by the **World Bank**: **eastern rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) to India; western rivers (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab) to Pakistan** |
| 1965 | Second war (after Pakistan's "Operation Gibraltar") → 🔥 **Tashkent Declaration (January 1966)**, **Lal Bahadur Shastri – Ayub Khan**, mediated by the USSR (Shastri died in Tashkent) |
| 1971 | Third war, linked to the **Bangladesh Liberation War**; Pakistan surrendered in Dhaka on **16 December 1971** |
| 🔥 **1972** | **Simla Agreement (2 July)**, **Indira Gandhi – Zulfikar Ali Bhutto**: disputes to be solved **bilaterally**; ceasefire line became the **LoC** |
| May 1998 | **Nuclear tests**: India (**Pokhran-II**, 11 & 13 May), Pakistan (**Chagai**, 28 & 30 May) |
| 1999 | **Lahore Declaration** (Vajpayee–Nawaz Sharif; Vajpayee's bus journey); then the 🔥 **Kargil War (May–July 1999)** |
| 2008 | **Mumbai attacks** (26 November) |
| 2019 | **Pulwama** suicide attack (14 February) → Indian air strike at **Balakot** (26 February) |
| 2019 | **Kartarpur Corridor** opened for Sikh pilgrims |
| 🔥 **2025** | **Pahalgam attack (22 April 2025)**: 26 people, mostly tourists, killed. India put the **Indus Waters Treaty "in abeyance"**. 🔥 **Operation Sindoor (7 May 2025)**: Indian strikes on Pakistan; Pakistan retaliated; **ceasefire on 10 May 2025**. Pakistan's army chief **Asim Munir** was made **Field Marshal** |

## Afghanistan (আফগানিস্তান) 🔥
- Capital **Kabul**; currency **Afghani**; landlocked; SAARC's 8th member (2007).
- Border with Pakistan: 🔥 the **Durand Line (1893)**, drawn by **Sir Mortimer Durand** with **Amir Abdur Rahman Khan**; Afghanistan has **never formally accepted** it.
- Independence from British control: **19 August 1919** (after the Third Anglo-Afghan War).

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **December 1979** | **Soviet invasion**; US-backed **mujahideen** resistance |
| 1988–89 | **Geneva Accords (1988)**; last Soviet troops left on **15 February 1989** |
| 🔥 **1996–2001** | **First Taliban rule** (leader **Mullah Mohammad Omar**); sheltered **al-Qaeda / Osama bin Laden** |
| 🔥 **October 2001** | After **9/11**, US-led invasion ("**Operation Enduring Freedom**"); Taliban overthrown; **Bonn Agreement** (December 2001); **Hamid Karzai** leader, later president |
| 2014–2021 | President **Ashraf Ghani** |
| 🔥 **29 February 2020** | **Doha Agreement** between the **USA and the Taliban**: US troop withdrawal |
| 🔥 **15 August 2021** | **Taliban took Kabul**; Ghani fled; US withdrawal completed **30 August 2021** |
| After 2021 | "**Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan**"; supreme leader **Hibatullah Akhundzada**; girls barred from secondary school and university |
| 🔥 **July 2025** | **Russia** became the **first country to formally recognise** the Taliban government |
| August–October 2025 | Deadly **Kunar earthquake** (31 August); **Afghanistan–Pakistan border clashes** (October), followed by talks mediated by **Qatar and Türkiye** |

- **Wakhan Corridor:** a narrow strip of Afghanistan that separates **Tajikistan** from **Pakistan** and touches **China**.

## Quick revision
- Instrument of Accession: **26 October 1947**, **Maharaja Hari Singh**.
- UNSC Resolution **47 (1948)**: plebiscite; LoC named by the **Simla Agreement (1972)**.
- Indus Waters Treaty **1960** (World Bank); Tashkent Declaration **1966**.
- Article 370 revoked **5 August 2019**; Kargil War **1999**; Siachen **1984**.
- Pahalgam attack **22 April 2025** → **Operation Sindoor** (7 May) → ceasefire **10 May 2025**.
- Durand Line **1893**; Soviet invasion **1979**; Doha Agreement **29 Feb 2020**; Taliban took Kabul **15 Aug 2021**.
- First country to recognise the Taliban government: **Russia (2025)**.
