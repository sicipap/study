# IT companies, founders & inventions

> **Why it matters:** "Who founded…", "Which company owns…", "Who invented…" and "Where is the headquarters…" questions appear in every exam. Learn the founder–company–year triplets from the tables.

## Big tech companies 🔥
| Company | Founder(s) | Year | Headquarters / key fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Microsoft** | **Bill Gates & Paul Allen** | **1975** | Redmond, Washington, USA; Windows, Office, Azure |
| 🔥 **Apple** | **Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak** & Ronald Wayne | **1976** | Cupertino, California; iPhone (2007), Mac |

- 🔥 **Moore's Law** (Gordon Moore, 1965): the number of transistors on a chip **doubles about every two years**.
- **Silicon Valley** is in **California, USA** — centre of the tech industry.

## Quick revision
- Microsoft: **Bill Gates & Paul Allen (1975)**.
- Apple: **Steve Jobs & Steve Wozniak (1976)**.
- Google: **Larry Page & Sergey Brin (1998)**; parent **Alphabet**.
- Facebook: **Mark Zuckerberg (2004)**; now **Meta**.
- YouTube (2005) co-founder **Jawed Karim** has Bangladeshi roots; Google owns YouTube.
- WhatsApp and Instagram belong to **Meta**; LinkedIn to **Microsoft**.
- Intel: **Robert Noyce & Gordon Moore**; **Moore's Law**.
- Mouse: **Douglas Engelbart**; telephone: **Graham Bell**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Who founded Microsoft together with Bill Gates?
(a) Steve Jobs (b) Paul Allen (c) Larry Ellison (d) Michael Dell

**2.** Which company owns YouTube?
(a) Google (b) Meta (c) Microsoft (d) Amazon

**3.** Jawed Karim, who has Bangladeshi roots, is a co-founder of:
(a) Facebook (b) Twitter (c) YouTube (d) Hotmail

**4.** Who invented the computer mouse?
(a) Ted Hoff (b) Charles Babbage (c) Bill Hewlett (d) Douglas Engelbart

**5.** WhatsApp was bought in 2014 by:
(a) Google (b) Microsoft (c) Facebook (d) Apple

**6.** Moore's Law is associated with which company's co-founder?
(a) Intel (b) IBM (c) Apple (d) HP

**7.** Huawei is a technology company of:
(a) Japan (b) China (c) South Korea (d) Taiwan

**8.** Who founded Amazon?
(a) Jack Ma (b) Larry Page (c) Jeff Bezos (d) Elon Musk

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Gates and Allen founded Microsoft in 1975 |
| 2 | a | Google bought YouTube in 2006 |
| 3 | c | YouTube was founded by Hurley, Chen and Karim |
| 4 | d | Engelbart invented the mouse in the 1960s |
| 5 | c | Facebook (now Meta) bought WhatsApp in 2014 |
| 6 | a | Gordon Moore co-founded Intel |
| 7 | b | Founded by Ren Zhengfei in China, 1987 |
| 8 | c | Jeff Bezos, 1994 |
