## Olympic Games (অলিম্পিক) 🔥
- 🔥 **Modern Olympics began in 1896, Athens**; founder **Baron Pierre de Coubertin** (France). Ancient Olympics: 776 BCE, Olympia.
- 🔥 **Five rings** = five inhabited continents; motto "**Faster, Higher, Stronger – Together**" (Citius, Altius, Fortius – Communiter; "Together" added 2021).
- **23 June = Olympic Day.** Held every **4 years**.

- 🔥 **First ODI World Cup: 1975, England**; winner **West Indies**. 🔥 **Australia** has the most ODI World Cup titles (**6**).

- **Bangladesh milestones:** 🔥 won the **ICC Trophy 1997** (Kuala Lumpur) → ODI status; 🔥 **Test status on 26 June 2000** (10th Test nation); **first Test: 10 November 2000 vs India, Dhaka**. Bangladesh women won the **Asia Cup T20 in 2018**, and Bangladesh won the **U-19 World Cup in 2020** (South Africa).
