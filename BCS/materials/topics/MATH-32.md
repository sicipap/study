# Mensuration: circle & sector

> **Why it matters:** Circle area and circumference (বৃত্তের ক্ষেত্রফল ও পরিধি), sectors, rings and wheel revolutions come up in bank, primary and BCS exams. With π = 22/7 and radius 7, 14 or 21, the numbers work out neatly.

## Key concepts
- **π (pi)** = circumference ÷ diameter ≈ **22/7** or **3.1416**. It is irrational.
- **Circumference (পরিধি)** is the perimeter of a circle.
- A **sector (বৃত্তকলা)** is the "pizza slice" between two radii; its angle at the centre is θ.
- A **ring (বৃত্তাকার পথ/বলয়)** is the region between two concentric circles.
- One full turn of a wheel moves it forward by **one circumference**.
- If the radius is multiplied by k, circumference × k and area × k².

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| 🔥 Circumference | **2πr = πd** |
| 🔥 Area | **πr²** |
| Semicircle area | πr²/2 |
| Semicircle perimeter | πr + 2r |
| 🔥 Arc length (angle θ) | (θ/360) × 2πr |
| 🔥 Sector area | (θ/360) × πr² = ½ × arc × r |
| Ring area (outer R, inner r) | π(R² − r²) |
| Wheel revolutions | distance ÷ circumference |
| Area and circumference numerically equal | r = 2 |
| Radius +x% | area changes by 2x + x²/100 % |

Handy values with π = 22/7: r = 7 → C = 44, A = 154; r = 14 → C = 88, A = 616; r = 21 → C = 132, A = 1386.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** The circumference of a circle is 88 cm. Find its area.
Steps: 2πr = 88 → r = 88 × 7/44 = 14. Area = 22/7 × 196 = 616.
**Answer: 616 cm²**

**Example 2.** A sector of a circle of radius 21 cm has angle 60°. Find its arc length and area.
Steps: Arc = (60/360) × 2 × 22/7 × 21 = 132/6 = 22. Area = (60/360) × 1386 = 231.
**Answer: 22 cm and 231 cm²**

**Example 3.** A wheel of radius 35 cm covers 1.1 km. How many revolutions does it make?
Steps: Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 35 = 220 cm. 1.1 km = 110,000 cm. 110000/220 = 500.
**Answer: 500**

**Example 4.** The radius of a circle is increased by 10%. By what percent does its area increase?
Steps: 10 + 10 + 100/100 = 21%.
**Answer: 21%**

**Example 5.** Find the area of a ring with outer radius 14 cm and inner radius 7 cm.
Steps: 22/7 × (196 − 49) = 22/7 × 147 = 462.
**Answer: 462 cm²**

## Common traps
- Using the diameter in πr² — halve it first.
- Semicircle perimeter must include the diameter (πr + 2r), not just πr.
- Convert km to cm (or m) before dividing by the circumference.
- Doubling the radius gives **4 times** the area but only **2 times** the circumference.

## Quick revision
- C = 2πr; A = πr².
- Arc = θ/360 × 2πr; sector = θ/360 × πr².
- Ring = π(R² − r²).
- Revolutions = distance ÷ circumference.
- r = 7 → 44 and 154.
