# Ethnic groups (ক্ষুদ্র নৃগোষ্ঠী)

> **Why it matters:** "Which group is matrilineal?", "Where do the Santals live?", "What is Boisabi?" and CHT Peace Accord questions come up in BCS, primary and NTRCA exams regularly.

## Legal status and numbers 🔥
- **Article 23A** of the Constitution (added by the **15th Amendment, 2011**) says the state will protect the culture of **"উপজাতি, ক্ষুদ্র জাতিসত্তা, নৃ-গোষ্ঠী ও সম্প্রদায়"**.
- **Small Ethnic Groups Cultural Institutions Act, 2010 (ক্ষুদ্র নৃ-গোষ্ঠীর সাংস্কৃতিক প্রতিষ্ঠান আইন ২০১০)**.
- A government gazette (**2019**) lists **50 ethnic groups**.
- **Census 2022:** ethnic population **16,50,159** (about **1%** of the population).
- **Largest group: চাকমা (Chakma)**; second **মারমা (Marma)**. The largest group in the **plain lands** is the **সাঁওতাল (Santal)**.

- **Matrilineal groups (মাতৃসূত্রীয়): গারো ও খাসিয়া** — the most asked fact.
- **বৈসাবি (Boisabi)** = **বৈ**সু (Tripura) + **সা**ংগ্রাই (Marma) + **বি**জু (Chakma): the hill new-year festival, held at the end of Chaitra / start of Baishakh.

## Chittagong Hill Tracts (পার্বত্য চট্টগ্রাম) 🔥🔥
- Three hill districts: **Rangamati, Khagrachhari, Bandarban**.
- Three traditional **circles**: **Chakma** (Rangamati), **Bohmong** (Bandarban), **Mong** (Khagrachhari).
- **CHT Peace Accord (পার্বত্য চট্টগ্রাম শান্তিচুক্তি):** signed on **2 December 1997** between the Government (National Committee on CHT, convener **Abul Hasnat Abdullah**) and the **PCJSS (পার্বত্য চট্টগ্রাম জনসংহতি সমিতি)** led by **Jyotirindra Bodhipriya Larma (Santu Larma)**.
- Results of the accord: **Ministry of CHT Affairs (1998)**, **CHT Regional Council (1998)** (chairman Santu Larma), **CHT Land Dispute Resolution Commission (2001)**.
- **Hill District Councils** were created earlier, in **1989**.
- **Shanti Bahini** (armed wing of PCJSS) surrendered arms in **February 1998** at Khagrachhari.

## Culture and institutions
- **Small Ethnic Groups Cultural Institutes** exist at Rangamati, Bandarban, Khagrachhari, Birishiri (Netrokona), Rajshahi and elsewhere.
- **9 August:** the UN's **International Day of the World's Indigenous Peoples**. The Government of Bangladesh officially uses the term **ক্ষুদ্র নৃগোষ্ঠী**, not "adivasi".

## Quick revision
- 50 groups (2019 gazette); ethnic population **16.5 lakh** (Census 2022).
- Largest: **Chakma**; largest in plains: **Santal**.
- Matrilineal: **Garo, Khasi**.
- Boisabi = **Boisu + Sangrai + Biju**.
- Garo festival **Wangala**; Santal **Sohrai**; Manipuri **Ras**.
- Peace Accord **2 December 1997** with **PCJSS (Santu Larma)**.
- Article **23A** (15th Amendment).

(a) Marma (b) Santal (c) Chakma (d) Tripura

(a) Garo (b) Chakma (c) Marma (d) Santal

**3.** "Biju" is the festival of which group?
(a) Marma (b) Tripura (c) Garo (d) Chakma

**4.** The CHT Peace Accord was signed on —
(a) 2 December 1997 (b) 16 December 1997 (c) 2 December 1996 (d) 10 February 1998

**5.** Which ethnic group is famous for betel-leaf cultivation in Sylhet?
(a) Manipuri (b) Khasi (c) Hajong (d) Oraon

**6.** Wangala is a festival of the —
(a) Santals (b) Rakhaines (c) Garos (d) Mros

**7.** Which Article of the Constitution deals with the culture of tribes and small ethnic groups?
(a) Article 23 (b) Article 28 (c) Article 11 (d) Article 23A

**8.** The Bohmong Circle is based in —
(a) Rangamati (b) Bandarban (c) Khagrachhari (d) Cox's Bazar

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |

| 1 | c | Chakma is the largest group. |
| 2 | a | Garo (and Khasi) society is matrilineal. |
| 3 | d | Biju is the Chakma new-year festival. |

| 5 | b | Khasis grow betel leaf in Sylhet–Moulvibazar hills. |
| 6 | c | Wangala is the Garo harvest festival. |
