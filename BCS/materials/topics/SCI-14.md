# Cell & tissue

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask who discovered the cell and nucleus, the "powerhouse"/"suicide bag" of the cell, plant vs animal cell, mitosis vs meiosis, and types of plant and animal tissues (xylem/phloem, tendon/ligament).

## Discovery of the cell 🔥
| Discovery | Scientist |
|---|---|
| **Cell (dead cork cells), 1665**, book *Micrographia* | **Robert Hooke** |
| First to see **living cells** (bacteria, protozoa), 1670s | **Antonie van Leeuwenhoek** (father of microbiology) |
| **Nucleus (1831)** | **Robert Brown** |
| **Cell theory (1838–39)** | **Schleiden** (plants) and **Schwann** (animals) |
| "All cells come from pre-existing cells" (1855) | **Rudolf Virchow** |
| Term "protoplasm" | Purkinje |

- The cell is the **structural and functional unit of life**.
- **Largest cell: ostrich egg**. **Smallest cell: Mycoplasma (PPLO)**. **Longest human cell: neuron (nerve cell)**. **Largest human cell: ovum**; the **sperm** is usually cited as the smallest human cell.
- **Mature mammalian RBCs have no nucleus**.

## Prokaryotic vs eukaryotic cells
| Prokaryotic (আদিকোষ) | Eukaryotic (প্রকৃতকোষ) |
|---|---|
| **No true nucleus** (no nuclear membrane) | True nucleus with membrane |
| No membrane-bound organelles (no mitochondria, chloroplast) | Has them |
| Examples: **bacteria, blue-green algae (cyanobacteria)** | Plants, animals, fungi, protozoa |

## Cell organelles (কোষ অঙ্গাণু) 🔥🔥
| Organelle | Function / nickname |
|---|---|
| **Nucleus** | **Control centre**; contains **chromosomes (DNA)**; nucleolus makes ribosomes |
| **Mitochondria** | **"Powerhouse of the cell"**; aerobic respiration (**Krebs cycle**), makes **ATP**; has its own DNA |
| **Chloroplast** | **Photosynthesis** ("kitchen of the cell"); contains **chlorophyll**; only in plants/algae; has its own DNA |
| **Ribosome** | **Protein synthesis**; no membrane; found in all cells |
| Endoplasmic reticulum | Transport; rough ER (with ribosomes) makes proteins, smooth ER makes lipids |
| **Golgi apparatus** (Camillo Golgi) | **Packaging and secretion**; forms lysosomes |
| **Lysosome** | **"Suicide bag"**; digestive enzymes (discovered by **Christian de Duve**) |
| **Centrosome / centriole** | Helps **cell division** (spindle); mainly in **animal cells** |
| **Vacuole** | Stores cell sap; **large and central in plant cells** |
| **Plasma (cell) membrane** | **Selectively (semi-) permeable**; controls entry and exit |
| **Cell wall** | Rigid outer layer: **cellulose in plants**, **chitin in fungi**, peptidoglycan in bacteria |

### Plant cell vs animal cell 🔥
| Plant cell | Animal cell |
|---|---|
| **Cell wall present** (cellulose) | Absent |
| **Chloroplast present** | Absent |
| **Large central vacuole** | Small/temporary vacuoles |
| Centrosome usually absent | **Centrosome present** |
| Stores food as **starch** | Stores food as **glycogen** |

## Cell division (কোষ বিভাজন) 🔥
| | **Mitosis (মাইটোসিস)** | **Meiosis (মিয়োসিস)** |
|---|---|---|
| Where | **Somatic (body) cells**; growth, repair | **Reproductive cells** (to form **gametes**: sperm, egg) |
| Daughter cells | **2**, identical to parent | **4**, different |
| Chromosome number | **Same** (2n → 2n) | **Halved (2n → n)** — "reduction division" |
| Stages | Prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase | Two divisions (Meiosis I & II); crossing over |

- The term **mitosis** was given by **Walther Flemming**. **Amitosis** (direct division) occurs in bacteria/yeast.
- **Humans have 46 chromosomes (23 pairs)** in body cells; **gametes have 23**.
- Uncontrolled mitosis leads to **cancer**.
- **Diffusion**: movement of molecules from high to low concentration. **Osmosis (অভিস্রবণ)**: movement of **water** through a semi-permeable membrane from **dilute to concentrated** solution (root hairs absorb water by osmosis; raisins swell in water; salted vegetables shrink).

## Tissue (টিস্যু/কলা) 🔥
A **tissue** is a group of similar cells doing a common function. **Histology** is the study of tissues; **Marcello Malpighi** is called the father of histology (the word "tissue" was popularised by Bichat).

### Plant tissues
| Type | Kind | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Meristematic (ভাজক)** | Dividing cells at **root and shoot tips** | Growth in length/girth |
| **Parenchyma** | Simple permanent | Storage, photosynthesis |
| **Collenchyma** | Simple permanent | Flexibility, mechanical support in young stems |
| **Sclerenchyma** | Simple permanent, dead thick-walled | Strength (fibres of **jute**, coconut husk) |
| **Xylem (জাইলেম)** | Complex | Carries **water and minerals upward** from roots; wood is xylem |
| **Phloem (ফ্লোয়েম)** | Complex | Carries **food (sugar)** from leaves to all parts |

- **Jute fibre** comes from the **phloem (bast) fibres** of the jute stem.

### Animal tissues
| Type | Examples / fact |
|---|---|
| **Epithelial (আবরণী)** | Skin surface, lining of gut; protection, secretion, absorption |
| **Connective (যোজক)** | **Bone, cartilage, blood (a fluid connective tissue)**, fat (adipose), **tendon** and **ligament** |
| **Muscular (পেশি)** | **Striated/skeletal (voluntary)**, **smooth (involuntary**, stomach, intestine, blood vessels), **cardiac** (heart; involuntary, striated, never tires) |
| **Nervous (স্নায়ু)** | **Neuron** — carries impulses; longest cell |

- **Tendon** joins **muscle to bone**. **Ligament** joins **bone to bone**.
- **Stem cells** are unspecialised cells that can become many cell types (bone marrow, umbilical cord blood).

## Quick revision
- **Robert Hooke (1665)** discovered the cell; **Robert Brown (1831)** the nucleus.
- Cell theory: **Schleiden & Schwann**; "omnis cellula e cellula": Virchow.
- **Mitochondria = powerhouse** (ATP); **lysosome = suicide bag**; ribosome = protein synthesis.
- Plant cell has **cell wall (cellulose), chloroplast, large vacuole**; animal cell has centrosome.
- **Mitosis → 2 identical cells; meiosis → 4 cells with half chromosomes**.
- Humans: **46 chromosomes**; gametes 23.
- **Xylem carries water; phloem carries food**.
- **Tendon: muscle–bone; ligament: bone–bone**; blood is connective tissue.
- Largest cell ostrich egg; longest human cell neuron; bacteria are prokaryotes.

**1.** Who discovered the cell?
(a) Robert Brown (b) Robert Hooke (c) Leeuwenhoek (d) Schwann

**2.** Which organelle is called the "powerhouse of the cell"?
(a) Ribosome (b) Golgi body (c) Lysosome (d) Mitochondria

**3.** Which of the following is absent in an animal cell?
(a) Cell wall (b) Mitochondria (c) Nucleus (d) Ribosome

**4.** Which organelle is known as the "suicide bag"?
(a) Lysosome (b) Vacuole (c) Centrosome (d) Chloroplast

**5.** Meiosis produces —
(a) two identical cells (b) four cells with half the chromosome number (c) four identical cells with double chromosomes (d) one cell

**6.** Which plant tissue carries food from the leaves to other parts?
(a) Xylem (b) Parenchyma (c) Phloem (d) Meristem

**7.** A tendon connects —
(a) muscle to bone (b) bone to bone (c) muscle to muscle (d) nerve to muscle

**8.** Protein synthesis in the cell takes place in the —
(a) nucleolus (b) vacuole (c) lysosome (d) ribosome

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Robert Hooke, 1665, in cork |
| 2 | d | Mitochondria make ATP by respiration |
| 3 | a | Only plant (and fungal/bacterial) cells have a cell wall |
| 4 | a | Lysosomes hold digestive enzymes |
| 5 | b | Reduction division: 2n → n, four cells |
| 6 | c | Phloem conducts food; xylem conducts water |
| 7 | a | Tendon: muscle to bone; ligament: bone to bone |
| 8 | d | Ribosomes are the site of protein synthesis |
