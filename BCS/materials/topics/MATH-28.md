# Triangles & Pythagoras theorem

> **Why it matters:** Triangles (ত্রিভুজ) are the most asked geometry topic in BCS and job exams: angle sum, exterior angle, sides that can form a triangle, Pythagorean triplets, ladder problems, congruence rules and the centres of a triangle.

## Key concepts
- 🔥 **Sum of the three angles of a triangle = 180°.**
- 🔥 **Exterior angle = sum of the two opposite interior angles.** The sum of the three exterior angles = 360°.
- **By sides:** equilateral (সমবাহু; all sides equal, each angle 60°), isosceles (সমদ্বিবাহু; two sides equal, base angles equal), scalene (বিষমবাহু; all sides different).
- **By angles:** acute (সূক্ষ্মকোণী), right (সমকোণী), obtuse (স্থূলকোণী). A triangle can have **at most one** right or obtuse angle.
- **Triangle inequality:** the sum of any two sides is **greater** than the third side (and the difference is less than the third).
- The **largest side is opposite the largest angle**.

## Pythagoras theorem (পিথাগোরাসের উপপাদ্য) 🔥
- In a right triangle: **(hypotenuse)² = (base)² + (height)²**, i.e. c² = a² + b². The hypotenuse (অতিভুজ) is opposite the right angle and is the longest side.
- Converse: if c² = a² + b², the triangle is right-angled. If c² > a² + b², it is obtuse; if c² < a² + b², acute.
- **Common triplets:** (3, 4, 5), (5, 12, 13), (8, 15, 17), (7, 24, 25), (20, 21, 29), (9, 40, 41) and their multiples (6, 8, 10), (9, 12, 15) …

## Congruence, similarity and centres
| Topic | Facts |
|---|---|
| Congruence (সর্বসমতা) rules | **SSS, SAS, ASA (AAS), RHS**. AAA and SSA are **not** congruence rules |
| Similar triangles (সদৃশ) | equal angles; sides in proportion; ratio of areas = (ratio of sides)² |
| **Centroid (ভরকেন্দ্র)** | meeting point of medians; divides each median **2 : 1** from the vertex |
| **Circumcentre (পরিকেন্দ্র)** | meeting point of perpendicular bisectors; equidistant from the **vertices**; midpoint of hypotenuse in a right triangle |
| **Incentre (অন্তঃকেন্দ্র)** | meeting point of angle bisectors; equidistant from the **sides** |
| **Orthocentre (লম্ববিন্দু)** | meeting point of altitudes; at the right-angle vertex in a right triangle |
| Midpoint theorem | the segment joining midpoints of two sides is parallel to the third and **half** of it |

## Formula and shortcut table
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| Height of equilateral triangle, side a | (√3/2)a |
| Median to hypotenuse | half the hypotenuse |
| Angles in ratio p : q : r | each = 180° × part/(p + q + r) |
| Isosceles, vertex angle V | each base angle = (180° − V)/2 |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find them.
Steps: 9 parts = 180° → 1 part = 20°. Angles 40°, 60°, 80°.
**Answer: 40°, 60°, 80°**

**Example 2.** A 13 m ladder stands with its foot 5 m from a wall. How high up the wall does it reach?
Steps: √(13² − 5²) = √(169 − 25) = √144 = 12.
**Answer: 12 m**

**Example 3.** An exterior angle of a triangle is 110° and one opposite interior angle is 45°. Find the other opposite interior angle.
Steps: 110° − 45° = 65°.
**Answer: 65°**

**Example 4.** Which set can be the sides of a triangle: (3, 4, 8) or (5, 6, 10)?
Steps: 3 + 4 = 7 < 8 (not possible). 5 + 6 = 11 > 10 (possible).
**Answer: 5, 6, 10**

**Example 5.** Find the height of an equilateral triangle of side 6 cm.
Steps: (√3/2) × 6 = 3√3.
**Answer: 3√3 cm**

## Common traps
- AAA proves similarity, **not** congruence.
- Sum of two sides must be strictly **greater** than the third (2 + 3 = 5 cannot make a triangle with 5).
- The hypotenuse is always the longest side; check which side is missing before using c² = a² + b².
- Centroid ratio is 2 : 1 from the **vertex**.

## Quick revision
- Angle sum 180°; exterior angle = sum of opposite interior angles.
- c² = a² + b²; triplets 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25.
- Congruence: SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS.
- Centroid 2 : 1; circumcentre equidistant from vertices; incentre from sides.
- Equilateral height = (√3/2)a.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3. The largest angle is:
(a) 60° (b) 90° (c) 120° (d) 100°

**2.** The legs of a right triangle are 5 cm and 12 cm. The hypotenuse is:
(a) 17 cm (b) 15 cm (c) 13 cm (d) 11 cm

**3.** Which of the following is NOT a Pythagorean triplet?
(a) 3, 4, 5 (b) 8, 15, 17 (c) 7, 24, 25 (d) 6, 8, 11

**4.** Which set of lengths cannot form a triangle?
(a) 2, 3, 6 (b) 3, 4, 5 (c) 4, 5, 8 (d) 5, 5, 5

**5.** An exterior angle of a triangle is 120° and one opposite interior angle is 50°. The other opposite interior angle is:
(a) 60° (b) 70° (c) 10° (d) 130°

**6.** The vertex angle of an isosceles triangle is 40°. Each base angle is:
(a) 70° (b) 40° (c) 140° (d) 50°

**7.** A 25 m ladder reaches a window 24 m high. How far is its foot from the wall?
(a) 5 m (b) 1 m (c) 49 m (d) 7 m

**8.** The centroid of a triangle divides each median in the ratio:
(a) 1 : 1 (b) 3 : 1 (c) 2 : 1 (d) 1 : 3

**9.** Which is NOT a rule for congruence of triangles?
(a) SSS (b) SAS (c) ASA (d) AAA

**10.** The height of an equilateral triangle of side 10 cm is:
(a) 5√3 cm (b) 10√3 cm (c) 5 cm (d) 5√2 cm
