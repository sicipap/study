# Sentence completion

> **Why it matters:** Job exams give the first half of a sentence ("No sooner had he reached the station…") or a gap, and you choose the correct ending. Most items test a **fixed structure**, so learning the patterns below solves most of them quickly.

## How to solve
1. Find the **signal word** (no sooner, lest, too, unless, wish, as if, it is time, though…).
2. Check **tense agreement** between the two parts.
3. Check the **logic** (cause–effect, contrast, condition).
4. Remove options with **double negatives** or **redundancy**.

## Fixed patterns 🔥
| Beginning / signal | Correct completion | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **No sooner had** + subject + V3 | **than** + past | No sooner had he reached the station **than the train left**. |
| **Hardly / Scarcely had** + subject + V3 | **when / before** + past | Hardly had we started **when it began to rain**. |
| **Lest** | **should** + V1 (no *not*) | Walk carefully lest you **should fall**. |
| **too … to** | V1 (no *not*) | He is too weak **to walk**. |
| **so … that** | clause with *can't / couldn't* | He is so weak **that he cannot walk**. |
| **Though / Although** | no *but* in the main clause (*yet* is allowed) | Though he is poor, **he is honest**. |
| **Not only … but also** | parallel parts | She is not only beautiful **but also intelligent**. |
| **Either … or / Neither … nor** | nearer subject decides verb | Neither the teacher nor the students **were** present. |
| **Unless** | positive verb | Unless you hurry, **you will miss the bus**. |
| **If** + present | **will** + V1 | If you work hard, **you will succeed**. |
| **If** + past | **would** + V1 | If I were rich, **I would help the poor**. |
| **If** + had + V3 | **would have** + V3 | If he had come, **I would have met him**. |
| **It is (high) time** | subject + past | It is high time **we left**. |
| **I wish** | past / had + V3 | I wish **I were a bird**. |
| **As if / As though** | were / had + V3 | He behaves as if **he were a king**. |
| **Would rather … than** | V1 … V1 | I would rather **die than beg**. |
| **Had better** | V1 (no *to*) | You had better **consult a doctor**. |
| **Since** (time) | present perfect in main clause | It **has been** five years since **he left**. |
| **Used to** + V1 | past habit | He used to **smoke**, but now he doesn't. |
| **Be used to / accustomed to** + V-ing | present habit | I am used to **walking** long distances. |
| **Look forward to / object to / with a view to** | V-ing | I look forward to **hearing** from you. |
| **It is no use / worth / can't help** | V-ing | It is no use **crying** over spilt milk. |
| **Make / let / see / hear** + object | bare V1 | He made me **laugh**. |
| **Until / till** | positive verb | Wait here until **I return**. |
| **Before / after / when** (future) | present tense in time clause | I will call you when **he comes**. |
| **The + comparative …, the + comparative** | | The more you read, **the more you learn**. |
| **Such … that / So … that** | result | It was such a hot day **that we stayed indoors**. |
| **In order that / so that** | may / might / can | He works hard so that **he may succeed**. |
| **Whether … or** | or (not *if … or*) | I don't know whether he will come **or not**. |
| **Both … and** | and (not *as well as*) | Both Rina **and Mina** passed. |
| **Prefer … to** | to + noun / V-ing | I prefer tea **to coffee**. |
| **Prefer to V1 … rather than V1** | | I prefer to walk **rather than ride**. |

## Tense matching tips
- Past in the first part → usually past in the second: *He said that he **was** ill.*
- Universal truth stays present even after a past verb: *The teacher said that the earth **moves** round the sun.*
- **By the time** + past → past perfect: *By the time the doctor came, the patient **had died**.*
- **By the time** + present → future perfect: *By the time you arrive, I **will have left**.*

## Logic-based completions
| Beginning | Logical ending |
|---|---|
| He is honest; **therefore** … | everybody trusts him. |
| He was ill; **nevertheless** … | he attended the meeting. |
| **Because** it was raining, … | the match was postponed. |
| **In spite of** his illness, … | he went to the office. (*in spite of / despite* + noun or V-ing) |
| **As** he was late, … | he missed the train. |

## Quick revision
- **No sooner … than**; **Hardly / Scarcely … when**.
- **Lest … should** (never *should not*).
- **Too … to** has a negative meaning; do not add *not*.
- **Though** does not take *but*.
- **It is high time + past**; **I wish + were**; **had better + V1**.
- **Look forward to + V-ing**; **used to + V1**; **be used to + V-ing**.
- **By the time + past → had + V3**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** No sooner had he reached the station ____.
(a) when the train left (b) than the train left (c) then the train left (d) the train had left

**2.** Walk carefully lest you ____.
(a) should fall (b) should not fall (c) will fall (d) fall not

**3.** It is high time we ____.
(a) start (b) will start (c) started (d) have started

**4.** Though he is poor, ____.
(a) but he is honest (b) and he is honest (c) so he is honest (d) he is honest

**5.** I look forward to ____ from you.
(a) hear (b) be heard (c) have heard (d) hearing

**6.** By the time the doctor came, the patient ____.
(a) had died (b) has died (c) died (d) will die

**7.** You had better ____ a doctor.
(a) to consult (b) consult (c) consulting (d) consulted

**8.** Hardly had we started ____.
(a) than it began to rain (b) then it began to rain (c) when it began to rain (d) it began raining

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | No sooner ... than |
| 2 | a | lest takes should + V1, no "not" |
| 3 | c | It is high time + past tense |
| 4 | d | Though and but are not used together |
| 5 | d | look forward to + V-ing |
| 6 | a | By the time + past, main clause past perfect |
| 7 | b | had better + bare infinitive |
| 8 | c | Hardly ... when |
