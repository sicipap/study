# Language Movement 1948–1952

> **Why it matters:** One of the most-asked Bangladesh topics: Tamaddun Majlish, Dhirendranath Datta's proposal, Jinnah's declaration, 21 February 1952 (8 Falgun 1358), the martyrs, the Shaheed Minar and UNESCO's recognition.

## Background
- Pakistan's rulers wanted **Urdu** as the only state language, though **Bangla** was the mother tongue of about **56%** of Pakistan's people.
- 🔥 **তমদ্দুন মজলিশ** was founded on **1 September 1947** by **Prof. আবুল কাশেম** (Dhaka University). It was the **first organisation** of the Language Movement.
- On **15 September 1947** it published the booklet **"পাকিস্তানের রাষ্ট্রভাষা: বাংলা না উর্দু?"**.
- The first **রাষ্ট্রভাষা সংগ্রাম পরিষদ** was formed in October 1947 (convener **নুরুল হক ভূঁইয়া**).

## Phase 1 — 1948 🔥
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **25 Feb 1948** | 🔥 **ধীরেন্দ্রনাথ দত্ত** (Cumilla) demanded in the **Pakistan Constituent Assembly** that Bangla also be used as a language of the Assembly. It was rejected |
| 2 Mar 1948 | The Sangram Parishad was re-formed (convener **শামসুল আলম**) |
| **11 Mar 1948** | First **general strike (হরতাল)** for the state language; **Sheikh Mujibur Rahman**, Shamsul Haque and others arrested |
| 15 Mar 1948 | Chief Minister **খাজা নাজিমউদ্দিন** signed an **8-point agreement** with the Sangram Parishad |
| **21 Mar 1948** | 🔥 **জিন্নাহ**, at the **Race Course (রেসকোর্স) meeting**, declared: **"Urdu and only Urdu shall be the state language of Pakistan"** |
| **24 Mar 1948** | He repeated it at the **Dhaka University convocation (Curzon Hall)**; students shouted **"No, no"** |

## Phase 2 — 1952 🔥🔥
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **27 Jan 1952** | Pakistan PM **খাজা নাজিমউদ্দিন**, at **Paltan**, again declared Urdu the only state language |
| **31 Jan 1952** | 🔥 **সর্বদলীয় কেন্দ্রীয় রাষ্ট্রভাষা সংগ্রাম পরিষদ** formed, chaired by **মওলানা আবদুল হামিদ খান ভাসানী**; convener **কাজী গোলাম মাহবুব** |
| 20 Feb 1952 | The **Nurul Amin** government (Chief Minister of East Bengal) imposed **Section 144** in Dhaka |
| **21 Feb 1952** | Students met at the **আমতলা** of Dhaka University (meeting chaired by **গাজীউল হক**) and decided to **break Section 144**. Police fired near **Dhaka Medical College**. Date in Bangla: **৮ ফাল্গুন ১৩৫৮** |
| 22 Feb 1952 | More killings during a mourning procession (Shafiur Rahman, child **Ohiullah**) |
| 23 Feb 1952 | Students built the **first Shaheed Minar** overnight; police **demolished it on 26 Feb** |

### Martyrs (ভাষা শহীদ) 🔥
- **রফিক উদ্দিন আহমদ** (commonly called the **first martyr**), **আবুল বরকত**, **আবদুল জব্বার** (21 Feb)
- **আবদুস সালাম** (wounded on 21 Feb, died **7 April 1952**)
- **শফিউর রহমান** (22 Feb), and the child **অহিউল্লাহ**
## Recognition 🔥
- The **1956 constitution** of Pakistan made **Bangla and Urdu** state languages (**Article 214**).
- The present **Central Shaheed Minar** was designed by **হামিদুর রহমান** (with sculptor **নভেরা আহমেদ**). It was inaugurated on **21 February 1963** by **হাসিনা বেগম**, mother of martyr Abul Barkat.
- 🔥 **UNESCO** declared **21 February** the **International Mother Language Day** on **17 November 1999**; it was first observed in **2000**. The initiative came from Canada-based expatriates **রফিকুল ইসলাম** and **আবদুস সালাম** (Mother Language Lovers of the World).
- **Sierra Leone** declared Bangla an **honorary official language** in **2002** (for the role of Bangladeshi UN peacekeepers).
- The **Ekushey Padak (একুশে পদক)**, the second-highest civilian award, was introduced in **1976**.

## Quick revision
- First organisation: **Tamaddun Majlish** (1 Sept 1947), **Prof. Abul Kashem**.
- Constituent Assembly demand: **Dhirendranath Datta**, **25 Feb 1948**.
- First language strike: **11 March 1948**.
- Jinnah's "Urdu only": **21 March 1948** (Race Course), **24 March** (Curzon Hall).
- All-Party Sangram Parishad: **31 Jan 1952**, chair **Bhashani**, convener **Kazi Golam Mahboob**.
- Section 144 imposed by the **Nurul Amin** government.
- 21 Feb 1952 = **8 Falgun 1358**.
- Shaheed Minar designer: **Hamidur Rahman**; inaugurated **21 Feb 1963**.
- UNESCO recognition: **17 November 1999**; first observed **2000**.
- Bangla a state language of Pakistan: **1956 constitution, Art. 214**.
