# Ancient civilizations

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask which river a civilization grew on, where it was (present-day country), its script, and its famous "firsts" (first writing, first law code, pyramids, Olympic Games). Match-the-pair questions are very common.

## River-valley civilizations at a glance 🔥
| Civilization | River | Present-day area | Script / key features |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Mesopotamian** (Sumerian, Babylonian, Assyrian) | **Tigris & Euphrates** | **Iraq** | **Cuneiform** (কীলক লিপি), the earliest writing (c. 3200 BCE); ziggurats; wheel; base-60 counting |
| 🔥 **Egyptian** | **Nile** | Egypt | **Hieroglyphics**; pyramids; mummies; papyrus; 365-day solar calendar |
| 🔥 **Indus Valley (Harappan)** | **Indus** (সিন্ধু) | Pakistan & north-west India | Script **still undeciphered**; planned cities; drainage |
| **Chinese** | **Huang He (Yellow River)** | China | Pictographic script; oracle bones (Shang dynasty) |

- **"Mesopotamia"** is Greek for **"the land between two rivers"**.
- 🔥 **"Egypt is the gift of the Nile"**: said by the Greek historian **Herodotus**.
- The **Huang He** is called **"China's Sorrow"** because of its deadly floods.

## Mesopotamia 🔥
- **Sumerians** invented **cuneiform** writing on clay tablets and are credited with the **wheel**.
- 🔥 **Code of Hammurabi** (c. 1754 BCE), king of **Babylon**: one of the **oldest written law codes**, carved on a stone pillar; "an eye for an eye".
- **Hanging Gardens of Babylon**: attributed to **Nebuchadnezzar II**; one of the Seven Wonders.
- The Sumerians used a **base-60 (sexagesimal)** system: that is why an hour has 60 minutes and a circle 360 degrees.
- *Epic of Gilgamesh*: one of the oldest known works of literature.

## Egypt
- Kings were called **Pharaoh (ফারাও)**.
- 🔥 **Great Pyramid of Giza**: built for Pharaoh **Khufu (Cheops)**; the **only one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World still standing**.
- **Sphinx**: lion body with a human head, at Giza.
- Bodies were preserved as **mummies (মমি)**.
- **Rosetta Stone** (found 1799): its text in hieroglyphic, Demotic and Greek let **Jean-François Champollion** decipher hieroglyphics (**1822**).
- **Tutankhamun's** tomb was discovered by **Howard Carter** in **1922**.

## Indus Valley Civilization (সিন্ধু সভ্যতা) 🔥
- Period: about **2600–1900 BCE** (mature phase); a **Bronze Age** civilization. Also called the **Harappan civilization**.

| Site | Location | Discovered by / key fact |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Harappa** | Punjab, Pakistan (on the **Ravi**) | **Daya Ram Sahni, 1921** (first site excavated) |
| 🔥 **Mohenjo-daro** ("mound of the dead") | Larkana, Sindh, Pakistan (on the **Indus**) | **R. D. Banerji (Rakhaldas Bandyopadhyay), 1922**; **Great Bath** |
| **Lothal** | Gujarat, India | Dockyard (ancient port) |
| **Kalibangan** | Rajasthan, India | Ploughed field |
| **Dholavira** | Gujarat, India | Water reservoirs; UNESCO site (2021) |

- Excavations were led under **Sir John Marshall**, Director-General of the Archaeological Survey of India.
- Features: **grid-plan cities, covered drains, burnt bricks, granaries, seals, cotton cloth**; worshipped a **mother goddess** and a "Pashupati" figure. No iron was used.

## Chinese civilization
- Dynasties: **Shang** (oracle bones), **Zhou**, **Qin** (first emperor **Qin Shi Huang** unified China in **221 BCE**; began joining the **Great Wall**; Terracotta Army), **Han**.
- Chinese inventions: 🔥 **paper** (**Cai Lun**, c. **105 CE**), **compass**, **gunpowder**, **printing**, **silk**.
- Thinkers: **Confucius** (ethics, family, respect for rulers) and **Lao Tzu** (Taoism).

## Greek civilization 🔥
- City-states (**polis**): **Athens** (democracy, learning) and **Sparta** (military).
- **Cleisthenes** is called the **father of democracy** (Athens, c. 508 BCE).
- The **ancient Olympic Games** began in **776 BCE** at **Olympia**.
- **Homer**: *Iliad* and *Odyssey* (Trojan War).

| "Father of …" | Person |
|---|---|
| 🔥 History | **Herodotus** |
| 🔥 Medicine | **Hippocrates** |
| Geometry | **Euclid** |
| 🔥 Political science | **Aristotle** (*Politics*) |
| Tragedy / comedy | Aeschylus / Aristophanes |

- **Socrates** → teacher of **Plato** (*The Republic*, founded the **Academy**) → teacher of **Aristotle** (founded the **Lyceum**) → tutor of **Alexander the Great**.
- **Pythagoras** (theorem), **Archimedes** (buoyancy, "Eureka").

## Roman civilization
- Legendary founding of Rome: **753 BCE** by **Romulus and Remus**.
- **Twelve Tables** (c. 450 BCE): the basis of **Roman law**.
- **Julius Caesar** introduced the **Julian calendar**; he was assassinated in **44 BCE**.
- **Augustus (Octavian)** became the **first Roman emperor** in **27 BCE**.
- Famous structures: **Colosseum**, aqueducts, roads. Language: **Latin**.
- The **Western Roman Empire fell in 476 CE**. The **Eastern (Byzantine) Empire** lasted until the **fall of Constantinople to the Ottomans in 1453**.

## Other civilizations
| Civilization | Region | Key fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Persian (Achaemenid)** | Iran | Founder **Cyrus the Great**; capital **Persepolis**; Zoroastrianism |
| **Phoenician** | Lebanon coast | Developed an early **alphabet**; great sea traders |
| **Hittite** | Anatolia (Türkiye) | Early users of **iron** |
| **Maya** | Central America (Mexico, Guatemala) | Advanced calendar, used the **zero** |
| **Aztec** | Mexico | Capital **Tenochtitlan** (today's **Mexico City**) |
| **Inca** | Peru (Andes) | Capital **Cusco**; **Machu Picchu** |

## Seven Wonders of the Ancient World
Great Pyramid of Giza (only survivor), Hanging Gardens of Babylon, Temple of Artemis (Ephesus), Statue of Zeus (Olympia), Mausoleum at Halicarnassus, Colossus of Rhodes, Lighthouse (Pharos) of Alexandria.

## Quick revision
- Mesopotamia = **Tigris–Euphrates**, Iraq; **cuneiform**; **Code of Hammurabi** (Babylon).
- "Egypt is the gift of the Nile" — **Herodotus**; script **hieroglyphics**; deciphered via the **Rosetta Stone**.
- **Great Pyramid of Giza** (Khufu) is the only surviving ancient wonder.
- **Harappa** 1921 (Daya Ram Sahni), **Mohenjo-daro** 1922 (R. D. Banerji); Indus script undeciphered.
- Huang He = **China's Sorrow**; paper invented by **Cai Lun**.
- Father of history: **Herodotus**; of medicine: **Hippocrates**; of democracy: **Cleisthenes**.
- Ancient Olympics began **776 BCE**; Western Roman Empire fell **476 CE**; Constantinople fell **1453**.
