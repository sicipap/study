# Sports & national awards

> **Why it matters:** Cricket and football milestones, sporting "firsts" (Everest, Channel swimming, chess GM) and the national awards (Independence Award, Ekushey Padak, gallantry awards) appear often in BCS and other exams.

## National game
- **কাবাডি (হা-ডু-ডু)** is the national game (declared **1972**). **Bangladesh Kabaddi Federation: 1974**.

## Cricket 🔥🔥
| Milestone | Date / fact |
|---|---|
| **BCB** (Bangladesh Cricket Board) founded | **1972** |
| **ICC associate** membership | **1977** |
| **First ODI** | **31 March 1986** vs **Pakistan** (Asia Cup, Sri Lanka); captain **গাজী আশরাফ হোসেন লিপু** |
| **ICC Trophy champion** | **1997** (Kuala Lumpur; beat **Kenya** in the final) — earned a World Cup place |
| **First World Cup** | **1999** (England); beat **Pakistan** and Scotland |
| **Test status** | **26 June 2000** — the **10th** Test-playing nation |
| **First Test** | **10–13 November 2000** vs **India**, Bangabandhu National Stadium, Dhaka; **আমিনুল ইসলাম বুলবুল** scored **145** (first Test century) |
| **First Test win** | **January 2005** vs **Zimbabwe**, Chattogram |
| Youngest Test centurion in history | **মোহাম্মদ আশরাফুল** (17 years, vs Sri Lanka, **2001**) |
| **Asian Games gold** | Men's cricket, **2010 Guangzhou** |
| **Women's Asia Cup** champion | **2018** (beat **India** in the final, Kuala Lumpur) |
| **U-19 World Cup** champion | **2020** (South Africa; captain **আকবর আলী**) |
| Home of cricket | **Sher-e-Bangla National Stadium, Mirpur** |
| **BPL** (franchise T20 league) | Started **2012** |

## Football 🔥
- **BFF** (Bangladesh Football Federation): **1972**.
- **Swadhin Bangla Football Team** played matches in India in **1971** to raise funds for the Liberation War.
- **SAFF Championship (men):** champion in **2003** (Dhaka).
- **SAFF Women's Championship:** champion in **2022** and **2024**, both in **Kathmandu**, both finals against **Nepal**.

## Other sporting firsts 🔥🔥
| Person | Achievement |
|---|---|
| **ব্রজেন দাস** | Swam the **English Channel (1958)** — first Asian to do so |
| **নিয়াজ মোরশেদ** | **First Grandmaster of South Asia (1987)** |
| **রানী হামিদ** | Legendary woman chess player |
| **আসিফ হোসেন খান** | **Shooting gold at the 2002 Commonwealth Games (Manchester)** |
| **মুসা ইব্রাহীম** | **First Bangladeshi on Everest (23 May 2010)** |
| **নিশাত মজুমদার** | **First Bangladeshi woman on Everest (19 May 2012)** |
| **ওয়াসফিয়া নাজরীন** | First Bangladeshi to climb the **Seven Summits** |
| **সিদ্দিকুর রহমান** | Golfer; first Bangladeshi to **qualify directly** for the Olympics (**Rio 2016**) |
| **রোমান সানা** | Archer; won Bangladesh's first archery medal at the World Championships (bronze, **2019**) |

- Bangladesh has never won an Olympic medal. It first took part in the **1984 Los Angeles Olympics**.
- **South Asian Games** hosted in Dhaka in **1985**, **1993** and **2010**.

## National awards 🔥🔥
| Award | Fact |
|---|---|
| **স্বাধীনতা পুরস্কার (Independence Award)** | **Highest state award**; introduced **1977**; given around **26 March** |
| **একুশে পদক** | **Second highest civilian award**; introduced **1976**; given in **February** for language, literature, culture, etc. |
| **বাংলা একাডেমি সাহিত্য পুরস্কার** | Introduced **1960** |
| **জাতীয় চলচ্চিত্র পুরস্কার** | Introduced **1975** |
| **বেগম রোকেয়া পদক** | Given on **9 December (Rokeya Day)** to women for outstanding work |
| **জাতীয় ক্রীড়া পুরস্কার** | For sportspersons |

## Gallantry awards for the Liberation War (বীরত্বসূচক খেতাব) 🔥🔥
| Award | Number |
|---|---|
| **বীরশ্রেষ্ঠ** (highest) | **7** |
| **বীর উত্তম** | **68** |
| **বীর বিক্রম** | **175** |
| **বীর প্রতীক** | **426** |
| **Total** | **676** |

- Gazette published on **15 December 1973**.
- **7 Bir Sreshtho:** **Captain Mohiuddin Jahangir**, **Sepoy Hamidur Rahman**, **Sepoy Mostafa Kamal**, **ERA Mohammad Ruhul Amin**, **Flight Lieutenant Matiur Rahman**, **Lance Nayek Munshi Abdur Rouf**, **Lance Nayek Nur Mohammad Sheikh**.
- **Women Bir Protik:** **Captain Dr Sitara Begum** and **Taramon Bibi**.
- **Only foreign gallantry-award holder:** **W. A. S. Ouderland** (Dutch-born Australian), **Bir Protik**.

## International recognition
- **Nobel Peace Prize 2006:** **Dr Muhammad Yunus** and **Grameen Bank**.

## Quick revision
- National game **kabaddi**.
- ICC Trophy **1997**; Test status **26 June 2000** (10th); first Test vs **India**.
- U-19 World Cup **2020**; Women's Asia Cup **2018**; Asian Games gold **2010**.
- SAFF Women's champion **2022 & 2024**; men's SAFF **2003**.
- Everest: **Musa Ibrahim (2010)**, **Nishat Majumdar (2012)**.
- Highest award **Independence Award (1977)**; Ekushey Padak **1976**.
- Gallantry: **7 / 68 / 175 / 426 = 676**.
