# Mega projects: Metro rail, Karnaphuli tunnel, Matarbari

> **Why it matters:** Opening dates, lengths, routes and funding partners of the metro rail, the Karnaphuli tunnel, Matarbari, the elevated expressway and the Cox's Bazar railway are among the most asked recent-affairs questions.

## Dhaka Metro Rail (মেট্রোরেল) 🔥🔥
- Operator: **DMTCL** – Dhaka Mass Transit Company Limited (formed **2013**). Main funder: **JICA (Japan)**.
- Plan: **6 MRT lines** for Dhaka by 2030.

### MRT Line-6 (the first metro)
| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Route | **Uttara (Diabari) – Motijheel** |
| Length | about **20.1 km**, elevated, **16 stations** (extension to **Kamalapur** under construction) |
| First section | **Uttara North – Agargaon** (11.73 km), inaugurated **28 December 2022** |
| Second section | **Agargaon – Motijheel**, inaugurated **4 November 2023** |
| Top speed | **100 km/h** |
| First female metro driver | **Mariam Afiza** |

### Other lines
- **MRT Line-1:** Bangladesh's **first underground metro** (Airport – Kamalapur, plus an elevated branch Notun Bazar – Purbachal). Work began in **2023**.
- **MRT Line-5 (North and South):** east–west routes (Hemayetpur – Bhatara, Gabtoli – Dasherkandi).

## Karnaphuli Tunnel (কর্ণফুলী টানেল) 🔥🔥
- The **first underwater road tunnel in South Asia**.
- Opened with the official name **Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman Tunnel**; commonly called the **Karnaphuli Tunnel**.
- Links **Patenga (city side)** with **Anwara (south bank)**, Chattogram, under the **Karnaphuli river**.
- Length: about **3.32 km** (each tunnel tube about **2.45 km**), plus approach roads.
- Built by **China Communications Construction Company (CCCC)** with a Chinese loan.
- Inaugurated on **28 October 2023**.
- Idea: **"One City, Two Towns"**, like Shanghai.

## Matarbari (মাতারবাড়ী) 🔥
- Place: **Matarbari, Moheshkhali, Cox's Bazar**.
- **Matarbari coal power plant:** **1,200 MW** (2 × 600 MW), ultra-supercritical, funded by **JICA**, run by CPGCBL.
- **Matarbari Deep Sea Port:** Bangladesh's **first deep-sea port**, under construction with **Japanese (JICA)** support. The channel was first dug to bring coal ships to the power plant. It will let large ships dock directly.

## Other mega projects 🔥
| Project | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Padma Bridge** | 6.15 km, opened **25 June 2022** (see BD-26) |
| **Padma Rail Link** | **Dhaka – Jashore** via Padma Bridge; Dhaka–Bhanga opened **10 October 2023**, full line to Jashore by **end of 2024** |
| **Dhaka Elevated Expressway** | Airport (Kawla) – Kutubkhali; first section **Airport – Farmgate** opened **2 September 2023** |
| **Dohazari – Cox's Bazar railway** | About **100 km**; inaugurated **11 November 2023**; passenger trains from **1 December 2023**; **Cox's Bazar Iconic Railway Station** is shaped like an **oyster/shell (ঝিনুক)** |
| **Dhaka – Mawa – Bhanga Expressway** | **First expressway** of Bangladesh, opened **12 March 2020** |
| **HSIA Third Terminal** | Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport; **soft opening 7 October 2023**; built by a **Japanese** consortium |
| **Rooppur NPP** | 2,400 MW, Russia (see BD-33) |
| **Payra Port** | 3rd seaport, Patuakhali (see BD-35) |
| **Jamuna Railway Bridge** | 4.8 km, dedicated rail bridge, opened **2025** |
| **Bangabandhu Satellite-1** | First satellite; launched **12 May 2018** (Bangladesh time) by **SpaceX Falcon 9**, Florida; built by **Thales Alenia Space**; orbital slot **119.1° E**; Bangladesh became the **57th** country with its own satellite |

> 🔄 Update: several projects were renamed after August 2024 (for example the tunnel and the satellite). Check the current official names and the status of Matarbari port, MRT Line-1 and the Kamalapur metro extension.

## Quick revision
- Metro rail first opened **28 Dec 2022** (Uttara–Agargaon); full Line-6 to Motijheel **4 Nov 2023**.
- Metro operator **DMTCL**; funder **JICA**.
- Karnaphuli tunnel: first underwater tunnel in South Asia, **28 Oct 2023**, **Patenga–Anwara**.
- Matarbari: **1,200 MW** coal + **first deep-sea port** (Japan).
- Elevated expressway opened **2 Sept 2023**; first expressway **Dhaka–Mawa–Bhanga (2020)**.
- Cox's Bazar railway: inaugurated **11 Nov 2023**; oyster-shaped station.
- Satellite launched **12 May 2018**, **57th** country.
