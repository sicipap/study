# Clock problems

> **Why it matters:** Clock (ঘড়ি) questions ask for the angle between the hands, when the hands meet or are opposite, how many times something happens in a day, mirror times, and fast or slow clocks. One formula solves most of them.

## Basic facts 🔥
| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Clock face | 360°, 12 hour marks, 60 minute marks |
| Angle between two hour marks | 30° |
| Minute hand speed | **6° per minute** (360° in 60 min) |
| Hour hand speed | **0.5° per minute** (30° in 60 min) |
| Minute hand gains on hour hand | **5.5° per minute** (= 11/2°) |
| Minute hand gains a full lap (hands meet again) | every **65 5/11 minutes** |

## Angle formula 🔥
**Angle = 30H − 5.5M**, then take the value **without its sign** (absolute value).
- If the result is more than 180°, subtract it from 360° to get the smaller angle.
- H = hour, M = minutes. Example: at 3:40 → 30 × 3 − 5.5 × 40 = 90 − 220 = −130 → **130°**.

## Special positions (count in 12 hours and in a day) 🔥
| Position | In 12 hours | In 24 hours |
|---|---|---|
| Hands together (coincide, 0°) | **11 times** | **22 times** |
| Hands opposite (straight line, 180°) | **11 times** | **22 times** |
| Hands in a straight line (0° or 180°) | 22 times | **44 times** |
| Hands at right angles (90°) | **22 times** | **44 times** |

Why 11 and not 12? Between 11 and 1 the hands meet only once (at 12:00).

## When exactly? (between H and H + 1 o'clock)
| Hands are … | Minutes past H |
|---|---|
| Together | **(60H) ÷ 11** = 5H × 12/11 |
| Opposite | (5H + 30) × 12/11, or (5H − 30) × 12/11 when 5H is more than 30 |
| At right angles | (5H + 15) × 12/11 and (5H − 15) × 12/11 (use the ones between 0 and 60) |

## Mirror image of a clock 🔥
- **Actual time = 11:60 − mirror time** (i.e. 12:00 − mirror time). Example: mirror shows 4:20 → 11:60 − 4:20 = **7:40**.
- If the mirror time is 11:xx or 12:xx, subtract from **23:60** instead. Example: 12:20 → 23:60 − 12:20 = **11:40**.
- The same rule works both ways (mirror ↔ actual).

## Fast and slow clocks
- A clock that **gains** x minutes per hour shows **more** than the true time; after t hours it is ahead by x × t minutes.
- Rotation of the hour hand: it turns 30° per hour, so from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. (6 hours) it turns **180°**.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Angle at 7:30?
30 × 7 − 5.5 × 30 = 210 − 165 = **45°**.

**Example 2.** Angle at 4:20?
120 − 110 = **10°**.

**Example 3.** At what time between 3 and 4 are the hands together?
60 × 3 ÷ 11 = 180/11 = **16 4/11 minutes past 3**.

**Example 4.** At what time between 2 and 3 are the hands opposite?
(5 × 2 + 30) × 12/11 = 40 × 12/11 = 480/11 = **43 7/11 minutes past 2**. Check: 30 × 2 − 5.5 × 43 7/11 = 60 − 240 = −180 → 180° ✔.

**Example 5.** A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. It is set right at 8 a.m. What does it show at 8 p.m. (true time)?
12 hours × 5 = 60 minutes ahead → it shows **9:00 p.m.**

## Quick revision
- Angle = 30H − 5.5M (ignore the sign; if more than 180°, use 360° minus it).
- Minute hand 6°/min, hour hand 0.5°/min, relative 5.5°/min.
- Coincide: 11 times in 12 hours, 22 in a day.
- Right angle: 22 times in 12 hours, 44 in a day.
- Hands meet every 65 5/11 minutes.
- Mirror time: 11:60 − time (use 23:60 for 11 and 12 o'clock).
- Hour hand turns 30° per hour.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:40?
(a) 120° (b) 130° (c) 140° (d) 150°

**2.** What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 4:20?
(a) 10° (b) 0° (c) 20° (d) 5°

**3.** What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 7:30?
(a) 30° (b) 60° (c) 45° (d) 50°

**4.** How many times do the hands of a clock coincide in a day?
(a) 24 (b) 44 (c) 11 (d) 22

**5.** At what time between 3 and 4 o'clock are the hands of a clock together?
(a) 16 4/11 min past 3 (b) 15 min past 3 (c) 16 min past 3 (d) 17 3/11 min past 3

**6.** The mirror image of a clock shows 4:20. What is the actual time?
(a) 8:40 (b) 7:40 (c) 7:20 (d) 8:20

**7.** How many times are the hands of a clock at right angles in a day?
(a) 22 (b) 48 (c) 44 (d) 24

**8.** Through how many degrees does the hour hand turn from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.?
(a) 150° (b) 210° (c) 90° (d) 180°

**9.** At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock are the hands of a clock opposite each other?
(a) 43 min past 2 (b) 40 min past 2 (c) 43 7/11 min past 2 (d) 45 5/11 min past 2

**10.** A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. It is set right at 8 a.m. What time will it show when the true time is 8 p.m.?
(a) 8:50 p.m. (b) 9:10 p.m. (c) 8:55 p.m. (d) 9:00 p.m.
