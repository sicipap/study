# Mensuration: triangle, rectangle, square

> **Why it matters:** Area and perimeter (ক্ষেত্রফল ও পরিসীমা) of triangles and four-sided figures are asked in every kind of job exam: Heron's formula, equilateral triangle area, diagonal of a rectangle/square, rhombus area and paths around fields.

## Key concepts
- **Perimeter (পরিসীমা):** total length of the boundary. **Area (ক্ষেত্রফল):** the surface enclosed, in square units.
- If every length is multiplied by k, the **area is multiplied by k²** (double the side → 4 times the area).
- **Land units:** 1 hectare = 10,000 m² ≈ 2.47 acres; 1 acre = 4,840 sq yards ≈ 4,047 m² = 100 decimals (শতাংশ); 1 decimal = 435.6 sq ft.

## Triangle formulas (ত্রিভুজ) 🔥
| Triangle | Area |
|---|---|
| Any (base b, height h) | **½ × b × h** |
| 🔥 Three sides a, b, c (Heron) | **√[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)]**, s = (a + b + c)/2 |
| Right triangle (legs a, b) | ½ab |
| 🔥 Equilateral (side a) | **(√3/4)a²**; perimeter 3a; height (√3/2)a |
| Isosceles (equal sides a, base b) | (b/4)√(4a² − b²) |
| Two sides and included angle θ | ½ab sin θ |

## Four-sided figures 🔥
| Figure | Area | Other |
|---|---|---|
| **Rectangle (আয়তক্ষেত্র)** | length × breadth | perimeter 2(l + b); diagonal √(l² + b²) |
| **Square (বর্গক্ষেত্র)** | a² = **d²/2** | perimeter 4a; diagonal **a√2** |
| **Parallelogram (সামান্তরিক)** | base × height | |
| **Rhombus (রম্বস)** | **½ × d₁ × d₂** | side = √[(d₁/2)² + (d₂/2)²] |
| **Trapezium (ট্রাপিজিয়াম)** | **½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height** | |
| Path of width w **outside** a rectangle l × b | (l + 2w)(b + 2w) − lb | |
| Path of width w **inside** | lb − (l − 2w)(b − 2w) | |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Find the area of a triangle with sides 13, 14 and 15 cm.
Steps: s = 21. Area = √(21 × 8 × 7 × 6) = √7056 = 84.
**Answer: 84 cm²**

**Example 2.** Find the area of an equilateral triangle of side 4 cm.
Steps: (√3/4) × 16 = 4√3.
**Answer: 4√3 cm²**

**Example 3.** Find the diagonal of a rectangle 12 m by 5 m.
Steps: √(144 + 25) = √169 = 13.
**Answer: 13 m**

**Example 4.** The diagonal of a square is 10 cm. Find its area.
Steps: d²/2 = 100/2 = 50.
**Answer: 50 cm²**

**Example 5.** A 2 m wide path runs around the outside of a 20 m × 10 m garden. Find the area of the path.
Steps: (20 + 4)(10 + 4) − 20 × 10 = 336 − 200 = 136.
**Answer: 136 m²**

## Common traps
- For a path outside, add **2w** (both sides) to each dimension, not w.
- Square area from diagonal is d²/2, not d².
- Heron's formula needs the **semi**-perimeter s, not the full perimeter.
- Doubling the side of a square makes the area 4 times, not 2 times.

## Quick revision
- Triangle ½bh; Heron √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)].
- Equilateral (√3/4)a².
- Rectangle diagonal √(l² + b²); square diagonal a√2.
- Rhombus ½d₁d₂; trapezium ½(a + b)h.
- Sides × k ⇒ area × k².
- 1 hectare = 10,000 m²; 1 acre = 100 decimals.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 8 cm is:
(a) 96 cm² (b) 48 cm² (c) 40 cm² (d) 20 cm²

**2.** The area of a triangle with sides 5, 12 and 13 cm is:
(a) 60 cm² (b) 32.5 cm² (c) 30 cm² (d) 65 cm²

**3.** The area of an equilateral triangle of side 6 cm is:
(a) 9√3 cm² (b) 36√3 cm² (c) 18√3 cm² (d) 6√3 cm²

**4.** A rectangle has perimeter 40 cm and length 12 cm. Its area is:
(a) 336 cm² (b) 80 cm² (c) 120 cm² (d) 96 cm²

**5.** The diagonal of a square is 12 cm. Its area is:
(a) 144 cm² (b) 72 cm² (c) 36 cm² (d) 48 cm²

**6.** The diagonal of a rectangle 8 m long and 6 m wide is:
(a) 10 m (b) 14 m (c) 12 m (d) 7 m

**7.** The diagonals of a rhombus are 10 cm and 24 cm. Its area is:
(a) 240 cm² (b) 60 cm² (c) 120 cm² (d) 34 cm²

**8.** A trapezium has parallel sides 10 cm and 14 cm and height 6 cm. Its area is:
(a) 144 cm² (b) 84 cm² (c) 60 cm² (d) 72 cm²

**9.** A 1 m wide path runs around the outside of a square field of side 20 m. The area of the path is:
(a) 84 m² (b) 80 m² (c) 44 m² (d) 76 m²

**10.** If the side of a square is doubled, its area becomes:
(a) 2 times (b) 3 times (c) 4 times (d) 8 times
