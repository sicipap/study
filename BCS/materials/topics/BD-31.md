# Industry: RMG, jute, EPZ, economic zones

> **Why it matters:** RMG firsts, Rana Plaza, Adamjee Jute Mills, the list of EPZs and the location of special economic zones are frequent MCQs in BCS and bank exams.

## Readymade garments (তৈরি পোশাক শিল্প, RMG) 🔥🔥
- RMG earns **over 80%** of Bangladesh's export income. Bangladesh is the **2nd largest garment exporter** in the world after **China** (Vietnam is close behind).
- **Pioneers:** **Reaz Garments** (Riaz Uddin) made the **first export shipment (1978, to France)**. **Desh Garments** (Nurul Quader Khan, 1978–79) was the first **100% export-oriented** joint venture, with **Daewoo (South Korea)**.
- Trade bodies: **BGMEA** (woven garments, 1983) and **BKMEA** (knitwear).
- **Tazreen Fashions fire:** **24 November 2012**, Ashulia.
- **Rana Plaza collapse:** **24 April 2013**, **Savar**; more than **1,100** workers died — the worst disaster in the garment industry. It led to the **Accord** (European buyers) and the **Alliance** (North American buyers) on factory safety.
- **Green factories:** Bangladesh has the **highest number of LEED-certified green garment factories in the world** (US Green Building Council).
- Main markets: **EU** (as a bloc) and the **USA** (as a single country).
- Minimum wage for garment workers was last revised in **2023** (Tk 12,500).

> 🔄 Update: check the latest RMG export figure and minimum wage in a newspaper or the Bangladesh Economic Review.

## Jute industry (পাট শিল্প) 🔥
- **Adamjee Jute Mills**, **Siddhirganj, Narayanganj** (1951) was the **largest jute mill in the world**. It closed on **30 June 2002**. The site is now **Adamjee EPZ**.
- **BJMC** (Bangladesh Jute Mills Corporation) stopped production in its state-owned mills in **July 2020**.
- **Mandatory Jute Packaging Act, 2010**: jute bags compulsory for many products.
- **Jute Research Institute:** Dhaka (see BD-30). **Jute Day:** 6 March.

## EPZs (রপ্তানি প্রক্রিয়াকরণ এলাকা) 🔥🔥
- Authority: **BEPZA** (Bangladesh Export Processing Zones Authority), set up under the **BEPZA Act 1980**.
- **First EPZ: Chattogram EPZ (1983).**
- **Total EPZs: 8.**

| EPZ | Location |
|---|---|
| **Chattogram EPZ** | Patenga, Chattogram (first, 1983) |
| **Dhaka EPZ** | **Savar** (Ashulia), Dhaka |
| **Mongla EPZ** | Bagerhat |
| **Ishwardi EPZ** | Pabna |
| **Cumilla EPZ** | Cumilla |
| **Uttara EPZ** | **Nilphamari** |
| **Adamjee EPZ** | Siddhirganj, Narayanganj |
| **Karnaphuli EPZ** | Chattogram |

- BEPZA also runs the **BEPZA Economic Zone** at Mirsharai.

## Economic zones (অর্থনৈতিক অঞ্চল) 🔥
- Authority: **BEZA** (Bangladesh Economic Zones Authority), under the **Bangladesh Economic Zones Act, 2010**.
- Target: **100 economic zones** by 2030.
- **Largest:** the industrial city at **Mirsharai–Sitakunda (Chattogram) and Sonagazi (Feni)**, launched as **Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujib Shilpa Nagar**; later renamed **National Special Economic Zone** (check current name).

| Government-to-government zone | Location |
|---|---|
| **Japanese Economic Zone** | **Araihazar, Narayanganj** |
| **Chinese Economic and Industrial Zone** | **Anwara, Chattogram** |
| **Indian Economic Zones** | Mongla (Bagerhat) and Mirsharai (Chattogram) |

- **Hi-Tech Parks:** Bangladesh Hi-Tech Park Authority (2010); the biggest is at **Kaliakair, Gazipur**.
- **BIDA** (Bangladesh Investment Development Authority, 2016) handles investment promotion.

## Quick revision
- RMG: **80%+** of exports; 2nd largest exporter after **China**.
- First exporter: **Reaz Garments (1978)**; Desh Garments with **Daewoo**.
- Rana Plaza: **24 April 2013, Savar**.
- Adamjee Jute Mills closed **30 June 2002** → Adamjee EPZ.
- BEPZA **1980**; first EPZ **Chattogram (1983)**; **8** EPZs; Uttara EPZ in **Nilphamari**.
- BEZA **2010**; Japanese EZ **Araihazar**; Chinese EZ **Anwara**.
- Most LEED green garment factories in the world: **Bangladesh**.
