# World War II & Cold War

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the start and end dates of WWII, the Axis and Allied powers, Pearl Harbor, Hiroshima–Nagasaki, the wartime conferences (Atlantic Charter, Yalta, Potsdam), and Cold War terms (Iron Curtain, Truman Doctrine, Marshall Plan, Berlin Wall, NAM).

## World War II (দ্বিতীয় বিশ্বযুদ্ধ) 🔥
- 🔥 Began **1 September 1939**, when **Germany invaded Poland**. Britain and France declared war on **3 September 1939**.
- Ended in Europe on **8 May 1945 (V-E Day)**. 🔥 Japan announced surrender on **15 August 1945** and formally signed it on **2 September 1945** aboard **USS *Missouri*** in Tokyo Bay — the **end of WWII**.
- Causes: harsh **Treaty of Versailles**, the **Great Depression (1929)**, rise of **Fascism (Italy)** and **Nazism (Germany)**, failure of the League, and **appeasement** (the **Munich Agreement, 1938**, by British PM **Neville Chamberlain**).

### The two sides
| Axis Powers (অক্ষশক্তি) | Allied Powers (মিত্রশক্তি) |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Germany, Italy, Japan** (Tripartite Pact, 27 September 1940) | **Britain, France, USSR, USA, China** and others |
| **Adolf Hitler** (Nazi Germany, "Führer") | **Winston Churchill** (UK PM from May 1940) |
| **Benito Mussolini** (Fascist Italy, "Il Duce") | **Franklin D. Roosevelt** (USA; died April 1945, succeeded by **Harry Truman**) |
| **Hideki Tojo** (Japan PM); Emperor **Hirohito** | **Joseph Stalin** (USSR); **Charles de Gaulle** (Free French); **Chiang Kai-shek** (China) |

### Key events
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 23 Aug 1939 | **Nazi–Soviet (Molotov–Ribbentrop) Non-Aggression Pact** |
| May–June 1940 | Fall of France; **Dunkirk** evacuation |
| 1940 | **Battle of Britain** (air war) |
| 22 June 1941 | **Operation Barbarossa**: Germany invaded the **USSR** |
| 🔥 **7 December 1941** | Japan attacked **Pearl Harbor** (Hawaii) → **USA entered the war** |
| June 1942 | **Battle of Midway**: turning point in the Pacific |
| 1942–43 | 🔥 **Battle of Stalingrad**: turning point in Europe; **El Alamein** in North Africa |
| 🔥 **6 June 1944** | **D-Day**: Allied landing in **Normandy**, France (**Operation Overlord**, commander **Eisenhower**) |
| 30 April 1945 | **Hitler** killed himself in Berlin (Mussolini was killed on 28 April) |
| 🔥 **6 August 1945** | Atomic bomb **"Little Boy"** on **Hiroshima** (dropped from the B-29 **Enola Gay**) |
| 🔥 **9 August 1945** | Atomic bomb **"Fat Man"** on **Nagasaki** |

- The **Holocaust**: about **6 million Jews** were killed by the Nazis.
- **War-crimes trials:** **Nuremberg** (Germany, 1945–46) and **Tokyo**.
- **Bangladesh/Bengal link:** the **Bengal Famine of 1943 (পঞ্চাশের মন্বন্তর, 1350 BS)** killed about **3 million** people; Japan bombed **Chattogram** (1942); the **Chattogram War Cemetery** holds Commonwealth soldiers of the **Burma campaign**.

### Wartime conferences 🔥
| Conference | Year | Who / outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Atlantic Charter** | **14 August 1941** | **Churchill & Roosevelt**, on a warship off **Newfoundland**; principles later used for the **UN** |
| **Tehran** | 1943 | Churchill, Roosevelt, Stalin: second front (D-Day) |
| 🔥 **Yalta** (Crimea) | **February 1945** | Churchill, Roosevelt, Stalin: division of Germany, **UN veto** agreed |
| **Potsdam** (near Berlin) | July–August 1945 | **Truman**, Stalin, Churchill (replaced by **Attlee**): Japan's surrender terms |

## Cold War (স্নায়ুযুদ্ধ / ঠান্ডা লড়াই) 🔥
- A state of tension **without direct war** between the **USA (capitalist, Western bloc)** and the **USSR (communist, Eastern bloc)**, from about **1947 to 1991**.
- The term was popularised by journalist **Walter Lippmann** (1947); **Bernard Baruch** used it in a 1947 speech (George Orwell had used the phrase in 1945).
- 🔥 **"Iron Curtain"** speech: **Winston Churchill**, **Fulton, Missouri (USA), 5 March 1946**.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1946 | **George Kennan's "Long Telegram"** → policy of **containment** |
| 🔥 1947 | **Truman Doctrine**: US help to countries resisting communism (Greece, Turkey) |
| 🔥 1948 | **Marshall Plan** (European Recovery Program): US aid to rebuild Western Europe |
| 1948–49 | **Berlin Blockade** by the USSR; Western **Berlin Airlift** |
| 🔥 **4 April 1949** | **NATO** founded |
| 1949 | USSR tested its first atomic bomb; **Communist victory in China** |
| 1950–53 | **Korean War** |
| 🔥 **14 May 1955** | **Warsaw Pact** (USSR-led; dissolved **1991**) |
| 1957 | USSR launched **Sputnik 1**, the first artificial satellite (space race) |
| 1961 | **Berlin Wall** built (13 August); **Yuri Gagarin**, first man in space |
| 🔥 **October 1962** | **Cuban Missile Crisis** (**Kennedy vs Khrushchev**): closest to nuclear war; "13 days" |
| 1955–75 | **Vietnam War** |
| 1970s | **Détente**; **SALT I** (1972) |
| 1979 | **Soviet invasion of Afghanistan** |
| 1985 | **Mikhail Gorbachev**: ***glasnost*** (openness) and ***perestroika*** (restructuring) |
| 🔥 **9 November 1989** | **Fall of the Berlin Wall**; December 1989 **Malta Summit** (Bush–Gorbachev) |
| 3 October 1990 | **German reunification** |
| 🔥 **26 December 1991** | **USSR dissolved** (Gorbachev resigned on 25 December): end of the Cold War |

### Non-Aligned Movement (NAM / জোটনিরপেক্ষ আন্দোলন)
- Grew from the **Bandung Conference (Indonesia, 1955)** of Asian and African states.
- First summit: 🔥 **Belgrade, 1961**. Founding leaders: **Nehru** (India), **Nasser** (Egypt), **Tito** (Yugoslavia), **Sukarno** (Indonesia), **Nkrumah** (Ghana).
- 🔥 **Bangladesh joined NAM in 1973** at the **Algiers** summit, attended by **Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman**.

## Quick revision
- WWII: **1 September 1939 – 2 September 1945**; started with the invasion of **Poland**.
- Axis: **Germany, Italy, Japan**; Pearl Harbor: **7 December 1941**.
- D-Day: **6 June 1944**, Normandy; Hiroshima **6 August**, Nagasaki **9 August 1945**.
- Atlantic Charter **1941** (Churchill–Roosevelt); Yalta **Feb 1945**; Potsdam **July–Aug 1945**.
- "Iron Curtain": **Churchill, 1946**; Truman Doctrine **1947**; Marshall Plan **1948**.
- NATO **1949**; Warsaw Pact **1955**; Cuban Missile Crisis **1962**.
- Berlin Wall: built **1961**, fell **9 Nov 1989**; USSR dissolved **26 Dec 1991**.
- NAM first summit **Belgrade 1961**; Bangladesh joined **1973 (Algiers)**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** World War II began when Germany invaded —
(a) France (b) Czechoslovakia (c) Poland (d) Austria

**2.** Which country was NOT an Axis Power?
(a) Germany (b) Italy (c) Japan (d) China

**3.** Japan attacked Pearl Harbor on —
(a) 7 December 1941 (b) 1 September 1939 (c) 6 June 1944 (d) 6 August 1945

**4.** The atomic bomb "Fat Man" was dropped on —
(a) Tokyo (b) Hiroshima (c) Osaka (d) Nagasaki

**5.** The Atlantic Charter was signed in 1941 by —
(a) Stalin and Churchill (b) Churchill and Roosevelt (c) Roosevelt and Stalin (d) Truman and Attlee

**6.** Who gave the famous "Iron Curtain" speech?
(a) Winston Churchill (b) Harry Truman (c) George Kennan (d) Joseph Stalin

**7.** The first summit of the Non-Aligned Movement was held in —
(a) Bandung (b) Cairo (c) Belgrade (d) Algiers

**8.** The Berlin Wall fell in —
(a) 1961 (b) 1985 (c) 1991 (d) 1989

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | Germany invaded Poland on 1 September 1939 |
| 2 | d | China fought on the Allied side against Japan |
| 3 | a | The attack on 7 December 1941 brought the USA into the war |
| 4 | d | Fat Man hit Nagasaki on 9 August 1945; Little Boy hit Hiroshima |
| 5 | b | Churchill and Roosevelt, off Newfoundland, 14 August 1941 |
| 6 | a | Churchill, Fulton, Missouri, 5 March 1946 |
| 7 | c | Belgrade, 1961; Bandung (1955) was the forerunner |
| 8 | d | The wall fell on 9 November 1989 |
