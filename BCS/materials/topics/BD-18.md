# Constitution: amendments

> **Why it matters:** "Which amendment did X?" is asked almost every year: emergency (2nd), one-party system (4th), state religion (8th), return of parliamentary system (12th), caretaker government (13th and 15th), removal of judges (16th), women's seats (17th).

## How the Constitution is amended 🔥
- **Article 142** (Part X): a bill to amend must be passed by at least **two-thirds of the total members** of Parliament.
- The President must assent within **7 days**; otherwise it is deemed assented.
- **Article 7B** (added 2011): the preamble, Parts I and II, most of Part III, and some other basic provisions **cannot be amended**.
- The Constitution has been amended **17 times** (1973–2018) up to the 17th amendment.

## Amendments table 🔥🔥
| No. | Year | Main change |
|---|---|---|
| **1st** | 1973 | Allowed **trial of war criminals**; added **Art. 47(3)** and **47A** |
| 🔥 **2nd** | 1973 | Added **emergency provisions (Part IXA)**; preventive detention |
| **3rd** | 1974 | Implemented the **Indira–Mujib Land Boundary Agreement (1974)** (Berubari exchange) |
| 🔥 **4th** | **25 Jan 1975** | **Presidential system**; **one-party** system (BAKSAL); curtailed judicial independence |
| 🔥 **5th** | 1979 | Ratified all martial-law acts from **15 Aug 1975 to 9 April 1979**; added **"Bismillah"**; replaced secularism with **"absolute trust and faith in Almighty Allah"**; "Bangladeshi" nationalism |
| **6th** | 1981 | Allowed the **Vice-President** to contest the presidential election without resigning (for Abdus Sattar) |
| **7th** | 1986 | Ratified **Ershad's martial-law** period (24 March 1982 – 11 Nov 1986) |
| 🔥 **8th** | 1988 | **Islam as state religion (Art. 2A)**; permanent **High Court benches outside Dhaka** (struck down, 1989); spelling changes **Dacca → Dhaka, Bengali → Bangla** |
| **9th** | 1989 | President and Vice-President to be **elected together** by direct vote; President limited to **two consecutive terms** |
| **10th** | 1990 | Extended **30 reserved women's seats** for another 10 years |
| **11th** | 1991 | Validated **Justice Shahabuddin Ahmed's** appointment as Vice-President/acting President and allowed his return to the Supreme Court |
| 🔥 **12th** | **1991** | **Parliamentary system restored**; President elected by Parliament; post of **Vice-President abolished**. Confirmed by **referendum (15 Sept 1991)** |
| 🔥 **13th** | **1996** | **Non-party caretaker government** for elections (Art. 58B–58E) |
| **14th** | 2004 | Women's reserved seats raised to **45**; retirement age of Supreme Court judges **65 → 67**; display of portraits of President and PM |
| 🔥 **15th** | **2011** (30 June) | **Abolished the caretaker system**; restored the four principles incl. **secularism** (kept Islam as state religion and Bismillah); women's seats **50**; added **Art. 7A, 7B, 18A, 23A, 4A**; added **5th–7th schedules** (7 March speech, 26 March declaration, 10 April Proclamation); elections within **90 days before** Parliament's term ends |
| 🔥 **16th** | **2014** | Returned the power to **remove Supreme Court judges to Parliament** (as in 1972) |
| **17th** | **2018** | Extended **50 reserved women's seats** for another **25 years** |

## Women's reserved seats — history 🔥
| Stage | Seats |
|---|---|
| Original constitution (1972) | **15** (for 10 years) |
| Martial-law proclamation (1978) | **30** |
| 10th amendment (1990) | 30, extended |
| 14th amendment (2004) | **45** |
| 15th amendment (2011) | **50** |
| 17th amendment (2018) | 50, extended for **25 years** |

## Court judgments on amendments 🔥
| Amendment | Judgment |
|---|---|
| **5th** | Declared **unconstitutional** by the **High Court Division (2005)**; upheld by the **Appellate Division (2010)** |
| **7th** | Declared **unconstitutional** (High Court 2010; Appellate Division 2011) |
| **8th** | The part creating **High Court benches outside Dhaka** was struck down in **1989** (the **Anwar Hossain Chowdhury case**) — the case that established the **"basic structure"** idea in Bangladesh |
| **13th** | The **Appellate Division** declared the caretaker system **void** on **10 May 2011** (prospectively, observing that the next two elections could be held under it if Parliament wished). The **15th amendment** then abolished it |
| **16th** | Declared **unconstitutional** by the **High Court Division (5 May 2016)**; upheld by the **Appellate Division (3 July 2017)**. Removal of judges went back to the **Supreme Judicial Council** |

> 🔄 Update: after August 2024, the 13th, 15th and 16th amendment judgments came before the courts again, and the interim government's reform commissions proposed wider constitutional changes (for example restoring a caretaker system and a referendum). The table above gives the position as written in the Constitution up to the 17th amendment. Check the latest court decisions and any new amendment before the exam.

## Quick revision
- Amendment needs **two-thirds** of total MPs (**Art. 142**).
- **2nd (1973)**: emergency provisions.
- **4th (1975)**: presidential + one-party system.
- **5th (1979)** and **7th (1986)**: validated martial law; both later declared unconstitutional.
- **8th (1988)**: Islam as state religion.
- **12th (1991)**: parliamentary system restored; referendum 15 Sept 1991.
- **13th (1996)**: caretaker government; declared void **10 May 2011**.
- **15th (2011)**: caretaker abolished; secularism restored; 50 women's seats.
- **16th (2014)**: judges' removal by Parliament; struck down (2016, 2017).
- **17th (2018)**: women's seats extended 25 years.
