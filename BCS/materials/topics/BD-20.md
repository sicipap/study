# Executive: President, PM, cabinet

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask how the President is elected, which two appointments he makes on his own, who acts as President, the first President/PM, the first woman PM, caretaker heads, and the cabinet's collective responsibility.

## The President (রাষ্ট্রপতি) 🔥
- **Head of State**; elected by the **Members of Parliament** (Art. 48).
- Qualifications: citizen, at least **35 years** old, qualified to be an MP.
- Term: **5 years**; no one may be President for more than **two terms** (Art. 50).
- 🔥 He acts on the **Prime Minister's advice**, **except** when appointing (Art. 48(3)):
  1. the **Prime Minister**, and
  2. the **Chief Justice**.
- Removal: **impeachment** by a **two-thirds** vote of MPs (Art. 52), or on grounds of incapacity (Art. 53).
- 🔥 If the office is vacant or the President is absent, the **Speaker** acts as President (Art. 54).
- Resigns by writing to the **Speaker**.

### Powers of the President
| Power | Article |
|---|---|
| Prerogative of **mercy** (pardon) | 49 |
| Appoints the PM and other ministers | 56 |
| **Supreme commander** of the defence services | 61 |
| Appoints the **Attorney-General** | 64 |
| Summons, prorogues and **dissolves Parliament** (on PM's advice) | 72 |
| Assent to bills (within 15 days) | 80 |
| Issues **ordinances** | 93 |
| Appoints judges, CEC and ECs, CAG, PSC members | 95, 118, 127, 138 |
| Proclaims **emergency** (with PM's countersignature) | 141A |

## The Prime Minister (প্রধানমন্ত্রী) 🔥
- The **head of government**; real executive power is exercised by or on the authority of the PM (Art. 55).
- 🔥 The President appoints as PM the MP who **appears to command the support of the majority** of MPs (Art. 56(3)).
- The PM's office falls vacant if he/she resigns, ceases to be an MP, or loses majority support (Art. 57).
- Leader of the House in Parliament.

## The Cabinet (মন্ত্রিসভা) 🔥
- Headed by the PM; **collectively responsible to Parliament** (Art. 55).
- Ranks: **Minister**, **State Minister (প্রতিমন্ত্রী)**, **Deputy Minister (উপমন্ত্রী)**.
- 🔥 At least **nine-tenths** of ministers must be MPs; up to **one-tenth** may be non-MPs ("technocrat ministers") (Art. 56(2)).
- Government business is run under the **Rules of Business** (1996) and the **Allocation of Business**.
- **Cabinet Division**, headed by the **Cabinet Secretary** — the senior-most civil servant. The **Bangladesh Secretariat** is in Dhaka.
- Warrant of precedence (first three): **President → Prime Minister → Speaker**.

## Quick revision
- President elected by **MPs** (Art. 48); age **35**; term **5 years**, max **two terms**.
- On his own he appoints only the **PM** and the **Chief Justice**.
- **Speaker** acts as President (Art. 54); impeachment needs **two-thirds** (Art. 52).
- PM = MP commanding **majority** (Art. 56(3)).
- Cabinet **collectively responsible** to Parliament (Art. 55).
- Up to **1/10** of ministers may be non-MPs.
- First President **Sheikh Mujibur Rahman**; first PM **Tajuddin Ahmad**.
- First woman PM **Khaleda Zia** (1991).
- First Chief Adviser (13th amendment): **Justice Muhammad Habibur Rahman** (1996).

(a) Directly by the people (b) Members of Parliament (c) The cabinet (d) Local government representatives

**2.** The President can appoint which of these WITHOUT the Prime Minister's advice?
(a) Chief Election Commissioner (b) Attorney-General (c) Chief Justice (d) Cabinet Secretary

**3.** Who acts as President if the office falls vacant?
(a) Chief Justice (b) Prime Minister (c) Deputy Speaker (d) Speaker

**4.** Who was the first Prime Minister of Bangladesh?
(a) Tajuddin Ahmad (b) Sheikh Mujibur Rahman (c) Syed Nazrul Islam (d) M. Mansur Ali

**5.** The minimum age to be President of Bangladesh is —
(a) 25 years (b) 30 years (c) 35 years (d) 40 years

**6.** Who was the first Chief Adviser of a caretaker government under the 13th amendment?
(a) Justice Shahabuddin Ahmed (b) Justice Muhammad Habibur Rahman (c) Justice Latifur Rahman (d) Fakhruddin Ahmed

**7.** The cabinet is collectively responsible to —
(a) the President (b) the Supreme Court (c) the people directly (d) Parliament

**8.** Who was the first President elected by direct popular vote?
(a) Abdur Rahman Biswas (b) Justice Abu Sayeed Chowdhury (c) H. M. Ershad (d) Ziaur Rahman
