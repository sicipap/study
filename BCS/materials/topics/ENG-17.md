# Preposition & appropriate preposition

> **Why it matters:** "Appropriate preposition" is asked in almost every job exam: *angry with/at, abide by, comply with, congratulate on, die of/from, differ from/with, superior to*. Learn the fixed pairs.

## Basic prepositions of time and place
| Use | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | exact time, small point | at 5 pm, at night, at the gate |
| **on** | days and dates, surface | on Friday, on 16 December, on the table |
| **in** | months, years, seasons, parts of day, big places | in 1971, in July, in the morning, in Dhaka |
| **since** | point of time (perfect tense) | since 2010, since Monday |
| **for** | period | for two hours |
| **by** | not later than | by 5 o'clock |
| **within** | before the end of a period | within a week |
| **between** | two | between you and me |
| **among** | more than two | Divide the mangoes among the boys. |
| **beside** | by the side of | He sat beside me. |
| **besides** | in addition to | Besides English, he knows French. |
| **into** | movement inside | He jumped into the river. |
| **in** | position inside | He is swimming in the river. |

### Noun + preposition
**access to, answer to, cure for, desire for, key to, reason for, respect for, solution to, sympathy for / with, lack of, need for, apology for**.
- "There is no **cure for** this disease." — "**Key to** the door." — "**Solution to** the problem."

## Rules to remember
- **Verb + no preposition** 🔥: **discuss** (not *discuss about*), **describe**, **enter** (a room), **reach** (a place), **order**, **resemble**, **attack**, **marry**, **request**, **investigate**, **emphasize**, **comprise**. "We discussed the matter." "She married a doctor." "He resembles his father."
- **Preposition + gerund**: "He is fond **of reading**."
- Do not put a preposition at the end if it is already used earlier: "the house in which he lives" = "the house which he lives **in**", not both.
- **On/at the time of** vs **in time** (early enough) vs **on time** (punctual).
- **By bus, by train, on foot, in a car**.

## Common mistakes
| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| He is angry on me. | He is angry **with** me. |
| We discussed about the matter. | We **discussed** the matter. |
| He died with cholera. | He died **of** cholera. |
| I congratulate you for your success. | I congratulate you **on** your success. |
| He is senior than me. | He is senior **to** me. |
| She is married with a doctor. | She is married **to** a doctor. |
| He entered into the room. | He **entered** the room. |
| He is good in English. | He is good **at** English. |
| Divide it between the three boys. | Divide it **among** the three boys. |

## Quick revision
- 🔥 **abide by, comply with, congratulate on, insist on, refrain from**.
- 🔥 **die of** a disease, **from** a cause, **for** a cause/country.
- **angry with** a person, **at** a thing.
- **agree with** a person, **to** a proposal.
- **between** two, **among** more than two.
- **discuss, enter, reach, resemble, marry** take no preposition.
- **Latin comparatives + to**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** "Everyone must abide ___ the law."
(a) with (b) by (c) to (d) in

**2.** "He died ___ malaria."
(a) of (b) from (c) with (d) by

**3.** "I congratulate you ___ your brilliant result."
(a) for (b) at (c) on (d) with

**4.** "She is angry ___ her brother."
(a) at (b) on (c) against (d) with

**5.** "You must comply ___ the rules."
(a) to (b) with (c) by (d) for

**6.** Which sentence is correct?
(a) We discussed about the plan. (b) He entered into the room. (c) She resembles to her mother. (d) We discussed the plan.

**7.** "He is suffering ___ fever."
(a) from (b) with (c) of (d) by

**8.** "Divide the sweets ___ the four children."
(a) between (b) among (c) in (d) with
