# Mixture & alligation

> **Why it matters:** Mixture (মিশ্রণ) questions — mixing two qualities of rice/tea, milk and water, replacing liquid — appear in bank and 9th–20th grade exams. The alligation rule (মিশ্রণ নিয়ম) turns them into one-line sums.

## Key concepts
- **Mean price (গড় মূল্য):** the cost per unit of the mixture.
- **Mean price when quantities are known** = total cost ÷ total quantity.
- **Rule of alligation:** when two ingredients of prices c (cheaper) and d (dearer) are mixed to get mean price m:
  - 🔥 **Quantity of cheaper : quantity of dearer = (d − m) : (m − c)**
- Draw it as a cross:

| Cheaper c | | Dearer d |
|---|---|---|
| | Mean m | |
| d − m | | m − c |

- Water is treated as an ingredient with price **0**.
- In milk–water problems, the **milk quantity stays the same when only water is added**.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Situation | Formula |
|---|---|
| Mean price | (q₁p₁ + q₂p₂)/(q₁ + q₂) |
| 🔥 Alligation | cheaper : dearer = (d − m) : (m − c) |
| Water added to milk worth p to sell at m (per litre) | water : milk = (p − m) : m |
| Milkman sells at cost price and gains g% | water : milk = g : 100 |
| 🔥 Repeated replacement: V litres, remove x litres and replace with water n times | Liquid left = V(1 − x/V)ⁿ |
| Two mixtures mixed in equal amounts | Average the milk fractions |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** In what ratio must rice at Tk 40/kg be mixed with rice at Tk 55/kg to get a mixture worth Tk 50/kg?
Steps: (55 − 50) : (50 − 40) = 5 : 10 = 1 : 2.
**Answer: 1 : 2**

**Example 2.** A 60-litre mixture has milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 1 : 2?
Steps: Milk 40, water 20. 40 : (20 + x) = 1 : 2 → 20 + x = 80 → x = 60.
**Answer: 60 litres**

**Example 3.** A container holds 40 litres of milk. 4 litres are taken out and replaced with water; this is done twice. How much milk is left?
Steps: 40 × (1 − 4/40)² = 40 × 0.81 = 32.4.
**Answer: 32.4 litres**

**Example 4.** Tea at Tk 120/kg and Tk 150/kg is mixed in the ratio 2 : 3. Find the price of the mixture.
Steps: (2 × 120 + 3 × 150)/5 = (240 + 450)/5 = 138.
**Answer: Tk 138/kg**

**Example 5.** How much tea at Tk 150/kg must be mixed with 6 kg of tea at Tk 200/kg to get a mixture worth Tk 180/kg?
Steps: Cheaper : dearer = (200 − 180) : (180 − 150) = 20 : 30 = 2 : 3. With 6 kg dearer → cheaper = 4 kg.
**Answer: 4 kg**

## Common traps
- Writing the alligation ratio the wrong way round: the **cheaper** quantity goes with **(dearer − mean)**.
- Adding water changes the water part only; the milk quantity is fixed.
- In repeated replacement, use the power n; do not subtract 4 litres twice (that gives 32, not 32.4).
- The mean price must lie **between** the two prices.

## Quick revision
- Mean price = total cost ÷ total quantity.
- Cheaper : dearer = (d − m) : (m − c).
- Water costs 0.
- Gain g% by adding water at cost price ⇒ water : milk = g : 100.
- Replacement: V(1 − x/V)ⁿ.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** In what ratio must rice at Tk 30/kg be mixed with rice at Tk 45/kg to get a mixture worth Tk 40/kg?
(a) 2 : 1 (b) 1 : 2 (c) 3 : 2 (d) 1 : 3

**2.** 3 kg of sugar at Tk 50/kg is mixed with 2 kg at Tk 60/kg. The price of the mixture per kg is:
(a) Tk 55 (b) Tk 56 (c) Tk 54 (d) Tk 52

**3.** A 40-litre mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 3 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 3 : 2?
(a) 10 litres (b) 20 litres (c) 5 litres (d) 15 litres

**4.** 30 litres of a solution contain 20% alcohol. How much water must be added to make it 15% alcohol?
(a) 5 litres (b) 8 litres (c) 12 litres (d) 10 litres

**5.** A vessel holds 80 litres of milk. 8 litres are removed and replaced by water; this is done twice. The milk left is:
(a) 64 litres (b) 64.8 litres (c) 72 litres (d) 65.6 litres

**6.** In what ratio must water be mixed with milk costing Tk 60 per litre to get a mixture worth Tk 50 per litre?
(a) 1 : 6 (b) 5 : 1 (c) 1 : 5 (d) 1 : 4

**7.** Two vessels contain milk and water in the ratios 3 : 1 and 5 : 3. Equal quantities are mixed. The ratio of milk to water in the new mixture is:
(a) 8 : 4 (b) 4 : 1 (c) 11 : 5 (d) 15 : 3

**8.** A milkman sells milk at cost price but mixes in water and gains 25%. The ratio of water to milk is:
(a) 1 : 4 (b) 1 : 5 (c) 1 : 3 (d) 4 : 1

**9.** How much tea at Tk 150/kg must be mixed with 6 kg of tea at Tk 200/kg to get a mixture worth Tk 180/kg?
(a) 9 kg (b) 3 kg (c) 6 kg (d) 4 kg

**10.** Sugar at Tk 36/kg and Tk 48/kg is mixed in the ratio 1 : 3. The price of the mixture is:
(a) Tk 42 (b) Tk 45 (c) Tk 39 (d) Tk 44

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | (45 − 40) : (40 − 30) = 5 : 10 = 1 : 2 |
| 2 | c | (150 + 120)/5 = 54 |
| 3 | a | Milk 30, water 10; 30/(10 + x) = 3/2 → x = 10 |
| 4 | d | Alcohol 6 L = 15% of 40 L → add 10 L |
| 5 | b | 80 × (0.9)² = 64.8 |
| 6 | c | Water (price 0) : milk = (60 − 50) : (50 − 0) = 1 : 5 |
| 7 | c | Milk fraction (3/4 + 5/8)/2 = 11/16 → 11 : 5 |
| 8 | a | Water : milk = 25 : 100 = 1 : 4 |
| 9 | d | Cheaper : dearer = 20 : 30 = 2 : 3 → 6 kg dearer needs 4 kg cheaper |
| 10 | b | (36 + 3 × 48)/4 = 180/4 = 45 |
