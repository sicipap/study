# World literature: Greek, Russian, French classics

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask "Who wrote *Oedipus Rex* / *War and Peace* / *Madame Bovary*?", "Who is the father of tragedy?", and characters such as Raskolnikov or Jean Valjean.

## A. Greek (and Latin) classics 🔥
| Writer | Works / facts |
|---|---|
| **Homer** 🔥 | ***Iliad*** (Trojan War: **Achilles, Hector, Agamemnon, Helen, Paris**) and ***Odyssey*** (**Odysseus/Ulysses** returns to **Ithaca**; wife **Penelope**, son **Telemachus**; the Cyclops **Polyphemus**). Called the blind poet. |
| **Hesiod** | *Works and Days*, *Theogony* |
| **Sappho** | Lyric poetess of Lesbos ("the tenth Muse") |
| **Pindar** | Odes (Pindaric ode) |
| **Aesop** | Fables |
| **Aeschylus** 🔥 | **Father of tragedy**; trilogy ***Oresteia*** (*Agamemnon*, *The Libation Bearers*, *The Eumenides*); *Prometheus Bound* |
| **Sophocles** 🔥 | ***Oedipus Rex (Oedipus the King)***, ***Antigone***, *Electra*, *Oedipus at Colonus*. Oedipus kills his father and marries his mother unknowingly |
| **Euripides** | ***Medea***, *The Trojan Women*, *The Bacchae*, *Hippolytus* |
| **Aristophanes** 🔥 | **Father of comedy**; *The Clouds* (mocks Socrates), *The Frogs*, *The Birds*, *Lysistrata*, *The Wasps* |
| **Herodotus** | **Father of history**; *The Histories* |
| **Plato** | ***The Republic*** (ideal state, philosopher-king), *Symposium*, *Apology* |
| **Aristotle** 🔥 | ***Poetics*** (first work of literary criticism; tragedy, **catharsis**, **mimesis**, **hamartia**), ***Politics*** |
| **Virgil** (Latin) | ***Aeneid*** (hero **Aeneas**, founding of Rome) |
| **Ovid** (Latin) | ***Metamorphoses*** |

**Three great Greek tragedians:** Aeschylus, Sophocles, Euripides.

## Quick revision
- Father of tragedy **Aeschylus**; father of comedy **Aristophanes**; father of history **Herodotus**.
- ***Oedipus Rex*** = **Sophocles**; ***Medea*** = **Euripides**; ***Poetics*** = **Aristotle**.
- ***War and Peace***, ***Anna Karenina*** = **Tolstoy**; ***Crime and Punishment*** (Raskolnikov) = **Dostoevsky**.
- ***The Cherry Orchard*** = **Chekhov**; ***Mother*** = **Gorky**; ***Fathers and Sons*** = **Turgenev**.
- ***Les Misérables*** (Jean Valjean) = **Hugo**; ***Madame Bovary*** = **Flaubert**; ***Candide*** = **Voltaire**.
- ***The Three Musketeers*** = **Dumas**; ***The Stranger*** = **Camus**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** *Oedipus Rex* was written by:
(a) Aeschylus (b) Euripides (c) Sophocles (d) Aristophanes

**2.** Who is called the "father of tragedy"?
(a) Aeschylus (b) Homer (c) Sophocles (d) Aristotle

**3.** Raskolnikov is the central character of:
(a) War and Peace (b) Crime and Punishment (c) Fathers and Sons (d) Dead Souls

**4.** *The Cherry Orchard* is a play by:
(a) Gogol (b) Tolstoy (c) Gorky (d) Chekhov

**5.** *Les Misérables* was written by:
(a) Victor Hugo (b) Alexandre Dumas (c) Émile Zola (d) Balzac

**6.** Emma is the heroine of Flaubert's:
(a) Germinal (b) Candide (c) Madame Bovary (d) The Stranger

**7.** *War and Peace* is set against the background of:
(a) the Crimean War (b) Napoleon's invasion of Russia (c) the Russian Revolution (d) World War I

**8.** *Poetics*, the earliest work of literary criticism, was written by:
(a) Plato (b) Horace (c) Homer (d) Aristotle

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | Sophocles' tragedy of Oedipus |
| 2 | a | Aeschylus, author of the *Oresteia* |
| 3 | b | Dostoevsky's 1866 novel |
| 4 | d | Chekhov's last play |
| 5 | a | Hugo, 1862; Jean Valjean |
| 6 | c | *Madame Bovary*, 1857 |
| 7 | b | Napoleon's invasion of 1812 |
| 8 | d | Aristotle's *Poetics*: catharsis, mimesis |
