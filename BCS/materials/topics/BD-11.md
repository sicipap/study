# 1970 election & 7 March speech

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the seat numbers of the 1970 election (160/162, 167/169, 288/300), the two non-Awami League winners, the events of 1–3 March 1971, and facts about the 7 March speech (place, length, UNESCO recognition).

## 1970 general election 🔥🔥
- Held under **Yahya Khan's Legal Framework Order (LFO)**, on the basis of **population**.
- **National Assembly:** **7 December 1970**. **Provincial Assembly:** **17 December 1970**.
- The **Bhola cyclone of 12 November 1970** (one of the deadliest cyclones in history) delayed polling in some coastal seats to January 1971.
- Awami League's symbol: **নৌকা (boat)**; it campaigned on the **Six-point**.

### National Assembly results
| | Total | East Pakistan | Awami League won |
|---|---|---|---|
| General seats | **300** | **162** | 🔥 **160** |
| Reserved women's seats | 13 | 7 | 7 |
| Total | **313** | **169** | 🔥 **167** |

- The Awami League won an **absolute majority** of the whole National Assembly (167 of 313) — although it won **no seat in West Pakistan**.
- 🔥 The two East Pakistan general seats **not** won by the Awami League went to **নুরুল আমিন** (PDP) and **রাজা ত্রিদিব রায়** (Chakma chief, independent).
- **জুলফিকার আলী ভুট্টো**'s **Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP)** came second with **81** general seats (all in the West; 88 with women's seats).

### East Pakistan Provincial Assembly results
- Total **310** seats (300 general + 10 women). 🔥 Awami League won **288 general** + **10 women's** = **298**.

## From victory to non-cooperation (March 1971) 🔥
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **1 March 1971** | Yahya **postponed** the National Assembly session (due on 3 March) → spontaneous protests |
| **2 March 1971** | 🔥 The **first flag of independent Bangladesh** was hoisted at the **Kala Bhaban (বটতলা), Dhaka University** by **আ স ম আবদুর রব** (DUCSU VP) |
| **3 March 1971** | **স্বাধীন বাংলা ছাত্র সংগ্রাম পরিষদ** read the **Manifesto of Independence (স্বাধীনতার ইশতেহার)** at **Paltan** (read by **শাজাহান সিরাজ**); "আমার সোনার বাংলা" was named the national anthem and Bangabandhu the leader of the movement |
| **7 March 1971** | Bangabandhu's historic speech at the Race Course |
| 16–24 March | Mujib–Yahya talks (Bhutto also joined) |
| **23 March 1971** | Observed as **প্রতিরোধ দিবস**; the Bangladesh flag was flown across the province, including at Bangabandhu's house (Dhanmondi 32) |
| 25 March 1971 | Talks collapse; **Operation Searchlight** (see BD-12) |

## The 7 March speech (৭ই মার্চের ভাষণ) 🔥🔥
- Place: **রেসকোর্স ময়দান** (now **সোহরাওয়ার্দী উদ্যান**), Dhaka.
- Length: about **18–19 minutes** (commonly **18 minutes**); about **1,100 words**.
- 🔥 Key line: **"এবারের সংগ্রাম আমাদের মুক্তির সংগ্রাম, এবারের সংগ্রাম স্বাধীনতার সংগ্রাম।"**
- Other famous lines: **"ঘরে ঘরে দুর্গ গড়ে তোলো"**, **"রক্ত যখন দিয়েছি, রক্ত আরও দেবো…"**.
- It announced a **non-cooperation movement** and put **four conditions** before joining the Assembly:
  1. **Withdraw martial law**
  2. **Send the army back to the barracks**
  3. **Inquire into the killings**
  4. **Hand over power to the elected representatives**
- 🔥 **UNESCO** listed the speech in its **Memory of the World International Register** on **30 October 2017** (documentary heritage).
- The speech was placed in the **Fifth Schedule** of the Constitution by the **15th amendment (2011)**.
- *Newsweek* (April 1971) called Bangabandhu the **"Poet of Politics"**.

> 🔄 Update: the list of national days and some constitutional provisions linked to this period (including the schedules) were revised or proposed for reform after August 2024. Check the current position before the exam.

## Quick revision
- Election dates: NA **7 Dec 1970**; PA **17 Dec 1970**.
- AL: **160/162** general seats in East; **167/313** in total NA.
- Non-AL winners in East: **Nurul Amin** and **Raja Tridiv Roy**.
- Provincial: AL **288** general + 10 women = **298/310**.
- PPP (Bhutto): **81** general seats.
- First flag hoisted: **2 March 1971**, **A. S. M. Abdur Rab**, DU Kala Bhaban.
- Manifesto of Independence: **3 March 1971**, Paltan, read by **Shahjahan Siraj**.
- 7 March speech: Race Course; ~**18 minutes**; **4 demands**.
- UNESCO Memory of the World: **30 October 2017**.

**2.** The National Assembly election of 1970 was held on —
(a) 17 December 1970 (b) 12 November 1970 (c) 7 December 1970 (d) 1 March 1971

**3.** Which of these leaders won a National Assembly seat in East Pakistan in 1970 without the Awami League?
(a) Maulana Bhashani (b) A. K. Fazlul Huq (c) Z. A. Bhutto (d) Nurul Amin

(a) Shahjahan Siraj (b) Tofail Ahmed (c) Nur-e-Alam Siddiqui (d) A. S. M. Abdur Rab

**5.** Where was the 7 March 1971 speech delivered?
(a) Race Course Maidan (b) Paltan Maidan (c) Dhaka University Bat-tala (d) Shaheed Minar

**6.** UNESCO included the 7 March speech in the Memory of the World Register on —
(a) 17 November 1999 (b) 30 October 2017 (c) 7 March 2017 (d) 26 March 2018

**7.** How many conditions did Bangabandhu set in the 7 March speech?

**8.** How many seats did the Awami League win in the East Pakistan Provincial Assembly (including women's seats)?
(a) 288 (b) 310 (c) 298 (d) 300

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | 160 of 162 general seats; 167 with women's seats |
| 2 | c | NA poll 7 Dec; provincial poll 17 Dec 1970 |
| 3 | d | Nurul Amin (PDP) and Raja Tridiv Roy were the two non-AL winners |
| 4 | d | A. S. M. Abdur Rab, DUCSU VP, at the Kala Bhaban of Dhaka University |
| 5 | a | Race Course, now Suhrawardy Udyan |
| 6 | b | Memory of the World International Register, 30 October 2017 |
| 7 | a | Four conditions: withdraw martial law, army to barracks, inquiry, transfer power |
