# Paper folding & cutting

> **Why it matters:** A sheet of paper is folded, punched or cut, and you must say how it looks when opened. The questions are usually pictures, but the logic is simple: every fold is a mirror line, and every layer gets the same hole or cut.

## Core rules 🔥
| Rule | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Each fold doubles the layers** | 1 fold → 2 layers; 2 folds → 4; 3 folds → 8 (if each fold covers the whole folded sheet) |
| **One punch goes through every layer** | Holes after unfolding = holes punched × number of layers |
| **Unfolding = reflecting** | Each hole is reflected across the fold line, like a mirror image |
| **Distance is kept** | A hole 1 cm from the fold appears 1 cm from the fold on the other side too |
| **Unfold in reverse order** | The last fold is opened first |

**Formula:** holes = h × 2ⁿ, where h = holes punched and n = number of full folds.

## Picturing it with a grid
Divide a square sheet into four quarters:
```
+------+------+
|  TL  |  TR  |
+------+------+
|  BL  |  BR  |
+------+------+
```
- **Fold 1:** fold the top half down onto the bottom half. Now TL lies on BL and TR lies on BR.
- **Fold 2:** fold the right half onto the left half. Now all four quarters are stacked on BL.
- **Punch one hole** in the stack → after unfolding there is **one hole in every quarter**, placed symmetrically (reflected across both centre lines).

## Diagonal folds
Folding a square along a diagonal makes the diagonal the mirror line.
```
Square with corners TL, TR, BL, BR.
Fold along the diagonal TL-BR, bringing the BL corner onto the TR corner.
A hole punched near the TR corner of the folded triangle
→ after unfolding: holes near TR AND near BL.
```
The hole's mirror image across the TL–BR diagonal lands at the opposite (BL) corner.

## Cutting a folded paper
- **A strip folded in half and cut once across** (not on the fold) gives **3 pieces**: the cut goes through 2 layers, making 2 cuts in the open strip.
- A cut **along the fold line itself** only separates the two halves → 2 pieces.
- A **notch cut on the fold** opens into a shape that is **symmetric about the fold** (a triangle notch becomes a diamond; a semicircle becomes a full circle).

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** A square sheet is folded in half once and one hole is punched. How many holes after unfolding?
1 × 2¹ = **2 holes**, mirror images across the fold.

**Example 2.** A sheet is folded in half, then in half again, and two holes are punched. How many holes?
2 × 2² = **8 holes**.

**Example 3.** A square sheet is folded along its vertical centre line. A hole is punched near the top edge, close to the fold. Where are the holes after unfolding?
**Two holes near the top, one on each side of the centre line, at equal distances from it.**

**Example 4.** A semicircle is cut out of the folded edge of a sheet folded in half. What shape appears when opened?
The semicircle is reflected across the fold → **a full circle**.

**Example 5.** A paper strip is folded in half and one straight cut is made across it, away from the fold. How many pieces?
Two layers cut → two cuts in the open strip → **3 pieces**.

## Quick revision
- Every fold is a mirror line; unfold by reflecting.
- Holes = punched holes × 2ⁿ for n full folds.
- 1 fold → 2, 2 folds → 4, 3 folds → 8 layers.
- Unfold the last fold first.
- A shape cut on the fold opens into a symmetric shape (semicircle → circle).
- A strip folded once and cut once across → 3 pieces.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** A square sheet of paper is folded in half once, and one hole is punched through it. How many holes will there be when it is unfolded?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4

**2.** A sheet is folded in half and then in half again. One hole is punched through all layers. How many holes appear when it is unfolded?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 8

**3.** A sheet is folded in half three times (each fold covering the whole folded sheet). One hole is punched. How many holes appear when it is unfolded?
(a) 8 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 16

**4.** A sheet is folded in half twice and then two holes are punched through all layers. How many holes appear when it is unfolded?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 16 (d) 8

**5.** A square sheet is folded along its vertical centre line. A hole is punched near the top edge, close to the fold. Where will the holes be after unfolding?
(a) One hole at the centre (b) Two holes, one at the top and one at the bottom (c) Two holes near the top, one on each side of the centre line (d) Four holes, one at each corner

**6.** A square sheet with quarters TL, TR, BL, BR is folded top half down onto the bottom half, then right half onto the left half. One hole is punched through the stack. In which quarters will holes appear after unfolding?
(a) Only BL (b) BL and BR (c) BL and TL (d) All four quarters

**7.** A square sheet is folded along the diagonal from the top-left corner to the bottom-right corner, bringing the bottom-left corner onto the top-right corner. A hole is punched near the top-right corner. Where will the holes be after unfolding?
(a) Near the top-right and bottom-left corners (b) Near the top-right and top-left corners (c) Near the top-right and bottom-right corners (d) Only near the top-right corner

**8.** A long paper strip is folded in half, and one straight cut is made across both layers, away from the fold. How many pieces are there when it is opened?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | 1 × 2¹ = 2 holes. |
| 2 | c | 1 × 2² = 4 holes. |
| 3 | a | 1 × 2³ = 8 holes. |
| 4 | d | 2 × 2² = 8 holes. |
| 5 | c | The hole is reflected across the vertical fold line. |
| 6 | d | All four quarters were stacked, so each gets one hole. |
| 7 | a | The mirror of the TR corner across the TL–BR diagonal is the BL corner. |
