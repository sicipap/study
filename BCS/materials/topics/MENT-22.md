# Mechanical: force, motion, basic physics

> **Why it matters:** Mechanical reasoning and general science papers ask about Newton's laws, units, simple motion calculations, pressure, floating and sinking, and energy. The same facts also appear in the BCS General Science section.

## Force and Newton's laws 🔥
| Law | Statement | Everyday example |
|---|---|---|
| **First law (law of inertia, জড়তার সূত্র)** | A body stays at rest or in uniform straight-line motion unless an outside force acts on it | Passengers fall forward when a bus brakes suddenly |
| **Second law** | Force = mass × acceleration: **F = ma** (rate of change of momentum) | A heavier cart needs more force to speed up equally |
| **Third law** | **Every action has an equal and opposite reaction** | **Rocket** flight, recoil of a gun, swimming, walking |

- **Inertia** depends on **mass**: more mass, more inertia.
- **Momentum = mass × velocity (p = mv)**, unit kg·m/s.

## Units to remember 🔥
| Quantity | SI unit | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Force | **newton (N)** | F = ma |
| Work, energy | **joule (J)** | W = F × distance |
| Power | **watt (W)** | P = work ÷ time |
| Pressure | **pascal (Pa)** = N/m² | P = force ÷ area |
| Acceleration | m/s² | (v − u) ÷ t |
| Frequency | hertz (Hz) | |
| 1 horsepower | about **746 W** | |

## Equations of motion
For constant acceleration (u = starting velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, t = time, s = distance):
- **v = u + at**
- **s = ut + ½at²**
- **v² = u² + 2as**
- Free fall: **g ≈ 9.8 m/s²** near the Earth's surface. Without air resistance, **a heavy and a light object fall at the same rate**.

## Mass, weight and gravity
- **Mass** (kg) is the amount of matter; it is the **same everywhere**.
- **Weight** = mass × g (in newtons); it **changes** with gravity.
- On the **Moon, weight is about one-sixth** of that on Earth.
- Weight is greatest at the **poles** and less at the equator.

## Energy
- **Kinetic energy = ½mv²** (energy of motion). **Potential energy = mgh** (energy of height).
- **Law of conservation of energy:** energy cannot be created or destroyed, only changed from one form to another.

## Pressure, floating and friction
- **Pressure = force ÷ area**: a **sharp knife** cuts well because a small area gives high pressure; **wide straps** on a bag spread the force and hurt less.
- **Liquid pressure increases with depth**, so a dam is **thicker at the bottom**.
- **Archimedes' principle:** a body in a liquid loses weight equal to the weight of the liquid it displaces.
- A body **floats** if its **density is less than** the liquid's. A ship floats because its hollow shape makes its average density less than water. It is easier to float in **sea water** (denser) than in river water.
- **Friction** opposes motion; rolling friction is **less** than sliding friction (that is why wheels and ball bearings are used).
- **Stability:** a body is more stable when its **centre of gravity is low** and its **base is wide**.
- **Circular motion:** a **centripetal force** acts towards the centre; that is why a cyclist leans inward on a curve.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** A car starts from rest and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 s. Final speed?
v = 0 + 2 × 5 = **10 m/s**.

**Example 2.** Force needed to give a 5 kg mass an acceleration of 4 m/s²?
F = 5 × 4 = **20 N**.

**Example 3.** Kinetic energy of a 2 kg ball moving at 3 m/s?
½ × 2 × 3² = **9 J**.

**Example 4.** A 60 kg person weighs about 588 N on Earth (60 × 9.8). On the Moon?
About one-sixth → **about 98 N** (mass stays 60 kg).

## Quick revision
- First law = inertia; second law F = ma; third law = action–reaction (rocket).
- Newton (force), joule (work), watt (power), pascal (pressure).
- v = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as.
- Weight on the Moon ≈ 1/6; mass does not change.
- KE = ½mv²; PE = mgh.
- Things float when less dense than the liquid.
- Low centre of gravity + wide base = stable.
