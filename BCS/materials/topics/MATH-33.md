# Mensuration: cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere

> **Why it matters:** Volume (আয়তন) and surface area (তলের ক্ষেত্রফল) of solids (ঘনবস্তু) appear in BCS and bank papers: cube diagonal, cuboid diagonal, water in a tank, cone slant height, sphere volume and the cone–cylinder 1 : 3 relation.

## Key concepts
- **Volume** is the space inside a solid (cubic units). **Surface area** is the total area of all faces (square units).
- **Curved/lateral surface area (বক্রতল)** excludes the top and bottom; **total surface area (সমগ্রতল)** includes them.
- 🔥 **1 m³ = 1,000 litres**; 1 litre = 1,000 cm³.
- If all lengths are multiplied by k: surface area × k², volume × k³.

## Formula table 🔥
| Solid | Volume | Surface area | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cube (ঘনক)**, edge a | **a³** | total 6a²; four walls 4a² | diagonal **a√3** |
| **Cuboid (আয়তাকার ঘনবস্তু)** l, b, h | **lbh** | 2(lb + bh + hl); four walls 2h(l + b) | diagonal **√(l² + b² + h²)** |
| **Cylinder (বেলন)** r, h | **πr²h** | curved 2πrh; total 2πr(r + h) | |
| **Cone (কোণক)** r, h | **⅓πr²h** | curved πrl; total πr(r + l) | slant **l = √(r² + h²)** |
| **Sphere (গোলক)** r | **(4/3)πr³** | **4πr²** | |
| **Hemisphere (অর্ধগোলক)** r | (2/3)πr³ | curved 2πr²; total 3πr² | |

- 🔥 A cone has **one-third** the volume of a cylinder with the same base and height.
- A cube has 6 faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Find the volume, total surface area and diagonal of a cube of edge 5 cm.
Steps: V = 125; TSA = 6 × 25 = 150; diagonal = 5√3.
**Answer: 125 cm³, 150 cm², 5√3 cm**

**Example 2.** Find the diagonal of a cuboid 12 cm × 4 cm × 3 cm.
Steps: √(144 + 16 + 9) = √169 = 13.
**Answer: 13 cm**

**Example 3.** Find the volume and curved surface area of a cylinder with r = 7 cm and h = 10 cm.
Steps: V = 22/7 × 49 × 10 = 1540. CSA = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 440.
**Answer: 1540 cm³, 440 cm²**

**Example 4.** A cone has radius 3 cm and height 4 cm. Find its slant height, volume and curved surface area.
Steps: l = √(9 + 16) = 5. V = ⅓ × π × 9 × 4 = 12π. CSA = π × 3 × 5 = 15π.
**Answer: 5 cm, 12π cm³, 15π cm²**

**Example 5.** A tank is 2 m long, 1.5 m wide and 1 m deep. How many litres of water does it hold?
Steps: 2 × 1.5 × 1 = 3 m³ = 3,000 litres.
**Answer: 3,000 litres**

## Common traps
- Using the height instead of the **slant height** in the cone's curved surface area.
- Forgetting the ⅓ in the cone's volume.
- Doubling the radius of a sphere makes the volume **8 times** (2³), not 4 times.
- Convert units (cm to m) **before** computing volume; 1 m³ = 10⁶ cm³.

## Quick revision
- Cube: a³, 6a², a√3.
- Cuboid: lbh, √(l² + b² + h²).
- Cylinder: πr²h, 2πrh.
- Cone: ⅓πr²h, πrl, l = √(r² + h²).
- Sphere: (4/3)πr³, 4πr².
- 1 m³ = 1000 L.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The total surface area of a cube is 96 cm². Its volume is:
(a) 16 cm³ (b) 64 cm³ (c) 96 cm³ (d) 216 cm³

**2.** The volume of a cuboid 10 cm × 8 cm × 6 cm is:
(a) 480 cm³ (b) 240 cm³ (c) 376 cm³ (d) 24 cm³

**3.** The diagonal of a cube of edge 4 cm is:
(a) 4√2 cm (b) 12 cm (c) 4√3 cm (d) 8 cm

**4.** The volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 20 cm is (π = 22/7):
(a) 880 cm³ (b) 1540 cm³ (c) 6160 cm³ (d) 3080 cm³

**5.** A cone has radius 6 cm and height 8 cm. Its curved surface area is:
(a) 60π cm² (b) 48π cm² (c) 96π cm² (d) 36π cm²

**6.** The volume of a sphere of radius 3 cm is:
(a) 12π cm³ (b) 27π cm³ (c) 36π cm³ (d) 108π cm³

**7.** The surface area of a sphere of radius 7 cm is (π = 22/7):
(a) 154 cm² (b) 308 cm² (c) 1437 cm² (d) 616 cm²

**8.** A cone and a cylinder have the same base and height. The ratio of their volumes is:
(a) 1 : 3 (b) 1 : 2 (c) 3 : 1 (d) 2 : 3

**9.** A tank 3 m long, 2 m wide and 1.5 m deep holds how much water?
(a) 900 litres (b) 6,500 litres (c) 9,000 litres (d) 90,000 litres

**10.** If the radius of a sphere is doubled, its volume becomes:
(a) 2 times (b) 8 times (c) 4 times (d) 6 times
