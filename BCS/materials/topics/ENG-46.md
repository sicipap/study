# South Asian & postcolonial writers in English

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask "Who wrote *The God of Small Things*?", Booker Prize winners from South Asia, Bangladeshi writers in English (Tahmima Anam, Monica Ali), and postcolonial theorists (Edward Said).

## A. Pioneers of Indian writing in English
| Writer | Work / fact |
|---|---|
| **Henry Louis Vivian Derozio** | Early Indian English poet; "To India – My Native Land" |
| **Michael Madhusudan Dutt** | Began in English: *The Captive Ladie* (1849); later great Bangla poet |
| **Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay** | ***Rajmohan's Wife*** (1864), regarded as the **first Indian novel in English** |
| **Toru Dutt** | "Our Casuarina Tree"; *Ancient Ballads and Legends of Hindustan* |
| **Rabindranath Tagore** 🔥 | ***Gitanjali: Song Offerings*** (English, 1912); Nobel **1913** |
| **Sarojini Naidu** 🔥 | "**Nightingale of India**"; ***The Golden Threshold*** (1905), *The Bird of Time*, *The Broken Wing* |
| **Nirad C. Chaudhuri** | Born in **Kishoreganj** (now Bangladesh); ***The Autobiography of an Unknown Indian*** (1951) |

## B. The "big three" of the Indian English novel 🔥
| Writer | Works / characters |
|---|---|
| **Mulk Raj Anand** | ***Untouchable*** (1935): sweeper boy **Bakha**; *Coolie* (1936) |
| **R. K. Narayan** 🔥 | Created the fictional town **Malgudi**; ***Swami and Friends*** (1935), ***The Guide*** (1958; guide **Raju**; Sahitya Akademi Award), *The English Teacher*, *The Financial Expert* |
| **Raja Rao** | ***Kanthapura*** (1938; Gandhian movement in a village), *The Serpent and the Rope* |

## E. African and Caribbean postcolonial writers
| Writer | Country | Works |
|---|---|---|
| **Chinua Achebe** 🔥 | Nigeria | ***Things Fall Apart*** (1958; **Okonkwo** of **Umuofia**; title from Yeats's "The Second Coming"), *No Longer at Ease*, *Arrow of God*; called the father of modern African literature |
| **Wole Soyinka** | Nigeria | *Death and the King's Horseman*; Nobel **1986** (first African) |
| **Ngũgĩ wa Thiong'o** | Kenya | *Weep Not, Child*, *A Grain of Wheat*, *Decolonising the Mind* |
| **Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie** | Nigeria | *Purple Hibiscus*, ***Half of a Yellow Sun***, *Americanah* |
| **Ben Okri** | Nigeria | *The Famished Road* (Booker 1991) |
| **J. M. Coetzee** | South Africa | ***Disgrace*** (Booker 1999), *Life & Times of Michael K* (Booker 1983), *Foe*; Nobel 2003 |
| **Derek Walcott** | Saint Lucia | ***Omeros***; Nobel 1992 |
| **Jean Rhys** | Dominica | ***Wide Sargasso Sea*** (1966): story of Bertha Mason (Antoinette) from *Jane Eyre* |

**"Writing back" to the empire:** *Wide Sargasso Sea* answers ***Jane Eyre***; Coetzee's *Foe* answers ***Robinson Crusoe***; Achebe attacked Conrad's ***Heart of Darkness***.

## F. Postcolonial theory 🔥
| Theorist | Key work / idea |
|---|---|
| **Edward Said** 🔥 | ***Orientalism*** (**1978**): the West's image of the "Orient"; *Culture and Imperialism* |
| **Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak** | "**Can the Subaltern Speak?**" |
| **Homi K. Bhabha** | ***The Location of Culture***; **hybridity**, **mimicry**, "third space" |
| **Frantz Fanon** | ***The Wretched of the Earth***, *Black Skin, White Masks* |
| **Ashcroft, Griffiths & Tiffin** | ***The Empire Writes Back*** (1989) |

## Quick revision
- ***Midnight's Children*** (Booker 1981) = **Rushdie**; hero **Saleem Sinai**.
- ***The God of Small Things*** (Booker 1997) = **Arundhati Roy**.
- ***The White Tiger*** (2008) = **Adiga**; ***The Inheritance of Loss*** (2006) = **Kiran Desai**.
- **Malgudi** = **R. K. Narayan**; ***Untouchable*** = **Mulk Raj Anand**.
- ***A Golden Age*** (1971 war) = **Tahmima Anam**; ***Brick Lane*** = **Monica Ali**.
- ***Things Fall Apart*** = **Achebe**; ***Orientalism*** = **Edward Said**.
- "Nightingale of India" = **Sarojini Naidu**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** *The God of Small Things* was written by:
(a) Kiran Desai (b) Anita Desai (c) Arundhati Roy (d) Jhumpa Lahiri

**2.** Malgudi is a fictional town created by:
(a) R. K. Narayan (b) Mulk Raj Anand (c) Raja Rao (d) Amitav Ghosh

**3.** *A Golden Age*, a novel set in the 1971 Liberation War, is by:
(a) Monica Ali (b) Tahmima Anam (c) Kamila Shamsie (d) Bapsi Sidhwa

**4.** Saleem Sinai is the narrator of:
(a) Shame (b) The Satanic Verses (c) A Suitable Boy (d) Midnight's Children

**5.** *Orientalism* (1978) was written by:
(a) Homi K. Bhabha (b) Frantz Fanon (c) Edward Said (d) Gayatri Spivak

**6.** Okonkwo is the hero of:
(a) Things Fall Apart (b) Weep Not, Child (c) Disgrace (d) Half of a Yellow Sun

**7.** Who is known as the "Nightingale of India"?
(a) Toru Dutt (b) Sarojini Naidu (c) Kamala Das (d) Anita Desai

**8.** *The White Tiger*, which won the Booker Prize in 2008, was written by:
(a) Vikram Seth (b) Amitav Ghosh (c) Salman Rushdie (d) Aravind Adiga

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | Arundhati Roy, Booker 1997 |
| 2 | a | Narayan's stories are set in Malgudi |
| 3 | b | Tahmima Anam, 2007 |
| 4 | d | Rushdie's *Midnight's Children*, 1981 |
| 5 | c | Edward Said, 1978 |
| 6 | a | Achebe's 1958 novel |
| 7 | b | Sarojini Naidu, *The Golden Threshold* |
| 8 | d | Aravind Adiga, Booker 2008 |
