# Metals, non-metals, alloys, ores

> **Why it matters:** Alloy-composition and ore-name tables are among the most repeated chemistry questions. Exams also ask rust prevention (galvanising), exceptions among metals/non-metals and the reactivity series.

## Metals vs non-metals 🔥
| Metals (ধাতু) | Non-metals (অধাতু) |
|---|---|
| Lustrous, **malleable** (sheets) and **ductile** (wires) | Brittle, dull (mostly) |
| Good conductors of heat and electricity | Poor conductors |
| Lose electrons → positive ions; oxides are basic | Gain electrons; oxides are acidic |

**Exceptions (asked often):**
- **Mercury** is a metal but **liquid** at room temperature.
- **Sodium and potassium** are **soft** (cut with a knife), light, and kept **under kerosene** (react violently with water/air).
- **Graphite** (non-metal, carbon) **conducts electricity**; used in electrodes and pencils.
- **Iodine** (non-metal) is **lustrous**. **Diamond** (non-metal) is the **hardest natural substance**.
- **Gold** is the most **malleable and ductile** metal; **silver** the best conductor; **tungsten** has the highest melting point.
- **Gold and platinum** occur **free (native)** in nature because they are very unreactive (noble metals).
- **Iron** is the **most used** metal; **aluminium** is the most abundant metal in the crust.

## Reactivity series (সক্রিয়তা সিরিজ)
**K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > (H) > Cu > Hg > Ag > Au > Pt**
- Metals above hydrogen displace H₂ from dilute acids. A more reactive metal displaces a less reactive one from its salt (e.g. **iron displaces copper** from CuSO₄ solution).

## Alloys (সংকর ধাতু) 🔥🔥
| Alloy | Composition | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Brass (পিতল)** | **Copper + Zinc** | Utensils, fittings, cartridges |
| **Bronze (কাঁসা/ব্রোঞ্জ)** | **Copper + Tin** | Statues, medals, utensils |
| **Bell metal** | Copper + Tin (more tin, ≈ 78:22) | Bells, plates (a type of bronze) |
| **Gun metal** | Copper + Tin + Zinc | Gun parts, gears |
| **German silver** | **Copper + Zinc + Nickel** (**no silver**) | Utensils, ornaments |
| **Steel (ইস্পাত)** | **Iron + Carbon** (≈ 0.1–1.5% C) | Construction, tools |
| **Stainless steel** | **Iron + Chromium + Nickel** (+ C) | Rust-proof cutlery, utensils, surgical tools |
| **Solder (ঝালাই)** | **Lead + Tin** | Joining electrical wires |
| **Duralumin** | **Aluminium + Copper** + Mg + Mn | **Aircraft bodies** (light, strong) |
| **Magnalium** | Aluminium + Magnesium | Aircraft parts, balances |
| **Nichrome** | Nickel + Chromium | **Heater coils** |
| **Invar** | Iron + Nickel | Clock pendulums, measuring tapes (low expansion) |
| **Alnico** | Aluminium + Nickel + Cobalt (+ Fe) | Permanent magnets |
| **Amalgam** | **Mercury + another metal** | Dental filling (silver-tin amalgam) |
| Type metal | Lead + Antimony + Tin | Printing type |
| **22-carat gold (গিনি সোনা)** | 22 parts gold + 2 parts copper (≈ 91.6% gold) | Jewellery; **24 carat = pure gold** |

## Ores (আকরিক) 🔥🔥
A **mineral** is a naturally occurring compound; an **ore** is a mineral from which a metal can be extracted profitably. All ores are minerals, but not all minerals are ores.

| Metal | Main ore(s) |
|---|---|
| **Aluminium** | **Bauxite (Al₂O₃·2H₂O)**; cryolite used in extraction |
| **Iron** | **Haematite (Fe₂O₃)** (main), **magnetite (Fe₃O₄)**, limonite, siderite (FeCO₃) |
| **Copper** | **Chalcopyrite / copper pyrites (CuFeS₂)**, cuprite, malachite |
| **Zinc** | **Zinc blende (ZnS)**, calamine (ZnCO₃) |
| **Lead** | **Galena (PbS)** |
| **Mercury** | **Cinnabar (HgS)** |
| **Tin** | **Cassiterite / tinstone (SnO₂)** |
| **Manganese** | Pyrolusite (MnO₂) |
| **Uranium** | **Pitchblende** |
| Sodium | Rock salt (NaCl) |
| Calcium | Limestone (CaCO₃), **gypsum** |
| Magnesium | Dolomite, magnesite, carnallite |

- **Aluminium** is extracted by **electrolysis** of alumina dissolved in molten cryolite (Hall–Héroult process).
- **Iron** is extracted in a **blast furnace** using **coke** and **limestone**. **Pig iron** has the most carbon (≈ 4%); **wrought iron** is the purest (least carbon).

## Corrosion 🔥
- **Rusting of iron** needs **both oxygen (air) and water (moisture)**; rust is **hydrated iron(III) oxide, Fe₂O₃·xH₂O**. Salt water speeds it up.
- Prevention: **galvanising** (coating with **zinc**), painting, oiling/greasing, **tin plating** (tin cans), alloying (stainless steel), electroplating (chromium).
- **Copper** gets a green coat (**verdigris**, basic copper carbonate). **Silver** turns black (**silver sulphide, Ag₂S**). Gold does not corrode.

- **Limestone**: Takerghat (Sunamganj), Joypurhat. **White clay**: Bijoypur (Netrokona). **Heavy mineral sand** (zircon, ilmenite, rutile, monazite): Cox's Bazar coast.

## Quick revision
- **Brass = Cu + Zn; bronze = Cu + Sn**; German silver = Cu + Zn + Ni (no silver).
- **Stainless steel = Fe + Cr + Ni**; solder = Pb + Sn; duralumin for aircraft.
- **Amalgam** = mercury alloy; 24-carat gold is pure; 22-carat has copper.
- **Bauxite → Al; haematite → Fe; galena → Pb; cinnabar → Hg; cassiterite → Sn; zinc blende → Zn**.
- Rust = **Fe₂O₃·xH₂O**; needs air + water; **galvanising = zinc coating**.
- Mercury: liquid metal; graphite: conducting non-metal; Na/K kept in kerosene.
- Most abundant metal in crust: **aluminium**; most used: **iron**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Brass is an alloy of —
(a) copper and tin (b) copper and zinc (c) iron and carbon (d) lead and tin

**2.** Bauxite is the main ore of —
(a) aluminium (b) iron (c) copper (d) zinc

**3.** Galvanisation means coating iron with —
(a) tin (b) chromium (c) copper (d) zinc

**4.** Which alloy contains no silver even though its name suggests it?
(a) Sterling silver (b) Amalgam (c) German silver (d) Solder

**5.** Galena is an ore of —
(a) mercury (b) tin (c) copper (d) lead

**6.** Which metal is kept under kerosene?
(a) Sodium (b) Mercury (c) Aluminium (d) Copper

**7.** Stainless steel is an alloy of iron with —
(a) copper and zinc (b) lead and tin (c) chromium and nickel (d) aluminium and magnesium

**8.** Which non-metal is a good conductor of electricity?
(a) Sulphur (b) Iodine (c) Diamond (d) Graphite

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Brass = copper + zinc |
| 2 | a | Bauxite (Al₂O₃·2H₂O) is aluminium's ore |
| 3 | d | Zinc coating protects iron from rust |
| 4 | c | German silver = Cu + Zn + Ni |
| 5 | d | Galena = PbS |
| 6 | a | Sodium reacts violently with air and water |
| 7 | c | Chromium and nickel make steel rust-proof |
| 8 | d | Graphite has free electrons that carry current |
