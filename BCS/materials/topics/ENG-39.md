# Synonyms

> **Why it matters:** 2–4 synonym questions appear in almost every BCS, bank, NTRCA and primary-teacher paper ("The synonym of *candid* is…"). The words below are the ones that repeat most often.

## Tips
- Know the **exact** meaning, not just a vague idea: *eminent* (famous) vs *imminent* (about to happen).
- Eliminate antonyms first; examiners often put the antonym among the options.
- Learn words in **families**: *abundant, copious, ample, plentiful*.

## Confusing look-alikes
| Word | Meaning | vs | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eminent** | famous | **Imminent** | about to happen |
| **Credible** | believable | **Credulous** | easily believing |
| **Industrious** | hard-working | **Industrial** | related to industry |
| **Judicious** | wise, sensible | **Judicial** | related to courts |
| **Continual** | repeated often | **Continuous** | without a break |
| **Economic** | related to economy | **Economical** | thrifty |

## Quick revision
- **Candid** = frank; **diligent** = industrious; **frugal** = thrifty.
- **Ephemeral** = short-lived; **obsolete** = outdated.
- **Dearth** = scarcity; **abundant** = plentiful.
- **Eminent** = famous; **imminent** = about to happen.
- **Obstinate** = stubborn; **prudent** = wise.
- **Futile** = useless; **affluent** = wealthy.

**5.** The synonym of "obsolete" is:
(a) outdated (b) modern (c) necessary (d) obscure

**6.** The synonym of "imminent" is:
(a) famous (b) permanent (c) distant (d) impending

**7.** The synonym of "obstinate" is:

**8.** The synonym of "futile" is:
