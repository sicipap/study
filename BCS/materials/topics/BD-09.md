# 1954 election, 1956 constitution, 1958 martial law, 1962

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask about the Awami Muslim League's founding, the United Front and its 21 points, the "boat" symbol, the 1956 and 1962 constitutions, Ayub Khan's martial law and Basic Democracy, and 17 September (Education Day).

## Awami Muslim League (1949) 🔥
- Founded **23 June 1949** at **রোজ গার্ডেন, Dhaka**.
- President **মওলানা আবদুল হামিদ খান ভাসানী**; General Secretary **শামসুল হক**; one of the Joint Secretaries **শেখ মুজিবুর রহমান** (then in jail).
- The word "Muslim" was dropped in **1955** → **Awami League** (open to all communities).

## 1954 provincial election and the United Front 🔥🔥
- **যুক্তফ্রন্ট (United Front)** was formed on **4 December 1953** by four parties:

| Party | Leader |
|---|---|
| **আওয়ামী মুসলিম লীগ** | মওলানা ভাসানী |
| **কৃষক শ্রমিক পার্টি** | **এ. কে. ফজলুল হক** |
| **নেজামে ইসলাম** | মওলানা আতাহার আলী |
| **গণতন্ত্রী দল** | হাজী মোহাম্মদ দানেশ |

- Election symbol: **নৌকা (boat)**. Manifesto: **২১ দফা (21-point programme)** — the first point was **Bangla as a state language**; others included autonomy for East Bengal, 21 February as a holiday and a Shaheed Minar.
- Election held in **March 1954**. The United Front won a **landslide**: about **223** of the 237 Muslim seats (some books give a slightly different total). The ruling **Muslim League got only 9 seats**.
- **A. K. Fazlul Huq** became **Chief Minister** of East Bengal (April 1954).
- The central government **dismissed** the ministry on **30 May 1954** and imposed **Governor's rule (Section 92A)**; **Iskander Mirza** was made Governor.

## 1956 constitution 🔥
- The **first constitution of Pakistan**; adopted **29 February 1956**, effective **23 March 1956**.
- Pakistan became the **"Islamic Republic of Pakistan"**; **parliamentary** system; **unicameral** legislature with **parity** between the two wings.
- **Bangla and Urdu** were both made state languages (**Article 214**).
- The **One Unit** scheme (October 1955) had merged the western provinces into **West Pakistan**; East Bengal came to be called **East Pakistan (পূর্ব পাকিস্তান)** (the name is dated to 1955 in some books and to the 1956 constitution in others).
- **Iskander Mirza** became the **first President** of Pakistan under it.
- **Kagmari Conference (February 1957):** Bhashani warned West Pakistan with his famous **"আসসালামু আলাইকুম"** (farewell) if East Pakistan's demands were ignored. He later formed the **National Awami Party (NAP)** in **1957**.

## 1958 martial law 🔥
- Violence in the East Pakistan Assembly: **Deputy Speaker শাহেদ আলী** was injured in the House and died (September 1958).
- **7 October 1958:** President **Iskander Mirza** abrogated the constitution and declared **martial law**; **General আইয়ুব খান** became Chief Martial Law Administrator.
- **27 October 1958:** Ayub removed Mirza and became **President** — Pakistan's **first military ruler**.

### Basic Democracy (মৌলিক গণতন্ত্র), 1959
- Ayub's system of **80,000 "Basic Democrats"** (**40,000 in each wing**), elected locally. They formed the **electoral college** for the President and the assemblies.
- Ayub was confirmed by a referendum of Basic Democrats in **1960**.

## 1962 constitution and movement 🔥
- **Second constitution** of Pakistan: promulgated **1 March 1962**, effective **8 June 1962**.
- **Presidential** system; **indirect election** of the President through the Basic Democrats. It concentrated power in the President.
- Protests against Ayub's rule and the constitution were led by students.
- 🔥 **শিক্ষা আন্দোলন (Education Movement) 1962:** against the **Sharif Commission** report on education. Police firing on **17 September 1962** killed students. **17 September is observed as শিক্ষা দিবস (Education Day)**.
- **1965:** Ayub defeated **ফাতেমা জিন্নাহ** in the presidential election (through Basic Democrats). The **17-day Indo-Pak war (1965)** showed that East Pakistan had been left undefended.

## Quick revision
- Awami Muslim League: **23 June 1949**, **Rose Garden**; president **Bhashani**.
- United Front: **4 parties**; symbol **boat**; **21 points**.
- 1954: Muslim League won just **9** seats; **Fazlul Huq** Chief Minister; dismissed under **92A**.
- 1956 constitution: in force **23 March 1956**; Bangla + Urdu state languages (**Art. 214**).
- First President of Pakistan: **Iskander Mirza**.
- Martial law: **7 October 1958**; Ayub President from **27 October 1958**.
- Basic Democrats: **80,000** (40,000 per wing).
- 1962 constitution: **presidential**, in force **8 June 1962**.
- Education Day: **17 September** (1962, Sharif Commission).
