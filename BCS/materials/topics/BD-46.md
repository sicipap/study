# Recent history 2024–26: July uprising, interim govt, reform commissions, 13th election

> **Why it matters:** Questions on the July–August 2024 mass uprising, the interim government, the reform commissions and the road to the 13th parliamentary election are now standard in BCS and all government job exams, including the viva.

## Background: the quota reform movement 🔥
- In **2018** the government abolished quotas in 9th–13th grade government jobs after student protests.
- On **5 June 2024** the **High Court** declared that 2018 circular illegal, bringing back the **30% freedom-fighter quota**.
- Students began protests in **early July 2024** under the banner **"বৈষম্যবিরোধী ছাত্র আন্দোলন" (Students Against Discrimination)**, with programmes such as **"বাংলা ব্লকেড"**.

## July–August 2024 mass uprising (জুলাই গণ-অভ্যুত্থান) 🔥🔥
| Date (2024) | Event |
|---|---|
| **16 July** | **আবু সাঈদ**, a student of **Begum Rokeya University, Rangpur**, was shot dead by police; he became the symbol of the movement |
| **18 July** onward | "Complete shutdown" programme; mobile and broadband **internet shut down** across the country |
| **19–20 July** | **Curfew** imposed; army deployed |
| **21 July** | **Appellate Division** verdict: **93% merit**, **5%** freedom fighters' children/grandchildren, **1%** ethnic minorities, **1%** persons with disabilities and third gender |
| **3 August** | At the **Central Shaheed Minar**, the movement announced a **one-point demand**: the government's resignation |
| **5 August** | "**March to Dhaka**"; Prime Minister **Sheikh Hasina resigned and left for India**. Supporters call the day **"৩৬ জুলাই"** |
| **6 August** | President **Mohammed Shahabuddin** **dissolved the 12th Jatiya Sangsad** |
| **8 August** | **Interim government** sworn in at **Bangabhaban**, with **Dr Muhammad Yunus** as **Chief Adviser (প্রধান উপদেষ্টা)** |

- The **UN Human Rights Office (OHCHR)** fact-finding report (**February 2025**) estimated that **up to about 1,400 people** may have been killed between 15 July and 5 August 2024, many of them young people and children.

## The interim government (অন্তর্বর্তীকালীন সরকার) 🔥🔥
- **Chief Adviser:** **Dr Muhammad Yunus** (Nobel Peace Prize 2006).
- Two student coordinators, **Nahid Islam** and **Asif Mahmud Shojib Bhuyain**, joined as advisers. Nahid Islam later resigned (February 2025) to lead the new **National Citizen Party (NCP)**, launched on **28 February 2025**.
- Legal basis: an advisory opinion of the **Appellate Division** (Article 106 reference) supported the formation.
- New key office-holders in August 2024 included Chief Justice **Syed Refaat Ahmed** and Bangladesh Bank Governor **Ahsan H. Mansur**; the Army Chief was **General Waker-Uz-Zaman**.
- **Chief Election Commissioner:** **A. M. M. Nasir Uddin** (appointed **November 2024**).
- Budget FY2025–26 (Tk 7.90 lakh crore) was presented on television by Finance Adviser **Dr Salehuddin Ahmed** (see BD-29).

## Reform commissions (সংস্কার কমিশন) 🔥🔥
The first **six commissions** were announced on **11 September 2024**:

| Commission | Head |
|---|---|
| **Constitution Reform** | **অধ্যাপক আলী রীয়াজ** |
| **Electoral System Reform** | **ড. বদিউল আলম মজুমদার** |
| **Judiciary Reform** | **Justice Shah Abu Nayeem Mominur Rahman** |
| **Police Reform** | **Safar Raj Hossain** |
| **Anti-Corruption Commission Reform** | **ড. ইফতেখারুজ্জামান** |
| **Public Administration Reform** | **Abdul Muyeed Chowdhury** |

- Later commissions covered **media, health, labour, women's affairs and local government**, making **11** in all.
- The first commissions submitted their reports in **January–February 2025**.
- **National Consensus Commission (জাতীয় ঐকমত্য কমিশন):** formed in **February 2025** to build agreement among parties on the reform proposals. **Chair: Muhammad Yunus**; **Vice-chair: Ali Riaz**.
- The result was the **July National Charter (জুলাই জাতীয় সনদ)**, signed by most political parties in **October 2025**. A **referendum** on it was planned together with the national election.
- In **May 2025** the interim government **banned all activities of the Awami League** under the Anti-Terrorism Act, pending trials, and the Election Commission suspended its registration.

## The 13th parliamentary election (ত্রয়োদশ জাতীয় সংসদ নির্বাচন)
- The Election Commission announced the schedule in **December 2025**, fixing the vote for **12 February 2026**, **on the same day as the referendum** on the July Charter.

## Quick revision
- HC verdict restoring quota: **5 June 2024**; Appellate verdict **21 July 2024 (93% merit)**.
- **Abu Sayed** killed **16 July 2024**, Rangpur.
- One-point demand: **3 August 2024**, Central Shaheed Minar.
- Sheikh Hasina resigned: **5 August 2024**; Parliament dissolved **6 August**.
- Interim government: **8 August 2024**, Chief Adviser **Dr Muhammad Yunus**.
- Constitution Reform Commission: **Ali Riaz**; Electoral: **Badiul Alam Majumdar**.
- Consensus Commission chair **Yunus**, vice-chair **Ali Riaz** → **July National Charter**.
- CEC: **A. M. M. Nasir Uddin**.
