# Solar system & space missions

> **Why it matters:** Planet "superlatives" (largest, hottest, red planet, ringed planet), Moon facts, first-in-space firsts (Sputnik, Gagarin, Apollo 11) and Bangladesh's satellite are asked in almost every exam; recent Moon/Mars missions come in current-affairs sections.

## The Sun and the solar system 🔥
- The **Sun** is a medium-sized **star**, about **4.6 billion years** old, made mostly of **hydrogen (≈ 73%) and helium**. Its energy comes from **nuclear fusion**. Surface ≈ **5,500–6,000 °C**; core ≈ **15 million °C**. It holds ≈ **99.8%** of the solar system's mass.
- Sunlight reaches Earth in **≈ 8 min 20 s**; mean Earth–Sun distance = **1 AU ≈ 15 crore km (149.6 million km)**.
- **Heliocentric theory** (Sun at the centre): **Nicolaus Copernicus (1543)**. Ptolemy had given the geocentric model. **Galileo** (1609–10) used a telescope and found **4 large moons of Jupiter**. **Kepler** gave the laws of planetary motion.
- **8 planets**. **Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet on 24 August 2006** by the IAU (Prague). Other dwarf planets: **Ceres, Eris, Haumea, Makemake**.
- Order from the Sun: **Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune**. (Hook: *My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Nuts*.) Inner four are rocky; outer four are gas/ice giants.

## Planet facts 🔥🔥
| Planet | Key exam facts |
|---|---|
| **Mercury (বুধ)** | **Smallest** planet, **closest to the Sun**; **shortest year (88 days)**; no moon |
| **Venus (শুক্র)** | **Hottest planet (≈ 465 °C)** due to thick CO₂ atmosphere; **brightest planet**; **"morning star / evening star" (শুকতারা/সন্ধ্যাতারা)**; rotates **east to west (retrograde)**; its **day (243 Earth days) is longer than its year (225 days)**; no moon; "Earth's twin" in size |
| **Earth (পৃথিবী)** | **Densest planet**; only known life; "blue planet"; 1 moon; rotation ≈ 23 h 56 min; year 365¼ days; axis tilted **23.5°** |
| **Mars (মঙ্গল)** | **"Red planet"** (iron oxide dust); moons **Phobos and Deimos**; **Olympus Mons** — highest volcano in the solar system |
| **Jupiter (বৃহস্পতি)** | **Largest and most massive**; **fastest rotation (≈ 10 hours)**; **Great Red Spot** (giant storm); moon **Ganymede = largest moon** of the solar system |
| **Saturn (শনি)** | **Most prominent rings**; **least dense** (would float on water); largest moon **Titan** (thick atmosphere); **most known moons** (274 recognised as of 2025) |
| **Uranus (ইউরেনাস)** | Discovered by **William Herschel (1781)** — first planet found with a telescope; **rotates on its side** (≈ 98° tilt); greenish-blue (methane) |
| **Neptune (নেপচুন)** | **Farthest planet**; found in **1846** (predicted mathematically by Le Verrier and Adams, seen by Galle); **strongest winds**; year ≈ 165 Earth years. Many guides call it the coldest planet (Uranus actually has the lowest recorded temperature) |

- **Asteroid belt**: between **Mars and Jupiter**; **Ceres** is the largest object there.
- **Halley's Comet**: returns about every **76 years**; last seen **1986**, next **2061**.

## The Moon (চাঁদ) 🔥
- Average distance **≈ 3,84,400 km**; diameter ≈ **3,474 km** (≈ ¼ of Earth's).
- Orbits Earth in **≈ 27.3 days** (sidereal); new moon to new moon **≈ 29.5 days**. We always see the **same face** (rotation = revolution period).
- **No atmosphere**, so no sound, sky looks black; **gravity ≈ 1/6** of Earth's. Moonlight takes **≈ 1.3 s** to reach Earth.
- **Tides** are caused mainly by the Moon's gravity (the Sun also helps; spring tides at new/full moon).
- **Solar eclipse** occurs on a **new moon (অমাবস্যা)** day; **lunar eclipse** on a **full moon (পূর্ণিমা)** night.

## Stars and galaxies
- We live in the **Milky Way (আকাশগঙ্গা / ছায়াপথ)**, a **spiral galaxy**. The nearest large galaxy is **Andromeda**.
- **Nearest star after the Sun: Proxima Centauri (≈ 4.2 light-years)**. **Brightest star in the night sky: Sirius**. **Pole star: Polaris (ধ্রুবতারা)**.
- **Big Bang theory**: Georges Lemaître; **Edwin Hubble (1929)** showed the universe is expanding. The **first image of a black hole** (M87*) was released in **2019** by the Event Horizon Telescope.

## Bangladesh in space 🔥
- **Bangabandhu Satellite-1** — Bangladesh's **first communication (geostationary) satellite**; launched **12 May 2018** (Bangladesh time) by a **SpaceX Falcon 9** from **Kennedy Space Center, Florida**; built by **Thales Alenia Space (France)**; orbital slot **119.1° East**. Bangladesh became the **57th country** to own a satellite. Operated by **Bangladesh Satellite Company Ltd (BSCL)**.
- **BRAC Onnesha** — **first Bangladeshi nanosatellite** (built by BRAC University students), launched in **June 2017** and released from the ISS in July 2017.

## Quick revision
- **8 planets**; Pluto a dwarf planet since **2006**.
- **Largest: Jupiter; smallest & closest: Mercury; hottest & brightest: Venus; red planet: Mars; rings & least dense: Saturn.**
- Venus rotates east to west; **Ganymede** is the largest moon; Saturn has the most moons.
- Moon: **3,84,400 km**, 27.3 days, gravity 1/6; solar eclipse on new moon.
- **Sputnik 1 (1957); Gagarin (12 April 1961); Tereshkova (1963); Apollo 11 (20 July 1969).**
- **Chandrayaan-3** near the lunar south pole (2023); **Chang'e 4** first far-side landing (2019).
- **Bangabandhu Satellite-1**: 12 May 2018, Falcon 9, **119.1° E**, 57th country.
