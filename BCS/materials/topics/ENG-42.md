# Nobel laureates in literature

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask "firsts" (first Asian, first woman, first African), laureates who declined the prize, famous works of laureates, and the latest winner. The latest-winner question changes every year, so keep it updated.

## Basic facts 🔥
- Founded by the will of **Alfred Nobel** (Swedish chemist, inventor of **dynamite**; died **1896**).
- First awarded in **1901**. Prize ceremony on **10 December** (Nobel's death anniversary).
- The literature prize is awarded by the **Swedish Academy** (Stockholm). The laureate is usually announced in **early October**.
- Not awarded in some years, e.g. **1914, 1918, 1935, 1940–1943**. The **2018** prize was announced in **2019** (together with the 2019 prize) after a scandal at the Academy.

## Tagore and the Nobel (for Bangladesh) 🔥
- **Rabindranath Tagore** won in **1913** for ***Gitanjali: Song Offerings***, his own English prose translations, published in London in **1912** with an introduction by **W. B. Yeats**.
- The Nobel medal was **stolen** from Visva-Bharati (Santiniketan) in **2004**.
- No Bangladeshi has won the literature prize. (Dr **Muhammad Yunus** won the **Peace** prize in **2006**.)

## Quick revision
- First laureate: **Sully Prudhomme (1901)**.
- First Asian: **Tagore (1913)**, for ***Gitanjali***.
- First woman: **Selma Lagerlöf (1909)**; youngest: **Kipling (1907)**.
- Declined: **Sartre (1964)**; forced to decline: **Pasternak (1958)**.
- **Churchill (1953)** won the literature prize, not the peace prize.
- First African: **Wole Soyinka (1986)**; first Arabic writer: **Naguib Mahfouz (1988)**.
- **Bob Dylan (2016)**; **Han Kang (2024)**; **Krasznahorkai (2025)**.

(a) 1901 (b) 1913 (c) 1921 (d) 1941

**7.** Which singer-songwriter won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 2016?

**8.** Wole Soyinka, the first African Nobel laureate in literature, is from:
(a) Kenya (b) South Africa (c) Nigeria (d) Egypt
