# World leaders (current heads of state/govt)

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask "Who is the current President/Prime Minister of …?", especially for neighbours, big powers and Muslim countries, plus "firsts" (first woman PM of Japan, first American Pope). These names change, so learn the list below and then **verify every name** in a newspaper just before your exam.

## How to read this note
- Everything below is **as of early 2026** (the latest information this note can confirm).
- 🔥 Before the exam, **re-check every row** — elections and resignations happen all the time.

> 🔄 Update: check the latest figure in a newspaper or the Bangladesh Economic Review. Leaders change often; confirm each name before the exam.

## Head of state vs head of government (রাষ্ট্রপ্রধান বনাম সরকারপ্রধান)
- **Presidential system** (e.g. USA): the **President** is both head of state and head of government.
- **Parliamentary system** (e.g. UK, India, Bangladesh): a **monarch or president** is head of state; the **Prime Minister** heads the government.
- Special titles: Japan — **Emperor**; Bhutan — **Druk Gyalpo** ("Dragon King"); Iran — **Supreme Leader** above the President; Vatican — **Pope**; Gulf states — **King / Emir / Sultan**.
