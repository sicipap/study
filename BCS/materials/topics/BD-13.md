# 1971: sectors, commanders, forces, Bir Sreshtho

> **Why it matters:** Exams love the sector table (which sector had no commander, which covered Dhaka, Z/K/S forces), the Bir Sreshtho list (place of death, youngest, only Air Force/Navy member) and the number of gallantry awards.

## Sectors (সেক্টর) 🔥
- For the war, Bangladesh was divided into **11 sectors**, decided at the **sector commanders' conference in Kolkata, July 1971**.
- 🔥 **Sector 10** had **no fixed area and no regular commander**: it was the **naval commando** sector (rivers and sea ports), under the **Commander-in-Chief** directly.
- Each sector had sub-sectors; the fighters were regular soldiers (**নিয়মিত বাহিনী**) and guerrillas (**গণবাহিনী / Freedom Fighters**).

| Sector | Main area | Commander(s) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chattogram, Chattogram Hill Tracts, east Noakhali | **Major জিয়াউর রহমান**, then **Major রফিকুল ইসলাম** |
| 2 | Dhaka, Cumilla, Faridpur, part of Noakhali | 🔥 **Major খালেদ মোশাররফ**, then **Major এ. টি. এম. হায়দার** |
| 3 | Brahmanbaria, Habiganj, Kishoreganj, part of Dhaka | **Major কে. এম. সফিউল্লাহ**, then **Major এ. এন. এম. নূরুজ্জামান** |
| 4 | North-east Sylhet (Moulvibazar side) | **Major সি. আর. দত্ত** |
| 5 | North-west Sylhet (Sunamganj side) | **Major মীর শওকত আলী** |
| 6 | Rangpur, part of Dinajpur | **Wing Commander এম. খাদেমুল বাশার** |
| 7 | Rajshahi, Pabna, Bogura, part of Dinajpur | **Major নাজমুল হক**, then **Major কাজী নূরুজ্জামান** |
| 8 | Kushtia, Jashore, part of Khulna and Faridpur | **Major আবু ওসমান চৌধুরী**, then **Major এম. এ. মঞ্জুর** |
| 9 | Barishal, Patuakhali, part of Khulna | **Major এম. এ. জলিল**, then others |
| 10 | Naval commandos (ports and rivers) | No regular commander |
| 11 | Mymensingh, Tangail | **Major আবু তাহের**, then **Squadron Leader এম. হামিদুল্লাহ খান** |

- Sector 6 is often noted as the sector whose headquarters stayed **inside Bangladesh** (Patgram area).

## Forces (বাহিনী) 🔥
### Brigade forces (named after commanders' initials)
| Force | Commander |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **জেড ফোর্স (Z Force)** — first brigade | **Major জিয়াউর রহমান** |
| **কে ফোর্স (K Force)** | **Major খালেদ মোশাররফ** |
| **এস ফোর্স (S Force)** | **Major কে. এম. সফিউল্লাহ** |

### Other forces
- **মুজিব বাহিনী (BLF)** — led by student and youth leaders (Sheikh Fazlul Haque Moni, Serajul Alam Khan, Abdur Razzak, Tofail Ahmed).
- **কাদেরিয়া বাহিনী** — **আবদুল কাদের সিদ্দিকী** (Tangail), "বঙ্গবীর".
- **হেমায়েত বাহিনী** (Gopalganj–Faridpur area) and **আফসার বাহিনী** (Mymensingh area).
- 🔥 **Naval commandos — "অপারেশন জ্যাকপট" (15–16 August 1971):** simultaneous attacks on ships at **Chattogram, Mongla, Chandpur and Narayanganj** ports.
- 🔥 **Bangladesh Air Force — "কিলো ফ্লাইট"**: formed on **28 September 1971** at **Dimapur (Nagaland, India)**; first attack on **3 December 1971** (fuel depots at Narayanganj and Chattogram). Led by Group Captain **A. K. Khandker** (with Squadron Leader Sultan Mahmud).
- 🔥 **Allied / Joint Command (যৌথ বাহিনী)** of Bangladesh and Indian forces formed on **21 November 1971** → **21 November = সশস্ত্র বাহিনী দিবস (Armed Forces Day)**.

## Bir Sreshtho (বীরশ্রেষ্ঠ) — 7 🔥🔥
| Name | Force | Died | Place |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ক্যাপ্টেন মহিউদ্দিন জাহাঙ্গীর** | Army | **14 Dec 1971** | Chapainawabganj (buried at Chhoto Sona Mosque premises) |
| **সিপাহী হামিদুর রহমান** | Army | 28 Oct 1971 | Dhalai, Srimangal (Moulvibazar) |
| **সিপাহী মোস্তফা কামাল** | Army | **18 Apr 1971** | Daruin, **Akhaura** (Brahmanbaria) |
| **ইঞ্জিনরুম আর্টিফিশার মোহাম্মদ রুহুল আমিন** | **Navy** | 10 Dec 1971 | Rupsha river, Khulna (ship **"পলাশ"**) |
| **ফ্লাইট লেফটেন্যান্ট মতিউর রহমান** | **Air Force** | **20 Aug 1971** | Thatta, Sindh (Pakistan) — tried to fly a T-33 trainer ("Bluebird") from Karachi to Bangladesh |
| **ল্যান্স নায়েক মুন্সী আবদুর রউফ** | EPR | 20 Apr 1971 | Burighat, Naniarchar (Rangamati) |
| **ল্যান্স নায়েক নূর মোহাম্মদ শেখ** | EPR | 5 Sep 1971 | Goalhati, Jashore |

- 🔥 **Youngest:** **Hamidur Rahman**. **First to die (earliest):** **Mostafa Kamal**. **Last to die:** **Mohiuddin Jahangir**.
- Only **officer** from the Army: **Mohiuddin Jahangir**. Only from the **Air Force**: **Matiur Rahman**; only from the **Navy**: **Ruhul Amin**. From the **EPR** (now BGB): **Munshi Abdur Rouf, Nur Mohammad Sheikh**.
- The remains of **Matiur Rahman** (from Karachi, 2006) and **Hamidur Rahman** (from Tripura, 2007) were brought home and reburied at **Mirpur Martyred Intellectuals' Graveyard**.

## Gallantry awards (খেতাব) 🔥
| Award | Number |
|---|---|
| **বীরশ্রেষ্ঠ** | **7** |
| **বীর উত্তম** | **68** |
| **বীর বিক্রম** | **175** |
| **বীর প্রতীক** | **426** |
| **Total** | **676** |

- Awards were announced in **December 1973**. (Some books give slightly different totals; 7 / 68 / 175 / 426 is the standard exam answer.)
- 🔥 The only two **women** with gallantry awards: **ক্যাপ্টেন ডা. সেতারা বেগম** and **তারামন বিবি** (both **Bir Protik**).
- The only **foreigner**: **W. A. S. Ouderland** (Dutch-born Australian), **Bir Protik**.

## Quick revision
- **11 sectors**; **Sector 10** = naval commandos, no regular commander.
- Sector 2 (Dhaka–Cumilla): **Khaled Mosharraf**; Sector 11: **Abu Taher**.
- Z Force **Ziaur Rahman**, K Force **Khaled Mosharraf**, S Force **K. M. Shafiullah**.
- Operation Jackpot: **15–16 August 1971**.
- Kilo Flight formed **28 September 1971**, Dimapur.
- Armed Forces Day: **21 November** (joint command 1971).
- Bir Sreshtho: **7**; youngest **Hamidur Rahman**; Air Force **Matiur Rahman**; Navy **Ruhul Amin**.
- Awards total **676** (7 + 68 + 175 + 426).
- Women Bir Protik: **Sitara Begum, Taramon Bibi**.
