# 1971: victory, surrender, martyred intellectuals, foreign role

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask about 16 December (who signed the surrender, where, who represented Bangladesh), 14 December and the martyred intellectuals, the first countries to recognise Bangladesh, and the roles of India, the USSR, the USA and China.

## Final phase of the war
- **3 December 1971:** Pakistan launched air attacks on Indian air bases → India joined the war fully. Bangladesh–India **joint forces (যৌথ বাহিনী / মিত্রবাহিনী)** advanced quickly.
- 🔥 **6 December 1971:** **Bhutan** and **India** recognised Bangladesh. (Bhutan's recognition is commonly counted **first**, a few hours before India's — the usual exam answer.)
- **USA** sent the **Seventh Fleet** (led by the aircraft carrier **USS Enterprise**) towards the Bay of Bengal in support of Pakistan.
- The **USSR vetoed** ceasefire resolutions in the **UN Security Council** that would have helped Pakistan.

## Victory and surrender (বিজয় ও আত্মসমর্পণ) 🔥🔥
- **Date: 16 December 1971** — **বিজয় দিবস (Victory Day)**.
- Place: **রেসকোর্স ময়দান** (now **সোহরাওয়ার্দী উদ্যান**), Dhaka; in the afternoon (commonly **4:31 pm**).
- 🔥 **Instrument of Surrender** signed by:
  - **Lt. Gen. এ. এ. কে. নিয়াজী** — Commander of Pakistan's Eastern Command (surrendered)
  - **Lt. Gen. জগজিৎ সিং অরোরা** — GOC-in-C of India's Eastern Command, commander of the **joint command** (accepted)
- 🔥 **Bangladesh was represented by Group Captain এ. কে. খন্দকার** (Deputy Chief of Staff). **General Osmani was not present.**
- About **93,000** Pakistani soldiers and personnel became prisoners of war.
- 🔥 **Bangabandhu** was released from Pakistani jail and returned to Dhaka on **10 January 1972** (via London and Delhi) — **স্বদেশ প্রত্যাবর্তন দিবস**.
- The Indian army left Bangladesh by **12 March 1972**.

## Martyred intellectuals (শহীদ বুদ্ধিজীবী) 🔥🔥
- Just before defeat, the Pakistan army and its local collaborators — especially the **আল-বদর** force — abducted and killed teachers, doctors, writers and journalists, mainly on **10–14 December 1971**, to cripple the new nation.
- 🔥 **14 December = শহীদ বুদ্ধিজীবী দিবস (Martyred Intellectuals Day).**
- Bodies were found at **রায়েরবাজার বধ্যভূমি** and **মিরপুর**.

| Martyred intellectual | Profession |
|---|---|
| **মুনীর চৌধুরী** | Teacher, playwright ("কবর") |
| **মোফাজ্জল হায়দার চৌধুরী** | Teacher, linguist |
| **শহীদুল্লাহ কায়সার** | Journalist, novelist ("সংশপ্তক") |
| **সিরাজুদ্দীন হোসেন** | Journalist (Ittefaq) |
| **সেলিনা পারভীন** | Journalist (the "শিলালিপি" magazine) |
| **ডা. ফজলে রাব্বী** | Cardiologist |
| **ডা. আলীম চৌধুরী** | Eye specialist |
| **আনোয়ার পাশা** | Teacher, novelist ("রাইফেল রোটি আওরাত") |
| **গিয়াসউদ্দিন আহমদ**, **রাশীদুল হাসান**, **সন্তোষচন্দ্র ভট্টাচার্য** | Dhaka University teachers |

- Killed on **25 March 1971**: **ড. জি. সি. দেব**, **জ্যোতির্ময় গুহঠাকুরতা**. **ধীরেন্দ্রনাথ দত্ত** was killed in Cumilla in March–April 1971.
- Filmmaker **জহির রায়হান** disappeared on **30 January 1972** at Mirpur while searching for his brother **Shahidullah Kaiser**.

## Foreign role 🔥
| Country / person | Role |
|---|---|
| **India** (PM **ইন্দিরা গান্ধী**) | Sheltered about **1 crore (10 million) refugees**; trained freedom fighters; joined the war on 3 Dec; recognised Bangladesh on 6 Dec |
| **USSR** | Supported Bangladesh; **Indo-Soviet Treaty of Peace, Friendship and Cooperation (9 August 1971)**; vetoes in the UN Security Council |
| **USA** (President **Nixon**, adviser **Kissinger**) | Government supported Pakistan; sent the **Seventh Fleet**. But US Senator **Edward Kennedy** and many Americans supported Bangladesh; Consul-General **Archer Blood** sent the "Blood Telegram" |
| **China** | Supported Pakistan |
| **Bhutan** | First to recognise Bangladesh (6 Dec 1971) |

### Supporters abroad
- 🔥 **Concert for Bangladesh:** **1 August 1971**, **Madison Square Garden, New York**, organised by **জর্জ হ্যারিসন** and **পণ্ডিত রবিশঙ্কর**.
- **Allen Ginsberg**'s poem **"September on Jessore Road"**.
- French writer **André Malraux** offered to fight for Bangladesh.

## Quick revision
- Victory Day: **16 December 1971**, Race Course (Suhrawardy Udyan).
- Surrender signed by **Niazi** (Pakistan) and **Jagjit Singh Aurora** (joint command).
- Bangladesh representative: **Group Captain A. K. Khandker**.
- About **93,000** POWs.
- Martyred Intellectuals Day: **14 December**; killers mainly **Al-Badr**.
- National Memorial, Savar: **Syed Mainul Hossain**.
- First recognition: **Bhutan** (6 Dec 1971), then India the same day.
- USA sent the **Seventh Fleet**; USSR used its **veto** for Bangladesh.
- Concert for Bangladesh: **1 August 1971**, **George Harrison & Ravi Shankar**.
- UN membership: **17 September 1974**, **136th** member.
