# Corruption & ACC, e-governance

> **Why it matters:** Questions cover the definition of corruption (TI and World Bank), Transparency International and its index, the UN Convention against Corruption, Bangladesh's Anti-Corruption Commission (ACC) Act 2004, and e-governance models and projects such as Union Digital Centres and e-GP.

## Corruption (দুর্নীতি) 🔥
| Definition | Source |
|---|---|
| "The **abuse of entrusted power for private gain**" | **Transparency International (TI)** |
| "The **abuse of public office for private gain**" | **World Bank** |

**Forms:** bribery (ঘুষ), embezzlement (আত্মসাৎ), fraud, extortion, **nepotism** (স্বজনপ্রীতি: favouring relatives), **cronyism** (favouring friends), favouritism, money laundering.
**Types:** **petty** (everyday, small officials) vs **grand** (high level, big projects); **political** corruption (by decision-makers).
**Causes:** weak accountability, low pay, complex procedures, discretionary power, political patronage, weak rule of law. **Effects:** less investment, poor services, inequality, loss of public trust.

## Transparency International and the CPI 🔥
- **TI** was founded in **1993** in **Berlin**, Germany, by **Peter Eigen**.
- It publishes the **Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI)**, first in **1995**. Scores run from **0 (highly corrupt) to 100 (very clean)**.
- **TIB (Transparency International Bangladesh)** is its national chapter.
- 🔥 Bangladesh was ranked **the most corrupt country in the CPI for five consecutive years, 2001–2005**.

> 🔄 Update: check the latest CPI score and rank of Bangladesh (published every January/February) in a newspaper.

## UN Convention against Corruption (UNCAC) 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Adopted by the UN General Assembly | **31 October 2003** |
| Signing conference | **Mérida, Mexico** (December 2003) |
| Entered into force | **14 December 2005** |
| Bangladesh acceded | **27 February 2007** |
| **International Anti-Corruption Day** | **9 December** |

## Anti-Corruption Commission (দুর্নীতি দমন কমিশন) 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Law | **Anti-Corruption Commission Act 2004** (দুর্নীতি দমন কমিশন আইন, ২০০৪) |
| Established | **21 November 2004** |
| Replaced | The **Bureau of Anti-Corruption** (দুর্নীতি দমন ব্যুরো), founded in **1957** |
| Nature | **Independent, impartial and self-governing** (স্বাধীন, নিরপেক্ষ ও স্বশাসিত) statutory body |
| Members | **3 Commissioners**, one of whom is **Chairman** |
| Term | **5 years**; not eligible for reappointment |
| Appointed by | The **President**, on the recommendation of a **selection committee** headed by a judge of the Appellate Division |
| First Chairman | **Justice Sultan Hossain Khan** |
| Hotline | **106** |
| Main offences it deals with | Offences in its schedule, e.g. bribery and criminal breach of trust under the **Penal Code**, the **Prevention of Corruption Act 1947**, and money laundering linked to corruption (**Money Laundering Prevention Act 2012**) |

The ACC also works on **prevention**: public hearings, integrity units, and corruption-prevention committees (দুর্নীতি প্রতিরোধ কমিটি).

> 🔄 Update: the ACC's current chairman and any reforms after the Anti-Corruption Reform Commission (2024–25) should be checked in a newspaper.

## E-governance (ই-গভর্ন্যান্স) 🔥
- **Meaning:** using **information and communication technology (ICT)** to deliver government services, share information and make administration **faster, cheaper, more transparent and accountable**.
- **E-government** is the government's use of ICT in its own work; **e-governance** is wider and includes citizens and businesses.

| Model | Between | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **G2C** | Government to Citizen | Online birth registration, land records, passports |
| **G2B** | Government to Business | e-tendering, trade licences, online tax |
| **G2G** | Government to Government | e-Nothi (electronic file system) between offices |
| **G2E** | Government to Employee | Online payroll, training |

### E-governance in Bangladesh
| Initiative | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Digital Bangladesh** | Vision announced in **2008** (Vision 2021) |
| **Union Digital Centres** (first called Union Information and Service Centres) | Launched **11 November 2010** |
| **e-GP** (electronic government procurement) | Launched **2011** by CPTU |
| **a2i** (Aspire to Innovate) | Government programme driving e-services |
| **National emergency service 999** | Launched **12 December 2017** |
| **National helpline 333** | Government information and service helpline |
| **Smart Bangladesh 2041** | Four pillars: **Smart Citizen, Smart Government, Smart Economy, Smart Society** |

**Benefits:** less face-to-face contact (so fewer bribes), speed, transparency, lower cost. **Challenges:** digital divide, cyber security, poor connectivity, low digital literacy.

## Quick revision
- TI: corruption = abuse of entrusted power for private gain; World Bank: abuse of public office.
- TI founded 1993, Berlin; CPI since 1995 (0 = most corrupt).
- Bangladesh bottom of the CPI 2001–2005.
- UNCAC adopted 31 October 2003; in force 14 December 2005; Bangladesh joined 27 February 2007.
- Anti-Corruption Day: 9 December.
- ACC Act 2004; ACC formed 21 November 2004; 3 commissioners; 5-year term; hotline 106.
- e-governance models: G2C, G2B, G2G, G2E.
- UDC 11 November 2010; e-GP 2011; 999 from 12 December 2017.
