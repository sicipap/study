# WTO, IMF, World Bank, ADB, AIIB, NDB

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for HQs (Washington, Geneva, Manila, Beijing, Shanghai), founding years, the Bretton Woods Conference, the five World Bank Group institutions, SDR, GATT → WTO, and when Bangladesh joined or received loans.

## Bretton Woods institutions 🔥
- 🔥 **Bretton Woods Conference:** **July 1944**, **New Hampshire, USA**; 44 nations. Key figures: **John Maynard Keynes** (UK) and **Harry Dexter White** (USA).
- It created the **IMF** and the **IBRD (World Bank)**, the "**Bretton Woods twins**".
- Tradition: the **IMF chief is a European**, the **World Bank president an American**.

### IMF (আন্তর্জাতিক মুদ্রা তহবিল)
- Aim: **international monetary stability**, balance-of-payments support, surveillance; "**lender of last resort**" for countries.
- Voting depends on **quota** (money contributed); the **USA** has the largest share and an effective veto on major decisions (85% majority needed).
- 🔥 **SDR (Special Drawing Rights)**, created **1969**: the IMF's reserve asset, also called "**paper gold**". Basket: **US dollar, euro, Chinese yuan (added 2016), Japanese yen, British pound**.
- Publishes the **World Economic Outlook**.

### World Bank Group (বিশ্বব্যাংক গ্রুপ) 🔥
| Institution | Year | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **IBRD** | 1944 | Loans to middle-income countries |
| 🔥 **IDA** (International Development Association) | **1960** | **Soft (concessional) loans** and grants to the poorest countries |
| **IFC** (International Finance Corporation) | 1956 | Lends to the **private sector** |
| **MIGA** | 1988 | Investment guarantees (political risk insurance) |
| **ICSID** | 1966 | Settles **investment disputes** |

- "The World Bank" usually means **IBRD + IDA**.
- 🔥 **Padma Bridge:** the World Bank **cancelled its $1.2 billion loan in June 2012**, alleging a corruption conspiracy; a **Canadian court** later dismissed the case (2017). Bangladesh built the bridge with **its own funds**; it opened on **25 June 2022**.
- Publishes the **World Development Report**.

## WTO (বিশ্ব বাণিজ্য সংস্থা) 🔥
- 🔥 **GATT** (General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade) signed **1947** (in force 1948). The **Uruguay Round (1986–94)** created the WTO through the **Marrakesh Agreement (April 1994)**.
- 🔥 **WTO began on 1 January 1995**, HQ **Geneva** (Centre William Rappard).
- **Members: 166** (as of 2024–25).
- **DG Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala** (Nigeria): **first woman and first African** DG (since March 2021; second term from September 2025).
- **Highest body: the Ministerial Conference**, meets about every two years: **MC12 Geneva (2022)**, **MC13 Abu Dhabi (2024)**, **MC14 Yaoundé, Cameroon (March 2026)**.
- **Doha Development Round** launched **2001** (never completed).
- Key agreements: **TRIPS** (intellectual property), **GATS** (services), **TRIMs**, **Agreement on Agriculture**.
- **Dispute settlement:** the **Appellate Body** has been unable to work since **December 2019** because the USA blocked new appointments.
- **Bangladesh:** in GATT since **1972**; WTO founding member; LDC benefits (e.g. TRIPS pharmaceutical waiver) will be affected after **LDC graduation (scheduled 24 November 2026)**.

## ADB (এশীয় উন্নয়ন ব্যাংক)
- Founded **19 December 1966**; HQ **Manila (Mandaluyong), Philippines**.
- **69 members** (49 from Asia-Pacific). **Japan and the USA** are the largest shareholders; the **president has always been Japanese**.
- Publishes the **Asian Development Outlook**. Soft-loan window: **Asian Development Fund**.

## AIIB (Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank) 🔥
- Proposed by **China (Xi Jinping, 2013)**; Articles of Agreement signed **June 2015**; began operations **16 January 2016**; HQ **Beijing**.
- **China** is the largest shareholder, **India** the second largest.
- First President **Jin Liqun** (2016 – January 2026); succeeded by **Zou Jiayi**.
- 🔥 **Bangladesh** is a **founding member**; a **Bangladesh power-distribution project** was among AIIB's **first four loans (June 2016)**.

## NDB (New Development Bank / BRICS Bank) 🔥
- Agreed at the **6th BRICS summit, Fortaleza (Brazil), July 2014**; began work in **2015**; HQ 🔥 **Shanghai**.
- Founders: **Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa** (equal shares). First President **K. V. Kamath** (India).
- 🔥 **Bangladesh joined in 2021** (with the UAE; Egypt joined later), the first new members beyond BRICS.
- Also created in 2014: the **Contingent Reserve Arrangement (CRA)**, $100 billion.

## Other banks worth knowing
| Bank | HQ | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Islamic Development Bank (IsDB)** | **Jeddah** | 1973/75; OIC-linked; Bangladesh is a member |
| **Bank for International Settlements (BIS)** | **Basel** | 1930; "central bank of central banks"; oldest international financial institution |
| **Asian Clearing Union (ACU)** | **Tehran** | 1974; Bangladesh is a member |

## Quick revision
- Bretton Woods: **July 1944** → **IMF + IBRD**; both HQ **Washington, D.C.**
- **SDR** 1969; basket of 5 currencies incl. **yuan (2016)**.
- World Bank Group = **IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID**; IDA gives **soft loans**.
- **WTO** 1 Jan 1995, **Geneva**, replaced **GATT (1947)**; DG **Okonjo-Iweala**.
- **ADB** 1966, **Manila**; **AIIB** 2016, **Beijing**; **NDB** 2015, **Shanghai**.
- Bangladesh: AIIB **founding member**; joined **NDB in 2021**.
- Padma Bridge loan cancelled by the World Bank in **2012**; bridge opened **25 June 2022**.
