# Major treaties: Westphalia, Camp David, Oslo, Simla

> **Why it matters:** Treaty questions ask "which treaty ended …", "who signed …", and the year. Westphalia, Versailles, Camp David, Oslo, Simla, Tashkent and the Indus Waters Treaty are the most frequent; Bangladesh's own treaties (Ganges Water Treaty, CHT Peace Accord, Land Boundary Agreement) are also key.

## Four headline treaties 🔥
### Peace of Westphalia (1648)
- 🔥 Two treaties signed at **Osnabrück and Münster** (Westphalia, Germany) in **1648**.
- Ended the 🔥 **Thirty Years' War (1618–1648)** in the Holy Roman Empire and the **Eighty Years' War** between Spain and the Dutch Republic.
- Recognised the **independence of the Netherlands and Switzerland**.
- 🔥 Laid the foundation of the **modern state system**: **state sovereignty**, **non-interference**, and legal equality of states ("Westphalian sovereignty").

### Camp David Accords (1978)
- 🔥 Signed **17 September 1978** at **Camp David** (US presidential retreat, Maryland), mediated by US President **Jimmy Carter**.
- Between 🔥 **Egyptian President Anwar Sadat** and **Israeli PM Menachem Begin**.
- Led to the **Egypt–Israel Peace Treaty (26 March 1979)**: Israel returned the **Sinai** to Egypt; **Egypt became the first Arab state to recognise Israel**.
- 🔥 **Sadat and Begin** shared the **Nobel Peace Prize 1978**. Egypt was suspended from the Arab League (1979–89). **Sadat was assassinated in 1981.**

### Oslo Accords (1993 & 1995)
- 🔥 Secret talks in **Oslo, Norway**. **Oslo I** signed at the **White House, Washington, on 13 September 1993**, with the famous handshake of 🔥 **Yitzhak Rabin (Israel PM)** and **Yasser Arafat (PLO)**, hosted by **Bill Clinton**. (The document itself was signed by **Shimon Peres** for Israel and **Mahmoud Abbas** for the PLO.)
- Mutual recognition: the PLO recognised Israel; Israel recognised the **PLO** as the representative of the Palestinians.
- Created the **Palestinian Authority (1994)** with limited self-rule in **Gaza and Jericho** first.
- **Oslo II (1995, Taba/Washington):** divided the West Bank into **Areas A, B and C**.
- 🔥 **Nobel Peace Prize 1994: Arafat, Rabin and Shimon Peres.** Rabin was **assassinated in 1995**.

### Simla Agreement (1972)
- 🔥 Signed **2 July 1972** at **Simla (Shimla), India**, by 🔥 **Indira Gandhi** (India) and **Zulfikar Ali Bhutto** (Pakistan), after the **1971 war**.
- Both agreed to settle disputes **bilaterally** and peacefully.
- The 1971 ceasefire line in Kashmir became the 🔥 **Line of Control (LoC)**.
- Paved the way for the return of **Pakistani prisoners of war** and (with the Delhi Agreement, 1974) Pakistan's recognition of Bangladesh (**22 February 1974**).

## Quick revision
- **Westphalia 1648**: ended the Thirty Years' War; birth of sovereign states.
- **Camp David 1978**: **Sadat – Begin**, mediated by **Carter**; Nobel 1978.
- **Oslo I 1993**: **Rabin – Arafat**, White House; Nobel 1994 (Arafat, Rabin, Peres).
- **Simla 2 July 1972**: **Indira Gandhi – Z. A. Bhutto**; LoC.
- **Tashkent 1966**: **Shastri – Ayub**; **Indus Waters 1960**: Nehru – Ayub (World Bank).
- **Nanking 1842**: Hong Kong to Britain; **Paris 1783**: US independence.
- **Ganges Treaty 12 Dec 1996** (30 years); **CHT Accord 2 Dec 1997**; **LBA 1974 → enclaves swapped 2015**.
