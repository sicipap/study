# Vocabulary from newspapers

> **Why it matters:** BCS preliminary and written papers, and especially bank exams, use words from English dailies (The Daily Star, The Business Standard, New Age) in synonym, gap-fill and comprehension questions. These words cover economy, politics, law, crime, disaster and diplomacy.

## A. Economy & finance 🔥
| Word | Meaning | Bangla |
|---|---|---|
| **Austerity** | Strict cutting of public spending | কৃচ্ছ্রসাধন |
| **Bailout** | Financial rescue of a failing firm/country | আর্থিক উদ্ধার সহায়তা |
| **Budget deficit** | Spending more than income | বাজেট ঘাটতি |
| **Capital flight** | Large outflow of money from a country | পুঁজি পাচার |
| **Default / defaulter** 🔥 | Failure to repay a loan / one who fails | খেলাপি |
| **Depreciation** | Fall in the value of a currency (market-driven) | অবমূল্যায়ন (বাজারভিত্তিক) |
| **Devaluation** | Official lowering of a currency's value | অবমূল্যায়ন (সরকারি) |
| **Embezzlement** | Stealing money placed in one's trust | আত্মসাৎ |
| **Fiscal** | Related to government revenue and spending | রাজস্ব-সংক্রান্ত |
| **Monetary** | Related to money supply and interest rates | মুদ্রা-সংক্রান্ত |
| **Inflation** 🔥 | General rise in prices | মূল্যস্ফীতি |
| **Stagflation** | Inflation + slow growth + unemployment | মন্দাস্ফীতি |
| **Recession** | Decline in economic activity | মন্দা |
| **Remittance** 🔥 | Money sent home by workers abroad | প্রবাসী আয় |
| **Reserve (forex)** | Foreign currency held by the central bank | বৈদেশিক মুদ্রার রিজার্ভ |
| **Subsidy** | Government financial support | ভর্তুকি |
| **Tariff** | Tax on imports/exports | শুল্ক |
| **Stimulus** | Spending to boost the economy | প্রণোদনা |
| **Money laundering** 🔥 | Making illegal money look legal | অর্থপাচার |
| **Write-off** | Cancelling a bad loan from accounts | অবলোপন |
| **Syndicate** 🔥 | Group controlling a market to raise prices | সিন্ডিকেট, চক্র |
| **Letter of credit (LC)** | Bank guarantee for import payment | ঋণপত্র |
| **Mega project** | Very large infrastructure project | বৃহৎ প্রকল্প |
| **LDC graduation** | Moving out of the Least Developed Country category | এলডিসি উত্তরণ |
| **Blue economy** | Economy based on sea resources | সমুদ্র অর্থনীতি |

## Quick revision
- **Remittance** = money sent by expatriate workers.
- **Inflation** = rising prices; **stagflation** = inflation + stagnation.
- **Incumbent** = present office-holder.
- **Nepotism** = favouring relatives.
- **Repatriation** = sending refugees home.
- **Arson** = deliberate setting of fire; **extortion** = চাঁদাবাজি.
- **Sanction / embargo** = penalties on trade/country.
- **Verdict** = court decision; **acquittal** = released as not guilty.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** "Remittance" means:
(a) money sent home by workers abroad (b) a bank loan (c) a government tax (d) foreign aid

**2.** The "incumbent" president is the one who:
(a) has retired (b) currently holds the office (c) lost the election (d) is a candidate

**3.** "Nepotism" means:
(a) favouring friends (b) favouring relatives (c) taking bribes (d) delaying work

**4.** "Arson" means:
(a) theft (b) murder (c) deliberately setting fire to property (d) kidnapping

**5.** Sending refugees back to their own country is called:
(a) emigration (b) migration (c) asylum (d) repatriation

**6.** A general rise in prices is called:
(a) deflation (b) recession (c) inflation (d) devaluation

**7.** An agreement between two countries is:
(a) multilateral (b) unilateral (c) trilateral (d) bilateral

**8.** "Verdict" means:
(a) a court's decision (b) a police report (c) an accusation (d) a lawyer's fee

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a | Earnings sent home by expatriates |
| 2 | b | Present holder of the office |
| 3 | b | Favouritism to relatives |
| 4 | c | Deliberate burning of property |
| 5 | d | Repatriation, e.g. of Rohingyas |
| 6 | c | Inflation = rising general price level |
| 7 | d | bi = two |
| 8 | a | Judgment or decision of a court |
