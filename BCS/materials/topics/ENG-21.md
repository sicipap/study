# Articles

> **Why it matters:** Article questions test sound-based "a/an" (an hour, a university, an MA, a one-taka note), "the" with rivers, seas, unique things and superlatives, and when no article is used (go to school, by bus).

## The articles
- **Indefinite:** **a, an** — one, any, not particular.
- **Definite:** **the** — a particular, known thing.
- Articles are a kind of **adjective** (demonstrative adjective / determiner).

## A or An? — it depends on SOUND, not spelling 🔥
| Use **an** before a vowel **sound** | Use **a** before a consonant **sound** |
|---|---|
| **an hour, an honest man, an heir, an honour** (silent h) | **a university, a union, a European, a useful book, a uniform** ("yu" sound) |
| **an MA, an MBBS doctor, an SSC certificate, an LLB, an FIR, an X-ray, an NGO, an MP, an SI** (letter name starts with a vowel sound: "em", "es", "el", "ef", "ex", "en") | **a one-taka note, a one-eyed man, a once-famous actor** ("w" sound) |
| an apple, an egg, an umbrella, an ugly face | **a BA, a BSc, a UN officer, a hotel, a historical event** (usually), a horse |
| **an F grade** | **a U-turn** |

Tip: say the word aloud. "**An** hour" (sounds "our"), "**a** university" (sounds "yu").

## Uses of "a / an"
- One: "He has **a** car."
- Profession: "He is **a** doctor."
- Rate: "Tk 50 **a** kilo", "twice **a** day", "60 km **an** hour".
- Exclamation: "What **a** beautiful day!" "Such **a** fool!"
- Before a proper noun used as a common noun: "He is **a** Shakespeare of our age." "**A** Mr Karim came to see you."
- Fixed phrases: **a few, a little, a lot of, a great deal of, a number of, in a hurry, at a loss, all of a sudden, make a noise, take a walk, have a headache**.

## Uses of "the" 🔥
| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| Something already mentioned or known | "I saw a dog. **The** dog was black." |
| **Unique things** | **the sun, the moon, the earth, the sky, the world** |
| **Rivers, seas, oceans, canals, gulfs** | **the Padma, the Jamuna, the Bay of Bengal, the Pacific, the Suez Canal** |
| **Mountain ranges, groups of islands, deserts** | **the Himalayas, the Andes, the Maldives, the Sahara** |
| **Holy books, newspapers, ships, trains** | **the Quran, the Bible, the Daily Star, the Titanic** |
| Countries with plural or "union/kingdom/states/republic" | **the USA, the UK, the Netherlands, the Philippines, the People's Republic of China** |
| **Superlatives and ordinals** | **the best, the tallest, the first, the last** |
| **the + adjective** = a class of people | **the rich, the poor, the brave, the young** |
| **the + singular noun** = the whole class | "**The** cow is a useful animal." "**The** rose is sweet." |
| Musical instruments | "She plays **the** piano / **the** guitar." |
| Nationality (people) | **the English, the Bangalees, the Japanese** |
| Directions, historical events | **the east, the north; the French Revolution, the Liberation War** |
| **the + comparative, the + comparative** | "**The** more, **the** merrier." |
| Proper noun with a qualifying phrase | "**the** Dhaka of today", "**the** Bangladesh of 1971" |
| Name of a great work as a comparison | "Kalidasa is called **the** Shakespeare of India." |

## No article 🔥
- **Proper names** of persons, cities, countries, single mountains, lakes: **Dhaka, Bangladesh, Mount Everest, Lake Victoria** (but *the Himalayas*).
- **Uncountable and abstract nouns** in a general sense: "**Honesty** is the best policy." "**Gold** is precious." "**Milk** is good for health."
- **Meals, games, languages, subjects:** "have **breakfast**", "play **cricket**", "speak **English**", "study **mathematics**".
- **School, college, church, mosque, hospital, prison, bed, market** used for their **main purpose**: "He goes **to school**" (to study). "He is **in hospital**" (as a patient). But "He went **to the school** to meet the teacher" (visit the building).
- **Transport with "by":** **by bus, by train, by air**; also **at night, at home, at noon, on foot**.
- Days, months, seasons (usually), and **man** meaning mankind: "**Man** is mortal."
- Titles used as names: "**Queen** Elizabeth", "**President** Lincoln"; but "**the** President of Bangladesh".

## Common mistakes
| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| He is a honest man. | He is **an** honest man. |
| She is an university student. | She is **a** university student. |
| He has a MA degree. | He has **an** MA degree. |
| Padma is a big river. | **The** Padma is a big river. |
| The honesty is the best policy. | **Honesty** is the best policy. |
| He goes to the bed at 10. | He goes **to bed** at 10. |
| She plays piano. (exam style) | She plays **the** piano. |
| He is best boy in the class. | He is **the** best boy in the class. |
| I came here by the bus. | I came here **by bus**. |

## Quick revision
- 🔥 a/an depends on **sound**: **an hour, an MA, a university, a one-taka note**.
- 🔥 **the** + rivers, seas, mountain ranges, unique things, superlatives.
- **the + adjective** = a class of people (the rich).
- **No article** with proper names, abstract/material nouns in general, meals, games, languages.
- **go to school / bed / hospital** (purpose) — no article.
- **by bus, by train, at night, on foot**.
- **the USA, the UK, the Himalayas**, but **Mount Everest**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** He is ___ honest man.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article

**2.** She is ___ university student.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article

**3.** ___ Padma is a mighty river.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) no article

**4.** He has passed ___ MBBS examination.
(a) an (b) a (c) the (d) no article

**5.** Which sentence is correct?
(a) The honesty is the best policy. (b) He goes to the bed early. (c) She plays the piano. (d) He came by the train.

**6.** ___ rich should help the poor.
(a) A (b) The (c) An (d) no article

**7.** He gave me ___ one-taka note.
(a) an (b) the (c) a (d) no article

**8.** Mount Everest is the highest peak of ___ Himalayas.
(a) a (b) an (c) no article (d) the

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | honest starts with a vowel sound, the h is silent |
| 2 | a | university starts with a "yu" consonant sound |
| 3 | c | the + name of a river |
| 4 | a | M is pronounced "em", a vowel sound |
| 5 | c | the + musical instrument |
| 6 | b | the + adjective = a class of people |
| 7 | c | one starts with a "w" sound |
| 8 | d | the + mountain range |
