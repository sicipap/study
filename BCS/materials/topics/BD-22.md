# Judiciary, Ombudsman, ACC

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the two divisions of the Supreme Court, retirement age, writ jurisdiction (Art. 102), separation of the judiciary (1 November 2007, Masdar Hossain case), the first Chief Justice and first woman judge, the Ombudsman (Art. 77) and the Anti-Corruption Commission (2004).

## Structure of the judiciary 🔥
- Article **94**: the **Supreme Court of Bangladesh** has two divisions:
  - **আপিল বিভাগ (Appellate Division)** — highest court of appeal.
  - **হাইকোর্ট বিভাগ (High Court Division)** — original (writ) and appellate jurisdiction.
- Headed by the **Chief Justice of Bangladesh (প্রধান বিচারপতি)**, who sits in the Appellate Division.
- Seat: **Dhaka** (Art. 100).
- Judges are appointed by the **President** (Art. 95); the Chief Justice is appointed by the President **on his own** (Art. 48(3)).
- 🔥 Retirement age of Supreme Court judges: **67 years** (raised from 65 by the **14th amendment, 2004**).
- Qualification (Art. 95): citizen, and at least **10 years** as an advocate of the Supreme Court or **10 years** in judicial office (or other qualifications set by law).
- The Supreme Court is a **court of record** (Art. 108) and can punish for **contempt**; its decisions **bind** all lower courts (Art. 111).

### Subordinate courts (অধস্তন আদালত)
- **Civil:** District Judge → Additional District Judge → Joint District Judge → Senior Assistant Judge → Assistant Judge.
- **Criminal:** Sessions Judge → Additional Sessions Judge → Joint Sessions Judge; **Chief Judicial / Metropolitan Magistrate** → Judicial Magistrates.
- Special courts and tribunals: **Administrative Tribunals** (Art. 117) for service matters, Labour Court, Family Courts, etc.
- **Village courts (গ্রাম আদালত)** under the Union Parishad settle small disputes locally.
- **National Legal Aid Services Organization** gives free legal help to poor people (Legal Aid Act 2000).

## Separation of the judiciary 🔥🔥
- Article **22** (a state principle) says the state shall separate the judiciary from the executive.
- 🔥 **মাসদার হোসেন মামলা:** in **1999** the Appellate Division gave a **12-point directive** to separate the lower judiciary from the executive.
- 🔥 The lower judiciary was formally separated on **1 November 2007** (during the caretaker government). Executive magistrates lost their judicial (trial) powers, and **judicial magistrates** took over.

## Removal of judges
- Original 1972 Constitution: removal by **Parliament**.
- Later: the **Supreme Judicial Council (সুপ্রিম জুডিশিয়াল কাউন্সিল)** — the **Chief Justice** and the **two next senior judges** of the Appellate Division — inquires, and the President removes.
- The **16th amendment (2014)** gave this power back to Parliament, but it was declared **unconstitutional** (High Court 2016; Appellate Division 2017), so the **Supreme Judicial Council** system applies.

## Writs (Article 102) — recap 🔥
- The High Court Division can issue **habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, certiorari and quo warranto** to enforce fundamental rights (via Art. 44) or to control unlawful public action.
- **Public Interest Litigation (PIL)** is also brought under Article 102.

## Ombudsman (ন্যায়পাল) 🔥
- The word and the institution come from **Sweden** (first Ombudsman **1809**).
- The Ombudsman examines **complaints against public officials and ministries** (maladministration).
- 🔥 In Bangladesh: **Article 77** of the Constitution; the **Ombudsman Act, 1980**.
- 🔥 **No general (parliamentary) Ombudsman has ever been appointed** in Bangladesh.
- A **Tax Ombudsman** was set up under the **Tax Ombudsman Act, 2005** (first appointed in 2006); the office was **abolished in 2011**.

## Anti-Corruption Commission (দুর্নীতি দমন কমিশন, দুদক) 🔥
- Established under the **Anti-Corruption Commission Act, 2004**; started work on **21 November 2004**.
- It replaced the **Bureau of Anti-Corruption** (দুর্নীতি দমন ব্যুরো).
- An **independent statutory body** (created by law, **not by the Constitution**).
- Three members: a **Chairman** and **two Commissioners**, appointed by the President on the recommendation of a selection committee.
- First Chairman: **বিচারপতি সুলতান হোসেন খান**.
- Headquarters: **Segunbagicha, Dhaka**.

## Quick revision
- Supreme Court = **Appellate + High Court Divisions** (Art. 94).
- Judges retire at **67** (14th amendment, 2004).
- Writs: **Art. 102**; court of record: **Art. 108**.
- **Masdar Hossain case** (1999) → separation on **1 November 2007**.
- Removal of judges: **Supreme Judicial Council** (16th amendment struck down).
- First Chief Justice: **Justice A. S. M. Sayem**.
- First woman High Court judge: **Justice Nazmun Ara Sultana**.
- Ombudsman: **Art. 77**, **Act of 1980**; never appointed; origin **Sweden**.
- ACC: **Act 2004**, started **21 November 2004**; statutory; 3 members.
