# Quadrilaterals & polygons (interior/exterior angles)

> **Why it matters:** Polygon angle formulas (বহুভুজ) and properties of quadrilaterals (চতুর্ভুজ) are asked in almost every BCS and bank exam: sum of interior angles, each angle of a regular polygon, number of sides from an exterior angle, and number of diagonals.

## Polygons: key concepts
- A **polygon (বহুভুজ)** is a closed figure made of straight line segments. A **regular polygon (সুষম বহুভুজ)** has all sides and all angles equal.
- Names: triangle 3, quadrilateral 4, **pentagon (পঞ্চভুজ) 5**, **hexagon (ষড়ভুজ) 6**, heptagon 7, **octagon (অষ্টভুজ) 8**, nonagon 9, **decagon (দশভুজ) 10**.
- 🔥 The **sum of the exterior angles** of any convex polygon is always **360°**.
- Interior angle + exterior angle = **180°** at each vertex.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item (n sides) | Formula |
|---|---|
| 🔥 Sum of interior angles | **(n − 2) × 180°** = (2n − 4) right angles |
| Each interior angle (regular) | (n − 2) × 180°/n |
| 🔥 Each exterior angle (regular) | **360°/n** |
| Number of sides from exterior angle | n = 360°/exterior |
| 🔥 Number of diagonals | **n(n − 3)/2** |
| Interior = k × exterior | exterior = 180°/(k + 1) |

Handy values (regular): pentagon 108°, hexagon 120°, octagon 135°, decagon 144°.

## Quadrilaterals (চতুর্ভুজ)
- Sum of the angles of a quadrilateral = **360°**.

| Shape | Key properties |
|---|---|
| **Parallelogram (সামান্তরিক)** | opposite sides parallel and equal; opposite angles equal; adjacent angles add to 180°; diagonals **bisect each other** |
| **Rectangle (আয়তক্ষেত্র)** | parallelogram with all angles 90°; diagonals **equal** |
| **Rhombus (রম্বস)** | all sides equal; diagonals bisect each other **at right angles** (not equal) |
| **Square (বর্গক্ষেত্র)** | all sides equal, all angles 90°; diagonals equal and perpendicular |
| **Trapezium (ট্রাপিজিয়াম)** | only **one pair** of opposite sides parallel |
| **Kite (ঘুড়ি)** | two pairs of equal adjacent sides; diagonals perpendicular |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Find the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon.
Steps: (6 − 2) × 180° = 720°.
**Answer: 720°**

**Example 2.** Find each interior angle of a regular octagon.
Steps: (8 − 2) × 180°/8 = 1080°/8 = 135°. (Or: exterior = 360°/8 = 45°, interior = 180° − 45° = 135°.)
**Answer: 135°**

**Example 3.** Each exterior angle of a regular polygon is 24°. How many sides does it have?
Steps: 360°/24° = 15.
**Answer: 15**

**Example 4.** How many diagonals does a decagon have?
Steps: 10 × 7/2 = 35.
**Answer: 35**

**Example 5.** Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 140°. Find the number of sides.
Steps: Exterior = 180° − 140° = 40°. n = 360/40 = 9.
**Answer: 9**

**Example 6.** The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3 : 4. Find the largest.
Steps: 10 parts = 360° → 36° each. Largest = 4 × 36° = 144°.
**Answer: 144°**

## Common traps
- Sum of interior angles uses (n − 2), not n.
- Sum of exterior angles is 360° for **every** polygon, whatever n is.
- A rhombus's diagonals are perpendicular but **not equal**; a rectangle's are equal but not perpendicular.
- Diagonals formula is n(n − 3)/2, not n(n − 1)/2 (that counts sides too).

## Quick revision
- Interior sum = (n − 2) × 180°.
- Each exterior (regular) = 360°/n.
- Diagonals = n(n − 3)/2.
- Quadrilateral angle sum = 360°.
- Parallelogram diagonals bisect; rectangle diagonals equal; rhombus diagonals perpendicular.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The sum of the interior angles of a pentagon is:
(a) 360° (b) 540° (c) 720° (d) 450°

**2.** Each interior angle of a regular hexagon is:
(a) 108° (b) 135° (c) 120° (d) 60°

**3.** Each exterior angle of a regular polygon is 40°. The number of sides is:
(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 9

**4.** The number of diagonals of a hexagon is:
(a) 9 (b) 6 (c) 12 (d) 15

**5.** A polygon has 20 diagonals. The number of sides is:
(a) 10 (b) 8 (c) 7 (d) 9

**6.** In which quadrilateral do the diagonals bisect each other at right angles but are not equal?
(a) rectangle (b) trapezium (c) rhombus (d) parallelogram

**7.** The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5 : 6. The largest angle is:
(a) 120° (b) 100° (c) 108° (d) 150°

**8.** Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 5 times its exterior angle. The number of sides is:
(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 6 (d) 15

**9.** One angle of a parallelogram is 70°. The angle adjacent to it is:
(a) 70° (b) 20° (c) 290° (d) 110°

**10.** The sum of the exterior angles of a regular decagon is:
(a) 1440° (b) 360° (c) 180° (d) 720°

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | (5 − 2) × 180° = 540° |
| 2 | c | 720°/6 = 120° |
| 3 | d | 360°/40° = 9 |
| 4 | a | 6 × 3/2 = 9 |
| 5 | b | n(n − 3)/2 = 20 → n = 8 |
| 6 | c | Rhombus diagonals are perpendicular bisectors, not equal |
| 7 | a | 18 parts = 360° → 6 × 20° = 120° |
| 8 | b | Exterior = 180°/6 = 30° → n = 12 |
| 9 | d | Adjacent angles of a parallelogram add to 180° |
| 10 | b | Sum of exterior angles of any polygon = 360° |
