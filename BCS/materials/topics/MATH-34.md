# Trigonometric ratios & identities

> **Why it matters:** Trigonometry (ত্রিকোণমিতি) questions in BCS and bank exams are usually one-step: standard values (sin 30°, tan 60°), finding other ratios from one ratio, and the three basic identities. Learn the value table by heart.

## Key concepts
In a right triangle with angle θ: **opposite (লম্ব)**, **adjacent (ভূমি)**, **hypotenuse (অতিভুজ)**.

| Ratio | Definition | Reciprocal |
|---|---|---|
| **sin θ** | opposite/hypotenuse | cosec θ = 1/sin θ |
| **cos θ** | adjacent/hypotenuse | sec θ = 1/cos θ |
| **tan θ** | opposite/adjacent = sin θ/cos θ | cot θ = 1/tan θ |

- Memory aid: "**Some People Have Curly Brown Hair Through Proper Brushing**" → sin = P/H, cos = B/H, tan = P/B.
- sin θ and cos θ are never greater than 1 (for real θ); sec θ and cosec θ are never less than 1 in size.
- **Radian (রেডিয়ান):** π radians = 180°, so 1 radian ≈ 57.3° (57°17′45″). 1° = π/180 radian.

## Standard values table 🔥
| θ | 0° | 30° | 45° | 60° | 90° |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sin θ | 0 | 1/2 | 1/√2 | √3/2 | 1 |
| cos θ | 1 | √3/2 | 1/√2 | 1/2 | 0 |
| tan θ | 0 | 1/√3 | 1 | √3 | undefined |

Trick: sin values are √0/2, √1/2, √2/2, √3/2, √4/2; cos is the same list reversed.

## Identities and shortcuts 🔥
| Identity | Form |
|---|---|
| 🔥 Pythagorean 1 | **sin²θ + cos²θ = 1** |
| Pythagorean 2 | **sec²θ − tan²θ = 1** |
| Pythagorean 3 | **cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1** |
| Complementary angles | sin(90° − θ) = cos θ; tan(90° − θ) = cot θ; sec(90° − θ) = cosec θ |
| If sec θ + tan θ = k | sec θ − tan θ = 1/k |
| Double angle | sin 2θ = 2 sin θ cos θ; cos 2θ = cos²θ − sin²θ |
| sin(A + B) | sin A cos B + cos A sin B |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** If sin θ = 3/5, find cos θ and tan θ.
Steps: Opposite 3, hypotenuse 5 → adjacent = √(25 − 9) = 4. cos θ = 4/5, tan θ = 3/4.
**Answer: 4/5 and 3/4**

**Example 2.** Find tan 45° + sin 30°.
Steps: 1 + 1/2 = 3/2.
**Answer: 3/2**

**Example 3.** Find sin 60° cos 30° + cos 60° sin 30°.
Steps: (√3/2)(√3/2) + (1/2)(1/2) = 3/4 + 1/4 = 1 (it equals sin 90°).
**Answer: 1**

**Example 4.** If sec θ + tan θ = 2, find sec θ − tan θ.
Steps: (sec θ + tan θ)(sec θ − tan θ) = sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 → 1/2.
**Answer: 1/2**

**Example 5.** If tan θ = 1 (θ acute), find sin θ + cos θ.
Steps: θ = 45° → 1/√2 + 1/√2 = 2/√2 = √2.
**Answer: √2**

## Common traps
- Mixing up sin 30° (1/2) and sin 60° (√3/2).
- tan 90° is **undefined**, not 0 or 1.
- sin²θ means (sin θ)², not sin(θ²).
- sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 (minus sign), while sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 (plus sign).

## Quick revision
- sin 30° = 1/2, cos 60° = 1/2, tan 45° = 1, tan 60° = √3.
- sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; sec²θ − tan²θ = 1; cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1.
- sin(90° − θ) = cos θ.
- π rad = 180°; 1 rad ≈ 57.3°.
- sec θ + tan θ = k ⇒ sec θ − tan θ = 1/k.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** sin 30° = ?
(a) 1/2 (b) √3/2 (c) 1/√2 (d) 1

**2.** tan 60° = ?
(a) 1/√3 (b) 1 (c) √3 (d) √3/2

**3.** If cos θ = 12/13, then tan θ = ?
(a) 12/5 (b) 5/12 (c) 5/13 (d) 13/12

**4.** sin²θ + cos²θ = ?
(a) 0 (b) 2 (c) tan²θ (d) 1

**5.** sec²45° − tan²45° = ?
(a) 1 (b) 0 (c) 2 (d) √2

**6.** sin(90° − θ) = ?
(a) sin θ (b) cos θ (c) tan θ (d) sec θ

**7.** 2 sin 30° cos 30° = ?
(a) 1/2 (b) 1 (c) √3/2 (d) √3

**8.** π/3 radians = ?
(a) 30° (b) 45° (c) 90° (d) 60°

**9.** If sec θ + tan θ = 3, then sec θ − tan θ = ?
(a) 1/3 (b) 3 (c) −3 (d) 2/3

**10.** cot 45° + cosec 30° = ?
(a) 2 (b) 3/2 (c) 3 (d) 1 + √2
