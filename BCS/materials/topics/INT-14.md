# Russia–Ukraine war

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for the start date of the full-scale invasion (24 February 2022), the Crimea annexation (2014), the Minsk agreements, the Budapest Memorandum, the Black Sea Grain Initiative, the ICC warrant for Putin, UN votes and the war's impact on Bangladesh.

## Basic facts
| Item | Ukraine | Russia |
|---|---|---|
| Capital | 🔥 **Kyiv** | **Moscow** |
| Currency | **Hryvnia** | **Ruble** |
| Parliament | 🔥 **Verkhovna Rada** | **Federal Assembly** (State **Duma** + Federation Council) |
| Leader (as of early 2026) | President **Volodymyr Zelensky** (since 2019) | President **Vladimir Putin** |

- Ukraine became independent on 🔥 **24 August 1991** (confirmed by referendum on 1 December 1991), when the USSR collapsed.

## Background (পটভূমি) 🔥
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **1994** | **Budapest Memorandum** (5 December): Ukraine gave up the Soviet nuclear weapons on its soil (then the **third-largest nuclear arsenal**) in return for security assurances from the **USA, UK and Russia** |
| 2004 | **Orange Revolution** (against a disputed election) |
| 2013–14 | **Euromaidan** protests after President **Viktor Yanukovych** rejected an EU Association Agreement; he fled in February 2014 |
| 🔥 **March 2014** | Russia **annexed Crimea** after a disputed referendum (16 March; annexation 18 March). UNGA Resolution 68/262 upheld Ukraine's territorial integrity |
| 2014 | War in the **Donbas** region: Russia-backed separatists declared the "**Donetsk**" and "**Luhansk**" People's Republics |
| July 2014 | Malaysia Airlines flight **MH17** shot down over eastern Ukraine |
| 🔥 **2014 & 2015** | **Minsk I and Minsk II** agreements (ceasefire plans; talks in the **Normandy format**: Russia, Ukraine, Germany, France) — never fully implemented |
| Nov 2021 – Feb 2022 | Russian troop build-up; Russia demanded that NATO stop expanding eastward |

## The full-scale war (from 2022) 🔥
- 🔥 **21 February 2022:** Russia recognised the "independence" of Donetsk and Luhansk.
- 🔥 **24 February 2022:** Putin announced a "**special military operation**" and Russia launched a **full-scale invasion** of Ukraine.
- The attack on **Kyiv** failed; Russian forces withdrew from the north (spring 2022). **Bucha** killings of civilians were uncovered.
- 🔥 **30 September 2022:** Russia claimed to annex **four regions: Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson** (none fully controlled); UNGA condemned it.
- Ukraine's counter-offensives freed **Kharkiv region** and **Kherson city** (November 2022); the **2023 counter-offensive** made little progress.
- Russia took **Bakhmut (2023)** and **Avdiivka (2024)**; Ukraine made an incursion into Russia's **Kursk region** (August 2024), later pushed back with help from **North Korean** troops.
- **Wagner Group mutiny** under **Yevgeny Prigozhin**, June 2023 (he died in a plane crash in August 2023).
- **Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant** — Europe's largest — has been under Russian control since March 2022.
- **Kakhovka dam** destroyed, June 2023.

### Diplomacy and international law 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| **UNGA Resolution ES-11/1** (2 March 2022) | Demanded Russian withdrawal; **141** votes in favour; 🔥 **Bangladesh abstained** |
| Human Rights Council | **Russia suspended**, April 2022 |
| 🔥 **ICC arrest warrant for Putin** | **17 March 2023**, for the unlawful **deportation of Ukrainian children** |
| 🔥 **Black Sea Grain Initiative** | **22 July 2022**, **Istanbul**, brokered by the **UN and Türkiye**; Russia **quit in July 2023** |
| Sanctions | Western sanctions on Russian banks, oil (price cap), and removal of some Russian banks from **SWIFT** |
| NATO | War led **Finland (2023)** and **Sweden (2024)** to join NATO |
| EU | Ukraine got **EU candidate status** in June 2022 |
| 2025 talks | Direct Russia–Ukraine talks in **Istanbul** (May 2025); **Trump–Putin summit in Alaska** (August 2025); US peace plan proposals (late 2025) |

## Impact on Bangladesh 🔥
- **Prices:** global prices of **wheat, fertiliser, LNG and fuel** rose sharply → higher **inflation** and pressure on **foreign-exchange reserves** in Bangladesh.
- 🔥 **MV Banglar Samriddhi**, a Bangladeshi ship, was hit by a missile at **Olvia port, Ukraine**, on **2 March 2022**; one engineer (**Hadisur Rahman**) was killed.
- **Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant** is built by Russia's **Rosatom**; sanctions complicated loan repayments. A sanctioned Russian ship (**Ursa Major**) carrying Rooppur equipment was **not allowed to dock** in Bangladesh (December 2022).
- Bangladesh called for peaceful settlement and **abstained** in several UN votes.

## Quick revision
- Ukraine independent: **24 August 1991**; parliament: **Verkhovna Rada**; capital **Kyiv**.
- **Budapest Memorandum 1994**: Ukraine gave up nuclear weapons.
- Crimea annexed: **March 2014**; Minsk agreements **2014–15**.
- Full-scale invasion: **24 February 2022**.
- Four regions "annexed": **Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson** (Sept 2022).
- Grain deal: **Istanbul, July 2022** (UN + Türkiye); Russia quit **July 2023**.
- ICC warrant for Putin: **17 March 2023**.
- Bangladesh **abstained** on UNGA ES-11/1; **Banglar Samriddhi** hit at Olvia.

**3.** By the Budapest Memorandum (1994), Ukraine agreed to —
(a) join NATO (b) give up nuclear weapons (c) lease Crimea to Russia (d) leave the CIS

**4.** Russia annexed Crimea in —
(a) 2008 (b) 2014 (c) 2019 (d) 2022

**5.** The Black Sea Grain Initiative was brokered by the UN and —
(a) Türkiye (b) China (c) India (d) Qatar

**6.** Why did the ICC issue an arrest warrant for Vladimir Putin in March 2023?
(a) Attack on Kyiv (b) Annexation of Crimea (c) Destruction of the Kakhovka dam (d) Deportation of Ukrainian children

**7.** The Bangladeshi ship hit by a missile at Olvia port in March 2022 was —
(a) MV Abdullah (b) MV Banglar Samriddhi (c) MV Banglar Jyoti (d) MV Banglar Urmi

**8.** Which region was NOT among the four Ukrainian regions Russia claimed to annex in 2022?
(a) Donetsk (b) Kherson (c) Kharkiv (d) Zaporizhzhia

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a | Putin announced the "special military operation" on 24 February 2022 |
| 2 | c | Ukraine's parliament is the Verkhovna Rada; Russia's lower house is the Duma |
| 3 | b | Ukraine gave up its Soviet-era nuclear arsenal for security assurances |
| 4 | b | Crimea was annexed in March 2014 after a disputed referendum |
| 5 | a | Signed in Istanbul in July 2022 with Türkiye's mediation |
| 6 | d | The charge was unlawful deportation of children |
| 7 | b | Banglar Samriddhi was hit on 2 March 2022; one engineer died |
| 8 | c | The four were Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson |
