# Nuclear treaties: NPT, CTBT, TPNW

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask when the NPT was signed and came into force, the five recognised nuclear-weapon states, which countries never joined, the CTBT (not yet in force), the TPNW (2017/2021), and Bangladesh's record (first South Asian country to ratify the CTBT; TPNW ratified in 2019).

## Nuclear basics 🔥
- 🔥 First nuclear test: **"Trinity", USA, 16 July 1945** (Manhattan Project; **J. Robert Oppenheimer**).
- 🔥 Only wartime use: **Hiroshima (6 August 1945, "Little Boy", uranium)** and **Nagasaki (9 August 1945, "Fat Man", plutonium)**.
- **Nuclear-armed states (9):** USA, Russia, UK, France, China (the five recognised by the NPT), plus **India, Pakistan, North Korea**, and **Israel** (never officially declared).

| Country | First nuclear test |
|---|---|
| USA | 1945 |
| USSR | 1949 |
| UK | 1952 |
| France | 1960 |
| China | 1964 |
| 🔥 **India** | **1974** ("**Smiling Buddha**", Pokhran); again **May 1998** (Pokhran-II) |
| 🔥 **Pakistan** | **May 1998** (**Chagai**, Balochistan) |
| **North Korea** | **2006** |

- **IAEA** (International Atomic Energy Agency): **1957**, HQ **Vienna**; "**Atoms for Peace**" (idea of US President **Eisenhower**, 1953); **Nobel Peace 2005** (with DG **Mohamed ElBaradei**); runs **safeguards** (inspections) under the NPT.

## NPT (Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty / পরমাণু অস্ত্র বিস্তার রোধ চুক্তি) 🔥
- 🔥 Opened for signature on **1 July 1968**; 🔥 **in force 5 March 1970**; **extended indefinitely in 1995**.
- **Three pillars:** **non-proliferation**, **disarmament**, and the **peaceful use** of nuclear energy.
- 🔥 **Recognised nuclear-weapon states (NWS): USA, Russia, UK, France, China** — those that tested before **1 January 1967** (also the **P5**).
- **About 191 parties** — the most widely joined arms-control treaty.
- 🔥 **Never joined: India, Pakistan, Israel, South Sudan.** **North Korea announced withdrawal in 2003.**
- **Review Conference** every **5 years** (the 2015 and 2022 conferences ended without an agreed final document; one was held in **2026**).
- 🔥 **Bangladesh ratified the NPT in 1979.**

## CTBT (Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty / সমন্বিত পরমাণু পরীক্ষা নিষিদ্ধ চুক্তি) 🔥
- Earlier: 🔥 **Partial Test Ban Treaty (PTBT), 1963, Moscow**: banned tests **in the atmosphere, outer space and under water** (but not underground).
- 🔥 CTBT **adopted by the UN General Assembly on 10 September 1996**; opened for signature **24 September 1996**.
- Bans **all nuclear explosions** everywhere.
- 🔥 **Not yet in force**: it needs ratification by all **44 "Annex 2" states** (countries with nuclear reactors in the 1990s). Non-ratifiers include the **USA, China, Egypt, Iran, Israel**; **India, Pakistan and North Korea** have not signed.
- **Russia withdrew its ratification (de-ratified) in November 2023.**
- Monitoring body: **CTBTO Preparatory Commission, Vienna** (International Monitoring System).
- 🔥 **Bangladesh was the first South Asian country to sign and ratify the CTBT** (signed 1996, ratified **2000**).

## TPNW (Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons / পরমাণু অস্ত্র নিষিদ্ধকরণ চুক্তি) 🔥
- 🔥 Adopted **7 July 2017** at the UN (122 votes); 🔥 **in force 22 January 2021** (after the 50th ratification).
- The **first legally binding treaty to fully ban nuclear weapons**: developing, testing, possessing, using, threatening to use, or hosting them.
- Campaign group **ICAN** (International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons) won the **Nobel Peace Prize 2017**.
- 🔥 **No nuclear-armed state and no NATO member has joined.**
- 🔥 **Bangladesh** signed on the opening day (**20 September 2017**) and **ratified on 26 September 2019**; it is among the first South Asian parties (Bangladeshi sources often call it the first in South Asia).

## Other arms-control treaties
| Treaty | Year | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Antarctic Treaty | 1959 | Antarctica for peaceful use only |
| Outer Space Treaty | 1967 | No nuclear weapons in orbit |
| **Treaty of Tlatelolco** | 1967 | 🔥 **First** nuclear-weapon-free zone in a populated region: **Latin America & Caribbean** |
| Treaty of Rarotonga / Bangkok / Pelindaba / Semipalatinsk | 1985 / 1995 / 1996 / 2006 | NWFZs of the **South Pacific / South-East Asia / Africa / Central Asia** |
| **SALT I** & **ABM Treaty** | 1972 | US–USSR limits (USA left ABM in 2002) |
| **INF Treaty** | 1987 | Banned intermediate-range missiles; **USA withdrew in 2019** |
| **START I** | 1991 | US–USSR strategic arms cut |
| 🔥 **New START** | **2010** (in force 2011) | Last US–Russia limit (1,550 deployed warheads each); Russia **suspended** participation in 2023; it **expired on 5 February 2026** |
| **Biological Weapons Convention** | 1972 (in force 1975) | Bans biological weapons |
| **Chemical Weapons Convention** | 1993 (in force 1997) | Bans chemical weapons; body **OPCW**, **The Hague**, Nobel Peace **2013** |
| **JCPOA** (Iran nuclear deal) | 2015 | Iran + P5 + Germany (+EU); **USA withdrew 2018** |

## Bangladesh's position 🔥
- Constitution **Article 25**: supports **general and complete disarmament**.
- Party to **NPT (1979)**, **CTBT (ratified 2000, first in South Asia)**, **TPNW (ratified 2019)**, BWC and CWC.
- **Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant** (Pabna), built with **Russia (Rosatom)**, is for **peaceful** electricity use under IAEA safeguards.

## Quick revision
- NPT: signed **1 July 1968**, in force **5 March 1970**, extended **indefinitely 1995**.
- 5 NWS: **USA, Russia, UK, France, China**; outside NPT: **India, Pakistan, Israel, South Sudan**; North Korea withdrew **2003**.
- PTBT **1963** (no atmospheric/space/underwater tests).
- CTBT: UNGA **10 Sept 1996**; **not in force**; needs **44 Annex 2** states.
- TPNW: adopted **7 July 2017**, in force **22 January 2021**; ICAN Nobel **2017**.
- Bangladesh: **first in South Asia** to ratify the **CTBT** (2000); ratified the **TPNW** in **2019**.
- IAEA **1957, Vienna**; New START expired **5 Feb 2026**.
