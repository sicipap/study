# Six-point 1966, Agartala case, 1969 uprising

> **Why it matters:** Standard questions: where and when the Six-point was placed, its nickname ("Magna Carta"), 7 June, the Agartala case (accused no. 1, Sergeant Zahurul Haq), the 11-point programme, Asad and Matiur, and who gave the title "Bangabandhu".

## Six-point programme (ছয় দফা) 🔥🔥
- Placed by **শেখ মুজিবুর রহমান** at a conference of opposition parties in **লাহোর** on **5 February 1966**.
- Called the **"বাঙালির মুক্তির সনদ" (Charter of Freedom)** and the **"Magna Carta of the Bengalis"**.
- It was based on the spirit of the **Lahore Resolution (1940)** and demanded **full autonomy** for East Pakistan.

| Point | Demand (in short) |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Federal, parliamentary government** on the basis of the Lahore Resolution; direct elections by adult franchise; supreme legislature |
| 2 | Federal (central) government to deal only with **defence and foreign affairs** |
| 3 | **Two separate but freely convertible currencies**, or one currency with measures to stop capital flight from East to West |
| 4 | **Power of taxation** and revenue collection with the federating units (provinces) |
| 5 | **Separate foreign-exchange accounts** for the two wings; provinces free to make trade links abroad |
| 6 | A separate **militia or paramilitary force** for East Pakistan |

- 🔥 **7 June 1966:** a general strike for the Six-point; police firing killed several people including worker **মনু মিয়া** (Tejgaon). **7 June = ছয় দফা দিবস**.
- Mujib and many leaders were arrested.

## Agartala Conspiracy Case (আগরতলা ষড়যন্ত্র মামলা) 🔥
- Official name: **"রাষ্ট্র বনাম শেখ মুজিবুর রহমান ও অন্যান্য" (State vs. Sheikh Mujibur Rahman and others)**.
- Charge: conspiring with India (at **Agartala**, Tripura) to make East Pakistan independent by armed revolt.
- **35 accused**; 🔥 **Sheikh Mujibur Rahman was accused no. 1**.
- Case filed in **January 1968**; trial began on **19 June 1968** in a special tribunal at **Kurmitola cantonment**, Dhaka.
- 🔥 Accused **সার্জেন্ট জহুরুল হক** was **shot dead in custody on 15 February 1969**. (Dhaka University's Iqbal Hall was later renamed **জহুরুল হক হল**.)
- Under the pressure of the mass uprising, the Government **withdrew the case on 22 February 1969** and released all accused.

## 1969 mass uprising (উনসত্তরের গণঅভ্যুত্থান) 🔥
- **ছাত্র সংগ্রাম পরিষদ** (Students' Action Committee) announced the **১১ দফা (11-point programme)** in **January 1969**. It included the Six-point demands plus student and peasant–worker demands.
- 🔥 **20 January 1969:** student leader **আসাদ (আমানুল্লাহ মোহাম্মদ আসাদুজ্জামান)** was killed by police; **Ayub Gate** was renamed **আসাদ গেট**. Poet **শামসুর রাহমান** wrote **"আসাদের শার্ট"**.
- 🔥 **24 January 1969:** school student **মতিউর রহমান** was killed. **24 January is observed as গণঅভ্যুত্থান দিবস**.
- **18 February 1969:** **Dr. শামসুজ্জোহা**, proctor of **Rajshahi University**, was killed by the army while protecting students — often called the **first martyred intellectual (teacher)** of the independence struggle.
- **22 February 1969:** Agartala case withdrawn; Mujib released.
- 🔥 **23 February 1969:** at a huge reception at the **Race Course**, Mujib was given the title **"বঙ্গবন্ধু"**. The title was announced by **তোফায়েল আহমেদ**, **DUCSU VP** and leader of the Students' Action Committee.
- **25 March 1969:** **Ayub Khan resigned**; General **ইয়াহিয়া খান** took power and imposed **martial law**.

## After the uprising
- **5 December 1969:** at a meeting remembering Suhrawardy, Bangabandhu said East Pakistan would be called **"বাংলাদেশ"**.
- Yahya issued the **Legal Framework Order (LFO)** on **30 March 1970** for elections and abolished **One Unit** (1 July 1970). Elections were announced on the basis of **population (one person, one vote)** — which gave East Pakistan a majority of seats (see BD-11).

## Quick revision
- Six-point: **5 February 1966, Lahore**; "Magna Carta of the Bengalis".
- Point 2: centre keeps only **defence and foreign affairs**; point 6: **militia** for East Pakistan.
- Six-point Day: **7 June**; martyr **Manu Mia**.
- Agartala case: **35 accused**, Mujib **no. 1**; Zahurul Haq killed **15 Feb 1969**.
- 11-point: **Students' Action Committee**, 1969.
- Asad: **20 Jan 1969** (Asad Gate); Matiur: **24 Jan 1969** (Mass Uprising Day).
- Dr. Shamsuzzoha (Rajshahi University): **18 Feb 1969**.
- "Bangabandhu": **23 Feb 1969**, announced by **Tofail Ahmed**.
- Ayub resigned **25 March 1969**; **Yahya Khan** took over.
