# Simple interest

> **Why it matters:** Simple interest (সরল মুনাফা/সরল সুদ) is a staple of bank, primary-teacher and 9th–20th grade exams: find interest, principal, rate or time, and "doubles in n years" questions.

## Key concepts
- **Principal (আসল, P):** the money borrowed or deposited.
- **Rate (মুনাফার হার, r):** interest per Tk 100 per year (% per annum).
- **Time (সময়, n or t):** usually in years. Convert months to years (÷ 12) and days to years (÷ 365).
- **Interest (মুনাফা/সুদ, I):** the extra money paid.
- **Amount (সবৃদ্ধিমূল, A):** principal + interest.
- In simple interest, the interest is the **same every year**, because it is always calculated on the original principal.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| 🔥 Simple interest | **I = P × r × n / 100** |
| Amount | A = P + I = P(1 + rn/100) |
| Principal | P = 100 × I / (r × n) |
| Rate | r = 100 × I / (P × n) |
| Time | n = 100 × I / (P × r) |
| 🔥 Sum doubles in n years | r = 100/n % |
| Sum becomes k times in n years | r = (k − 1) × 100 / n % |
| Amount A₁ after n₁ years and A₂ after n₂ years | Interest per year = (A₂ − A₁)/(n₂ − n₁); P = A₁ − n₁ × (yearly interest) |
| If a sum doubles in n years, it becomes k times in | (k − 1) × n years |
| Difference of interest at two rates r₁, r₂ | P × (r₁ − r₂) × n / 100 |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Find the simple interest on Tk 5,000 at 8% per annum for 3 years.
Steps: I = 5000 × 8 × 3/100 = 1200.
**Answer: Tk 1,200**

**Example 2.** At what rate of simple interest does a sum double in 8 years?
Steps: r = 100/8 = 12.5%.
**Answer: 12.5%**

**Example 3.** A sum amounts to Tk 7,000 in 3 years and Tk 8,000 in 5 years. Find the principal.
Steps: Interest for 2 years = 1000 → 500 per year. P = 7000 − 3 × 500 = 5,500.
**Answer: Tk 5,500**

**Example 4.** In how many years will Tk 2,000 become Tk 2,600 at 5% simple interest?
Steps: I = 600. n = 100 × 600/(2000 × 5) = 6.
**Answer: 6 years**

**Example 5.** A sum becomes 3 times itself in 20 years at simple interest. Find the rate.
Steps: r = (3 − 1) × 100/20 = 10%.
**Answer: 10%**

## Common traps
- "Becomes 3 times" means interest = **2P**, not 3P.
- Time in months must be divided by 12 (9 months = 3/4 year).
- A rate given "per month" must be multiplied by 12 for a yearly rate (1% a month = 12% a year).
- If a sum doubles in 6 years, it becomes 4 times in **18** years (interest 3P), not 12.

## Quick revision
- I = Prn/100; A = P + I.
- Doubles in n years ⇒ r = 100/n.
- k times in n years ⇒ r = (k − 1) × 100/n.
- Yearly interest = (A₂ − A₁)/(n₂ − n₁).
- Simple interest is equal every year.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** What is the simple interest on Tk 3,000 at 6% per annum for 4 years?
(a) Tk 720 (b) Tk 600 (c) Tk 780 (d) Tk 180

**2.** At what rate of simple interest does a sum double in 10 years?
(a) 5% (b) 10% (c) 20% (d) 12%

**3.** A sum gives Tk 450 simple interest in 3 years at 5%. The sum is:
(a) Tk 2,500 (b) Tk 4,500 (c) Tk 1,500 (d) Tk 3,000

**4.** A sum amounts to Tk 1,400 in 2 years and Tk 1,600 in 4 years at simple interest. The principal is:
(a) Tk 1,000 (b) Tk 1,300 (c) Tk 1,200 (d) Tk 1,100

**5.** A sum doubles itself in 6 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become 4 times?
(a) 12 years (b) 24 years (c) 20 years (d) 18 years

**6.** At what rate will Tk 1,500 earn Tk 360 simple interest in 3 years?
(a) 8% (b) 6% (c) 7.2% (d) 12%

**7.** What is the simple interest on Tk 10,000 for 1½ years at 1% per month?
(a) Tk 150 (b) Tk 1,800 (c) Tk 1,500 (d) Tk 1,200

**8.** A sum becomes 7/4 of itself in 6 years at simple interest. The rate is:
(a) 10% (b) 11.5% (c) 12.5% (d) 15%

**9.** In how many years will Tk 1,000 become Tk 1,300 at 5% simple interest?
(a) 6 years (b) 5 years (c) 3 years (d) 8 years

**10.** The difference between the simple interest at 5% and at 4% on a sum for 3 years is Tk 90. The sum is:
(a) Tk 9,000 (b) Tk 3,000 (c) Tk 2,500 (d) Tk 4,500
