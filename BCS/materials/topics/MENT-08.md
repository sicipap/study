# Direction sense

> **Why it matters:** Direction (দিক) questions ask where a person ends up after several turns, how far he is from the start, or which way a shadow falls. A quick sketch and the Pythagoras rule solve almost all of them.

## The eight directions 🔥
```
         N
    NW       NE
 W               E
    SW       SE
         S
```
- On a map: **North up, South down, East right, West left**.
- Between two main directions lies the corner direction: NE is between N and E, and so on.
- Opposites: N ↔ S, E ↔ W, NE ↔ SW, NW ↔ SE.

## Turning rules 🔥
| Facing | After a right turn (90° clockwise) | After a left turn (90° anticlockwise) |
|---|---|---|
| North | East | West |
| East | South | North |
| South | West | East |
| West | North | South |

- A **right turn = 90° clockwise**; a **left turn = 90° anticlockwise**; an about-turn = 180°.
- For turns of 45° and more, use bearings: N = 0°, NE = 45°, E = 90°, SE = 135°, S = 180°, SW = 225°, W = 270°, NW = 315°. Add for clockwise, subtract for anticlockwise, then reduce by 360° if needed.

## Distance: Pythagoras rule
Shortest distance from the start = **√(total east–west distance² + total north–south distance²)**.
Common triples: **3-4-5, 6-8-10, 5-12-13, 8-15-17**.

## Shadow rules 🔥
- The sun rises in the **east** (morning) and sets in the **west** (evening).
- A shadow falls **opposite the sun**: in the morning a shadow falls to the **west**; in the evening it falls to the **east**.
- At noon the sun is high in the sky and shadows are at their shortest. Exam questions usually use morning or evening.

## Method
1. Draw the start point and mark N at the top.
2. Draw each move as a line; write the distance.
3. Add up the net east–west and net north–south moves.
4. Use Pythagoras for the straight-line distance and read the direction from the sketch.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** A man walks 3 km north and then 4 km east. How far is he from the start?
√(3² + 4²) = √25 = **5 km** (to the north-east).

**Example 2.** Rina walks 10 m south, turns left and walks 5 m, turns left again and walks 10 m. Where is she?
South 10 → left of south is east: 5 m east → left of east is north: 10 m north, back to the starting line. **5 m east of the start.**

**Example 3.** One evening, Rahim and Karim were talking face to face. Rahim's shadow fell exactly to his right. Which way was Rahim facing?
Evening → the sun is in the west → shadows fall east. East is on Rahim's right, so he faces **north**.

**Example 4.** If south-east is called north and north-east is called west, what is west called?
SE (135°) → N (0°): every direction is turned back by 135°. Check: NE 45° − 135° = −90° = 270° = W ✔. West 270° − 135° = 135° = **south-east**.

**Example 5.** A man faces west, turns 45° clockwise, then 180° clockwise, then 270° anticlockwise. Where does he face now?
270 + 45 = 315; 315 + 180 = 495 → 135; 135 − 270 = −135 → 225° = **south-west**.

## Quick revision
- Right turn = clockwise; left turn = anticlockwise.
- Morning shadow falls west; evening shadow falls east.
- 3-4-5 and 6-8-10 are the most used triangles.
- Use bearings (N = 0°, clockwise) for 45° turns.
- If directions are renamed, find the angle of rotation from one pair and apply it to all.
- Always draw a quick sketch.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** A man walks 3 km north, then 4 km east. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 7 km (b) 5 km (c) 6 km (d) 1 km

**2.** You face north, turn right, turn right again, then turn left. Which way do you face now?
(a) North (b) South (c) West (d) East

**3.** Rina walks 10 m south, turns left and walks 5 m, then turns left again and walks 10 m. Where is she from the starting point?
(a) 5 m east (b) 5 m west (c) 10 m north (d) 15 m east

**4.** Soon after sunrise, Karim stood facing the sun. In which direction did his shadow fall?
(a) East (b) North (c) West (d) South

**5.** One evening, Rahim and Karim were talking face to face. Rahim's shadow fell exactly to his right. Which direction was Rahim facing?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West

**6.** A man walks 6 km west, then turns right and walks 8 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 14 km north-west (b) 10 km north-east (c) 10 km north-west (d) 10 km south-west

**7.** If south-east is called north and north-east is called west, what will west be called?
(a) North-east (b) South-east (c) South-west (d) South

**8.** A man facing west turns 45° clockwise, then 180° clockwise, then 270° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) North-west (b) South (c) West (d) South-west

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | √(3² + 4²) = 5 km. |
| 2 | d | North → East → South → (left) East. |
| 3 | a | South 10, east 5, north 10: net 5 m east. |
| 4 | c | The sun is in the east, so the shadow falls west. |
| 5 | a | Evening shadow falls east; east on his right means he faces north. |
| 6 | c | 6 west and 8 north: √(36 + 64) = 10 km, north-west. |
| 7 | b | Rotation of −135°: 270° − 135° = 135° = south-east. |
| 8 | d | 270 + 45 + 180 − 270 = 225° = south-west. |
