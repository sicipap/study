# British rule: Permanent Settlement, Titumir, Faraizi, Indigo, 1857

> **Why it matters:** Common questions: who introduced the Permanent Settlement and when (Cornwallis, 1793), Titumir's bamboo fort, founder of the Faraizi movement, "Nil Darpan", and where the 1857 revolt began.

## Land revenue systems 🔥
| Year | System | Introduced by |
|---|---|---|
| 1772 | **পাঁচসালা বন্দোবস্ত** (five-year settlement) | **Warren Hastings** |
| 1777 | **একসালা বন্দোবস্ত** (one-year settlement) | Warren Hastings |
| 1790 | **দশসালা বন্দোবস্ত** (ten-year settlement) | **Lord Cornwallis** |
| **22 March 1793** | 🔥 **চিরস্থায়ী বন্দোবস্ত (Permanent Settlement)** | **Lord Cornwallis** |

### Permanent Settlement — key points
- Zamindars became the **permanent owners** of land; the revenue they paid to the Government was **fixed forever**.
- **সূর্যাস্ত আইন (Sunset Law):** if a zamindar failed to pay revenue by sunset on the fixed day, his estate was auctioned.
- Effects: a new loyal **zamindar class** grew; peasants (রায়ত) were exploited; many old zamindar families lost their land; absentee landlordism and sub-infeudation (পত্তনি) spread.
- It was finally ended after 1947 by the **East Bengal State Acquisition and Tenancy Act, 1950 (জমিদারি প্রথা উচ্ছেদ)**.

| Period | Movement | Leader / key person |
|---|---|---|
| c. 1760–1800 | **ফকির-সন্ন্যাসী বিদ্রোহ** | **মজনু শাহ** (Fakir leader), **ভবানী পাঠক** |
| 1818 onward | **ফরায়েজি আন্দোলন** | **হাজী শরীয়তউল্লাহ**, then his son **দুদু মিয়া** |

| 1857 | **Sepoy Revolt (সিপাহি বিদ্রোহ)** | **মঙ্গল পান্ডে** and others |
| 1859–1860 | **নীল বিদ্রোহ (Indigo revolt)** | Indigo peasants; **দীনবন্ধু মিত্র** wrote "নীলদর্পণ" |

## Titumir (তিতুমীর) 🔥
- Real name **সৈয়দ মীর নিসার আলী**; born in **1782** at **Chandpur (বারাসাত), 24 Parganas**, West Bengal.
- He was influenced by **সৈয়দ আহমদ বেরেলভী**'s reform movement (তরিকা-ই-মুহাম্মদিয়া).
- He fought against oppressive **zamindars and indigo planters** (a local zamindar even imposed a tax on beards — "দাড়ির খাজনা").
- He built the famous **বাঁশের কেল্লা (bamboo fort)** at **নারকেলবাড়িয়া** in **1831**.
- He was killed fighting British troops on **19 November 1831**; the fort was destroyed by cannon.

## Faraizi movement (ফরায়েজি আন্দোলন) 🔥
- Founder: **হাজী শরীয়তউল্লাহ** (born **1781**, **শামাইল, Madaripur**). He started it after returning from Makkah, in **1818** (some sources: 1820).
- "Faraiz" = compulsory religious duties. It aimed to purify Muslim society and also stood up for peasants.
- His son **দুদু মিয়া (মুহসীনউদ্দীন আহমদ)** turned it into an organised **peasant movement** against zamindars and indigo planters. He declared **"জমির মালিক আল্লাহ"** — land belongs to God, so no one may charge rent on it.

## Indigo revolt (নীল বিদ্রোহ) 🔥
- European planters forced peasants to grow indigo on bad terms (**দাদন** advance system) and tortured those who refused.
- The great revolt of **1859–1860** spread across Bengal.
- 🔥 **"নীলদর্পণ" (1860)**, a play by **দীনবন্ধু মিত্র**, showed the planters' cruelty. It was translated into English (commonly credited to **মাইকেল মধুসূদন দত্ত**) and published by **Rev. James Long**, who was fined and jailed.
- **হরিশচন্দ্র মুখোপাধ্যায়**'s paper **"হিন্দু প্যাট্রিয়ট"** supported the peasants.
- The Government formed the **Indigo Commission (1860)**; forced indigo cultivation declined.

## Sepoy Revolt of 1857 (সিপাহি বিদ্রোহ) 🔥
- Immediate cause: the new **Enfield rifle cartridges**, rumoured to be greased with cow and pig fat. Deeper causes: **Doctrine of Lapse (স্বত্ববিলোপ নীতি)** of **Lord Dalhousie**, economic exploitation, discrimination against Indian sepoys.
- 🔥 Began at **ব্যারাকপুর**: sepoy **মঙ্গল পান্ডে** revolted on **29 March 1857** (he was hanged).
- Large-scale revolt broke out at **মিরাট on 10 May 1857**; rebels marched to Delhi and declared **বাহাদুর শাহ জাফর** emperor.
- Other leaders: **Rani Lakshmibai** (Jhansi), **Nana Sahib**, **Tantia Tope**.
- In **Chattogram**, sepoys revolted under **হাবিলদার রজব আলী**.
- In **Dhaka**, sepoys at **Lalbagh** revolted; many were hanged at **Antaghar Maidan**, now **বাহাদুর শাহ পার্ক** (earlier Victoria Park).
- **Bahadur Shah Zafar** — the **last Mughal emperor** — was exiled to **রেঙ্গুন (Yangon)**, where he died (1862).
- **Results:** **Government of India Act 1858**: Company rule ended; India came under the **British Crown**. **Lord Canning** became the **first Viceroy**.

## Quick revision
- Permanent Settlement: **22 March 1793**, **Lord Cornwallis**; Sunset Law.
- Five-year settlement **1772** (Hastings); ten-year **1790** (Cornwallis).
- Fakir leader: **Majnu Shah**.
- Titumir: **Mir Nisar Ali**; bamboo fort at **Narkelbaria**, **1831**; died 19 Nov 1831.
- Faraizi founder: **Haji Shariatullah**; "land belongs to God" — **Dudu Miyan**.
- "Nil Darpan" (1860): **Dinabandhu Mitra**.
- 1857 began at **Barrackpore** (Mangal Pandey); spread from **Meerut** (10 May).
- Last Mughal emperor **Bahadur Shah Zafar**, exiled to Rangoon.
- After 1857: Crown rule (1858); first Viceroy **Lord Canning**.
