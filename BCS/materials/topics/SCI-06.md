# Light: reflection, refraction, lens, dispersion

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the speed of light, which mirror/lens is used where, eye defects and their correction, why the sky is blue, how a rainbow forms and total internal reflection examples.

## Nature and speed of light 🔥
- Light is an **electromagnetic, transverse wave**; it has a **dual nature** (wave and particle/photon). Photon idea: Einstein's **photoelectric effect** (Nobel 1921).
- **Speed in vacuum ≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/s** (3 lakh km/s). It is slower in water (≈2.25 × 10⁸) and glass (≈2 × 10⁸).
- **Sunlight reaches Earth in ≈ 8 minutes 20 seconds (≈500 s)**. Moonlight reaches in ≈ 1.3 s.
- Visible light wavelength: about **400 nm (violet) to 700 nm (red)** (some books: 3.9 × 10⁻⁷ – 7.8 × 10⁻⁷ m).
- Light travels in a **straight line** → shadows, eclipses, **pinhole camera** (image real and inverted).

## Reflection (প্রতিফলন) 🔥
- **Laws:** (1) angle of incidence = angle of reflection; (2) incident ray, reflected ray and the normal lie in the same plane.
- **Plane mirror image:** **virtual, erect, same size, laterally inverted** (left ↔ right), as far behind as the object is in front. To see your full body you need a mirror of at least **half your height**.
- Two plane mirrors at angle θ → number of images = **(360/θ) − 1** (e.g. 90° → 3 images; parallel mirrors → infinite images).
- **Periscope** uses two plane mirrors (at 45°); **kaleidoscope** uses multiple reflection.

| Mirror | Uses |
|---|---|
| **Concave (অবতল) — converging** | **Shaving/make-up mirror**, **dentist's mirror**, **reflectors of torch, car headlight, searchlight**, solar cooker, reflecting telescope |
| **Convex (উত্তল) — diverging** | **Rear-view (side) mirror of vehicles** (gives a wide field of view; image virtual, erect, diminished), street-corner mirrors |

## Refraction (প্রতিসরণ) 🔥
- Bending of light when it passes from one medium to another because its **speed changes**.
- **Snell's law:** sin i / sin r = constant (**refractive index, μ**). **μ = speed in vacuum ÷ speed in medium**.
- Refractive index: water **1.33**, glass **1.5**, **diamond 2.42** (very high).
- Everyday effects: **a stick looks bent in water**; **a pond looks shallower** and a coin in water appears raised; **stars twinkle** (atmospheric refraction); the Sun is visible a little **before actual sunrise and after sunset**.

### Total internal reflection (পূর্ণ অভ্যন্তরীণ প্রতিফলন) 🔥
- Occurs when light goes from a **denser to a rarer medium** and the angle of incidence **exceeds the critical angle** (glass ≈ 42°, diamond ≈ 24°).
- Examples: **sparkling of diamond**, **mirage (মরীচিকা)** in deserts / hot roads, **optical fibre** (used in telecommunication and **endoscopy**), a glass crack appearing silvery. Optical fibre pioneer: **Narinder Singh Kapany** (often called the father of fibre optics).

## Lenses (লেন্স) 🔥
| Lens | Nature | Power sign | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Convex (উত্তল)** | Converging, thick in middle | **+** | **Magnifying glass**, camera, projector, microscope, telescope; corrects **long-sightedness (hypermetropia)** |
| **Concave (অবতল)** | Diverging, thin in middle | **−** | Corrects **short-sightedness (myopia)**; peep-hole of doors |

- **Power of a lens (P) = 1/f**, f in metres; unit **dioptre (D)**. A lens of f = +50 cm has P = +2 D.
- A convex lens in water loses much of its power; an air bubble in water acts as a **concave lens**.

## The human eye and its defects 🔥
- Eye lens is **convex**; image on the **retina** is **real and inverted**; the brain makes it upright.
- **Rods** work in dim light; **cones** detect **colour**. **Iris** controls pupil size. **Near point ≈ 25 cm**; far point = infinity.
- **Persistence of vision ≈ 1/16 s (≈0.1 s)** → basis of cinema.

| Defect | Problem | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| **Myopia (হ্রস্বদৃষ্টি)** | Can't see **far**; image forms in front of retina | **Concave lens** |
| **Hypermetropia (দীর্ঘদৃষ্টি)** | Can't see **near**; image behind retina | **Convex lens** |
| **Presbyopia (চালশে)** | Old age; weak accommodation | **Bifocal lens** (invented by **Benjamin Franklin**) |
| **Astigmatism** | Uneven cornea; blurred in some directions | **Cylindrical lens** |
| **Cataract (ছানি)** | Lens becomes cloudy | Surgery / artificial lens |

## Dispersion, scattering and colour 🔥
- **Dispersion (বিচ্ছুরণ):** white light splits into 7 colours through a **prism** — shown by **Isaac Newton (1666)**. Order **VIBGYOR** (বেনীআসহকলা): Violet, Indigo, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red.
- **Red**: longest wavelength, **least deviation**, least scattering. **Violet**: shortest wavelength, **most deviation**.
- **Rainbow (রংধনু)**: formed by **dispersion + refraction + total internal reflection** in raindrops; seen in the sky **opposite the Sun**; red on the outer side (primary bow).
- **Scattering (বিক্ষেপণ)** (Rayleigh; scattering ∝ 1/λ⁴): **sky is blue**; **Sun looks red at sunrise/sunset**; **danger signals are red** (least scattered, seen from far); the sky looks **black to astronauts** in space (no air to scatter light).
- **Raman effect**: C. V. Raman (Nobel Prize in Physics, **1930**) — change in wavelength of scattered light.
- **Primary colours of light: red, green, blue**. Red + green + blue = white. **Complementary pairs**: red–cyan, green–magenta, blue–yellow.
- An object looks red because it **reflects red and absorbs others**; a red rose in green light looks **black**.

## Quick revision
- Speed of light **3 × 10⁸ m/s**; sunlight takes **≈8 min 20 s**.
- Rear-view mirror = **convex**; shaving mirror & headlight reflector = **concave**.
- Myopia → **concave lens**; hypermetropia → **convex lens**; bifocal → Franklin.
- Diamond sparkles & optical fibre work by **total internal reflection**; mirage too.
- **Sky blue, sunset red**: scattering; danger signal red.
- **VIBGYOR**; red least deviated, violet most.
- Power of lens in **dioptre**, P = 1/f (m).
- Raman effect (1930 Nobel); dispersion by Newton.
