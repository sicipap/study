# Local government structure

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the lowest tier (Union Parishad), its members (chairman + 12), the constitutional basis (Art. 59–60), the laws of 2009, the upazila system (1982), the father of local self-government (Lord Ripon) and the number of city corporations.

## Constitutional basis 🔥
- **Article 11**: democracy, with effective participation of the people through elected representatives in administration at all levels.
- 🔥 **Article 59**: local government in every administrative unit shall be entrusted to bodies of **elected persons**.
- **Article 60**: Parliament shall give these bodies powers, including the power to **impose taxes**, prepare budgets and keep funds.
- Ministry: **Local Government Division**, under the **Ministry of Local Government, Rural Development and Co-operatives (LGRD)**.

## Rural local government 🔥
### Union Parishad (ইউনিয়ন পরিষদ) — lowest tier 🔥🔥
- The **oldest** and **lowest unit** of local government.
- Law: **Local Government (Union Parishad) Act, 2009**.
- 🔥 Members: **1 Chairman + 9 general ward members + 3 reserved women members = 13**.
- Each union has **9 wards**; each reserved woman member represents **3 wards**.
- Term: **5 years**. The **Secretary (সচিব)** is the government employee who runs the office.
- Runs the **village court (গ্রাম আদালত)**, keeps **birth and death registration**, and handles local development and relief.

### Upazila Parishad (উপজেলা পরিষদ)
- Upazila system started in **1982** (Ershad); first upazila elections **1985**. Abolished in **1991**; revived by the **Upazila Parishad Act 1998** and elections in **2009**.
- 🔥 Directly elected: **1 Chairman + 2 Vice-Chairmen** (one of whom must be a **woman**).
- Union Parishad chairmen and pourashava mayors of the upazila are also members.
- The **Upazila Nirbahi Officer (UNO)** is the chief executive/secretary of the Parishad.
- Under the 2009 law, the local **MP** is the **adviser** of the Upazila Parishad.

### Zila Parishad (জেলা পরিষদ)
- Law: **Zila Parishad Act, 2000**. The first Zila Parishad elections were held in **December 2016**.
- **Indirectly elected**: chairman and members are elected by the **local government representatives** of the district (UP, upazila and pourashava members).
- Structure: 1 Chairman, **15 general members**, **5 reserved women members**.
- The **three hill districts** have separate **Hill District Councils** (1989) instead.

## Urban local government 🔥
### Pourashava (পৌরসভা, municipality)
- Law: **Local Government (Pourashava) Act, 2009**.
- Headed by an elected **Mayor**, with ward **councillors** and reserved women councillors.
- Classified as **A, B, C** mainly by annual income.
- Dhaka's municipality dates from **1864**.

### City Corporation (সিটি কর্পোরেশন)
- Law: **Local Government (City Corporation) Act, 2009**. Headed by an elected **Mayor**.
- 🔥 **12 city corporations**: **Dhaka North, Dhaka South**, Chattogram, Khulna, Rajshahi, Sylhet, Barishal, Rangpur, Cumilla, Narayanganj, Gazipur, Mymensingh.
- 🔥 Dhaka City Corporation was **split into North and South in 2011**.

## Special system: Chattogram Hill Tracts
| Body | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Rangamati, Khagrachhari, Bandarban Hill District Councils** | Hill District Local Government Council Acts, **1989** |
| **CHT Regional Council (পার্বত্য চট্টগ্রাম আঞ্চলিক পরিষদ)** | **CHT Regional Council Act, 1998**, after the **CHT Accord (1997)** |

## Summary table
| Tier | Head | Law | Election |
|---|---|---|---|
| Union Parishad | Chairman | UP Act 2009 | Direct |
| Upazila Parishad | Chairman (+2 Vice-Chairmen) | Upazila Parishad Act 1998 (amended 2009) | Direct |
| Zila Parishad | Chairman | Zila Parishad Act 2000 | Indirect |
| Pourashava | Mayor | Pourashava Act 2009 | Direct |
| City Corporation | Mayor | City Corporation Act 2009 | Direct |

> 🔄 Update: after August 2024 many elected local government heads (mayors, chairmen) were removed and **administrators** were appointed, and a Local Government Reform Commission proposed changes. The number of unions, upazilas and pourashavas also changes over time (about 495 upazilas and over 4,500 unions as of the early 2020s). Check current figures and the present structure.

## Quick revision
- Constitutional basis: **Art. 59–60**.
- Father of local self-government: **Lord Ripon** (1882).
- Lowest tier: **Union Parishad**; **13 members** (1 + 9 + 3); **9 wards**.
- Upazila system: **1982**; first elections **1985**; Parishad has **1 Chairman + 2 Vice-Chairmen**.
- UNO = chief executive of the Upazila Parishad.
- Zila Parishad: **indirect** election; first election **2016**.
- **12 city corporations**; Dhaka split in **2011**.
- Hill District Councils **1989**; CHT Regional Council **1998**.

**8.** How many wards are there in a Union Parishad?
(a) 3 (b) 12 (c) 5 (d) 9

| Q | Ans | Explanation |

| 1 | d | The Union Parishad is the lowest and oldest tier |
| 2 | a | 1 chairman + 9 ward members + 3 reserved women = 13 |
| 3 | b | Lord Ripon's resolution of 1882 |
| 4 | c | Articles 59 and 60 |
| 5 | d | 12, including Dhaka North and Dhaka South |
