# International current affairs (last 12 months)

> **Why it matters:** Every preliminary exam has several questions on events of the past year: new members of organizations, summits and their hosts, new leaders, treaties coming into force, and big conflicts. This note lists events from **late 2025 to 2026** that this note can confirm, then gives a **checklist** of what to look up in newspapers right now.

> 🔄 Update: check the latest figure in a newspaper or the Bangladesh Economic Review. This note covers events confirmed up to early 2026; read a monthly current-affairs digest for the months since.

## Late 2025 🔥
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 17 September 2025 | 🔥 **Pakistan and Saudi Arabia** signed a **Strategic Mutual Defence Agreement** (an attack on one is an attack on both) |
| 21–22 September 2025 | 🔥 The **UK, Canada, Australia, Portugal** and then **France** (and several others) **recognised the State of Palestine** at the UN General Assembly |
| 28 September 2025 | UN sanctions on **Iran** reimposed through the "**snapback**" mechanism of the 2015 nuclear deal |
| September 2025 | **80th UN General Assembly** opened; its President is **Annalena Baerbock** (Germany). Nepal's **Gen Z protests** brought down the government; **Sushila Karki** became Nepal's **first woman (interim) PM** |
| 6–13 October 2025 | **Nobel Prizes 2025** announced; 🔥 **Peace: María Corina Machado** (Venezuela); **Literature: László Krasznahorkai** (Hungary) (see INT-25) |
| 🔥 10–13 October 2025 | **Gaza ceasefire** under US President Trump's **20-point plan** took effect (10 October); hostages and prisoners released; **Sharm el-Sheikh peace summit** (Egypt, 13 October) |
| 🔥 21 October 2025 | **Sanae Takaichi** became **Japan's first woman Prime Minister** |
| 24 October 2025 | **UN turned 80** |
| 🔥 26 October 2025 | **Timor-Leste** became the **11th member of ASEAN** (47th ASEAN Summit, **Kuala Lumpur**); **Thailand and Cambodia** signed a peace declaration there after their July 2025 border clashes |
| October 2025 | **Afghanistan–Pakistan** border clashes; ceasefire talks mediated by **Qatar and Türkiye** |
| 🔥 10–21 November 2025 | **COP30** held in **Belém, Brazil** (in the Amazon), 10 years after the Paris Agreement |
| November 2025 | **Khaled El-Enany** (Egypt) took office as **UNESCO Director-General** |
| 17 November 2025 | **UNSC Resolution 2803** endorsed the Gaza plan's "**Board of Peace**" and an **International Stabilization Force** |
| 🔥 22–23 November 2025 | **G20 summit in Johannesburg** — the **first G20 summit in Africa** (the USA stayed away) |
| December 2025 | Myanmar's military began **phased elections** (widely rejected as not free) |
