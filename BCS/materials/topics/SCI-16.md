# Animal classification

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask scientific names (tiger, hilsa, human, mosquitoes), which phylum an animal belongs to, "which of these is not a fish/insect/mammal", heart chambers of vertebrate classes and the five-kingdom system.

## Classification basics 🔥
- **Aristotle** is called the **father of biology and zoology**. **Carl Linnaeus** (Sweden) introduced **binomial nomenclature (দ্বিপদ নামকরণ)** — *Systema Naturae* (1758 edition for animals) — and is the **father of taxonomy**.
- A scientific name has **two parts: genus + species** (genus capitalised, species small, written in italics), e.g. ***Homo sapiens***.
- **Taxonomic hierarchy**: **Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species**. **Species** is the basic unit.
- **Five-kingdom system: R. H. Whittaker (1969)** — **Monera** (bacteria, cyanobacteria), **Protista** (Amoeba, Paramecium, Plasmodium, Euglena), **Fungi**, **Plantae**, **Animalia**.

## Major animal phyla (invertebrates) 🔥
| Phylum | Key features | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Porifera** | Body full of **pores**; simplest multicellular animals | **Sponges** |
| **Cnidaria (Coelenterata)** | Stinging cells (cnidocytes), one opening | **Hydra, jellyfish, sea anemone, coral** |
| **Platyhelminthes** | **Flatworms**; mostly parasites | **Tapeworm**, liver fluke, planaria |
| **Nematoda** | **Roundworms**; unsegmented | **Ascaris (roundworm), hookworm, filarial worm**, pinworm |
| **Annelida** | **Segmented worms** | **Earthworm** (farmer's friend; breathes through **skin**), **leech** (secretes anticoagulant **hirudin**) |
| **Arthropoda** | **Jointed legs**, chitin exoskeleton; **largest phylum** | Insects, **spiders**, scorpion, **crab, prawn**, centipede |
| **Mollusca** | **Soft body**, usually a shell | **Snail, oyster** (pearl), mussel, **octopus, squid, cuttlefish** |
| **Echinodermata** | **Spiny skin**, water-vascular system, marine only | **Starfish**, sea urchin, sea cucumber |
| **Chordata** | **Notochord**, dorsal nerve cord | Fish, frogs, reptiles, birds, mammals |

- **Insects**: **6 legs, 3 body parts** (head, thorax, abdomen), 1 pair of antennae. **Spiders and scorpions have 8 legs** — they are **arachnids, not insects**.
- **Cockroach** has a 13-chambered heart; its blood is colourless (no haemoglobin).
- **Octopus has 3 hearts**. **Hydra** can regenerate.
- **Coral**: **St. Martin's Island** is Bangladesh's **only coral island**.

## Vertebrate classes (মেরুদণ্ডী প্রাণী) 🔥🔥
| Class | Key features | Heart chambers | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pisces (fish)** | **Gills**, fins, scales; cold-blooded | **2** | Hilsa, rohu, shark, **seahorse** |
| **Amphibia** | Live on land and water; moist skin; larva (**tadpole**) has gills; adult breathes by lungs and **skin**; cold-blooded | **3** | Frog, toad, salamander |
| **Reptilia** | Dry scaly skin, lay shelled eggs; cold-blooded | **3** (**crocodile has 4**) | Snake, lizard, turtle, crocodile |
| **Aves (birds)** | **Feathers**, beak, **hollow bones**, lay eggs; **warm-blooded** | **4** | Magpie robin, pigeon, penguin |
| **Mammalia** | **Hair**, **mammary glands**, external ear, diaphragm; mostly give birth; warm-blooded | **4** | Human, cow, **whale, bat** |

- **Cold-blooded (poikilothermic, শীতল রক্ত)**: fish, amphibians, reptiles. **Warm-blooded (homeothermic)**: **birds and mammals**.

## Record animals
- **Largest animal: blue whale**. **Largest land animal: African elephant**. **Largest bird: ostrich**; **smallest bird: hummingbird**. **Fastest land animal: cheetah**. Largest reptile: saltwater crocodile.

## Quick revision
- Linnaeus: **binomial nomenclature**; Whittaker: **five kingdoms (1969)**.
- **Homo sapiens**; tiger *Panthera tigris*; hilsa *Tenualosa ilisha*; doyel *Copsychus saularis*.
- Mango *Mangifera indica*; rice *Oryza sativa*; jute *Corchorus*.
- **Anopheles → malaria; Aedes → dengue; Culex → filaria.**
- **Arthropoda = largest phylum**; insects 6 legs, spiders 8.
- Heart: fish 2, amphibian 3, reptile 3 (crocodile 4), birds & mammals 4.
- **Prawn is an arthropod; whale and bat are mammals; seahorse is a fish.**
- Earthworm breathes through skin; leech makes hirudin.
