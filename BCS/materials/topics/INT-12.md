> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for HQs (Jeddah, Vienna, Riyadh, London), founding years, member counts, the newest members (BRICS expansion, AU in G20), and Bangladesh's links (joined OIC in 1974 and the Commonwealth in 1972).

## OIC (Organisation of Islamic Cooperation / ইসলামি সহযোগিতা সংস্থা) 🔥
- Founded at the **Rabat (Morocco) summit, 25 September 1969**, after the **arson attack on Al-Aqsa Mosque** (21 August 1969).
- 🔥 Renamed from "Organisation of the **Islamic Conference**" to "Organisation of **Islamic Cooperation**" in **2011**.
- 🔥 **HQ: Jeddah** (temporary; the Charter names **Al-Quds/Jerusalem** as the permanent seat).
- 🔥 **57 members** — the **second-largest intergovernmental organization after the UN**. Members from South America: **Guyana, Suriname**.
- 🔥 **Bangladesh joined at the 2nd summit, Lahore, February 1974**, attended by **Bangabandhu**.
- Bangladesh hosted the **45th Council of Foreign Ministers**, Dhaka, **2018**.
- Linked body: **Islamic Development Bank (Jeddah)**.

## OPEC (Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries / ওপেক) 🔥
- 🔥 Founded at the **Baghdad Conference, 14 September 1960** by **5 countries: Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Venezuela**.
- 🔥 **HQ: Vienna, Austria** (moved from Geneva in 1965) — HQ is in a **non-member** country.
- **12 members (as of 2025):** Algeria, Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Libya, Nigeria, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Venezuela.
- Left: **Qatar (2019)**, **Ecuador (2020)**, **Angola (2024)**; Indonesia suspended membership (2016).
- **OPEC+** (from 2016): OPEC plus about 10 other producers led by **Russia**.
- The **1973 oil embargo** by Arab producers caused the first oil shock.

## GCC (Gulf Cooperation Council / উপসাগরীয় সহযোগিতা পরিষদ) 🔥
- Founded **25 May 1981, Abu Dhabi**; 🔥 **HQ: Riyadh**.
- 🔥 **6 members: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman.**
- **Qatar blockade** by Saudi Arabia, UAE, Bahrain and Egypt: **2017 – January 2021** (ended by the **Al-Ula Declaration**).
- Joint force: **Peninsula Shield Force**.
- Millions of Bangladeshi workers live in the GCC; **Saudi Arabia** and the **UAE** are top remittance sources.

## Commonwealth (কমনওয়েলথ) 🔥
- Roots: **Balfour Declaration (1926)** and **Statute of Westminster (1931)**. 🔥 The modern Commonwealth began with the **London Declaration (1949)**.
- 🔥 **HQ (Secretariat): Marlborough House, London.**
- **56 members.** Head: **King Charles III** (since 2022; the head is chosen, not hereditary by law).
- 🔥 **Bangladesh joined on 18 April 1972.** Pakistan left in 1972 in protest and rejoined in 1989.
- Non-British-colony members: **Mozambique (1995)**, **Rwanda (2009)**, **Gabon and Togo (2022)**.
- **CHOGM** (Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting): every two years — **2024 Apia, Samoa**; next **2026, Antigua and Barbuda**.
- **Commonwealth Day:** second Monday of March. Also the **Commonwealth Games** (see INT-26).

## G7 (Group of Seven)
- Began as **G6 at Rambouillet, France, 1975** (France, West Germany, Italy, Japan, UK, USA); **Canada** joined **1976** → G7.
- 🔥 **Members: USA, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Canada** (+ the **EU** takes part).
- **Russia** joined in **1997** (G8) and was **suspended in 2014** after annexing **Crimea**.
- Summits: **2025 Kananaskis, Canada**; **2026 France (Évian)**.

## G20 (Group of Twenty) 🔥
- Founded **1999** as a forum of finance ministers after the **Asian financial crisis**; first **leaders' summit 2008, Washington** (global financial crisis).
- 🔥 **Members:** Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Germany, India, Indonesia, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Russia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, South Korea, Türkiye, UK, USA + **EU** + 🔥 **African Union (joined 2023)**.
- Covers about **85% of world GDP** and **two-thirds of world population**.
- Summits: **2023 New Delhi**; **2024 Rio de Janeiro**; 🔥 **2025 Johannesburg** — the **first in Africa**; **2026 USA**.
- 🔥 **Bangladesh** was invited as a **guest country** at the **New Delhi summit (2023)**.

## BRICS (ব্রিকস) 🔥
- 🔥 The term **"BRIC"** was coined by **Jim O'Neill** of **Goldman Sachs** (**2001**).
- **First summit: Yekaterinburg, Russia, 2009** (Brazil, Russia, India, China). **South Africa** joined in **2010** → BRICS.
- 🔥 **Expansion (1 January 2024): Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE.** (**Saudi Arabia** was invited but has not formally confirmed.)
- 🔥 **Indonesia** joined in **January 2025** → **10 members**.
- A "**partner country**" category was created at the **Kazan summit (2024)**.
- Summits: **2024 Kazan (Russia)**, **2025 Rio de Janeiro**, **2026 India** (chair).
- Its bank: **New Development Bank, Shanghai** (see INT-10).
- **Bangladesh** applied for membership in **2023** but was not in the first expansion; it is a member of the **NDB**.

## Quick revision
- OIC: **1969 Rabat**; HQ **Jeddah**; **57** members; Bangladesh joined **1974 (Lahore)**.
- OPEC: **1960 Baghdad**; HQ **Vienna**; 5 founders; **12** members (2025).
- GCC: **1981**; HQ **Riyadh**; **6** members.
- Commonwealth: **1949**; HQ **London**; **56** members; Bangladesh joined **18 April 1972**.
- G7 began **1975**; Russia suspended **2014**.
- G20: **19 + EU + AU (2023)**; 2025 summit in **Johannesburg**, first in Africa.
- BRICS: term by **Jim O'Neill**; 2024 new members **Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE**; **Indonesia 2025**.
