# Carbon compounds, polymers, fuels

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask allotropes of carbon, main component of natural gas/LPG/biogas, which gas ripens fruit, natural vs synthetic polymers (nylon, PVC, Teflon, Bakelite), coal types, petroleum fractions and octane number.

## Carbon and its allotropes (কার্বনের রূপভেদ) 🔥
- Carbon forms long chains and rings with itself (**catenation**), so it has millions of compounds. **Organic chemistry = chemistry of carbon compounds**.
- **Friedrich Wöhler (1828)** made **urea** in the laboratory (from ammonium cyanate), ending the "vital force" theory; he is called the father of organic chemistry.

| Allotrope | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Diamond** | **Hardest natural substance**; each C bonded to 4 others (tetrahedral); **bad conductor**; cuts glass |
| **Graphite** | Soft, slippery layers; **conducts electricity**; pencil "lead", lubricant, electrodes, moderator in reactors |
| **Fullerene (C₆₀, buckyball)** | Discovered **1985** (Kroto, Curl, Smalley — Nobel 1996) |
| **Graphene** | Single layer of graphite; isolated **2004** by **Geim & Novoselov** (Nobel Physics 2010) |
| **Carbon nanotube** | Reported by **Sumio Iijima (1991)** |
| Amorphous forms | Coal, charcoal, coke, **lampblack/soot**, animal charcoal (bone black; used to decolourise sugar) |

## Hydrocarbons and important compounds 🔥
| Compound | Formula | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Methane** | **CH₄** | **Marsh gas**; main component of **natural gas** (≈ 95%+ in Bangladesh gas), **biogas** (≈ 55–70%) and **CNG** |
| Ethane | C₂H₆ | Alkane |
| **Propane + butane** | C₃H₈, C₄H₁₀ | **LPG (liquefied petroleum gas)**; a smelly compound (**ethyl mercaptan**) is added to detect leaks |
| **Ethylene (ethene)** | **C₂H₄** | **Natural fruit-ripening hormone/gas**; makes polythene |
| **Acetylene (ethyne)** | **C₂H₂** | **Oxy-acetylene welding**; produced from **calcium carbide (CaC₂)**, which is **illegally used to ripen fruit** (harmful) |
| **Methanol** | CH₃OH | **Wood spirit**; **poisonous** — can cause **blindness** and death |
| **Ethanol** | C₂H₅OH | Drinking alcohol; made by **fermentation** of molasses/sugar by **yeast**; rectified spirit ≈ 95%, absolute alcohol 100%; **methylated/denatured spirit** = ethanol + methanol |
| **Formaldehyde** | HCHO | **Formalin = 37–40% solution**; preserves biological specimens; **illegal in food** |
| **Benzene** | C₆H₆ | Ring structure proposed by **Kekulé** |
| Chloroform | CHCl₃ | Early anaesthetic |
| Glycerol | C₃H₈O₃ | From fats; used in cosmetics, explosives (nitroglycerine) |

## Polymers (পলিমার) 🔥🔥
A polymer is a giant molecule made of repeating units (**monomers**).

| Natural polymers | Monomer |
|---|---|
| **Starch, cellulose, glycogen** | **Glucose** |
| **Protein** (silk, wool, hair) | **Amino acids** |
| **Natural rubber** | **Isoprene** |
| DNA / RNA | Nucleotides |

| Synthetic polymer | Monomer / fact | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Polythene (polyethylene)** | **Ethylene** | Bags, bottles |
| **PVC (polyvinyl chloride)** | **Vinyl chloride** | **Pipes**, raincoats, wire insulation |
| **Teflon (PTFE)** | Tetrafluoroethylene | **Non-stick cookware** |
| **Nylon** | **First fully synthetic fibre**, **Wallace Carothers (DuPont), 1935** | Ropes, parachutes, fishing nets |
| **Polyester (terylene/dacron)** | — | Clothing |
| **Bakelite** | **First synthetic plastic, Leo Baekeland (1907)**; **thermosetting** | Electrical switches, handles |
| Melamine | Thermosetting | Crockery |
| Polystyrene | Styrene | Packaging foam (thermocol) |
| Acrylic (PMMA / Perspex) | — | Sheets, "artificial wool" (acrylic fibre) |
| Rayon | **Artificial silk**, made from cellulose (semi-synthetic) | Textiles |

- **Thermoplastics** soften on heating and can be remoulded (polythene, PVC). **Thermosetting plastics** set permanently (Bakelite, melamine).
- **Vulcanisation**: heating rubber with **sulphur** to make it hard and elastic — **Charles Goodyear (1839)**.
- **Bangladesh banned polythene shopping bags in 2002** (among the first countries). **"Sonali bag"**: a biodegradable jute-cellulose bag developed by scientist **Dr. Mubarak Ahmad Khan**.

## Fuels (জ্বালানি) 🔥
- **Fossil fuels**: **coal, petroleum, natural gas** — formed from buried plants/animals over millions of years; non-renewable.

| Coal type | Carbon content |
|---|---|
| **Peat** | Lowest (first stage) |
| **Lignite (brown coal)** | Low |
| **Bituminous** | Medium–high (most common; Barapukuria coal is bituminous) |
| **Anthracite** | **Highest (≈ 90%+)**, best quality |

**Petroleum (crude oil, খনিজ তেল)** is separated by **fractional distillation**. Fractions from top (low boiling point) to bottom: **refinery gas/LPG → petrol (gasoline) → kerosene (jet fuel) → diesel → lubricating oil → paraffin wax → bitumen (asphalt, for roads)**.

- **Octane number** measures **petrol quality** (resistance to **knocking**); **cetane number** measures diesel quality. **Tetraethyl lead** was an anti-knock agent (now banned due to lead pollution).
- **Calorific value**: heat per unit mass. **Hydrogen has the highest calorific value** (≈ 150 kJ/g) and burns clean (product: water).
- Gas mixtures: **water gas = CO + H₂**; **producer gas = CO + N₂**; **coal gas** = H₂ + CH₄ + CO.
- **Natural gas in Bangladesh**: first discovered at **Haripur, Sylhet (1955)**. It is the **main commercial energy source** and used for power, fertilizer (urea), CNG and cooking.
- **Carbon monoxide (CO)** from incomplete burning is **poisonous**: it binds **haemoglobin** more strongly than oxygen.

## Fire safety
- Fire needs **fuel, heat and oxygen** (fire triangle).
- **CO₂ extinguishers** cut off oxygen. **Do not use water on oil or electrical fires** — use **foam, CO₂ or dry powder**.

## Quick revision
- **Diamond hardest, non-conductor; graphite conducts**; fullerene C₆₀; graphene 2004.
- Natural gas, biogas, CNG: mainly **methane (CH₄)**; LPG: **propane + butane**.
- **Ethylene ripens fruit**; **calcium carbide** (acetylene) illegally used for ripening.
- Methanol poisonous (blindness); ethanol by yeast fermentation; formalin = 37–40% HCHO.
- **Nylon: first synthetic fibre (Carothers)**; **Bakelite: first synthetic plastic**; **Teflon: non-stick**; PVC: pipes.
- **Vulcanisation = rubber + sulphur** (Goodyear).
- Coal: **anthracite best**, peat lowest; octane number = petrol quality.
- Wöhler synthesised **urea (1828)**.

(a) Ethylene (b) Methane (c) Acetylene (d) Carbon dioxide

**3.** Which of the following is a natural polymer?
(a) Nylon (b) PVC (c) Teflon (d) Cellulose

**4.** Teflon is used mainly for —
(a) pipes (b) non-stick cookware (c) electrical switches (d) fishing nets

**5.** The best quality coal with the highest carbon content is —
(a) peat (b) lignite (c) bituminous (d) anthracite

**6.** Vulcanisation of rubber is done by heating it with —
(a) sulphur (b) carbon (c) chlorine (d) phosphorus

**7.** LPG is mainly a mixture of —
(a) methane and ethane (b) propane and butane (c) carbon monoxide and hydrogen (d) hydrogen and oxygen

**8.** Who first synthesised an organic compound (urea) in the laboratory?
(a) Kekulé (b) Lavoisier (c) Friedrich Wöhler (d) Berzelius

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | Natural gas is mostly methane (CH₄) |
| 2 | a | Ethylene (C₂H₄) is the ripening hormone |
| 3 | d | Cellulose is a natural glucose polymer |
| 4 | b | PTFE (Teflon) is non-stick |
| 5 | d | Anthracite has about 90%+ carbon |
| 6 | a | Goodyear heated rubber with sulphur |
| 7 | b | LPG = propane + butane |
| 8 | c | Wöhler made urea in 1828 |
