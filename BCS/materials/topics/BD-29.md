# Economy: GDP, per capita, budget, Economic Review

> **Why it matters:** Every exam has 2–4 questions on the latest budget size, GDP growth, per capita income, the Economic Review and budget "firsts". The numbers change every year, so learn the pattern and update the figures.

## Basics 🔥
- **Fiscal year (অর্থবছর):** **1 July – 30 June**.
- **Bangladesh Economic Review (বাংলাদেশ অর্থনৈতিক সমীক্ষা):** published by the **Finance Division, Ministry of Finance**, every year **just before the budget** (usually early June). It reviews the outgoing year. Sources of its data: **BBS**, Bangladesh Bank, NBR, EPB.
- **GDP and per-capita figures** come from the **Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS)**, established **1974**. Current **base year: 2015–16** (changed from 2005–06 in 2021).
- **Constitution:** the budget is the **"Annual Financial Statement" (বার্ষিক আর্থিক বিবৃতি)** under **Article 87**; money bills under **Article 81**.
- **Bangladesh Bank** (central bank): established **16 December 1971** (Bangladesh Bank Order, 1972, with effect from that date). Currency **Taka** introduced **4 March 1972**.
- **NBR (জাতীয় রাজস্ব বোর্ড)**: 1972. Largest source of tax revenue: **VAT (মূল্য সংযোজন কর)**.

## Income status 🔥
- **Lower-middle-income country** (World Bank) from **1 July 2015**.
- **LDC graduation:** UN CDP recommended it in **February 2021**; UN General Assembly adopted it in **November 2021**; graduation is scheduled for **24 November 2026**. Bangladesh met all three criteria — **per capita GNI, Human Assets Index (HAI), Economic Vulnerability Index (EVI)** — in 2018 and again in 2021.

## Budget FY2025–26 🔥🔥
- Size: **Tk 7,90,000 crore** — the **first budget smaller than the previous year's** (FY2024–25: Tk 7,97,000 crore).
- Presented on **2 June 2025** by **Finance Adviser Dr Salehuddin Ahmed** of the interim government, **through television** (no parliament was sitting), and passed by **ordinance**.
- **ADP (Annual Development Programme):** **Tk 2,30,000 crore**.
- Total revenue target: **Tk 5,64,000 crore** (NBR share **Tk 4,99,000 crore**).
- Deficit: about **Tk 2,26,000 crore** (about 3.6% of GDP).

> 🔄 Update: budget FY2026–27 was due in June 2026 under the new elected government. Note its size, the finance minister's name, ADP size, revenue target and deficit.

## Budget history and "firsts" 🔥
| Fact | Answer |
|---|---|
| **First budget** of Bangladesh | **FY1972–73**, presented **30 June 1972** by **Tajuddin Ahmad**; size **Tk 786 crore** |
| Most budgets presented | **M. Saifur Rahman** and **A. M. A. Muhith** — **12 each** |
| First budget above Tk 1 lakh crore | **FY2009–10** (AMA Muhith) |
| Budget placed without parliament | Under military/caretaker/interim governments, e.g. **2007–08, 2008–09** and **2025–26** |

## Planning
- **Planning Commission**: 1972; its **General Economics Division (GED)** prepares plans.
- **First Five-Year Plan:** **1973–78**. **8th Five-Year Plan:** **July 2020 – June 2025**.
- **Perspective Plan 2021–2041**: long-term vision.
- **Delta Plan 2100**: long-term water/climate plan (see BD-45).

## Quick revision
- Fiscal year **July–June**; Economic Review by **Finance Division**.
- GDP base year **2015–16**.
- Budget = Annual Financial Statement, **Article 87**.
- Lower-middle income **2015**; LDC graduation **24 November 2026**.
- FY2025–26 budget **Tk 7.90 lakh crore**, first smaller than the previous one; ADP **Tk 2.30 lakh crore**.
- First budget **Tk 786 crore** by **Tajuddin Ahmad** (30 June 1972).
- Most budgets: **Saifur Rahman & Muhith (12 each)**.
- Per capita income FY25 (provisional) **US$ 2,820**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Who publishes the Bangladesh Economic Review?
(a) Bangladesh Bank (b) Finance Division, Ministry of Finance (c) Planning Commission (d) BBS

**2.** Under which Article of the Constitution is the budget placed as the "Annual Financial Statement"?
(a) Article 81 (b) Article 7 (c) Article 93 (d) Article 87

**3.** What was the size of the first budget of Bangladesh?
(a) Tk 786 crore (b) Tk 500 crore (c) Tk 1,000 crore (d) Tk 686 crore

**4.** What is the current base year for Bangladesh's GDP calculation?
(a) 1995–96 (b) 2005–06 (c) 2015–16 (d) 2020–21

**5.** What was the size of the FY2025–26 budget?
(a) Tk 7,97,000 crore (b) Tk 8,48,000 crore (c) Tk 7,61,785 crore (d) Tk 7,90,000 crore

**6.** Bangladesh's graduation from LDC status is scheduled for —
(a) 24 November 2026 (b) 1 July 2026 (c) 24 November 2024 (d) 1 January 2027

**7.** Which is the largest source of tax revenue in Bangladesh?
(a) Income tax (b) Import duty (c) VAT (d) Land tax

**8.** Which two finance ministers presented the most budgets (12 each)?
(a) Tajuddin Ahmad and Kibria (b) Saifur Rahman and AMA Muhith (c) AMA Muhith and Mustafa Kamal (d) Saifur Rahman and Kibria

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | The Finance Division publishes it before the budget. |
| 2 | d | Article 87: Annual Financial Statement. |
| 3 | a | Tajuddin Ahmad's FY1972–73 budget was Tk 786 crore. |
| 4 | c | BBS rebased GDP to 2015–16 in 2021. |
| 5 | d | Tk 7.90 lakh crore, presented 2 June 2025. |
| 6 | a | UNGA resolution of November 2021 set 24 November 2026. |
| 7 | c | VAT is the largest source of NBR revenue. |
