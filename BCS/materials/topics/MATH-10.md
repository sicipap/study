# Partnership (অংশীদারি কারবার)

> **Why it matters:** Partnership questions are short ratio problems that appear in bank, primary-teacher and 9th–20th grade exams: sharing profit by capital, by capital × time, and working-partner salary.

## Key concepts
- **Partnership (অংশীদারি কারবার):** two or more people invest money (মূলধন, capital) in a business and share the profit or loss.
- **Simple partnership (সরল অংশীদারি):** all partners invest for the **same time**. Profit is shared in the ratio of **capitals**.
- **Compound partnership (মিশ্র অংশীদারি):** partners invest for **different times**. Profit is shared in the ratio of **capital × time** (also called "monthly capital" or effective capital).
- **Working / sleeping partner:** a working partner (কর্মরত অংশীদার) may first get a salary or a percentage of the profit; the rest is shared by capital.
- Loss is shared in the same ratio as profit.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Situation | Profit ratio |
|---|---|
| Same time, capitals C₁, C₂, C₃ | C₁ : C₂ : C₃ |
| 🔥 Different times t₁, t₂ | **C₁t₁ : C₂t₂** |
| Capital changed during the year | Add each part: C₁ × months + C₂ × months |
| Partner joins after m months (year = 12 months) | Capital × (12 − m) |
| Capitals in ratio a : b, times in ratio x : y | ax : by |
| Share of partner | Total profit × (his part ÷ sum of parts) |
| Manager gets k% first | Remaining (100 − k)% is shared by capital |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** A invests Tk 5,000 and B Tk 7,000 for one year. The profit is Tk 3,600. Find A's share.
Steps: Ratio 5 : 7 (12 parts). One part = 300. A = 5 × 300 = 1,500.
**Answer: Tk 1,500**

**Example 2.** A starts a business with Tk 3,000. B joins after 4 months with Tk 4,000. The year's profit is Tk 3,400. Find each share.
Steps: A = 3000 × 12 = 36,000; B = 4000 × 8 = 32,000 → 9 : 8 (17 parts). One part = 200 → A 1,800, B 1,600.
**Answer: A Tk 1,800, B Tk 1,600**

**Example 3.** A, B and C invest in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. The profit is Tk 12,000. What does C get?
Steps: 5/10 × 12000 = 6,000.
**Answer: Tk 6,000**

**Example 4.** A invests Tk 8,000 and withdraws half after 6 months. B invests Tk 6,000 for the whole year. What is the profit ratio?
Steps: A = 8000 × 6 + 4000 × 6 = 72,000; B = 6000 × 12 = 72,000.
**Answer: 1 : 1**

**Example 5.** A is a working partner and gets 10% of the Tk 10,000 profit as salary. The rest is shared equally with B. What does A get in total?
Steps: Salary 1,000. Remaining 9,000 → 4,500 each. A = 1,000 + 4,500 = 5,500.
**Answer: Tk 5,500**

## Common traps
- Joining "after 4 months" means the money works for **8** months, not 4.
- If the capital changes in the middle of the year, calculate each part separately.
- Pay the working partner's salary **before** dividing the rest.
- If shares are given as 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4, convert to 6 : 4 : 3 first.

## Quick revision
- Same time ⇒ ratio of capitals.
- Different times ⇒ ratio of capital × time.
- Joins after m months ⇒ time = 12 − m.
- Capital ratio a : b, time ratio x : y ⇒ profit ax : by.
- Salary first, then share the rest.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** A and B invest Tk 20,000 and Tk 30,000 for a year. Of a profit of Tk 10,000, B gets:
(a) Tk 4,000 (b) Tk 6,000 (c) Tk 5,000 (d) Tk 7,500

**2.** A invests Tk 5,000 for 12 months and B Tk 6,000 for 10 months. From a profit of Tk 8,000, A gets:
(a) Tk 4,000 (b) Tk 3,636 (c) Tk 4,364 (d) Tk 4,800

**3.** A starts with Tk 45,000. B joins 4 months later with Tk 60,000. Of a year-end profit of Tk 17,000, B gets:
(a) Tk 9,000 (b) Tk 8,500 (c) Tk 8,000 (d) Tk 9,714

**4.** A, B and C invest in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. From a profit of Tk 24,000, A gets:
(a) Tk 8,000 (b) Tk 10,000 (c) Tk 7,200 (d) Tk 6,000

**5.** A, B and C invest in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4. From a profit of Tk 6,500, C gets:
(a) Tk 2,000 (b) Tk 1,500 (c) Tk 3,000 (d) Tk 2,167

**6.** A's capital is 3 times B's, but B's money stays in the business twice as long as A's. Of a profit of Tk 10,000, A gets:
(a) Tk 6,000 (b) Tk 7,500 (c) Tk 4,000 (d) Tk 5,000

**7.** A and B invest for the same time and share profit in the ratio 2 : 3. If A's capital is Tk 8,000, B's capital is:
(a) Tk 10,000 (b) Tk 16,000 (c) Tk 12,000 (d) Tk 5,333

**8.** A, a working partner, gets 10% of a Tk 10,000 profit as salary, and the rest is shared equally with B. A's total is:
(a) Tk 5,000 (b) Tk 6,000 (c) Tk 4,500 (d) Tk 5,500

**9.** A starts with Tk 6,000. B joins later with Tk 8,000. At the year's end profit is shared 3 : 2. After how many months did B join?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 3

**10.** The capitals of A and B are in the ratio 5 : 7 and their times in the ratio 4 : 3. Of a profit of Tk 8,200, A gets:
(a) Tk 4,000 (b) Tk 4,200 (c) Tk 3,417 (d) Tk 4,783

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | 2 : 3 → 3/5 × 10000 = 6000 |
| 2 | a | 60,000 : 60,000 = 1 : 1 → 4000 |
| 3 | c | 45000 × 12 : 60000 × 8 = 9 : 8 → 8/17 × 17000 = 8000 |
| 4 | d | 3/12 × 24000 = 6000 |
| 5 | b | Ratio 6 : 4 : 3 → 3/13 × 6500 = 1500 |
| 6 | a | 3 × 1 : 1 × 2 = 3 : 2 → 3/5 × 10000 = 6000 |
| 7 | c | 8000 × 3/2 = 12,000 |
| 8 | d | 1000 + 9000/2 = 5500 |
| 9 | b | 72,000 : 8000t = 3 : 2 → t = 6 months worked → joined after 6 months |
