# Nobel Prizes (latest)

> **Why it matters:** Every exam has at least one Nobel question: the latest winners (especially Peace and Literature), the founder and awarding bodies, and South Asian/Bangladeshi laureates (Tagore 1913, Yunus 2006). The 2026 prizes are announced in October 2026 — look them up as soon as they are out.

## About the prize 🔥
- 🔥 Founded by the will of **Alfred Nobel** (Sweden, 1833–1896), chemist and inventor of **dynamite (1867)**. He signed his will in **1895** and died on **10 December 1896**.
- 🔥 **First prizes: 1901.** Prizes are presented on **10 December** (Nobel's death anniversary): in **Stockholm**, except the **Peace Prize**, which is given in 🔥 **Oslo (Norway)**.
- **Six categories:** Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature, Peace (the original five) + **Economic Sciences**, created in **1968** by **Sveriges Riksbank** (Sweden's central bank); first awarded in **1969** (Ragnar Frisch & Jan Tinbergen).
- Each prize can be shared by **at most three** people (organizations can win the Peace Prize). It is **not given posthumously** (rule since 1974).
- Prize money: **11 million Swedish kronor** per full prize (2023–2025). Winners get a **gold medal** and a **diploma**.

| Prize | Awarded by |
|---|---|
| Physics, Chemistry, Economics | **Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences** |
| Physiology or Medicine | **Nobel Assembly at Karolinska Institutet** |
| Literature | **Swedish Academy** |
| 🔥 Peace | **Norwegian Nobel Committee** (appointed by the Norwegian Parliament, Storting) |

## Record facts 🔥
- 🔥 **Marie Curie:** first woman laureate (Physics **1903**) and the only person to win in **two different sciences** (Chemistry **1911**).
- **Linus Pauling:** two **unshared** prizes (Chemistry 1954, Peace 1962).
- **ICRC** (Red Cross) won the Peace Prize **three times** (1917, 1944, 1963); **UNHCR** twice (1954, 1981).
- Declined: **Jean-Paul Sartre** (Literature 1964), **Le Duc Tho** (Peace 1973).
- **Dag Hammarskjöld** (1961) is the only person to receive the Peace Prize after death (before the 1974 rule).
- UN-linked Peace laureates: UN & **Kofi Annan** (2001), **IAEA** (2005), **IPCC** & Al Gore (2007), **OPCW** (2013), **ICAN** (2017), **WFP** (2020).
