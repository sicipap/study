# Mirror & water images

> **Why it matters:** Mirror image (দর্পণ প্রতিবিম্ব) and water image (জল প্রতিবিম্ব) questions show a word, number, clock or figure and ask how it looks when reflected. Without pictures, you can still solve them with a few fixed rules and letter lists.

## The two basic rules 🔥
| Image | Where the mirror is | What changes | What stays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mirror image** (vertical mirror, usually to the right) | Standing upright beside the object | **Left and right swap** | Top and bottom stay |
| **Water image** (horizontal surface below) | Lying flat below the object | **Top and bottom swap** (upside down) | Left and right stay |

Think of it this way: in a mirror, your right hand appears as the left hand of the image. In a lake, a tree appears upside down.

## What happens to a word
- **Mirror image of a word:** the **order of letters is reversed** (last letter comes first) **and each letter is flipped left–right**.
  - EXAM in a mirror reads, from left to right, M A X E, with E flipped. (Since M, A and X are symmetric, only the E looks strange.)
- **Water image of a word:** the **order stays the same**, but **each letter is turned upside down** (flipped top–bottom).

## Letters that do not change 🔥
| Image type | Capital letters that look the same |
|---|---|
| Mirror (left–right symmetry) | **A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y** |
| Water (top–bottom symmetry) | **B, C, D, E, H, I, K, O, X** |
| Both mirror and water | **H, I, O, X** |

Numbers: **0 and 8** look the same in both a mirror and water (in the usual printed style); **3** looks the same in water but not in a mirror. Some letters depend on the font; exams use the standard block capitals above.

## Words that look the same
- **In a mirror:** the word must use only mirror-symmetric letters **and** read the same backwards: **MOM, WOW, TOOT, OTTO**.
- **In water:** the word only needs letters with top–bottom symmetry (order is kept): **BOX, HIDE, CHECK, DICE, COOKBOOK**.

## Clock in a mirror 🔥
**Actual time = 11:60 − mirror time.** For 11:xx and 12:xx use 23:60.
- Mirror shows 3:15 → 11:60 − 3:15 = **8:45**.
- Mirror shows 10:25 → 11:60 − 10:25 = **1:35**.

## Water image of a clock (rarely asked)
Top and bottom swap, so the numbers trade places: **12 ↔ 6, 1 ↔ 5, 2 ↔ 4, 7 ↔ 11, 8 ↔ 10**, while **3 and 9 stay**. A minute hand at 12 appears at 6, and one at 3 stays at 3. Mirror-clock questions are far more common.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Which of these letters looks the same in a mirror: B, C, A, E?
A has left–right symmetry. **Answer: A.**

**Example 2.** Which word looks the same in a mirror: DAD, MOM, NUN, POP?
D, N and P are not mirror-symmetric. MOM uses symmetric letters and is a palindrome. **Answer: MOM.**

**Example 3.** Which word looks the same in water: BOX, CAT, MAN, TOP?
B, O and X all have top–bottom symmetry. **Answer: BOX.**

**Example 4.** A clock seen in a mirror shows 3:15. What is the real time?
11:60 − 3:15 = **8:45**.

## Quick revision
- Mirror: left ↔ right. Water: top ↔ bottom.
- Mirror image of a word: reverse the order and flip each letter.
- Water image of a word: same order, each letter upside down.
- Mirror-symmetric capitals: A H I M O T U V W X Y.
- Water-symmetric capitals: B C D E H I K O X.
- Same in both: H, I, O, X (and digits 0, 8).
- Mirror clock: 11:60 − time.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Which capital letter looks exactly the same in a vertical mirror?
(a) B (b) C (c) A (d) E

**2.** Which capital letter looks exactly the same in its water image?
(a) A (b) M (c) T (d) E

**3.** Which word looks exactly the same when seen in a mirror placed to its right?
(a) DAD (b) MOM (c) NUN (d) POP

**4.** Which word looks exactly the same in its water image?
(a) BOX (b) CAT (c) MAN (d) TOP

**5.** A clock seen in a mirror shows 3:15. What is the actual time?
(a) 9:45 (b) 8:45 (c) 8:15 (d) 9:15

**6.** Which digit looks the same in both its mirror image and its water image?
(a) 3 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 9

**7.** In the mirror image of the word EXAM (mirror on the right), which letter appears first from the left?
(a) E (b) X (c) A (d) M

**8.** Which group of capital letters looks unchanged in both mirror and water images?
(a) H, I, O, X (b) A, M, T, U (c) B, C, D, E (d) F, G, J, L

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | A has left–right symmetry; B, C, E do not. |
| 2 | d | E has top–bottom symmetry; A, M, T do not. |
| 3 | b | MOM uses mirror-symmetric letters and reads the same backwards. |
| 4 | a | B, O, X all have top–bottom symmetry. |
| 5 | b | 11:60 − 3:15 = 8:45. |
| 6 | c | 8 is symmetric both left–right and top–bottom. |
| 7 | d | A mirror reverses the order, so the last letter M comes first. |
| 8 | a | H, I, O, X are symmetric in both directions. |
