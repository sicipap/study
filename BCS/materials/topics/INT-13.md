# Israel–Palestine & Middle East

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for the Balfour Declaration, UN Partition Resolution 181, the 1967 war, PLO/Hamas founding, Oslo and Camp David, and recent events (7 October 2023, the Gaza war and the 2025 ceasefire). Other Middle East crises (Iran, Syria, Yemen, Gulf Wars) are also asked.

## Background (ইসরায়েল–ফিলিস্তিন সংকট) 🔥
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1896–97 | **Theodor Herzl**, father of **Zionism**, wrote *The Jewish State*; First Zionist Congress, **Basel** (1897) |
| 🔥 **2 Nov 1917** | **Balfour Declaration**: British Foreign Secretary **Arthur Balfour** backed a "national home for the Jewish people" in Palestine (letter to **Lord Rothschild**) |
| 1920–1948 | **British Mandate** over Palestine |
| 🔥 **29 Nov 1947** | **UN General Assembly Resolution 181**: **partition** into a Jewish state and an Arab state; **Jerusalem under international control**. 29 November is now the **International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People** |
| 🔥 **14 May 1948** | **Israel declared independence**; first PM **David Ben-Gurion**, first President **Chaim Weizmann** |
| 🔥 **15 May 1948** | **Nakba (catastrophe)**: about **700,000** Palestinians fled or were expelled; first Arab–Israeli war |
| 1949 | **UNRWA** set up for Palestine refugees |
| 1956 | **Suez Crisis** (Israel, UK, France vs Egypt) |
| 🔥 **1964** | **PLO** founded (Cairo); **Yasser Arafat** led it from 1969 to 2004 |
| 🔥 **June 1967** | **Six-Day War**: Israel captured the **West Bank and East Jerusalem** (from Jordan), **Gaza and Sinai** (from Egypt), and the **Golan Heights** (from Syria). **UNSC Resolution 242** ("land for peace") |
| October 1973 | **Yom Kippur / Ramadan War**; UNSC Resolution 338; Arab **oil embargo** |
| 1978–79 | **Camp David Accords** → Egypt–Israel peace treaty; Egypt got **Sinai** back |
| 🔥 **1987** | **First Intifada** (uprising); **Hamas** founded by **Sheikh Ahmed Yassin** |
| 15 Nov 1988 | **State of Palestine declared** in **Algiers**; **Bangladesh recognises Palestine** |
| 🔥 **1993 & 1995** | **Oslo Accords** (Rabin–Arafat) → **Palestinian Authority (1994)**; West Bank divided into Areas **A, B, C** |
| 2000 | **Second (Al-Aqsa) Intifada** |
| 2004 | **ICJ advisory opinion**: Israel's **separation wall** is illegal |
| 2005–2007 | Israel withdrew settlers from Gaza (2005); **Hamas** won elections (2006) and took control of **Gaza (2007)**; Israeli–Egyptian **blockade** of Gaza |
| 🔥 **29 Nov 2012** | Palestine became a UN **non-member observer state** |
| 2018 | USA moved its embassy to **Jerusalem** |
| 2020 | **Abraham Accords**: **UAE and Bahrain** (then Morocco, Sudan) normalised ties with Israel |
| July 2024 | **ICJ advisory opinion**: Israel's occupation of Palestinian territory is **unlawful** |

- **Palestinian territories:** **West Bank** (administrative centre **Ramallah**) and the **Gaza Strip**. Palestinians claim **East Jerusalem** as their capital.
- 🔥 **Al-Aqsa Mosque** (Jerusalem) was the **first qibla** of Muslims and is Islam's **third holiest** site.
- **Two-state solution:** Israel and Palestine side by side, roughly on the **pre-1967 borders**.

## The Gaza war (from 7 October 2023) 🔥
- 🔥 **7 October 2023:** Hamas attacked southern Israel ("**Operation Al-Aqsa Flood**"): about **1,200** killed in Israel and about **250** taken hostage.
- Israel began a massive military campaign in Gaza. By late 2025 the Gaza health ministry reported **more than 60,000 Palestinians killed**; most of Gaza was destroyed and famine was declared in parts of Gaza (August 2025, IPC).
- 🔥 **South Africa v. Israel** at the **ICJ** under the **Genocide Convention** (filed **December 2023**); the Court ordered provisional measures in 2024.
- 🔥 **ICC arrest warrants (21 November 2024)** for Israeli PM **Benjamin Netanyahu** and ex-defence minister **Yoav Gallant** (and Hamas commander Mohammed Deif).
- Leaders killed: Hamas political chief **Ismail Haniyeh** (Tehran, July 2024); Hamas leader **Yahya Sinwar** (Gaza, October 2024); Hezbollah chief **Hassan Nasrallah** (Beirut, September 2024).
- A ceasefire of January 2025 broke down in March 2025.
- 🔥 **October 2025 ceasefire** under US President **Trump's 20-point plan**: took effect **10 October 2025**; living hostages released and Palestinian prisoners freed; **Sharm el-Sheikh (Egypt) peace summit**, 13 October 2025.
- **UNSC Resolution 2803 (November 2025)** endorsed the plan's "**Board of Peace**" and an **International Stabilization Force** for Gaza.
- **Recognition wave, September 2025:** 🔥 the **UK, Canada, Australia, Portugal** and **France** (and others) recognised the State of Palestine at the UN General Assembly; about **three-quarters of UN members** now recognise Palestine.

> 🔄 Update: check the latest figure in a newspaper or the Bangladesh Economic Review. Check the current status of the Gaza ceasefire, casualty figures, and the number of states recognising Palestine.

## Other Middle East crises
| Crisis | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Iranian Revolution** | **1979**, Ayatollah **Khomeini** overthrew the Shah |
| **Iran–Iraq War** | **1980–1988** |
| 🔥 **Gulf War** | Iraq (**Saddam Hussein**) invaded **Kuwait**, **2 Aug 1990**; US-led **Operation Desert Storm** freed Kuwait, **1991** |
| **Iraq War** | US-led invasion **March 2003**; Saddam executed **December 2006** |
| 🔥 **Syria** | Civil war from **2011** (Arab Spring); **Bashar al-Assad fell on 8 December 2024** and fled to Moscow; **Ahmed al-Sharaa** became interim president (2025) |
| **Yemen** | War since 2014–15; **Houthis (Ansar Allah)** control Sana'a; Houthi attacks on **Red Sea shipping** from November 2023 |
| **Lebanon** | Israel–Hezbollah ceasefire **November 2024**; President **Joseph Aoun** elected January 2025 |
| **Iran nuclear deal (JCPOA)** | Signed **2015**; **USA withdrew 2018**; UN "snapback" sanctions reimposed **September 2025** |
| 🔥 **Israel–Iran war** | **13–24 June 2025** ("12-day war"); US struck **Fordow, Natanz and Isfahan** nuclear sites on **22 June 2025** |
| **Saudi–Iran détente** | Diplomatic ties restored, **March 2023**, brokered by **China** in Beijing |

## Bangladesh's position 🔥
- Bangladesh **does not recognise Israel** and supports an **independent Palestinian state** with **East Jerusalem** as its capital, on the pre-1967 borders.
- The words "**except Israel**" were removed from Bangladeshi passports in 2021 and **restored in 2025**.
- Bangladesh recognised Palestine in **1988**; Palestine has an embassy in Dhaka.

## Quick revision
- Balfour Declaration: **2 November 1917**.
- UN Partition Resolution **181**: **29 November 1947**; Israel founded **14 May 1948**.
- Six-Day War **1967**: West Bank, East Jerusalem, Gaza, Sinai, Golan; UNSC Res **242**.
- PLO **1964**; Hamas **1987** (Sheikh Ahmed Yassin); Oslo **1993**.
- Palestine UN observer state: **29 November 2012**.
- Hamas attack **7 October 2023**; ICC warrant for **Netanyahu**, **Nov 2024**; ceasefire **10 October 2025**.
- Assad regime fell **8 December 2024**.
