# Countries: capitals, currencies, parliaments

> **Why it matters:** "Capital of …", "currency of …" and "name of the parliament of …" are asked in every exam. Focus on neighbours, Muslim countries, big powers, and unusual names (Knesset, Storting, Diet, Majlis, Rada). Recently changed capitals and currencies are favourites.

## Patterns to remember 🔥
- **Rupee:** India, Pakistan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, **Mauritius**, **Seychelles**. (Indonesia **Rupiah**, Maldives **Rufiyaa**.)
- **Dinar:** Iraq, Kuwait, Bahrain, Jordan, Libya, Algeria, Tunisia, Serbia.
- **Riyal/Rial:** Saudi Arabia, Qatar (riyal); Iran, Oman, Yemen (rial). **Dirham:** UAE, Morocco.
- **Krone/Krona:** Norway, Denmark (krone); Sweden (krona); Iceland (króna).
- **Euro:** used by **21 EU countries** from 2026 (Bulgaria joined on 1 January 2026); also used by some non-EU states such as Montenegro and Kosovo.
- **Capital moves:** Myanmar (Yangon → **Naypyidaw**, 2005–06), Kazakhstan (Almaty → **Astana**, 1997), Nigeria (Lagos → **Abuja**, 1991), Brazil (Rio → **Brasília**, 1960), Pakistan (Karachi → **Islamabad**, 1960s), Indonesia (Jakarta → **Nusantara**, in progress).
- **Countries with more than one capital:** **South Africa (3)**, **Bolivia** (Sucre constitutional, La Paz seat of government), **Sri Lanka**, **Netherlands**, **Malaysia**.

> 🔄 Update: check the latest figure in a newspaper or the Bangladesh Economic Review. Currencies and capitals rarely change, but check new euro adopters and capital moves (e.g. Indonesia's Nusantara).

## Quick revision
- **Knesset** — Israel; **Storting** — Norway; **Diet** — Japan; **Duma** — Russia; **Verkhovna Rada** — Ukraine; **Riksdag** — Sweden; **Folketing** — Denmark; **Althing** — Iceland; **Sejm** — Poland; **Majlis** — Iran/Maldives; **Tshogdu** — Bhutan; **Pyidaungsu Hluttaw** — Myanmar.
- Capitals often confused: **Canberra**, **Ottawa**, **Brasília**, **Abuja**, **Astana**, **Naypyidaw**, **Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte**, **Bern**, **Wellington**.
- Currencies often asked: **Ngultrum** (Bhutan), **Rufiyaa** (Maldives), **Kyat** (Myanmar), **Ringgit** (Malaysia), **Baht** (Thailand), **Won** (Korea), **Rand** (South Africa), **Naira** (Nigeria), **Real** (Brazil), **Hryvnia** (Ukraine).

## Practice MCQ
**1.** What is the name of the parliament of Israel?
(a) Majlis (b) Knesset (c) Diet (d) Storting

**2.** The currency of Bhutan is —
(a) Ngultrum (b) Rufiyaa (c) Kyat (d) Afghani

**3.** Storting is the parliament of —
(a) Sweden (b) Denmark (c) Norway (d) Finland

**4.** What is the capital of Australia?
(a) Sydney (b) Melbourne (c) Perth (d) Canberra

**5.** The currency of Malaysia is —
(a) Rupiah (b) Baht (c) Ringgit (d) Dong

**6.** Which country has three capitals?
(a) South Africa (b) Bolivia (c) Netherlands (d) Sri Lanka

**7.** The parliament of Japan is called the —
(a) Duma (b) Sejm (c) Riksdag (d) Diet

**8.** Which country's currency is the Rand?
(a) Nigeria (b) South Africa (c) Kenya (d) Ghana

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Israel's parliament is the Knesset |
| 2 | a | Bhutan uses the Ngultrum |
| 3 | c | Norway's parliament is the Storting; Sweden's is the Riksdag |
| 4 | d | Canberra is Australia's capital |
| 5 | c | Malaysia's currency is the Ringgit |
| 6 | a | Pretoria, Cape Town and Bloemfontein |
| 7 | d | Japan's parliament is the Diet (Kokkai) |
| 8 | b | The Rand is South Africa's currency |
