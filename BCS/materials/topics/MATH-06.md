# Profit & loss

> **Why it matters:** Profit and loss (লাভ-ক্ষতি) is one of the most frequently asked arithmetic topics in BCS, bank and 9th–20th grade exams: finding cost price from a loss, "CP of x = SP of y", two items at the same price, and false weights.

## Key concepts
- **Cost price (ক্রয়মূল্য, CP):** the price at which an article is bought (including transport, repair etc.).
- **Selling price (বিক্রয়মূল্য, SP):** the price at which it is sold.
- **Profit (লাভ)** = SP − CP (when SP > CP). **Loss (ক্ষতি)** = CP − SP (when CP > SP).
- 🔥 Profit % and loss % are always calculated on the **cost price**, unless stated otherwise.
- **Marked price (তালিকা মূল্য, MP):** the printed price. **Discount (কমিশন/ছাড়)** is given on the **marked price**.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| Profit % | (Profit ÷ CP) × 100 |
| SP from profit % | SP = CP × (100 + p)/100 |
| SP from loss % | SP = CP × (100 − l)/100 |
| CP from SP | CP = SP × 100/(100 + p) or SP × 100/(100 − l) |
| 🔥 CP of x articles = SP of y articles | Profit % = (x − y)/y × 100 |
| 🔥 Two articles sold at the **same SP**, one at x% gain, the other at x% loss | Always a **loss** of x²/100 % |
| False weight (sells at CP but gives less) | Gain % = (error)/(true value − error) × 100 |
| Successive discounts a%, b% | Single discount = a + b − ab/100 % |
| Mark-up m% then discount d% | Profit % = m − d − md/100 |
| Same profit at SP₁ as loss at SP₂ | CP = (SP₁ + SP₂)/2 |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Selling an article for Tk 450 causes a 10% loss. At what price should it be sold to gain 10%?
Steps: CP = 450 × 100/90 = 500. SP for 10% gain = 500 × 110/100 = 550.
**Answer: Tk 550**

**Example 2.** The cost price of 20 articles equals the selling price of 16. Find the profit %.
Steps: (20 − 16)/16 × 100 = 25%.
**Answer: 25% profit**

**Example 3.** Two phones are each sold for Tk 990. One gives a 10% gain, the other a 10% loss. Find the overall result.
Steps: Loss % = 10²/100 = 1%.
**Answer: 1% loss**

**Example 4.** A shopkeeper uses a 900 g weight for 1 kg and sells at cost price. Find his gain %.
Steps: Error = 100 g. Gain = 100/900 × 100 = 11 1/9%.
**Answer: 11 1/9%**

**Example 5.** The marked price is Tk 1000. Successive discounts of 10% and 5% are given. Find the SP.
Steps: 1000 × 0.90 × 0.95 = 855.
**Answer: Tk 855**

## Common traps
- Taking profit % on SP instead of CP.
- Adding successive discounts (10% + 5% ≠ 15%; it is 14.5%).
- "Same SP, x% gain and x% loss" is never "no profit no loss" — it is always a small **loss**.
- In "buy 6 for Tk 5, sell 5 for Tk 6" type sums, compare the price of **one** item (or use LCM of the counts).

## Quick revision
- Profit % = profit/CP × 100.
- CP = SP × 100/(100 ± %).
- CP of x = SP of y ⇒ (x − y)/y × 100.
- Same SP, x% gain & x% loss ⇒ loss x²/100 %.
- False weight gain = error/(true − error) × 100.
- Successive discounts: a + b − ab/100.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** An article bought for Tk 120 is sold for Tk 150. The profit % is:
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 30% (d) 15%

**2.** A fan was sold for Tk 1,140 at a loss of 5%. Its cost price was:
(a) Tk 1,197 (b) Tk 1,083 (c) Tk 1,200 (d) Tk 1,180

**3.** Selling a watch for Tk 720 gives a 20% loss. To gain 25%, it must be sold for:
(a) Tk 900 (b) Tk 1,125 (c) Tk 1,080 (d) Tk 1,000

**4.** The cost price of 15 pens equals the selling price of 12 pens. The profit % is:
(a) 20% (b) 3% (c) 30% (d) 25%

**5.** A man buys oranges at 6 for Tk 5 and sells them at 5 for Tk 6. His profit % is:
(a) 44% (b) 20% (c) 36% (d) 22%

**6.** Successive discounts of 20% and 10% are equal to a single discount of:
(a) 30% (b) 28% (c) 25% (d) 32%

**7.** Two articles are each sold for Tk 1,200. One gives a 20% profit, the other a 20% loss. The overall result is:
(a) no profit no loss (b) 4% profit (c) 2% loss (d) 4% loss

**8.** A shopkeeper marks goods 25% above cost and allows a 10% discount. His profit % is:
(a) 15% (b) 12.5% (c) 10% (d) 13.5%

**9.** A dishonest trader uses an 800 g weight for 1 kg and sells at cost price. His gain % is:
(a) 20% (b) 22.5% (c) 25% (d) 18%

**10.** The profit on selling an article for Tk 425 equals the loss on selling it for Tk 355. Its cost price is:
(a) Tk 380 (b) Tk 400 (c) Tk 385 (d) Tk 390

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Profit 30; 30/120 × 100 = 25% |
| 2 | c | CP = 1140 × 100/95 = 1200 |
| 3 | b | CP = 720 × 100/80 = 900; 900 × 1.25 = 1125 |
| 4 | d | (15 − 12)/12 × 100 = 25% |
| 5 | a | CP of 1 = 5/6, SP of 1 = 6/5; (6/5 − 5/6) ÷ 5/6 = 11/25 = 44% |
| 6 | b | 20 + 10 − 200/100 = 28% |
| 7 | d | Loss = 20²/100 = 4% |
| 8 | b | 1.25 × 0.90 = 1.125 → 12.5% profit |
| 9 | c | 200/800 × 100 = 25% |
| 10 | d | CP = (425 + 355)/2 = 390 |
