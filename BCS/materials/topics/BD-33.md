# Power & energy: Rooppur NPP, renewables

> **Why it matters:** Rooppur (location, capacity, partner country), the only hydro plant, big coal plants and solar/wind "firsts" are regular MCQs; installed-capacity figures appear in written and viva too.

## Power sector basics 🔥
- Main fuel for electricity: **natural gas**; then furnace oil, coal, imports, and a small share of renewables.
- **100% electricity coverage** was declared on **21 March 2022** (at the Payra plant).
- Installed capacity (grid, including imports and renewables) passed **25,000 MW in 2022** and was about **27,000–28,000 MW by 2024**; actual generation is much lower because of fuel shortages. Figures differ by source.

## Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant (রূপপুর পারমাণবিক বিদ্যুৎকেন্দ্র) 🔥🔥
- Location: **Rooppur, Ishwardi, Pabna**, on the bank of the **Padma**.
- Capacity: **2 units × 1,200 MW = 2,400 MW**.
- Reactor type: **VVER-1200** (Russian, Generation III+).
- Built by **Rosatom** (Russia) with a Russian state loan of about **US$ 11.38 billion**.
- First concrete: **Unit 1 on 30 November 2017**, **Unit 2 on 14 July 2018**.
- **Nuclear fuel handed over: 5 October 2023** — Bangladesh became the **33rd country** with nuclear power generation capability.
- The idea dates back to **1961** (Pakistan period).

## Major power plants 🔥
| Plant | Place | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Karnaphuli Hydro Power Station (Kaptai)** | Rangamati | **Only hydro** plant, **230 MW**, started **1962** |
| **Payra Thermal Power Plant** | Kalapara, **Patuakhali** | **1,320 MW** coal; **ultra-supercritical**; Bangladesh–China joint venture |
| **Maitree Super Thermal (Rampal)** | **Bagerhat** | **1,320 MW** coal; Bangladesh–India joint venture (BIFPCL) |
| **Matarbari** | Moheshkhali, **Cox's Bazar** | **1,200 MW** coal; **JICA** funding |
| **Barapukuria** | Dinajpur | Only plant using **local coal** |
| **Ghorasal** | Narsingdi | Oldest large gas-based complex |

### Power import 🔥
- From **India**: first through the **Bheramara (Kushtia) HVDC** link (**2013**); also from Tripura, and from the **Adani Godda plant (Jharkhand)** from **2023**.
- From **Nepal**: first power import through the Indian grid began in **November 2024** (40 MW, trilateral deal).

## Renewable energy (নবায়নযোগ্য জ্বালানি) 🔥
- Renewables are still a small share of generation (about **4–5%** of capacity).
- **Solar Home Systems (SHS):** run by **IDCOL**; one of the **largest off-grid solar programmes in the world** (about 6 million systems).
- **Largest solar park:** **Teesta Solar Park, Sundarganj, Gaibandha (200 MW)**, 2023.
- **First grid-connected solar park:** **Sarishabari, Jamalpur (3 MW)**, 2017.
- **Wind:** pilot plants at **Sonagazi (Feni)** and **Kutubdia**; a larger wind farm at **Khurushkul, Cox's Bazar (60 MW)**.
- **Biogas** and **solar irrigation pumps** are growing in rural areas.
- **Renewable Energy Policy 2025** set targets of **20% by 2030** and **30% by 2040** (earlier policies: 10% target in the 2008 policy).

## Quick revision
- 100% electricity coverage: **21 March 2022**.
- Rooppur: **Ishwardi, Pabna**; **2,400 MW**; **VVER-1200**; **Russia/Rosatom**.
- Fuel handover **5 Oct 2023** → **33rd** nuclear-power nation.
- Only hydro plant: **Kaptai, 230 MW (1962)**.
- Payra (China JV), Rampal (India JV), Matarbari (JICA): all coal.
- Largest solar park: **Teesta Solar, Gaibandha (200 MW)**.
- Research reactor: **TRIGA Mark II, Savar (1986)**.
