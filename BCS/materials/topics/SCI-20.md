# Diseases (viral/bacterial) & vaccines

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask "which disease is caused by a virus/bacterium/protozoan?", carriers (vectors), who discovered which germ or vaccine, what BCG protects against, and Bangladesh's EPI and disease-elimination milestones.

## Disease by pathogen type 🔥🔥🔥
| Pathogen | Diseases |
|---|---|
| **Virus (ভাইরাস)** | **COVID-19**, influenza (flu), **common cold**, **dengue**, **chikungunya**, Zika, yellow fever, **polio**, **measles (হাম)**, **mumps**, rubella, **chickenpox**, **smallpox**, **rabies (জলাতঙ্ক)**, **hepatitis A, B, C, E (জন্ডিস)**, **HIV/AIDS**, **Nipah**, Ebola, bird flu (H5N1), swine flu (H1N1), herpes, mpox |
| **Bacterium (ব্যাকটেরিয়া)** | **Tuberculosis (যক্ষ্মা)**, **cholera (কলেরা)**, **typhoid**, **diphtheria**, **whooping cough (pertussis)**, **tetanus (ধনুষ্টংকার)**, **leprosy (কুষ্ঠ)**, **plague**, **anthrax**, bacterial pneumonia, **bacillary dysentery** (Shigella), syphilis, gonorrhoea, botulism |
| **Protozoa (প্রোটোজোয়া)** | **Malaria** (*Plasmodium*), **kala-azar** (*Leishmania donovani*), **amoebic dysentery** (*Entamoeba histolytica*), sleeping sickness (*Trypanosoma*) |
| **Fungus (ছত্রাক)** | **Ringworm (দাদ)**, athlete's foot, dandruff, candidiasis (note: **scabies** is caused by a **mite**, not a fungus) |
| **Worms (কৃমি)** | **Filaria / elephantiasis (গোদ)** (*Wuchereria bancrofti*), roundworm (*Ascaris*), hookworm, tapeworm |

- **Antibiotics kill bacteria, not viruses.**
- **Jaundice** is a symptom (high bilirubin), most often from viral **hepatitis**. **Hepatitis B and C** spread through **blood** and can cause liver cancer; **A and E** spread through contaminated food/water.
- **Pneumonia** can be bacterial or viral.

## Carriers (vectors) 🔥
| Carrier | Disease |
|---|---|
| **Female *Anopheles* mosquito** | **Malaria** |
| ***Aedes aegypti*** (and *Ae. albopictus*) | **Dengue, chikungunya**, Zika, yellow fever |
| ***Culex*** mosquito | **Filaria**, Japanese encephalitis |
| **Sandfly** | **Kala-azar** |
| **Housefly** | Cholera, typhoid, dysentery (carries germs mechanically) |
| **Rat flea** | **Plague** |
| **Dog (bite)**, cat, jackal, fox | **Rabies** |
| **Fruit bat** (via raw date-palm juice, খেজুরের কাঁচা রস) | **Nipah virus** (first Bangladesh outbreak: **Meherpur, 2001**) |
| Tsetse fly | Sleeping sickness |

## Discoverers of germs, vaccines and drugs 🔥🔥
| Discovery | Person / year |
|---|---|
| **Smallpox vaccine** (from cowpox) — **father of immunology** | **Edward Jenner (1796)** |
| **Rabies vaccine** (1885), anthrax vaccine, **pasteurisation**, germ theory | **Louis Pasteur** |
| **TB bacterium** (1882), cholera bacterium (1883) | **Robert Koch** |
| Malaria parasite in blood (1880) | Alphonse Laveran |
| **Mosquito transmits malaria** (1897) | **Ronald Ross** (Nobel 1902) |
| Leprosy bacterium | Gerhard Armauer Hansen |
| **Penicillin — first antibiotic (1928)** | **Alexander Fleming** |
| Streptomycin (first effective TB antibiotic) | Selman Waksman |
| **Injectable polio vaccine (IPV, 1955)** | **Jonas Salk** |
| **Oral polio vaccine (OPV)** | **Albert Sabin** |
| **BCG vaccine** (against TB, 1921) | **Calmette & Guérin** |
| **HIV** identified (1983) | Luc Montagnier & Françoise Barré-Sinoussi |
| Artemisinin for malaria (Nobel 2015) | Tu Youyou |
| Quinine (old malaria drug) | From **cinchona** bark |

- **BCG = Bacillus Calmette–Guérin**, given at birth **against tuberculosis**.
- **Smallpox** is the **only human disease eradicated** worldwide — WHO declared it in **1980**.
- **ORS (oral saline)** treats dehydration from **diarrhoea/cholera**; **icddr,b (Dhaka)** played a key role in developing and proving it. Zinc is given with ORS.

## Bangladesh: EPI and milestones 🔥
- **EPI (Expanded Programme on Immunization)** in Bangladesh started on **7 April 1979**; it expanded nationwide from **1985**.
- Main EPI vaccines: **BCG** (TB), **Pentavalent** (**diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus, hepatitis B, Hib**), **OPV and IPV** (polio), **PCV** (pneumococcal pneumonia), **MR** (measles-rubella), and **TT/Td** for women. **HPV vaccine** (against cervical cancer) for girls was introduced in **2023**; a nationwide **typhoid conjugate vaccine** campaign followed later.
- Bangladesh was **certified polio-free in March 2014** (with the WHO South-East Asia Region).
- WHO validated Bangladesh's elimination of **lymphatic filariasis (2023)** and of **kala-azar** as a public-health problem (**October 2023**, the **first country** to be validated for kala-azar).

> 🔄 Update: check the latest EPI schedule and new vaccine campaigns in a newspaper or the DGHS website.

## COVID-19 and other key facts
- **COVID-19** is caused by **SARS-CoV-2** (a coronavirus); first reported in **Wuhan, China (December 2019)**; WHO declared a **pandemic on 11 March 2020**. Bangladesh reported its first cases on **8 March 2020**. WHO ended the global health emergency on **5 May 2023**.
- **Dengue**: no specific antiviral drug; **platelet count falls**; early test **NS1 antigen**. Aedes breeds in **clean, stagnant water**.
- **Rabies** is almost always fatal once symptoms appear; post-bite vaccination is essential. Also called **hydrophobia** (fear of water).
- **Tetanus** causes "lockjaw"; spores in soil/rust enter through wounds.
- Important days: **World TB Day 24 March**, **World Malaria Day 25 April**, **World AIDS Day 1 December**, World Health Day 7 April (WHO founded 1948, HQ **Geneva**).

## Quick revision
- **Virus**: COVID-19, dengue, polio, measles, rabies, hepatitis, AIDS, Nipah, chickenpox.
- **Bacteria**: TB, cholera, typhoid, diphtheria, tetanus, leprosy, plague, whooping cough.
- **Protozoa**: malaria, kala-azar, amoebic dysentery. **Fungus**: ringworm.
- **Anopheles → malaria; Aedes → dengue; Culex → filaria; sandfly → kala-azar.**
- **Jenner: smallpox vaccine (1796); Pasteur: rabies vaccine; Koch: TB germ; Fleming: penicillin (1928).**
- **Salk: IPV; Sabin: OPV; BCG protects against TB.**
- Smallpox eradicated **1980**; Bangladesh polio-free **2014**; EPI began **1979**.
