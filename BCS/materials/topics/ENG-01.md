# Noun: kinds, number, gender

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for the kind of a noun, the plural of irregular words (datum, crisis, mother-in-law), the feminine of a masculine noun, and nouns that are always singular or always plural.

## Kinds of noun
A **noun** is the name of a person, place, thing, quality or idea.

| Kind | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Proper** | Name of a particular person/place/thing (capital letter) | Dhaka, Rabindranath, the Padma |
| **Common** | Name shared by every member of a class | boy, city, river, book |
| **Collective** | Name of a group taken as one whole | army, team, jury, family, class, committee, herd, flock |
| **Material** | Name of a substance (usually uncountable) | gold, water, rice, milk, iron |
| **Abstract** 🔥 | Name of a quality, state or action (cannot be touched) | honesty, childhood, freedom, beauty, laughter |

- **Countable** nouns have a plural (pen/pens); **uncountable** nouns don't (water, advice).
- Proper → common: "He is **the Newton** of our college" (= a great scientist). "Dhaka is **the London** of Bangladesh."
- Material/abstract as common: "a **stone**", "**kindnesses**", "**youths**" (= young men).

## Number
### Regular rules
- add **-s/-es**: book → books; box → boxes; hero → **heroes**, potato → **potatoes**; but photo → **photos**, piano → **pianos**, radio → radios.
- consonant + y → **-ies** (lady → ladies); vowel + y → -s (day → days).
- -f/-fe → **-ves** (knife → knives, leaf → leaves, wolf → wolves); but **roof → roofs, chief → chiefs, proof → proofs, belief → beliefs, safe → safes**.

### Irregular and foreign plurals 🔥
| Singular | Plural | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| man | **men** | **datum** | **data** |
| foot | **feet** | **medium** | **media / mediums** |
| tooth | **teeth** | **memorandum** | **memoranda** |
| mouse | **mice** | **phenomenon** | **phenomena** |
| ox | **oxen** | **criterion** | **criteria** |
| child | **children** | **crisis** | **crises** |
| **formula** | **formulae / formulas** | **analysis** | **analyses** |
| **radius** | **radii** | **basis** | **bases** |
| **alumnus** | **alumni** | **thesis** | **theses** |
| **stimulus** | **stimuli** | **hypothesis** | **hypotheses** |
| **index** | **indices / indexes** | **axis** | **axes** |
| **bureau** | **bureaux / bureaus** | **cherub** | **cherubim / cherubs** |
| **agendum** | **agenda** | **oasis** | **oases** |

### Compound nouns 🔥
The **main word** takes the plural: **mothers-in-law, sons-in-law, commanders-in-chief, passers-by, men-of-war, courts-martial**. Exceptions: **spoonfuls, handfuls**. man/woman as first word: **men-servants, women-doctors**.

### Same form in singular and plural
**sheep, deer, swine, fish** (usually), **aircraft, series, species, salmon**. Numbers after a figure: **two dozen eggs, five hundred taka, a ten-mile walk, a five-year plan** (no -s).

### Always plural (take plural verb)
**scissors, trousers, spectacles, tongs, pliers, thanks, alms, riches, premises, cattle, people, police, poultry, gentry, clergy**. "The **police have** arrested him." "**Cattle are** grazing."

### Look plural but singular 🔥
**news, physics, mathematics, economics, politics** (as a subject), **measles, mumps, billiards**. "**The news is** good." "**Mathematics is** my favourite."

### Always singular (uncountable, no -s) 🔥
**information, advice, furniture, luggage, baggage, scenery, machinery, poetry, equipment, bread, knowledge, mischief**. Say "a piece of advice", "an item of furniture" — **never "advices", "furnitures", "sceneries"**.

## Gender
| Masculine | Feminine | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|---|
| bachelor | **spinster / maid** | fox | **vixen** |
| drake | **duck** | gander | **goose** |
| stag | **hind** | horse | **mare** |
| bull/ox | **cow** | colt | **filly** |
| dog | **bitch** | cock | **hen** |
| hero | **heroine** | lad | **lass** |
| tiger | **tigress** | lion | **lioness** |
| wizard | **witch** | monk | **nun** |
| nephew | **niece** | sir | **madam** |
| emperor | **empress** | sultan | **sultana** |
| **testator** | **testatrix** | executor | **executrix** |
| **drone** | **bee** | peacock | **peahen** |
| **earl** | **countess** | beau | **belle** |

- **Common gender:** both sexes — parent, child, friend, teacher, student, baby, cousin, person.
- **Neuter:** lifeless things — book, table.
- Personification: **sun, death, time, war** → masculine (he); **moon, earth, nature, ship, country, peace** → feminine (she).

## Common mistakes
| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| He gave me many advices. | He gave me **much advice / many pieces of advice**. |
| The furnitures are new. | The **furniture is** new. |
| The news are true. | The news **is** true. |
| I bought two dozens eggs. | I bought **two dozen** eggs. |
| My mother-in-laws are kind. | My **mothers-in-law** are kind. |
| The cattle is grazing. | The cattle **are** grazing. |
| The scenery of Sylhet are charming. | The scenery **is** charming. |

## Quick revision
- 🔥 Plural of **datum = data**, **criterion = criteria**, **phenomenon = phenomena**, **crisis = crises**.
- 🔥 **mother-in-law → mothers-in-law**; **passer-by → passers-by**.
- **advice, information, furniture, scenery, luggage** are uncountable: no -s.
- **news, physics, mathematics** take singular verbs.
- **cattle, police, people** take plural verbs.
- Feminine of **fox = vixen**, **drake = duck**, **stag = hind**, **bachelor = spinster**.
- Abstract noun of **child = childhood**, **thief = theft**, **wise = wisdom**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** What is the plural of "datum"?
(a) datums (b) data (c) datas (d) datae

**2.** Which of the following is an abstract noun?
(a) gold (b) jury (c) honesty (d) Dhaka

**3.** The correct plural of "mother-in-law" is —
(a) mother-in-laws (b) mothers-in-laws (c) mothers-in-law (d) mother-ins-law

**4.** What is the feminine of "fox"?
(a) vixen (b) foxess (c) doe (d) hind

**5.** Which sentence is correct?
(a) He gave me many advices. (b) The news are true. (c) I bought two dozens eggs. (d) The furniture is new.

**6.** "Army" is a —
(a) material noun (b) proper noun (c) abstract noun (d) collective noun

**7.** The plural of "phenomenon" is —
(a) phenomenons (b) phenomena (c) phenomenas (d) phenomeni

**8.** Which noun always takes a plural verb?
(a) cattle (b) news (c) mathematics (d) scenery

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | datum → data (Latin plural) |
| 2 | c | Honesty is a quality, so abstract |
| 3 | c | The main word "mother" takes the plural |
| 4 | a | fox → vixen |
| 5 | d | furniture is uncountable and takes a singular verb |
| 6 | d | An army is a group taken as one whole |
| 7 | b | phenomenon → phenomena (Greek plural) |
| 8 | a | cattle is always plural: cattle are |
