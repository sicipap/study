# Blockchain, crypto, VR/AR, metaverse

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask who created Bitcoin, what a blockchain is, the difference between VR and AR, and where the word "metaverse" came from. Bank exams also ask about Bangladesh Bank's position on cryptocurrency.

## Blockchain (ব্লকচেইন) 🔥
- A **decentralised, distributed digital ledger** that records transactions in **blocks** linked together in a **chain** using **cryptographic hashes**.
- Each block holds: transaction data, a **timestamp**, its own **hash** and the **hash of the previous block**. Changing one block breaks the chain, so records are practically **immutable (tamper-proof)**.
- Copies of the ledger are kept on many computers (**nodes**) in a **peer-to-peer** network; there is **no central authority**.
- **Consensus mechanisms** (how nodes agree):
  - 🔥 **Proof of Work (PoW):** miners solve hard puzzles — **Bitcoin**.
  - **Proof of Stake (PoS):** validators lock up (stake) coins — **Ethereum** (switched in **2022**, "The Merge").
- **Types:** **public** (Bitcoin, Ethereum), **private** (one organisation), **consortium/federated** (a group of banks), hybrid.
- 🔥 **Smart contract:** a self-executing program on a blockchain that runs automatically when conditions are met. The idea came from **Nick Szabo** (1994); **Ethereum** made it popular.
- **Uses beyond crypto:** land records, supply chain tracking, trade finance, digital identity, voting, healthcare records.

## Cryptocurrency (ক্রিপ্টোকারেন্সি) 🔥
- **Digital money** secured by cryptography and usually run on a blockchain; not issued by any central bank.

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Bitcoin (BTC)** | **First cryptocurrency**. Created by **"Satoshi Nakamoto"** (pseudonym; real identity unknown). White paper **2008**; network launched **January 2009** |
| Bitcoin supply limit | **21 million** coins |
| Smallest unit of Bitcoin | **Satoshi** = 0.00000001 BTC (10⁻⁸) |
| 🔥 **Ethereum (ETH / Ether)** | **Vitalik Buterin**; launched **2015**; smart contracts |
| Other coins | Tether (USDT, a **stablecoin**), BNB, Solana, Ripple (XRP), Dogecoin |
| **Mining** | Verifying transactions and adding blocks, for a reward |
| **Crypto wallet** | Stores the private keys; **hot wallet** (online) or **cold wallet** (offline) |
| **Altcoin** | Any crypto other than Bitcoin |
| **First country to make Bitcoin legal tender** | **El Salvador** (September 2021) |

- **NFT (Non-Fungible Token):** a unique digital token on a blockchain showing ownership of a digital item (art, music). "Non-fungible" = cannot be swapped one-for-one.
- **CBDC (Central Bank Digital Currency):** digital money **issued by a central bank** (e.g. China's e-CNY, India's e-Rupee). It is **not** a cryptocurrency, because it is centralised.
- 🔥 **Bangladesh:** Bangladesh Bank has repeatedly warned that transactions in cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin are **not authorised/legal tender** in Bangladesh and may break foreign-exchange and anti-money-laundering laws.

> 🔄 Update: rules on crypto change; check the latest Bangladesh Bank circulars.

## Virtual reality, augmented reality, mixed reality 🔥
| Term | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **VR (Virtual Reality)** | A **fully computer-made 3-D environment** that replaces the real world; needs a **headset (HMD)** | **Meta Quest (Oculus)**, flight and surgery simulators, VR games |
| 🔥 **AR (Augmented Reality)** | **Adds digital objects on top of the real world**; real world stays visible | **Pokémon Go** (2016), Snapchat/Instagram filters, Google Lens, IKEA Place |
| **MR (Mixed Reality)** | Real and virtual objects **interact** with each other | Microsoft **HoloLens** |
| **XR (Extended Reality)** | **Umbrella term** for VR, AR and MR | |

- **HMD** = Head-Mounted Display. **Ivan Sutherland** made the first HMD, the **"Sword of Damocles" (1968)**; he is called the father of computer graphics.
- **Jaron Lanier** popularised the term **"virtual reality"** in the 1980s.
- **Haptic technology:** gives a sense of **touch** (vibration, force) in VR.
- Uses: education, medical training, military training, tourism, real estate, gaming.

## Metaverse (মেটাভার্স) 🔥
- A **shared, 3-D virtual world** on the internet where people meet, work, play and trade as **avatars**, using VR/AR, blockchain and AI.
- 🔥 The word **"metaverse"** was coined by **Neal Stephenson** in his science-fiction novel ***Snow Crash*** (**1992**).
- **Facebook** changed its company name to **Meta Platforms** in **October 2021** to focus on the metaverse.
- Examples of virtual worlds: Second Life, Roblox, Decentraland, Fortnite events.
- **Avatar:** a user's digital character. **Digital twin:** a virtual copy of a real object or system.
- **Web3:** idea of a decentralised internet built on blockchain.

## Quick revision
- Blockchain = **decentralised, distributed, immutable ledger**.
- Bitcoin creator: **Satoshi Nakamoto** (2008 paper, 2009 launch); max **21 million**; smallest unit **satoshi**.
- Ethereum: **Vitalik Buterin (2015)**, smart contracts; PoS since 2022.
- Bitcoin uses **Proof of Work**.
- First country with Bitcoin as legal tender: **El Salvador (2021)**.
- VR = fully virtual; AR = digital **over** the real world (**Pokémon Go**).
- "Metaverse" coined by **Neal Stephenson (Snow Crash, 1992)**; Facebook → **Meta (2021)**.
- Bangladesh Bank: crypto **not authorised** in Bangladesh.
