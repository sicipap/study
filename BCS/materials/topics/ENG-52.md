# Word formation: prefix & suffix

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask "The noun form of *decide* is…", "The adjective of *courage* is…", "The verb of *strong* is…", and "Which prefix makes the opposite of *regular*?". Knowing the common prefixes and suffixes answers these quickly.

## A. Prefixes (added at the beginning)
### Negative prefixes 🔥
| Prefix | Rule / meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **un-** | not | unhappy, unable, unfair |
| **in-** | not | inactive, incomplete, invisible |
| **im-** | before **b, m, p** | impossible, immortal, imbalance, impatient |
| **il-** | before **l** | illegal, illiterate, illogical |
| **ir-** | before **r** | irregular, irrelevant, irresponsible |
| **dis-** | not / opposite | dishonest, disagree, disappear |
| **non-** | not | non-violence, nonsense, non-resident |
| **mis-** | wrongly | misunderstand, mislead, misuse |
| **mal-** | badly | malnutrition, malpractice, malfunction |
| **a- / an-** | without | amoral, atheist, anarchy |
| **anti-** | against | antisocial, antibiotic, anticlimax |
| **counter-** | against, opposite | counterattack, counterpart |

## B. Suffixes (added at the end)
| Forms | Suffixes | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Nouns** | -ness, -ment, -tion/-sion, -ity, -hood, -ship, -dom, -ism, -ance/-ence, -er/-or, -ist, -ee | kindness, development, decision, ability, childhood, friendship, freedom, heroism, importance, teacher, scientist, employee |
| **Adjectives** | -ful, -less, -able/-ible, -ous, -ive, -al, -ic, -ish, -y, -ly (adjective) | useful, careless, readable, dangerous, active, national, historic, childish, cloudy, friendly |
| **Verbs** | -ize/-ise, -en, -fy, -ate | modernize, strengthen, beautify, activate |
| **Adverbs** | -ly, -wise, -ward(s) | quickly, clockwise, backward |

## Quick revision
- **il-** before l, **ir-** before r, **im-** before b/m/p.
- **en-** makes verbs: enlarge, enrich, endanger, encourage.
- **-en** makes verbs: strengthen, widen, deepen, lengthen.
- **strong → strength, wide → width, deep → depth, high → height**.
- **pronounce → pronunciation**; **maintain → maintenance**.
- **die → death → dead**; **lose → loss**; **choose → choice**.
- **poor → poverty**; **humble → humility**; **wise → wisdom**.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The noun form of "strong" is:
(a) strongly (b) strengthen (c) strength (d) strongness

**2.** The opposite of "regular" is:
(a) irregular (b) unregular (c) inregular (d) disregular

**3.** The verb form of "courage" is:
(a) courageous (b) encourage (c) couraging (d) courageously

**4.** The noun form of "pronounce" is:
(a) pronouncement (b) pronounciation (c) pronouncing (d) pronunciation

**5.** The noun form of "poor" is:
(a) poorness (b) poorly (c) poverty (d) impoverish

**6.** Which prefix means "half"?
(a) semi- (b) multi- (c) trans- (d) poly-

**7.** The verb form of "wide" is:
(a) width (b) widely (c) widest (d) widen

**8.** The noun form of "decide" is:
(a) decisive (b) decision (c) decidedly (d) decider

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | strong becomes strength; strengthen is the verb |
| 2 | a | ir- before words starting with r |
| 3 | b | en- + courage = encourage |
| 4 | d | pronunciation has no "o" after "n" |
| 5 | c | poor becomes poverty |
| 6 | a | semi- = half, as in semicircle |
| 7 | d | -en forms verbs: widen |
| 8 | b | decide becomes decision |
