# Mineral resources: gas fields, coal, limestone

> **Why it matters:** "Which is the first/largest gas field?", "Where is the only coal mine?", "Where is white clay found?" — these location-type questions repeat in almost every exam.

## Natural gas (প্রাকৃতিক গ্যাস) 🔥🔥
- Natural gas is Bangladesh's **main commercial energy source**; mostly **methane**. The **largest user is the power sector**.
- **First gas field discovered:** **হরিপুর, Sylhet (1955)**.
- **First gas production:** **ছাতক (1957/1960)**, supplied to the Chhatak cement factory.
- **Largest gas field by reserves:** **তিতাস (Brahmanbaria)**, discovered **1962**.
- **Largest producer today:** **বিবিয়ানা (Habiganj)**, operated by **Chevron**.
- **First offshore gas field:** **সাঙ্গু** (Bay of Bengal, discovered 1996); production has stopped.
- **Bhola:** **শাহবাজপুর** gas field (1995) and later fields in Bhola.
- **Total gas fields:** **29** according to Petrobangla (older books say 26–28).
- **National Energy Security Day (জাতীয় জ্বালানি নিরাপত্তা দিবস):** **9 August**. On **9 August 1975** the government bought **5 gas fields** from **Shell**: **Titas, Bakhrabad, Habiganj, Rashidpur, Kailashtila**.
- **LNG import** began in **2018** through a floating terminal (FSRU) at **Moheshkhali, Cox's Bazar**.

## Oil (খনিজ তেল)
- **Only oil field:** **হরিপুর, Sylhet** (discovered **1986**); production stopped in the 1990s.
- **Only refinery:** **Eastern Refinery**, Patenga, Chattogram (**1968**).

## Organisations
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **Petrobangla** (Bangladesh Oil, Gas and Mineral Corporation) | Apex state body for oil, gas, minerals |
| **BAPEX** (1989) | State exploration company |
| **BPC** (Bangladesh Petroleum Corporation) | Imports and distributes petroleum |
| **GSB** (Geological Survey of Bangladesh) | Finds minerals |

## Coal (কয়লা) 🔥🔥
- Bangladeshi coal is high-quality **bituminous** coal of the **Gondwana** age, found deep in the north-west.

| Coal field | District | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **জামালগঞ্জ** | **Joypurhat** | **Largest reserve**, but very deep |
| **বড়পুকুরিয়া** | **Parbatipur, Dinajpur** | **Only producing coal mine** (since **2005**); feeds the Barapukuria power plant |
| **ফুলবাড়ী** | **Dinajpur** | Open-pit plan dropped after protests (2006) |
| **খালাশপীর** | **Pirganj, Rangpur** | |
| **দীঘিপাড়া** | **Nawabganj, Dinajpur** | |

- **Peat (পিট কয়লা):** Gopalganj, Faridpur, Khulna (low-lying beels such as Baghia and Chanda beel).

## Other minerals 🔥
| Mineral | Where |
|---|---|
| **Limestone (চুনাপাথর)** | **Joypurhat** (largest deposit), **টাকেরঘাট, Sunamganj**, Bagalibazar and Bhangarghat (Sunamganj) |
| **Hard rock / granite (কঠিন শিলা)** | **মধ্যপাড়া, Parbatipur, Dinajpur** — the only hard-rock mine |
| **White clay (চীনামাটি / কেওলিন)** | **বিজয়পুর, Durgapur, Netrokona** |
| **Glass sand (কাচবালি)** | Balijuri (Jamalpur), Shahjibazar (Habiganj), Chauddagram (Cumilla) |
| **Heavy mineral sand** (ilmenite, zircon, rutile, monazite) | **Cox's Bazar beach**; Beach Sand Minerals Exploitation Centre, Cox's Bazar |
| **Iron ore** | **ইসবপুর, Hakimpur, Dinajpur** — found by GSB in **2019** |
| **Salt (লবণ)** | Made from sea water in **Cox's Bazar** |
| **Gravel / stone (পাথর)** | Sylhet (Jaflong, Bholaganj), Panchagarh |

- **Memory hook:** "Dinajpur is the mineral district": Barapukuria (coal), Madhyapara (hard rock), Phulbari and Dighipara (coal), Isabpur (iron).

## Quick revision
- First gas field **Haripur (1955)**; largest **Titas**; top producer **Bibiyana (Chevron)**.
- First offshore gas field **Sangu**.
- 9 August = **Energy Security Day** (5 fields bought from Shell, 1975).
- Only oil field **Haripur**; only refinery **Eastern Refinery**.
- Only coal mine **Barapukuria (Dinajpur)**; largest coal reserve **Jamalganj (Joypurhat)**.
- Hard rock **Madhyapara**; white clay **Bijoypur**; heavy sand **Cox's Bazar**.
- LNG import from **2018** (Moheshkhali).
