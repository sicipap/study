# Arithmetic progression (সমান্তর ধারা)

> **Why it matters:** Arithmetic progression (সমান্তর ধারা) is asked almost every year in BCS and often in bank exams: nth term, number of terms, sum of n terms, sum of odd/even numbers and sums of multiples.

## Key concepts
- An **arithmetic progression (AP)** is a sequence in which each term differs from the previous one by a **fixed number**, the **common difference (সাধারণ অন্তর), d**.
  - 3, 7, 11, 15, … (a = 3, d = 4)
  - 20, 17, 14, … (d = −3)
- **a** = first term (প্রথম পদ), **l** = last term, **n** = number of terms, **Sₙ** = sum of n terms.
- d = any term − its previous term.
- **Arithmetic mean (সমান্তর গড়)** of a and b = (a + b)/2. Three numbers in AP are best taken as a − d, a, a + d.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| 🔥 nth term | **tₙ = a + (n − 1)d** |
| 🔥 Sum of n terms | **Sₙ = n/2 × [2a + (n − 1)d]** |
| Sum when last term is known | Sₙ = n/2 × (a + l) |
| Number of terms | n = (l − a)/d + 1 |
| Sum of first n natural numbers | n(n + 1)/2 |
| Sum of first n odd numbers | n² |
| Sum of first n even numbers | n(n + 1) |
| nth term from sum | tₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁ |
| Given pth and qth terms | d = (t_q − t_p)/(q − p) |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Find the 20th term of 3, 7, 11, …
Steps: a = 3, d = 4. t₂₀ = 3 + 19 × 4 = 79.
**Answer: 79**

**Example 2.** Find the sum of the first 20 terms of 3, 7, 11, …
Steps: S₂₀ = 20/2 × (2 × 3 + 19 × 4) = 10 × 82 = 820.
**Answer: 820**

**Example 3.** How many terms are there in 5, 9, 13, …, 101?
Steps: n = (101 − 5)/4 + 1 = 24 + 1 = 25.
**Answer: 25**

**Example 4.** The 3rd term of an AP is 7 and the 7th term is 19. Find the 10th term.
Steps: 4d = 19 − 7 = 12 → d = 3; a = 7 − 2 × 3 = 1. t₁₀ = 1 + 9 × 3 = 28.
**Answer: 28**

**Example 5.** Find the sum of all two-digit numbers divisible by 3.
Steps: 12, 15, …, 99. n = (99 − 12)/3 + 1 = 30. S = 30/2 × (12 + 99) = 15 × 111 = 1665.
**Answer: 1665**

## Common traps
- Using n instead of (n − 1) in the nth-term formula.
- Counting terms as (l − a)/d without the **+1**.
- Common difference can be negative (20, 17, 14 → d = −3).
- The sum 1 + 3 + 5 + … + 99 has 50 terms (not 99), so the sum is 50² = 2500.

## Quick revision
- tₙ = a + (n − 1)d.
- Sₙ = n/2[2a + (n − 1)d] = n/2(a + l).
- n = (l − a)/d + 1.
- 1 + 2 + … + n = n(n + 1)/2.
- Odd numbers: n²; even numbers: n(n + 1).
- tₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The 10th term of 2, 5, 8, … is:
(a) 32 (b) 29 (c) 30 (d) 26

**2.** The common difference of 7, 3, −1, … is:
(a) 4 (b) −3 (c) −4 (d) 3

**3.** How many terms are there in 7, 13, 19, …, 205?
(a) 33 (b) 35 (c) 32 (d) 34

**4.** 2 + 4 + 6 + … + 100 = ?
(a) 2550 (b) 2500 (c) 5050 (d) 2600

**5.** The sum of the first 15 terms of 5, 8, 11, … is:
(a) 375 (b) 405 (c) 390 (d) 420

**6.** The 4th term of an AP is 11 and the 9th term is 26. The 15th term is:
(a) 41 (b) 44 (c) 47 (d) 50

**7.** The arithmetic mean of 12 and 30 is:
(a) 18 (b) 20 (c) 42 (d) 21

**8.** Which term of 3, 8, 13, … is 78?
(a) 15th (b) 16th (c) 17th (d) 14th

**9.** The sum of all numbers from 1 to 100 that are divisible by 5 is:
(a) 1050 (b) 1000 (c) 1100 (d) 1005

**10.** If the sum of the first n terms of an AP is n² + 2n, its 5th term is:
(a) 35 (b) 9 (c) 13 (d) 11

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | 2 + 9 × 3 = 29 |
| 2 | c | 3 − 7 = −4 |
| 3 | d | (205 − 7)/6 + 1 = 33 + 1 = 34 |
| 4 | a | n(n + 1) with n = 50 → 2550 |
| 5 | c | 15/2 × (10 + 14 × 3) = 15/2 × 52 = 390 |
| 6 | b | 5d = 15 → d = 3, a = 2; 2 + 14 × 3 = 44 |
| 7 | d | (12 + 30)/2 = 21 |
| 8 | b | (78 − 3)/5 + 1 = 16 |
| 9 | a | 5 + 10 + … + 100: 20 terms; 20/2 × 105 = 1050 |
| 10 | d | S₅ − S₄ = 35 − 24 = 11 |
