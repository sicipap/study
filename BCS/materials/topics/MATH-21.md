# Quadratic equations & roots

> **Why it matters:** Quadratic equations (দ্বিঘাত সমীকরণ) appear in BCS and bank papers as: solve the equation, sum/product of roots, nature of roots (discriminant), forming an equation from its roots, and finding k for equal roots.

## Key concepts
- Standard form: **ax² + bx + c = 0**, where a ≠ 0. It has **two roots (মূল)**.
- **Methods:** factorization (middle-term split), completing the square, or the formula.
- 🔥 **Quadratic formula:** x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)] / 2a
- 🔥 **Discriminant (নিশ্চায়ক):** D = b² − 4ac. It decides the **nature of the roots**:

| D | Nature of roots |
|---|---|
| D > 0 and a perfect square | Real, unequal, **rational** |
| D > 0, not a perfect square | Real, unequal, **irrational** |
| **D = 0** | Real and **equal** (each = −b/2a) |
| **D < 0** | **No real roots** (imaginary / complex) |

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| Sum of roots α + β | **−b/a** |
| Product of roots αβ | **c/a** |
| Equation with roots α, β | x² − (α + β)x + αβ = 0 |
| α² + β² | (α + β)² − 2αβ |
| 1/α + 1/β | (α + β)/αβ |
| (α − β)² | (α + β)² − 4αβ |
| Equal roots condition | b² = 4ac |
| Roots reciprocal of each other | c = a |
| One root 0 | c = 0 |
| Roots equal in size, opposite sign | b = 0 |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0.
Steps: (x − 2)(x − 3) = 0.
**Answer: x = 2 or 3**

**Example 2.** Find the sum and product of the roots of 2x² − 7x + 3 = 0.
Steps: Sum = −b/a = 7/2. Product = c/a = 3/2.
**Answer: 7/2 and 3/2**

**Example 3.** What is the nature of the roots of x² + 2x + 5 = 0?
Steps: D = 4 − 20 = −16 < 0.
**Answer: No real roots**

**Example 4.** For what value of k does x² − kx + 9 = 0 have equal roots?
Steps: k² − 36 = 0 → k = ±6.
**Answer: k = ±6**

**Example 5.** Form the equation whose roots are 3 and −2.
Steps: Sum 1, product −6 → x² − x − 6 = 0.
**Answer: x² − x − 6 = 0**

**Example 6.** If α and β are the roots of x² − 5x + 4 = 0, find α² + β².
Steps: α + β = 5, αβ = 4. 25 − 8 = 17.
**Answer: 17**

## Common traps
- Sum of roots is **−b/a** (note the minus sign).
- In x² = 5x, do **not** divide by x — you lose the root x = 0. Write x(x − 5) = 0.
- "Equal roots" gives k = **±** value; check whether the options include both signs.
- a must not be 0, otherwise the equation is linear, not quadratic.

## Quick revision
- x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)]/2a.
- D = b² − 4ac; D = 0 equal, D < 0 no real roots.
- α + β = −b/a; αβ = c/a.
- Equation: x² − (sum)x + product = 0.
- α² + β² = (α + β)² − 2αβ.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The roots of x² − 7x + 10 = 0 are:
(a) 2, 5 (b) −2, −5 (c) 1, 10 (d) −2, 5

**2.** The sum of the roots of 3x² + 6x − 9 = 0 is:
(a) 2 (b) −2 (c) −3 (d) 3

**3.** The product of the roots of 2x² − 5x − 12 = 0 is:
(a) 6 (b) 5/2 (c) −6 (d) −12

**4.** The roots of x² − 4x + 4 = 0 are:
(a) real and unequal (b) imaginary (c) irrational (d) real and equal

**5.** For what value of k does 4x² + kx + 9 = 0 have equal roots?
(a) ±12 (b) ±6 (c) ±36 (d) ±9

**6.** The quadratic equation whose roots are 4 and −3 is:
(a) x² + x − 12 = 0 (b) x² − x − 12 = 0 (c) x² − 7x + 12 = 0 (d) x² + 7x − 12 = 0

**7.** If α and β are the roots of x² − 6x + 8 = 0, then 1/α + 1/β = ?
(a) 4/3 (b) 8/6 (c) 3/4 (d) 1/6

**8.** One root of x² − 5x + k = 0 is 2. The other root is:
(a) 3 (b) −3 (c) 7 (d) 6

**9.** The equation 2x² + 3x + 4 = 0 has:
(a) two equal real roots (b) no real roots (c) two rational roots (d) two irrational roots

**10.** The solution of x² = 5x is:
(a) x = 5 only (b) x = 0 only (c) x = 0 or 5 (d) x = ±√5
