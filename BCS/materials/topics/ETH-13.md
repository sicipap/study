# Social justice, public interest, conflict of interest

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask what social justice means, Rawls's theory of justice, Aristotle's types of justice, where social justice appears in the Constitution of Bangladesh, what "public interest" and public interest litigation are, and how a civil servant should handle a conflict of interest.

## Justice and social justice 🔥
- **Justice (ন্যায়বিচার/ন্যায়)** comes from the Latin **"jus"**, meaning right or law.
- **Social justice (সামাজিক ন্যায়বিচার)** means a **fair distribution of wealth, opportunities and privileges** in society, so that no one is denied basic needs or opportunities because of birth, class, religion, sex or ethnicity.
- Key elements: **equality of opportunity**, **fair distribution of resources**, **protection of the weak**, **human dignity**, **participation**.
- **World Day of Social Justice: 20 February** (declared by the UN General Assembly in 2007; observed from 2009).

## Thinkers on justice 🔥
| Thinker | Idea |
|---|---|
| **Plato** (*The Republic*) | Justice = each class doing its own proper work |
| **Aristotle** | **Distributive justice** (sharing goods and honours according to merit) and **corrective (rectificatory) justice** (setting right wrongs in dealings) |
| **John Rawls**, ***A Theory of Justice* (1971)** | **Justice as fairness**; principles chosen in the **original position** behind a **"veil of ignorance"** |
| **Amartya Sen**, *The Idea of Justice* (2009) | Focus on removing real injustices and expanding people's **capabilities** |

### Rawls's two principles of justice 🔥
1. **Equal basic liberties** for everyone.
2. Social and economic inequalities are allowed only if:
   - (i) positions are open to all under **fair equality of opportunity**, and
   - (ii) they give the **greatest benefit to the least advantaged** (the **difference principle**).

## Social justice in the Constitution of Bangladesh 🔥
| Place | Content |
|---|---|
| **Preamble** | A society with the rule of law, fundamental human rights and freedom, **equality and justice, political, economic and social**, for all citizens |
| **Article 8** | Fundamental principles of state policy: **nationalism, socialism, democracy and secularism** (socialism here aims at a just society free from exploitation) |
| **Article 14** | **Emancipation of peasants and workers** from all forms of exploitation |
| **Article 15** | Provision of **basic necessities** (food, clothing, shelter, education, medical care) |
| **Article 17** | Free and compulsory education |
| **Article 19** | **Equality of opportunity** for all citizens |
| **Article 20** | Work as a right and duty |

> 🔄 Update: article wording refers to the Constitution as it stood before the 2024–26 reform process; check for later amendments.

## Public interest (জনস্বার্থ) 🔥
- **Public interest** means the **common good or welfare of the general public**, as opposed to the private interest of a person or group.
- A public official must always **put public interest above private interest**.
- **Public Interest Litigation (PIL, জনস্বার্থে মামলা):** a case filed in court to protect the public interest, often by someone not personally harmed. In Bangladesh, PIL gained ground with the **Dr. Mohiuddin Farooque v. Bangladesh** case (**1996**, the FAP-20 flood action plan case), where the Appellate Division widened **locus standi** (the right to bring a case) under **Article 102**.
- **Whistleblower protection:** জনস্বার্থ সংশ্লিষ্ট তথ্য প্রকাশ (সুরক্ষা প্রদান) আইন, **2011**, protects people who disclose wrongdoing in the public interest.

## Conflict of interest (স্বার্থের দ্বন্দ্ব) 🔥
- **Definition (OECD, 2003):** a conflict between the **public duty and the private interests** of a public official, in which the official's private interests could **improperly influence** the performance of official duties.
- **Types:**
| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Actual** | The conflict exists now | An officer awards a tender to his brother's firm |
| **Apparent / perceived** | It looks like a conflict, even if none exists | An officer judges a case involving a close friend |
| **Potential** | It may arise in future | An officer owns shares in a company that may apply for a licence |

- **Common sources:** family and relatives, business interests and shares, gifts and hospitality, outside jobs, post-retirement jobs ("**revolving door**"), political links.
- **How to manage it:**
  1. **Disclose** (declare the interest in writing).
  2. **Recuse** (withdraw from the decision).
  3. **Divest** (give up the interest, e.g. sell shares).
  4. Follow **gift rules** and **asset declarations**.
  5. Observe a **cooling-off period** before joining a private firm you regulated.
- **Nepotism** (favouring relatives) and **cronyism** (favouring friends) are results of unmanaged conflicts of interest.

## Quick revision
- Justice ← Latin "jus"; social justice = fair distribution of wealth and opportunities.
- World Day of Social Justice: 20 February.
- Aristotle: distributive and corrective justice.
- Rawls (1971): justice as fairness, veil of ignorance, difference principle.
- Constitution: Preamble (political, economic and social justice), Articles 14, 15, 19.
- PIL in Bangladesh: Mohiuddin Farooque case (1996), Article 102.
- Conflict of interest: actual, apparent, potential; manage by disclosure, recusal, divestment.
