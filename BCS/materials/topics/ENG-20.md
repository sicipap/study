# Romantic: Byron, Shelley, Keats

> **Why it matters:** The younger Romantics are asked through famous lines ("A thing of beauty is a joy for ever", "If Winter comes, can Spring be far behind?"), epithets ("Poet of beauty", "revolutionary poet"), *Adonais*, *Don Juan* and their early deaths.

## Second-generation Romantics
All three died young and abroad: **Keats (25, Rome, 1821)**, **Shelley (29, drowned in Italy, 1822)**, **Byron (36, Missolonghi, Greece, 1824)**.

## Lord Byron (George Gordon, 1788–1824) 🔥
- Aristocrat, rebel, satirist; the **"Byronic hero"** — proud, moody, mysterious, defiant outsider.
- After *Childe Harold* I–II (1812) he said: "**I awoke one morning and found myself famous.**"
- Died at **Missolonghi** while helping the **Greek War of Independence**; a national hero in Greece.

## Percy Bysshe Shelley (1792–1822) 🔥
- Expelled from **Oxford** for ***The Necessity of Atheism* (1811)**.
- Epithets: **"Revolutionary poet"** / poet of revolt and idealism; **Matthew Arnold** called him "**a beautiful and ineffectual angel, beating in the void his luminous wings in vain**".
- Wife: **Mary Shelley**, author of ***Frankenstein* (1818)**.
- **Drowned** in the Gulf of **La Spezia**, Italy, in **1822**; his body was cremated on the beach.

## John Keats (1795–1821) 🔥
- Son of a stable-keeper in London; trained as an **apothecary/surgeon**.
- Epithets: **"Poet of beauty"**, "worshipper of beauty", "poet of the senses" (sensuousness).
- His critical ideas are in his **letters**: "**Negative Capability**" (1817) — the ability to live with "uncertainties, mysteries, doubts" — and the "**egotistical sublime**" (about Wordsworth).
- Died of **tuberculosis** in **Rome** in 1821, aged **25**. His epitaph: "**Here lies one whose name was writ in water.**"

## Quick revision
- 🔥 "**A thing of beauty is a joy for ever**" — Keats, *Endymion*.
- 🔥 "**Beauty is truth, truth beauty**" — Keats, *Ode on a Grecian Urn*.
- 🔥 "**If Winter comes, can Spring be far behind?**" — Shelley, *Ode to the West Wind*.
- ***Adonais*** — Shelley's elegy on **Keats**.
- "**Poets are the unacknowledged legislators of the world**" — Shelley.
- 🔥 ***Don Juan*** — Byron, **ottava rima**; Byron died at **Missolonghi**.
- Keats = "**Poet of beauty**"; "**Negative Capability**".
- "Beautiful and ineffectual angel" — **Arnold** on Shelley.
