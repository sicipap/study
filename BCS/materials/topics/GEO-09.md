# Disaster management in BD: cycle, SOD, early warning

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the stages of the disaster management cycle, what the Standing Orders on Disaster (SOD) are and their years, who heads the national and district committees, the Disaster Management Act 2012, the Cyclone Preparedness Programme, and the Sendai Framework.

## Disaster management cycle (দুর্যোগ ব্যবস্থাপনা চক্র) 🔥🔥
Disaster management is a **continuous cycle** of activities **before, during and after** a disaster.

| Stage | Phase | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| **Before** | 🔥 **Mitigation / prevention (প্রশমন)** | Reduce the **long-term risk**: embankments, cyclone shelters, building codes, afforestation, land-use planning |
| **Before** | 🔥 **Preparedness (প্রস্তুতি)** | Get ready: **early warning**, evacuation plans, drills, stockpiling food and medicine, volunteer training |
| **During / just after** | 🔥 **Response (সাড়া প্রদান)** | **Rescue**, evacuation, emergency relief (food, water, shelter), first aid |
| **After** | 🔥 **Recovery (পুনরুদ্ধার)** | **Rehabilitation (পুনর্বাসন)** and **reconstruction (পুনর্গঠন)**; "build back better" |

- Memory hook: **M-P-R-R** — **M**itigation → **P**reparedness → **R**esponse → **R**ecovery.
- **Risk reduction** (mitigation + preparedness) is now given more importance than relief alone — the approach Bangladesh follows (**comprehensive disaster management**).

## Legal and policy framework in Bangladesh 🔥
| Instrument | Year / fact |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Standing Orders on Disaster (SOD) — দুর্যোগ বিষয়ক স্থায়ী আদেশাবলি** | First issued **1997**; revised **2010**; latest **2019**. Sets out the **roles and responsibilities** of ministries, departments, committees (national to union level), NGOs and volunteers **before, during and after** disasters |
| 🔥 **Disaster Management Act 2012 (দুর্যোগ ব্যবস্থাপনা আইন, ২০১২)** | Gave legal basis to disaster management; created the **Department of Disaster Management (DDM)** |
| **National Disaster Management Policy** | **2015** |
| **National Plan for Disaster Management (NPDM)** | Five-year plans, e.g. **2021–2025** |

## Institutions 🔥
| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **Ministry of Disaster Management and Relief (MoDMR)** | Lead ministry for disasters |
| **Department of Disaster Management (DDM)** | Implementing agency under MoDMR (formed under the 2012 Act) |
| **National Disaster Management Council (NDMC)** | 🔥 **Highest policy body**, headed by the **Head of Government (Prime Minister)** |
| **Inter-Ministerial Disaster Management Coordination Committee (IMDMCC)** | Headed by the **Minister for Disaster Management and Relief**; coordinates ministries |
| 🔥 **District Disaster Management Committee (DDMC)** | Headed by the **Deputy Commissioner (DC)** |
| **Upazila, Pourashava and City Corporation DMCs** | Coordinate at local level (City Corporation DMC headed by the **Mayor**) |
| 🔥 **Union Disaster Management Committee (UDMC)** | Headed by the **Union Parishad Chairman** |
| **National Emergency Operation Centre (NEOC)** | Round-the-clock coordination during disasters |

## Early warning systems 🔥🔥
| Agency / system | Job |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD)** | **Weather and cyclone forecasts and warnings**; runs the **Storm Warning Centre (SWC)** and issues port signals |
| 🔥 **Flood Forecasting and Warning Centre (FFWC)** | **Flood forecasts**; under the **Bangladesh Water Development Board (BWDB)**; set up in **1972** |
| **SPARRSO** (Space Research and Remote Sensing Organization) | Satellite images for cyclones, floods and crops |
| 🔥 **Cyclone Preparedness Programme (CPP)** | Joint programme of the **Government and the Bangladesh Red Crescent Society**, started in **1972** after the 1970 cyclone. Tens of thousands of trained **volunteers (about half women)** spread warnings using **megaphones, sirens and signal flags**, and help evacuate people to shelters |
| **1090** | **Free phone (IVR) number** to hear disaster early-warning messages |
| **999** | National emergency service (fire, police, ambulance) |
| **Fire Service and Civil Defence** | Rescue in fires, building collapses, floods |

- **Cyclone shelters (ঘূর্ণিঝড় আশ্রয়কেন্দ্র):** usually **multipurpose** — used as schools in normal times. **Killa (কিল্লা):** raised earthen mounds to protect **livestock**.
- Bangladesh's cyclone deaths have fallen sharply (about 3–5 lakh in 1970 → about 1.4 lakh in 1991 → a few thousand in Sidr 2007 → very few in recent cyclones), which is widely cited as a **global model of community-based preparedness**.

## International frameworks 🔥
| Framework | Year / place | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Yokohama Strategy** | 1994, Yokohama, Japan | First global DRR strategy |
| **Hyogo Framework for Action** | **2005–2015**, adopted in **Kobe (Hyogo), Japan** | Building resilience of nations and communities |
| 🔥 **Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction** | **2015–2030**, adopted in **Sendai, Japan (March 2015)** | **4 priorities** and **7 global targets** — reduce disaster deaths, affected people, economic losses and damage to infrastructure; more early warning |

- **UNDRR** (UN Office for Disaster Risk Reduction) leads global DRR work.
- **13 October:** International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction.
- **10 March:** National Disaster Preparedness Day in Bangladesh.

## Key terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Vulnerability (ঝুঁকিপূর্ণতা/অরক্ষিততা)** | How easily people or assets can be harmed |
| **Resilience (সহনশীলতা)** | Ability to resist, absorb and recover from a disaster |
| **Capacity** | Strengths and resources available to cope |
| **DRR** | Disaster Risk Reduction |
| **CBDRM** | Community-Based Disaster Risk Management |
| **Contingency plan** | Advance plan for a specific expected emergency |
| **Evacuation** | Moving people from danger to safe places |

## Quick revision
- Cycle: **Mitigation → Preparedness → Response → Recovery**.
- Early warning and drills = **preparedness**; embankments/building codes = **mitigation**; rescue = **response**; rehabilitation = **recovery**.
- SOD: **1997, 2010, 2019** (latest).
- Disaster Management Act: **2012**; policy: **2015**.
- NDMC headed by the **Prime Minister/Head of Government**; DDMC by the **DC**; UDMC by the **UP Chairman**.
- Cyclone warnings: **BMD**; flood warnings: **FFWC (BWDB)**.
- CPP: **1972**, Government + **Bangladesh Red Crescent**.
- Sendai Framework: **2015–2030**, 7 targets; Hyogo: 2005–2015.

|---|---|---|

| 3 | a | The DC chairs the DDMC |
| 4 | d | Disaster Management Act 2012 |
| 5 | c | FFWC under the BWDB |
