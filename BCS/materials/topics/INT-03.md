# Renaissance, American & French revolutions, Industrial Revolution

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for dates (1776, 1789, 14 July), slogans ("Liberty, Equality, Fraternity", "No taxation without representation"), the authors of key books (*The Social Contract*, *The Spirit of the Laws*, *The Prince*) and the inventors of the Industrial Revolution.

## Renaissance (রেনেসাঁ / নবজাগরণ) 🔥
- **"Renaissance"** is a French word meaning **"rebirth"**: the revival of classical Greek and Roman learning, art and humanism in Europe, roughly the **14th–16th centuries**.
- 🔥 It began in **Italy**, especially **Florence**, supported by rich patrons such as the **Medici family**.
- The **fall of Constantinople (1453)** sent Greek scholars and manuscripts to Italy and sped it up.
- **Printing press** with movable type: **Johannes Gutenberg**, c. **1440**, Mainz (Germany). It spread new ideas fast.

| Person | Known for |
|---|---|
| **Petrarch** | **Father of humanism** |
| **Dante** | *Divine Comedy* |
| 🔥 **Leonardo da Vinci** | ***Mona Lisa***, ***The Last Supper*** |
| **Michelangelo** | **Sistine Chapel ceiling**, statue of ***David*** |
| **Raphael** | *The School of Athens* |
| 🔥 **Niccolò Machiavelli** | ***The Prince*** (1513) |
| **Nicolaus Copernicus** | **Heliocentric theory** (Sun at the centre), 1543 |
| **Galileo Galilei** | Telescope observations supporting Copernicus |
| **William Shakespeare** | English Renaissance drama |

**Related movements**
- **Reformation:** **Martin Luther** posted his **95 Theses** in **1517** (Wittenberg, Germany) against the sale of indulgences → **Protestantism**. **Henry VIII** broke with Rome and founded the **Church of England** (1534).
- **Age of Discovery:** **Bartolomeu Dias** rounded the **Cape of Good Hope** (1488); **Columbus** reached the Americas (**1492**); **Vasco da Gama** reached **Calicut, India (1498)**; **Magellan's** expedition first sailed round the world (1519–1522).

## American Revolution (আমেরিকান বিপ্লব) 🔥
- **13 British colonies** on the east coast of North America rebelled against Britain.
- Causes: taxes without consent, e.g. the **Stamp Act (1765)**. 🔥 Slogan: **"No taxation without representation."**
- **Boston Tea Party:** **16 December 1773**, colonists dumped British tea into Boston harbour.
- War began at **Lexington and Concord (April 1775)**.
- **Thomas Paine's** pamphlet ***Common Sense*** (1776) urged independence.
- 🔥 **Declaration of Independence:** **4 July 1776**, drafted mainly by **Thomas Jefferson**. 4 July is US Independence Day.
- **Saratoga (1777)**: turning point; France then joined the colonists. The British surrendered at **Yorktown (1781)**.
- **Treaty of Paris (1783):** Britain recognised US independence.
- **US Constitution:** written in **1787** (in force **1789**) — the **oldest written national constitution still in use**. **George Washington** became the **first President (1789)**. The **Bill of Rights** (first 10 amendments) came in **1791**.

## French Revolution (ফরাসি বিপ্লব) 🔥
- Began in **1789**, under King **Louis XVI** and Queen **Marie Antoinette**.
- Causes: royal debt and absolute monarchy; privileges of the clergy (First Estate) and nobles (Second Estate); heavy taxes on the Third Estate; Enlightenment ideas.

| Philosopher | Book / idea |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Rousseau** | ***The Social Contract*** (1762): "Man is born free, and everywhere he is in chains"; general will |
| 🔥 **Montesquieu** | ***The Spirit of the Laws*** (1748): **separation of powers** |
| **Voltaire** | Freedom of speech and religion; attacked the Church |

**Timeline**
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 5 May 1789 | **Estates-General** met |
| 20 June 1789 | **Tennis Court Oath** by the Third Estate (National Assembly) |
| 🔥 **14 July 1789** | **Storming of the Bastille** (prison in Paris) — French **national day** |
| 26 August 1789 | **Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen** |
| 21 January 1793 | **Louis XVI executed** by guillotine |
| 1793–94 | **Reign of Terror** under **Robespierre** |
| 1799 | **Napoleon Bonaparte** seized power (coup of 18 Brumaire) |

- 🔥 Slogan: **"Liberty, Equality, Fraternity" (স্বাধীনতা, সাম্য, মৈত্রী)**.
- **Napoleon:** became **Emperor in 1804**; **Napoleonic Code (1804)**; defeated at **Waterloo (1815)**; died in exile on **St Helena** (1821).
- The **Congress of Vienna (1815)** then redrew Europe's map.

## Industrial Revolution (শিল্প বিপ্লব) 🔥
- 🔥 Began in **Britain (England)** around **1760**, first in the **textile (cotton)** industry. The term was popularised by historian **Arnold Toynbee**.
- Why Britain: plenty of **coal and iron**, colonies (raw materials and markets), capital, and a stable government.

| Invention | Inventor | Year |
|---|---|---|
| Flying shuttle | **John Kay** | 1733 |
| 🔥 **Spinning jenny** | **James Hargreaves** | 1764 |
| Water frame | **Richard Arkwright** | 1769 |
| 🔥 **Improved steam engine** | **James Watt** | 1769 |
| Spinning mule | **Samuel Crompton** | 1779 |
| Power loom | **Edmund Cartwright** | 1785 |
| Cotton gin (USA) | **Eli Whitney** | 1793 |
| 🔥 **Locomotive** "Rocket"; father of railways | **George Stephenson** | 1829 |
| Steel (Bessemer process) | **Henry Bessemer** | 1856 |

- The **first public steam railway** was **Stockton–Darlington (1825)**, England.
- **Thomas Newcomen** built an earlier steam engine (1712); Watt made it efficient.
- **Adam Smith**, ***The Wealth of Nations* (1776)**: father of economics; laissez-faire.
- Effects: factory system, **capitalism**, **urbanisation**, a new working class, child labour, and later **Karl Marx's** critique (*Communist Manifesto*, 1848).
- **Fourth Industrial Revolution (4IR):** term popularised by **Klaus Schwab** of the **World Economic Forum** (2016): AI, robotics, IoT.

## Quick revision
- Renaissance = "rebirth"; began in **Florence, Italy**; *The Prince* by **Machiavelli** (1513).
- Luther's 95 Theses: **1517**; Columbus: **1492**; Vasco da Gama at Calicut: **1498**.
- US Declaration of Independence: **4 July 1776**, **Thomas Jefferson**.
- Bastille stormed: **14 July 1789**; slogan **Liberty, Equality, Fraternity**.
- *The Social Contract*: **Rousseau**; *The Spirit of the Laws*: **Montesquieu**.
- Industrial Revolution began in **Britain** (~1760); steam engine: **James Watt**; spinning jenny: **Hargreaves**; locomotive: **Stephenson**.
