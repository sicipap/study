# Embedded figures / spatial ability

> **Why it matters:** Embedded figure (লুক্কায়িত চিত্র) questions ask you to find a simple shape hidden inside a complex figure. Spatial-ability questions ask what a shape looks like after rotation, which pieces fit together, or how many blocks are in a stack. The rules below let you solve them even when described in words.

## Embedded figures: the rules 🔥
1. The hidden figure must appear **at the same size** (not bigger or smaller).
2. It must appear in the **same orientation**: not rotated or flipped, unless the question allows it.
3. **All its lines** must be present in the complex figure; extra lines around it do not matter.
4. Tip: find the **most unusual part** of the simple figure (a sharp angle, a curve, a short line) and look for it first.

## Rotation vs reflection 🔥
| Change | What happens | Letter example |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation 90° | Figure turns a quarter | **N** turned 90° looks like **Z** |
| Rotation 180° | Figure turns upside down **and** left–right | **b** → **q**; **d** → **p**; **6** → **9** |
| Mirror (left–right flip) | Only left and right swap | **b** → **d**; **p** → **q** |
| Water (top–bottom flip) | Only top and bottom swap | **b** → **p**; **d** → **q** |

- A rotated figure can always be turned back; a **mirror image cannot** be produced by rotation alone (a left hand cannot be rotated into a right hand).
- Capital letters unchanged by a 180° rotation: **H, I, N, O, S, X, Z**.

## Combining shapes
| Pieces joined | Result |
|---|---|
| Two identical right-angled isosceles triangles, along the long side (hypotenuse) | **Square** |
| Two identical right-angled triangles, along the hypotenuse, one turned round | **Rectangle** |
| Two identical equilateral triangles, along one side | **Rhombus** |
| Six identical equilateral triangles round one point | **Regular hexagon** |
| Two identical squares side by side | **Rectangle** (2 : 1) |

## Counting blocks in a stack
- Count **layer by layer**, from the top down.
- **Hidden blocks** under visible ones are counted if the stack must be supported (blocks cannot float).
- A solid box of l × w × h unit cubes has **l × w × h** cubes.
- A staircase with steps of 1, 2, 3 … n cubes has 1 + 2 + … + n = **n(n + 1) ÷ 2** cubes.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** A solid block is 3 cubes long, 2 cubes wide and 2 cubes high. How many unit cubes does it contain?
3 × 2 × 2 = **12**.

**Example 2.** A staircase has columns of 1, 2, 3 and 4 cubes. How many cubes in all?
4 × 5 ÷ 2 = **10**.

**Example 3.** Two identical equilateral triangles are joined along one side. What shape is formed?
All four sides are equal, but the angles are 60° and 120°, not 90° → **rhombus**.

**Example 4.** How does the small letter "b" look after a rotation of 180°?
It turns upside down and left–right → **q**.

**Example 5.** Which of these can appear hidden in a figure made of a large square and its two diagonals: a small right-angled triangle, a circle, a pentagon, a hexagon?
The diagonals cut the square into triangles → **right-angled triangle**.

## Quick revision
- Embedded figure: same size, same orientation, all lines present.
- Rotation 180°: b → q, d → p, 6 → 9.
- Mirror: b → d; water: b → p.
- A mirror image cannot be made by rotation alone.
- Two isosceles right triangles on the hypotenuse → square.
- Two equilateral triangles → rhombus; six → regular hexagon.
- Staircase of n steps: n(n + 1)/2 cubes.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** In an embedded figure question, the simple figure must appear in the complex figure with the same:
(a) colour only (b) size and orientation (c) number of lines doubled (d) mirror image

**2.** Two identical right-angled isosceles triangles are joined along their longest sides. What shape is formed?
(a) Rectangle with sides 1 : 2 (b) Trapezium (c) Square (d) Pentagon

**3.** Two identical equilateral triangles are joined along one side. What shape is formed?
(a) Rhombus (b) Square (c) Hexagon (d) Trapezium

**4.** Six identical equilateral triangles are placed round a common point with no gaps. What shape do they form?
(a) Circle (b) Pentagon (c) Octagon (d) Regular hexagon

**5.** A solid block is 3 cubes long, 2 cubes wide and 2 cubes high. How many unit cubes does it contain?
(a) 12 (b) 10 (c) 7 (d) 14

**6.** How does the small letter "b" look after it is rotated through 180°?
(a) d (b) p (c) q (d) b

**7.** A staircase is built of unit cubes: the columns have 1, 2, 3 and 4 cubes. How many cubes are used in all?
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 12 (d) 10

**8.** Which capital letter looks the same after a rotation of 180°?
(a) N (b) R (c) E (d) K

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | The hidden figure keeps its size and orientation. |
| 2 | c | Two isosceles right triangles on the hypotenuse make a square. |
| 3 | a | Four equal sides with 60° and 120° angles: a rhombus. |
| 4 | d | 6 × 60° = 360°, forming a regular hexagon. |
| 5 | a | 3 × 2 × 2 = 12. |
| 6 | c | A 180° rotation turns b into q. |
| 7 | d | 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10. |
| 8 | a | N turned upside down is still N. |
