# Time, speed & distance

> **Why it matters:** Time, speed and distance (সময়, দূরত্ব ও গতিবেগ) is asked in almost every job exam: unit conversion, average speed, meeting/overtaking, and "late/early" problems. It is also the base for trains and boats.

## Key concepts
- **Distance = Speed × Time.** Speed = Distance ÷ Time. Time = Distance ÷ Speed.
- 🔥 **km/h → m/s: multiply by 5/18.** m/s → km/h: multiply by 18/5. (1 km/h = 5/18 m/s.)
- For a fixed distance, **speed and time are inversely proportional**: if the speed ratio is a : b, the time ratio is b : a.
- **Average speed** = total distance ÷ total time (never the simple average of the speeds, unless the **times** are equal).
- **Relative speed (আপেক্ষিক গতিবেগ):**
  - Moving **towards each other** (opposite directions): add speeds.
  - Moving in the **same direction**: subtract speeds.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Situation | Formula |
|---|---|
| Unit change | km/h × 5/18 = m/s; m/s × 18/5 = km/h |
| 🔥 Average speed, equal distances at x and y | **2xy/(x + y)** |
| Equal times at x and y | (x + y)/2 |
| Meet when approaching, gap D | D ÷ (x + y) |
| Catch up, gap D | D ÷ (x − y) |
| Speed ratio a : b | Time ratio b : a |
| Goes at new speed = (a/b) of usual and is t minutes late | Usual time = a × t/(b − a) … e.g. 3/4 speed, 20 min late → 3 × 20 = 60 min |
| Late at speed x by t₁, early at speed y by t₂ | Distance = xy/(y − x) × (t₁ + t₂) (times in hours) |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Convert 72 km/h into m/s.
Steps: 72 × 5/18 = 20.
**Answer: 20 m/s**

**Example 2.** A man goes to a place at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. Find his average speed.
Steps: 2 × 40 × 60/(40 + 60) = 4800/100 = 48.
**Answer: 48 km/h**

**Example 3.** Walking at 5 km/h a student is 10 minutes late; at 6 km/h he is 5 minutes early. Find the distance.
Steps: Time difference = 15 min = 1/4 h. d/5 − d/6 = 1/4 → d/30 = 1/4 → d = 7.5.
**Answer: 7.5 km**

**Example 4.** Two places are 100 km apart. Two people start from them towards each other at 30 km/h and 20 km/h. When will they meet?
Steps: Relative speed 50 km/h → 100/50 = 2.
**Answer: After 2 hours**

**Example 5.** Walking at 3/4 of his usual speed, a man is 20 minutes late. Find his usual time.
Steps: New time = 4/3 of usual. Extra = 1/3 of usual = 20 → usual = 60.
**Answer: 60 minutes**

## Common traps
- Taking (40 + 60)/2 = 50 as average speed. For equal **distances** use 2xy/(x + y) = 48.
- Mixing units: convert km/h and metres/seconds before calculating.
- Same direction → **subtract** speeds; opposite → **add**.
- Late/early problems: the time difference is late **plus** early (10 + 5 = 15 minutes).

## Quick revision
- D = S × T.
- × 5/18 for km/h → m/s.
- Average speed (equal distances) = 2xy/(x + y).
- Towards each other: add; same way: subtract.
- Speed ratio a : b ⇒ time ratio b : a.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** 54 km/h is equal to:
(a) 15 m/s (b) 18 m/s (c) 12 m/s (d) 20 m/s

**2.** 25 m/s is equal to:
(a) 72 km/h (b) 80 km/h (c) 90 km/h (d) 100 km/h

**3.** A bus covers 360 km in 4½ hours. Its speed is:
(a) 75 km/h (b) 80 km/h (c) 90 km/h (d) 72 km/h

**4.** A man goes to his office at 30 km/h and returns at 20 km/h. His average speed is:
(a) 25 km/h (b) 26 km/h (c) 22 km/h (d) 24 km/h

**5.** Two cars 300 km apart start towards each other at 40 km/h and 35 km/h. They meet after:
(a) 3 hours (b) 4 hours (c) 5 hours (d) 7.5 hours

**6.** The speeds of two cars are in the ratio 3 : 4. The ratio of times taken to cover the same distance is:
(a) 4 : 3 (b) 3 : 4 (c) 9 : 16 (d) 16 : 9

**7.** Walking at 4 km/h a man reaches 10 minutes late; at 5 km/h he reaches 5 minutes early. The distance is:
(a) 4 km (b) 6 km (c) 5 km (d) 7.5 km

**8.** Travelling at 5/6 of his usual speed, a man reaches 10 minutes late. His usual time is:
(a) 60 minutes (b) 40 minutes (c) 45 minutes (d) 50 minutes

**9.** A car covers 120 km in 3 hours and the next 180 km in 2 hours. Its average speed is:
(a) 65 km/h (b) 60 km/h (c) 50 km/h (d) 70 km/h

**10.** A thief is 200 m ahead of a policeman. The thief runs at 10 km/h and the policeman at 12 km/h. The policeman catches him in:
(a) 10 minutes (b) 5 minutes (c) 6 minutes (d) 12 minutes

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a | 54 × 5/18 = 15 |
| 2 | c | 25 × 18/5 = 90 |
| 3 | b | 360 ÷ 4.5 = 80 |
| 4 | d | 2 × 30 × 20/50 = 24 |
| 5 | b | 300 ÷ (40 + 35) = 4 |
| 6 | a | Time is inverse of speed → 4 : 3 |
| 7 | c | d/4 − d/5 = 1/4 → d = 5 |
| 8 | d | New time = 6/5 T; T/5 = 10 → T = 50 |
| 9 | b | 300 km ÷ 5 h = 60 |
| 10 | c | Relative speed 2 km/h; 0.2 km ÷ 2 = 0.1 h = 6 min |
