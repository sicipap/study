# Phrases & idioms

> **Why it matters:** Every BCS, bank and NTRCA paper asks 2–4 questions like "The idiom 'a white elephant' means…". The same idioms repeat year after year, so learn the table below thoroughly.

## How to learn
- An **idiom** is a group of words whose meaning cannot be guessed from the individual words.
- Learn by **picture + Bangla meaning**. Many idioms come from myths (Achilles' heel, Herculean task, Midas touch, Gordian knot) or animals (black sheep, dark horse, crocodile tears).

## Quick revision
- **White elephant** = costly but useless.
- **Bolt from the blue** = sudden shock.
- **At sixes and sevens** = in disorder.
- **Crocodile tears** = false sorrow (মায়াকান্না).
- **Red tape** = bureaucratic delay.
- **Achilles' heel** = weak point; **apple of discord** = cause of quarrel.
- **French leave** = leave without permission.
- **Once in a blue moon** = very rarely.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The idiom "a white elephant" means:
(a) a rare animal (b) a costly but useless possession (c) a powerful person (d) a sign of peace

**2.** "At sixes and sevens" means:
(a) in disorder (b) in a hurry (c) very rarely (d) in great danger

**3.** "Crocodile tears" means:
(a) tears of joy (b) deep sorrow (c) false sorrow (d) sudden anger

**4.** "Achilles' heel" means:
(a) a strong point (b) a fast runner (c) a hidden treasure (d) a weak point

**5.** "Red tape" refers to:
(a) official delay and formality (b) a danger signal (c) a political party (d) a ceremony

**6.** "Once in a blue moon" means:
(a) at night (b) very rarely (c) every month (d) never

**7.** "To take French leave" means:
(a) to go to France (b) to take a long holiday (c) to be absent without permission (d) to resign

**8.** "Apple of discord" means:
(a) a dear person (b) a cause of quarrel (c) a forbidden fruit (d) a rare gift

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Costly to maintain but of no use |
| 2 | a | In confusion or disorder |
| 3 | c | Pretended sorrow, মায়াকান্না |
| 4 | d | Achilles was vulnerable only in his heel |
| 5 | a | Bureaucratic delay |
| 6 | b | Very rarely |
| 7 | c | Leave without permission |
| 8 | b | From the golden apple that caused the Trojan War |
