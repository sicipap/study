# Education, health & SDG indicators

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask about education commissions and policies, first universities, disease-elimination milestones (polio, kala-azar, filariasis), ICDDR,B and ORS, and basic SDG facts.

## Education (শিক্ষা) 🔥
| Fact | Answer |
|---|---|
| **First education commission** of Bangladesh | **কুদরাত-এ-খুদা শিক্ষা কমিশন** (formed **1972**, report **1974**) |
| Current education policy | **National Education Policy 2010 (জাতীয় শিক্ষানীতি ২০১০)**; committee headed by **Prof. Kabir Chowdhury** |
| Primary schools nationalised | **1973** (about 36,000 schools); another about 26,000 in **2013** |
| **Compulsory primary education** | **Primary Education (Compulsory) Act 1990**, applied nationwide from **1993** |
| Constitutional basis | **Article 17**: free and compulsory education |
| Textbooks | **NCTB** (National Curriculum and Textbook Board); free textbooks on the first day of the year since 2010 |
| Education boards | **11** (9 general + Madrasah + Technical) |
| **UGC** (University Grants Commission) | **1973** |

## Health (স্বাস্থ্য) 🔥🔥
| Milestone | Date / fact |
|---|---|
| **EPI** (Expanded Programme on Immunization) launched | **7 April 1979** |
| **Polio-free certification** (WHO South-East Asia region) | **27 March 2014** |
| **Maternal & neonatal tetanus eliminated** | **2008** |
| **Lymphatic filariasis (গোদরোগ) eliminated** as a public-health problem | WHO validation **2023** |
| **Kala-azar eliminated** as a public-health problem | WHO validation **October 2023** — **Bangladesh was the first country in the world** to be validated |
| **Community clinics** | Started **1998**; one for about every 6,000 rural people |
| **MDG award** | **2010**, for reducing child mortality (MDG 4) |

- **ICDDR,B** (International Centre for Diarrhoeal Disease Research, Bangladesh), **Mohakhali, Dhaka**: began as the **Cholera Research Laboratory (1960)**; became ICDDR,B in **1978**. It developed and tested **Oral Rehydration Saline (ORS, খাবার স্যালাইন)**. *The Lancet* called ORS **"potentially the most important medical advance of the 20th century"**.
- **Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS)**, Mohakhali; **IEDCR** (disease surveillance).
- Main indicators (approximate, SVRS 2022–23): life expectancy **about 72 years**; maternal and infant mortality have fallen sharply since 1990.

> 🔄 Update: check the latest SVRS figures for life expectancy, maternal mortality (MMR), infant mortality (IMR) and literacy in a newspaper or the Bangladesh Economic Review.

## Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) 🔥🔥
- Adopted at the UN on **25 September 2015** ("Transforming our World: the 2030 Agenda").
- Period: **2016–2030**.
- **17 goals, 169 targets**, about **231 unique indicators**.
- Predecessor: **MDGs** — **8 goals**, **2000–2015**.
- In Bangladesh the **General Economics Division (GED), Planning Commission** leads SDG planning and monitoring; the government publishes SDG progress reports.

| SDG | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1 | **No poverty** |
| 2 | **Zero hunger** |
| 3 | **Good health and well-being** |
| 4 | **Quality education** |
| 5 | **Gender equality** |
| 6 | Clean water and sanitation |
| 7 | Affordable and clean energy |
| 13 | **Climate action** |
| 14 | **Life below water** (blue economy) |
| 16 | Peace, justice and strong institutions |
| 17 | **Partnerships for the goals** |

- **Poverty (HIES 2022):** **18.7%**; extreme poverty **5.6%**.

## Quick revision
- First education commission: **Qudrat-e-Khuda (1972–74)**; policy **2010**.
- Compulsory primary education act **1990**.
- First university **Dhaka (1921)**; first private **North South (1992)**; first after independence **Islamic University (1979)**.
- EPI **7 April 1979**; polio-free **27 March 2014**.
- Kala-azar: **first country** validated by WHO (**2023**).
- ORS from **ICDDR,B**.
- SDG: **17 goals, 169 targets, 2016–2030**; MDG: **8 goals**.
