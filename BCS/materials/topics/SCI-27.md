# Renewable energy & nanotechnology

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask renewable vs non-renewable sources, what a solar cell is made of, Bangladesh's only hydroelectric plant (Kaptai), biogas composition, SREDA/IDCOL, and nanotechnology basics (1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m, Feynman, carbon nanotubes, uses).

## Renewable vs non-renewable energy 🔥
| Renewable (নবায়নযোগ্য) | Non-renewable (অনবায়নযোগ্য) |
|---|---|
| **Solar, wind, hydro (water), biomass, biogas, geothermal, tidal, wave**, green hydrogen | **Coal, petroleum, natural gas** (fossil fuels), **nuclear fuel (uranium)** |
| Naturally replenished; low carbon emission | Limited stock; fossil fuels emit CO₂ |

- The **Sun** is the ultimate source of almost all energy on Earth (except nuclear, geothermal and tidal).

## Solar energy (সৌরশক্তি) 🔥🔥
- **Solar cell (photovoltaic cell)** converts **sunlight directly into electricity** (DC) by the **photovoltaic effect** (first observed by **Edmond Becquerel, 1839**).
- Most solar cells are made of **silicon** (a semiconductor). The first practical silicon solar cell was made at **Bell Labs in 1954**. Commercial panels are about **15–22% efficient**.
- Solar panels + **battery + charge controller + inverter** (DC → AC) make a solar home system.
- **Solar water heaters and solar cookers** use sunlight as heat (solar thermal).
- **Bangladesh**: the **Solar Home System (SHS)** programme, financed mainly through **IDCOL** (Infrastructure Development Company Ltd), installed **millions (≈ 6 million)** of systems in off-grid rural homes — one of the **largest off-grid solar programmes in the world**. Solar irrigation pumps and rooftop solar with **net metering (guideline 2018)** are expanding.
- Largest solar park in Bangladesh (as of 2023): **Teesta solar plant, Sundarganj, Gaibandha (≈ 200 MW)**.

## Other renewable sources 🔥
| Source | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Hydroelectric (জলবিদ্যুৎ)** | Potential energy of stored water → turbine → electricity. **Kaptai Hydroelectric Plant** on the **Karnaphuli river, Rangamati** — **Bangladesh's only hydropower plant**; started in **1962**; capacity ≈ **230 MW** |
| **Wind (বায়ু)** | Wind turbines; best at coasts and open areas. Early Bangladesh projects: **Sonagazi (Feni)** and **Kutubdia (Cox's Bazar)**; a ≈ **60 MW** wind farm at **Khurushkul, Cox's Bazar** began supplying the grid in 2023. China leads the world in solar and wind |
| **Biogas (বায়োগ্যাস)** | From **cow dung**, kitchen and poultry waste by **anaerobic** bacteria; **≈ 55–70% methane (CH₄)**, rest mostly CO₂; leftover **slurry is good fertilizer** |
| **Biomass** | Wood, crop residue, rice husk, jute sticks; bio-ethanol, biodiesel |
| **Geothermal (ভূ-তাপ)** | Heat from inside the Earth; **Iceland**, Philippines, USA, Indonesia use it widely |
| **Tidal (জোয়ার-ভাটা)** | **La Rance, France (1966)** — first large tidal power station; Sihwa Lake (South Korea) is the largest |
| **Hydrogen / fuel cell** | Fuel cell combines H₂ and O₂ → **electricity + water** (no pollution). **Green hydrogen** is made by electrolysis using renewable power |

## Institutions and policy 🔥
| Body | Fact |
|---|---|
| **SREDA** (Sustainable and Renewable Energy Development Authority) | Bangladesh's nodal agency for renewables and energy efficiency, set up in **2014** (Act 2012) |
| **IDCOL** | Finances SHS, solar irrigation, biogas plants |
| **Renewable Energy Policy (2008)** | Bangladesh's first; aimed for 10% of power from renewables by 2020 (not achieved) |
| **IRENA** (International Renewable Energy Agency) | Founded **2009**; HQ **Abu Dhabi**, UAE |
| **International Solar Alliance (ISA)** | Launched **2015** by **India and France** at COP21; HQ **Gurugram, India** |

- Renewables still make up only a **small share** of Bangladesh's electricity; natural gas remains the main source.

## Nanotechnology (ন্যানোপ্রযুক্তি) 🔥🔥
- **"Nano"** comes from the Greek *nanos* = **dwarf**. **1 nanometre (nm) = 10⁻⁹ m** (one billionth of a metre).
- Nanotechnology works with materials at about **1–100 nm**, where they show new properties (colour, strength, conductivity, reactivity) because of large surface area and quantum effects.
- **Richard Feynman**'s 1959 lecture ***"There's Plenty of Room at the Bottom"*** first described the idea → he is called the **father of nanotechnology**. The term **"nanotechnology"** was coined by **Norio Taniguchi (1974)**; **K. Eric Drexler** popularised it (*Engines of Creation*, 1986).
- Scale sense: a human hair is ≈ **80,000–100,000 nm** thick; DNA double helix ≈ **2 nm** wide.
- **Approaches**: **top-down** (cutting bulk material smaller, e.g. chip lithography) and **bottom-up** (building from atoms/molecules, self-assembly).

| Tool / material | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Scanning tunnelling microscope (STM)** | **Binnig & Rohrer (1981)**, Nobel 1986 — "see" and move single atoms |
| Atomic force microscope (AFM) | 1986 |
| **Fullerene (C₆₀)** | 1985 |
| **Carbon nanotube** | **Sumio Iijima (1991)** — very strong, light, conducts electricity |
| **Graphene** | 2004, Geim & Novoselov (Nobel 2010) — one-atom-thick carbon |
| **Quantum dots** | Nobel Chemistry **2023** (Bawendi, Brus, Ekimov) — QLED TV screens, bio-imaging |

**Uses of nanotechnology:**
- **Medicine**: **targeted drug delivery** (e.g. cancer drugs), nano-sensors for diagnosis, antibacterial **silver nanoparticles** in dressings.
- **Cosmetics**: **zinc oxide and titanium dioxide nanoparticles** in **sunscreens** (transparent UV blockers).
- **Electronics**: smaller, faster chips (transistors a few nm in size), memory, displays.
- **Energy and environment**: better solar cells and batteries, water filters, **self-cleaning glass**, stain-resistant fabrics.
- **Materials**: stronger, lighter composites (sports goods, cars, aircraft).
- **Concerns**: possible toxicity of nanoparticles to health and environment.

## Quick revision
- Renewable: **solar, wind, hydro, biomass, biogas, geothermal, tidal**; nuclear is **non-renewable**.
- **Solar cell = silicon**, photovoltaic effect, gives DC; inverter converts DC → AC.
- **Kaptai (Karnaphuli, Rangamati)** = Bangladesh's only hydropower plant (1962, ≈230 MW).
- **Biogas ≈ 60% methane**, made anaerobically from cow dung.
- **SREDA (2014)**; IDCOL funds solar home systems; IRENA HQ **Abu Dhabi**.
- **1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m**; nanoscale **1–100 nm**; nano = dwarf.
- **Feynman (1959)** idea; **Taniguchi (1974)** coined the term; STM 1981.
- Carbon nanotube: **Iijima (1991)**; ZnO/TiO₂ nanoparticles in sunscreens.
