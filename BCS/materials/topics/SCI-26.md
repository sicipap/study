# Inventions & inventors

> **Why it matters:** "Who invented the telephone / radio / X-ray / dynamite?" and "which law was given by whom?" are asked in every job exam. Bangladeshi scientists (J. C. Bose, S. N. Bose, Fazlur Rahman Khan) are favourite questions too.

## Bangladeshi / Bengali scientists 🔥
| Scientist | Known for |
|---|---|
| **Jagadish Chandra Bose** (born in Mymensingh; ancestral home Rarikhal, Bikrampur, Munshiganj) | **Plants have life/response**; **crescograph**; pioneering **microwave/radio** research |
| **Satyendra Nath Bose** (Dhaka University) | **Bose–Einstein statistics** (1924); particles called **bosons** |
| **Dr. Muhammad Qudrat-e-Khuda** | Chemist; led the **first education commission** of Bangladesh (1972) |
| **Fazlur Rahman Khan** | Structural engineer; **"tube" system** for skyscrapers; **Sears (Willis) Tower**, John Hancock Center (Chicago); called the "Einstein of structural engineering" |
| **Jamal Nazrul Islam** | Cosmologist; ***The Ultimate Fate of the Universe*** (1983) |
| **Dr. Maqsudul Alam** | Led decoding of the **jute genome** (2010) |
| **M. Zahid Hasan** | Led the team that observed **Weyl fermions** (2015) |
| **Mustafa Jabbar** | **Bijoy** Bangla keyboard (1988) |

## Quick revision
- **Telephone — Bell (1876); radio — Marconi (J. C. Bose also a pioneer); TV — Baird (1926).**
- **Bulb and phonograph — Edison; dynamo — Faraday; battery — Volta.**
- **Aeroplane — Wright brothers (1903); steam engine — James Watt; locomotive — Stephenson.**
- **Dynamite — Alfred Nobel; safety lamp — Humphry Davy; lightning conductor — Franklin.**
- **X-ray — Röntgen (1895); penicillin — Fleming; stethoscope — Laennec; barometer — Torricelli.**
- **Printing press — Gutenberg; WWW — Tim Berners-Lee; mobile phone — Martin Cooper.**
- **Boyle's law — Boyle; relativity — Einstein; quantum theory — Planck.**
- Fazlur Rahman Khan — skyscraper "tube" design; S. N. Bose — boson.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Who invented the telephone?
(a) Thomas Edison (b) Alexander Graham Bell (c) Marconi (d) Samuel Morse

**2.** X-ray was discovered by —
(a) Wilhelm Röntgen (b) Henri Becquerel (c) Marie Curie (d) J. J. Thomson

**3.** Dynamite was invented by —
(a) Humphry Davy (b) Rudolf Diesel (c) Alfred Nobel (d) James Watt

**4.** Who invented the television?
(a) Marconi (b) Edison (c) Lumière brothers (d) John Logie Baird

**5.** The barometer was invented by —
(a) Galileo (b) Torricelli (c) Pascal (d) Boyle

**6.** Which Bangladeshi-born engineer designed the Sears (Willis) Tower in Chicago?
(a) Jamal Nazrul Islam (b) Qudrat-e-Khuda (c) Fazlur Rahman Khan (d) Maqsudul Alam

**7.** Who invented the crescograph?
(a) Jagadish Chandra Bose (b) Satyendra Nath Bose (c) C. V. Raman (d) Meghnad Saha

**8.** The World Wide Web was invented by —
(a) Charles Babbage (b) Bill Gates (c) Martin Cooper (d) Tim Berners-Lee

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Bell, 1876 |
| 2 | a | Röntgen discovered X-rays in 1895 |
| 3 | c | Alfred Nobel, 1867 |
| 4 | d | John Logie Baird, 1926 |
| 5 | b | Torricelli, 1643 |
| 6 | c | Fazlur Rahman Khan developed the tube system |
| 7 | a | J. C. Bose's crescograph measures plant growth |
