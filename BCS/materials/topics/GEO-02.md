# Atmosphere, winds, climate zones

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the composition and layers of the atmosphere (where the ozone layer and ionosphere are), the planetary wind belts, famous local winds (Loo, Chinook, Harmattan), and Bangladesh's climate and seasons.

## Composition of air 🔥
| Gas | Share by volume (dry air) |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Nitrogen (N₂)** | **About 78%** |
| 🔥 **Oxygen (O₂)** | **About 21%** |
| **Argon (Ar)** | About 0.93% (most common **inert/noble gas** in air) |
| **Carbon dioxide (CO₂)** | About **0.04%** (roughly 420 ppm, and rising) |
| Others | Neon, helium, methane, ozone, water vapour (varies, 0–4%), dust |

## Layers of the atmosphere (bottom → top) 🔥🔥
| Layer | Height (approx.) | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Troposphere (ট্রপোমণ্ডল)** | 0 to **about 8 km at the poles, 16–18 km at the equator** | **All weather** (clouds, rain, storms) happens here; contains most air and water vapour; **temperature falls about 6.5 °C per km** (normal lapse rate). Upper boundary: **tropopause** |
| 🔥 **Stratosphere (স্ট্র্যাটোমণ্ডল)** | Up to **about 50 km** | Contains the **ozone layer** (about **15–35 km**), which absorbs **ultraviolet (UV)** rays; temperature **rises** with height; calm air, so **jet aircraft** fly in its lower part |
| **Mesosphere** | About 50–80/85 km | **Coldest layer** (down to about −90 °C); **meteors burn up** here |
| 🔥 **Thermosphere** | About 80 to 600+ km | Temperature rises sharply; contains the **ionosphere**, which **reflects radio waves** back to Earth; **auroras** occur here; the International Space Station orbits here |
| **Exosphere** | Above about 600 km | Outermost; very thin air (hydrogen, helium) merging into space |

- Memory hook: **T-S-M-T-E** — "**T**he **S**trong **M**an **T**hrew **E**ggs".
- The ionosphere is not a separate layer; it is an electrically charged zone mostly within the thermosphere.

## Air pressure and pressure belts
- Average sea-level pressure: **1013.25 millibars (hPa)** = 76 cm of mercury. Measured with a **barometer** (invented by **Torricelli**).
- Pressure **falls with height**.
- **Isobar:** a line joining places of **equal pressure**.

| Pressure belt | Latitude | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Equatorial low** | 0°–5° N/S | **Doldrums** — calm, rising hot air |
| **Subtropical high** | About **30° N/S** | **Horse latitudes** — calm, dry; most hot deserts lie here |
| **Subpolar low** | About **60° N/S** | |
| **Polar high** | 90° N/S | Cold, sinking air |

## Winds 🔥
Wind blows from **high pressure to low pressure**. Because of Earth's rotation, the **Coriolis force** deflects winds **to the right in the Northern Hemisphere** and **to the left in the Southern Hemisphere** (**Ferrel's law**).

### Planetary (permanent) winds
| Wind | From → to | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Trade winds (আয়ন বায়ু)** | Subtropical high → **equatorial low** | **North-east trades** (N. Hemisphere), **south-east trades** (S. Hemisphere); steady; helped sailing ships |
| 🔥 **Westerlies (পশ্চিমা বায়ু)** | Subtropical high → subpolar low (30°–60°) | Very strong in the Southern Hemisphere: **Roaring Forties, Furious Fifties, Shrieking (Screaming) Sixties** |
| **Polar easterlies** | Polar high → subpolar low | Cold, dry |

### Periodic winds
- 🔥 **Monsoon (মৌসুমি বায়ু):** seasonal reversal of winds. The word comes from Arabic **"mausim" (season)**. **South-west monsoon** (summer) brings rain to South Asia and Bangladesh; **north-east monsoon** (winter) is dry.
- **Sea breeze:** from sea to land by **day**. **Land breeze:** from land to sea at **night**.

### Local winds 🔥
| Wind | Region | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Loo** | **North India, Pakistan** plains | Hot, dry summer wind |
| **Chinook** | East slopes of the **Rocky Mountains** (USA/Canada) | Warm, dry; called **"snow eater"** |
| **Foehn** | **Alps** (Switzerland) | Warm, dry |
| **Sirocco** | From the **Sahara** to Italy/Mediterranean | Hot, dusty |
| **Harmattan** | **West Africa** | Dry, dusty; called **"the doctor"** because it relieves humid heat |
| **Mistral** | Rhône valley, **France** | **Cold**, dry |
| **Khamsin** | **Egypt** | Hot, dusty |
| **Santa Ana** | **California** | Hot, dry; spreads wildfires |
| 🔥 **Kalbaishakhi (কালবৈশাখী, nor'wester)** | **Bangladesh**, West Bengal | Violent thunderstorms from the **north-west** in the **pre-monsoon (March–May)**, especially in Baishakh |

## Climate zones 🔥
- **Weather** = short-term condition of the atmosphere; **climate** = average weather over a long time (usually **30+ years**).
- **Köppen climate classification** (Wladimir Köppen) — main groups: **A** tropical, **B** dry, **C** temperate, **D** continental (cold), **E** polar.
- Simple heat zones: **Torrid zone** (between the tropics), **Temperate zones** (tropics to polar circles), **Frigid zones** (inside the polar circles).
- Mediterranean climate: **dry summers, rainy winters**.

## Bangladesh climate 🔥
- Bangladesh has a **tropical monsoon climate (ক্রান্তীয় মৌসুমি জলবায়ু)**.
- Three main seasons: **Summer / pre-monsoon (March–May)**, **Rainy / monsoon (June–October)**, **Winter (November–February)**. Culturally Bangladesh has **six seasons (ষড়ঋতু)**.
- About **70–80%** of the annual rain falls in the monsoon.
- Wettest area: **Lalakhal, Sylhet**; driest: **Lalpur, Natore**.
- Lowest recorded temperature: **2.6 °C at Srimangal (1968)**.

## World weather records and instruments
- **Wettest place:** **Mawsynram**, Meghalaya, India (Cherrapunji is close by). **Driest:** **Atacama Desert**, Chile.
- **Highest recorded temperature:** **Death Valley, USA** (56.7 °C, 1913). **Lowest:** **Vostok station, Antarctica** (−89.2 °C, 1983).
- Instruments: **barometer** (pressure), **anemometer** (wind speed), **wind vane** (direction), **hygrometer** (humidity), **rain gauge** (rainfall), **thermometer** (temperature).

## Quick revision
- Air: **N₂ 78%, O₂ 21%**, Ar 0.93%, CO₂ 0.04%.
- All weather: **troposphere**; ozone layer: **stratosphere**; meteors burn: **mesosphere**; radio waves reflected: **ionosphere (thermosphere)**.
- Temperature falls **6.5 °C per km** in the troposphere.
- Coriolis: right in the north, left in the south (**Ferrel's law**).
- **Roaring Forties** = westerlies of the Southern Hemisphere.
- Loo = India; Chinook = Rockies ("snow eater"); Harmattan = West Africa ("doctor").
- Bangladesh: **tropical monsoon**; **Kalbaishakhi** in March–May.
- Wettest place in Bangladesh: **Lalakhal**; driest: **Lalpur**.
