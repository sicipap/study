# Constitution: making, preamble, fundamental principles

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the key dates (4 November / 16 December 1972), the drafting committee chairman (Dr. Kamal Hossain), the number of parts and articles, and "which article says what" for Part I and Part II (state principles).

## Making of the Constitution 🔥🔥
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 11 Jan 1972 | **Provisional Constitution Order** (অস্থায়ী সংবিধান আদেশ) — parliamentary system |
| 23 Mar 1972 | Constituent Assembly (গণপরিষদ) Order issued |
| **10 Apr 1972** | **First session of the Constituent Assembly**; Speaker **শাহ আবদুল হামিদ** |
| 11 Apr 1972 | **34-member drafting committee** formed; chairman 🔥 **ড. কামাল হোসেন** (Law Minister) |
| 12 Oct 1972 | Draft bill placed in the Constituent Assembly |
| 🔥 **4 Nov 1972** | **Constitution adopted** by the Constituent Assembly — **Constitution Day (সংবিধান দিবস)** |
| 14 Dec 1972 | Members signed the handwritten copy |
| 🔥 **16 Dec 1972** | **Constitution came into force** |

- The drafting committee's only **woman member**: **রাজিয়া বানু**. The only **opposition member**: **সুরঞ্জিত সেনগুপ্ত** (he did not sign in protest).
- The members of the Constituent Assembly were those elected in 1970 to the National and Provincial Assemblies from East Pakistan (the total is commonly given as **403**).
- The original was **handwritten** (calligraphy by **এ. কে. এম. আবদুর রউফ**); artwork by a team under **শিল্পাচার্য জয়নুল আবেদিন** (including **হাশেম খান**).
- There are **Bangla and English** texts; if they conflict, the **Bangla text prevails** (Article 153).

## Features 🔥
- **Written** and **rigid** (amendment needs a **two-thirds** majority of the total MPs — Article 142).
- **Unitary** state; **parliamentary** government (restored in 1991); **unicameral** legislature.
- **Supremacy of the Constitution** (Article 7); independent judiciary with **judicial review**.
- Structure today: **11 parts**, **153 articles**, **7 schedules** (the 5th–7th schedules were added in 2011).

| Part | Subject | Articles |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Republic | 1–7B |
| II | Fundamental Principles of State Policy | 8–25 |
| III | Fundamental Rights | 26–47A |
| IV | The Executive | 48–64 |
| V | The Legislature | 65–93 |
| VI | The Judiciary | 94–117 |
| VII | Elections | 118–126 |
| VIII | Comptroller and Auditor-General | 127–132 |
| IX | Services of Bangladesh | 133–141 |
| IXA | Emergency Provisions | 141A–141C |
| X | Amendment of the Constitution | 142 |
| XI | Miscellaneous | 143–153 |

## Part I — The Republic 🔥
| Article | Subject |
|---|---|
| **1** | Bangladesh is a **unitary, independent, sovereign Republic** — **"গণপ্রজাতন্ত্রী বাংলাদেশ"** |
| **2** | Territory of the Republic |
| **2A** | **State religion: Islam**; other religions have equal status and equal rights |
| 🔥 **3** | **State language: Bangla** |
| **4** | **National anthem, flag and emblem** (anthem: first **10 lines** of "আমার সোনার বাংলা") |
| **4A** | Portrait of the Father of the Nation (added 2011) |
| **5** | **Capital: Dhaka** |
| **6** | **Citizenship** — people are known as **Bangalee as a nation** and **Bangladeshi as citizens** |
| 🔥 **7** | **Supremacy of the Constitution**; all powers belong to the people |
| **7A** | Abrogating or suspending the Constitution is an offence (**sedition**) |
| **7B** | Basic provisions (preamble, Parts I–II, most of Part III, etc.) **cannot be amended** |

## Preamble (প্রস্তাবনা)
- Begins with **"বিসমিল্লাহির-রহমানির রহিম"** (added in 1979 by the 5th amendment; kept by the 15th amendment with a Bangla meaning).
- Starts: **"আমরা, বাংলাদেশের জনগণ…"** ("We, the people of Bangladesh…"), having proclaimed independence on **26 March 1971**.
- States the high ideals: **nationalism, socialism, democracy and secularism**, and a society with the **rule of law**, fundamental human rights, and political, economic and social **equality and justice**.
- After 2011 the preamble is among the **unamendable** basic provisions (Article 7B).

## Part II — Fundamental Principles of State Policy (রাষ্ট্র পরিচালনার মূলনীতি) 🔥🔥
- 🔥 **Article 8** names **four fundamental principles**: **জাতীয়তাবাদ (nationalism), সমাজতন্ত্র (socialism), গণতন্ত্র (democracy), ধর্মনিরপেক্ষতা (secularism)**. (Secularism was removed in 1979 and restored by the **15th amendment, 2011**.)
- 🔥 These principles are **not enforceable in court** (Article 8(2)), but they guide law-making and government.

| Article | Principle |
|---|---|
| 9 | Nationalism |
| 10 | Socialism and freedom from exploitation |
| 11 | Democracy and human rights |
| 12 | Secularism and freedom of religion |
| 13 | Principles of **ownership**: state, cooperative, private |
| 14 | Emancipation of peasants and workers |
| 15 | Provision of **basic necessities** (food, clothing, shelter, education, medical care) |
| 16 | Rural development and agricultural revolution |
| 🔥 17 | **Free and compulsory education** |
| 18 | Public health and morality |
| 18A | **Protection of environment and biodiversity** (added 2011) |
| 19 | Equality of opportunity |
| 20 | Work as a right and duty |
| 21 | Duties of citizens and public servants |
| 🔥 22 | **Separation of the judiciary from the executive** |
| 23 | National culture |
| 23A | Culture of tribes, minor races, ethnic sects and communities (added 2011) |
| 24 | National monuments |
| 🔥 25 | **Promotion of international peace, security and solidarity** |

> 🔄 Update: after August 2024 a Constitution Reform Commission proposed changes (for example to the fundamental principles, the preamble and Article 4A). The notes above give the text as written; check whether any reform has since been adopted.

## Quick revision
- Adopted **4 November 1972**; in force **16 December 1972**.
- Drafting committee: **34 members**, chair **Dr. Kamal Hossain**; woman member **Razia Banu**.
- **11 parts, 153 articles, 7 schedules**; Bangla text prevails (**Art. 153**).
- Art. **3** Bangla; Art. **5** Dhaka; Art. **7** supremacy of the Constitution.
- Art. **2A** state religion; Art. **6** citizenship.
- Art. **8**: four fundamental principles; not enforceable in court.
- Art. **17** free education; Art. **22** separation of judiciary; Art. **25** international peace.
- Art. **18A** environment; **23A** ethnic culture (both 2011).
