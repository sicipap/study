# Fiber optics, satellite, 1G–5G

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the principle of optical fibre (total internal reflection), the height of a geostationary orbit, the first artificial satellite, and what each mobile generation introduced, plus when 3G/4G/5G came to Bangladesh.

## Optical fibre (অপটিক্যাল ফাইবার) 🔥
- Carries data as **pulses of light** through thin strands of **glass (silica) or plastic**.
- 🔥 Works on the principle of **total internal reflection (পূর্ণ অভ্যন্তরীণ প্রতিফলন)**.
- Structure (inside → out): **core** (light travels here; higher refractive index) → **cladding** (lower refractive index, reflects light back) → **buffer/jacket** (protection).
- Light source: **LED** or **laser**.
- 🔥 **Narinder Singh Kapany** is called the **father of fibre optics**. **Charles K. Kao** won the **2009 Nobel Prize in Physics** for showing glass fibre could carry signals over long distances.

| Type | Features |
|---|---|
| **Single-mode fibre** | Very thin core; one light path; **long distance**; uses laser |
| **Multi-mode fibre** | Thicker core; many light paths; **short distance** (inside buildings) |

**Advantages:** **highest speed/bandwidth**, very low signal loss, **no electromagnetic interference**, hard to tap (secure), light weight. **Disadvantages:** costly, fragile, difficult to join (splice).
- **Submarine cables** under the sea are optical fibre cables.

## Satellite communication 🔥
- A **communication satellite** receives a signal from an earth station (**uplink**), amplifies it and sends it back to earth (**downlink**). The device doing this is a **transponder**.
- 🔥 **First artificial satellite:** **Sputnik 1**, launched by the **USSR** on **4 October 1957**.
- **Arthur C. Clarke** proposed the idea of geostationary communication satellites (**1945**); the geostationary orbit is also called the **Clarke orbit**.

| Orbit | Height (approx.) | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **LEO** (Low Earth Orbit) | About **160–2,000 km** | Earth observation, ISS, **Starlink** internet |
| **MEO** (Medium Earth Orbit) | About 2,000–35,786 km | **Navigation (GPS)** |
| 🔥 **GEO** (Geostationary orbit) | **About 35,786 km** (≈ 36,000 km) above the **equator** | TV broadcasting, weather, communication |

- A **geostationary satellite** takes **24 hours** (one sidereal day, about 23 h 56 min) to orbit, the same as Earth's rotation, so it **looks fixed** from the ground. **Three** such satellites, spaced 120° apart, can cover almost the whole Earth.
- **Satellite navigation systems:** **GPS** (USA), **GLONASS** (Russia), **Galileo** (European Union), **BeiDou** (China), NavIC (India).
- **VSAT** (Very Small Aperture Terminal): small dish earth station for data links.
- Bangladesh's first satellite earth station: **Betbunia, Rangamati** (1975). Bangladesh's own satellite: **Bangladesh Satellite-1** (formerly Bangabandhu Satellite-1, launched May 2018) — see ICT-23.

## Mobile generations (1G–5G) 🔥🔥
| Gen | Launched (world) | Technology | Key feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1G** | **1979** (Japan, NTT); 1980s widely | **Analog**; AMPS | **Voice only**; poor quality, no security |
| **2G** | **1991** (**Finland**, GSM) | **Digital**: **GSM**, CDMA | Digital voice, **SMS**, MMS. **2.5G = GPRS**, **2.75G = EDGE** |
| **3G** | **2001** (Japan, NTT Docomo) | **UMTS/WCDMA**, CDMA2000, HSPA | **Mobile internet, video calls** |
| **4G** | **2009** (Norway & Sweden, TeliaSonera) | 🔥 **LTE** (Long Term Evolution), WiMAX; **all-IP** network | High-speed data, HD streaming, **VoLTE** |
| **5G** | **2019** (**South Korea** first nationwide) | **NR (New Radio)**, **millimetre waves**, massive MIMO | Up to about **10–20 Gbps**, **very low latency (~1 ms)**, IoT, self-driving cars, smart cities |

- **Martin Cooper** (Motorola) made the **first handheld mobile phone call** in **1973**; he is called the **father of the mobile phone**.
- **GSM** = Global System for Mobile Communications; **CDMA** = Code Division Multiple Access.
- **SIM** = Subscriber Identity Module. **IMEI** = International Mobile Equipment Identity (**15 digits**).
- Mobile networks are called **cellular** because the area is divided into **cells**, each served by a **BTS** (Base Transceiver Station, mobile tower).

## Mobile technology in Bangladesh 🔥
| Event | Year |
|---|---|
| First mobile operator: **Citycell** (Pacific Bangladesh Telecom, CDMA/AMPS) | **1993** |
| **Grameenphone** starts GSM service (26 March) | **1997** |
| **3G**: **Teletalk** first (trial in October 2012); licence auction for all | **2012–2013** |
| 🔥 **4G** launched (19 February) | **2018** |
| 🔥 **5G** trial launched by **Teletalk** (12 December) | **2021** |

## Quick revision
- Optical fibre principle: **total internal reflection**.
- Father of fibre optics: **Narinder Singh Kapany**; Nobel 2009: **Charles Kao**.
- First satellite: **Sputnik 1 (USSR, 4 October 1957)**.
- Geostationary height: **about 35,786 km** above the equator; period 24 h.
- 1G analog voice; 2G digital + **SMS**; 3G **video call**; 4G **LTE**; 5G **low latency, mmWave**.
- First handheld mobile call: **Martin Cooper, 1973**.
- Bangladesh: 4G in **2018**, 5G trial in **2021** (Teletalk).
- GPS = USA; GLONASS = Russia; BeiDou = China; Galileo = EU.
