# Lines, angles, parallel lines

> **Why it matters:** Basic geometry (জ্যামিতি) — complementary and supplementary angles, types of angles, angles made by a transversal with parallel lines, and clock-hand angles — gives easy marks in BCS, primary and bank papers.

## Key concepts
- **Point (বিন্দু):** has position but no length, breadth or thickness.
- **Line (রেখা):** extends endlessly both ways; **ray (রশ্মি)** has one end point; **line segment (রেখাংশ)** has two end points.
- Through two distinct points exactly **one** straight line can be drawn. Two distinct lines meet in at most **one** point.
- Euclid's *Elements* (13 books) is the classic source of geometry; Euclid is called the **father of geometry**.

## Types of angles (কোণের প্রকারভেদ)
| Angle | Measure |
|---|---|
| **Acute (সূক্ষ্মকোণ)** | between 0° and 90° |
| **Right (সমকোণ)** | exactly 90° |
| **Obtuse (স্থূলকোণ)** | between 90° and 180° |
| **Straight (সরলকোণ)** | exactly 180° |
| **Reflex (প্রবৃদ্ধ কোণ)** | between 180° and 360° |
| **Complete (পূর্ণ কোণ)** | 360° |

## Pairs of angles 🔥
| Pair | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Complementary (পূরক)** | sum = **90°** |
| **Supplementary (সম্পূরক)** | sum = **180°** |
| **Adjacent (সন্নিহিত)** | common vertex and common arm, no overlap |
| **Linear pair** | adjacent and supplementary (on a straight line) |
| 🔥 **Vertically opposite (বিপ্রতীপ)** | always **equal** |

## Parallel lines and a transversal (ছেদক) 🔥
When a transversal cuts two **parallel** lines:
| Angles | Relation |
|---|---|
| **Corresponding (অনুরূপ)** | equal |
| **Alternate interior (একান্তর)** | equal |
| Alternate exterior | equal |
| **Co-interior / same-side interior** | sum = 180° |

If any one of these relations holds, the two lines are parallel (converse).

## Clock angle shortcut
- Angle between the hands at H hours M minutes = the difference between 30H and 5.5M (ignore the sign). If the result is above 180°, subtract it from 360°.
- The minute hand moves 6° per minute; the hour hand moves 0.5° per minute.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Find the supplement of 110°.
Steps: 180° − 110° = 70°.
**Answer: 70°**

**Example 2.** Which angle is equal to its complement?
Steps: x = 90 − x → x = 45°.
**Answer: 45°**

**Example 3.** An angle is 4 times its supplement. Find it.
Steps: x = 4(180 − x) → 5x = 720 → x = 144°.
**Answer: 144°**

**Example 4.** Find the angle between the clock hands at 3:30.
Steps: 30 × 3 = 90; 5.5 × 30 = 165; difference = 75°.
**Answer: 75°**

**Example 5.** Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One co-interior angle is 65°. Find the other.
Steps: 180° − 65° = 115°.
**Answer: 115°**

## Common traps
- Complementary = 90°, supplementary = 180° — do not swap them.
- Co-interior angles are **supplementary**, not equal.
- Clock angles: the hour hand also moves during the minutes (at 3:30 it is halfway between 3 and 4).
- A reflex angle is **more than 180°**, not more than 90°.

## Quick revision
- Complement: 90° − x; supplement: 180° − x.
- Vertically opposite angles are equal.
- Parallel lines: corresponding and alternate angles equal; co-interior sum 180°.
- Clock: difference of 30H and 5.5M.
- Sum of angles around a point = 360°.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The complement of 62° is:
(a) 118° (b) 38° (c) 28° (d) 298°

**2.** The supplement of 75° is:
(a) 105° (b) 15° (c) 285° (d) 115°

**3.** Two supplementary angles are such that one is twice the other. The smaller angle is:
(a) 30° (b) 45° (c) 90° (d) 60°

**4.** An angle of 200° is:
(a) obtuse (b) straight (c) reflex (d) complete

**5.** Vertically opposite angles are always:
(a) complementary (b) equal (c) supplementary (d) right angles

**6.** When a transversal cuts two parallel lines, alternate interior angles are:
(a) equal (b) supplementary (c) complementary (d) unequal

**7.** Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One co-interior angle is 70°. The other is:
(a) 70° (b) 20° (c) 290° (d) 110°

**8.** The angle between the hands of a clock at 4:00 is:
(a) 90° (b) 120° (c) 100° (d) 240°

**9.** The angle between the hands of a clock at 2:20 is:
(a) 40° (b) 60° (c) 50° (d) 70°

**10.** An angle whose supplement is 4 times its complement is:
(a) 60° (b) 45° (c) 30° (d) 36°

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | 90° − 62° = 28° |
| 2 | a | 180° − 75° = 105° |
| 3 | d | x + 2x = 180° → x = 60° |
| 4 | c | Between 180° and 360° → reflex |
| 5 | b | Vertically opposite angles are equal |
| 6 | a | Alternate interior angles are equal for parallel lines |
| 7 | d | Co-interior angles add to 180° |
| 8 | b | 30 × 4 = 120° |
| 9 | c | 30 × 2 = 60; 5.5 × 20 = 110; difference 50° |
| 10 | a | 180 − x = 4(90 − x) → 3x = 180 → 60° |
