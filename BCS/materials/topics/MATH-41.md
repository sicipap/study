# Coordinate geometry basics (distance, slope)

> **Why it matters:** Coordinate geometry (স্থানাঙ্ক জ্যামিতি) questions in BCS and bank exams are short formula applications: distance between two points, midpoint, slope (ঢাল), equation of a line, quadrants and area of a triangle.

## Key concepts
- A point is written **(x, y)**: x = **abscissa (ভুজ)**, y = **ordinate (কোটি)**. The origin is (0, 0). René **Descartes** introduced this system (Cartesian plane).
- **Quadrants (চতুর্ভাগ):**

| Quadrant | Signs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | (+, +) | (3, 4) |
| II | (−, +) | (−3, 4) |
| III | (−, −) | (−4, −5) |
| IV | (+, −) | (2, −6) |

- A point on the x-axis has y = 0; a point on the y-axis has x = 0.
- **Slope (ঢাল), m:** the steepness of a line = rise ÷ run = tan θ, where θ is the angle with the positive x-axis.
  - Slope of the **x-axis** (or any horizontal line) = **0**; slope of the **y-axis** (vertical line) is **undefined**.
  - 🔥 **Parallel lines:** equal slopes (m₁ = m₂). **Perpendicular lines:** **m₁ × m₂ = −1**.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| 🔥 Distance between (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂) | **√[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]** |
| Distance from origin | √(x² + y²) |
| 🔥 Midpoint | ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2) |
| Point dividing in ratio m : n (internally) | ((mx₂ + nx₁)/(m + n), (my₂ + ny₁)/(m + n)) |
| Centroid of a triangle | ((x₁ + x₂ + x₃)/3, (y₁ + y₂ + y₃)/3) |
| 🔥 Slope through two points | **(y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)** |
| Slope of ax + by + c = 0 | −a/b |
| Slope–intercept form | y = mx + c (c = y-intercept) |
| Point–slope form | y − y₁ = m(x − x₁) |
| Intercept form | x/a + y/b = 1 |
| Triangle with vertices at (0, 0), (a, 0), (0, b) | area = ½ab |

**Area of a triangle** with vertices (x₁, y₁), (x₂, y₂), (x₃, y₃) = ½ × the absolute value of [x₁(y₂ − y₃) + x₂(y₃ − y₁) + x₃(y₁ − y₂)]. If this is 0, the points are **collinear (সমরেখ)**.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Find the distance between (1, 2) and (4, 6).
Steps: √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5.
**Answer: 5**

**Example 2.** Find the midpoint of (2, 3) and (6, 7).
Steps: ((2 + 6)/2, (3 + 7)/2) = (4, 5).
**Answer: (4, 5)**

**Example 3.** Find the slope of the line through (1, 2) and (3, 8).
Steps: (8 − 2)/(3 − 1) = 6/2 = 3.
**Answer: 3**

**Example 4.** Find the slope and y-intercept of 2x + 3y = 6.
Steps: 3y = −2x + 6 → y = −(2/3)x + 2.
**Answer: slope −2/3, y-intercept 2**

**Example 5.** Find the area of the triangle with vertices (0, 0), (4, 0) and (0, 3).
Steps: Right angle at the origin: ½ × 4 × 3 = 6.
**Answer: 6 square units**

## Common traps
- Subtracting coordinates in different orders in the slope formula (take y₂ − y₁ **and** x₂ − x₁ from the same point order).
- Slope of ax + by + c = 0 is **−a/b**, not a/b or −b/a.
- Perpendicular slope is the **negative reciprocal** (2/3 → −3/2), not just the reciprocal.
- A vertical line has an undefined slope, not 0.

## Quick revision
- Distance √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²].
- Midpoint = average of coordinates.
- Slope = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁); for ax + by + c = 0 it is −a/b.
- Parallel: equal slopes; perpendicular: product −1.
- y = mx + c.
- Quadrant II (−, +); III (−, −); IV (+, −).

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The distance between (2, 3) and (5, 7) is:
(a) 7 (b) 5 (c) 25 (d) √7

**2.** The distance of the point (−6, 8) from the origin is:
(a) 2 (b) 14 (c) 10 (d) 100

**3.** The midpoint of (−2, 4) and (6, 8) is:
(a) (4, 12) (b) (−4, −2) (c) (2, 6) (d) (8, 4)

**4.** The slope of the line through (2, 5) and (4, 11) is:
(a) 3 (b) 1/3 (c) 6 (d) −3

**5.** The slope of the line 3x − 4y + 7 = 0 is:
(a) −3/4 (b) 4/3 (c) −4/3 (d) 3/4

**6.** A line has slope 2/3. The slope of a line perpendicular to it is:
(a) 2/3 (b) 3/2 (c) −3/2 (d) −2/3

**7.** The point (−4, −5) lies in the:
(a) first quadrant (b) second quadrant (c) fourth quadrant (d) third quadrant

**8.** The equation of the line with slope 3 and y-intercept −2 is:
(a) y = 3x − 2 (b) y = −2x + 3 (c) y = 3x + 2 (d) 3y = x − 2

**9.** The area of the triangle with vertices (0, 0), (6, 0) and (0, 4) is:
(a) 24 square units (b) 12 square units (c) 10 square units (d) 20 square units

**10.** The centroid of the triangle with vertices (1, 2), (3, 4) and (5, 9) is:
(a) (9, 15) (b) (3, 4) (c) (4.5, 7.5) (d) (3, 5)
