# Good governance: concept & indicators

> **Why it matters:** Good governance (সুশাসন) is the core idea of the ethics section. Exams ask who first used the term, how the World Bank and UNDP define governance, the characteristics of good governance, and the six Worldwide Governance Indicators.

## Governance and good governance 🔥
- **Governance** means the **process of decision-making and the process by which decisions are implemented**. It involves not only the government but also the **private sector and civil society**.
- The word comes from the Greek **"kybernan"** (to steer), through the Latin **"gubernare"**.
- **World Bank (1992, *Governance and Development*):** governance is "the manner in which power is exercised in the management of a country's economic and social resources for development".
- **UNDP (1997):** governance is "the exercise of economic, political and administrative authority to manage a country's affairs at all levels".
- 🔥 The term **"good governance"** came into wide use through the **World Bank's 1989 report on Sub-Saharan Africa** (*From Crisis to Sustainable Growth*), which blamed Africa's problems on a "crisis of governance".
- **Good governance** = governance that is participatory, accountable, transparent, responsive, effective and follows the rule of law, so that corruption is minimised and the voices of the most vulnerable are heard.

## Three actors of governance (UNDP)
| Actor | Role |
|---|---|
| **State** | Creates a political and legal environment |
| **Private sector** | Creates jobs and income |
| **Civil society** | Mobilises groups to take part in political, economic and social life |

## Characteristics of good governance 🔥
**UNDP (1997)** listed **nine** characteristics:
1. **Participation**
2. **Rule of law**
3. **Transparency**
4. **Responsiveness**
5. **Consensus orientation**
6. **Equity**
7. **Effectiveness and efficiency**
8. **Accountability**
9. **Strategic vision**

**UN-ESCAP** lists **eight**: participatory, consensus-oriented, accountable, transparent, responsive, effective and efficient, equitable and inclusive, and follows the rule of law. (Some books quote eight, some nine: note which organisation the question names.)

## ADB's four pillars 🔥
The **Asian Development Bank** (policy paper *Governance: Sound Development Management*, 1995) named **four elements (pillars)**:
**Accountability, Participation, Predictability, Transparency.**

## Worldwide Governance Indicators (WGI) 🔥
The **World Bank** measures governance through **six dimensions**:
| # | Dimension |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Voice and Accountability** |
| 2 | **Political Stability and Absence of Violence/Terrorism** |
| 3 | **Government Effectiveness** |
| 4 | **Regulatory Quality** |
| 5 | **Rule of Law** |
| 6 | **Control of Corruption** |

## Obstacles to good governance in Bangladesh
- Corruption and weak accountability.
- Politicisation of administration and institutions.
- Weak parliamentary oversight and confrontational politics.
- Delays and backlog in the justice system.
- Lack of transparency and limited access to information.
- Weak local government.

## Tools for good governance in Bangladesh
| Tool | Year |
|---|---|
| Anti-Corruption Commission Act | **2004** |
| Right to Information Act | **2009** |
| National Human Rights Commission Act | **2009** |
| Whistleblower (public-interest information disclosure) protection Act | **2011** |
| **National Integrity Strategy (জাতীয় শুদ্ধাচার কৌশল)** | **2012** |
| Citizen Charter (নাগরিক সনদ) in government offices | **2007** |
| e-Government procurement (e-GP) | 2011 |

## Quick revision
- Good governance became a common term through the World Bank's 1989 Africa report.
- World Bank 1992: governance = manner of exercising power in managing resources for development.
- UNDP 1997: 9 characteristics; UN-ESCAP: 8.
- ADB four pillars: accountability, participation, predictability, transparency.
- World Bank WGI: 6 dimensions (voice and accountability … control of corruption).
- Three actors: state, private sector, civil society.
- Bangladesh's NIS was adopted in 2012.
