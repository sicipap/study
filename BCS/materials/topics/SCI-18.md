# Human body: nervous, respiratory, excretory, hormones

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask which part of the brain controls balance/heartbeat, functional unit of kidney, breathing rate, the "master gland", which hormone controls blood sugar or causes goitre, and bone counts.

## Nervous system (স্নায়ুতন্ত্র) 🔥
- The **neuron** is the structural and functional unit; the **longest cell** of the body. A junction between neurons is a **synapse**; a common chemical messenger is **acetylcholine**.
- Adult brain weighs about **1.3–1.4 kg**; protected by the skull and meninges; cerebrospinal fluid cushions it.

| Part of brain | Main function |
|---|---|
| **Cerebrum (গুরুমস্তিষ্ক)** | **Largest part**; **intelligence, memory, thinking, will, speech**, senses |
| **Cerebellum (লঘুমস্তিষ্ক)** | **Balance (ভারসাম্য)** and **coordination** of muscles; affected by alcohol |
| **Medulla oblongata (সুষুম্নাশীর্ষক)** | **Involuntary actions**: **heartbeat, breathing**, swallowing, coughing, vomiting |
| **Hypothalamus** | **Body temperature**, hunger, thirst; controls the pituitary |
| **Spinal cord (সুষুম্নাকাণ্ড)** | **Reflex actions** (e.g. pulling hand from a hot object), message pathway |

- **12 pairs of cranial nerves** and **31 pairs of spinal nerves**.
- **Reflex action** is quick and automatic, controlled mainly by the **spinal cord**.
- **Senses**: eye (retina — rods & cones), ear (**cochlea → hearing**; **semicircular canals → balance**), nose, tongue, skin. **Skin is the largest organ**.
- Skeleton: an **adult has 206 bones** (a newborn has about 300, which fuse). **Longest bone: femur (thigh)**; **smallest: stapes (ear)**.

## Respiratory system (শ্বসনতন্ত্র) 🔥
- Air path: **nose → pharynx → larynx (voice box, "Adam's apple") → trachea (windpipe) → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli**.
- **Alveoli (বায়ুথলি)** are the site of **gas exchange** by diffusion; about **30 crore (300 million)** in the two lungs.
- **Epiglottis** closes the windpipe while swallowing. **Right lung has 3 lobes, left lung 2** (space for the heart).
- **Diaphragm** is the main breathing muscle; it contracts (flattens) during inhalation.
- **Normal breathing rate of an adult: ≈ 12–20 times per minute** (many books: 16–20); higher in infants.
- Tidal volume ≈ **500 mL**; vital capacity ≈ 4–5 L.

| Gas | Inhaled air | Exhaled air |
|---|---|---|
| **Oxygen** | **≈ 21%** | ≈ 16% |
| **Carbon dioxide** | **≈ 0.04%** | **≈ 4%** |
| Nitrogen | ≈ 78% | ≈ 78% |

- O₂ is carried mainly by **haemoglobin** (as oxyhaemoglobin); CO₂ mostly as **bicarbonate** in plasma.
- **Cellular respiration** occurs in **mitochondria**; anaerobic respiration in muscle makes **lactic acid** (causes fatigue/cramps).

## Excretory system (রেচনতন্ত্র) 🔥
- **Kidneys (বৃক্ক)**: bean-shaped, ≈ 150 g each. **Functional unit: nephron** — about **10 lakh (1 million)** per kidney. Parts: **glomerulus** and **Bowman's capsule** (filtration), tubules (reabsorption).
- **Urea** is made in the **liver** and removed by the kidneys. It is the main nitrogenous waste in humans.
- Adults pass about **1–1.5 litres of urine per day** (≈ 95% water). Yellow colour: **urochrome**.
- **Kidney stones** are mostly **calcium oxalate**. Kidney failure is treated by **dialysis** or transplant (first successful kidney transplant: **1954, Joseph Murray**, USA).
- Other excretory organs: **lungs (CO₂), skin (sweat), liver (bile pigments)**.
- **ADH (vasopressin)** makes kidneys reabsorb water; lack of ADH → **diabetes insipidus** (lots of dilute urine).

## Hormones and endocrine glands (হরমোন ও অন্তঃক্ষরা গ্রন্থি) 🔥🔥
- Endocrine glands are **ductless**; they release **hormones** directly into the **blood**. The word "hormone" was used by **Bayliss and Starling (1905)**; the first hormone discovered was **secretin**.

| Gland | Hormone | Job / disorder |
|---|---|---|
| **Pituitary (পিটুইটারি)** | Growth hormone, TSH, ACTH, **ADH**, **oxytocin**, prolactin | **"Master gland"** — controls other glands; pea-sized, at the base of the brain. Excess GH → **gigantism** (child) / acromegaly (adult); lack → **dwarfism**. **Oxytocin** → childbirth contractions, milk ejection |
| **Thyroid (থাইরয়েড)** | **Thyroxine** (contains **iodine**) | Controls **metabolism**; **largest endocrine gland**. **Iodine lack → goitre (গলগণ্ড)**; lack in children → **cretinism**; in adults → myxoedema; excess → hyperthyroidism (**Graves' disease**, bulging eyes) |
| **Parathyroid** | **Parathormone** | **Calcium** and phosphate balance in blood |
| **Adrenal (অ্যাড্রিনাল)** | **Adrenaline** (medulla), cortisol, aldosterone (cortex) | Adrenaline = **"emergency / fight-or-flight hormone"**: raises heartbeat, BP, blood sugar |
| **Pancreas — islets of Langerhans** | **Insulin** (β-cells), **glucagon** (α-cells) | **Insulin lowers blood sugar**; its lack → **diabetes mellitus**. Glucagon raises blood sugar |
| **Testis** | **Testosterone** | Male characteristics |
| **Ovary** | **Oestrogen, progesterone** | Female characteristics; progesterone maintains **pregnancy** |
| **Pineal** | **Melatonin** | **Sleep–wake cycle** (biological clock) |
| **Thymus** | Thymosin | Immunity (T-cells) in childhood |

- **Insulin** was discovered by **Frederick Banting and Charles Best (1921)**; Banting shared the Nobel Prize in 1923.
- **Normal fasting blood glucose ≈ 3.9–5.5 mmol/L (70–100 mg/dL)**; **fasting ≥ 7.0 mmol/L** indicates diabetes (WHO).
- **Pancreas is a mixed gland** (both exocrine and endocrine).

## Quick revision
- **Cerebellum = balance**; **medulla = heartbeat & breathing**; **cerebrum = intelligence/memory**.
- Reflex action: **spinal cord**; 12 pairs cranial, 31 pairs spinal nerves.
- **206 bones**; femur longest, stapes smallest; skin largest organ.
- Gas exchange in **alveoli**; breathing ≈ 12–20/min; exhaled CO₂ ≈ 4%.
- **Nephron = unit of kidney**; urea made in liver; kidney stone = calcium oxalate.
- **Pituitary = master gland**; **thyroxine needs iodine** (goitre).
- **Insulin lowers blood sugar** (Banting & Best, 1921); **adrenaline = emergency hormone**.
- Melatonin: sleep; oxytocin: childbirth and milk ejection.

**3.** Which gland is called the "master gland"?
(a) Thyroid (b) Pituitary (c) Adrenal (d) Pancreas

**4.** Goitre is caused by deficiency of —
(a) calcium (b) iron (c) iodine (d) sodium

**5.** Which hormone lowers blood sugar?
(a) Glucagon (b) Adrenaline (c) Thyroxine (d) Insulin

**6.** Heartbeat and breathing are controlled by the —
(a) medulla oblongata (b) cerebrum (c) cerebellum (d) spinal cord

**7.** Exchange of gases in the lungs takes place in the —
(a) trachea (b) bronchi (c) larynx (d) alveoli

**8.** How many bones are there in an adult human body?
(a) 196 (b) 206 (c) 216 (d) 300

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | Cerebellum coordinates balance and movement |
| 2 | a | About 1 million nephrons per kidney |
| 3 | b | Pituitary controls other endocrine glands |
| 4 | c | Iodine is needed to make thyroxine |
| 5 | d | Insulin from β-cells lowers blood glucose |
| 6 | a | Medulla controls involuntary vital actions |
| 7 | d | Alveoli have thin walls and capillaries for diffusion |
| 8 | b | Adults have 206 bones |
