## 1975: a year of coups 🔥🔥
- **15 August 1975:** a group of army officers assassinated **Bangabandhu** and most of his family at **Dhanmondi 32**. His daughters **Sheikh Hasina** and **Sheikh Rehana** survived because they were abroad. **খন্দকার মোশতাক আহমদ** became President.
- **26 September 1975:** the **Indemnity Ordinance** barred trial of the killers. (It was repealed in **1996**; the killers were later tried and several executed.)
- 🔥 **3 November 1975 — জেল হত্যা:** four national leaders — **সৈয়দ নজরুল ইসলাম, তাজউদ্দীন আহমদ, এম. মনসুর আলী, এ. এইচ. এম. কামারুজ্জামান** — were killed inside **Dhaka Central Jail**. The same day **Brigadier Khaled Mosharraf** led a counter-coup.
- **6 November 1975:** Chief Justice **আবু সাদাত মোহাম্মদ সায়েম** became President (and Chief Martial Law Administrator).
- **7 November 1975:** a soldiers' uprising freed **Major General জিয়াউর রহমান**; Khaled Mosharraf was killed. (Parties describe this day differently.)

## Ziaur Rahman (1975–1981)
- Became **President on 21 April 1977**; confirmed by a **referendum (30 May 1977)**.
- 🔥 **First presidential election by direct vote: 3 June 1978** — Zia defeated **General M. A. G. Osmani**.
- Founded the **Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP)** on **1 September 1978**.
- **Second parliamentary election: 18 February 1979** (BNP won a majority). The **5th amendment (1979)** ratified the martial-law period.
- Zia was **assassinated on 30 May 1981** at the **Chattogram Circuit House**. Vice-President **Justice আবদুস সাত্তার** became acting President and then won the presidential election (November 1981).

## H. M. Ershad (1982–1990)
- 🔥 **24 March 1982:** Army chief **হুসেইন মুহম্মদ এরশাদ** seized power in a bloodless coup and declared **martial law**.
- Founded the **Jatiya Party** (1 January 1986). Parliamentary elections: **3rd — 7 May 1986**, **4th — 3 March 1988** (boycotted by the main opposition).
- **8th amendment (1988):** Islam made the state religion.
- Anti-Ershad movement: **নূর হোসেন** was killed on **10 November 1987** with "স্বৈরাচার নিপাত যাক, গণতন্ত্র মুক্তি পাক" written on his body. **Dr. মিলন** was killed on 27 November 1990.
- 🔥 **6 December 1990:** Ershad **resigned** after a mass uprising. Chief Justice **সাহাবুদ্দীন আহমদ** became acting President and headed a neutral interim (caretaker) government.

## 2024 change of government 🔥
- **July–August 2024:** a student-led **quota-reform movement** grew into a mass uprising against the government; hundreds of people were killed.
- **5 August 2024:** Prime Minister **Sheikh Hasina resigned and left the country**.
- **6 August 2024:** President **মোহাম্মদ সাহাবুদ্দিন** dissolved the 12th parliament.
- **8 August 2024:** an **interim government** was sworn in, headed by **Chief Adviser ড. মুহাম্মদ ইউনূস** (Nobel Peace laureate, 2006). It set up reform commissions (constitution, election, judiciary, public administration, police, anti-corruption, etc.).

> 🔄 Update: a national election and constitutional reforms were planned after the interim period. Check the result of the latest parliamentary election and the current Prime Minister and President in a newspaper.

## Quick revision
- First parliamentary election: **7 March 1973** (AL 293/300).
- 4th amendment / one-party system: **25 January 1975**; BAKSAL **24 February 1975**.
- Bangabandhu assassinated: **15 August 1975**.
- Jail killing of four national leaders: **3 November 1975**.
- First direct presidential election: **3 June 1978** (Zia vs Osmani).
- BNP founded **1 September 1978**; Jatiya Party **1 January 1986**.
- Ershad's coup **24 March 1982**; resigned **6 December 1990**.
- First woman PM: **Khaleda Zia (1991)**.
- Caretaker system: **13th amendment (1996)**; abolished by **15th (2011)**.
- Interim government under **Dr. Muhammad Yunus**: **8 August 2024**.
