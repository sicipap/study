# National symbols, anthem, flag, emblem

> **Why it matters:** Flag ratio and design, the anthem's writer and line count, the emblem's elements and the list of national symbols are asked in nearly every job exam, including primary and bank exams.

## Constitution
- **Article 3:** State language — **Bangla**.
- **Article 4:** **National anthem, national flag and national emblem**.
- **Article 5:** Capital — **Dhaka**.

## National anthem (জাতীয় সংগীত) 🔥🔥
- Song: **"আমার সোনার বাংলা, আমি তোমায় ভালোবাসি"**.
- Writer and composer: **রবীন্দ্রনাথ ঠাকুর**; written in **1905** during the movement against the **Partition of Bengal (বঙ্গভঙ্গ)**.
- The full song has **25 lines**; the **first 10 lines** are the national anthem.
- The tune is based on the Baul song **"আমি কোথায় পাব তারে"** by **গগন হরকরা**.
- Declared as the anthem on **3 March 1971** (Paltan meeting of the Swadhin Bangla Chhatra Sangram Parishad); officially adopted by the government on **13 January 1972**.
- English translation: **সৈয়দ আলী আহসান**.
- Instrumental version: first **4 lines**.

## National march song (রণসংগীত) 🔥
- **"চল্‌ চল্‌ চল্‌, ঊর্ধ্বগগনে বাজে মাদল"** by **কাজী নজরুল ইসলাম** (from **"সন্ধ্যা"**).
- The first **21 lines** are used. Adopted on **13 January 1972**.

## National flag (জাতীয় পতাকা) 🔥🔥
- A **red circle** on a **dark green** rectangle.
  - Green = youthfulness and the green land; red = the **blood of the martyrs** and the rising sun.
- **Ratio length : width = 10 : 6 (5 : 3)**.
- **Radius of the circle = 1/5 of the length**.
- The centre of the circle is placed at **9/20 of the length from the pole side** (slightly towards the pole), on the middle line of the width.
- **First hoisted:** **2 March 1971** at the **Kala Bhaban, University of Dhaka**, by **আ স ম আবদুর রব** (the original flag had a yellow map in the red circle, designed by **শিব নারায়ণ দাশ**).
- The map was removed and the **final design** by **পটুয়া কামরুল হাসান** was adopted on **17 January 1972**.
- Rules: **Bangladesh Flag Rules, 1972 (পতাকা বিধিমালা)**.

| Use | Sizes |
|---|---|
| Buildings | 10′ × 6′, 5′ × 3′, 2½′ × 1½′ |
| Cars | 15″ × 9″, 10″ × 6″ |
| Table (for meetings) | 10″ × 6″ |

## National emblem (জাতীয় প্রতীক) 🔥🔥
- A **শাপলা (water lily)** floating on **water**,
- bordered on both sides by **ears of paddy (ধানের শীষ)**,
- with **three connected jute leaves (পাটপাতা)** above it,
- and **two stars on each side** of the leaves (**4 stars** in all).

## National Memorial (জাতীয় স্মৃতিসৌধ) 🔥
- At **Nabinagar, Savar, Dhaka**; architect **সৈয়দ মাইনুল হোসেন**.
- Foundation stone laid on **16 December 1972**; completed in **1982**.
- Height **150 feet (45.72 m)**; made of **7 pairs of triangular walls**, representing seven stages of the independence struggle (**1952, 1954, 1956, 1962, 1966, 1969, 1971**).

## Central Shaheed Minar (কেন্দ্রীয় শহীদ মিনার)
- Near Dhaka Medical College; designed by **হামিদুর রহমান** (with **নভেরা আহমেদ**).
- The mother figure with fallen sons; the red sun behind.

## Quick revision
- Anthem: **Rabindranath**, **1905**, first **10 lines** of 25; adopted **13 Jan 1972**.
- March song: **Nazrul's "Chol Chol Chol"**, **21 lines**.
- Flag ratio **10:6**; circle radius **1/5** of length; final design **Quamrul Hassan**, adopted **17 Jan 1972**.
- First flag hoisted **2 March 1971**, DU, by **ASM Abdur Rab**.
- Emblem: **water lily, paddy, 3 jute leaves, 4 stars**.
- National tree **mango**; fruit **jackfruit**; bird **doyel**; game **kabaddi**.
- Memorial: **Syed Mainul Hossain**, **7 pairs** of walls, **150 ft**.
