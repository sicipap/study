# Human rights & gender equality

> **Why it matters:** Exams regularly ask about the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (dates, number of articles), the two 1966 Covenants, the generations of rights, CEDAW, the Beijing Conference, Bangladesh's National Human Rights Commission, and constitutional and legal protections for women.

## Human rights: concept 🔥
- **Human rights (মানবাধিকার)** are rights every person has **simply by being human**, regardless of nationality, sex, religion, race or any other status.
- Features: **universal, inalienable** (cannot be taken away), **indivisible, interdependent**, and equal for all.

## Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR) 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Adopted by | **UN General Assembly**, in **Paris** |
| Date | **10 December 1948** (hence **Human Rights Day, 10 December**) |
| Articles | **30** |
| Drafting committee chaired by | **Eleanor Roosevelt** |
| Legal status | A **declaration** (not a binding treaty), but highly influential |

- **International Bill of Human Rights** = UDHR + **ICCPR** (International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights) + **ICESCR** (International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights).
- Both Covenants were adopted in **1966** and came into force in **1976**.

## Generations of human rights
Idea of **Karel Vasak** (1977), linked to the French Revolution slogan:
| Generation | Rights | Linked to |
|---|---|---|
| **First** | **Civil and political** (life, liberty, speech, vote, fair trial) | **Liberty** |
| **Second** | **Economic, social and cultural** (work, education, health, housing) | **Equality** |
| **Third** | **Collective / solidarity** (development, peace, healthy environment) | **Fraternity** |

## UN human rights bodies
- **UN Human Rights Council** (Geneva): created in **2006**, replacing the **Commission on Human Rights** (1946); **47** member states.
- **Office of the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR)**: created in **1993**.

## National Human Rights Commission, Bangladesh 🔥
- Established under the **National Human Rights Commission Act 2009** (an earlier commission was set up by ordinance in 2008).
- A **statutory, independent** body: a **Chairman and members** (the Act allows up to six members besides the Chairman).
- Can investigate human rights violations, visit prisons, recommend action and compensation, and raise awareness.

## Gender equality (লিঙ্গ সমতা) 🔥
- **Sex** = biological difference; **gender** = **socially constructed** roles and expectations of men and women.
- **Gender equality:** equal rights, responsibilities and opportunities. **Gender equity:** fairness, including special measures to correct past disadvantage.

| Instrument / event | Fact |
|---|---|
| **CEDAW** (Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women) | Adopted **18 December 1979**; in force **3 September 1981**; called the "**international bill of rights for women**"; **30 articles** |
| **Bangladesh and CEDAW** | Ratified in **1984**, with reservations; reservations remain on **Article 2** and **Article 16.1(c)** |
| **Fourth World Conference on Women** | **Beijing, 1995**; the Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action set **12 critical areas of concern** |
| **International Women's Day** | **8 March** |
| **UN Women** | Created in **2010** |
| **SDG 5** | **Gender equality** |
| **Global Gender Gap Index** | Published by the **World Economic Forum** since **2006** |

## Women's rights in Bangladesh 🔥
| Provision / law | Content |
|---|---|
| Constitution **Article 27** | Equality before law |
| **Article 28(1)–(2)** | No discrimination on grounds of sex; **women shall have equal rights with men in all spheres of the State and of public life** |
| **Article 28(4)** | State may make **special provisions for women, children** or backward sections |
| **Article 65(3)** | **Reserved seats for women** in Parliament (**50** seats under the 17th Amendment, 2018) |
| **Women and Children Repression Prevention Act 2000** | Death penalty for rape added by amendment in **2020** |
| **Domestic Violence (Prevention and Protection) Act 2010** | Protection from family violence |
| **Child Marriage Restraint Act 2017** | Minimum age **18 for girls, 21 for boys** |
| **Dowry Prohibition Act 2018** | Replaced the 1980 Act |
| **National Women Development Policy 2011** | Earlier policy in 1997 |

> 🔄 Update: the reserved-seat system and several of these laws were under review in the 2024–26 reform process; check the latest position.

## Quick revision
- UDHR: 10 December 1948, Paris, 30 articles; Eleanor Roosevelt chaired drafting.
- ICCPR and ICESCR: 1966, in force 1976.
- Generations: 1st civil-political (liberty), 2nd socio-economic (equality), 3rd solidarity (fraternity).
- Human Rights Council 2006, Geneva, 47 members.
- Bangladesh NHRC Act 2009.
- CEDAW: 18 December 1979; Bangladesh ratified 1984 (reservations on Articles 2 and 16.1(c)).
- Beijing Conference 1995: 12 critical areas.
- Article 28(2): equal rights for women in all spheres of State and public life.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted on:
(a) 24 October 1945 (b) 10 December 1948 (c) 16 December 1966 (d) 18 December 1979

**2.** How many articles does the Universal Declaration of Human Rights have?
(a) 30 (b) 25 (c) 35 (d) 40

**3.** CEDAW is often called the international bill of rights for:
(a) children (b) refugees (c) workers (d) women

**4.** The Fourth World Conference on Women (1995) was held in:
(a) Nairobi (b) Mexico City (c) Beijing (d) Copenhagen

**5.** First-generation human rights are mainly:
(a) economic and social rights (b) civil and political rights (c) environmental rights (d) the right to development

**6.** The National Human Rights Commission of Bangladesh works under an Act passed in:
(a) 2009 (b) 2000 (c) 2011 (d) 2018

**7.** Which article of the Constitution of Bangladesh says women shall have equal rights with men in all spheres of the State and of public life?
(a) Article 27 (b) Article 32 (c) Article 65 (d) Article 28

**8.** Under the Child Marriage Restraint Act 2017, the minimum legal age of marriage for a girl in Bangladesh is:
(a) 16 years (b) 21 years (c) 18 years (d) 20 years

**9.** The UN Human Rights Council, which replaced the Commission on Human Rights, was created in:
(a) 1993 (b) 2006 (c) 1946 (d) 2010

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Adopted in Paris on 10 December 1948 (Human Rights Day). |
| 2 | a | The UDHR has 30 articles. |
| 3 | d | CEDAW is the international bill of rights for women. |
| 4 | c | Beijing, 1995: Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action. |
| 5 | b | First generation = civil and political rights (liberty). |
| 6 | a | National Human Rights Commission Act 2009. |
| 7 | d | Article 28(2). |
| 8 | c | 18 for girls and 21 for boys. |
| 9 | b | Created in 2006, based in Geneva. |
