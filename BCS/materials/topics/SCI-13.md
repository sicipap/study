# Everyday chemistry: fertilizer, soap, glass, cement, common names

> **Why it matters:** "Chemical name/formula of baking soda, washing soda, alum, blue vitriol, plaster of Paris?" is one of the most repeated question types. Fertilizers, soap, glass and cement facts also come every year.

## Fertilizers (সার) 🔥
- **Primary plant nutrients: N, P, K** (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium).

| Fertilizer | Nutrient | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Urea — CO(NH₂)₂** | **Nitrogen (≈ 46% N)** | **Most used fertilizer in Bangladesh**; made from **natural gas** (ammonia + CO₂) |
| **TSP (triple super phosphate)** | **Phosphorus** | |
| **MoP (muriate of potash, KCl)** | **Potassium** | |
| **DAP (diammonium phosphate)** | **N + P** | |
| Ammonium sulphate | N + S | |
| **Gypsum** | **Sulphur** (+ calcium) | |
| **Zinc sulphate** | **Zinc** | Zinc deficiency causes "khaira" disease of rice |
| Compost, cow dung, vermicompost | Organic | Improves soil structure |

- **Ammonia (NH₃)** is made by the **Haber (Haber–Bosch) process** (N₂ + 3H₂, iron catalyst). **Sulphuric acid** by the **contact process**.
- Urea factories in Bangladesh include **Ghorasal–Palash (Narsingdi)**, **Jamuna (Sarishabari, Jamalpur)**, **Shahjalal (Fenchuganj, Sylhet)**, Ashuganj, **KAFCO** and Chittagong Urea (Chattogram).
- Nitrogen deficiency → leaves turn **yellow**; nitrogen-fixing bacteria (**Rhizobium**) live in legume root nodules.

## Soap, detergent and hard water 🔥
- **Soap = sodium or potassium salt of long-chain fatty acids**. Made by **saponification (সাবানায়ন)**: **fat/oil + NaOH (or KOH) → soap + glycerol**.
- **Sodium soap = hard soap** (washing); **potassium soap = soft soap** (shaving cream, liquid soap).
- Soap molecule has a water-loving (hydrophilic) head and oil-loving (hydrophobic) tail → forms **micelles** that remove grease.
- **Hard water** contains **Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺** salts; soap forms **scum** and does not lather.
  - **Temporary hardness**: bicarbonates of Ca/Mg — removed by **boiling** (or Clark's method, adding lime).
  - **Permanent hardness**: chlorides/sulphates of Ca/Mg — removed by **washing soda**, ion-exchange (permutit).
- **Detergents** (sulphonates) work well even in **hard water**; some are non-biodegradable and pollute water.

## Glass (কাচ) 🔥
- Glass is an **amorphous solid / super-cooled liquid** (no fixed melting point).
- **Ordinary (soda-lime) glass**: **sand (SiO₂) + soda ash (Na₂CO₃) + limestone (CaCO₃)** melted together.

| Type | Special ingredient / use |
|---|---|
| **Pyrex (borosilicate)** | Boron oxide; **heat-resistant** lab ware, ovenware |
| **Flint / crystal (lead) glass** | Lead oxide; lenses, prisms, decorative crystal |
| Photochromic glass | Silver halide; sunglasses that darken in sunlight |
| Safety / bullet-proof glass | Layers of glass and plastic |
| Optical fibre | Very pure silica glass |

| Colouring agent | Colour of glass |
|---|---|
| **Cobalt oxide** | **Blue** |
| **Chromium oxide** | **Green** |
| **Manganese dioxide** | **Purple / violet** |
| **Gold (chloride)** | **Ruby red** |
| Cuprous oxide | Red |

- Glass is **etched by hydrofluoric acid (HF)**.

## Cement and construction 🔥
- **Portland cement** was invented by **Joseph Aspdin (England, 1824)**; set cement looked like Portland stone.
- Raw materials: **limestone + clay** (heated to form "clinker"); **gypsum** is added to **slow down setting**.
- Main compounds: calcium silicates and calcium aluminate.
- **Mortar** = cement + sand + water. **Concrete** = cement + sand + gravel/stone chips + water. **RCC** = concrete reinforced with steel rods.
- Cement sets by **hydration** (reaction with water); curing (keeping it wet) makes it strong.
- **Chhatak Cement Factory (Sunamganj)** is the oldest cement factory in Bangladesh.

## Other everyday chemistry
- **Gunpowder**: potassium nitrate + sulphur + charcoal (≈ 75 : 10 : 15).
- **Dynamite**: **Alfred Nobel (1867)**, based on **nitroglycerine**. TNT = trinitrotoluene.
- Matchbox side has **red phosphorus**; match head has potassium chlorate and antimony trisulphide.
- Photography uses **silver bromide (AgBr)**. Mirror backing: silver (now often aluminium).
- Fluoride in toothpaste prevents tooth decay.

## Quick revision
- **Baking soda NaHCO₃; washing soda Na₂CO₃·10H₂O; caustic soda NaOH.**
- **Quicklime CaO; slaked lime Ca(OH)₂; limestone CaCO₃.**
- **Plaster of Paris CaSO₄·½H₂O; gypsum CaSO₄·2H₂O.**
- **Blue vitriol (তুঁতে) CuSO₄·5H₂O; alum (ফিটকিরি) purifies water.**
- **Bleaching powder CaOCl₂; laughing gas N₂O; silver nitrate for voting ink.**
- **Urea ≈ 46% N**, made from natural gas; TSP = P; MoP = K.
- Soap: **saponification** (fat + NaOH); hard water has Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺.
- Glass = sand + soda ash + limestone; **Pyrex** heat-resistant; cobalt → blue.
- Portland cement: **Joseph Aspdin, 1824**; gypsum slows setting.

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Baking soda = sodium bicarbonate, NaHCO₃ |
| 2 | d | Heating gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) gives CaSO₄·½H₂O |
| 3 | a | Blue vitriol = CuSO₄·5H₂O |
| 4 | c | Urea contains about 46% nitrogen |
| 5 | b | Alum (ফিটকিরি) makes suspended particles settle |

| 8 | a | Gypsum controls (retards) setting time |
