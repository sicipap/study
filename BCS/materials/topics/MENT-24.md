# Spelling & sentence rearrangement

> **Why it matters:** "Choose the correctly spelt word" and "arrange the parts to make a meaningful sentence or paragraph" appear in both the mental ability and English sections of bank, PSC and BCS papers. A short list of trap words and a few ordering clues give easy marks.

## Useful spelling rules
1. **i before e, except after c** (believe, achieve; but receive, ceiling). Exceptions: seize, weird, foreign, neither.
2. **Doubling the final consonant:** a short word or a word **stressed on the last syllable** ending consonant-vowel-consonant doubles it: begin → **beginning**, occur → **occurred**, refer → **referred**. If the stress is not on the last syllable, do not double: benefit → **benefited**, visit → **visited**.
3. **Silent e:** drop it before a vowel suffix (hope → hoping, write → writing) but keep it before a consonant suffix (hope → hopeful). Keep it after soft c or g before -able/-ous: **noticeable, manageable, courageous**.
4. **-ful** has one l (**careful, beautiful**); **full** alone has two.

## Sentence rearrangement: clues 🔥
| Clue | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Subject (noun or pronoun) | Usually comes first in a sentence |
| **Noun before pronoun** | "Rahim … he" – the pronoun must come after the noun it refers to |
| **"a/an" before "the"** | A new thing is introduced with "a", then referred to with "the" |
| Linking words | **First, then, next, finally**; however; therefore; but; as a result |
| Time order | Earlier events first |
| Verb after subject; object after verb | Basic English order: Subject + Verb + Object + Place + Time |

**Method:** find the **opening part** (usually has the subject and no linker), then the **closing part** (often a conclusion, "finally", or a full stop idea), then join the middle using pronouns and linkers. Check the options: often only one starts correctly.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** "The farmer" + P: in the field / Q: was working / R: when it started / S: to rain heavily.
Subject + verb + place + time clause: **Q P R S** → "The farmer was working in the field when it started to rain heavily."

**Example 2.** P: Then he boiled the water. Q: First, Rahim filled the kettle with water. R: Finally, he poured the tea into a cup. S: Next, he added tea leaves to the boiling water.
"First" (with the name Rahim) → then → next → finally: **Q P S R**.

**Example 3.** Which is correct: Accomodation, Accommodation, Acommodation?
Double c and double m → **Accommodation**.

## Quick revision
- Accommodation, committee, millennium, necessary, separate, occurrence.
- Receive (after c: ei); believe (ie).
- Beginning, occurred, referred; but benefited, visited.
- Noticeable, manageable, courageous keep the e.
- In paragraphs: noun before pronoun, "a" before "the", first → then → next → finally.
- Find the opening part first, then the closing part.
