# E-commerce & e-governance

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the types of e-commerce (B2B, B2C, C2C), examples of each, the meaning of G2C/G2B, and Bangladesh's e-governance services such as Union Digital Centres, 999, 333 and e-GP. Bank exams add digital payment systems (BEFTN, RTGS, MFS).

## E-commerce (ই-কমার্স) 🔥
- **Buying and selling goods and services over the internet** (electronic commerce).
- **M-commerce:** e-commerce through **mobile phones**. **F-commerce:** selling through **Facebook** pages (very common in Bangladesh). **S-commerce:** social commerce.
- **E-business** is broader: all business activities done electronically (including supply chain, CRM).

### Types of e-commerce 🔥🔥
| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **B2B** (Business to Business) | Business sells to another business | **Alibaba.com**, wholesale suppliers |
| 🔥 **B2C** (Business to Consumer) | Business sells to individual buyers | **Amazon**, **Daraz**, Chaldal, Rokomari |
| 🔥 **C2C** (Consumer to Consumer) | Individuals sell to each other | **eBay**, **Bikroy.com**, OLX |
| **C2B** (Consumer to Business) | Individuals sell to businesses | Freelancers, stock-photo sellers |
| **B2G** (Business to Government) | Businesses sell to government | Online tenders (e-GP) |

- **Amazon:** founded by **Jeff Bezos** (1994), began as an online bookstore.
- **Alibaba:** founded by **Jack Ma** (1999), China. Alibaba bought **Daraz** in **2018**.
- **eBay:** founded by **Pierre Omidyar** (1995), online auctions.

### Online payment terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **EFT** | Electronic Funds Transfer |
| **Payment gateway** | Service that processes card/online payments for websites (e.g. SSLCOMMERZ in Bangladesh) |
| **COD** | Cash on Delivery — most common payment method in Bangladesh e-commerce |
| **Escrow** | A third party holds the buyer's money until the product is delivered |
| **MFS** (Mobile Financial Service) | **bKash** (BRAC Bank subsidiary, 2011), **Rocket** (Dutch-Bangla Bank, 2011), **Nagad** (Bangladesh Post Office's digital service, 2019) |
| **Digital wallet** | Stores payment information on a phone |

### Bangladesh Bank payment systems 🔥
| System | Full form / job |
|---|---|
| **BACH** | Bangladesh Automated Clearing House: **BACPS** (cheque clearing, 2010) + **BEFTN** (electronic fund transfer, 2011) |
| 🔥 **RTGS** | **Real Time Gross Settlement**: high-value transfers settled instantly, one by one (Bangladesh, **2015**) |
| **NPSB** | National Payment Switch Bangladesh: connects ATMs, POS and internet banking of different banks (2012) |

- In Bangladesh, e-commerce is regulated by the **Ministry of Commerce**, which issued the **Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines in 2021** (after scandals such as Evaly). Trade body: **e-CAB** (e-Commerce Association of Bangladesh).

## E-governance (ই-গভর্ন্যান্স) 🔥
- **Using ICT to deliver government services and information** to citizens, businesses and other government bodies, making government **transparent, efficient and accountable**.
- **E-government** = digital delivery of services; **e-governance** is broader (includes citizen participation).

| Model | Meaning | Bangladesh example |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **G2C** (Government to Citizen) | Services to citizens | Online birth registration, e-passport, land records, **Union Digital Centres** |
| **G2B** (Government to Business) | Services to businesses | Online trade licence, **e-GP** tenders, online VAT/tax |
| **G2G** (Government to Government) | Between offices | **e-Nothi** (digital file system) |
| **G2E** (Government to Employee) | For government staff | Online pay fixation, iBAS++ (salary/budget system) |

### E-governance services in Bangladesh 🔥
| Service | Key fact |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **Union Digital Centre (UDC / ইউনিয়ন ডিজিটাল সেন্টার)** | One-stop service centres at every union; launched nationwide on **11 November 2010** (4,501 centres) |
| **a2i (Aspire to Innovate)** | Government programme (with UNDP support) behind many digital services |
| **National Portal** | **bangladesh.gov.bd** — tens of thousands of government office websites in one framework |
| 🔥 **999** | **National emergency service** (police, fire, ambulance), free; launched **2017** |
| **333** | **National call centre** for government information and services |
| **e-GP** | **Electronic Government Procurement**, run by **CPTU** (now the **Bangladesh Public Procurement Authority, BPPA**), started **2011** |
| **e-Return** | Online income-tax return (National Board of Revenue) |
| **NID / e-Passport** | E-passport launched **January 2020**; NID managed by the Election Commission |
| **Ekpay** | Single platform for paying utility bills |

- **Digital Bangladesh** was announced as a national vision in **December 2008** (target 2021). **12 December** has been observed as **Digital Bangladesh Day** (from 2017). A later vision, "Smart Bangladesh 2041", was announced in 2022.

> 🔄 Update: government programme names and rankings (e.g. the UN E-Government Development Index) change; check the latest news.

## Quick revision
- Amazon, Daraz = **B2C**; Alibaba = **B2B**; eBay, Bikroy = **C2C**.
- M-commerce = via **mobile**; F-commerce = via **Facebook**.
- bKash **2011** (BRAC Bank); Rocket (Dutch-Bangla Bank); Nagad (Post Office, 2019).
- RTGS = **Real Time Gross Settlement** (2015 in Bangladesh).
- G2C = government to **citizen**; e-Nothi = **G2G**.
- Union Digital Centres: **11 November 2010**.
- 999 = **national emergency service**; 333 = **national call centre**.
- e-GP = online government procurement (**2011**).

## Practice MCQ
**1.** Daraz selling products to individual buyers is an example of:
(a) B2B (b) C2C (c) B2C (d) G2B

**2.** eBay and Bikroy.com mainly follow which model?
(a) C2C (b) B2B (c) G2C (d) B2G

**3.** Online birth registration for citizens is an example of:
(a) G2B (b) G2G (c) G2E (d) G2C

**4.** What is the national emergency service number in Bangladesh?
(a) 333 (b) 999 (c) 16263 (d) 109

**5.** Union Digital Centres were launched across Bangladesh in:
(a) 2008 (b) 2014 (c) 2010 (d) 2017

**6.** RTGS stands for:
(a) Real Time Gross Settlement (b) Rapid Transfer Gateway System (c) Regional Treasury General Service (d) Real Transaction Global Switch

**7.** e-Nothi, used for moving files between government offices, falls under:
(a) G2C (b) B2C (c) C2C (d) G2G

**8.** Alibaba, founded by Jack Ma, is mainly known as a platform of:
(a) G2C (b) C2B (c) B2B (d) G2G

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | Business selling to consumers |
| 2 | a | Individuals sell to individuals |
| 3 | d | Government service to citizens |
| 4 | b | 999 for police, fire and ambulance |
| 5 | c | 11 November 2010, 4,501 centres |
| 6 | a | High-value real-time settlement |
| 7 | d | Between government offices |
| 8 | c | Alibaba.com connects businesses |
