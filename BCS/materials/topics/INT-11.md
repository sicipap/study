## NATO (North Atlantic Treaty Organization / ন্যাটো) 🔥
- 🔥 **North Atlantic Treaty signed 4 April 1949, Washington, D.C.** A Western military alliance against the USSR in the Cold War.
- 🔥 **HQ: Brussels, Belgium** (moved from Paris in 1967).
- **12 founding members:** USA, Canada, UK, France, Italy, Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Denmark, Norway, Iceland, Portugal.
- Main expansions:

| Year | New members |
|---|---|
| 1952 | Greece, Türkiye |
| 1955 | West Germany |
| 1982 | Spain |
| 1999 | Poland, Hungary, Czech Republic (first ex-Warsaw Pact states) |
| 2004 | 7 states incl. the Baltic states (Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania) |
| 2009 / 2017 / 2020 | Albania & Croatia / Montenegro / North Macedonia |
| 🔥 **2023** | **Finland** (31st, 4 April 2023) |
| 🔥 **2024** | **Sweden** (32nd, 7 March 2024) |

- 🔥 **Article 5:** an armed attack on one member is an attack on **all** (collective defence). It has been invoked **only once**, after the **9/11 attacks (2001)** on the USA.
- The top military commander (**SACEUR**) is always an American.
- **France** left NATO's integrated military command in **1966** (de Gaulle) and returned in **2009**.
- **The Hague summit (June 2025):** members agreed to raise defence spending to **5% of GDP by 2035**.
- Finland and Sweden joined after **Russia's invasion of Ukraine (2022)**.
- **Warsaw Pact** (USSR-led, **1955**) was NATO's rival; it was **dissolved in 1991**.

## QUAD (Quadrilateral Security Dialogue / কোয়াড) 🔥
- 🔥 **Members: USA, Japan, India, Australia.**
- Roots: cooperation after the **2004 Indian Ocean tsunami**; idea pushed by Japan's PM **Shinzo Abe (2007)**; dropped, then **revived in 2017**.
- **First leaders' summit:** virtual, **March 2021**; first in-person summit, **Washington, September 2021**.
- It is **not a formal military alliance** and has **no secretariat**. Focus: a "**free and open Indo-Pacific**", maritime security, vaccines, critical technology.
- Linked naval exercise: **Malabar** (India–USA from 1992; Japan and Australia later joined).
- China criticises QUAD as an "Asian NATO".

## AUKUS (অকাস) 🔥
- 🔥 A **trilateral security pact of Australia, the UK and the USA**, announced **15 September 2021**.
- **Pillar I:** helping **Australia acquire nuclear-powered (but conventionally armed) submarines**. **Pillar II:** advanced technologies (AI, cyber, hypersonics, quantum).
- Australia cancelled a submarine deal with **France**, which briefly **recalled its ambassadors** from the USA and Australia.
- Seen as a response to China's rise in the Indo-Pacific.

## SCO (Shanghai Cooperation Organisation / সাংহাই সহযোগিতা সংস্থা) 🔥
- Began as the **"Shanghai Five" (1996)**: China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan.
- 🔥 **SCO founded 15 June 2001, Shanghai**, when **Uzbekistan** joined.
- **Secretariat: Beijing**; **Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure (RATS): Tashkent**.
- Official languages: **Russian and Chinese**.
- 🔥 **India and Pakistan** became full members in **2017**; **Iran** in **2023**; **Belarus** in **2024** → **10 members**.
- Members (10): China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, India, Pakistan, Iran, Belarus.
- Focus: fighting the "**three evils**" — **terrorism, separatism and extremism**; also trade and security.
- **2025 summit: Tianjin, China.**

## Quick revision
- NATO: **4 April 1949**; HQ **Brussels**; **32** members; newest **Finland (2023)** and **Sweden (2024)**.
- Article 5 invoked only once: after **9/11**.
- QUAD: **USA, Japan, India, Australia**; revived **2017**.
- AUKUS: **Australia, UK, USA**; **2021**; nuclear-powered submarines for Australia.
- SCO: **2001, Shanghai**; secretariat **Beijing**; **10** members; newest **Belarus (2024)**.
- Pakistan was a member of both **SEATO** and **CENTO**.
