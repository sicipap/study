# Statistics: mean, median, mode

> **Why it matters:** Basic statistics (পরিসংখ্যান) — mean (গড়), median (মধ্যক) and mode (প্রচুরক) — are asked in BCS, bank and primary exams as quick calculations, plus the relation Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean.

## Key concepts
- **Data (উপাত্ত):** raw facts in numbers. **Primary data** is collected first-hand; **secondary data** is taken from existing sources.
- **Measures of central tendency (কেন্দ্রীয় প্রবণতার পরিমাপ):** mean, median, mode.
- **Mean (গাণিতিক গড়):** sum of values ÷ number of values. It is affected most by **extreme values**.
- **Median (মধ্যক):** the **middle value** after arranging the data in order.
  - n odd → the **(n + 1)/2-th** value.
  - n even → average of the **n/2-th** and **(n/2 + 1)-th** values.
- **Mode (প্রচুরক):** the value that occurs **most often**. A data set can have no mode or more than one mode.
- **Range (পরিসর):** largest value − smallest value (a measure of dispersion, বিস্তার).
- **Frequency (গণসংখ্যা):** how many times a value occurs.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| Mean (raw data) | Σx / n |
| Mean (frequency table) | Σfx / Σf |
| Median (odd n) | value at position (n + 1)/2 |
| Median (even n) | average of positions n/2 and n/2 + 1 |
| 🔥 Empirical relation | **Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean** |
| Grouped median | L + [(n/2 − F)/f] × h |
| Grouped mode | L + [f₁/(f₁ + f₂)] × h, where f₁ = f − f(before), f₂ = f − f(after) |
| New data ax + b | new mean = a × old mean + b |
| Range | max − min |

(L = lower limit of the class, F = cumulative frequency before it, f = its frequency, h = class width.)

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Find the mean of 4, 8, 6, 10, 12.
Steps: 40/5 = 8.
**Answer: 8**

**Example 2.** Find the median of 7, 3, 9, 5, 11.
Steps: Order: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11. Middle (3rd) value = 7.
**Answer: 7**

**Example 3.** Find the median of 2, 8, 4, 6, 10, 12.
Steps: Order: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. Average of 3rd and 4th = (6 + 8)/2 = 7.
**Answer: 7**

**Example 4.** Find the mode of 3, 5, 3, 7, 5, 3, 9.
Steps: 3 occurs three times (most).
**Answer: 3**

**Example 5.** In a distribution, mean = 20 and median = 22. Estimate the mode.
Steps: 3 × 22 − 2 × 20 = 66 − 40 = 26.
**Answer: 26**

**Example 6.** Find the mean: x = 1, 2, 3 with frequencies 2, 3, 5.
Steps: Σfx = 2 + 6 + 15 = 23; Σf = 10 → 2.3.
**Answer: 2.3**

## Common traps
- Finding the median **without sorting** the data first.
- For an even count, the median is the average of the **two** middle values.
- Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean (not 3 Mean − 2 Median).
- Mean is pulled by outliers; median is not. Median is better for skewed data such as income.

## Quick revision
- Mean = Σx/n; with frequencies Σfx/Σf.
- Median: sort first; (n + 1)/2-th value for odd n.
- Mode = most frequent value.
- Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean.
- Range = max − min.
- Mean is most affected by extreme values.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** The mean of the first five prime numbers is:
(a) 5 (b) 5.6 (c) 6 (d) 3.6

**2.** The median of 12, 5, 9, 15, 7, 11 is:
(a) 9 (b) 11 (c) 10 (d) 9.8

**3.** The mode of 2, 4, 4, 6, 6, 6, 8 is:
(a) 4 (b) 5.1 (c) 8 (d) 6

**4.** The mean of 5 numbers is 18. If the number 10 is removed, the mean of the rest is:
(a) 20 (b) 22 (c) 16 (d) 18

**5.** In a distribution, the median is 15 and the mean is 16. The mode is:
(a) 17 (b) 13 (c) 15.5 (d) 14

**6.** The range of 23, 7, 45, 12, 30 is:
(a) 45 (b) 23 (c) 38 (d) 33

**7.** The median of the first 10 natural numbers is:
(a) 5.5 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 10

**8.** The mean of some values of x is 10. The mean of the values 2x + 3 is:
(a) 20 (b) 13 (c) 26 (d) 23

**9.** The values 10, 20, 30 have frequencies 3, 5, 2. The mean is:
(a) 20 (b) 19 (c) 18 (d) 21

**10.** Which measure is most affected by extreme values?
(a) mode (b) median (c) mean (d) none of these

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | (2 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 11)/5 = 28/5 = 5.6 |
| 2 | c | Sorted 5, 7, 9, 11, 12, 15 → (9 + 11)/2 = 10 |
| 3 | d | 6 occurs three times |
| 4 | a | (90 − 10)/4 = 20 |
| 5 | b | 3 × 15 − 2 × 16 = 13 |
| 6 | c | 45 − 7 = 38 |
| 7 | a | (5 + 6)/2 = 5.5 |
| 8 | d | 2 × 10 + 3 = 23 |
| 9 | b | (30 + 100 + 60)/10 = 19 |
| 10 | c | Mean uses every value, so outliers pull it most |
