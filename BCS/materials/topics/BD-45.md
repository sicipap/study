# Blue economy, climate change, Delta Plan 2100

> **Why it matters:** Maritime area, the meaning of "blue economy", Swatch of No Ground, the Climate Change Trust Fund and Delta Plan 2100 hotspots are frequent MCQs and written-exam points.

## Blue economy (সুনীল অর্থনীতি) 🔥🔥
- Meaning: using **ocean and sea resources** for economic growth, jobs and better livelihoods **while keeping the ocean healthy**.
- The term is linked to Belgian economist **Gunter Pauli** (book *The Blue Economy*, 2010).
- Bangladesh's sea area after the **2012 (Myanmar)** and **2014 (India)** verdicts: about **1,18,813 sq km** (territorial sea, EEZ and continental shelf) — see BD-25.
- **Blue Economy Cell** (2017) coordinates the work (under the Energy and Mineral Resources Division).
- Related SDG: **SDG 14 – Life below water**.

### Sectors
| Sector | Examples |
|---|---|
| Fisheries & aquaculture | Deep-sea fishing, marine fish, seaweed |
| Energy | **Offshore oil and gas** blocks, wind and tidal power |
| Shipping & ports | Chattogram, Mongla, Payra, **Matarbari** deep-sea port; shipbuilding |
| Minerals | **Heavy mineral sand** (Cox's Bazar), salt |
| Tourism | Cox's Bazar (**longest unbroken natural sea beach**, about 120 km), Kuakata, St Martin's |
| Marine biotechnology | Medicines, cosmetics from marine life |

### Marine protected areas
- **Swatch of No Ground (সোয়াচ অব নো গ্রাউন্ড):** a deep **submarine canyon** in the Bay of Bengal; home of dolphins, whales and sharks. Declared Bangladesh's **first Marine Protected Area** in **2014**.
- **Nijhum Dwip** marine area and **St Martin's** are also protected.
- **Kuakata (Patuakhali)** is called **"সাগরকন্যা"** — both sunrise and sunset can be seen from the beach.

## Climate change (জলবায়ু পরিবর্তন) 🔥🔥
- Bangladesh is one of the **most climate-vulnerable countries** (low, flat delta; cyclones; floods; salinity; sea-level rise).
- A **1 metre sea-level rise** could flood about **17%** of the land (commonly cited figure).
- **Germanwatch Global Climate Risk Index 2021:** Bangladesh **7th** most affected country (2000–2019).

### Policies and funds 🔥
| Policy / fund | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **NAPA** (National Adaptation Programme of Action) | **2005** | First adaptation plan |
| **BCCSAP** (Bangladesh Climate Change Strategy and Action Plan) | **2009** | 6 pillars |
| **Bangladesh Climate Change Trust Fund (BCCTF)** | **2009–10** | Among the first **national climate funds financed from a country's own budget** |
| **Climate Change Trust Act** | **2010** | |
| **National Adaptation Plan (NAP) 2023–2050** | **2022** | Submitted to UNFCCC |
| **Updated NDC** | **2021** | Emission-reduction targets under the Paris Agreement |
| **Mujib Climate Prosperity Plan** | **2022** | Climate prosperity roadmap to 2041 |

- Bangladesh chaired the **Climate Vulnerable Forum (CVF)** and V20 in **2020–2022**.
- **Global Center on Adaptation** opened its **South Asia office in Dhaka** (2020).

## Bangladesh Delta Plan 2100 (বাংলাদেশ ব-দ্বীপ পরিকল্পনা ২১০০) 🔥🔥
- Approved by the **NEC (National Economic Council)** on **4 September 2018**.
- Prepared by the **General Economics Division (GED), Planning Commission**, with help from the **Netherlands**.
- A **long-term (100-year) techno-economic plan** for water, climate, food security and environment.
- **Investment target:** about **2.5% of GDP** per year; **80 projects** in the first phase (to 2030).
- **3 higher-level (national) goals** and **6 specific goals**.
- **6 hotspots (হটস্পট):**
  1. **Coastal zone (উপকূলীয় অঞ্চল)** — the largest hotspot
  2. **Barind and drought-prone areas**
  3. **Haor and flash-flood areas**
  4. **Chattogram Hill Tracts**
  5. **River systems and estuaries**
  6. **Urban areas**
- Model: the Netherlands' own **Delta Programme**.

## Quick revision
- Sea area **1,18,813 sq km**; blue economy term by **Gunter Pauli**.
- First MPA: **Swatch of No Ground (2014)**.
- Kuakata = **Sagar Kanya**; Cox's Bazar = longest natural sea beach.
- BCCSAP **2009**; Climate Change Trust Fund from **own resources**.
- Paris Agreement **2015**; Loss & Damage Fund **COP27 (2022)**.
- Delta Plan 2100 approved **4 Sept 2018**; **6 hotspots**; partner **Netherlands**; **2.5% of GDP**.
