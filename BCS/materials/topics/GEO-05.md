# Physiography & soils of Bangladesh

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask the three physiographic divisions of Bangladesh with their shares (12% / 8% / 80%), the Pleistocene terraces (Barind, Madhupur, Lalmai), the highest peak, haors and beels, and soil types (red soil, alluvial, peat, saline).

## Basic facts
- Bangladesh is mostly part of the **world's largest delta**, the **Bengal (Ganges–Brahmaputra–Meghna) delta**.
- Area: **1,47,570 sq km** (the official figure used in most textbooks; some BBS sources give about **1,48,460 sq km**).
- Most of the land is **less than 10 m above sea level**; the land generally **slopes from north to south**.

## Three physiographic divisions (NCTB) 🔥🔥
| Division | Share of area | Age | Areas |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Tertiary hills (টারশিয়ারি যুগের পাহাড়সমূহ)** | **About 12%** | Formed in the **Tertiary period**, when the Himalayas rose | South-eastern and north/north-eastern hills |
| 🔥 **Pleistocene terraces (প্লাইস্টোসিনকালের চত্বরভূমি)** | **About 8%** | About **25,000 years** old (Pleistocene) | **Barind Tract, Madhupur & Bhawal Tract, Lalmai Hills** |
| 🔥 **Recent floodplains (সাম্প্রতিক কালের প্লাবন সমভূমি)** | **About 80%** | Most recent; built by river silt | Most of the country |

### 1. Tertiary hills
| Group | Districts / facts |
|---|---|
| **South-eastern hills (দক্ষিণ-পূর্বাঞ্চলের পাহাড়সমূহ)** | **Rangamati, Khagrachhari, Bandarban** (Chittagong Hill Tracts), **Chattogram, Cox's Bazar**. Made of sandstone, siltstone and shale; average height about 600 m |
| **North and north-eastern hills (উত্তর ও উত্তর-পূর্বাঞ্চলের পাহাড়সমূহ)** | Northern **Mymensingh and Netrokona** (Garo hills area), **Sylhet, Moulvibazar, Habiganj**. Local hills are called **"টিলা" (tila)** in Sylhet |

- 🔥 **Highest peak:** textbooks and official sources name **Tajingdong (তাজিংডং), also called Bijoy (বিজয়)**, in **Bandarban**. Older books named **Keokradong (কেওক্রাডং)**. Modern GPS surveys show **Saka Haphong (সাকা হাফং / Mowdok Mual), Bandarban**, about **1,052 m**, to be the highest. **In exams, give Tajingdong (Bijoy) unless Saka Haphong is among the options and Tajingdong is not.**
- **Chimbuk** hill and **Nilgiri** are in Bandarban; **Sitakunda's Chandranath hill** is in Chattogram.

### 2. Pleistocene terraces 🔥
| Terrace | Area | Location / soil |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Barind Tract (বরেন্দ্রভূমি)** | **About 9,320 sq km** — **largest** terrace | Greater **Rajshahi, Dinajpur, Rangpur, Bogura**, Joypurhat, Naogaon; **reddish/grey** clay soil; **dry, low rainfall** |
| 🔥 **Madhupur and Bhawal Tract (মধুপুর ও ভাওয়ালের গড়)** | **About 4,103 sq km** | **Tangail, Gazipur**, Mymensingh; **red and brown soil (লালচে মাটি)**; sal forest |
| **Lalmai Hills (লালমাই পাহাড়)** | **About 34 sq km** — smallest | **Cumilla**; reddish soil with sand and gravel; Mainamati Buddhist ruins nearby |

- The soils of these terraces are **red or reddish-brown** because of **iron oxide**, and are **acidic**.

### 3. Recent floodplains 🔥
Formed by the **Padma (Ganges), Jamuna (Brahmaputra), Meghna, Teesta** and their branches. NCTB divides them into:
- **Piedmont (foothill) plain (পাদদেশীয় সমভূমি):** **Rangpur–Dinajpur**, formed by rivers from the Himalayan foothills.
- **Floodplains (প্লাবন সমভূমি):** along the major rivers — flooded every year, very fertile.
- **Deltaic plain (ব-দ্বীপ সমভূমি):** **Kushtia, Jashore, Faridpur, Khulna** region.
- **Sylhet basin (সিলেট অববাহিকা):** low bowl-shaped land with **haors**.
- **Cumilla plain**, **Chattogram coastal plain**, and **tidal/coastal plains (স্রোতজ ও উপকূলীয় সমভূমি)** in the south (Khulna, Barishal, Patuakhali, Noakhali).

### Haors, beels and wetlands 🔥
| Feature | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Haor (হাওর)** | Large **bowl-shaped depression**, flooded in the monsoon; mostly in **Sunamganj, Sylhet, Moulvibazar, Habiganj, Netrokona, Kishoreganj, Brahmanbaria** |
| 🔥 **Hakaluki Haor** | **Largest haor** of Bangladesh (Moulvibazar–Sylhet) |
| 🔥 **Tanguar Haor** | Sunamganj; Bangladesh's **2nd Ramsar site (2000)** |
| **Beel (বিল)** | Smaller low-lying water body |
| 🔥 **Chalan Beel (চলন বিল)** | **Largest beel** — Pabna, Natore, Sirajganj |
| **Arial Beel** | Dhaka–Munshiganj |

## Soils of Bangladesh 🔥
| Soil type | Where | Features |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Floodplain (alluvial) soil — পলি মাটি** | **About 80% of the country** | **Most fertile**; renewed by silt every flood; best for **rice and jute** |
| 🔥 **Terrace soil (red/brown soil — লাল মাটি)** | **Barind, Madhupur, Lalmai** | Red/reddish-brown (iron oxide), **acidic**, less fertile; good for jackfruit, pineapple, sal trees |
| **Hill soil (পাহাড়ি মাটি)** | Chittagong Hill Tracts, Sylhet hills | Acidic, sandy/loamy, erodes easily; good for **tea** (Sylhet), fruit; **jhum** (shifting) cultivation |
| **Peat soil (পিট/জৈব মাটি)** | **Gopalganj, Khulna** low basins (e.g. Baghia beel), parts of the Sylhet basin | Very high in organic matter; waterlogged |
| **Saline soil (লবণাক্ত মাটি)** | **Coastal districts: Satkhira, Khulna, Bagerhat**, Patuakhali, Barguna | Salt from sea water; reduces crop yield; increasing with sea-level rise |
| **Acid sulphate soil** | Parts of the coast, Chattogram and Cox's Bazar mangrove areas | Very acidic when drained |

- **By texture:** **sandy (বেলে)**, **loamy (দোআঁশ)** — **best for farming**, and **clay (এঁটেল)**. Loam is a mix of sand, silt and clay.
- **SRDI (Soil Resource Development Institute)** carries out soil surveys in Bangladesh.

## Quick revision
- Divisions: **Tertiary hills 12%**, **Pleistocene terraces 8%**, **Recent floodplains 80%**.
- Pleistocene terraces: **Barind (largest, ~9,320 sq km)**, **Madhupur–Bhawal (~4,103 sq km)**, **Lalmai (~34 sq km, Cumilla)**.
- Terraces are about **25,000 years** old, with **red soil**.
- Highest peak: **Tajingdong (Bijoy)** in textbooks; Saka Haphong in modern surveys.
- Largest haor: **Hakaluki**; largest beel: **Chalan Beel**.
- Most fertile soil: **alluvial floodplain soil**; peat soil: **Gopalganj/Khulna**; saline soil: **coastal south-west**.
- Best soil texture for crops: **loam (দোআঁশ)**.
