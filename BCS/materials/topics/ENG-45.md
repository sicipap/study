# One-word substitution

> **Why it matters:** "One who does not believe in God is called…" type questions appear in nearly every BCS, bank and NTRCA paper. Learning the roots (*-cide*, *-cracy*, *-phile*, *-logy*) lets you answer even unfamiliar items.

## Useful roots
| Root | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **-cide** | killing | homicide, regicide |
| **-cracy** | rule, government | democracy, autocracy |
| **-phile / phil-** | lover of | bibliophile, philanthropist |
| **mis- / miso-** | hatred | misanthrope, misogynist |
| **-logy** | study of | biology, etymology |
| **omni-** | all | omnipotent, omniscient |
| **-vorous** | eating | carnivorous, herbivorous |
| **-gamy** | marriage | monogamy, bigamy |
| **in- / im- / il- / ir- + -ible/-able** | cannot be… | invincible, illegible |

## Quick revision
- Atheist / theist / **agnostic**.
- **Misanthrope** hates mankind; **philanthropist** loves mankind.
- **Philatelist** = stamps; **numismatist** = coins; **bibliophile** = books.
- **Regicide** = killing a king; **fratricide** = brother.
- **Plutocracy** = rule of the rich; **theocracy** = rule by religious leaders.
- **Invincible**, **inevitable**, **illegible**, **infallible**, **incorrigible**.
- **Posthumous** = after death; **epitaph** = words on a tomb.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** One who collects stamps is called a:
(a) numismatist (b) philatelist (c) bibliophile (d) philanthropist

**2.** One who does not believe in God is:
(a) an atheist (b) a theist (c) an agnostic (d) a heretic

**3.** Government by the rich is called:
(a) aristocracy (b) autocracy (c) oligarchy (d) plutocracy

**4.** That which cannot be conquered is:
(a) invisible (b) invincible (c) inevitable (d) indelible

**5.** The killing of a king is called:
(a) regicide (b) patricide (c) homicide (d) genocide

**6.** Handwriting that cannot be read is:
(a) eligible (b) inaudible (c) illegible (d) indelible

**7.** One who hates mankind is a:
(a) misogynist (b) philanthropist (c) misanthrope (d) misogamist

**8.** A child born after the death of the father is called a ____ child.
(a) anonymous (b) orphan (c) contemporary (d) posthumous

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Philatelist = stamp collector |
| 2 | a | Atheist rejects belief in God |
| 3 | d | pluto = wealth |
| 4 | b | Invincible = unconquerable |
| 5 | a | regi = king, cide = killing |
| 6 | c | Illegible = cannot be read |
| 7 | c | mis = hate, anthrop = man |
| 8 | d | Posthumous = after death |
