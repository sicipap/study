# World's largest/longest: rivers, mountains, deserts

> **Why it matters:** "Largest / longest / highest / deepest" questions are among the most common geography MCQs in every Bangladeshi exam. Learn the tables; where sources disagree, the usual exam answer is given with a note.

## Rivers 🔥
| Record | River | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Longest river in the world** | **Nile** (Africa), about **6,650 km** | Some recent studies claim the **Amazon** is longer; exams accept **Nile** |
| 🔥 **Largest river by volume/discharge and basin** | **Amazon** (South America) | Carries the most water of any river |
| Longest river in **Asia** | **Yangtze (Chang Jiang)**, China, about 6,300 km | 3rd longest in the world |
| Longest in **North America** | **Mississippi–Missouri** | |
| Longest in **Europe** | **Volga** (Russia) | Flows into the Caspian Sea |
| Longest in **Australia** | **Murray** (Murray–Darling system) | |
| River through the **most countries** | **Danube** (Europe, 10 countries) | |
| **Deepest river** | **Congo** (Africa) | **Crosses the equator twice** |
| 🔥 **Largest delta** | **Ganges–Brahmaputra delta (Bengal delta)** | Bangladesh and West Bengal |
| **Sorrow rivers** | Huang He (Yellow River) = **"Sorrow of China"**; Damodar = "Sorrow of Bengal" | |

## Mountains and peaks 🔥🔥
| Record | Name | Height (approx.) / notes |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Highest peak** | **Mount Everest** (Nepal–China border) | **8,848.86 m** (Nepal–China joint measurement, **2020**; older books give **8,848 m**). Nepali name **Sagarmatha**; Tibetan **Chomolungma**. First climbed on **29 May 1953** by **Edmund Hillary & Tenzing Norgay** |
| 🔥 **2nd highest** | **K2 (Godwin-Austen)**, Pakistan–China (Karakoram) | About **8,611 m** |
| **3rd highest** | **Kangchenjunga** (Nepal–India) | About 8,586 m |
| Highest in **South America** (and outside Asia) | **Aconcagua** (Argentina, Andes) | About 6,961 m |
| Highest in **North America** | **Denali** (Mount McKinley), Alaska | About 6,190 m |
| Highest in **Africa** | **Kilimanjaro** (Tanzania) | About 5,895 m; a dormant volcano |
| Highest in **Europe** | **Elbrus** (Russia, Caucasus) | About 5,642 m (Mont Blanc is highest in the Alps/Western Europe) |
| Highest in **Antarctica** | **Vinson Massif** | About 4,892 m |
| Highest in **Oceania** | **Puncak Jaya** (Indonesia) | About 4,884 m; **Kosciuszko** is highest on mainland Australia |
| **Tallest from base** | **Mauna Kea** (Hawaii) | Over 10,000 m from the ocean floor |
| 🔥 **Longest mountain range** | **Andes** (South America) | About 7,000 km |
| **Highest mountain range** | **Himalayas** | Young **fold mountains** |
| 🔥 **"Roof of the World"** | **Pamir Plateau** (Central Asia) | **Tibetan Plateau** is the highest and largest plateau |

- **First Bangladeshi to climb Everest:** **Musa Ibrahim (2010)**. **First Bangladeshi woman:** **Nishat Majumder (2012)**.
- **Highest peak of Bangladesh:** **Saka Haphong** (Bandarban) is the highest by most modern measurements (about **1,052 m**); officially and in older books, **Tajingdong (Bijoy)** or **Keokradong** are named. See GEO-05.

## Quick revision
- Longest river: **Nile**; largest by volume: **Amazon**; longest in Asia: **Yangtze**.
- Largest delta: **Bengal (Ganges–Brahmaputra) delta**.
- Everest: **8,848.86 m** (2020); second: **K2**; third: **Kangchenjunga**.
- Longest mountain range: **Andes**; "Roof of the World": **Pamir**.
- Largest hot desert: **Sahara**; driest: **Atacama**; largest overall: **Antarctica**.
- Largest lake: **Caspian Sea**; deepest: **Baikal**; largest freshwater by area: **Superior**.
- Largest island: **Greenland**; highest waterfall: **Angel Falls**.
- First Bangladeshi on Everest: **Musa Ibrahim (2010)**.

## Practice MCQ

(a) Amazon (b) Nile (c) Yangtze (d) Mississippi

**2.** The second-highest mountain peak in the world is:
(a) Kangchenjunga (b) Makalu (c) K2 (d) Lhotse

**3.** Which is the largest hot desert in the world?
(a) Sahara (b) Gobi (c) Arabian (d) Kalahari

**4.** The deepest lake in the world is:
(a) Caspian Sea (b) Lake Superior (c) Lake Victoria (d) Lake Baikal

**5.** Which is the longest mountain range in the world?
(a) Himalayas (b) Rockies (c) Andes (d) Alps

**6.** The driest desert in the world is:
(a) Thar (b) Atacama (c) Namib (d) Sahara

**7.** Which is the largest island in the world?
(a) Greenland (b) New Guinea (c) Borneo (d) Madagascar

**8.** Kilimanjaro, the highest peak of Africa, is in:
(a) Kenya (b) Uganda (c) Ethiopia (d) Tanzania

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | Nile, about 6,650 km (Amazon is largest by volume) |
| 2 | c | K2 (Godwin-Austen), about 8,611 m |
| 3 | a | Sahara, about 9 million sq km |
| 4 | d | Baikal, Siberia, over 1,600 m deep |
| 5 | c | Andes, about 7,000 km |
| 6 | b | Atacama in Chile |
| 7 | a | Greenland; Australia counts as a continent |
| 8 | d | Kilimanjaro is in Tanzania |
