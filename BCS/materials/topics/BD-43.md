# Bangladesh in UN, OIC, SAARC, BIMSTEC; peacekeeping

> **Why it matters:** Dates of joining international bodies, SAARC and BIMSTEC founding facts, and Bangladesh's UN peacekeeping record are asked in almost every BCS and bank exam.

## Bangladesh in the United Nations 🔥🔥
- Membership: **17 September 1974**, **136th** member.
- **Sheikh Mujibur Rahman** gave the first **speech in Bangla at the UN General Assembly** on **25 September 1974** (29th session).
- **UN Security Council (non-permanent member):** **1979–80** and **2000–01**.
- **President of the UN General Assembly:** **হুমায়ুন রশীদ চৌধুরী**, **41st session (1986)**.
- **International Mother Language Day:** UNESCO declared **21 February** on **17 November 1999**; observed worldwide from **2000**.
- **UNESCO Memory of the World Register:** the **7 March 1971 speech** (inscribed **30 October 2017**).

## OIC (ইসলামি সহযোগিতা সংস্থা) 🔥
- Founded **1969** (Rabat); secretariat **Jeddah, Saudi Arabia**; **57** members.
- Bangladesh joined in **1974** at the **Lahore summit**; Pakistan recognised Bangladesh (**22 February 1974**) at the same time.
- OIC institution in Bangladesh: **Islamic University of Technology (IUT)**, **Board Bazar, Gazipur**.

## SAARC (সার্ক) 🔥🔥
- Idea proposed by **President Ziaur Rahman** (**1980**).
- Founded on **8 December 1985** at the **first summit in Dhaka**. **8 December = SAARC Charter Day**.
- **8 members:** Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, and **Afghanistan** (the **8th**, joined **2007**).
- Secretariat: **Kathmandu, Nepal** (1987). **First Secretary-General: আবুল আহসান (Bangladesh)**.
- **SAFTA** (South Asian Free Trade Area): signed **2004** (Islamabad), in force from **2006**.
- The last summit was the **18th (Kathmandu, 2014)**; the 19th (Islamabad, 2016) was postponed and SAARC has been largely inactive since.

## BIMSTEC (বিমসটেক) 🔥🔥
- Full name: **Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation**.
- Founded on **6 June 1997**, **Bangkok** (as BIST-EC).
- **7 members:** **Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand** (5 South Asian + 2 South-East Asian).
- **Permanent Secretariat: Dhaka** (Gulshan), opened **2014**.
- **BIMSTEC Charter** adopted at the **5th summit, Colombo (30 March 2022)**.
- **6th summit: Bangkok, 4 April 2025** — **Bangladesh took over the chairmanship** (Chief Adviser Muhammad Yunus).

## Other groupings
- **D-8** (Developing-8 Muslim countries): founded **1997**, Istanbul; members include Bangladesh, Egypt, Indonesia, Iran, Malaysia, Nigeria, Pakistan, Turkey.
- **Climate Vulnerable Forum (CVF):** Bangladesh chaired it **2020–2022**.
- **ASEAN Regional Forum (ARF):** Bangladesh joined in **2006**.

## UN Peacekeeping (শান্তিরক্ষা) 🔥🔥
- **First participation:** **1988**, **UNIIMOG** (**Iran–Iraq**) with **15 army observers**.
- **Police** first joined in **1989** (**UNTAG, Namibia**).
- Bangladesh is consistently among the **top troop- and police-contributing countries** (often **first or second**).
- **First all-female Formed Police Unit** from Bangladesh: deployed to **Haiti (2010)**.
- **Sierra Leone** declared **Bangla** an honorary official language (**2002**) in gratitude to Bangladeshi peacekeepers.
- Training centre: **BIPSOT** (Bangladesh Institute of Peace Support Operation Training), **Rajendrapur, Gazipur**.
- **International Day of UN Peacekeepers:** **29 May**.

## Quick revision
- UN: **17 Sept 1974**, **136th** member; Mujib's Bangla speech **25 Sept 1974**.
- UNSC terms: **1979–80, 2000–01**; UNGA President **Humayun Rasheed Choudhury (1986)**.
- OIC: joined **1974**, Lahore; IUT in **Gazipur**.
- SAARC: **8 Dec 1985, Dhaka**; idea of **Ziaur Rahman**; HQ **Kathmandu**; 8th member **Afghanistan**.
- BIMSTEC: **6 June 1997, Bangkok**; **7** members; HQ **Dhaka**; Bangladesh chair from **April 2025**.
- Peacekeeping from **1988 (UNIIMOG)**.
