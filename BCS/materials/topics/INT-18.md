# Climate: UNFCCC, Kyoto, Paris Agreement, COPs

> **Why it matters:** Exams ask for the Earth Summit (1992), the UNFCCC secretariat (Bonn), the Kyoto Protocol's dates and mechanisms, the Paris Agreement's temperature goals, where each important COP was held and what it decided, the IPCC, and Bangladesh's climate role.

## The framework (জলবায়ু কাঠামো) 🔥
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Stockholm Conference** | **1972**, first UN conference on the human environment → **UNEP** (Nairobi); **5 June = World Environment Day** |
| 🔥 **IPCC** (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change) | Set up in **1988** by **WMO and UNEP**; HQ **Geneva**; shared the **Nobel Peace Prize 2007** with **Al Gore** |
| 🔥 **Earth Summit** (UN Conference on Environment and Development) | **Rio de Janeiro, June 1992**; produced the **UNFCCC**, the **Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD)**, **Agenda 21** and the Rio Declaration |
| 🔥 **UNFCCC** | Adopted **May 1992** (New York), opened for signature at Rio, **in force 21 March 1994**; secretariat 🔥 **Bonn, Germany**; **198 parties** (as of 2025) |
| **COP** | **Conference of the Parties**: the UNFCCC's supreme body; meets every year. 🔥 **COP1: Berlin, 1995** |

- Principle: "**Common but differentiated responsibilities (CBDR)**" — rich industrial countries caused most emissions, so they should do more.
- Main **greenhouse gases**: carbon dioxide (CO₂), methane (CH₄), nitrous oxide (N₂O), HFCs, PFCs, SF₆ (these six are covered by Kyoto).

## Kyoto Protocol (কিয়োটো প্রটোকল) 🔥
- 🔥 Adopted at **COP3, Kyoto (Japan), December 1997**; **in force 16 February 2005** (after **Russia** ratified).
- Set **legally binding emission cuts only for developed (Annex I) countries**: about **5%** below **1990** levels on average in the **first commitment period (2008–2012)**.
- **Doha Amendment (2012)**: second period **2013–2020**.
- 🔥 **Three market mechanisms:** **Clean Development Mechanism (CDM)**, **Joint Implementation (JI)**, **Emissions Trading**.
- The **USA never ratified** it; **Canada withdrew** (2011/12).

## Paris Agreement (প্যারিস চুক্তি) 🔥
- 🔥 Adopted at **COP21, Paris, 12 December 2015**; signed on **22 April 2016** (Earth Day) in New York; 🔥 **in force 4 November 2016**.
- 🔥 **Goal:** keep global warming **well below 2°C** above pre-industrial levels and **pursue efforts to limit it to 1.5°C**.
- Every country sets its own targets: **Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs)**, updated every **5 years**.
- **Global Stocktake** every 5 years; the **first was completed at COP28 (2023)**.
- **Article 6**: carbon markets; **Article 9**: finance; **Article 8**: loss and damage.
- **USA:** withdrew under Trump (effective **November 2020**), rejoined under Biden (**February 2021**), and withdrew again (announced **January 2025**, effective **January 2026**).

## Important COPs 🔥
| COP | Year | Place | Key outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| COP1 | 1995 | **Berlin** | First COP; Berlin Mandate |
| 🔥 COP3 | 1997 | **Kyoto**, Japan | **Kyoto Protocol** |
| COP13 | 2007 | **Bali**, Indonesia | Bali Action Plan |
| COP15 | 2009 | **Copenhagen** | Copenhagen Accord; rich nations pledged **$100 billion a year by 2020** |
| COP16 | 2010 | **Cancún**, Mexico | 🔥 **Green Climate Fund** (HQ **Songdo, South Korea**) |
| COP18 | 2012 | **Doha** | Doha Amendment to Kyoto |
| COP19 | 2013 | **Warsaw** | Warsaw Mechanism on **loss and damage** |
| 🔥 COP21 | 2015 | **Paris** | **Paris Agreement** |
| COP24 | 2018 | **Katowice**, Poland | Paris "rulebook" |
| COP26 | 2021 | **Glasgow**, UK | Glasgow Climate Pact; coal "**phase down**" |
| 🔥 COP27 | 2022 | **Sharm el-Sheikh**, Egypt | Agreed to create a **Loss and Damage Fund** |
| 🔥 COP28 | 2023 | **Dubai**, UAE | Loss & damage fund made operational; first **Global Stocktake**; call to "**transition away from fossil fuels**" |
| 🔥 COP29 | 2024 | **Baku**, Azerbaijan | "Finance COP": new climate finance goal (**NCQG**) of at least **$300 billion a year by 2035**; carbon-market rules (Article 6) |
| 🔥 COP30 | 2025 | **Belém**, Brazil (in the Amazon) | 10 years after Paris; focus on forests, adaptation and implementation |
| COP31 | 2026 | **Antalya, Türkiye** (Australia to lead the negotiations) | Scheduled for **November 2026** |

## Other environmental agreements
| Agreement | Year | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| **Ramsar Convention** | 1971 | **Wetlands** (Bangladesh's Ramsar sites: **Sundarbans** and **Tanguar Haor**) |
| **Vienna Convention** | 1985 | Ozone layer |
| 🔥 **Montreal Protocol** | **1987** | Phasing out **ozone-depleting substances (CFCs)**; 16 September = Ozone Day |
| **Kigali Amendment** | 2016 | Phase-down of **HFCs** (to the Montreal Protocol) |
| **Basel Convention** | 1989 | Hazardous waste movement |
| **UNCCD** | 1994 | **Desertification** |
| **Cartagena Protocol** / **Nagoya Protocol** | 2000 / 2010 | Biosafety / access and benefit-sharing (under the CBD) |
| **Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework** | 2022 | "**30 by 30**": protect 30% of land and sea by 2030 |

## Bangladesh and climate change 🔥
- Among the **most climate-vulnerable** countries (sea-level rise, cyclones, salinity, floods).
- **Bangladesh Climate Change Strategy and Action Plan (BCCSAP): 2009**; **Bangladesh Climate Change Trust Fund**: set up in **2010** with **its own money** — among the first such funds by a developing country.
- **National Adaptation Plan (NAP) 2023–2050** submitted to the UNFCCC (2022).
- Bangladesh has chaired the **Climate Vulnerable Forum (CVF)** (**2011–2013** and **2020–2022**).
- The **Global Center on Adaptation** has a South Asia office in **Dhaka**.
- Bangladesh has two **Ramsar sites**: **Sundarbans (1992)** and **Tanguar Haor (2000)**.

## Quick revision
- Earth Summit: **Rio, 1992** → UNFCCC, CBD, Agenda 21.
- UNFCCC in force **21 March 1994**; secretariat **Bonn**; COP1 **Berlin 1995**.
- Kyoto: **1997**, in force **16 Feb 2005**; mechanisms **CDM, JI, Emissions Trading**.
- Paris: adopted **12 Dec 2015**, in force **4 Nov 2016**; **well below 2°C, aim 1.5°C**; **NDCs**.
- Green Climate Fund: COP16 **Cancún**; HQ **Songdo**.
- Loss and damage fund: agreed **COP27 (Sharm el-Sheikh)**, made operational **COP28 (Dubai)**.
- COP29 **Baku** ($300 bn NCQG); COP30 **Belém, Brazil**.
- IPCC: **1988**, WMO + UNEP; Nobel **2007**. Montreal Protocol: **1987**.
