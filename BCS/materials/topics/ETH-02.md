# Values: types, social values, values education

> **Why it matters:** Questions on values (মূল্যবোধ) ask for the definition, the types (moral, social, democratic, aesthetic …), examples of social values, who classified terminal and instrumental values, and the role of the family and education in building values.

## What are values? 🔥
- **Values (মূল্যবোধ)** are the **beliefs and standards about what is good, right, desirable and important**. They guide a person's choices and behaviour.
- The English word "value" comes from the Latin **"valere"**, meaning "to be worth" or "to be strong".
- Values are the **standards by which we judge** actions; **norms** are the specific rules of behaviour that come from values.

## Characteristics of values
- **Learned** through socialisation (family, school, society); not inborn.
- **Relative**: they differ between societies and change over time.
- **Abstract**: they are ideas, seen only through behaviour.
- Arranged in a **hierarchy**: some values matter more to a person than others.
- **Guide behaviour** and help judge right and wrong.
- Enforced mainly by **conscience and social approval**, not by courts.

## Types of values
| Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Moral values (নৈতিক মূল্যবোধ)** | Sense of right and wrong | Honesty, truthfulness, justice, kindness |
| **Social values (সামাজিক মূল্যবোধ)** | Shared standards that keep society together | Cooperation, tolerance, respect for elders, discipline, fellow-feeling |
| **Democratic / political values (গণতান্ত্রিক মূল্যবোধ)** | Support democratic life | **Tolerance, respect for others' opinions, freedom, equality, majority rule with minority rights**, rule of law |
| **Religious / spiritual values** | Based on faith | Piety, charity, compassion |
| **Cultural values** | From tradition and heritage | Language, festivals, hospitality |
| **Aesthetic values (নান্দনিক মূল্যবোধ)** | Sense of **beauty and art** | Love of music, literature, nature |
| **Economic values** | About wealth and work | Hard work, thrift, dignity of labour |
| **Personal values** | Chosen by an individual | Ambition, self-respect |

## Terminal and instrumental values 🔥
- The psychologist **Milton Rokeach** (***The Nature of Human Values*, 1973**) divided values into:
  - **Terminal values**: desired **end-states** (e.g. freedom, equality, happiness, a world at peace).
  - **Instrumental values**: preferred **ways of behaving** to reach them (e.g. honesty, responsibility, courage).
- His **Rokeach Value Survey** lists **18 terminal and 18 instrumental values**.

## Social values in Bangladesh
Commonly listed social values: **honesty, cooperation, tolerance, discipline, patriotism, respect for elders, hospitality, communal harmony, fellow-feeling, justice and equality**. Their decline (মূল্যবোধের অবক্ষয়) is linked to **corruption, excessive materialism, lack of good role models, family breakdown, drug abuse and misuse of media**.

## Values education (মূল্যবোধ শিক্ষা) 🔥
- **Aim:** to build **character**, moral judgement and responsible citizenship, not just knowledge or skills.
- **Agents of values education:**
  1. **Family**: the first and most important agent (the child's first school).
  2. **Educational institutions**: teachers as role models, curriculum, co-curricular activities.
  3. **Religion** and religious institutions.
  4. **Society and peer group.**
  5. **Mass media.**
  6. **State**: laws, policies and leaders' examples.
- **Methods:** role modelling, storytelling, discussion of moral dilemmas, community service, sports and cultural activities.

## Values, ethics and good governance
Values are the base of ethics; ethics guides conduct; ethical conduct in public institutions produces **good governance**. The National Integrity Strategy (2012) of Bangladesh names the **family** and **educational institutions** as non-state institutions for building integrity.

## Quick revision
- Values = standards of what is good and desirable; "value" ← Latin "valere".
- Values are learned, relative, abstract and hierarchical.
- Tolerance and respect for others' opinions are democratic values.
- Aesthetic values relate to beauty and art.
- Rokeach (1973): 18 terminal + 18 instrumental values.
- The family is the first agent of values education.
- Values education aims at character building.
