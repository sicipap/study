# Sultanate: Bakhtiyar Khalji, Iliyas Shahi, Hussain Shahi

> **Why it matters:** Standard questions: year of Muslim conquest (1204), first independent sultan (Fakhruddin Mubarak Shah), "Shah-i-Bangalah" (Iliyas Shah), golden age (Hussain Shah), Adina and Sixty Dome mosques, and Ibn Battuta.

## Bakhtiyar Khalji and the Muslim conquest 🔥
- **ইখতিয়ার উদ্দিন মুহম্মদ বিন বখতিয়ার খলজি**, a Turkish general, attacked **Nadia** in **1204** (some sources: 1203 or 1205) with about **17–18 horsemen**. **Lakshman Sena** fled.
- He made **লখনৌতি (Lakhnauti / Gaur)** his capital. This began **Muslim rule in Bengal**.
- He built mosques, madrasas and khanqahs.
- His **Tibet campaign failed**; on return he died at **দেবকোট** in **1206** (he was killed by **Ali Mardan Khalji**).
- The main source: **মিনহাজ-উস-সিরাজ**, **"তবকাত-ই-নাসিরী"**.

## After Bakhtiyar (1206–1338)
- **গিয়াসউদ্দিন ইওয়াজ খলজি** strengthened Lakhnauti and built a navy.
- Bengal often rebelled against Delhi, so historian **জিয়াউদ্দিন বারানী** called it **"বুলগাকপুর" (land of rebels)**.
- Delhi governors ruled from three centres: **Lakhnauti, Satgaon, Sonargaon**.

## Independent Sultanate (স্বাধীন সুলতানি যুগ, 1338–1538) 🔥
- **ফখরুদ্দিন মুবারক শাহ** declared independence at **সোনারগাঁও** in **1338**: the **first independent sultan** of (east) Bengal.
- 🔥 The Moroccan traveller **ইবনে বতুতা** visited Bengal in **1346** in his reign. He called Bengal **"দোজখপুর নিয়ামত"** (a hell full of good things). His travel book is **"কিতাবুল রেহলা"**.
- The independent sultanate lasted about **200 years (1338–1538)**.

## Iliyas Shahi dynasty (ইলিয়াস শাহী বংশ) 🔥
| Ruler | Key facts |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **শামসুদ্দিন ইলিয়াস শাহ** | United Lakhnauti, Satgaon and Sonargaon (by **1352**). Title **"শাহ-ই-বাঙ্গালাহ"**; the whole region came to be called **বাঙ্গালাহ**. Capital **পাণ্ডুয়া**. Resisted Delhi Sultan **ফিরোজ শাহ তুঘলক** (siege of **একডালা দুর্গ**) |
| **সিকান্দার শাহ** | Built the **আদিনা মসজিদ** at Pandua (1370s), the largest mosque of its time in the subcontinent |
| 🔥 **গিয়াসউদ্দিন আযম শাহ** | Famous for justice (story of the **Qazi's judgment**). Exchanged letters with Persian poet **হাফিজ**. Poet **শাহ মুহম্মদ সগীর** wrote **"ইউসুফ-জোলেখা"** in his time |

- **রাজা গণেশ** briefly seized power; his son **যদু** became Muslim as **জালালউদ্দিন মুহম্মদ শাহ**.
- The Iliyas Shahi line was **restored** under **নাসিরউদ্দিন মাহমুদ শাহ**. In his time **খান জাহান আলী** built the 🔥 **ষাট গম্বুজ মসজিদ (Sixty Dome Mosque), Bagerhat** — a **UNESCO World Heritage Site (1985)**.
- **রুকনুদ্দিন বারবক শাহ** brought in **Habshi (Abyssinian)** guards. Poet **মালাধর বসু** ("শ্রীকৃষ্ণবিজয়") got the title **"গুণরাজ খান"** from him.

## Habshi rule (1487–1493)
- Abyssinian (Habshi) slave-officers seized the throne and ruled for about **6 years** — a period of disorder.

## Hussain Shahi dynasty (হোসেন শাহী বংশ, 1493–1538) 🔥
| Ruler | Key facts |
|---|---|
| 🔥 **আলাউদ্দিন হোসেন শাহ** (1493–1519) | Best sultan; his rule is called the **golden age** of the sultanate. Hindu poets praised him as **"নৃপতি তিলক"**, **"জগৎভূষণ"**, **"কৃষ্ণাবতার"**. **শ্রীচৈতন্য** lived in his time. **ছোট সোনা মসজিদ** (Shibganj, Chapainawabganj) was built in his reign |
| **নাসিরউদ্দিন নুসরত শাহ** | Built **বড় সোনা মসজিদ** and **কদম রসুল** at Gaur |
| **গিয়াসউদ্দিন মাহমুদ শাহ** | Last Hussain Shahi sultan; defeated by **শেরশাহ** in **1538** |

- Bangla literature flourished: Hussain Shah's generals **পরাগল খান** and **ছুটি খান** (Chattogram) sponsored Bangla **Mahabharata** translations (**কবীন্দ্র পরমেশ্বর**, **শ্রীকর নন্দী**). **বিজয়গুপ্ত** wrote **"মনসামঙ্গল"**.

## Sher Shah and the Karranis
- **শেরশাহ** defeated Humayun at **Chausa (1539)** and **Kanauj (1540)**.
- He built the **Grand Trunk Road ("সড়ক-ই-আজম")** from **Sonargaon to Peshawar**, introduced the **"রুপি"** coin, and started horse-post (ডাক) service.
- The **কররানী** dynasty followed. **দাউদ খান কররানী**, the **last independent sultan of Bengal**, was defeated and killed by the Mughals at the **Battle of Rajmahal (1576)**.

## Quick revision
- Muslim conquest of Bengal: **1204**, **Bakhtiyar Khalji**; capital **Lakhnauti**.
- Source: **Tabaqat-i-Nasiri** by **Minhaj-us-Siraj**.
- "Bulgakpur" — **Ziauddin Barani**.
- First independent sultan: **Fakhruddin Mubarak Shah** (1338, Sonargaon).
- **Ibn Battuta** came in **1346**.
- "Shah-i-Bangalah": **Shamsuddin Iliyas Shah**; capital **Pandua**.
- Adina Mosque: **Sikandar Shah**. Sixty Dome Mosque: **Khan Jahan Ali**.
- Golden age: **Alauddin Hussain Shah**.
- Grand Trunk Road: **Sher Shah**. Last independent sultan: **Daud Khan Karrani** (1576).
