# Puzzles & problem solving

> **Why it matters:** Short puzzles (ধাঁধা) about heads and legs, handshakes, tournaments, trees on a road, frogs in wells, ages and socks turn up often in bank and BCS mental ability sections. Each type has a quick formula or trick.

## Formula table 🔥
| Puzzle type | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Handshakes among n people (each pair once) | **n(n − 1) ÷ 2** | 10 people → 45 |
| Knockout tournament with n teams | **n − 1 matches** | 32 teams → 31 |
| Round-robin (league) with n teams, each pair once | **n(n − 1) ÷ 2** | 8 teams → 28 |
| Cuts and pieces of a rope (straight line) | **pieces = cuts + 1** | 6 pieces need 5 cuts |
| Cuts in a ring (closed loop) | **pieces = cuts** | |
| Trees along a straight road, both ends included | **length ÷ gap + 1** | 100 m, every 10 m → 11 |
| Trees round a closed path (circle, park boundary) | **length ÷ gap** | |
| Clock strikes n times | **intervals = n − 1** | |
| Heads and legs (hens and cows) | **cows = (legs − 2 × heads) ÷ 2** | |
| Pigeonhole (socks in the dark) | **colours + 1** draws for a matching pair | 2 colours → 3 draws |
| Coin weighing (one heavier coin) | 3 weighings for up to 27; **2 for up to 9** | split into 3 groups |

## Classic tricks
- **Frog in a well:** if it climbs up a metres by day and slips down b metres at night, it gains (a − b) per full day. On the last day it climbs out **without slipping**. Days = (height − a) ÷ (a − b) + 1 (round up the division).
- **Age problems:** let the present age be x, write "after/before n years" as x + n or x − n, and solve one equation.
- **Pigeonhole:** the worst case is getting one of each colour first.
- **Balance puzzles:** divide the coins into **three** groups, not two.

## Worked examples
**Example 1 (heads and legs).** A farm has hens and cows: 20 heads and 56 legs. How many cows?
If all were hens: 40 legs. Extra 16 legs ÷ 2 = **8 cows** (12 hens).

**Example 2 (frog).** A frog climbs 3 m each day and slips 2 m each night in a 10 m well. When does it get out?
(10 − 3) ÷ (3 − 2) + 1 = 7 + 1 = **8th day**. After 7 days and nights it is at 7 m; on day 8 it climbs 3 m to the top.

**Example 3 (ages).** A father is three times as old as his son. In 10 years he will be twice as old. Son's age now?
3x + 10 = 2(x + 10) → x = **10 years** (father 30).

**Example 4 (clock strikes).** A clock takes 6 seconds to strike 6. How long does it take to strike 12?
6 strikes = 5 intervals → 1.2 s each. 12 strikes = 11 intervals → **13.2 s**.

**Example 5 (socks).** A drawer has 10 black and 10 white socks. Minimum draws in the dark for a matching pair?
Worst case: one black, one white; the 3rd always matches → **3**.

## Quick revision
- Handshakes and league matches: n(n − 1)/2.
- Knockout matches: n − 1.
- Rope pieces = cuts + 1; closed loop pieces = cuts.
- Trees on a straight road: length ÷ gap + 1; round a closed path: length ÷ gap.
- Clock strikes: intervals = strikes − 1.
- Frog: the last climb has no slip.
- Pigeonhole: colours + 1.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** A farm has hens and cows with 20 heads and 56 legs in all. How many cows are there?
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 10 (d) 6

**2.** At a meeting, 10 people each shake hands once with every other person. How many handshakes take place?
(a) 90 (b) 45 (c) 100 (d) 50

**3.** In a knockout tournament, 32 teams take part. How many matches are played to find the winner?
(a) 32 (b) 16 (c) 31 (d) 64

**4.** A frog in a 10 m deep well climbs 3 m each day and slips back 2 m each night. On which day does it get out?
(a) 10th (b) 7th (c) 9th (d) 8th

**5.** Trees are planted every 10 m along one side of a straight 100 m road, including both ends. How many trees are planted?
(a) 11 (b) 10 (c) 9 (d) 12

**6.** A drawer has 10 black and 10 white socks. What is the minimum number of socks you must take out in the dark to be sure of a matching pair?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 11 (d) 20

**7.** A rope is cut into 6 pieces. How many cuts were made?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 5 (d) 4

**8.** A father is three times as old as his son. After 10 years, he will be twice as old as his son. What is the son's present age?
(a) 12 years (b) 15 years (c) 8 years (d) 10 years

**9.** Among 9 coins, one is heavier than the rest. What is the minimum number of weighings on a balance needed to find it for certain?
(a) 3 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 1

**10.** A clock takes 6 seconds to strike 6 o'clock. How long will it take to strike 12 o'clock?
(a) 12 s (b) 14 s (c) 13.2 s (d) 11 s
