# Blood relations

> **Why it matters:** Blood relation (রক্তের সম্পর্ক) questions are very common: "Pointing to a photo, a man said …" or coded relations (A + B means A is the father of B). You win them by breaking the sentence into small steps and drawing a family tree.

## Basic relation words 🔥
| Relation | English term | Bangla |
|---|---|---|
| Father's father / Mother's father | Grandfather | দাদা / নানা |
| Father's mother / Mother's mother | Grandmother | দাদি / নানি |
| Father's brother | Uncle (paternal) | চাচা |
| Father's sister | Aunt (paternal) | ফুফু |
| Mother's brother | Uncle (maternal) | মামা |
| Mother's sister | Aunt (maternal) | খালা |
| Uncle's or aunt's child | Cousin | চাচাতো/মামাতো/ফুফাতো/খালাতো ভাই-বোন |
| Brother's or sister's son / daughter | Nephew / Niece | ভাতিজা-ভাগ্নে / ভাতিজি-ভাগ্নি |
| Son's wife | Daughter-in-law | পুত্রবধূ |
| Daughter's husband | Son-in-law | জামাই |
| Wife's or husband's brother; sister's husband | Brother-in-law | শ্যালক/দেবর/ভাসুর; দুলাভাই |
| Only son of my father | Me (if I am male) | |
| Only daughter of my mother | Me (if I am female) or my only sister | |

🔥 Key shortcut phrases:
- "My father's only son" = **me** (the speaker is male).
- "The only daughter of my mother", said by a **woman** = **herself**.
- "The only daughter of my mother", said by a **man** = **his sister**.

## Method
1. **Start from the end** of the sentence (the part closest to "my") and move one step at a time.
2. Use a **family tree**: generations go up and down; siblings go side by side. Mark males with + and females with −.
3. Note the **gender** only when it is stated. If a gender is not given, you may not be able to name the relation exactly (e.g. "child" not "son").

## Coded relations
Example code: **A + B** means A is the father of B; **A − B** means A is the wife of B; **A × B** means A is the brother of B.
To read **P − Q + R**: P is the wife of Q, and Q is the father of R → **P is the mother of R**.
Read the code from left to right, converting each symbol into a sentence.

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** Pointing to a boy, a man said, "He is the son of my father's only son."
My father's only son = me. The boy is my son → **son**.

**Example 2.** Pointing to a man, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother."
The only daughter of my mother = the woman herself. So she is the man's **mother**.

**Example 3.** Pointing to a photo, Karim said, "Her mother is the only daughter of my mother."
Karim is male, so the only daughter of his mother is his sister. The girl is his sister's daughter → **niece** (ভাগ্নি).

**Example 4.** A is B's brother. C is A's father. D is C's brother. How is D related to B?
C is also B's father. D is the father's brother → **uncle (paternal, চাচা)**.

**Example 5.** P is the brother of Q, Q is the sister of R, and R is the father of S. How is P related to S?
P, Q and R are siblings. P is a male sibling of S's father → **uncle (চাচা)**.

## Quick revision
- Father's brother = চাচা; mother's brother = মামা; father's sister = ফুফু; mother's sister = খালা.
- "My father's only son" = the speaker himself.
- The child of an uncle or aunt is a cousin.
- Break the sentence into small steps; start from "my".
- Draw a tree and mark each person's gender.
- Coded relations: turn each symbol into a sentence, left to right.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** A man said, "This boy is the son of my father's only son." How is the boy related to the man?
(a) Brother (b) Nephew (c) Son (d) Cousin

**2.** Pointing to a woman, Rahim said, "She is the daughter of the only son of my paternal grandfather." How is the woman related to Rahim?
(a) Sister (b) Cousin (c) Niece (d) Aunt

**3.** A is B's brother. C is A's father. D is C's brother. How is D related to B?
(a) Father (b) Uncle (c) Brother (d) Grandfather

**4.** Pointing to a man, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to the man?
(a) Sister (b) Aunt (c) Grandmother (d) Mother

**5.** P is the brother of Q, Q is the sister of R, and R is the father of S. How is P related to S?
(a) Uncle (b) Father (c) Brother (d) Grandfather

**6.** Your mother's brother's son is your:
(a) Brother (b) Nephew (c) Cousin (d) Uncle

**7.** If A + B means A is the father of B, A − B means A is the wife of B, and A × B means A is the brother of B, what does P − Q + R mean?
(a) P is the sister of R (b) P is the mother of R (c) P is the aunt of R (d) P is the grandmother of R

**8.** A's mother is B's sister. B is the daughter of C, who is a man. How is C related to A?
(a) Uncle (b) Father (c) Paternal grandfather (d) Maternal grandfather

**9.** Your father's sister's husband is your:
(a) Cousin (b) Uncle (c) Brother-in-law (d) Nephew

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | c | My father's only son is me, so the boy is my son. |
| 2 | a | The only son of my paternal grandfather is my father; his daughter is my sister. |
| 3 | b | C is the father of both A and B; D is the father's brother. |
| 4 | d | The only daughter of my mother is the woman herself, so she is his mother. |
| 5 | a | P, Q, R are siblings; P is the brother of S's father. |
| 6 | c | Mother's brother (মামা) → his son is your cousin (মামাতো ভাই). |
| 7 | b | P is the wife of Q and Q is the father of R, so P is R's mother. |
| 8 | d | A's mother and B are both C's daughters; C is A's mother's father (নানা). |
| 9 | b | Father's sister's husband is an uncle (ফুফা). |
