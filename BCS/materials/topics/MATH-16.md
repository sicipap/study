# Boats & streams, trains

> **Why it matters:** Boats and streams (নৌকা ও স্রোত) and train problems (ট্রেন সংক্রান্ত) are applications of speed and relative speed. Bank and 9th–20th grade exams ask them regularly; the formulas below solve almost all of them.

## Boats and streams: key concepts
- **Still-water speed (স্থির পানিতে নৌকার বেগ) = u; stream speed (স্রোতের বেগ) = v.**
- **Downstream (স্রোতের অনুকূলে):** speed = **u + v**.
- **Upstream (স্রোতের প্রতিকূলে):** speed = **u − v**.
- If downstream speed = D and upstream speed = U:
  - 🔥 **u = (D + U)/2**, **v = (D − U)/2**.

## Trains: key concepts
- When a train passes a **pole, tree, man standing, signal post**, it covers **its own length**.
- When it passes a **platform, bridge, tunnel or another train**, it covers **its length + the other length**.
- Opposite directions → add speeds; same direction → subtract speeds.
- Convert km/h to m/s (× 5/18) because lengths are usually in metres.

## Formula and shortcut table 🔥
| Situation | Time / formula |
|---|---|
| Downstream / upstream | u + v / u − v |
| Boat speed / stream speed | (D + U)/2 / (D − U)/2 |
| Train (length L) passes a pole | L ÷ speed |
| 🔥 Train passes platform/bridge of length P | (L + P) ÷ speed |
| Two trains, opposite directions | (L₁ + L₂) ÷ (s₁ + s₂) |
| Two trains, same direction | (L₁ + L₂) ÷ (s₁ − s₂) |
| Train passes a man walking (same direction) | L ÷ (train speed − man speed) |
| Pole in t₁ s and platform P in t₂ s | Speed = P/(t₂ − t₁); L = speed × t₁ |

## Worked examples
**Example 1.** A boat's downstream speed is 18 km/h and upstream speed is 12 km/h. Find the boat's speed in still water and the stream's speed.
Steps: u = (18 + 12)/2 = 15; v = (18 − 12)/2 = 3.
**Answer: 15 km/h and 3 km/h**

**Example 2.** A boat goes 30 km downstream in 2 hours and 24 km upstream in 3 hours. Find the speed of the stream.
Steps: D = 15, U = 8. v = (15 − 8)/2 = 3.5.
**Answer: 3.5 km/h**

**Example 3.** A 150 m long train runs at 54 km/h. How long does it take to pass a pole?
Steps: 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s. 150/15 = 10.
**Answer: 10 seconds**

**Example 4.** A 200 m train running at 72 km/h crosses a 300 m platform. Find the time.
Steps: 72 km/h = 20 m/s. (200 + 300)/20 = 25.
**Answer: 25 seconds**

**Example 5.** Trains 120 m and 180 m long run in opposite directions at 50 km/h and 40 km/h. How long do they take to cross each other?
Steps: Relative speed = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. (120 + 180)/25 = 12.
**Answer: 12 seconds**

## Common traps
- Forgetting to add the platform's length.
- Forgetting to convert km/h to m/s when lengths are in metres.
- Using the sum of speeds for trains moving the **same** way.
- Stream speed is **half the difference** of downstream and upstream speeds, not the whole difference.

## Quick revision
- Downstream u + v; upstream u − v.
- u = (D + U)/2; v = (D − U)/2.
- Pole/man → own length; platform/bridge → both lengths.
- Opposite → add speeds; same → subtract.
- 36 km/h = 10 m/s; 54 = 15; 72 = 20; 90 = 25.

## Practice MCQ
**1.** A boat's speed in still water is 12 km/h and the stream flows at 3 km/h. Its upstream speed is:
(a) 15 km/h (b) 9 km/h (c) 7.5 km/h (d) 12 km/h

**2.** A boat's downstream speed is 20 km/h and upstream speed is 14 km/h. The speed of the stream is:
(a) 6 km/h (b) 17 km/h (c) 3 km/h (d) 4 km/h

**3.** A 180 m train runs at 72 km/h. It passes a pole in:
(a) 9 seconds (b) 10 seconds (c) 2.5 seconds (d) 12 seconds

**4.** A 240 m train at 54 km/h crosses a 360 m bridge in:
(a) 16 seconds (b) 24 seconds (c) 30 seconds (d) 40 seconds

**5.** A train passes a pole in 12 seconds and a 200 m platform in 20 seconds. The length of the train is:
(a) 200 m (b) 240 m (c) 300 m (d) 360 m

**6.** Trains 100 m and 150 m long run in opposite directions, each at 45 km/h. They cross each other in:
(a) 20 seconds (b) 10 seconds (c) 5 seconds (d) 12 seconds

**7.** Two trains run in the same direction at 60 km/h and 42 km/h. The faster train passes a man sitting in the slower train in 20 seconds. The length of the faster train is:
(a) 100 m (b) 120 m (c) 333 m (d) 90 m

**8.** A man rows 16 km downstream in 2 hours and 8 km upstream in 2 hours. His speed in still water is:
(a) 4 km/h (b) 8 km/h (c) 2 km/h (d) 6 km/h

**9.** A man rows at 5 km/h in still water. Rowing upstream takes him twice as long as rowing the same distance downstream. The speed of the stream is:
(a) 2.5 km/h (b) 5/3 km/h (c) 1 km/h (d) 2 km/h

**10.** A train at 90 km/h passes a man walking at 9 km/h in the same direction in 10 seconds. The length of the train is:
(a) 250 m (b) 275 m (c) 225 m (d) 200 m

## Answer key
| Q | Ans | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | b | 12 − 3 = 9 |
| 2 | c | (20 − 14)/2 = 3 |
| 3 | a | 72 km/h = 20 m/s; 180/20 = 9 |
| 4 | d | 54 km/h = 15 m/s; 600/15 = 40 |
| 5 | c | Speed = 200/(20 − 12) = 25 m/s; L = 25 × 12 = 300 |
| 6 | b | 90 km/h = 25 m/s; 250/25 = 10 |
| 7 | a | 18 km/h = 5 m/s; 5 × 20 = 100 m |
| 8 | d | D = 8, U = 4 → (8 + 4)/2 = 6 |
| 9 | b | 5 + v = 2(5 − v) → 3v = 5 → v = 5/3 |
| 10 | c | 81 km/h = 22.5 m/s; 22.5 × 10 = 225 m |
