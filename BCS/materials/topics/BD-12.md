# 1971: Operation Searchlight, declaration, Mujibnagar Govt

> **Why it matters:** Very frequently asked: the night of 25 March, the first resistance (Rajarbagh), the declaration of independence and Kalurghat radio, the Proclamation of Independence (10 April), the oath at Baidyanathtala (17 April) and the ministers of the Mujibnagar Government.

## Operation Searchlight (অপারেশন সার্চলাইট) 🔥🔥
- The Pakistan army's planned crackdown on the night of **25 March 1971**.
- Planned by **General টিক্কা খান** and **Major General রাও ফরমান আলী** (with Maj. Gen. Khadim Hussain Raja). Tikka Khan is remembered as the **"Butcher of Bengal"**.
- Main targets in Dhaka:
  - **Dhaka University** — **জগন্নাথ হল**, **ইকবাল হল** (now Zahurul Haq Hall), teachers' quarters. Teachers killed that night included **ড. গোবিন্দচন্দ্র দেব** and **জ্যোতির্ময় গুহঠাকুরতা** (died of wounds).
  - 🔥 **রাজারবাগ পুলিশ লাইন্স** — the police put up the **first armed resistance** of the Liberation War.
  - **পিলখানা** (EPR headquarters).
- Foreign journalist **সাইমন ড্রিং** (*Daily Telegraph*) hid in Dhaka and reported the massacre to the world. **Anthony Mascarenhas**'s article **"Genocide"** (*Sunday Times*, June 1971) also exposed it.
- 🔥 In 2017 the Jatiya Sangsad declared **25 March** as **গণহত্যা দিবস (Genocide Day)**.

## Declaration of independence 🔥🔥
- In the early hours of **26 March 1971**, before his arrest, **বঙ্গবন্ধু শেখ মুজিবুর রহমান** declared the independence of Bangladesh; the message was sent out by wireless (EPR). He was then **arrested** and flown to West Pakistan, where he was imprisoned and put on trial.
- **26 March = স্বাধীনতা দিবস (Independence Day).**
- 🔥 On **26 March**, Awami League leader **এম. এ. হান্নান** (Chattogram) read the declaration on the radio from **কালুরঘাট (Chattogram)**.
- The radio became **স্বাধীন বাংলা বেতার কেন্দ্র**. On **27 March 1971**, **Major জিয়াউর রহমান** read a declaration of independence from Kalurghat **on behalf of Bangabandhu**.
- The Constitution (after the 15th amendment, 2011) records Bangabandhu's declaration telegram in its **Sixth Schedule**.
- Swadhin Bangla Betar later broadcast from Kolkata. Famous programmes: **"চরমপত্র"** (**এম. আর. আখতার মুকুল**) and **"জল্লাদের দরবার"**.

> Note: Who "declared" independence has been a politically debated question. For exams, give the answers above: Bangabandhu's declaration on 26 March; M. A. Hannan's broadcast on 26 March; Ziaur Rahman's broadcast on behalf of Bangabandhu on 27 March.

## Proclamation of Independence (স্বাধীনতার ঘোষণাপত্র) 🔥
- Issued on **10 April 1971** by the elected representatives, at **Mujibnagar**; it gave the new government legal basis and took effect **from 26 March 1971**.
- It is sometimes called the **first (interim) constitution** of Bangladesh. It now forms the **Seventh Schedule** of the Constitution (15th amendment).

## Mujibnagar Government (মুজিবনগর সরকার) 🔥🔥
- **Formed: 10 April 1971.** **Oath: 17 April 1971** at the mango grove of **বৈদ্যনাথতলা, মেহেরপুর** — renamed **মুজিবনগর**. The **first government of Bangladesh**.
- The oath was administered and the Proclamation read by **অধ্যাপক ইউসুফ আলী** (MNA).
- **17 April = মুজিবনগর দিবস**.
- Its headquarters and secretariat worked from **8 Theatre Road, Kolkata**.

| Post | Person |
|---|---|
| **President** | **বঙ্গবন্ধু শেখ মুজিবুর রহমান** (in Pakistani jail) |
| **Vice-President & Acting President** | 🔥 **সৈয়দ নজরুল ইসলাম** |
| **Prime Minister** | 🔥 **তাজউদ্দীন আহমদ** (first PM of Bangladesh) |
| Foreign Affairs & Law | **খন্দকার মোশতাক আহমদ** |
| Finance | **এম. মনসুর আলী** |
| Home, Relief & Rehabilitation | **এ. এইচ. এম. কামারুজ্জামান** |
| **Commander-in-Chief** | **কর্নেল (অব.) এম. এ. জি. ওসমানী** |
| Chief of Staff | **Lt. Col. আবদুর রব** |
| Deputy Chief of Staff (and Air Force head) | **Group Captain এ. কে. খন্দকার** |

- Main tasks: running the war (sectors, forces), diplomacy abroad, caring for **about 1 crore refugees** in India, and broadcasting through Swadhin Bangla Betar.

## Quick revision
- Operation Searchlight: night of **25 March 1971**; planners **Tikka Khan, Rao Farman Ali**.
- First armed resistance: **Rajarbagh Police Lines**.
- Genocide Day: **25 March** (declared 2017).
- Kalurghat broadcasts: **M. A. Hannan (26 March)**, **Ziaur Rahman (27 March)**.
- Proclamation of Independence: **10 April 1971**; effective from 26 March.
- Oath: **17 April 1971**, **Baidyanathtala, Meherpur** → Mujibnagar.
- Acting President **Syed Nazrul Islam**; PM **Tajuddin Ahmad**; C-in-C **M. A. G. Osmani**.
- Oath administered by **Prof. Yusuf Ali**.
- "Charampatra" — **M. R. Akhtar Mukul**.
