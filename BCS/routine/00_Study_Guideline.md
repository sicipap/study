# BCS Preparation: 6-Month Study Guideline (4 Hours a Day)

**Plan period:** 1 October 2026 → 31 March 2027 (182 days)
**Daily study:** 4 hours (4 blocks of 50 min study + 10 min break)
**Weekly rhythm:** Saturday–Thursday = new topics / revision · Friday = test + review

> Check the latest BCS circular and syllabus on the PSC website (bpsc.gov.bd) before you start. If the exam pattern or date changes, keep the order of topics and move the dates.

---

## 1. Know the exam

### Preliminary (MCQ): 200 marks, 2 hours, −0.5 for each wrong answer

| Subject | Marks | Hours per week in this plan |
|---|---|---|
| Bangla Language & Literature | 35 | 4 |
| English Language & Literature | 35 | 4 |
| Bangladesh Affairs | 30 | 3 |
| International Affairs | 20 | 2 |
| Geography, Environment & Disaster Management | 10 | 1 |
| General Science | 15 | 2 |
| Computer & Information Technology | 15 | 2 |
| Mathematical Reasoning | 15 | 3 |
| Mental Ability | 15 | 2 |
| Ethics, Values & Good Governance | 10 | 1 |
| **Total** | **200** | **24 + Friday** |

Hours follow the marks. Math gets a little extra because it only improves with regular practice.

### Written (general cadre): 900 marks
Bangla 200 · English 200 · Bangladesh Affairs 200 · International Affairs 100 · Math & Mental Ability 100 · General Science & Technology 100.
**Viva:** 200 marks.

This 6-month plan targets the **Preliminary**. Everything you learn here is also the base for the Written exam. Start Written-specific practice (essays, translation, analytical writing) after the Preliminary.

## 2. The three phases

| Phase | Days | Dates | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Foundation** | 1–112 (16 weeks) | 1 Oct 2026 – 20 Jan 2027 | Study the full syllabus once, one topic per hour. Make short notes. Weekly test every Friday, and a 200-mark monthly model test on the last Friday of each month. |
| **2. Revision + PYQ** | 113–154 (6 weeks) | 21 Jan – 3 Mar 2027 | Revise every topic a second time, 2–3 topics per hour. Solve **previous BCS questions (10th–latest)** for each topic. Subject test every Friday. |
| **3. Model tests** | 155–182 (4 weeks) | 4 Mar – 31 Mar 2027 | 11 full 200-mark model tests (Sat/Mon/Wed), rapid revision of hot topics on the other days, final look in the last 2 days. |

## 5. How to study each hour (the 50-minute method)

1. **10 min:** Read the topic from the textbook or guide.
2. **20 min:** Write **short notes** in your own notebook (one notebook per subject). Put key facts, dates and formulas in a box.
3. **15 min:** Solve **previous BCS MCQs** on that topic (from a question bank).
4. **5 min:** Write the mistakes in a **"Mistake Notebook"**. This notebook is what you revise in the final month.

---

## 6. Negative-marking strategy (−0.5)

- Answer when you are **sure**, or when you can **eliminate 2 of the 4 options**. That is a good bet.
- If you are completely blind on a question, skip it.
- A safe target: attempt **160–175**, with **85%+ accuracy**. That usually clears the cutoff comfortably.
- In every model test, record: attempted · correct · wrong · net score. Track the trend in a table.

## 7. Books and resources

| Subject | Resources |
|---|---|
| Base for all subjects | **NCTB textbooks, Classes 6–10** (Bangla grammar, BGS, Science, ICT, Math) |
| Previous questions | Any complete BCS question bank with explanations (e.g. *Jobs Solution*, *Professor's*, *MP3*) |
| Bangla | NCTB *Bangla Bhasar Byakoron* (Class 9–10), a BCS Bangla literature guide |
| English | A grammar book (e.g. *Saifur's*), a vocabulary book, a BCS English literature guide |
| Math | *Khairul's Basic Math* or a similar book, NCTB Class 8–10 math |
| Bangladesh Affairs | **Constitution of Bangladesh**, **Bangladesh Economic Review** (Finance Division), BBS Census 2022 summary |
| International Affairs | A monthly current affairs magazine, an international affairs guide |
| Current affairs | Daily newspaper and a monthly magazine; make a one-page summary each month |

Don't collect too many books. **One main book per subject plus a question bank is enough.** Revise the same book many times.

---

## 8. Golden rules

1. **Consistency beats intensity.** 4 hours every day for 6 months is better than 10 hours for a few days.
2. **Hot topics first** (🔥 in the routine). About 20% of topics produce about 60% of the questions.
3. **Revise every Friday.** Without revision you will forget about 70% within a month.
4. **Previous questions are your syllabus map.** Many questions repeat or come from the same area.
5. **Don't skip the tests.** The exam is 2 hours of pressure, and only timed tests train you for that.
6. **Missed a day?** Don't panic or skip ahead. Use Friday's "Backlog" hour to catch up.
7. Sleep 7 hours, exercise 20 minutes, and eat properly. Your brain is your main tool.
